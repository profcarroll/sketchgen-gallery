This sketch depicts an evolving abstract vegetable garden where organic forms grow and shift across the canvas. Each "plant" begins as a small colored cluster that gradually expands and changes hue over time. The forms move slowly and unpredictably, creating a living tapestry of color and motion. The composition continuously evolves with new plants appearing and old ones disappearing, simulating the natural process of growth and change in a garden ecosystem. The visual effect is achieved through simple yet effective particle-like behaviors that create an organic, ever-changing landscape without any explicit sound or user interaction beyond the initial canvas load.
