Brief

The viewer encounters a colossal, liminal space defined by massive pathways of deeply rusted steel. These curved, monumental plates guide the movement, echoing the relentless continuity of industrial architecture. The predominant color is deep umber and oxidized orange, textured with the patina of time. The experience is structured by the body’s movement through the confined passage. Suddenly, the seemingly endless sweep of the curvature arrests, shifting into a sharply perpendicular wall that forces an abrupt stop and redirects the pathway's flow, maximizing the visual and physical interruption.
