This sketch creates an immersive, industrial environment using WebGL rendering to depict a vast rusted steel pathway. The composition features curved, monumental plates in deep umber and oxidized orange tones that guide the viewer's eye through space. A sudden perpendicular wall interrupts the flow, creating a dramatic visual and spatial shift. The scene responds to mouse movement through interactive lighting and perspective adjustments, while subtle animations simulate the passage of time and the weight of industrial architecture.
