This sketch creates a hypnotic pulsing effect using concentric rings that expand and contract from the center of the screen. The rings cycle through a vibrant palette of gold, deep green, and rich red, creating a rhythmic breathing pattern. Each ring moves at its own speed and direction, with the entire system evolving in a steady, mesmerizing loop. The visual effect relies on simple geometric shapes and color interpolation to produce an engaging, continuously shifting composition that fills the canvas without any external input.
