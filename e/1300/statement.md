This is a slow drone flight through a campus that nobody has used in years. The buildings are raw-concrete slabs, cantilevered blocks sitting on recessed cores, and narrow stair towers on plinths, all laid out on a street grid and pierced by deep-set window grids. Walkways cross the streets overhead, so the drone passes under them as it follows an L-shaped route with rounded turns. It banks into each turn, drifts side to side, and rises and sinks a little. Everything is projected by hand as flat grey-beige polygons painted from far to near. Fog swallows the distance, each face has its own hard tone, and rain stains streak the near walls. Puddles, weeds and worn lane dashes mark the ground, dust hangs in the air, and a thin overlay shows altitude, heading, speed, a timecode and a blinking record light.
