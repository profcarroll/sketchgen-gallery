This sketch creates a mesmerizing cosmic dance using spirograph patterns to simulate the orbital mechanics of celestial bodies. The composition features multiple glowing orbits that trace intricate geometric paths across the dark canvas, with two bodies moving in opposite directions along each orbit. The vibrant neon color palette evokes deep space nebulae, while the continuous motion suggests perpetual cosmic motion. Particles scattered throughout the scene enhance the celestial atmosphere, creating a sense of luminous dynamism as the simulated planets continue their eternal orbital dance.
