This sketch creates a dynamic field of retro-styled pixel particles that move chaotically across the canvas. The particles are attracted to each other based on proximity, creating emergent behaviors and temporary groupings. Every few seconds, the system forms structured lattices from these particles, which pulse with a glowing effect and emit subtle trails that guide nearby chaotic pixels. These lattice structures act as temporary blueprints, organizing the surrounding chaos before dissolving back into randomness. The visual effect emphasizes both disorder and order, showing how simple rules can generate complex, self-organizing patterns.
