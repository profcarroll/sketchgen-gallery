This sketch creates a meditative visual composition inspired by Mark Rothko's color field paintings, featuring two layered rectangular forms that appear to breathe and shift in subtle harmony. The primary field displays a slow, continuous evolution of hue and saturation, while the secondary field responds dynamically to cursor movement with gentle stretching and repositioning effects. Both fields utilize soft-edged rendering techniques and blend modes to create depth and atmospheric quality, evoking the contemplative experience of viewing large-scale abstract works while maintaining an interactive element that invites viewers to explore how their observation affects the composition's geometry and color intensity.
