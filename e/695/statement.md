This sketch presents a steampunk-inspired sculpture composed of a central rotating cube surrounded by orbiting orbs and intricate glowing tracery. The piece evokes the idea of a living, bioluminescent structure through dynamic movement and shifting light effects. The emerald glass effect is achieved using translucent fills and pulsating particles that simulate internal glow, while the copper-like metallic elements appear in the rotating cube's structure. All motion occurs autonomously without user input, creating an immersive, self-contained scene that demonstrates complex orbital mechanics within a constrained visual framework.
