This sketch simulates the growth and movement of a complex biological mass resembling slime mold, rendered in three-dimensional space with realistic lighting. The structure continuously evolves through organic expansion and shifting forms, featuring both soft, folded regions and sharp, crystalline edges. The visual effect emphasizes intricate surface details through intersecting layers of striations and vein patterns that appear to shift and interact dynamically across the mass. The piece explores themes of biological emergence and geometric complexity through procedural animation and layered visual textures.
