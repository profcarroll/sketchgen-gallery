This sketch presents a serene yet dynamic alpine winter scene that transforms upon interaction. Initially static and composed of icy blue peaks and rugged foreground rocks, the landscape responds to a viewer's click by triggering an avalanche. The debris settles into the foreground channels, creating pockets of mineral-dusted water that refract light through their crevices, producing vivid jewel-toned highlights against the cold palette. The scene employs WEBGL rendering for depth and spatial complexity, with particle systems simulating dust settling and water pooling to convey the transformation from stillness to motion.
