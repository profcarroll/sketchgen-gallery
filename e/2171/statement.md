This sketch depicts a dynamic cross-section of layered rock undergoing continuous tectonic stress and failure. The visualization shows colorful strata that shift subtly over time, with stress lines appearing as faint cracks and larger faults forming where segments break away. Fine crumbled material accumulates in the gaps, creating an unstable landscape that constantly reforms. The perpetual motion represents the slow but relentless forces of geological change, where each frame captures a moment in the ongoing process of earth's reshaping.
