This sketch creates a dynamic steampunk sculpture centered on a rotating copper cube surrounded by orbiting globe splines with emerald glass tracery. The composition features a central core that spins slowly while decorative elements circle around it in intricate orbits. The emerald glass components display a dense crystalline lattice work through thousands of individually animated particles that create depth and complexity within the transparent surfaces. The entire structure moves organically with smooth, continuous motion, emphasizing the interplay between metallic tarnish and luminous green glass. The scene demonstrates how complex visual systems can be built using minimal geometric primitives and careful attention to color relationships and movement rhythms.
