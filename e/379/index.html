Brief

The canvas presents a fluid visualization composed of horizontal bands of color. These bands are animated in concert, smoothly rising and falling in a rhythmic, slow sine wave pattern, mimicking the passage of tides. The overall structure is continuous and cyclical. A distinctive, darker band marks the current high-water level, appearing within the overall movement. The entire visualization undergoes slow, automatic movement, creating the impression of breath and ebb, with no user input required to observe the phenomenon.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This visualization depicts a fluid, tidal landscape composed of horizontal color bands that move in harmony with a slow sine wave rhythm. Each band represents a layer of water at different depths, shifting smoothly to mimic the ebb and flow of tides. A distinctive dark band marks the current high-water level, creating a visual anchor within the continuous motion. The entire composition breathes naturally without any external input, offering a meditative representation of fluid dynamics through pure color and movement.
