This sketch creates an abstract, geometric quilt surface composed of large, tessellating structural modules that interlock across the entire canvas. Each module is a repeating pattern of squares, triangles, hexagons, or diamonds that subtly ripples and reshapes over time. The modules are arranged in a grid that fills the screen, forming rhythmic, continuous patterns that shift through a dynamic color palette representing the passage of time. The modules themselves morph slowly with wave-like motion, creating a fluid transition between shapes and colors while maintaining their structural coherence across the composition.
