This sketch visualizes an underground network of tunnels and flowing water streams. It depicts the dynamic interaction between solid rock walls—represented by brown, winding paths—and vibrant blue and cyan streams that flow through them. When a viewer clicks anywhere on the canvas, that point initiates a new tunnel segment, causing surrounding paths to widen and triggering the formation of new streams at the intersection. The system creates a continuous sense of movement and growth, simulating how subterranean currents might evolve and expand from a single point of origin.
