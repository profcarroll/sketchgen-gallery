This sketch depicts deep, oscillating plumes of gas and smoke rising from an unseen subsurface source, rendered as a field of glowing particles that pulse and drift across a gradient background. The visual effect is achieved through a particle system in WEBGL mode, where each particle has its own vertical oscillation, horizontal drift, and pulsing size to simulate the slow movement of expanding gas. The color palette shifts from deep indigo to sapphire blue with phosphorescent white highlights that give the impression of glowing smoke, while the constant motion creates a rhythmic, hypnotic quality as the plumes ebb and flow across the canvas without any user input required.
