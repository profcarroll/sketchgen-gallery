This sketch creates a living lattice of glowing cells that simulate a biological life cycle. Each cell pulses with its own rhythm, growing bright when born and fading as it ages, while a gentle wave effect subtly darkens and brightens the entire field. The system evolves autonomously without any user input, with new cells constantly being born in a natural, organic pattern. The visual effect captures the essence of a dynamic biological ecosystem where life cycles intersect and overlap, creating an ever-shifting tapestry of light and shadow that pulses with its own internal rhythm.
