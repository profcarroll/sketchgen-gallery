This sketch creates a richly textured textile pattern with deep jewel tones and strict symmetry. The composition features a repeating lattice grid where each cell contains a stylized floral motif composed of interconnected curves, petals, and geometric elements. The pattern maintains perfect bilateral symmetry through alternating motifs and consistent color relationships. Distinct border bands showcase specialized geometric patterns that frame the central textile design, creating a cohesive artisanal artwork. The piece demonstrates how complex visual structures can emerge from simple geometric principles and mathematical repetition, resulting in an intricate yet harmonious composition that appears to pulse with subtle motion despite being static.
