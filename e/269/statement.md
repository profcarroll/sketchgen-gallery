This sketch visualizes a complex digital network data flow using WebGL rendering for 3D depth and motion. It simulates interconnected pathways of glowing particles that move dynamically through space, forming temporary geometric patterns before continuing their chaotic dance. The visualization uses a combination of particle systems, grid-based connections, and dynamic color shifts to represent the flow and interaction of data across a virtual network. The scene maintains constant motion through procedural generation and mathematical relationships, creating an immersive but computationally efficient representation of digital information movement.
