This sketch creates a mesmerizing ripple effect using concentric grayscale rings that emanate from the center of the canvas. Each ring expands outward at its own speed, creating a wave-like pattern reminiscent of water ripples or sound waves. The rings fade out as they expand, and their opacity varies to create depth and visual interest. The animation is entirely self-contained, requiring no user input to function, and the continuous motion ensures that the canvas changes visibly over time.
