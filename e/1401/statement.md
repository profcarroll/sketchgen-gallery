This sketch creates a dense, intricate web of overlapping cables and wires that fill the canvas in a chaotic, entangled pattern. The system consists of multiple twisted cable paths drawn in dark, muted colors, forming an organic tangle that suggests a complex electrical network. Within this static structure, vibrant particles flow along the cables, creating streams of light that pulse and shimmer with rhythmic intensity. The visual effect conveys the continuous flow of electrical energy through a disorganized system, where the glowing particles represent signals traveling through the network. The motion appears to flow perpetually through the chaotic web, creating an illusion of constant activity within the otherwise stationary structure.
