This sketch creates an intricate pattern of interlocking crystalline bands that evoke the precision and mechanical beauty of an antique plotting machine. The structures appear to emerge from a central point, expanding outward in layered bands that pulse with shifting energy gradients. Each crystal formation consists of multiple concentric rings that subtly ripple and undulate, creating a sense of dynamic movement within rigid geometric forms. The color palette flows continuously through the spectrum, with each band displaying its own hue that shifts over time. Connecting lines between formations create a network effect, suggesting the mechanical interplay between individual components. The overall composition fills the canvas with a hypnotic pattern that appears to breathe and flow, capturing both the mathematical precision and organic energy of crystalline structures.
