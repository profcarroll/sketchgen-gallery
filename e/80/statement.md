This sketch creates a perpetually cycling atmospheric cityscape that transitions smoothly between day and night phases. The scene features bioluminescent flora that pulse with vibrant, shifting colors, casting ripples across the reflective wet surfaces of futuristic architecture. The entire environment operates through mathematical motion without user interaction, driven by time-based color transitions and procedural generation. The WebGL renderer creates a three-dimensional space where glowing elements interact dynamically with the environment, producing an immersive, ever-changing urban landscape that feels alive with subtle, organic movement.
