Brief

The canvas is filled with a dense, intricate lattice of vibrant, glowing ethereal lines that crisscross across the screen. Periodically, geometric crystalline structures emerge and pulse with internal light within this network. As these structures pulse, they emit a visible resonant frequency. This frequency causes the adjacent glowing lines in the lattice to visibly vibrate and ripple in response, creating a mesmerizing, harmonic oscillation throughout the entire structure.
