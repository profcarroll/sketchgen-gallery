This sketch creates a mesmerizing mandala pattern composed of thousands of individual sand grains in earthy hues, initially appearing stable and intricate. Over time, an invisible wind force gradually destabilizes the structure, causing grains to vibrate and detach one by one from their positions. The simulation shows how even the most elaborate patterns can slowly dissolve under constant, gentle pressure. As grains break free, they scatter independently across the canvas, transforming the ordered composition into a field of static particles. The effect demonstrates the fragility of complex structures and how persistent small forces can gradually erode even the most stable-looking formations.
