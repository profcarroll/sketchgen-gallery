This sketch portrays a vast, rusted celestial clockwork mechanism viewed through the delicate weave of a loom. The composition features intricate gears with varying numbers of teeth, interconnected springs, and geometric linkages that move in complex, cyclical patterns. Using the WEBGL renderer, it creates a three-dimensional perspective where metallic hues dominate—a palette of aged copper, deep rust reds, and dark oiled browns. The movement is patient and eternal, with each element subtly shifting and realigning to form glowing geometric patterns across the canvas. The viewer witnesses a cosmic rhythm repeating endlessly through the lens of this mechanical tapestry, where the precise alignment of components generates an ever-renewing visual symphony of light and metal.
