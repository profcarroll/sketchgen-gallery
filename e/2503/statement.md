This sketch creates an evolving crystalline landscape driven by a simulated pendulum mechanism. Like geological time unfolding through mineral veins, the display shows structures that grow and morph with each swing of an invisible pendulum. The crystals begin as simple geometric forms and gradually develop complex branching patterns influenced by the rhythmic motion. Colors shift subtly between blues and purples, creating a sense of depth and time passing. The viewer experiences a mesmerizing blend of organic growth and mechanical rhythm, where each frame reveals new arrangements in an endless cycle of formation and dissolution.
