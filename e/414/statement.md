This sketch depicts a dynamic, evolving ecosystem beneath a substrate surface, rendering amorphous biological growth and thread-like mycelial filaments in real-time. Using the WEBGL renderer, it creates a wet, textured environment with volumetric lighting effects that give the slime mold structures a realistic, organic appearance. The scene continuously evolves through time-based animations, where growth patterns shift slowly and persistently across the canvas, capturing the essence of biological expansion and movement without any user interaction.
