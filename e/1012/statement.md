This sketch presents a static arrangement of five musical pads arranged horizontally across the canvas. Each pad is a rounded rectangle with a distinct color and corresponds to a different musical note played through the p5.sound library. When a viewer clicks any one of these pads, it instantly illuminates in its full color and plays a corresponding tone. The interaction is immediate and visual—clicking a pad causes both a flash of light and an audible sound. The design remains still otherwise, with no animations or automatic changes unless triggered by user input. This minimal interface explores the relationship between visual feedback and musical output through direct interaction.
