This sketch creates a static, abstract composition of macaroni-like forms arranged in a dense, interlocking field. Each form is rendered as a cluster of curved, interconnected shapes with sharp edges and clear structural separation from its neighbors. The palette draws from rich, creamy yellow-orange hues that create depth and cohesion across the canvas. The work explores the tension between organic complexity and geometric clarity through carefully modulated curves and layered forms. The static nature ensures that viewers can study the interplay of structure and form without motion distraction.
