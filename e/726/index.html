Brief

The canvas displays an abstract interplay between solid, monolithic architectural planes and luminous energy. Computational rays are cast from an unseen source, intersecting the rigid geometry and mapping complex shadows onto the surfaces. These energy streams are continuously tracked, showing temporal dissipation and leaving behind visible, etched trails or crystalline patterns where the energy meets the solid structure. The interplay creates a shifting field of light and residual color, providing a dynamic visualization of geometric interaction over time.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch visualizes the dynamic interplay between rigid architectural forms and luminous energy fields. Through a combination of geometric planes that rotate slowly in space and energetic rays that move independently through 3D space, it creates an abstract representation of light interacting with solid matter. The rays leave glowing trails that dissipate over time, simulating the temporal nature of energy flow. Architectural planes cast shadows and interact with these energy streams, creating complex visual patterns where solid structures meet luminous force. The result is a constantly shifting field of light and shadow that suggests both the physical properties of geometric forms and the invisible dynamics of energy flow through space.
