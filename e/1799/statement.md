This sketch depicts a vast desert landscape where the interplay of light and shadow creates an hypnotic, cyclical rhythm. The dark field is populated with thousands of individually textured sand particles that shift subtly in hue and brightness to create a realistic sandy surface. A pulsing glow sweeps across the canvas from center to edge, illuminating the terrain with a warm, ethereal light that momentarily reveals the intricate texture of the desert sand. As the glow moves, it creates an illusion of wave-like motion across the landscape, with areas of illumination revealing the rich, varied tones and subtle variations in the sand's surface texture. The effect is mesmerizing and perpetually shifting, capturing the essence of a desert under changing light conditions.
