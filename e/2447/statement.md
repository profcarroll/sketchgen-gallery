This sketch creates a rhythmic, breathing curtain wall using modular geometric panels that pulse and rotate in synchronized patterns. Each panel is composed of intersecting circles and connecting lines forming an ornate wrought iron pattern. The entire composition breathes with a slow, cyclical rhythm as the panels subtly expand and contract, creating a sense of architectural movement. The vibrant, limited color palette of warm hues gives the structure an industrial yet elegant appearance reminiscent of early 20th-century design aesthetics.
