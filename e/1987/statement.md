This sketch depicts subterranean magma flow as seen from a satellite view, with fluid, cyclical pulses of bright gold and vibrant green light. The visualization features concentric rings that expand and contract rhythmically, creating a pulsing effect that simulates the movement of magma beneath the earth's surface. The center of this energetic pulse travels in a continuous figure-eight pattern across the screen, representing the dynamic flow of molten material deep underground. The gold and green color scheme emphasizes the intense heat and luminous quality of the magma, while the rhythmic pulsing creates a sense of cyclical geological processes at work beneath our feet.
