This sketch depicts a serene alpine valley scene with mountains, a winding river, and snow-covered peaks. The composition uses cool blues, whites, and pine greens to create an atmospheric winter landscape. The scene features subtle animated snow particles floating gently down the canvas. When clicked, the peaceful moment is shattered by an explosive avalanche that sweeps across the valley floor, sending debris cascading through the landscape before settling back into stillness. The technique uses layered drawing with gradient backgrounds, procedural mountain generation, and particle systems to create both the static environment and the dynamic avalanche effect. The viewer experiences a transition from calm serenity to dramatic chaos triggered by user interaction.
