This sketch creates a mesmerizing fluid constellation of bioluminescent forms that drift across a dark canvas. Each form pulses rhythmically with shifting blue and purple hues, accompanied by ethereal smoke-like tails that trail behind them. The system uses a simple physics model where each form moves independently, bounces off the edges of the canvas, and continuously generates glowing trails. The pulsing effect creates a rhythmic heartbeat that makes the forms feel alive and responsive to the dark environment. The visual result is an ever-shifting constellation that maintains its own dynamic energy while respecting the technical constraints of performance and memory usage.
