This sketch depicts an organic, glowing expanse of luminous shapes resembling bacterial cultures drifting across the canvas. Using noise-based movement, each culture cluster follows a slow, exploratory search pattern that mimics slime mould behavior. The bioluminescent palette—deep blues and greens with varying opacity—creates a meditative flow as clusters weave through space. The visual effect emerges from many soft, irregular polygons moving independently yet in harmony, creating an ever-shifting landscape of living light.
