This sketch visualizes the slow, patient process of crystallization deep underground, where mineral structures gradually form and evolve over time. The scene displays a vast network of glowing lines that connect points representing atoms or crystal nuclei, creating an intricate lattice that shifts subtly with each frame. Like geological time itself, the patterns emerge organically, with connections forming and dissolving as elements settle into their positions. The gentle pulsing background color evokes the slow pressure and transformation beneath the earth's surface, while the glowing connections suggest the energy of formation and the lasting residue of ancient processes.
