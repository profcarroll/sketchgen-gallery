This sketch creates an organic, ever-expanding network of interconnected lines that evoke the structure of root systems. Using a recursive algorithm, it generates branching patterns that grow outward from random starting points across the canvas. The system uses a pulsing animation to create gentle, continuous motion throughout the entire composition, giving the impression of perpetual growth and expansion. The color palette shifts subtly over time, creating a warm, fibrous texture that mimics knitted wool. The visual field remains in constant, soft motion without any external interaction, presenting an evolving natural form that fills the entire canvas with its organic complexity.
