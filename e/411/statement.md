This sketch creates a mesmerizing grid of pulsating squares that breathe with warm, organic rhythms against a dark canvas. Each square has its own independent breathing pulse, creating a subtle, living field of light. When a viewer clicks anywhere on the canvas, a ripple wave emanates from the point of impact, causing affected squares to flash momentarily and temporarily shift their base color. The wave effect creates a cascading visual experience that moves outward in a smooth, expanding circle, with each square reacting uniquely to the disturbance. The entire composition operates as a self-contained, evolving system where the interaction between the viewer's input and the natural rhythm of the grid creates an engaging, responsive visual meditation on motion and stillness.
