Brief

The canvas reveals a deep, cylindrical tunnel composed of countless crystalline facets. These facets explode outwards and reflect light, creating a continuous, rich kaleidoscope of polychromatic colors. The entire field of vision is unified by the appearance of a single, dark hexagon that acts as the primary mask. The camera appears to travel through this structure, but instead of a rapid rush, the movement slows dramatically, giving the impression of a gentle, rhythmic pulse—a slow, breathing inhale and exhale of light and color that cycles endlessly.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive cylindrical tunnel of crystalline facets that appear to pulse and breathe with light. Using the WEBGL renderer, it generates thousands of faceted spheres that float within a dark space, each animated independently to create a sense of organic movement. The camera slowly traverses through this environment, moving in a gentle, rhythmic pattern that mimics breathing. A dark hexagonal mask overlays the entire scene, creating a unified focal point while allowing the kaleidoscopic colors and light patterns to flow around it. The animation is driven entirely by mathematical functions, creating an endless, hypnotic cycle of light and color that fills the entire canvas without any external input.
