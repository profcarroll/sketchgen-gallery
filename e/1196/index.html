Brief

On a black canvas, rhythmic pulses of red and black rhythmically expand outward. As each pulse spreads, it leaves a persistent trail of glowing light residue that lingers behind its movement. Occasionally, these expanding waves distort into jagged, asymmetric ripples before reforming or drifting across the void as large, interconnected networks. The scene evolves continuously without requiring user interaction, filling the screen with evolving geometric patterns and fading trails.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a continuously evolving visual landscape of rhythmic, expanding pulses that emanate from random points across a black canvas. Each pulse grows outward with a pulsing red hue, leaving behind glowing trails that fade gradually. The system introduces occasional distortions—jagged ripples—that disrupt the smooth expansion before reforming into new patterns. The result is an organic, ever-changing field of geometric light, where persistence and motion interplay to create a sense of continuous transformation without any user interaction.
