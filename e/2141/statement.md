This sketch creates an ever-shifting grid of organic, asymmetrical rectangles that move gently across the canvas. The color palette uses deep, cool tones that transition smoothly over time, creating a sense of depth and motion. Each cell in the grid has its own size variation and subtle undulation, giving the overall pattern an irregular, natural feel rather than a rigid structure. The movement is slow and continuous, with no user interaction required to maintain the animation, resulting in a calming, immersive visual experience that feels like watching smoke or liquid flow through space.
