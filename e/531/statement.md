This sketch creates an immersive data center environment with interconnected server racks and flowing data streams. Viewers observe thousands of luminous particles weaving through a structured grid of metal frames, simulating the constant flow of computational data. The scene breathes with continuous movement as particle systems pulse and intertwine between hardware components, creating an illusion of massive simultaneous data transfer. The visualization uses WebGL rendering to efficiently handle thousands of elements while maintaining performance within strict frame budget constraints.
