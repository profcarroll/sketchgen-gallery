This sketch creates a highly intricate, symmetrical woven pattern reminiscent of a classic Persian rug. The composition features a central field with stylized floral motifs arranged within a lattice grid using deep, saturated jewel tones. The entire design is bordered by a continuous interlocking chain pattern made of alternating chevron and triangle shapes that gives the periphery a dynamic yet stable feel. The work presents a highly ordered visual rhythm through careful repetition and mathematical symmetry, resulting in a fixed decorative textile design that captures the complexity and beauty of traditional Persian weaving techniques without any motion or user interaction.
