Brief

The canvas displays vast, shifting planes of color. These luminous fields, dominated by cool aquamarine, cyan, and cerulean tones, constantly shift and intersect, suggesting deep underwater currents. Interrupted within these glowing currents are highly defined, sculptural forms that appear to float or drift. The entire scene is one of fluid, aqueous transformation. Although the fields move autonomously, the sculptural forms subtly react to the viewer's pointer, appearing to warp or intensify their glow when the cursor passes near them, while the luminous backdrop continues its slow, mesmerizing transformation.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive underwater scene with shifting luminous fields and floating sculptural forms. The background consists of multiple translucent, rotating spheres in cool aquamarine and cerulean hues that move autonomously, suggesting deep ocean currents. Within this fluid environment, eight distinct sculptural forms float freely, each with its own size and pulsing glow. When the viewer's cursor approaches these forms, they subtly intensify their luminosity, creating an interactive element that responds to the viewer's presence while maintaining the overall mesmerizing flow of the scene. The entire composition uses WebGL rendering for smooth 3D effects and continuous animation without any user input.
