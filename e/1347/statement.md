This sketch creates a mesmerizing, flowing network of vibrant light particles that emerge from and follow the structure of a loaded photograph. Each particle samples color directly from the image at its current position, creating streams of liquid light that move according to the image's underlying energy patterns. The particles weave intricate trails across the canvas, forming a constantly shifting tapestry where intersecting flows create sharply defined, brilliantly illuminated junctions. The visualization is entirely self-contained and continuously evolving without any user input, presenting a dynamic, breathing field of color that pulses with the rhythm of the source image's luminosity.
