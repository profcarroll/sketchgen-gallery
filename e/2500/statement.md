This sketch explores the tension between mathematical precision and organic chaos through a dynamic visual transformation. It begins with a rigid black-and-white grid that suggests typographic order, but over time, the lines begin to destabilize and morph into flowing, unpredictable shapes. The grid elements waver with noise-based motion while occasional chaotic forms emerge and evolve, creating a continuous dialogue between structured symmetry and fluid unpredictability. The WEBGL renderer allows for a more immersive, three-dimensional perspective on this transformation, emphasizing how the viewer's perception shifts from ordered geometry to emergent complexity.
