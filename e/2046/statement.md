This sketch renders a turbulent, textural field resembling coarse knitted wool disturbed by sudden wind. It captures the chaotic motion of frozen particles exploding and reforming in dense bursts. Using WebGL's batched point rendering, thousands of particles move with randomized trajectories influenced by simulated wind forces and turbulence. The visual texture emerges from a palette of pale blues and whites, creating an unstable curtain of frozen energy that continuously shifts and evolves. The viewer observes an energetic, self-contained scene of violent yet beautiful natural motion.
