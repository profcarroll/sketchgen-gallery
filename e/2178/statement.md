This sketch depicts a network of luminous, glowing roots spreading across a textured newsprint surface. The visual style captures the organic flow of wet ink bleeding into porous paper, with branching shapes that pulse rhythmically in a steady, life-like cadence. The roots are composed of warm orange and yellow hues with subtle glows, creating an effect that mimics fluid movement through organic channels. The network topology is established through spatial connections between nearby points, forming a dynamic web that pulses in unison while maintaining its structural coherence. The newsprint background, generated procedurally with noise-based texture, provides the grainy, porous surface that makes the luminous roots appear to bleed into the paper.
