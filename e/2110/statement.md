This sketch creates a mesmerizing, self-generating field of interconnected geometric planes that resemble the deep, glowing fractures found in petrified wood. The visualization uses rotating, orbiting planes with subtle crack-like deformations and pulsing connections between them. Vibrant luminous colors shift across the composition as the forms slowly move through space, creating an effect of deepening cracks spreading across a three-dimensional canvas. Floating nodes and glowing lines form a network that pulses and flickers in harmony with the overall motion. Two text blocks drift slowly across the scene, adding a human element to the otherwise natural, organic geometry. The entire visualization operates without any user input, generating its own dynamic patterns through mathematical motion and color transitions.
