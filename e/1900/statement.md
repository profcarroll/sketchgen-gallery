This sketch depicts a continuous cycle of geometric destruction and reconstruction using angular, shard-like forms. The animation begins with a stable structure made of cube fragments that vibrate gently before suddenly disintegrating into individual pieces under explosive force. These fragments then fall away from view but are drawn back together by invisible forces following a precise hexagonal lattice pattern. As they reassemble, the components lock into perfect interlocking arrays that gradually fill the visible space, creating an endless loop of deconstruction and perfect re-equilibration. The visual effect emphasizes both the violence of fragmentation and the mathematical precision of the reassembly process through clean geometric forms and structured motion patterns.
