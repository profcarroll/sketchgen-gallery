This sketch visualizes a detailed, cutaway view of an internal combustion engine using p5.js's WEBGL renderer to create a three-dimensional perspective. The engine shows synchronized mechanical movement with pistons oscillating within cylinders, connecting rods linking to a rotating crankshaft, and a camshaft driving the timing chain. The visualization demonstrates how these components work together in precise synchronization, with the timing chain continuously linking the crankshaft to the camshaft to maintain proper engine timing. The viewer observes a mechanical dance of moving parts that illustrates the fundamental principles of how internal combustion engines operate, with each component positioned and animated to show their interdependent motion within the engine's functional architecture.
