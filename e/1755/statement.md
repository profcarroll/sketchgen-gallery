This sketch renders a highly detailed Persian rug pattern within a tight lattice grid structure, evoking the rich textures and symmetrical complexity of traditional Persian textiles. The composition features a central field filled with stylized vine motifs arranged in a repeating grid, each motif rendered with jewel-toned colors and vertical symmetry for visual balance. Encircling this intricate center is a prominent border composed entirely of continuous interlocking chevron shapes that form a structured geometric framework around the entire canvas. The piece emphasizes both the mathematical precision and artistic elegance found in classical Persian rug weaving through its precise repetition and harmonic color relationships.
