This sketch renders a stylized equine figure in mid-gallop using continuous flowing curves to convey motion and power. The horse's body is constructed from a series of interconnected points that form a smooth, dynamic shape, while the spine features a sweeping curve that emphasizes fluid movement. The legs execute synchronized, exaggerated extensions that capture the full follow-through of a galloping stride. Through carefully orchestrated animation timing and a rich color palette, the sketch creates a sense of dynamic energy that captures the essence of powerful, rhythmic motion. The use of WEBGL rendering enables a three-dimensional perspective that enhances the feeling of movement and power.
