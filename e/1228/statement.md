This sketch presents a detailed three-dimensional visualization of an internal combustion engine's mechanical components. It features a cutaway view showing pistons moving within cylinders, connected to a rotating crankshaft, with synchronized intake and exhaust valves that open and close in precise coordination with the piston cycle. The engine operates in real-time, demonstrating the timing and interaction between all major moving parts. The visualization emphasizes the dynamic relationship between valve operation and piston motion, illustrating how gas exchange occurs during the engine's four-stroke cycle.
