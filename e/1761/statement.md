This sketch depicts a slow, organic transformation of a structured digital grid. Bright pixels form an initial geometric pattern that gradually dissolves as moss-like growth spreads across the canvas. As the vegetation advances, it causes the underlying pixels to shatter into splinters of vibrant color. These splinters don't disperse randomly but instead gather into flowing streams of liquid light that move and evolve across the screen, creating a dynamic interplay between structured order and organic chaos. The visual effect is both serene and energetic, showing nature reclaiming artificial space through continuous expansion and transformation.
