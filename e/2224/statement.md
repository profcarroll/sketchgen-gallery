This sketch creates an immersive, microscopic view of a Mandelbrot fractal's antenna transformed into a pulsing vascular network. Using a combination of particle systems and dynamic connections, it renders intricate vein-like filaments in deep crimson, translucent amber, and biological pink against a dark void. The scene continuously zooms deeper into the branching structures while allowing panning through mouse drag, creating an endless organic depth that draws viewers into the mathematical beauty of fractal geometry.
