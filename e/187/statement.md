This sketch creates an autonomous garden landscape where vibrant flowers bloom with dynamic, natural movement. Each flower features a stem, leaves that sway gently, and petals that subtly shift in position over time. The key visual element is the luminous particles that gather and accumulate in the center of each flower—these particles move slowly and erratically, suggesting a continuous process of pollination and life. The entire scene evolves without any user interaction, with particles being continuously generated and attracted to flower centers, creating an organic, cyclical ecosystem that feels both serene and alive.
