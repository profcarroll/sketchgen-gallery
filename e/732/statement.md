This sketch creates a mesmerizing cosmic field of orbiting particles that trace luminous streaks across the canvas. Each particle follows a circular path while maintaining a visible trail that fades over time, forming an intricate tapestry of color and motion. The system introduces occasional deviations where particles drift into elongated elliptical orbits before returning to their cyclical patterns. The interplay between regular orbital motion and unpredictable elliptical excursions creates a constantly evolving visual pattern reminiscent of celestial mechanics, with the entire composition shifting and flowing in an endless cosmic dance.
