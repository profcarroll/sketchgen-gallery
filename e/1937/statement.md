This sketch creates a haunting industrial scene of a vast, decaying ventilation system with a massive swinging pendulum at its center. The viewer observes an endless mechanical space where rusted metal structures form a complex geometric framework, illuminated by dynamic light that moves with the pendulum's rhythmic swing. The pendulum's motion creates shifting patterns of light and shadow across the entire scene, revealing the internal structure of the industrial environment through its constant, hypnotic movement. The composition balances architectural detail with organic motion, using WebGL rendering to create a volumetric sense of space while maintaining performance within strict frame budget constraints.
