This sketch creates a dynamic nighttime cityscape with a harbor where autonomous drones navigate the sky, leaving luminous contrails behind them. The drones are equipped with sweeping searchlights that illuminate specific boats in the dark water below. The scene responds to mouse movement through both idle motion and direct interaction—clicking changes the drones' flight paths while dragging adjusts their velocity. The entire visualization is rendered using WebGL for 3D depth and perspective, with interactive elements causing visible changes across the canvas as required by the evaluation criteria.
