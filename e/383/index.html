Brief

The canvas depicts vast, ethereal cloud formations set against a shifting gradient from midnight blue to deep violet. The primary cloud structures are comprised of flowing, dense lines that pulse subtly in opacity. Embedded deep within these cores are numerous small, glowing pinpoints of light. These light sources cast delicate, colored reflections across the surrounding nebulous gas. The entire scene embodies a continuous, gentle cosmic movement, giving the impression of a deep, breathing celestial cloud bank.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic scene with flowing cloud formations that pulse gently in opacity against a shifting midnight blue to deep violet gradient background. The clouds are drawn as soft, organic shapes that move slowly across the canvas, their edges flowing and breathing with subtle motion. Embedded within these celestial masses are hundreds of tiny glowing lights that pulse with varying rhythms, casting delicate colored reflections that enhance the cosmic atmosphere. The entire composition embodies a sense of continuous, gentle movement - a deep, breathing celestial cloud bank that evolves organically over time without any external input. The visual effect is achieved through layered transparent shapes, smooth motion curves, and carefully modulated color transitions that create an immersive, otherworldly environment.
