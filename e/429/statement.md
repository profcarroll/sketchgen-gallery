This sketch implements an interactive jigsaw puzzle where users can drag and position pieces to form a complete image. Each piece is a rectangle containing a visual fragment of the overall picture, positioned irregularly on the canvas. When a user clicks and drags a piece, it moves freely until released, at which point it snaps into place if it's near its correct grid position. The puzzle uses a simple grid-based snapping mechanism to guide users toward completing the image. The visual design emphasizes clarity with contrasting colors and clear edges, making the interactive elements obvious while maintaining an aesthetic balance between functionality and form.
