This sketch creates a dynamic particle trail that follows the mouse cursor as it moves across the canvas. The particles appear vividly at the cursor's position and then gradually fade out while shrinking, leaving behind a lingering afterglow effect. The system maintains a fixed maximum number of particles to ensure smooth performance, with each particle having its own fading and scaling behavior. The result is an ethereal, dissipating trail that reflects the entire path of mouse movement, creating a visually pleasing and responsive interactive experience.
