This sketch creates a mesmerizing mechanical abrasion effect through the interplay of rotating gears and cams. The viewer observes an extreme close-up of layered materials where brass mechanisms operate in synchronized motion, gradually revealing hidden patterns through their relentless action. Rather than drawing lines or applying color, the visualization shows pure absence—ghostly paths where graphite residue has been gently removed by the mechanical process. The particle system simulates sanding, with each point representing a microscopic abrasion that reveals underlying patterns over time. The visual rhythm emerges from the precise, mechanical motion of the components, creating an elegant representation of how physical forces can gradually transform surfaces to expose new forms and structures.
