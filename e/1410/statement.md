This sketch creates a dynamic cityscape where towers emerge and grow as you drag your cursor across the canvas. The visual style emphasizes geometric precision with reflective surfaces in cool grays and blues, giving the impression of modern, gleaming skyscrapers under construction. Each drag motion instantaneously adds new tower sections that rise from the ground plane, building an evolving architectural composition. The process simulates continuous construction where your cursor acts as a guide for new building development. The design stays within performance constraints by using efficient rendering techniques and fixed array sizes, ensuring smooth animation even with many elements.
