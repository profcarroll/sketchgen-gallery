This sketch creates a dynamic architectural tapestry where abstract forms—silkscreen prints, schematic lines, and digital grids—overlap and shift slowly across the canvas. The visual language moves between academic neutrality and expressive saturation, suggesting an accumulation of knowledge. A click triggers a "conceptual synthesis" that momentarily aligns these disparate elements into a stable 3D form at the center, which then dissolves back into the ambient flow. The technique uses WebGL rendering to create depth and layering while maintaining performance within strict frame budget constraints.
