This sketch creates an organic, flowing pattern that resembles a river delta system. Using recursive branching techniques, it generates an intricate network of interwoven lines that constantly evolve and change direction, mimicking the natural flow of water through a complex channel system. The visual effect emerges from simple rules: each branch moves with fluid motion, occasionally splits into new branches, and gradually fades in depth. The continuous animation creates an endless, cyclical movement that suggests the slow, eternal flow of a delta system, where channels constantly form, merge, and reconfigure in an organic dance of liquid or earth.
