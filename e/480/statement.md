This sketch creates a meditative cosmic environment through the continuous flow of translucent, overlapping circles that move across the canvas. The visual dynamics center on glowing wakes and dissipating light trails that form complex, faint filamentous patterns in the background. The system uses a combination of particle systems and streaming elements to simulate the sensation of deep space movement—where light structures appear and fade in an endless cycle of creation and dissolution. The result is a serene, evolving landscape of cosmic motion where patterns emerge from simple rules, evoking the quiet complexity of celestial phenomena.
