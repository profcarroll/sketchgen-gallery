Brief

The canvas presents a vast, reflective cityscape defined by sharp, angular structures and deep, wet surfaces. The color palette shifts dramatically through the cycle of day and night, moving from bright, ambient tones to deep, luminous blues and indigos. The scene is brought to life by embedded bioluminescent plants that pulse gently, casting distinct, colored projections onto the slick architecture. Complementing this rhythm are slow-moving, glowing clusters of luminous plankton that drift and accumulate across the wet surfaces, giving the entire setting a continuous, organic flow of light and color.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive, ever-changing cityscape that shifts through day and night cycles. Using WebGL rendering, it builds a reflective urban environment with sharp angular structures that respond to time-based color transitions. The scene pulses with organic life: bioluminescent plants gently pulse in rhythm, while slow-moving plankton clusters drift across wet surfaces, creating an interplay of artificial geometry and natural light patterns. The viewer experiences a continuous flow of color and motion without any input, as the entire composition evolves autonomously through time-based transformations.
