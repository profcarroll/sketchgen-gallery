This sketch creates an interactive stovetop interface where users can rotate a dial to control gas flames on four burner elements. The system uses a circular arrangement of burners with dynamic flame effects that respond to the dial's rotation, showing how each burner lights up proportionally to its angular distance from the dial's position. When burners are active, their metal casings glow with a vibrant red hue indicating heat. The interaction is designed to be responsive and visually engaging, simulating realistic flame dynamics using particle systems while maintaining performance within the frame budget constraints of the environment.
