This sketch depicts a subterranean rock cross-section animated with slow-flowing molten mineral veins. The visual effect is created through layered hexagonal patterns that pulse and shift in thermal gradients, rendering the surface with warm reds and oranges that subtly blend into cooler blues along diagonal pathways. The animation suggests ongoing geothermal energy flow beneath the visible structure, with dynamic color transitions and flowing lines that emphasize the movement of minerals through the rock. The technique uses multiple layered shapes with time-based color shifts to create an organic, evolving thermal pattern across the entire canvas.
