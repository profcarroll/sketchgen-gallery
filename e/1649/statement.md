This sketch creates an abstract sonar radar visualization where geometric lines emanate from the center and propagate outward in rhythmic pulses. The expanding trails exhibit distinct pulsating patterns that create a dynamic, wave-like effect across the canvas. Unlike a constant sweep, each line's glow intensity varies rhythmically over time, creating a mesmerizing pattern of pulsing light waves that move continuously without any user interaction required. The deep blue to violet color palette enhances the ethereal quality of the visualization, while the central point serves as the origin from which all patterns radiate.
