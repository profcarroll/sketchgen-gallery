This sketch creates an atmospheric sonar visualization featuring spiraling helix patterns that emanate from the center of the canvas. The visual design employs a deep blue-to-violet color palette with glowing, crystalline structures that pulse rhythmically. Each helix is composed of multiple interconnected points that form defined spiral patterns, creating a mesmerizing effect of propagating geometric forms. The patterns do not sweep continuously but exhibit distinct, rhythmic pulsations, with crystal-like nodes appearing along the helices at regular intervals. The visualization is entirely self-contained and autonomous, evolving without any user interaction while maintaining a consistent, hypnotic quality that draws the viewer's eye across the screen in an endless cycle of geometric expansion and contraction.
