This sketch creates an immersive deep-sea environment where bioluminescent organisms drift slowly through darkness. The scene features ethereal creatures with pulsing bodies and flowing tentacles, rendered in deep blues and greens that shift in intensity. Tiny particles float throughout the space, creating a sense of depth and movement. The organisms move autonomously using a spatial hash grid for efficient collision detection and connection drawing, simulating the gentle undulations of life in the abyss. The entire composition is self-contained, evolving without user input to evoke the quiet majesty of underwater existence.
