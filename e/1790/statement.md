This sketch creates a mesmerizing, continuously evolving pattern of overlapping rings composed of concentric circles. Each ring expands steadily outward from the center while cycling through vivid jewel tones in a smooth, hypnotic color transition. The animation is entirely self-contained and requires no interaction from the viewer. The visual effect emerges from the careful balance between multiple expanding circles within each ring, creating a sense of depth and motion that shifts gradually across the canvas. The palette transitions through saturated hues that blend seamlessly, producing an ever-changing yet harmonious display of color and form.
