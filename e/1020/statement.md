This sketch creates a dynamic ink trail system that responds to cursor movement speed. As the user drags their mouse across the canvas, particles are generated that accumulate into dense clusters. The system uses a combination of particle physics and spatial connectivity to form crystalline structures that intensify with velocity. Fast movements produce sharp, geometric patterns while slow motions create more organic, flowing trails. The visual effect emerges from the interaction between individual particles and their connections, creating an evolving landscape of concentrated ink patterns that persist over time through subtle canvas fading.
