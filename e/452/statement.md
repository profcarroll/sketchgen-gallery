This sketch creates a dynamic field of colorful particles that respond to both natural motion and user interaction. The system uses a grid-based spatial partitioning method to efficiently manage thousands of particles while maintaining performance. When the mouse approaches, particles scatter chaotically, but they also naturally gravitate toward nearby neighbors in a lattice-like pattern. The visual effect simulates an underlying order emerging from randomness, with particles temporarily organizing into structured formations before dissolving back into motion. The interplay between individual particle behavior and collective patterns creates a mesmerizing display of organized chaos.
