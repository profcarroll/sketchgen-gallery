This sketch depicts a serene corn field under the gentle glow of an hovering UFO. The scene features a disc-shaped craft that floats above the agricultural landscape, casting two dynamic light beams downward. These beams rhythmically pulse in diameter, creating shifting patterns of illumination and shadow across the corn stalks below. The effect is achieved through careful manipulation of color blending and geometric shapes to simulate the interaction between intense light and the field's vegetation. The continuous motion gives the impression of a living, responsive environment where artificial light disrupts the natural stillness of the countryside.
