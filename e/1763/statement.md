This sketch visualizes the molecular structure of DNA as a double helix composed of labeled base pairs (A, T, C, G) connected by hydrogen bonds. The animation begins with a stable helical structure and then demonstrates the unwinding process where specific base pairs separate. As the double helix unzips, the individual strands detach from their complementary partners and begin to drift away in independent trajectories, creating a dynamic visualization of DNA separation that reflects the breaking of hydrogen bonds during replication or transcription processes.
