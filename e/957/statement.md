This sketch presents a dynamic, cutaway cross-section of a combustion engine rendered in 3D using p5.js's WEBGL mode. The viewer observes the synchronized motion of pistons moving up and down within cylinders, connected to a rotating crankshaft via connecting rods. The animation demonstrates how linear piston motion translates into rotational force. Simultaneously, timing gears mesh and rotate, controlling intake and exhaust valves that open and close in perfect synchronization with the crankshaft's rotation. The engine block, cylinders, pistons, connecting rods, crankshaft, and timing gears all move together in a continuous mechanical dance, illustrating the fundamental principles of internal combustion engine operation through visual animation.
