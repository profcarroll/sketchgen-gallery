This sketch presents a meditative exploration of mathematical symmetry through spirographic patterns. Using precise trigonometric formulas, it generates interlocking polygonal forms that fill the canvas in an endlessly evolving yet motionless composition. The work emphasizes color saturation and geometric structure to create depth within a static field of form, where each pattern emerges from simple mathematical relationships—radius, angle, and harmonic frequency—transformed into complex visual tapestries. The viewer encounters a contemplative space of ordered complexity, observing the beauty inherent in mathematical precision without temporal movement.
