This sketch creates a chaotic flurry of cut-paper fragments that fill the canvas with rapid, violent motion. The composition features thousands of sharp-edged polygons—each representing a torn piece of paper—that collide and scatter continuously across the blank surface. Through continuous boundary bouncing, random velocity changes, and periodic reinitialization, the fragments form a dynamic debris field that never settles into stillness. Each fragment is rendered as an irregular polygon with randomized rotation, size, and movement, creating an overwhelming sense of perpetual motion and collision. The semi-transparent background allows for visual trail effects that emphasize the continuous nature of the chaos, while the constant addition and removal of fragments maintains the energy of the system over time.
