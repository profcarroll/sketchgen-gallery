This sketch depicts a peaceful sky filled with gently floating balloons that drift slowly across the canvas. Each balloon has its own size, speed, and color, creating a varied and organic feel. The balloons move with subtle bobbing motions, giving them a buoyant quality. When the user clicks anywhere on the canvas, all balloons momentarily adjust their positions and then resume their gentle drifting pattern. The visual effect is calming and serene, emphasizing the peaceful movement through simple geometric shapes and smooth animations.
