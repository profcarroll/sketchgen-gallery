Brief

The canvas displays scrolling red waveforms that pulse in rhythm, filling the screen with a continuous rhythmic beat. Clicking anywhere pauses the oscillation and draws a jagged flatline instead. Dragging across the surface erases the current trace before restarting the pattern at the cursor's location. The colors shift from deep crimson to bright orange as the beat accelerates automatically. No external sound input is required or utilized; the animation proceeds solely based on time and interaction events without needing WebGL rendering.
