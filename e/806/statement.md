This sketch creates a dynamic, abstract geometric composition using warped diagonal planes that shift and float in three-dimensional space. The visual effect emerges from the interplay between multiple vibrant, highly saturated color planes that continuously morph and realign around fixed anchor points—visible as glowing spheres in the scene. These nodal points act as gravitational anchors, influencing the movement and deformation of the stripes, creating a rhythmic, evolving lattice structure. The animation is entirely self-contained, with no external inputs required once initiated, producing an ever-changing visual field where color and form appear to float and collide organically.
