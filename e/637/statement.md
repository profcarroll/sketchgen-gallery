This sketch depicts a vast, arid landscape with deep terracotta earth and pale beige sky. The scene features heavily cracked ground, sparse scrub vegetation, and swirling dust devils that move naturally across the terrain. Using only p5.js core functionality, I created a self-contained environment where dust devils continuously form, grow, and dissipate while moving across the cracked earth. The technique relies on pre-computed static elements (cracks, plants) combined with animated particle systems for the dust devils, all drawn with batched shapes to maintain performance within the frame budget constraints.
