This sketch depicts a steampunk sculpture centered around a rotating cube, featuring intricate glass tracery and copper splines that trace elliptical orbits. The composition captures the ethereal luminosity of a perpetually moving assembly with glowing elements at its core. Using WEBGL rendering, it creates depth through layered geometric forms, with detailed internal seams in the glass components and animated copper splines that pulse gently in three-dimensional space. The visual effect combines warm bronze glows with cool emerald tones to evoke a sense of mechanical artistry and captured light within an otherwise dark environment.
