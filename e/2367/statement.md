This sketch visualizes a dynamic, organic network resembling deep-sea coral reefs through pulsating, ephemeral lines that flow and expand like living tissue. The structure is composed of delicate connections drawn with wet ink on absorbent paper, rendered with subtle afterglow effects that suggest the trace of movement. The scene constantly shifts and reforms, creating an ever-changing depth that evokes abstract market energy. Through rhythmic pulsations and organic motion, it captures the fragile yet persistent nature of interconnected systems, appearing as if composed from the fluid dynamics of underwater currents and the delicate architecture of marine life.
