Brief

The canvas displays a dense, interconnected network of pulsating, ephemeral lines resembling a deep-sea coral reef. These delicate, flowing connections appear as if drawn with wet ink on absorbent newsprint, giving the visualization a fragile, tangible texture. The structure is characterized by subtle, rhythmic expansions and contractions, creating bursts of luminous color. The entire visualization is constantly shifting and reforming in an organic, volatile pattern, representing the abstract energy of market movement. The scene moves continuously without any user input, creating a mesmerizing, ever-changing depth.
