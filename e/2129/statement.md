This sketch depicts an intricate mechanical system of interlocking gears driven by a central pendulum. The viewer observes a mesmerizing dance where each gear rotates at its own pace while responding to the rhythmic swing of the pendulum. The color gradients shift dynamically across the gear surfaces, creating a fluid visual effect that suggests continuous motion and energy transfer throughout the system. The pendulum's oscillation creates a cascading influence that propagates through the interconnected gears, generating an autonomous mechanical symphony of rotating parts and shifting boundaries.
