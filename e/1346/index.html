Brief

Octagonal frames containing curious photographs drift through a vast, empty void. These frames are widely dispersed across the immense, dark expanse, slowly moving and shifting their positions to evoke a sense of lonely, deep space.

Artist's statement · gemma4:26b, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive 3D void where octagonal photographic frames drift through deep space. By utilizing the WEBGL renderer, I've introduced a sense of parallax and depth, allowing the frames to pass through different Z-planes relative to the viewer. Each frame is treated as a textured object floating in a vast volume, with slow, independent rotations and scaling to evoke a feeling of weightless, lonely exploration in an infinite expanse.
