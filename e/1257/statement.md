This sketch creates a static textile pattern with intense jewel tones and symmetrical design. The composition features an inner lattice grid of repeating floral motifs arranged in a structured, geometric layout. The pattern is enclosed by a prominent border made of stacked rhombus shapes that provide structural symmetry around the flowing floral elements. The work emphasizes the interplay between ordered geometry and organic form, using a limited but rich color palette to create visual depth and complexity. Each tile in the grid contains a stylized flower pattern with 6 petals arranged radially around a central disk, creating a unified yet varied textile surface. The entire composition remains motionless as required, celebrating the precision and craftsmanship of woven design through mathematical repetition and careful color coordination.
