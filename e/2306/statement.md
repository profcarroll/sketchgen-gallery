This sketch creates an immersive underwater environment where linguistic structures emerge as glowing crystalline formations. The visual metaphor represents how language might exist in a deep, silent cognitive space—organic, ever-changing patterns that pulse with meaning. Using a limited palette of electric blues, emerald greens, and phosphorescent violets, the formations drift slowly across the screen, their geometric shapes dissolving and reforming into new arrangements. The continuous animation simulates an unconscious current of thought, where word-like structures bubble into existence, only to fade and reconfigure into entirely fresh patterns. The viewer witnesses this abstract representation of language as a living, evolving system beneath the surface of conscious awareness.
