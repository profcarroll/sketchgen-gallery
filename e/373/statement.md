This sketch creates a dynamic system of colored particles that orbit around a central point with physics-driven motion. The particles continuously accelerate toward the center, simulating gravitational or centripetal force, while maintaining fluid, unpredictable trajectories due to random perturbations. When clicked, the center of attraction shifts instantly to the new location, causing all particles to immediately re-orbit around it. By dragging vertically across the canvas, users can dynamically adjust the orbital radius, effectively changing the perceived strength of the centripetal force—making orbits tighter or looser. The visual effect is a mesmerizing dance of motion that responds directly to user input while maintaining an underlying natural physics simulation.
