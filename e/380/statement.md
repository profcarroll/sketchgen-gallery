This sketch depicts a cosmic flow of interconnected, vibrant shapes that move dynamically across the screen. Each shape has a glowing core and leaves behind a fading trail of spent energy. As they move, these forms connect with nearby shapes through delicate lines, creating an intricate webbing that pulses with ethereal light. The entire scene is a perpetual cycle of motion, connection, and gentle luminosity, evoking the feeling of deep space where energy flows and interweaves in constant, mesmerizing patterns.
