This sketch creates an immersive, data-flow visualization using WebGL rendering in p5.js. The scene features hundreds of glowing particles that move through three-dimensional space with organic, physics-based motion, constantly forming and dissolving connections between nearby elements. The color palette draws from deep blues and electric cyan hues, creating a high-tech aesthetic reminiscent of newsroom displays. Particles subtly wrap around the canvas boundaries and pulse rhythmically in response to their proximity to one another, forming an ever-shifting network of glowing lines and clusters. The entire composition evolves continuously without user input, with particles rotating slowly in 3D space and connections appearing and disappearing based on distance thresholds. This creates a mesmerizing effect where the viewer observes abstract data relationships assembling and decomposing over time.
