Brief

The canvas displays numerous bright particles moving in smooth, continuous orbits around a central point. These particles follow nearly perfect paths, creating visible, streaking trails. These trails are composed of fading color that gradually dissipates, allowing the viewer to trace the particle's journey over a noticeable period. The entire system runs autonomously, displaying only the intricate, hypnotic dance of the orbiting lights as they slowly fade into the background.
