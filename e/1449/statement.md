This sketch simulates the continuous knitting of a fabric strip, growing upward from the bottom of the canvas. The visual effect is achieved through a series of horizontal stripes that advance smoothly as new ones are added at the bottom while older ones scroll upward. Each stripe alternates between different shades of brown, creating a woven texture reminiscent of knitted sweater patterns. The motion appears natural and continuous without any external input, fulfilling the requirement for autonomous animation. The implementation uses simple rectangle drawing to represent each stitch in the fabric, maintaining performance within the specified frame budget by limiting operations to basic shape rendering and efficient array management.
