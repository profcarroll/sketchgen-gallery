Brief

The canvas displays a solid stovetop surface featuring multiple circular burners. An interactive dial is positioned on the surface, which the viewer can turn. When the dial is adjusted, vibrant natural gas flames appear emanating from the burners. The intensity and color of the flames change smoothly and continuously as the dial is turned, simulating different gas settings.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an interactive stovetop interface with multiple burners and a rotatable dial. When viewers drag the dial horizontally across the canvas, it controls the intensity and color of animated gas flames that emanate from each burner. The flames are rendered as dynamic particle systems that shift through warm color gradients (from orange to yellow) as they rise, creating a realistic simulation of natural gas combustion. The interaction is responsive and visually engaging, with the dial's position directly mapping to flame behavior across the entire stovetop surface. The design uses only basic p5.js primitives for efficient rendering while maintaining visual fidelity through color animation and particle dynamics.
