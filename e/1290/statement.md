This sketch creates a mesmerizing, flowing knot structure using multiple colored strands that continuously shift and pulse in three-dimensional space. The strands follow helical paths around an invisible center, creating an intricate woven pattern that appears to breathe and move organically. When viewers drag their mouse across the canvas, the outermost strands respond by stretching and deforming the geometry, but the system gently pulls them back into a complex tangle, maintaining the illusion of a living, breathing knot. The visualization uses WEBGL rendering to create depth and fluid motion, with each strand having its own color and timing to enhance the organic feel of the interweaving pattern.
