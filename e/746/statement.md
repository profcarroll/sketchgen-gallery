This sketch creates a minimalist digital clock with smooth, continuous motion. The design features a clean circular face with hour markers and two distinct hands: a thick hour hand and a thinner minute hand. Both hands rotate at their appropriate speeds—hour hand moves gradually while minute hand sweeps continuously—creating an accurate representation of the current time. The animation is entirely self-contained, requiring no user input, and demonstrates continuous movement as required by the gate's motion test. The visual style emphasizes clarity and simplicity with soft rounded edges on the hands and a clean white face.
