This sketch creates a stylized cornfield scene with an overhead hovering disc-shaped object casting intense cyan light onto the ground. The light manifests as a dynamic semicircular glow that pulses with rapid, erratic flashes of varying intensity. The technique uses p5.js's drawing capabilities to simulate the atmospheric lighting effect through layered shapes and transparency, creating a sense of movement and energy without requiring any external libraries or audio components. The viewer observes a field of corn stalks beneath an animated light source that shifts and flickers with unpredictable timing, producing a hypnotic visual rhythm that fills the entire canvas.
