This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic scene where translucent circles flow slowly across the canvas in a mesmerizing tide. Scattered throughout this fluid environment are pointillist stars that leave behind bright, glowing arcing light trails. When these luminous wakes interact with the flowing circles, temporary flares expand into radiating rings of light that dissipate over time. The scene is filled with faint structural glows from the lingering effects of these interactions, creating an immersive, continuously evolving spectacle. The entire animation runs without any input, driven purely by the natural flow of the elements, creating a hypnotic visual experience that feels both organic and otherworldly.
