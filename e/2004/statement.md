This sketch depicts a chaotic swarm of derelict mechanical dirigible frames falling through a smog-stained sky. Using the WEBGL renderer, it creates a three-dimensional scene where hundreds of metallic structures tumble and drift downward with unpredictable rotation and motion. The muted industrial palette of rusted browns and ochres against a darkening sky evokes the feeling of a heavy, oppressive atmosphere. Each frame is rendered as a simplified cube-like structure with rotating axes to simulate the tumbling effect, creating an overwhelming sense of continuous, dynamic movement that fills the entire canvas.
