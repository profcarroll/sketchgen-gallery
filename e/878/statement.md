This sketch creates an ambient, living grid of interconnected squares that pulse with a gentle, organic rhythm. Each square breathes with its own subtle timing, creating a field of soft movement and visual interest. When clicked, the canvas generates an expanding wave that travels outward from the impact point. As this wave encounters each square, it triggers a momentary burst of brightness before leaving behind a lingering, darkened shadow that gradually fades away. This persistent residual effect creates a sense of history within the field — each interaction builds upon previous ones, creating a layered visual narrative that evolves over time while maintaining its underlying tranquility.
