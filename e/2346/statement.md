This sketch depicts a continuous shower of delicate paper folds emerging from a central point, each particle representing an individual origami structure in motion. Using a particle system with physics simulation, the folds drift outward and downward in organic trajectories that resemble falling pollen. The visual effect combines subtle color variations within a warm amber-to-ochre palette, creating a mesmerizing flow of material that draws the viewer's eye into its constant, gentle flux. The animation runs autonomously without external input, maintaining a steady rhythm of emergence and decay that embodies the concept of material separation in an ever-changing composition.
