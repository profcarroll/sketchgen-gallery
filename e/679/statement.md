This sketch creates a dynamic, immersive environment of floating geometric forms that evolve through time. Using the WEBGL renderer, it generates hundreds of particles that drift in three-dimensional space, each with its own velocity and color. The particles form intricate connection networks as they move, creating a constantly shifting web of luminous lines that pulse with electric cyan and deep violet hues. The scene is wrapped in a dark void, emphasizing the vibrant energy of the structures within. As time progresses, the camera slowly orbits around the space, revealing the complex, fractal-like patterns formed by the interconnecting particles. The visual effect suggests a living, breathing crystalline network that continuously reforms itself in an endless dance of light and geometry.
