This sketch creates a geological visualization of crystallizing mineral formations. Through flowing geometric threads that weave across the canvas, it depicts the slow, patient process of underground crystal growth. The system begins with a network of luminous pathways that gradually extend and branch, forming an intricate underground web. At every intersection point, vibrant crystalline formations emerge—these crystals pulse and grow over time, suggesting mineral deposits slowly taking shape. When clicked, users trigger new branching veins that instantly radiate from their cursor position, causing nearby crystals to flash brightly before returning to their natural growth rhythm. The work captures the patient, almost meditative process of geological formation through a blend of organic flowing lines and sharp, structured crystalline forms.
