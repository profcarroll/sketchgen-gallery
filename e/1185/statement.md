This sketch creates a serene seascape at sunset, blending warm gradients of orange and pink with rhythmic ocean waves. The composition features a stylized black sailboat drifting steadily across the horizon against a glowing sky. Waves are generated using sine functions to create undulating ripples that move autonomously across the canvas, with each wave having its own speed and amplitude. The scene is rendered entirely through p5.js drawing primitives without any external assets or audio components, relying solely on procedural generation for all visual elements. The gentle motion of waves and boat creates a peaceful, meditative quality while maintaining the visual richness requested in the brief.
