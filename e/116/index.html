Brief

The canvas reveals an abstract, intricate lattice structure representing complex circuitry. This network is traversed by luminous streams of data, depicted as glowing, ephemeral currents of light. These data streams constantly flow, and crucially, they leave behind visible, dissipating glowing trails that map the pathways of the energy. The overall visualization is a continuously morphing, dynamic display of electric computation, providing a rich, energetic sense of depth and movement without requiring any user input.

The composition is a dynamic, self-animating visualization of electric computation—no user input required.
