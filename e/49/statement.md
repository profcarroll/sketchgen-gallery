This sketch presents an architectural visualization of a continuous steel pathway with a sudden, disruptive interruption. Using WebGL rendering, it creates a helical structure in deep rust tones that appears to flow endlessly through space. The work captures the tension between predictable geometry and abrupt disruption — a curved path is suddenly broken by a sharp, angular shift that jolts the viewer's perception of continuous form. The composition emphasizes the visual and spatial experience of architecture, where the change in curvature becomes the defining moment rather than the flow itself. The stillness of the piece reinforces the contemplative quality of architectural observation, inviting viewers to notice how the interruption creates a new relationship between the geometric elements.
