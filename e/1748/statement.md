This sketch presents a poetic meditation on time's passage through the lens of maritime decay and mineralization. Whimsical paper-cut ships drift across a translucent canvas, their weathered forms bearing signs of age—patina, moss, tears—that gradually dissipate as they move. As these vessels navigate, they leave behind trails of sediment that slowly solidify into fragile crystalline structures, forming brittle scaffolds that map the ship's journey. The scene captures both the ephemeral and permanent aspects of decay, where fleeting moments of beauty transform into enduring geological records. The visual rhythm of the animation mirrors the cyclical nature of erosion and accumulation, presenting a quiet narrative about time's dual capacity to destroy and preserve.
