This sketch presents an architectural composition that grows vertically from a base platform, constructed entirely from uniform rectangular prisms in varying shades of gray. The structure appears to rise endlessly through stacked segments, each matching the dimensions of its predecessor, creating a sense of industrial continuity and vertical stability. When clicked, the canvas responds by adding another segment atop the existing formation, maintaining perfect alignment and scale. The rendering uses WebGL for 3D depth and perspective, with shading that subtly darkens toward the base to emphasize the structure's verticality. The scene remains static otherwise, allowing viewers to contemplate the growing form without animation or motion.
