This sketch creates a mesmerizing visualization of mineral crystallization patterns emerging from a central point. The rings expand outward with organic, wave-like motion that mimics the growth of rust formations on steel surfaces. Each ring pulses with dynamic color shifts between vibrant hues and subtle grayscale transitions, creating an effect reminiscent of crystalline structures forming in mineral deposits. The animation combines steady expansion with irregular rippling effects that give the impression of complex geological processes unfolding across the canvas. The continuous cycle of ring creation, expansion, and fading produces an ever-changing landscape of color and form that captures both the ordered progression of crystal growth and the chaotic beauty of natural rust patterns.
