This sketch simulates the growth of mold filaments on a rigid substrate using a dynamic network of delicate, interconnected strands. The scene employs three-dimensional rendering techniques to emphasize texture and depth, with filaments continuously evolving and expanding across the surface. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of particle-based line drawing and procedural growth algorithms that create an organic, constantly shifting pattern. The camera slowly orbits around the scene to reveal the complex structure from multiple angles, while the substrate provides a contrasting base that highlights the amorphous nature of the filaments' growth.
