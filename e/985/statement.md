This sketch simulates the mesmerizing flow of liquid into a container, capturing the essence of fluid dynamics through visual effects. The scene depicts a continuous stream of colored liquid pouring into a basin, where impacts generate swirling vortices that spawn countless bubbles. These bubbles rise through the liquid with varying speeds and sizes, growing denser as they ascend before bursting at the surface. The technique uses particle systems for both bubbles and vortices, with each element having its own physics properties and lifecycle. The visual palette shifts between warm hues for the liquid stream and cooler tones for the bubbles, creating a dynamic interplay of color and motion that captures the fluid's organic behavior without explicit simulation of complex physics.
