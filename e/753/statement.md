This sketch creates an immersive, futuristic cityscape characterized by geometrically complex structural planes interwoven with pulsating emerald green energy seams. The composition features a grid-based architecture where orthogonal planes are laced with rhythmic energy flows that pulse and vibrate, particularly at critical connection nodes. The scene is populated with crystalline energy nodes that shimmer and pulse in synchronization with the structural vibrations, creating an effect of overwhelming luminous tension. The viewer experiences a sense of dynamic instability as the geometric forms appear to warp and shift in response to the rhythmic energy flows, evoking the feeling of a city structure under intense structural stress. The technique uses WEBGL rendering to create depth and volume while maintaining performance through batched drawing operations and fixed particle counts.
