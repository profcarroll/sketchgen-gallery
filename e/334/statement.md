This sketch creates an ethereal underwater-like environment of drifting bioluminescent forms that pulse and leave glowing trails behind them. The scene features multiple glowing shapes that continuously drift across the canvas with smooth, organic motion patterns. Each form pulses with a rhythmic glow and leaves behind a trail that dynamically changes in thickness and opacity as it moves. The entire composition subtly reacts to changes in background brightness, causing the central forms to brighten or dim in response to the ambient darkness. The visual effect is achieved through layered drawing techniques using p5.js's color blending and shadow effects, creating a dreamlike atmosphere of floating light particles that continuously evolve without any user interaction required.
