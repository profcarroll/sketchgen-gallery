This sketch depicts a dynamic, cross-sectional view of a combustion engine's core mechanisms in motion. It features four pistons moving rhythmically within their cylinders, connected to visible connecting rods that transfer linear motion to a rotating crankshaft. The system simulates an internal spark explosion that pushes pistons downward, demonstrating how this mechanical power translates through the connecting rods to turn the transaxle and connected gearing system. The engine runs in a constant, repeating cycle showing the interplay between mechanical parts in real-time. The visualization uses WEBGL rendering to create a 3D perspective of the mechanical components while maintaining performance within strict frame budget constraints.
