Brief

The canvas displays complex, three-dimensional geometric structures composed of multiple intersecting glowing planes and connecting lines. These structures are constantly in motion, featuring a visible, directional wave pattern that travels across the surfaces of the planes, causing the entire glow to oscillate and pulse rhythmically. The visualization is a shifting, intricate abstract spectacle of light and geometry, continuously flowing and changing without requiring any user interaction.
