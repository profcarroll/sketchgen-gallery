This sketch creates an ever-shifting, luminous architecture of intersecting geometric planes that pulse and flow in synchronized yet independent motion. Through a combination of rotating surfaces, wave-displaced geometry, and connecting lines, it visualizes abstract mathematical relationships in three-dimensional space. The glowing planes appear to breathe and undulate with a rhythmic pattern that travels across their surfaces, creating an immersive spectacle of light and form. Each plane moves independently while maintaining harmonic relationships with others, forming a complex yet coherent whole that evolves continuously without external input.
