Brief

The canvas displays a stylized cross-section of a tidal zone, showing distinct horizontal bands of varying colors. These bands are in constant motion, cycling through rapid cycles of compression and expansion. The animation is dynamic and unpredictable, as the oscillation speeds are varied. This movement generates complex, visible swirling patterns of turbulence within the water column. The boundaries between the different tidal levels are emphasized by sharp, saturated color gradients, giving the visualization a vibrant and energetic appearance.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a dynamic visualization of a tidal zone using horizontal bands of color that oscillate with complex, unpredictable motion. Each band has its own speed, amplitude, and frequency of wave movement, creating swirling turbulence patterns within the water column. The animation relies on sine waves and Perlin noise to generate natural-looking, chaotic motion that evolves continuously without user input. The vibrant color gradients between bands emphasize the boundaries of different tidal levels, while the constant compression and expansion of each band produces an energetic, fluid appearance that captures the dynamic nature of coastal waters.
