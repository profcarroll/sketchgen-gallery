Brief

A vast, orbiting solar system is displayed in three dimensions. The massive central star illuminates surrounding planets, each rendered with detailed surface textures and swirling atmospheric gas. The celestial bodies revolve around the star, following visible orbits. When the user presses the mouse down on the canvas, the entire system enters a dynamic, dramatic animation cycle, causing the planets to shift and glow dramatically.

This sketch renders a three-dimensional solar system with a central star and orbiting planets. When the user clicks the canvas, the entire system enters a dramatic animation cycle, where planets shift dramatically in position and glow intensely, creating a dynamic visual spectacle against the star's radiant light.
