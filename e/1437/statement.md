This sketch renders a steampunk kinetic sculpture in three-dimensional space, featuring a central molten bronze core that emits intense light and glowing particles orbiting around it. The composition uses oxidized copper hues for the mechanical elements and blue-green sea glass fragments to contrast the aged metal with the vibrant glow. The entire structure rotates continuously, showing the intricate interplay between mechanical precision and luminous energy. The visualization employs WebGL rendering to create a dynamic, self-contained mechanical system that moves without external input, embodying the contrast between ancient materials and intense, otherworldly light.
