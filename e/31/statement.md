This sketch presents a carefully composed still life arrangement rendered in three-dimensional space using p5.js's WEBGL mode. The scene features everyday objects—a wooden cube, glass jar, table top, lamp, small cube, and ball—positioned to create depth and visual interest through geometric forms and lighting. The dramatic contrast between light and shadow enhances the sense of physical presence and texture, while the grounded color palette emphasizes realism. All elements are static and deliberately composed, creating a fixed, representational view that invites contemplation of form, space, and materiality without any motion or interaction.
