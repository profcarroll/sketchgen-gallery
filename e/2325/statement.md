This sketch renders a still, intricate cross-section of fossilized geological formations resembling solidified beeswax. The composition features interwoven organic pathways—represented as flowing threads—that grow and branch within a deep amber-toned landscape. These structures are punctuated by crystalline veins and sediment deposits that create a sense of ancient subterranean journeys. The piece captures the illusion of a stabilized, layered tapestry viewed at an intimate scale, where detail emerges from the complexity of form and color without any animated motion to disrupt its contemplative stillness.
