This sketch creates a mesmerizing visual field of concentric rings that pulse and expand rhythmically. Each ring features a vibrant fluorescent color palette that shifts smoothly over time, creating an hypnotic effect. The animation consists of two interwoven motions: each ring ripples outward in a fluid, liquid-like motion, while simultaneously expanding to a larger final size. The combination of these effects produces a dynamic, ever-changing pattern that fills the entire canvas without requiring any user interaction. The rings maintain their structured arrangement throughout the animation, yet appear to flow and breathe with organic motion.
