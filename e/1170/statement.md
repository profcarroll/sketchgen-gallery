This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of expanding geometric rings that originate from the center of the canvas and propagate outward with accelerating speed. Each ring exhibits a wave-like undulation as it moves, creating an organic flow across the screen. The rings gradually increase in velocity as they expand, building up a sense of dynamic energy and motion. The visual effect is achieved through careful control of each ring's expansion rate, angular rotation, and fading opacity, resulting in a continuous, evolving pattern that fills the entire canvas with rhythmic, pulsing geometry.
