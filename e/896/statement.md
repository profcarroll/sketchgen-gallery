This sketch depicts a dramatic mechanical shredding scene where books are being torn apart by rapidly rotating gears. The composition features a vertical stack of books falling toward a central shredding mechanism, with visible gear teeth spinning in the foreground. As the gears turn, they generate sparks that fly out in all directions and splinters that break off from the paper pulp. Suspended in the air are countless smoke particles rising slowly, adding to the chaotic energy of the scene. The entire effect is achieved through particle systems for sparks, splinters, and smoke, all animated independently to create a dynamic, immersive mechanical environment.
