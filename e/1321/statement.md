This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of nested circular rings composed of segmented rectangular pieces. Each ring is made up of multiple segments that oscillate smoothly in size, creating a rhythmic expansion and contraction effect. The visual rhythm is enhanced by a consistent phase shift applied to each successive ring — meaning that as you move outward from the center, each ring lags behind the previous one in its oscillation cycle. The color palette transitions through shades of blue, purple, and magenta, giving the whole structure a vibrant, pulsing appearance. The motion is entirely self-generated, driven by time-based oscillations, and requires no external input once started.
