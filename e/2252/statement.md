This sketch presents a vast industrial assembly of radio receivers styled after 1920s Bauhaus principles. The deep metallic bodies of the receivers form a framework for constantly shifting geometric patterns of pure light. Through tessellated lines and cross-hatching, each receiver displays an intricate dance of shapes that pulse and rotate in sync with the overall scene. The viewer observes what appears to be colossal, stationary machines humming with ephemeral energy—a visual representation of the dynamic electromagnetic forces within radio technology, rendered in a stark, geometric aesthetic of the early modernist movement.
