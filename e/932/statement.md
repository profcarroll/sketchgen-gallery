This sketch creates a dynamic, abstract composition using bold geometric bands that intersect and shift continuously. The bands are drawn as large angular shapes with sharp, rigid edges that separate distinct color regions. Fine linear stripes weave through the composition, moving independently to create additional layers of visual complexity. The entire structure evolves without external input, driven by continuous rotation and motion of its components. The result is a constantly morphing tapestry of color and form where depth and overlap are created through careful layering and movement. The harsh boundaries between colored regions emphasize the angular, architectural quality of the design while the flowing stripes add fine detail and visual tension throughout the composition.
