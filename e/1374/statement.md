This sketch creates a mesmerizing, fluid field of textual elements that swirl and dance across the screen like a continuous stream of consciousness. The characters—letters, numbers, and Greek symbols—move in subtle, organic patterns, constantly overlapping and dissolving into one another. In their natural state, they drift slowly with semi-transparent hues, creating an aqueous, hypnotic atmosphere. When the viewer interacts by clicking or dragging, these textual fragments momentarily coalesce into more solid, suggestive forms before quickly returning to their fluid motion. Sound input causes a similar temporary solidification effect, making the abstract visual field respond to audio stimuli in real-time. The work explores themes of mental flow, linguistic fragmentation, and the dynamic relationship between perception and interaction.
