This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic environment using a continuous flow of translucent, overlapping circles that drift slowly across the canvas. The circles move in gentle, organic patterns, creating a sense of fluid motion and depth. Interspersed throughout this flowing tapestry are thousands of pointillist stars that pulse gently with varying intensities, adding a sense of luminous life to the scene. The entire composition shifts and glows without any user interaction, presenting a mesmerizing, self-contained cosmic dance that fills the entire canvas with subtle, shimmering movement.
