This sketch depicts a slow, organic geyser of viscous sediment spreading across the canvas. Using a flow field algorithm, particles move in subtle, continuous patterns that mimic the behavior of poured concrete cooling. The warm earth tones—ranging from ochre to burnt sienna—create a cohesive palette that emphasizes the material quality of the movement. Particles age and regenerate over time, creating evolving patterns of soft-focus connections that slowly expand and shift across the surface. The effect is entirely self-contained, requiring no interaction from the viewer to persist, with the entire canvas gradually filled with an amorphous, bubbling landscape of sedimentary flow.
