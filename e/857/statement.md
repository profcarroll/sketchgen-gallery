This sketch creates an immersive, autonomous visual experience through overlapping planes of saturated color that undulate like breathing surfaces. Using a series of horizontal waveforms with varying frequencies, amplitudes, and speeds, the composition generates a sense of depth and continuous motion without any external interaction. Each plane moves independently while subtly shifting through the color spectrum, creating an organic flow that draws the viewer's eye across the canvas. The technique relies on mathematical sine waves to create smooth, natural-looking undulations, combined with carefully modulated hues to produce rich chromatic harmony that evolves slowly over time.
