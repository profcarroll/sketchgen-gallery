This sketch creates a dynamic, unstable digital architecture using glowing neon lines that form a grid. The grid appears fragile with lines randomly breaking into staggered bursts before snapping back into place, creating an impression of digital instability. Simultaneously, the connections between cells pulse rhythmically with internal energy, while individual lines flicker with a subtle noise-based animation. The entire composition evolves without external input, driven by time-based mathematical functions that create a constantly shifting yet structured field of light. Viewers witness an architecture that exists in a state of perpetual flux—neither fully stable nor completely chaotic.
