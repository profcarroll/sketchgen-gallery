Brief

Seven interlocking geometric shapes in varying shades of beige float across a dark background, forming an incomplete picture. When the viewer drags with the mouse, pieces slide into new positions to reveal hidden colors beneath, snapping back or rotating slightly upon release. The arrangement remains static until interaction occurs, maintaining a clean, minimalist aesthetic where each click or drag reconfigures the spatial relationship of the components without external animation.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a minimalist composition of seven geometric shapes in varying shades of beige floating on a dark background. The arrangement creates an incomplete, abstract image that responds to user interaction. When a piece is dragged, it slides into new positions, revealing hidden colors beneath through subtle color transitions. Each shape maintains its own rotation and movement characteristics, creating a dynamic yet controlled visual experience. The subtle animation during idle periods adds a sense of life without distraction, while the snapping behavior upon release provides satisfying feedback to user actions.
