This sketch creates a dynamic, evolving lattice of geometric lines and nodes that pulse and flow across the canvas. Using WebGL rendering, it generates a structured network where nodes are positioned in a grid and animated through time-based movement along the Z-axis. Lines connect nodes when they come within a certain distance, forming an intricate web that constantly reshapes itself. The entire visualization runs autonomously without any user interaction, with each frame building upon the previous one to create an ethereal field of motion. The bright, saturated colors against a dark background make the connections and nodal points stand out clearly, emphasizing the structured yet ever-changing nature of the pattern.
