This sketch creates a continuous ripple effect by generating concentric rings that emanate from the center of the canvas. Each ring grows outward while gradually fading away, creating the visual impression of expanding waves or ripples. The animation is entirely self-contained and requires no user input—rings are continuously added at the center point, and existing rings expand and fade over time. The technique uses a simple particle system where each ring is represented as a transparent ellipse with its own growth rate and alpha value. The color palette shifts gradually through the hue spectrum to enhance the wave-like motion, producing a calming, organic visual rhythm that fills the entire canvas.
