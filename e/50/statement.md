This sketch creates a dynamic, autonomous visual system of interconnected geometric forms that continuously transform and evolve. Each shape is defined by its position, size, rotation, and color, all of which change over time through mathematical oscillations and noise functions. The jagged, organic edges of each form emerge from the interplay between regular polygonal geometry and perlin noise, creating an effect of fluid motion while maintaining clear geometric structure. The shapes flow across the canvas in synchronized yet independent patterns, occasionally reconfiguring their positions to create a sense of continuous reorganization. The color palette shifts gradually over time, emphasizing the rhythmic nature of the transformation without overwhelming the underlying geometric relationships. This autonomous system demonstrates how simple mathematical rules can generate complex, visually engaging behavior that evolves continuously without any external input.
