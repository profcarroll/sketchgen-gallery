This sketch creates an evolving, geological landscape of interwoven crystalline structures that pulse with deep, cyclical colors. The pattern emerges from a field of slowly growing geometric forms, each with its own motion and color palette, rendered in 3D space using WebGL for rich depth and lighting effects. As time passes, the crystals subtly shift and grow, forming an intricate tessellation across the canvas. A viewer's click introduces localized energy that accelerates growth in nearby structures, creating a ripple effect that temporarily disrupts the natural rhythm before the deep, timeless process resumes its slow, mesmerizing dance. The visual experience evokes the feeling of geological time unfolding through crystalline formations.
