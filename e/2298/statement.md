This sketch depicts an underwater scene of petrified mangrove roots under deep-sea pressure, with self-organized glowing vector schematics that pulse and flow. The organic root structures are rendered as subtly swaying, irregular shapes in deep blue tones, while the vector schematics appear as dynamic lines that pulse with bioluminescent energy. A rhythmic pendulum motion influences all elements, creating continuous, fluid movement across the canvas. The color palette emphasizes deep sapphires and electric cyan to highlight the contrast between the static organic forms and the lively geometric patterns. The schematics organize themselves through a combination of noise-based movement and periodic pendulum forces, creating an autonomous system that evolves continuously without external input.
