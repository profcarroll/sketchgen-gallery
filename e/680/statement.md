This sketch renders a continuously evolving, three-dimensional cityscape composed of stacked architectural segments that grow upward from the viewer's perspective. Each segment varies in width, depth, and height, creating a sense of dynamic construction and vertical progression. The color palette shifts gradually through muted industrial tones as the structure ascends, using HSB color mode to maintain visual coherence. The scene automatically evolves over time with new segments added periodically, simulating continuous urban development. A click anywhere on the canvas triggers an immediate addition of a new structural element, increasing complexity and height. The rendering uses WebGL for 3D capabilities, ensuring smooth performance within the frame budget constraints while maintaining visual fidelity across the entire composition.
