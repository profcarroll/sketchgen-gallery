This sketch creates a mesmerizing, self-animating geometric composition that evokes the bold aesthetic of 1970s design. Using a combination of diagonal stripes, halftone dot textures, and continuous motion patterns, it generates an endlessly shifting visual field that appears to slice, fold, and recompose itself across the canvas. The animation is driven purely by time-based calculations, ensuring constant visual evolution without any user interaction required. Each stripe incorporates subtle color variations and positional shifts that create a hypnotic rhythm, while the halftone dots add texture and depth reminiscent of vintage print design techniques. The entire composition maintains a consistent pace and visual language, creating an immersive experience where the eye is drawn through the constantly transforming geometric relationships.
