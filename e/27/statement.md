This sketch creates an immersive, ever-changing cityscape that exists on a reflective wet surface, continuously shifting through day and night cycles. The scene features rigid architectural structures rising into the sky, softened by organic plant life at ground level. As the simulated sun moves across the sky, its light reflects off the wet surfaces, creating vibrant, constantly evolving color pools and distorted reflections that dance across buildings and foreground. The entire composition operates without user input, driven purely by time-based animation to deliver a mesmerizing, self-contained visual experience.
