This sketch depicts a whimsical underwater scene where aged paper-cut ships sail across a textured water surface. Each ship is rendered with visible wear—tears, patina, and dissolved paper sections—to suggest prolonged exposure to water. As they move, each vessel leaves behind dynamic wake trails composed of mineral leaching discoloration that accumulate and fade over time. The entire composition moves outward from the center, creating a continuous motion as ships continuously sail away from the viewer's perspective.
