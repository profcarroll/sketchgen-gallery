This sketch creates a dynamic architectural scene where light rays emanate from a powerful source and cast shifting shadows across building structures. The viewer observes a rotating 3D environment containing geometric buildings and hundreds of light rays that calculate real-time shadow projections. The continuous rotation and mathematical ray casting produce an ever-changing pattern of deep, dramatic shadows that creep and shift across the architectural façade. The piece demonstrates how light interacts with solid geometry through a simplified but effective shadowing algorithm that calculates intersections between rays and building volumes.
