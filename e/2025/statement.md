This sketch depicts a rhythmic sedimentation process within a translucent container, where fine crystalline particles cascade downward in response to spring-like oscillations. The particles form distinct geometric layers as they settle, with their movement governed by simulated physics that mimics the recoil of a spring under tension. The visual effect captures both the granular nature of the settling process and the ordered buildup of sedimentary strata, creating a sense of textured, cyclical growth within the container.
