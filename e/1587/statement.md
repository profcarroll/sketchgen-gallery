This sketch renders a dynamic three-dimensional model of the DNA double helix using p5.js's WEBGL renderer. It visualizes the molecular structure with large, colored spheres representing nitrogenous bases (A, T, C, G) arranged in a helical backbone. The model rotates gracefully through space while showing temporary hydrogen bonds forming and breaking between complementary base pairs (A-T and C-G). The visualization demonstrates the dynamic nature of DNA pairing through a continuous animation that updates the positions of bases and their interactions over time, creating an organic, flowing representation of molecular behavior without any user input required.
