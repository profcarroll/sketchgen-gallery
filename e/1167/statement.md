This sketch renders a serene, mineral-dusted alpine winter landscape using p5.js's WEBGL mode. The composition features jagged mountain peaks draped in pristine snow, with delicate mineral dust particles creating subtle color gradients across the snowy surfaces. Through careful control of particle density and color temperature, the work evokes the ethereal stillness of a frozen environment where fine mineral deposits have settled into untouched snow, transforming the monochromatic landscape into a tapestry of muted, crystalline hues. The static nature of the scene captures the profound quietude of such environments, emphasizing the interplay between cold purity and warm mineral undertones.
