This sketch depicts a natural transformation from earthbound fire to celestial patterns. At the bottom of the canvas, orange sparks rise from a campfire, gradually cooling and fading as they drift upward. As they reach the upper portion of the screen, these particles reorganize into bright white and blue points that dynamically connect to form constellation-like patterns. The visualization captures the poetic idea of terrestrial combustion evolving into cosmic order, with each spark's journey from warmth to coolness mirrored in its transformation from orange-red to brilliant white. The continuous loop creates a meditative flow between earthly and celestial realms.
