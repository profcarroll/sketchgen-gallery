This sketch creates a mesmerizing cosmic scene filled with luminous orbs that drift through space, guided by subtle group dynamics. Each orb pulses gently and leaves behind delicate, fading trails that stretch across the dark canvas like cosmic smoke. The composition captures the fluid, organic movement of celestial bodies, with orbs forming temporary clusters before dispersing again. The interplay between individual motion and collective behavior creates an ever-shifting tapestry of light and shadow, evoking the vast, mysterious nature of the universe where particles dance in invisible currents.
