This sketch depicts a cyclical garden landscape where flowers continuously transform through their life stages. Each plant in the grid moves through four distinct phases: seed, bud, bloom, and fade. The visual system uses color, shape complexity, and timing to show this progression — from tiny seed forms that gradually develop into full blossoms with vibrant petals, to fading blooms that dissolve back into delicate seed states. The constant motion and color shifts create an immersive experience of natural time passing, where each plant's development is synchronized but individualized through randomization of position, size, and initial state. The scene evolves without external input, demonstrating the continuous, organic cycles of growth and dormancy found in nature.
