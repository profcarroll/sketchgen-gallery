This sketch renders a perpetual, looping broadcast environment dominated by glowing, neon lines and structured, segmented geometries that evoke the slow accretion of calcium carbonate formations. The composition features hundreds of crystalline structures that grow and pulse slowly in an endless cycle, each one representing a cross-section of petrified forest. These structures are rendered in a limited palette of deep blacks, electric blues, magenta, and cyan, creating a rhythmic motion that simulates data streams and complex network infrastructure. When clicked, the canvas reacts with a sharp flash of colored static—a geometric data spike—that momentarily disrupts the pattern before returning to its slow, pulsating cycle. The visual effect mimics the geological process of fossilization while maintaining the aesthetic of digital architecture and data visualization.
