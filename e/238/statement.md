This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of glowing, bioluminescent particles that drift and bloom across the screen. Each particle leaves behind an ethereal trail that stretches and fades as it moves, creating a sense of continuous motion and luminous flow. The particles pulse with shifting hues, cycling through the color spectrum to simulate otherworldly light. The composition is entirely self-contained, generating its own patterns without user interaction, filling the canvas with a fluid tapestry of light that evolves organically over time.
