This sketch creates a grid of small squares on a deep, near-black background infused with a warm hue. Each square pulses gently and independently, creating a subtle rhythmic breathing motion across the entire grid. When clicked anywhere on the canvas, a wave radiates outward from the point of impact. As this wave passes over the squares, those affected briefly flash a bright intensity before quickly returning to their natural, steady pulse. The effect combines organic movement with responsive interaction, creating an immersive visual experience that evolves through user engagement.
