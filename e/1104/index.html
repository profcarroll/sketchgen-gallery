Brief

Interlocking polygonal shapes drift gently across the screen before snapping into perfect alignment when clicked or dragged, revealing a colorful image behind them until all pieces lock place. The fragments possess a slight independent wander that pauses instantly upon user interaction, inviting reassembly through mouse clicks to rotate pieces and drags to slide them around the fixed 800 by 600 pixel workspace.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a collection of polygonal shapes that drift gently across the screen in a subtle, randomized motion. When pieces come into proximity with one another, they snap into perfect alignment, revealing a hidden colorful pattern beneath them. Users can interact by clicking and dragging individual pieces to reposition or rotate them, temporarily pausing their autonomous movement. The visual effect combines organic wandering with precise geometric snapping, creating an engaging interplay between chaos and order as the shapes form new configurations through user manipulation.
