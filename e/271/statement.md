This sketch explores the concept of architectural entropy through a collection of geometric planes that slowly decay and reform in an impossible space. Using WebGL rendering, it creates a scene where solid forms gradually dissolve into wisps of particulate mist, with each plane undergoing subtle rotations and transformations over time. The muted teal color palette evokes a sense of calm dissolution while the constant motion suggests ongoing instability. The viewer observes an elegant degradation of structure, where the buildings never collapse completely but instead fragment and reform without external intervention, creating a meditation on the passage of time and the fragility of form.
