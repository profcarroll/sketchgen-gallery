This sketch simulates the passage of time on a vintage paper surface by rendering a continuous stream of text characters that flow across the canvas. As the characters accumulate, they subtly distort the paper's appearance through warping and creasing effects, while small fibers fray away from the printed words, suggesting physical exhaustion. The visual decay is rendered in real-time, creating an endless, evolving narrative on an aging surface where the medium itself becomes part of the story.
