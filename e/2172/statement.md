This sketch depicts a chaotic fungal network pulsing with bioluminescent light across a forest floor. The network consists of thousands of glowing hyphae that pulse at different rates and phases, creating an organic, living pattern. The visualization uses spatial hashing for performance, efficiently connecting nearby elements to form a dynamic web. The intense green glow pulses rhythmically, simulating the slow, rhythmic heartbeat of a fungal mycelium. The network fills the entire canvas in a way that evokes the vast interconnectedness of forest ecosystems at night.
