This sketch creates an immersive tapestry of interwoven organic fibers that pulse and shift in hypnotic rhythm. Through careful manipulation of geometric forms—polygons with varying segment counts—the composition suggests the meticulous craftsmanship of a loom, where earth tones and vibrant highlights dance together in an endless pattern. The gentle pulsation of each fiber gives the impression of self-sustaining movement, as if the fibers are breathing and flowing with their own inner life. The texture is achieved through layered polygons that interlock and overlap, creating depth while maintaining the clean, structured appearance of woven material. The subtle background animation adds to the sense of constant, gentle motion without overwhelming the central pattern.
