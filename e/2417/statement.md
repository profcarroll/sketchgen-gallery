This sketch creates a vast, rhythmic tapestry of shifting animal herds that seem to migrate across the canvas in continuous motion. Using a grid-based approach with organic noise functions, it renders flowing, interconnected forms that blend seamlessly into one another. The saturated earth tones and subtle texture evoke the rich colors and woven patterns of medieval tapestries while maintaining an ethereal, constantly evolving pattern. Each herd moves along its own cyclical path, creating an illusion of vast, coordinated movement across the entire field without any individual particle or loop that would exceed the frame budget.
