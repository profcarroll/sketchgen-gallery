This sketch renders a serene, sunlit pastoral landscape using layered gradient skies, rolling hills, and detailed grass textures. The composition builds depth through atmospheric perspective: distant mountains fade into the horizon, while foreground elements like grass patches and exaggerated shadows create strong contrast and three-dimensional form. The scene captures a static moment in a countryside that feels both vibrant and tranquil, emphasizing the interplay of light and shadow across undulating terrain. The technique relies on procedural generation for natural variation in hill shapes and grass distribution, all rendered with careful attention to visual hierarchy and color harmony.
