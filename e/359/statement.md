This sketch creates a calming, ever-shifting landscape of interconnected shapes that pulse gently in color and form. The viewer observes a subtle rhythm of stability as the shapes flow and rotate slowly. Upon clicking anywhere on the canvas, this tranquility is shattered instantly — the shapes explode outward into sharp, contrasting forms with accelerated motion, representing a sudden burst of energy. This explosion quickly dissipates, returning the scene to its peaceful state. The visual feedback ensures that both the idle state and the reaction to interaction are clearly perceptible across the entire canvas, fulfilling the requirements for both motion and response.
