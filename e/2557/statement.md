This sketch presents a microscopic cross-section of an ancient, extinct language through layered visual strata rendered in muted blue and rich ochre tones. The composition unfolds slowly over time, revealing deeper layers of glyphs and markings that shift subtly with animation. Viewers can drag across the canvas to explore the structure and reveal previously hidden information, emphasizing the complex, accumulative nature of forgotten knowledge. The technique uses progressive layering with geometrically folded panels that respond to both time-based animation and user interaction, creating a sense of depth and systematic accretion within the confined space of the screen.
