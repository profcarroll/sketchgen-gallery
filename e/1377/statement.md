This sketch renders a still life composition in three-dimensional space using p5.js's WEBGL renderer. The scene is set within a dark room featuring a brown table as the central element, upon which are arranged a clear water jug, silver candlesticks with a lit candle, and a colorful fruit platter. Through careful lighting design and material properties, the work emphasizes the sculptural quality of each object through strong contrasts of light and shadow. The composition is deliberately static—no animation occurs—creating an immersive moment frozen in time that invites contemplation of form, texture, and color within the confined architectural space.
