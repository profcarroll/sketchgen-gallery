Brief

The canvas displays a view through a heavily rain-streaked windowpane. In the collected puddles, visible crystalline cityscapes are rendered upside down. These structures are characterized by internal, flashing lights that do not simply appear, but instead pulse outward, generating visible, radial ripple patterns across the fragile, crystalline surfaces. The movement is continuous, driven by the streaming rain and the pulsing light show within the puddles.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch depicts a rain-streaked windowpane through which viewers can see puddles collecting on the surface. Within these puddles, crystalline cityscapes appear upside down and begin to pulse with internal light flashes that radiate outward in visible ripple patterns. The effect is created by rendering cityscape graphics within each puddle and adding dynamic ripples when a raindrop impacts the puddle surface, creating a continuous animation of light pulses and reflections that simulate the interaction between water droplets and light inside the puddles.
