This sketch creates a serene, floating environment where translucent bubbles and text fragments slowly rise from the bottom of the screen. The elements emerge naturally from the base, drift upward at gentle speeds, and gradually fade as they reach the top. The motion suggests a dreamlike state of floating thoughts and ideas, with each bubble and text piece having its own unique size, speed, and opacity. The visual effect is one of continuous, soft animation without any user interaction, creating an immersive field of floating elements that moves organically across the canvas.
