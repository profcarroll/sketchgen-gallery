This sketch creates a 3D visualization of boiling water in a container using p5.js's WEBGL renderer. It features rising spherical bubbles that grow as they ascend from the bottom, steam clouds that drift upward with wind effects, and a turbulent water surface with dynamic ripples. The scene responds to user interaction through mouse clicks that intensify the boiling effect. The rendering uses 3D primitives for realistic depth and motion, with particles created using spheres that dynamically change size and position over time. The visual effect simulates the physics of boiling water including buoyancy, vapor formation, and surface tension effects.
