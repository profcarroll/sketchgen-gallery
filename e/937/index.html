Brief

The canvas displays an intricate, deeply detailed visualization of natural biological and geological processes occurring within a semi-submerged substrate. Delicate, filamentous mold structures span the surface, forming an organic lattice of thin, colorful filaments. Within the deepest crevices and around the thicker primary connections, sediment accumulates, giving the surface a rich, stratified quality. Over time, visible crystalline formations of mineral deposits slowly emerge, encasing and crystallizing around the underlying mold filaments. The entire structure is viewed in three dimensions, allowing the interplay of light and shadow to reveal the complexity of the growth.
