This sketch creates an abstract urban environment that responds dynamically to simulated audio input, evoking the energy of a skatepark. It features dozens of geometric shapes—rectangles, ellipses, and triangles—that move fluidly across the screen, changing size, color, and direction over time. The visual rhythm is driven by a simulated audio signal that causes the shapes to expand, contract, and shift color palettes in response to beat-like changes. Connections between nearby shapes form a network of glowing lines that pulse with the movement, creating a sense of kinetic energy and urban flow. The entire scene is in constant flux, with no external input required to maintain the animation, making it a self-contained visualization of rhythmic motion through geometric forms.
