This sketch visualizes a microscopic geode formation rendered as folded origami structures in a crystalline lattice. The composition features a series of interconnected, rotating triangular segments that fold and unfold like paper cranes, creating an intricate network of mineral formations. The warm, saturated colors pulse with subtle rhythm, mimicking the flow of energy through geological structures. Each geode rotates independently while orbiting around the central axis, creating a mesmerizing interplay of light and form that suggests both mechanical precision and organic growth patterns.
