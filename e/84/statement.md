This sketch creates a retro-style driving simulation where the viewer experiences the illusion of motion as the road rushes past a vehicle. The scene features a curved road that appears to extend into the distance, with road markings moving to simulate speed. The player controls the car's lateral movement using arrow keys and can apply strong braking with the spacebar. The vehicle automatically accelerates and decelerates, maintaining a constant speed unless overridden by user input or braking. The visual design uses simple geometric shapes to represent the sky, ground, road, and car, creating a nostalgic arcade-style driving experience that demonstrates basic principles of perspective and motion in computer graphics.
