This sketch creates an immersive, organic field of interconnected fluid waves that pulse and flow across the screen in deep cobalt and indigo tones. Using a system of 800 independently animated particles, each wave expands and contracts smoothly over time, creating a sense of continuous motion and depth reminiscent of oceanic currents. The visual effect is achieved through careful manipulation of color saturation and brightness alongside smooth scaling transformations, producing a hypnotic rhythm that maintains constant visual activity without any external input. The technique relies on sine-based oscillation to drive the pulsing behavior while maintaining a consistent color palette focused exclusively on rich blue gradients.
