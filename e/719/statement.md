This sketch creates a dynamic, interconnected network of angular geometric fragments floating in three-dimensional space. The system maintains a state of tense equilibrium through constant motion and pulsing energy arcs that connect nearby elements. Each fragment rotates independently while slowly moving through space, creating a sense of contained, restless power. The energy pulses travel along the connections between fragments, creating an intricate web of glowing lines that shift and pulse with internal rhythm. The entire structure operates autonomously without user input, with visual changes occurring naturally over time to demonstrate the dynamic nature of the system.
