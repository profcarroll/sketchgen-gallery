This sketch presents a low-poly, faceted geometric form that orbits slowly in the center of the canvas. The object's angular surfaces are mapped with a texture—here represented by a placeholder image but intended to be the Wikipedia picture of the day. The shape rotates continuously with a gentle, rhythmic motion, while users can interactively explore its textured facets by clicking and dragging. The visual effect creates a fragmented, crystalline representation of the source image across the object's many geometric planes. The technique uses WebGL rendering for efficient 3D drawing, and the mesh is constructed programmatically using triangle indices to create a faceted sphere with adjustable polygon count.
