This sketch creates a dynamic, sound-responsive field of geometric lines that organically evolve across the canvas. The system begins with simple line segments moving independently, but periodically reorganizes into clear hexagonal and honeycomb patterns. Sound input modulates both the density and speed of movement, while also controlling how frequently these structural transformations occur. The visual effect suggests a living lattice structure that breathes and pulses with rhythm, responding to audio in real time through subtle changes in motion and organization.
