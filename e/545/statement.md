This sketch immerses the viewer in a vast, architectonic space defined by intersecting geometric planes that pulse with emerald energy. Through carefully orchestrated animation and light effects, it evokes a sense of controlled dynamism where structural elements glow and shift subtly. The composition features multiple translucent planes with visible "arterial seams" that pulse rhythmically, creating a sense of internal flow and life within the space. Luminous nodes appear at key intersection points, while crystalline particles tessellate throughout the environment, reinforcing the feeling of an immense, living structure. The piece relies on WEBGL rendering to create depth and scale, with constant motion that keeps the viewer engaged without overwhelming them.
