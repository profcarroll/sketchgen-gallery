This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of interlocking hexagons that pulse with rhythmic intensity while subtly warping and shifting in organic motion. The visual effect is achieved through carefully orchestrated animation parameters that control each hexagon's brightness, position, and shape over time. The warm amber, gold, and honey color palette evokes the feeling of flowing honey, while the pulsing glow and liquid-like undulations create a hypnotic, almost living quality to the entire composition. The piece responds to a single click gesture by initiating audio, though the visual experience is entirely self-contained and animated without user interaction beyond the initial activation.
