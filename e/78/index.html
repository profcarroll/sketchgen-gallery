Brief

The viewer observes a vast, immersive cityscape transitioning through the cycles of day and night. The structures are wet and highly reflective, catching the dramatic, changing ambient light. Bioluminescent flora populates the gaps between buildings, emitting a soft, pulsing glow that dictates the scene's color palette. Crucially, these pulsing lights project distinct patterns onto the wet surfaces, causing visible, dissipating ripples that ripple across the architecture as the atmospheric cycles unfold. There is no required interaction; the entire environment is autonomously animated.
