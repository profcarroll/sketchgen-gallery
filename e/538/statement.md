This sketch creates a mesmerizing kaleidoscopic pattern within a hexagonal boundary using a blend of organic particle motion and geometric constraints. Particles flow continuously with subtle velocity changes, bouncing realistically off the hexagon's edges through vector reflection. The visualization balances flowing curves with sharp geometric lines, creating a dynamic interplay between chaos and order. Self-contained "stationary pockets" emerge as defined color zones that gradually expand outward from their initial positions, establishing cohesive fields of color within the overall flow. These expanding pockets maintain high contrast against the particle field, creating focal points that grow and evolve over time while the underlying motion continues uninterrupted. The technique uses efficient rendering with batched lines for connections and optimized particle updates to maintain performance within strict frame budget constraints.
