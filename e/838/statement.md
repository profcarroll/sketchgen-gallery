This sketch creates a mesmerizing, self-contained field of pulsating concentric arcs that rhythmically expand and contract in unison. Each ring pulses with a slightly different timing, creating a harmonious wave-like motion across the canvas. The colors smoothly cycle through the entire visible spectrum, shifting from one hue to the next as the rings pulse, producing a continuous, hypnotic visual effect. The entire system operates autonomously without any external input, demonstrating a simple yet elegant form of generative art that evolves continuously over time.
