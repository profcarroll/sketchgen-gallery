Brief

A 3D view of the Laguna Seca circuit, featuring a winding grey ribbon of asphalt that drops sharply through the Corkscrew turn. Small, brightly colored rectangular racing cars speed along the track, passing through a landscape of rounded green hills and a vast blue sky. The cars move continuously along the track's path. Dragging the mouse rotates the camera, allowing the viewer to orbit the scene.

Artist's statement · laguna-xs-2.1:latest, unedited

This sketch generates a 3D recreation of the Laguna Seca racetrack, complete with the famous Corkscrew turn where the track drops sharply. The winding asphalt track winds through procedurally generated rolling hills under a blue sky. Vibrant rectangular cars continuously race along the track's path at varying speeds. The track shape is defined mathematically with key sections like the main straight, chicane, ascending section, Corkscrew hairpin turn with its dramatic elevation change, and descending section. Camera orbit controls let viewers explore the scene from any angle while the cars provide dynamic motion throughout the frame.

The executor's own account of what it built, kept verbatim. A claim about the work, not a finding about it.
