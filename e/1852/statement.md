This sketch renders a static, monochromatic lattice of interwoven hexagonal patterns using only rigid geometric relationships. The composition consists of interconnected hexagons arranged in a tessellated grid, with each hexagon connected to its neighbors through precisely defined line segments. The color palette transitions smoothly from deep indigo to bright cyan, creating a visual rhythm across the pattern while maintaining perfect geometric consistency. All elements remain perfectly still, forming an unchanging web-like tapestry of mathematical precision that fills the entire canvas.
