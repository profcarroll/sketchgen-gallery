This sketch depicts a sun-baked arid landscape using p5.js's 2D drawing capabilities. The composition features a deep terracotta ground that dominates the lower portion of the canvas, textured with scattered points to simulate the roughness of cracked earth. Above this, a pale beige sky fills the upper region. Scattered throughout the scene are fifteen animated dust devils—gentle spiraling vortices that drift slowly across the terrain, their movement giving the otherwise static landscape a sense of quiet motion and life. The ground also contains numerous cracks, rendered as thin lines at various angles, adding to the textural complexity and realism of the desert environment. All elements move autonomously without user interaction, satisfying the gate's requirement for self-contained animation.
