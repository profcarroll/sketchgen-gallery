This sketch depicts two fluid streams of colored particles that move in opposition yet maintain a consistent distance between them. One stream pulses rhythmically, expanding and contracting in sync with an increasing pulse frequency, creating a visual effect of rapid expansion and contraction. The particles follow simple rules: they repel each other within their own group to avoid clumping, while being attracted to particles in the opposing group. The visual result is a dynamic, flowing system where two distinct flocks move together yet maintain separation, with one flock pulsing dramatically over time to create a sense of rhythmic expansion and contraction.
