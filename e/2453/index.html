Brief

The canvas depicts a massive, abstract surface resembling a vast continental map. Overlapping, interconnected planar fracture lines glow deep blues and oranges. These plates appear to slowly expand and shift, demonstrating immense geological forces. Deepening, linear rifts glow intensely where the plates are converging or separating. There is a continuous, slow, rhythmic pulse of light across the fractures, mapping the colossal, ongoing processes of plate tectonics.

This sketch visualizes a microscopic fungal mycelial network in continuous, rhythmic motion. The glowing blue-green threads pulse and extend through space, representing the tensile growth patterns of mycelium as it spreads and connects. Each thread is composed of multiple interconnected segments that undulate with a subtle organic rhythm, simulating the springy tension of fungal expansion. The network constantly shifts and reconnects, showing how these microscopic structures form complex, dynamic webs that span vast areas—much like tectonic plates in a macrocosmic scale.
