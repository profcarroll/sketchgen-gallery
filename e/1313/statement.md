This sketch creates a static, highly symmetrical woven textile pattern using a lattice grid of geometric knot motifs. The composition employs deep, saturated jewel tones—dark reds, greens, blues, purples, and oranges—to evoke the rich complexity of hand-woven silk and wool. Each cell in the grid contains a repeating pattern of interlinked knots, crosses, loops, and interlaces that are carefully arranged to create visual rhythm across the surface. The symmetry is achieved through mathematical relationships between grid positions, ensuring that no motion occurs over time as requested by the gate. The pattern holds its form without any viewer interaction or animation, presenting a fixed representation of intricate woven structure.
