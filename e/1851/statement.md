This sketch depicts an ethereal cycle of transformation through fluid, luminous particles that evolve from steam tendrils into stable glyphs and finally fragment into glowing crystals. The visual narrative unfolds as particles curl and dissipate, forming ancient-looking characters that drift slowly across the screen before fracturing into geometric crystal structures. These crystals briefly maintain their form before dissolving back into the original steam, creating a continuous, cyclical process of material transformation. The work employs noise-based movement to simulate organic flow while using particle systems and spatial hashing to manage complex interactions within the constrained computational environment.
