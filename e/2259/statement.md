This sketch creates a mesmerizing digital lattice structure that evokes the aesthetic of an imagined 1980s arcade cabinet display. Through thousands of glowing neon lines that continuously shift and reorganize, it simulates the rapid calculations of a digital system in motion. The composition uses noise-based animation to create organic yet structured movement, with lines intersecting and flowing in complex patterns that pulse and evolve over time. The visual effect emerges from simple geometric elements arranged in a way that suggests both mathematical precision and natural organic growth, creating an ever-changing architectural field that fills the entire canvas.
