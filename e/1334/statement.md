This sketch creates a dreamlike collage of drifting, sepia-toned photographs that slowly move across the canvas with subtle rotations and gentle bouncing. Each image has torn edges and a warm, vintage color palette, evoking memories floating through consciousness. Light leak particles drift slowly upwards, adding to the nostalgic atmosphere. Users can click on images to bring them to the foreground or click anywhere else to shift the color tint of the topmost image. Dragging allows users to reposition the images freely, creating a tactile, interactive memory-scene where each interaction alters the drifting composition. The visual effect is achieved through layered movement and simple physics, giving the impression of soft, floating fragments of the past.
