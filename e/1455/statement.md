This sketch depicts an autonomous ecosystem of growing, pulsing cells that spontaneously divide. Starting with a single cell at the center, each cell expands rhythmically while slowly drifting across the canvas. When a cell reaches a critical size, it halts its movement and splits into two daughter cells, which then begin their own growth cycle. The process repeats continuously, creating an ever-increasing population of vibrant, pulsing entities that fill the screen. Each cell maintains its own color hue while exhibiting synchronized rhythmic pulses before splitting, visualizing a simple yet mesmerizing form of biological replication in a digital space.
