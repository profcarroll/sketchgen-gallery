This sketch creates a hypnotic display of circular discs suspended from an invisible central axis, each swinging with its own rhythmic motion. The entire array slowly drifts across the canvas, creating a sense of controlled instability as the discs sway and swing in varying amplitudes. The visual effect is enhanced by subtle trails that follow each disc's movement, forming a continuous flow of color and motion. The system uses a combination of harmonic oscillation and random phase variations to create natural-looking mechanical motion while maintaining a cohesive overall rhythm.
