This sketch creates an immersive underground cavern scene with luminescent crystals that drip from the ceiling and cast shifting colored shadows on the walls. The crystals pulsate with inner light, creating a hypnotic glow effect as they slowly descend. A glowing orb follows mouse movements, illuminating different crystal formations while dragging reveals hidden color veins within the rock. The cave depth subtly expands and contracts to suggest vast underground chambers, with particles falling from the ceiling to simulate sediment accumulation. The scene animates smoothly without any user interaction beyond mouse movement, creating an entrancing visual experience that captures the mysterious beauty of a glowing underground world.
