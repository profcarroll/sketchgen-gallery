This sketch creates a vibrant jigsaw puzzle where colorful rectangular pieces are scattered across the canvas. Each piece has a distinct color and maintains its own position, allowing users to click and drag them around. The interface is simple yet engaging—pieces can be moved freely, but they do not snap into place, encouraging exploration of how fragments might fit together. The visual design uses solid fills with visible borders to clearly define each jigsaw piece, creating a dynamic interaction that responds directly to user input.
