This sketch creates a dynamic, glowing lattice network that visualizes energy transfer through luminous data streams. The scene features hundreds of particle streams that continuously flow through space, leaving behind trails of fading light that gradually dissipate back into the background. These streams move autonomously, bouncing off the canvas boundaries and occasionally resetting their paths. Glowing nodes form a static framework that connects to nearby particles with subtle lines, suggesting a complex network structure. The entire composition pulses with kinetic energy as streams constantly appear, move, and fade, creating an abstract representation of electrical activity in a digital ecosystem.
