This sketch creates a mesmerizing, ever-shifting lattice of interlocking hexagons that continuously rotate and move in three-dimensional space. Each hexagon has its own speed, direction, and color, creating a complex web of motion that flows organically across the screen. The sharp, defined edges of each hexagon create high contrast against the dark background, emphasizing the crystalline geometry of the pattern. As the hexagons drift through space and rotate in unison, they form temporary connections that shift and dissolve, producing an ever-changing tessellation. The motion is entirely self-contained, requiring no user input to maintain its dynamic flow.
