This sketch creates an immersive deep space nebula visualization where cosmic particles flow and pulse in response to a massive pendulum's rhythmic swing. The composition features thousands of glowing particles that move in wave-like patterns, responding to the pendulum's arc which creates shifting radial gradients across the canvas. As the pendulum sweeps through its motion, the entire nebula intensifies and dims in synchronized waves, creating an ethereal cosmic dance. The color palette transitions from deep blues to vibrant purples and pinks, with particles pulsing and flowing like celestial gas clouds under the influence of massive gravitational forces. The visualization captures the mesmerizing interplay between order and chaos, where predictable pendulum motion generates the dynamic, fluid beauty of a cosmic nebula.
