Brief

Under the mouse cursor, thousands of brightly colored particles trail dynamically, creating a continuous, ephemeral wake. As the cursor moves across the canvas, these particles quickly scatter and fade, but before they vanish, they momentarily stain the background. This leaves behind a faint, residual line of deep ink that hangs on the canvas, slowly dissipating over time and subtly darkening the passage the cursor has recently made. The drawing is entirely dependent on the user's movement.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an interactive particle system that responds to mouse movement. Thousands of colorful particles emerge from the cursor position and scatter outward with physics-based motion, leaving a lingering trail that gradually fades. The particles are dynamically generated and managed in a fixed pool, creating an ephemeral wake effect. As the cursor moves across the canvas, particles quickly disperse and fade, but before disappearing they briefly stain the background with their color, forming a persistent visual trace. The system uses a combination of physics simulation and temporal fading to create a continuous, evolving pattern that captures the essence of motion through particle dynamics.
