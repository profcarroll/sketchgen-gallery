This sketch creates a serene beach scene with animated waves and interactive sand. The composition features pale yellow sand piles arranged near a wooden stall under a bright sky. Gentle waves roll in from the left, creating subtle undulations in the sand line. When viewers drag their cursor across the sand, virtual particles respond by forming temporary mounds or scoops that gradually fade away. The scene uses a soft sun-drenched color palette of blues, yellows, and browns to evoke calm and warmth. The interaction is immediate and tactile, allowing users to reshape the virtual landscape with simple mouse movement while maintaining the peaceful, meditative quality of the environment.
