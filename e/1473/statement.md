This sketch presents a serene yet responsive clock that embodies the tension between stillness and motion. The hand pulses gently like a heartbeat, maintaining a constant rhythm that draws the viewer's attention to its subtle movement. When clicked, the entire system responds with an intense acceleration — the hand spins rapidly in a chaotic spiral before gradually returning to its natural, pulsing cadence. The interaction is immediate and visceral: a single gesture transforms the quiet clock into a dynamic whirlwind of motion, only to yield gracefully back to its contemplative state. This simple interaction explores how a small user input can trigger a dramatic shift in behavior while maintaining a consistent underlying aesthetic.
