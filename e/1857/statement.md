This sketch presents a monumental steel pathway that defies expectation through its dramatic ascent and sudden transformation. The viewer begins by ascending along a curved, oxidized steel structure that climbs toward the center of an empty space. At its peak, the path makes a sharp perpendicular turn, dropping vertically before resolving into a vast, uninterrupted horizontal plane. The sculpture's geometry challenges linear progression, using heavy, textured surfaces to convey weight and deliberate construction. The transition from verticality to stillness creates a powerful sense of resolution, where the viewer's gaze is drawn across an expansive, stark platform that terminates the journey with overwhelming scale and quiet permanence.
