This sketch visualizes a molecular structure as an animated double helix composed of colored spheres representing nucleotide bases. The helix exhibits organic, flowing motion that causes the structure to wind and unwind continuously. As the helix moves, individual base spheres occasionally come into close proximity, triggering the formation of temporary, luminous connections between complementary pairs (A-T and G-C). These dynamic bonds illustrate hydrogen-bonding interactions in a visually engaging way. The entire structure rotates slowly to provide different viewing perspectives, while the pulsating size and position of the spheres create an organic feel that suggests molecular dynamics. The visualization uses WebGL for 3D rendering and employs performance optimizations to maintain smooth animation despite the complex interactivity.
