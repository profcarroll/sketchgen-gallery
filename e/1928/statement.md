This sketch presents a cross-sectional view of a monumental concrete structure, revealing the intricate layers and crystalline formations within. Through slow, geological motion, distinct sedimentary layers rise and fall in mesmerizing counter-gravity patterns, evoking the passage of vast time through the rigid framework of the earth. The muted earth tones and subtle crystalline whites create a sense of deep, patient transformation. The visualization uses batched point rendering to efficiently display thousands of particles while maintaining the slow, meditative pace that characterizes geological processes.
