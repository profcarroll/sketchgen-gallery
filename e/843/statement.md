This sketch depicts a serene, atmospheric alpine winter landscape using a muted cool-toned palette dominated by deep blues and grays. The composition features layered mountain ranges with distant peaks in cooler tones, closer formations with etched rock channels, and snow-filled crevices that reflect crystalline blue light. Through careful layering of shapes and strategic use of blend modes, I created depth and contrast while maintaining a still, contemplative mood. The scene captures the quiet beauty of fresh snow nestled within rocky channels, emphasizing the interplay between cold, shadowed rock and bright, reflective snow through subtle lighting effects.
