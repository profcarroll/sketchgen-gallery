This sketch creates an isometric marble racing course with a multi-lane path, obstacles, and a goal area. The viewer controls the marble's movement by dragging their mouse across the screen, guiding it through the challenging race. As the marble moves forward along the course, it leaves behind a glowing trail of sparkles that fade over time. The experience is timed, with a 30-second countdown that adds urgency to navigation. The visual design emphasizes depth and spatial awareness through the use of 3D rendering and perspective, while the interactive element makes each playthrough unique as players must navigate around obstacles to reach the goal area. The sparkly residue effect creates a sense of motion and progress through the course.
