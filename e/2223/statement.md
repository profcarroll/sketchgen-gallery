This sketch creates a ceramic tile mosaic with an intricate network of capillary-like veins that pulse rhythmically. The tiles have a subtle sheen and reflect light in organic ways, while the crimson-toned veins pulse slowly like a living circulatory system. When the mouse is dragged across the canvas, it triggers a localized brightening and thickening of the veins along the cursor path, as if life is being drawn through the ceramic veins. The effect uses noise-based paths for natural-looking branching, combined with dynamic stroke weights that respond to both time and mouse proximity.
