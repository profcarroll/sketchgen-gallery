This sketch creates a fluid, watery chessboard where dozens of colored pieces drift autonomously across a grid. The pieces—modeled as simple geometric forms with gentle rotation—move smoothly between randomly assigned positions, creating an organic flow that suggests being carried by water. Each piece has its own speed and direction, but they all respond to the same underlying rhythm, creating a harmonious yet unpredictable pattern. The color palette evokes aquatic tones, and the constant motion generates a sense of perpetual change without any viewer input. The composition feels alive and dynamic, like an underwater ecosystem where the rules of chess have been transformed into a flowing dance.
