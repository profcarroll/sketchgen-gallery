This sketch simulates the experience of watching text appear on vintage paper, as if typed by an old mechanical typewriter. Characters flow across the page in a continuous stream, gradually accelerating to create a sense of increasing fluency and confidence. The underlying paper subtly warps and shifts rhythmically beneath the typing action, enhancing the tactile feeling of mechanical effort and progress. The effect creates an immersive, meditative experience that captures both the deliberate nature of early typewriters and their eventual smoothness as the typist gains mastery.
