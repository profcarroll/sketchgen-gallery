This sketch creates an endless cascade of colorful rectangular blocks that fall and spiral downward in a mesmerizing pattern. The blocks vary in size, shape, and rotation, creating an unpredictable avalanche effect as they move through the canvas. Each block is influenced by gravity and friction, bouncing off the edges of the screen while maintaining their dynamic movement. A central vortex draws the eye into the scene, reinforcing the spiral motion without ever reaching a center point. The visual rhythm combines constant motion with subtle variations in color and geometry to produce an hypnotic, ever-changing display that captivates the viewer's attention through pure visual flow.
