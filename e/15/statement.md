This sketch creates a three-dimensional scene of a marble rolling through a curved geometric funnel using p5.js's WEBGL renderer. The marble follows a natural physics-based motion influenced by gravity, bouncing along the funnel's inner surface as it descends. The cool color palette features reflective stone tones and bright metallic accents that emphasize the dynamic path of the rolling ball. The animation runs continuously without user interaction, with the marble resetting to its starting position when it exits the funnel structure, creating an endless loop of naturalistic motion.
