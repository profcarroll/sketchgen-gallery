This sketch visualizes the interplay between natural organic forms and geometric structures through a dynamic 3D environment. It depicts petrified mangrove roots rendered in deep earth tones against a shimmering blue background, while luminous vector schematics pulse and flow with rhythmic mechanical energy. The entire composition responds to a simulated pendulum motion that drives both the organic sway of root forms and the geometric movement of the glowing line structures. The piece suggests an underlying technological life within natural formations, creating a tension between ancient organic processes and artificial mechanical rhythms, all rendered in a continuously shifting field of light and shadow.
