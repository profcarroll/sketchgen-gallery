This sketch creates an immersive visualization of Venetian glass mosaics through a rhythmic, pulsing field of glowing particles. The system is governed by an unseen pendulum's swing that generates cyclical patterns which expand and contract across the canvas. Each particle responds to both its own natural rhythm and to external pulses introduced by viewer interaction. When clicked, the canvas introduces sharp bursts of energy that momentarily distort the smooth, organic decay of the patterns, creating a dynamic interplay between order and chaos. The color palette shifts through jewel-toned hues while maintaining an underlying harmony that reflects the intricate beauty of glass craftsmanship.
