This sketch creates an ethereal display of luminous orbs drifting through space, each leaving behind faint glowing trails that trace their path. The orbs are drawn with pulsing effects and glow, moving along invisible spiraling currents that originate from the center of the canvas. The trails are created by storing previous positions of each orb over time, forming delicate ribbons of light that blend into the background. The scene continuously evolves without any user input, creating an autonomous tapestry of color and motion that feels both organic and otherworldly.
