This sketch simulates the aesthetic of a retro camera viewfinder, reminiscent of old mobile phone screens. It renders a dark viewing area with numerous horizontal scanlines that move in a consistent wave pattern, creating an effect of signal interference or deep processing within the camera's view. The animation is driven by time-based sine waves that shift each line vertically, producing a continuous, hypnotic motion across the entire canvas without any flickering or random behavior. The visual effect mimics the static and signal distortion often seen in vintage electronic devices, offering a nostalgic yet dynamic representation of early digital imaging interfaces.
