This sketch depicts the slow, continuous weathering of massive stone facades through a simulation of mineral dissolution and fragmentation. The composition features dark, monolithic stone structures that gradually release particles in a cascading pattern reminiscent of falling dominoes. As time progresses, fine dust and small fragments detach from the stone surfaces and fall at varying speeds and trajectories, creating an ongoing process of erosion. The technique uses a combination of static stone elements with dynamically generated particles that follow physics-based movement patterns, producing a perpetual transformation of the landscape without requiring any user interaction. The viewer witnesses a continuous cycle of dissolution where the solid, weathered facades slowly give way to settling dust and debris, capturing the essence of geological time through visual motion.
