This sketch visualizes the periodic table as an organized grid where each element is represented by a colored block displaying its chemical symbol and atomic number. The blocks pulse gently with subtle animation to give a sense of living data. Elements are color-coded by group, and faint lines connect elements within the same group to show relationships. When a viewer clicks on any element, that element expands and brightens significantly to reveal additional information such as atomic mass and electron configuration. The visualization maintains a structured aesthetic while providing an interactive experience that allows users to explore the periodic table's data through simple clicks.
