This sketch visualizes the microscopic world of polished quartz, capturing how light interacts with its crystalline structure. The scene features a lattice of diffraction gratings—thin, rod-like structures that split light into colorful beams. These gratings are arranged in a three-dimensional space, creating an intricate network where light particles bounce around and create dynamic interference patterns. As the viewer's perspective rotates slowly through the space, these interactions produce shifting, blooming color patterns that evoke the ethereal beauty of crystalline light manipulation. The technique uses batched rendering to maintain performance while achieving the visual complexity of a highly detailed, animated crystal surface.
