This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of expanding concentric rings that emanate from the center of the canvas. Each ring pulses outward while simultaneously exhibiting a dynamic wave-like ripple effect, creating an organic, flowing motion that feels both mechanical and alive. The rings are rendered with vibrant fluorescent colors that fade into the background, emphasizing their continuous expansion. The visual effect is achieved through careful management of each ring's radius, speed, and phase-based ripple animation, all updated independently to create a complex but harmonious pattern that evolves without external input.
