This visualization renders the cross-sections of massive fossilized ocean shells as delicate chalk markings on a dark slate background, evoking deep geological history. The scene pulses with a slow, decaying electric rhythm that simulates visual noise from an old broadcast transmission. When clicked, the structured lines rapidly scatter into bright, fragmented points, creating a glitch effect that disrupts the ordered fossil geometry. The sketch uses WEBGL to create layered planar structures that breathe and pulse, with energy bursts bleeding across the chalk outlines like data corruption or chemical reactions. The visual metaphor captures both the fragility of ancient geological formations and the sudden disruption of digital systems.
