This sketch visualizes a dynamic lattice system undergoing a phase transition from fluid hexagonal structure to crystalline form. The work depicts an electrical field that pulses through the material, causing it to gradually solidify and develop mineral-like formations. As the energy intensifies, the lattice becomes increasingly strained, eventually reaching a point of structural collapse where the entire system fractures and reforms. The visualization uses mathematical geometry to create the hexagonal grid, animated pulse effects to simulate electrical energy, and progressive crystallization that builds up to dramatic disruption. The viewer witnesses an abstract representation of material transformation through energy interaction, moving from ordered structure toward chaotic release of tension.
