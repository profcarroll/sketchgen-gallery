This sketch renders an arid, sun-baked landscape dominated by deep terracotta earth tones and a contrasting beige sky. Sparse acacia trees stand across the ground, which is noticeably clearer of dense scrub than typical depictions. The only movement comes from subtle, wispy dust devils that drift slowly across the terracotta plane. Using simple geometric shapes and color blending, the composition emphasizes the contrast between the bright sky and rich earth tones. The dust devils move with gentle, organic motion, creating a sense of open stillness without overwhelming detail or complexity.
