This sketch creates an mesmerizing cascade of falling dominoes that spiral toward the center of the canvas. The dominoes continuously pour from the edges, bouncing off walls and gradually spiraling inward due to their physics-based movement and rotation. Each domino has unique properties including size, speed, and color, creating a dynamic, flowing pattern that never collapses into a single point but instead maintains perpetual motion around the center. The visual effect is enhanced by a subtle trail behind each domino and a central spiral vortex that guides the viewer's eye toward the heart of the composition. The system uses physics simulation to create natural-looking interactions while maintaining performance within the strict frame budget constraints.
