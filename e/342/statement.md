This sketch creates a late-1990s network broadcast visualization with intersecting gradient layers that slowly rotate and breathe. The composition features a field of data particles that pulse rhythmically and connect to nearby neighbors through subtle lines. Kinetic typography floats across the screen, complementing the data flow structure. The entire visualization moves at a slow, hypnotic pace using only p5.js's core functionality—no sound or external libraries. The color palette emphasizes deep purples and teals with soft transparency effects that create a layered, data-driven atmosphere typical of early digital network displays.
