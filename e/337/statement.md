This sketch creates an ambient, ever-shifting nebula scene where vast cloud structures drift slowly across a deep violet background. The clouds are formed from flowing, organic shapes that pulse with subtle opacity changes and gentle movements, creating a sense of depth and motion without any user interaction. Within the denser areas of these clouds, tiny bright points appear randomly to simulate starlight, adding visual interest and emphasizing the cosmic scale. The entire composition evolves continuously through color shifts in the background and the rhythmic pulsing of the cloud structures, creating an immersive, tranquil atmosphere that feels both infinite and serene.
