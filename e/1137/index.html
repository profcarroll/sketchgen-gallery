Brief

Two separate, continuous streams of vibrant colored particles traverse the canvas. The streams maintain their individual identities as they drift. As the two distinct streams draw nearer and enter a shared proximity, their internal attraction causes them to gently separate and curve gracefully away from each other, maintaining a separation distance after their closest approach.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch visualizes two distinct streams of colorful particles moving across the canvas. Each stream maintains its own identity while drifting independently through space. As the streams approach each other, their particles experience a gentle repulsive force that causes them to curve away from one another, creating an elegant separation. The system demonstrates emergent behavior where individual particle interactions result in coordinated group motion. The visual effect emphasizes fluid continuity and natural-looking avoidance dynamics, with particles maintaining smooth trajectories while respecting their own stream boundaries.
