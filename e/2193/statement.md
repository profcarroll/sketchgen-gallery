This sketch creates an immersive subterranean environment using WebGL rendering to depict a vast bioluminescent cave system. The scene features deep blue gradients that evoke the feeling of endless underwater depths, with crystalline formations that gently pulse and shift in hue to simulate internal light sources. Soft, drifting particles represent nebula dust that flows through the cavern space, creating a sense of movement and depth. The rendering uses efficient batching techniques to maintain performance while achieving the atmospheric quality described in the brief. The viewer is invited to experience the mysterious scale and organic grandeur of this imagined underground world.
