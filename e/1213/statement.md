This sketch creates two continuous streams of particles that flow across the screen with distinct colors and movement patterns. The red-orange stream (particles 1) and blue-green stream (particles 2) move independently while maintaining their unique trajectories. Each particle leaves a subtle fading trail behind it, creating a sense of motion and temporal continuity. The particles wrap around the edges of the canvas, ensuring continuous flow. The visual effect emphasizes the contrast between the two streams while showing how they interact dynamically across the screen space.
