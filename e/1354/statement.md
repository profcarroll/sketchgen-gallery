This sketch simulates a bubbling liquid environment with deep blue water at the bottom and rising, expanding bubbles that transform into white foam at the surface. The motion is governed by a rhythmic heat level that increases with time and user interaction, causing bubbles to rise faster and more intensely when clicked. The visual effect captures the essence of a simmering pot of water with steam-like wisps drifting across the surface, all rendered in a single continuous flow without any external audio or visual artifacts.
