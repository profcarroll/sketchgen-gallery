This sketch visualizes orbital mechanics through a system of luminous particles that follow stable, repeating paths around an invisible central anchor. Each particle traces a glowing trail as it orbits, but unlike typical animations, these trails don't simply disappear—they undergo a gradual decay process. As particles complete their orbits, the trailing light fragments into scattered, faint points that slowly fade away. This creates a mesmerizing effect where continuous motion meets dissipative processes, illustrating both centripetal force and the natural decay of energy in orbital systems. The visualization emphasizes the elegant balance between order and dissolution, showing how stable patterns can emerge from dynamic forces while simultaneously revealing the subtle breakdown of those patterns over time.
