This sketch creates an immersive, liminal space centered around a tranquil pool of water. Using WebGL rendering, it simulates the subtle, continuous ripples that emanate from stillness, generating complex, liquid-like patterns across the water's surface. The muted concrete tones of the surrounding environment contrast with deep cyan and cerulean blues, which shift dynamically to reflect the movement of the water. The entire scene exists in a state of suspended, ethereal stillness—only the slow, mesmerizing motion of the water itself breaks the silence, evoking a sense of calm contemplation within an expansive, empty space.
