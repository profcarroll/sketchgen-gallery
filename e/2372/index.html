Brief

The sketch begins with a brilliant, focused point of light that rapidly expands and decays. As it expands, the initial light structure breaks down into intricate, high-speed geometric forms. These forms flare out, losing coherence and color depth in a dramatic collapse of pure energy. The movement is continuous and accelerating, transforming the pure decay into a structured, vibrant tableau. The final state reveals the pattern settling into the hard, vibrant borders and saturated colours characteristic of a stained-glass panel, solidifying the chaotic energy into lasting, jeweled clarity.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch depicts the transformation of structured cyclical energy into layered geological strata, rendered entirely in poured concrete aesthetics. The composition features horizontal bands of color that shift and flow with wave-like motion, representing the sedimentary layers building up over time. At the center, a dynamic geometric core expands and contracts, its forms breaking down from pure symmetry into complex, interlocking shapes. The visual language combines the rhythmic motion of geological time with the explosive energy of structural collapse, creating a sense of both stability and turbulence within the same frame. The concrete palette—deep grays and muted earth tones—gives weight to the imagery while the geometric patterns suggest both natural and artificial construction.
