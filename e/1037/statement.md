This sketch creates a still, intricate Persian rug pattern using p5.js. It generates a symmetrical woven design with a deep jewel tone palette dominated by browns, purples, and golds. The composition features a repeating grid structure filled with stylized floral motifs at regular intervals, all contained within a structured framework. A prominent border along the bottom edge displays a rhythmic sequence of alternating large squares and diamond shapes, reinforcing the architectural quality of the pattern. The work is purely decorative and remains completely still, embodying the rich, detailed aesthetic of traditional Persian carpet weaving through digital means.
