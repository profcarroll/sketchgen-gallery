This sketch creates an evolving network of delicate, crystalline lines that appear to grow and self-organize across the canvas. Using Perlin noise to guide each line's direction and movement, the system generates a constantly shifting pattern reminiscent of mineral formation or cellular growth. The soft color palette and fine line weights emphasize the organic, almost paper-like texture of the composition. Each line segment follows its own path while subtly responding to neighboring movements through shared noise fields, creating emergent patterns that expand across the entire canvas without any explicit collision detection or spatial hashing. The result is a mesmerizing display of continuous, low-detail growth that captures the essence of natural self-assembly.
