This sketch creates a dynamic nighttime harbor scene populated by dozens of illuminated drones that fly in complex patterns across the water and over buildings. The visualization uses p5.js's WEBGL renderer to create a three-dimensional space where drones move with fluid motion between targets. Drones are generated with random starting positions, colors, and speeds, then follow smooth trajectories that can be interrupted by user interaction. Moving the mouse causes nearby drones to respond with subtle gliding motions, while clicking triggers an immediate transformation of the entire drone fleet into one of several pre-defined impressive formations. The scene features a dark night sky, water surface, and urban buildings to ground the aerial spectacle, creating an immersive experience that demonstrates both fluid motion and responsive interaction in a single continuous animation loop.
