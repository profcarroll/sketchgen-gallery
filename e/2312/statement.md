This sketch visualizes the ethereal interior of a deep-sea siphonophore mantle through flowing, semi-translucent sheets that undulate with organic rhythm. The warm-to-cool thermal gradients pulse and shift across the canvas, mimicking biological processes within the creature's gelatinous structure. The visualization uses layered polygonal forms that expand and contract in harmony with a slow, rhythmic pulse, creating an immersive representation of life in the abyss where light never reaches.
