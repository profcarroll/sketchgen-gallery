This sketch creates an interactive stovetop interface where users manipulate a central dial to control four burner flames. The visualization demonstrates the physics of combustion with realistic flame effects—particles rising and fading, color gradients from yellow to orange to red. Crucially, it implements a localized glowing effect that appears directly beneath active burners, illuminating the surrounding metal surface in vivid red to simulate heat transfer. The interface responds dynamically to user interaction through dragging the dial, changing flame intensity across all burners simultaneously while maintaining the precise glow mapping under each flame source.
