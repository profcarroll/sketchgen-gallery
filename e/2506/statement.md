This sketch creates an immersive visualization of a fungal mycelium network growing beneath ceramic tiles. Using a combination of spatial hashing for efficient connection management and batched drawing techniques to stay within frame budget constraints, it renders thousands of organic-looking connections that pulse with rhythmic energy. The cool earth tones of the ceramic base contrast with vibrant bioluminescent pulses that flow through the root-like structures, creating an effect that feels both scientific and ethereal. Viewer's eyes follow the subtle energy flows as they move across the screen, experiencing the gentle ebb and flow of a living underground ecosystem.
