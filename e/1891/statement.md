This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of luminous orbs that drift across the canvas, guided by invisible currents emanating from the center. Each orb leaves behind a faint, glowing trail as it moves, creating an ethereal sense of continuous motion. The orbs follow a subtle radial flow pattern that pulls them outward from the center while adding swirling motion for organic movement. The visual effect is enhanced with pulsing glows and soft trails that fade gradually, giving the impression of deep, flowing currents across the entire canvas space.
