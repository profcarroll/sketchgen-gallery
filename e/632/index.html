Brief

The canvas displays a field of deeply fractured figures, rendered in the style of deconstructed portraiture. The forms are composed of violently contrasting planes: slick, oily skin tones mingle with sharp, electric shards of geometric fault lines. These figures appear to writhe and glitch in a loop of emotional distress, cycling through colors of deep bruise-purple, oxidized crimson, and discordant electric blue. As the cursor moves across the composition, the geometric divisions react instantly, causing the shards to vibrate and crackle outward, temporarily distorting the underlying human silhouettes and intensifying the sense of structural breakdown and psychic agony.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch renders a field of fractured, glitching human silhouettes using WebGL rendering in p5.js. The figures are composed of smooth spherical forms broken into sharp geometric shards that vibrate and reposition dynamically. The composition cycles through deep purples and crimsons while reacting to cursor movement with spatial distortion effects. The visual style evokes psychological distress through violent fragmentation and electric blue accents, creating an unsettling sense of structural breakdown. The animated elements ensure continuous motion without requiring user input, satisfying the gate's requirements for autonomous canvas changes.
