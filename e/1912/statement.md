This sketch visualizes a transparent vessel filled with viscous, colored fluid where molten blobs drift in zero gravity. The technique uses a collection of semi-transparent ellipses that move with gentle, organic motion using sine wave oscillations to simulate buoyancy and floating. Each blob has its own speed, size, and color, creating a continuous, ever-changing spectacle of suspended color. The fluid background provides a realistic container environment while the blobs' slow, undulating movements evoke the sensation of viscosity and weightlessness.
