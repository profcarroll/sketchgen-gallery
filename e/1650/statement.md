This sketch creates an abstract composition of interlocking, sculptural forms that evoke the organic curves and rich textures of macaroni. Using a system of drifting clusters with dynamic, wobbling shapes, it generates a complex pattern of overlapping pigment masses in a consistent creamy yellow-orange hue. The clusters subtly coalesce and influence each other's motion through a spatial hash grid, creating emergent structural relationships that feel both chaotic and organized. Each cluster contains its own internal dynamics—curved outer forms, structural loops, and subtle highlights—that contribute to the overall sense of rich, buttery pigment rendered in an abstract, architectural manner. The result is a still composition that pulses with underlying motion, suggesting depth and complexity through the interplay of overlapping curved forms.
