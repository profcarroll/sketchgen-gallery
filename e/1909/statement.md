This sketch depicts a cosmic dance of luminous orbs drifting through dark space, guided by invisible currents emanating from the center. Each orb carries its own color and pulse, leaving crystallized trails that define their path through the void. The motion is perpetual yet unpredictable, as if the universe itself is slowly expanding and flowing around a central force. The viewer observes this eternal choreography of light and shadow, where individual particles blend into an ever-shifting constellation of glowing matter.
