This sketch renders an abstract aerial view of a bustling cityscape using only monochromatic tones and flowing light streaks. The urban environment is built from randomly generated buildings with window patterns, creating a sense of density and structure. Overlaid are thousands of light streaks that move independently across the canvas, their paths converging to form repeating floral motifs. These organic shapes emerge from the geometric city grid, suggesting both order and natural flow. The result is a meditative composition that balances urban complexity with delicate, flowing patterns, evoking the feeling of movement through a metropolitan sprawl viewed from above.
