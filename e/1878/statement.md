This sketch presents a mesmerizing display of neon-lit geometric forms in perpetual motion, where octahedra, hexagons, and tetrahedra cascade and pulse across the screen. The shapes move through synchronized rectilinear shifts and helical spirals, creating an intricate dance of form and space. When two forms collide, they instantly fragment into sharp, colored shards that scatter briefly before reconstituting themselves into perfect tetrahedral structures—a cycle of destruction and reconstruction that embodies the kinetic spectacle described in the brief. The visual effect is achieved through careful management of shape transformations, collision detection, and particle systems, all rendered in WebGL for optimal performance while maintaining the vibrant, high-energy aesthetic requested.
