This sketch creates an abstract data cityscape where luminous pathways pulse and drift across a dark canvas, forming a network of interconnected information flows. The visual metaphor captures the invisible yet dynamic nature of data movement through systems. Glowing pathways flow with subtle undulations, while junction points—where three or more pathways converge—pulse with heightened intensity to emphasize critical nodes in the network. The composition uses color and motion to suggest both chaos and organization, evoking the feeling of a living system where information constantly flows and connects. The entire scene is self-contained, generating its own motion without external input, creating a mesmerizing display of abstract digital architecture.
