This sketch presents a sprawling, reflective cityscape where angular buildings gleam with wet surfaces under a perpetual day-night cycle. The scene features geometric reflections cast by bioluminescent pools that pulse gently in sync with the ambient light changes. The architecture is composed of sharp, towering structures that reflect their surroundings dynamically, while the pools sit stably on the ground, casting precise, geometric ripples and circular patterns onto the wet stone and metal surfaces. The continuous motion creates an immersive environment where the interplay between light, shadow, and reflection shifts gradually, evoking a sense of vastness and quiet majesty.
