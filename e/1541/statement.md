This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of animated diagonal stripes that pulse and shift rhythmically across the canvas. Using a fixed number of geometric elements, each stripe rotates at its own pace while oscillating in size, creating a hypnotic pattern of motion and color. The stripes continuously reform through their synchronized yet individual movements, producing a sense of organic flow and dynamic energy without any user interaction. The visual effect emerges from simple mathematical relationships—sinusoidal motion and rotation—combined with vibrant color transitions that maintain sharp contrast while allowing for subtle blending. The result is an autonomous animation that pulses with its own internal rhythm, filling the entire canvas with shifting geometric forms that appear to breathe and evolve over time.
