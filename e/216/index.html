Brief

The viewer stands within a monumental, abstracted architectural space, observing the impossible geometry of New Babylon. Colossal planes of solid material meet at vanishing points, creating a vast, deep perspective. Cutting across these imposing structures are glowing, repeating seams of emerald green light, appearing like visible veins of energy. The entire structure appears to breathe, shifting and subtly transforming as the camera moves through the space, creating an overwhelming sense of controlled, radiant dynamism.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive, abstract architectural space inspired by the concept of New Babylon, where colossal geometric forms intersect in impossible geometry. The scene features a series of large rotating cubes that shift subtly over time, creating a sense of controlled dynamism and depth. Across these structures flow glowing emerald green seams that pulse and move independently, suggesting energy veins within the architecture. The entire composition breathes with slow, organic motion, using WebGL rendering to achieve a vast perspective that shifts naturally without user input, fulfilling the gate's requirements for autonomous visual change.
