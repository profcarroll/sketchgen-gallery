This sketch creates a geological visualization that evokes the slow, rhythmic process of mineral deposition across layered strata. Using a palette of earthy tones and jagged crystalline forms, it layers distinct horizontal planes that represent millions of years of compression and crystallization. The interlocking veins and fracture lines suggest immense pressure and the natural, gradual buildup of mineral formations. Each layer is rendered with subtle variations in color and form to simulate the complex, repetitive patterns found in geological rock formations. The technique relies on layered rectangles with irregular vein shapes and fracture lines to create a sense of depth and structure that mimics real stratified landscapes.
