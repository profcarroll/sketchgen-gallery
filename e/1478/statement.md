This sketch depicts an underground ecosystem where ants tunnel through earthen paths, creating a network of interconnected passages. The visual design uses warm browns and ochres to evoke the texture of soil and rock. When clicked, the entire colony's focus shifts to that point, initiating a dramatic change in the tunnel system - the main tunnel rapidly extends toward the click location, widening as it carves its path. The ants continue their natural behavior, following the tunnels while occasionally emerging from the main passage. The effect is both dynamic and organic, showing how a single action can reshape an entire underground environment through continuous, evolving patterns of excavation and movement.
