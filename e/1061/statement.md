This sketch creates a hypnotic kaleidoscope effect within a hexagonal mask using WebGL rendering. The composition features an intricate interplay between flowing globular particles and sharp crystalline structures that form dynamic fractal networks. The visual tension emerges from the perpetual motion of geometric lines that crisscross the space, while smooth, glowing particles move through the environment following fluid paths. The entire scene rotates slowly, creating a cosmic lattice effect where curves and straight lines constantly resist and define one another, resulting in an ever-evolving pattern of luminous tension.
