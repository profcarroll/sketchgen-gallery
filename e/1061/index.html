Brief

The canvas displays a hypnotic, continuous kaleidoscope pattern contained within a single hexagonal mask. The inner space is defined by an intricate interplay between smooth, flowing globular splines and sharp, geometrically defined crystalline structures. These sharp elements are perpetually coalescing into elaborate, interlocking fractal networks that crisscross the entire interior.
