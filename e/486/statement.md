This sketch renders a serene yet dramatic savanna landscape using p5.js. The composition is structured around a rich palette of terracotta earth tones against a soft beige sky, capturing the essence of an arid environment. Distant jagged rock formations break the horizon, while intricate ground textures reveal detailed cracks and fissures that emphasize the harshness of the terrain. Gentle dust devils drift across the expanse, adding subtle motion to the otherwise static scene. The piece emphasizes texture and light through careful layering and color transitions, creating a moment frozen in time that evokes the stillness and beauty of a remote landscape.
