This sketch creates an ethereal nebula scene with flowing cloud structures that pulse gently in the background. The composition features a deep gradient from midnight blue to violet, with hundreds of translucent, slowly moving clouds that form organic, cosmic shapes. Embedded within these structures are small glowing lights that pulse with subtle rhythmic variations, casting colored reflections across nearby gas formations. Every few seconds, these light sources flare into bright bursts, causing detached glowing fragments to scatter outward, creating dramatic moments of visual intensity against the calm, flowing background. The effect is achieved through layered translucent shapes, continuous motion, and carefully timed pulsing that creates a sense of vast, cosmic time and gentle energy within the still canvas.
