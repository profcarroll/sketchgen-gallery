This sketch creates a static, highly detailed woven textile pattern inspired by traditional Persian rugs. It constructs a central field filled with repeating stylized floral motifs in rich jewel tones, arranged within a structured grid lattice. The composition is framed by an elaborate border composed of interlocking chevron and triangle shapes that form a continuous chain around the canvas edges. The design employs mathematical precision to ensure perfect symmetry and rhythmic repetition throughout the pattern, evoking the careful artistry of handwoven tapestry while remaining completely static and unchanging.
