This sketch creates an immersive, data-flow inspired environment where geometric forms pulse and shift in response to audio input. The visual field is populated with interlocking grids and glowing planes that expand and contract in rhythmic patterns, evoking the sensation of digital information flowing through a network. The neon color palette—electric cyan, magenta, and green—creates a futuristic, almost holographic quality that suggests both structure and chaos. The entire composition reacts dynamically to sound, with low-frequency input causing the geometric elements to pulse more intensely, creating an organic rhythm that transforms the static canvas into a living, breathing system of interconnected forms.
