This sketch creates a spiraling ceramic clock mechanism composed of interlocking tiles that continuously rotate and move in a perpetual motion. Each tile displays a pastoral landscape pattern with seamless optical flow, featuring varied tones of green and blue that mimic sunlit fields and sky. The composition revolves constantly without user input, with tiles arranged in a spiral formation that creates an infinite looping effect. The ceramic tiles maintain their structural integrity while the entire mechanism flows in a cyclical motion, creating a mesmerizing visual representation of time's continuous passage through a landscape-based metaphor.
