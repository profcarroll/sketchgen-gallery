This sketch creates an ever-shifting landscape of organic sand dunes that flow across the canvas in a continuous, breathing motion. Using a combination of overlapping ellipses and procedural noise functions, each dune is rendered as a layered, undulating form with subtle color variations that shift between warm ochre and burnt sienna tones. The system employs time-based movement and perlin noise to simulate the slow, geological forces that shape desert landscapes, creating an immersive visualization of perpetual change without any user interaction required. The visual effect suggests vastness and timelessness, with dunes constantly repositioning themselves in a gentle, hypnotic dance across the entire canvas.
