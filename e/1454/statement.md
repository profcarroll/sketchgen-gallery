This sketch depicts a detailed cross-sectional view of a combustion engine's internal mechanisms in motion. The visualization shows four pistons moving up and down within their cylinders, driven by the mechanical action of connecting rods that link to a rotating crankshaft. A key feature is the spark ignition effect that visually pushes pistons down, creating the cyclic motion of the engine cycle. The entire assembly includes a meshing gear system that demonstrates how mechanical energy flows through the transaxle. The scene renders in 3D using WEBGL with metallic surfaces and particle effects for sparks, showing the continuous interplay between combustion and mechanical transmission in an internal combustion engine.
