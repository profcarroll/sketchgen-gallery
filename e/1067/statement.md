This sketch presents a static arrangement of five musical pads, each with a distinct color and subtle vertical gradient. The pads are evenly spaced horizontally across the canvas with rounded corners, creating a clean and modern interface. When a user clicks on any pad, it visually illuminates and plays a unique musical note corresponding to that pad's position. The interaction is immediate and responsive, providing both visual and auditory feedback. The design emphasizes simplicity and clarity, using minimal shapes and colors to convey functionality while maintaining an elegant aesthetic.
