This sketch renders a dense, geometric lattice structure composed of interlocking hexagonal motifs. Each motif is a perfectly rigid hexagon with internal connecting lines, arranged in a grid pattern across the canvas. The monochromatic cyan-to-indigo color scheme creates a subtle yet defined visual hierarchy, emphasizing the structural integrity and mathematical precision of the web-like formation. The fixed geometry ensures no warping or distortion occurs, presenting a stable, crystalline support network that fills the entire canvas with its repetitive, ordered pattern.
