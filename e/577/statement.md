This sketch presents a serene winter landscape of snow-draped alpine peaks rendered with atmospheric depth and subtle animation. The scene features a gradient sky, layered mountain silhouettes, and thousands of falling snow particles that gently drift across the canvas. When clicked, the viewer triggers an avalanche effect that sweeps down the slopes, disrupting the tranquil scene by scattering debris and repositioning rock formations into the foreground. The technique uses p5.js's drawing primitives to create a cohesive winter scene with dynamic elements that respond to user input, all within the performance constraints of a headless environment.
