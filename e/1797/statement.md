This sketch depicts a vintage paper sheet that gradually deteriorates as text scrolls across it in a typewriter rhythm. The paper begins with subtle warping effects and evolves through permanent creases, hairline fractures, and finally fiber fraying—each stage visualized through layered visual elements that respond to the accumulating text. The viewer observes a continuous, self-contained process of decay that mirrors the physical stress of writing on aged paper, all without any external interaction required.
