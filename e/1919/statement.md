This sketch depicts a lighthouse beam sweeping across an ocean environment in real-time. The visualization combines geometric modeling of the lighthouse structure with dynamic particle effects to simulate the ocean's surface. A rotating beam of light moves across the canvas, casting dramatic illumination that affects the surrounding water particles. The ocean is rendered as a collection of thousands of particles that continuously shift and react to the sweeping beam, creating an animated, colorful environment. The scene uses WebGL rendering for efficient 3D particle handling while maintaining the visual complexity described in the brief. The viewer observes a rhythmic, cyclical light beam interacting with a dynamic underwater world of shifting colors and motion.
