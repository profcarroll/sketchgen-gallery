Brief

The canvas displays a vast, interconnected lattice composed of glowing biological cells. These cells and their structural connections are in a state of constant, organic flux, subtly shifting and reforming over time. When a new cell is spontaneously born within the lattice, the connections immediately surrounding that new cell flare up, emitting a bright, intense glow that visually highlights the point of birth. The entire system pulses with soft, diffused light, creating the impression of a dynamic, living neural network that continually evolves and reorganizes itself on the screen.
