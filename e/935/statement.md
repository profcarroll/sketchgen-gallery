This sketch renders a living lattice of glowing biological cells that continuously evolve and reorganize. Cells are born randomly within the system, triggering intense glows around their birth points. The entire network pulses softly with diffused light, mimicking a dynamic neural structure. Connections between cells shift organically over time, forming and breaking links in a constant state of flux. The visual effect conveys organic complexity through subtle motion and vibrant highlights that emphasize new growth within the system.
