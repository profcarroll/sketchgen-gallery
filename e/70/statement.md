This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic landscape where translucent circles flow slowly across the canvas like a tidal current, their movement creating a sense of continuous motion without any user input. Within this fluid environment, thousands of pointillist stars pulse gently with varying brightness and size, adding depth and rhythm to the scene. The interplay between the slow-moving circles and the pulsing stars creates an immersive, dreamlike visualization that feels alive and constantly evolving. The entire composition exists in a state of continuous motion, with no external interaction required to maintain its mesmerizing flow.
