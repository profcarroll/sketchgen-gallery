This sketch depicts a vast, reflective pool of water within an architectural liminal space, rendered using WebGL for immersive depth and movement. The scene is dominated by deep blues that evoke both calm and mystery, with stark contrasts between brilliant highlights and deep shadows. The water surface is never still—gentle ripples constantly shift and distort the environment, creating a sense of life and motion. These ripples are not merely visual effects but are part of an evolving light system where sharp beams of light penetrate the water's surface, casting vivid highlights that dance across both the pool floor and surrounding walls. The interplay between these elements creates a mesmerizing spectacle that transforms with each frame, capturing the ethereal quality of light interacting with water in an architectural space.
