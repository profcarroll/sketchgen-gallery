This sketch creates an ethereal field of gas clouds that slowly drift through space, each one pulsating with a gentle neon glow. The visual effect is achieved through multiple layered spheres that create a volumetric appearance, with colors shifting softly between electric blues, magentas, and cyans. The clouds move at subtly different speeds and phases, creating a continuous, flowing motion that suggests vast cosmic depths. No interaction is required—just observe the tranquil, ever-changing dance of these glowing particles that seem to float in an endless void.
