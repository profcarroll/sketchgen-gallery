This sketch creates a mesmerizing cosmic scene using 2D canvas rendering to simulate the vastness of space. It features a deep indigo background with swirling Milky Way patterns, countless twinkling stars of varying brightness, and occasional supernova bursts that illuminate the darkness. The subtle motion and glowing effects create an hypnotic sense of celestial flow, with all elements shifting gently over time to evoke the slow, infinite dance of the universe.
