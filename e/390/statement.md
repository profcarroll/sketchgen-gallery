This sketch creates a mesmerizing visualization of abstract, glowing geometric planes that rotate and move through three-dimensional space. The planes are rendered in deep neon colors with a subtle pulsing glow effect when they approach each other. As the planes navigate their virtual environment, connecting lines form between their edges, creating an intricate web of light that intensifies when planes come into proximity. The entire scene operates as a continuous, self-contained simulation where motion and interaction drive the visual complexity, producing an ethereal, dynamic display that feels both organic and algorithmically precise.
