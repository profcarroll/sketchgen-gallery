This sketch visualizes an evolving kaleidoscopic pattern through the interplay of crystalline lattices and smooth globular splines. It simulates a dynamic tension system where particles are connected by springs, creating a constantly shifting structure. The tension lines amplify forces within this system, causing the underlying spline curves to warp and distort across the canvas. The visual effect emerges from the continuous motion of particles interacting through spring physics, with tension lines dynamically adjusting their positions and strength over time. The result is an organic yet structured pattern that evolves without external input, demonstrating how opposing forces create complex geometric forms.
