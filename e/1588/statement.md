This sketch presents a continuous cycle of structural transformation through geometric forms in 3D space. It begins with a stable arrangement of colored cubes floating in space, each subtly animated with gentle motion and rotation. Every few seconds, the system enters a violent collapse phase where these fragments explode outward in chaotic directions, their motion governed by random forces and gravity. After this destruction, the scene resets and begins to reassemble itself into a cohesive helical spiral structure, using the same particles that were once scattered. The viewer witnesses an endless loop of formation and dissolution, with the transition between states smoothly animated through a morphing effect that bridges the gap between the fragmented state and the spiral reconstruction. This creates a mesmerizing visual rhythm that reflects themes of destruction and rebirth in geometric form.
