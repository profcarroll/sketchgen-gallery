This sketch depicts a mechanical shredding apparatus in continuous operation, with books falling into a complex system of rotating gears and cutting plates. The scene features a stack of books slowly descending through the machine while being torn apart by active machinery. Fine dust particles rise above the central gears as debris accumulates, and wisps of steam vent from friction points between the machinery and resisting materials. The entire scene operates autonomously without user input, driven by constant mechanical motion that creates an ongoing visual narrative of destruction and processing. All elements are rendered in 3D using WEBGL with dynamic particle systems for dust and steam effects.
