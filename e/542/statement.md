This sketch recreates the experience of using a retro Nokia N900 camera's viewfinder, complete with simulated analog static, flickering movement, and camera effects. The interface displays a pixellated greenish scene that subtly shifts and moves to simulate a live feed, while overlaying authentic camera information like a flash indicator and focus box. When clicked, the viewfinder captures the scene and transitions through a digital processing animation before resetting to its live view state, mimicking the behavior of the original device's capture cycle.
