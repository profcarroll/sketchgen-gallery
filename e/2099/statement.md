This sketch renders a cosmic visualization of planetary rings composed of thousands of glowing particles in a 3D space. The rings exhibit dynamic motion through orbital resonances and tidal forces, with particles moving in complex, wave-like patterns that create ripples and clumping effects. The scene uses WebGL rendering for performance, batching all particles into a single draw call to maintain frame rate while creating an immersive field of glowing particles that shift and flow over time. The visualization captures the interplay of gravitational forces through periodic bursts of energy that radiate outward across the cosmic scale.
