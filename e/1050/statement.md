This sketch simulates seismic activity beneath a dark grey landscape, with jagged fault lines pulsing and shifting according to mouse interaction. Thick horizontal cracks fracture across the canvas at varying speeds and opacities, responding dynamically to user drag gestures. Random vertical tremors bloom from below, while occasional bright white lightning bolts shoot upward when the cursor releases a drag near the bottom edge. The entire field subtly vibrates on its own, creating an atmosphere of constant tectonic tension that intensifies with human presence.
