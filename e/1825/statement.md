This sketch depicts an autonomous system of cellular replication where individual cells grow and mature through a process of pulsating expansion. When mature, cells enter a contraction phase that visually pinches off new daughter cells, creating a continuous cycle of reproduction. The visual design distinguishes between developing and mature cells: growing cells exhibit soft boundaries with pulsating effects, while mature cells display rigid, stable membranes. The system evolves organically across the canvas, generating complex clusters of interconnected cells through self-sustained replication without external input or control.
