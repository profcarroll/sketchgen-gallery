This sketch creates a dynamic, glitchy mosaic of neon geometric shapes that shift and morph across the screen in rhythmic bursts. Using overlapping gradients and blocky vector forms, it simulates early digital transmission artifacts with a vibrant color palette of electric blue, magenta, and yellow. The motion is driven by cyclical transformations and simulated scanline jitter effects, evoking the feel of broadcast transitions and abstract information fields being formed and dissolved in rapid succession.
