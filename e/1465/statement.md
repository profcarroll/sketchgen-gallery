This sketch presents an interactive subway map that responds to user clicks by dynamically reshaping its network. When a viewer clicks on any station, the entire system fluidly transforms—lines redraw and stations reposition themselves according to the clicked station's influence. The visualization demonstrates a force-directed graph layout algorithm where each station moves away from the clicked point, creating an organic and intuitive representation of how subway connections shift and realign in response to user interaction. The design emphasizes clean, readable lines and color-coded stations while maintaining a smooth, animated transition that makes the transformation visually apparent across the entire canvas.
