This sketch creates a geological visualization of deep-time rock formation with mineral veins that bloom and expand. The canvas displays rich, textured rock surfaces rendered in WEBGL, with delicate crystalline veins slowly growing across the terrain. When clicked, an impact point triggers rapid expansion of nearby veins, simulating sudden pressure or energy release. The system uses noise-based textures for realistic rock appearance and manages thousands of particles efficiently within p5's performance constraints. The viewer witnesses both slow, natural geological processes and dramatic bursts of mineral growth that respond to interaction.
