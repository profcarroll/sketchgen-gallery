This sketch visualizes a deep-sea botanical ecosystem rendered in an electric, otherworldly palette. The viewer observes luminous geometric structures composed of intersecting planes that drift slowly through three-dimensional space. Each structure pulses with internal energy, and when adjacent planes come into critical proximity, connecting lines erupt in sudden bursts of light. The visualization captures the rhythmic, glowing energy of a colossal flora's vascular root systems etched in copper, rendered at the scale of a single garden bed within a Victorian botanical illustration. The effect emerges from subtle wave distortions and proximity-triggered pulses that create an organic sense of life within the rigid geometric forms.
