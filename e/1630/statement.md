This sketch creates an immersive underwater environment using thousands of semi-transparent geometric particles that drift slowly through a gradient blue-green background. The composition features organic shapes—ellipses, rectangles, and triangles—that flow continuously with subtle sway and depth effects, creating a sense of volumetric movement beneath the surface. Soft ripples periodically emerge to simulate water disturbances, while the entire scene maintains a constant, ethereal motion without any viewer interaction. The visual field is filled with dynamic, overlapping elements that shift and blend together, evoking the feeling of being submerged in a vast aquatic space where light and form gently transform.
