This sketch simulates a bioluminescent field of flowing traces resembling deep-sea bacterial biofilms. The particles move organically through the dark space, leaving trails that fade over time and interact with each other's movement. When a user drags their cursor across the canvas, the search mechanism activates — drawing vivid luminous tracks that propagate through the field, momentarily intensifying the pattern and visualizing the path of the bioluminescence. The color palette uses cool blues and teals to evoke the mysterious depths of the ocean, while the flow dynamics create an ever-shifting network of light that responds both to natural motion and user interaction.
