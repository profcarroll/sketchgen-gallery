This sketch presents a highly abstracted self-portrait rendered through interlocking geometric planes arranged in three-point perspective. The composition emphasizes depth and structure through carefully placed shadow lines that sharply define each surface. The form is dramatically structured with facets reflecting light unevenly across the surface, creating distinct shifts in color and reflectivity. Each plane has its own orientation and hue, creating a complex interplay of light and shadow that maintains static composition while conveying volume and spatial relationships through geometric complexity.
