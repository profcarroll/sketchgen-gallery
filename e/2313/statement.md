This sketch creates a dynamic topographical map of sedimentary layers that evokes the feeling of geological time preserved in newsprint. The composition features deep ochre and slate gray horizontal striations that shift slowly over time, mimicking how subterranean currents might continuously re-pigment the surface. The scene includes petrified forest impressions embedded within the sedimentary layers, rendered as organic tree-like structures that pulse gently with the passage of time. A textured newsprint background provides a damp, saturated quality to the entire composition, while overlapping wet ink effects add depth and movement to the geological structures. The piece captures the slow, continuous evolution of geological formations through subtle color shifts and undulating patterns that suggest unseen forces at work beneath the surface.
