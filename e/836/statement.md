This sketch creates an immersive, accelerating passage through abstract molecular and atomic space using WebGL rendering in p5.js. The scene features a tunnel of glowing particles that move in rhythmic patterns, with crystalline geometric structures rotating and pulsing in harmony with a bioluminescent background. The visual experience is entirely self-generated without any user input, continuously evolving through time-based animations. The motion is driven by the forward thrust of the particle system, combined with rotating crystal forms that flare and distort with shifting hues, creating a hypnotic, flowing visual experience that changes continuously.
