This sketch renders a dramatic arid savanna landscape under a pale beige sky, featuring deep terracotta earth with visible cracks and fissures. The composition includes a distant, rugged rock formation anchoring the horizon, lit by a low sun that casts exaggerated shadows across the textured ground. The scene is populated with sparse scrub and acacia-like vegetation, while subtle dust devils drift slowly across the landscape, occasionally forming mesmerizing swirling patterns. The only motion comes from these floating dust devils, creating a quiet, rhythmic life within the still terrain. The technique uses layered shapes with varying stroke weights and fills to create depth and texture, with procedural elements for the cracks and moving particles.
