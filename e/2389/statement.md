This sketch creates a vast, geometric lattice inspired by early 20th-century suspension bridge engineering, rendered in the Bauhaus aesthetic. The structure consists of interwoven vertical supports, horizontal tension cables, and diagonal braces that create a sense of complex structural weight and fragile tensile movement. Through subtle oscillating motion and varying stroke weights, the piece conveys constant stress and dynamic tension while maintaining an architectural precision that evokes both the monumental scale and delicate intricacy of steel bridge frameworks. The aged metal color palette and the perpetual motion suggest a structure under continuous strain, functioning as a self-contained system of stressed geometry.
