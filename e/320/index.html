Brief

The canvas displays a continuous, uniform grid of small, interconnected squares set against a near-black ground. Each square exhibits a subtle, slow pulsation, giving the impression of quiet, internal breathing across the entire grid. When the viewer interacts by clicking anywhere on the surface, a visible wave propagates outward from the point of impact. This wave does not simply pass through; it momentarily impacts the breathing rhythm of the squares it hits, causing a temporary, localized, and unpredictable alteration in their pulsation pattern before the grid returns to its steady, subtle rhythm.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a subtle, breathing grid of squares that pulse gently in unison. Each square has its own rhythm, creating a sense of quiet life across the canvas. When clicked, a ripple wave emanates from the point of impact, temporarily disrupting the local pulsation patterns before the grid settles back into its tranquil state. The visual effect uses minimal shapes and smooth animations to convey both individuality and collective harmony, with interactions that are immediately perceivable yet gentle in their disruption.
