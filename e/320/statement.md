This sketch creates a subtle, breathing grid of squares that pulse gently in unison. Each square has its own rhythm, creating a sense of quiet life across the canvas. When clicked, a ripple wave emanates from the point of impact, temporarily disrupting the local pulsation patterns before the grid settles back into its tranquil state. The visual effect uses minimal shapes and smooth animations to convey both individuality and collective harmony, with interactions that are immediately perceivable yet gentle in their disruption.
