This sketch renders an ornate, jewel-toned textile pattern reminiscent of a Persian rug, with a symmetrical lattice grid of stylized floral motifs in deep sapphire, emerald, ruby, violet, plum, and ocean blue. The composition is framed by a prominent border of interlocking crescent shapes that define the entire work. The design maintains a fixed, stationary composition with no motion or animation, presenting a still, highly detailed tapestry that captures the richness and complexity of traditional Persian rug weaving while adhering to strict performance constraints for frame budget compliance.
