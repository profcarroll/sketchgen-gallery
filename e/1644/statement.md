This sketch creates a cozy library scene with vertical shelves filled with luminous books. The shelves gently flicker with a horizontal ripple animation that shifts across them, creating an idle glow effect as if the room is softly breathing. Books can be clicked to toggle their borrowed state – they lift slightly and change color, while a visual indicator highlights those marked as taken. The entire scene is rendered with soft glows and subtle lighting effects that give it a dreamy, ethereal quality without being overwhelming. The animation runs smoothly within the frame budget using batching techniques and efficient drawing methods.
