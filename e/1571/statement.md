This sketch depicts a whimsical maritime scene with stylized paper-cut ships sailing across a water body. The ships are rendered with weathered wood textures and leave behind delicate dissipating trails. The environment features gentle water ripples that continuously shift and distort reflections, creating a natural flow. The entire scene possesses a ceaseless movement where ships drift slowly across the canvas while water ripples animate in the background, giving the impression of an ever-changing seascape.
