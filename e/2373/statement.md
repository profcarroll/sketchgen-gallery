This sketch simulates the deteriorating surface of aged newsprint, where thousands of textual fragments appear as marginalia and crossed-out words. The work depicts a continuous process of decay through wet ink bleeding and spreading across the paper. Text fragments slowly move and fade, while organic ink blobs bloom and smear unpredictably across the canvas. The sepia-toned palette evokes the aged appearance of newspaper, with faded black text on cream-colored background. The constant motion and spontaneous dissolution of elements create an impression of perpetual instability and textual failure, capturing the essence of paper that has been subjected to time, moisture, and physical stress.
