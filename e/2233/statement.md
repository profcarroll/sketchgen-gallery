This sketch presents a geological visualization of a submerged leviathan's mineralized skeleton undergoing slow decay. Through the use of 3D geometry and dynamic lighting, it depicts the gradual collapse of massive ribcage structures into fine particulate matter. The interplay of deep turquoise and sepia tones, punctuated by oxidizing reds and ochres, captures the essence of calcium carbonate erosion over geological time. The viewer witnesses the slow dissolution of these monumental forms, where grand curves slowly flake away into sediment, creating an atmosphere of eternal transformation. The simulation uses a particle-based system to model structural breakdown, with fragments splitting and drifting according to physics-based motion, embodying both the monumentality and fragility of ancient mineralized remains.
