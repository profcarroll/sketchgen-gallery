This sketch creates an immersive visualization of deep-sea energy fields through a dynamic web of crystalline structures that shift and reform over time. The scene features a muted palette of oxidized tones—chalky grays and corrugated browns—interrupted by vibrant bioluminescent pulses that emanate from the geometric formations. Structures move slowly through space in cyclical patterns, mimicking geological processes, while their connections form an ever-shifting network of energy pathways. The viewer's interaction with the canvas introduces localized disturbances that amplify these energy networks, creating a sense of adjusting a hidden signal frequency. The visual effect combines the elegance of crystalline geometry with the organic flow of tidal currents and digital energy fields, resulting in a meditation on time, transformation, and hidden forces beneath the surface.
