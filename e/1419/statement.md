This sketch creates a dreamlike seascape with whimsical paper-cut ships sailing autonomously across a body of water. Each vessel is rendered with detailed wood grain textures and subtle patinas, moving with gentle randomness that mimics real ocean navigation. As ships traverse the canvas, they leave behind ethereal wake trails that gradually fade, creating a shifting, dreamlike quality to the composition. The constant motion ensures the canvas changes visibly without any user input, fulfilling the requirement for autonomous visual progression.
