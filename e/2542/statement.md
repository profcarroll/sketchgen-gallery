This sketch depicts a subterranean network of glowing, pulsating clusters that animate within the tight fissures of ceramic grout. The forms pulse rhythmically with deep blue luminescence, generating soft, shifting smoke-like trails that drift slowly and gather. The energy flows through an intricate web of connections between nearby forms, mimicking the structure of an underground infrastructure. The movement is entirely self-generated, creating a sense of ethereal life within the rigid architecture of the tile floor. The visual effect emerges from spatially hashed particle interactions, with each form maintaining its own organic motion while connecting to others in a dynamic network.
