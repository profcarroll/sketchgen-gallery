This sketch creates an immersive underwater sunset scene with fluid, organic shapes that drift and transform across the canvas. The composition features a gradient background shifting from deep tropical blues to faded golds, evoking calm water at dusk. Hundreds of gently moving spheres—representing both distant ocean swells and stylized lounge lamps—flow across the screen with subtle rotations and movements. When clicked, the entire scene shifts into a more intense color palette and briefly coalesces into an elegant geometric pattern before dissolving back into the ambient flow. The technique uses WEBGL rendering for efficient 3D particle effects, with shapes that continuously transform their positions and rotations to create an organic, breathing quality. The viewer experiences a meditative blend of natural motion and subtle geometric precision, capturing both the tranquility of water and the elegance of design.
