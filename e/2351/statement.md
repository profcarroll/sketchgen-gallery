This sketch portrays a colossal celestial loom constructed from polished brass gears, suspended in an infinite expanse of deep space. The scene captures the slow, rhythmic motion of massive mechanical components that interlock and rotate, casting fractured beams of light across the vast mechanism. The viewer observes the endless weaving process as gears turn with ancient precision, their surfaces reflecting cosmic patterns and light. The composition conveys both the immense scale of the machinery and the quiet, hypnotic rhythm of its operation, creating a sense of awe at the grandeur of this astronomical structure.
