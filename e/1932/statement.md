This sketch creates a dynamic, organic surface that mimics newsprint with constantly evolving ink stains. The canvas features a textured background that simulates the grainy quality of paper, upon which random blotches of highly saturated color bloom and dissolve in unpredictable patterns. These stains are not static but continuously grow, shift, and fade away, creating an ever-changing landscape of wet ink. Viewers can interact by dragging their cursor across the surface, which instantly generates new concentrated stains that immediately begin blooming and spreading. The effect is achieved through a combination of particle-based growth mechanics, noise-driven motion, and careful control over opacity and color saturation to create the illusion of wet, dissolving ink on paper.
