This sketch visualizes centripetal force through a system of glowing particles that orbit a central point. The particles move in smooth, closed loops, demonstrating how force constantly pulls them toward the center while their momentum keeps them in motion. When the viewer drags their mouse across the canvas, they alter the perceived strength of this force — particles spiral outward or tighten their orbits accordingly. The system is stable by default but responsive to interaction, creating a tangible representation of physics principles through visual movement.
