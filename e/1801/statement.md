This sketch creates a mesmerizing stained-glass mosaic that pulses with erratic, explosive light bursts. The geometric panels form a rigid architectural structure, but the vibrant colors and dynamic lighting create an ethereal glow. Every few frames, sharp, colorful fragments burst from random points across the canvas and scatter outward in chaotic trajectories, creating the illusion of light splintering through the glass. These fragments fade quickly, leaving trails that shimmer across the surface, giving the entire structure a scintillating, glittering energy. The interplay between the structured panels and the unpredictable light effects creates a sense of dynamic tension within the otherwise rigid geometric composition.
