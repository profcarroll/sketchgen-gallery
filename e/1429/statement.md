This sketch presents an architectural space dominated by monolithic, curved steel planes that create a sense of weight and permanence. The composition features a series of arched steel sheets arranged in a circular pattern, each one textured with a mottled rust finish to emphasize their material presence. A dramatic perpendicular shift interrupts the continuous arc at the center, creating a jarring visual break that emphasizes the fixed geometry and imposing structure of the space. The scene is rendered in a static, immersive environment where viewers can observe the interplay of light and shadow on the steel surfaces, with the entire composition rotating slowly to reveal different aspects of its curved forms. The work captures the feeling of standing within a vast, echoing architectural hall defined solely by its own imposing structure.
