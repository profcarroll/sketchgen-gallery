This sketch depicts a close-up view of an intricate arcade machine's internal circuitry, where cosmic energy flows through glowing copper paths. The scene features luminous geometric forms—tori and spheres—that orbit in synchronized patterns, their emerald and cyan hues pulsing with rhythmic intensity. Ethereal light branches spread from these forms, tracing complex networks that connect and evolve across the dark void of the machine's core. The visualization captures the essence of energy flowing through a crystalline structure, rendered in a way that emphasizes both the precision of the circuitry and the dynamic, living quality of the light itself.
