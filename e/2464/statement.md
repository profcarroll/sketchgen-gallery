This sketch creates an atmospheric scene of rain falling on a windowpane, with puddles that reflect inverted cityscapes. The raindrops fall at varying speeds and sizes, creating a natural rhythm. When drops hit the puddles, they trigger bright flashes that illuminate geometric patterns within the reflections. The entire composition is bathed in a soft ambient glow, evoking the fragile, crystalline quality of water droplets on glass. The visual effect suggests hidden worlds within the water, where light bursts reveal complex, glowing structures before fading back to subtle patterns.
