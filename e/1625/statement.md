This sketch creates a dynamic kite visualization that responds to mouse movement. The kite is suspended from a series of strings that stretch and adjust their angles as the cursor moves across the canvas. As the viewer drags their mouse, the kite smoothly follows the cursor's path while maintaining its stylized shape and color. The strings create a visible connection between the kite and the cursor, forming a continuous visual trail that demonstrates the relationship between the two points. The design uses simple geometric shapes and smooth physics-based movement to create an engaging, interactive experience where the viewer's input directly controls the kite's motion and string tension.
