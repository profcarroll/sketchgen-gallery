Brief

The canvas displays impossible architectures rendered in soft, ethereal gradients of dusty lavender, muted teal, and fog-gray. Structures drift and warp slowly, giving the feeling of being suspended in time. As the viewer moves the cursor or clicks the canvas, the geometries respond by shifting their perceived depth, allowing the viewer to subtly navigate through the fragile, unreal spaces. The shifting elements maintain a perpetual sense of unsettling, gentle flux.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive, ethereal environment where impossible architectural forms float and shift in a dreamlike space. Using WebGL rendering, it generates soft, flowing geometries with gradients of lavender, teal, and gray that subtly morph over time. The viewer's interaction—through cursor movement and clicks—introduces gentle perturbations that alter the perceived depth and orientation of these structures, inviting exploration of an unstable, surreal realm.
