This sketch creates a dynamic weaving pattern that simulates the motion of threads on a loom. The canvas displays a grid structure formed by fixed warp threads (vertical) and weft threads (horizontal), which provide the framework for the weaving. Over this static structure, many colorful threads move in unpredictable paths, weaving through the grid. These threads have lifespans, appearing and disappearing as they travel across the canvas, creating an ever-shifting tapestry of motion and color. The visual effect is a continuous, fluid interweaving that never settles into a fixed pattern, resembling a loom in constant motion where new patterns are always forming and dissolving.
