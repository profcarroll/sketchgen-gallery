This sketch depicts a cyclical cityscape where architecture transforms through time and light. Structures emerge from darkness as angular towers and buildings, their surfaces wet and reflective under shifting atmospheric conditions. Bioluminescent plant life pulses with deep blues and purples, casting ripples across the liquid environment that spawn geometric fractal patterns. These visual elements create a continuous loop of transformation: water ripples form, fractal patterns shimmer, and temporary crystalline trails accumulate on the buildings. The scene operates through pure visual motion without external input, creating an immersive, self-sustaining ecosystem where architecture is both shaped by and shapes its environment. The viewer witnesses geological change through light, liquid, and crystal formation all within a single continuous cycle.
