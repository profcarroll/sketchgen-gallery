This sketch presents a monumental, curved pathway carved from massive, deeply rusted steel. Using the WEBGL renderer, it creates an immersive structure that dominates the viewer's space through flowing geometry and textured surfaces. The piece simulates the effects of oxidation and wear through carefully modulated color variations and noise-based surface irregularities. Mineral runoff channels etch the steel's surface with sharp, perpendicular interruptions that maximize visual contrast and spatial tension. The viewer is positioned to examine the overwhelming scale of this form, with detailed textures and abrupt shifts in direction creating a sense of both permanence and decay. The work remains deliberately still, focusing on the static grandeur of the structure rather than motion or change.
