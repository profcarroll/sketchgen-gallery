Brief

The canvas presents a deep, tranquil body of water whose surface is perpetually active with fluid ripples. These ripples constantly spread and evolve across the visual field, maintaining a deep, calm appearance. The water's movement is dynamically influenced by ambient sound. As the sound level rises, the surface ripples respond significantly: they expand vertically to cover a much wider portion of the screen, and at peak sound intensity, the ripples become brightly illuminated, fragmented, and highly erratic.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a serene yet dynamically responsive underwater scene where fluid ripples continuously emanate and evolve across the canvas. The water's surface appears calm and deep, but its movement reacts to ambient sound levels — from gentle undulations in silence to dramatic, fragmented bursts of light during high-volume moments. The ripples grow and shrink over time, with their visual properties (brightness, hue, size) shifting based on audio input, creating an immersive and emotionally responsive aquatic environment that changes visibly with sound without requiring user interaction beyond the initial click to activate audio.
