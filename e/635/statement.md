This sketch creates an immersive cosmic web where vibrant geometric particles drift and pulsate in endless motion. Each particle carries its own color and rhythm, glowing with a soft pulse that varies over time. As they move through space, particles form fleeting connections with nearby neighbors—these pathways shimmer with intense energy, creating a living network of luminous threads. The entire system operates without interaction, driven solely by physics and time, giving the impression of an ever-flowing cosmic current that fills the canvas with mesmerizing depth and movement.
