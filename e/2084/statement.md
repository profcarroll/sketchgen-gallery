This sketch creates a dynamic visual metaphor for information flow and organization. Characters swirl in a perpetual vortex of semi-transparent text, representing the constant stream of data that surrounds us. When clicked, this fluid field momentarily organizes itself into legible lines of text, creating a fleeting moment of clarity reminiscent of newsprint. The effect is both a visual representation of how we make sense of information and a poetic meditation on the ephemeral nature of understanding. The characters dissolve back into their flowing state after the structured moment, suggesting that order is temporary while chaos persists.
