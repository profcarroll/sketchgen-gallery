This sketch renders a dynamic, crystalline lattice structure composed of interlocking hexagonal tessellations that pulse with electrical energy. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of geometric animation and procedural stress fracture generation. Each hexagon subtly changes size and color over time, while glowing connections between adjacent structures create a sense of tension and perpetual motion. Electrical energy pulses radiate from random points across the lattice as stress fractures form and dissipate, creating an impression of immense pressure building toward a catastrophic collapse. The entire scene slowly rotates to emphasize its mechanical nature and continuous strain.
