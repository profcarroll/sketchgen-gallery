This sketch creates an immersive visualization of a dark stone surface hosting a luminous network of bismuth crystals. The scene features a procedurally generated stone texture with organic noise patterns that ground the composition. Hundreds of geometric crystal structures slowly form and pulse with iridescent rainbow hues, creating a mesmerizing effect that mimics the kinetic chain reaction of falling dominoes. The crystals are connected by subtle lines that propagate through the branching network, suggesting growth and transformation over time. The entire scene exists in a three-dimensional space with dynamic lighting and movement, where each crystal's formation and glow are individually animated to create an organic, living quality to the composition.
