This sketch creates a visual metaphor for the cyclical nature of climate patterns, depicting epochs of drought and flood through concentric rings that expand from the center. Each ring represents a distinct period, with abrupt color transitions between drought (warm yellows and oranges) and flood (cool blues and greens) states. The rings grow outward with irregular jumps and sudden shifts in their visual properties, creating an asymmetrical temporal progression that emphasizes the chaotic nature of environmental change. The viewer observes a dynamic field of expanding bands where each cycle brings unexpected color and density changes, reflecting how natural systems can shift abruptly rather than gradually.
