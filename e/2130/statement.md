This sketch presents a monumental bronze heart structure rendered in a volumetric cross-section, evoking the slow, continuous processes of geological formation. The composition builds upon the concept of deep time through layered material accumulation, but transforms it into an organic, pulsing biological form. The heart's walls are composed of horizontal strata that subtly shift and pulse with time, suggesting the rhythmic beating of a city-sized organ. Through constant, imperceptible motion, the layers build up and redefine themselves, creating a sense of perpetual evolution and growth within a single moment. The muted bronze tones and volumetric rendering create an atmosphere of ancient durability and organic vitality simultaneously.
