This sketch creates a meditative, organic color transformation that evokes the slow, viscous flow of melted beeswax. Through a combination of slowly moving gradient pools and independently animated particles, it simulates the natural diffusion of pigments. The colors blend softly without harsh edges, creating an immersive visual experience where hues gradually merge and evolve across the canvas. The technique uses radial gradients for pool effects and noise-based particle movement to achieve fluid, unpredictable yet controlled motion that mimics natural wax flow patterns.
