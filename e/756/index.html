Brief

The canvas displays an intricate, dense, and complex network composed of numerous intersecting wires and cables. Within this structure, numerous luminous data streams pulse with rhythmic energy, crisscrossing the mesh. The movement is continuous and dynamic: when these bright data streams pass through any cable intersection or junction, they instantly generate a sudden, bright flare of energy, temporarily illuminating the surrounding cables before the gentle, rhythmic pulsing continues through the entire system.
