This sketch presents a steampunk kinetic sculpture rendered in real-time 3D, where brass splines form an intricate orbiting network around a central molten bronze core. The piece captures the essence of a living mechanism through perpetual motion — each brass particle orbits with subtle vertical oscillation while maintaining connections that trace elegant spline paths between them. The sculpture's metallic surfaces are rendered with specular lighting to simulate highly reflective brass, catching and warping the glow from the luminous central core. The entire structure rotates slowly, creating a mesmerizing interplay of light and shadow across its complex geometry, evoking the dynamic beauty of a mechanical organism in constant motion.
