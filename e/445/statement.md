This sketch renders a sweeping arid landscape with deep terracotta earth and cracked textures, under a stark beige sky. The scene features sparse acacia trees and subtle dust devils that drift across the terrain. Through careful layering of procedural elements—cracked terrain, rock formations, tree placements, and animated dust particles—the work captures the feeling of vast dry silence punctuated by gentle, ceaseless motion. The composition emphasizes minimalism and texture over complexity, using p5.js's drawing capabilities to create an immersive yet restrained desert environment that evolves subtly over time without user interaction.
