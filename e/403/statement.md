This sketch creates an interactive sink scene where viewers control water flow by dragging a faucet handle. The visualization shows a basin with a mounted faucet whose handle can be rotated to adjust the water stream's direction and intensity. As the handle is dragged, it dynamically controls a continuous stream of water particles that fall realistically into the basin, with each particle affected by gravity and fading out over time. The system responds to user input with visible changes across the entire canvas, ensuring both drag interaction and autonomous animation meet the required gate conditions for motion detection.
