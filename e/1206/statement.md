This interactive sketch presents a horizontal arrangement of five musical pads, each glowing with distinct colors and producing a corresponding tone when clicked. The pads appear to lift slightly from the background through subtle drop shadows that enhance their three-dimensional presence. When activated by a click, each pad emits a light glow and plays a musical note — creating an engaging visual and auditory experience that transforms the canvas into an interactive musical instrument. The implementation uses p5.js for both graphics rendering and audio generation, with careful attention to the frame budget and user gesture requirements for browser audio context activation.
