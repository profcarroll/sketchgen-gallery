This sketch creates a continuously evolving tapestry of vibrant threads that weave through the canvas according to strict geometric rules. The threads move independently with varying directions and speeds, leaving trails behind them. Where threads come close together, they form connections that intersect at points where exactly three distinct colors meet—a rule that governs the entire composition. These intersections create a complex, living mosaic of crossing lines that never settles into repetition, producing an ever-shifting visual pattern reminiscent of a dynamic textile being woven itself. The interplay between movement, color, and geometric constraint results in a mesmerizing display of organic complexity.
