This sketch creates an intricate glowing vector grid that pulses with a slow, rhythmic heartbeat. The grid consists of interconnected neon lines that subtly expand and contract over time, forming an architectural symphony of light. The pulsing effect is achieved through sine wave modulation that varies the opacity and intensity of connections between grid points. Staggered bursts of light create temporary fractal patterns that carve negative spaces within the structure, producing a mesmerizing interplay between order and chaos. The visual field remains self-contained and mesmerizing without requiring complex particle systems or excessive computational overhead.
