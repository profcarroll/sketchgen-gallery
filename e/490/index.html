Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch renders a dynamic, three-dimensional model of the DNA double helix using p5.js's WEBGL renderer. The structure continuously writhes and twists in space, with each nucleotide base (A, T, C, G) represented as a colored sphere labeled accordingly. Complementary base pairs—adenine with thymine, cytosine with guanine—temporarily form bonds that appear and disappear as the helix moves through its animation cycle. The visualization captures the molecular dance of DNA replication, showing how sections of the helix can unzip and reassemble under dynamic motion, giving a sense of biological activity within an abstracted geometric space.
