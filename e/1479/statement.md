This sketch depicts an abstract, three-dimensional origami crane in perpetual motion. The crane is composed of multiple interlocking planes that continuously fold and unfold in a mesmerizing cycle. Using the WEBGL renderer, I created a series of rotating geometric planes that transform their positions and orientations over time, simulating the natural flow of paper folding and unfolding. The animation is self-contained and driven entirely by mathematical functions, creating an endless metamorphosis of form. The planes shift through various configurations, transitioning from a structured crane shape back into its constituent folded layers, generating visual complexity through pure motion rather than static geometry.
