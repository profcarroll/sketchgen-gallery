Brief

The canvas simulates a retro, text-based terminal console, providing a visual aesthetic of a secret computer system. The background is deep black, filled with glowing green or white monospace text that mimics the appearance of an old command-line interface. A narrative detective mystery is revealed through structured blocks of text, suggesting a continuous program output. The viewer interacts by clicking anywhere on the canvas. Each click executes a simulated command, advancing the storyline, executing a clue investigation, or revealing a crucial piece of evidence needed to solve the central case.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a retro terminal interface that simulates a detective investigation system. Viewers interact by clicking to advance through a narrative about a mysterious disappearance, with text appearing as if from an old command-line interface. The visual aesthetic uses deep black backgrounds and glowing green monospace text reminiscent of classic computer terminals. Each click executes a simulated command, revealing clues and advancing the story through structured blocks of text that mimic program output. The experience is deliberately static until interaction occurs, then reveals narrative elements through carefully timed text appearance, creating an immersive detective mystery environment that responds to user input.
