This sketch creates a medical monitor heartbeat visualization with a glowing neon line that scrolls continuously from left to right. The waveform is generated using sine waves to create rhythmic patterns of sharp spikes and shallow dips that mimic real ECG readings. The line pulses with a neon glow effect using HSB color space for dynamic brightness changes, while sharp white spikes represent the peaks of the heartbeat and darker dots show the troughs. The continuous scrolling motion gives the impression of an endless medical monitor reading, with the visual rhythm matching the steady pulse of a heartbeat.
