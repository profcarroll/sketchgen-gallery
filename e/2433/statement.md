This sketch presents a vast, decaying cathedral library rendered in three-dimensional space using p5.js's WEBGL mode. The scene features thousands of interlocking cedar shelves arranged in deep perspective, creating an overwhelming sense of architectural scale and depth. Through subtle animations—slow camera movement, gentle shelf oscillations, and drifting dust particles—the viewer experiences a continuous process of decay and accumulation. The warm, lacquered wood tones shift slightly over time, while particles settle gradually, accumulating on surfaces to suggest the passage of time. The entire composition moves independently, offering an immersive, silent panorama that visualizes the slow collapse of forgotten memory through geometric structure and material detail.
