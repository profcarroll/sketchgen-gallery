This sketch creates a mesmerizing, flowing network of light trails that move through space guided by an invisible force field derived from the luminance of a source photograph. The particles sample color directly from the image but subtly bleed and diffuse into adjacent trails, creating rich, interconnected patterns that shift and ripple across the canvas. The motion is generated autonomously without any user interaction, with particles following a dynamic flow field that responds to the visual structure of the input image. The result is an organic, ever-changing field of color that feels both structured and alive, as if it's being pulled through space by an unseen current.
