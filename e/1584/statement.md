This sketch creates a mesmerizing, evolving tapestry of neon geometry that pulses with life. The canvas is filled with a grid of animated lines that subtly shift and pulse in sync with a hidden rhythm, creating an ever-changing foundation. Intermittently, architectural fragments emerge from this grid — classical and modern structures that appear, stabilize for a moment, then fade back into the background. These fragments are not static but respond to the underlying pulse, their visibility modulated by gentle sine waves. The entire piece embodies a sense of emergence and dissolution, where structure and chaos dance together in an endless cycle of appearance and disappearance, creating a visual meditation on form and transformation.
