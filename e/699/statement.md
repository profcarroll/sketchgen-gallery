This sketch creates two distinct fluid flocks of colored particles that move independently across the canvas while maintaining a natural flocking behavior. Each flock follows separation, alignment, and cohesion rules to create organic group movement patterns. As the flocks approach each other, they actively avoid overlap through repulsive forces, creating a mesmerizing choreography of avoidance. The visual effect emphasizes continuous motion with smooth transitions, where particles flow together in cohesive groups before diverging when proximity is detected. The implementation uses a fixed particle count and optimized spatial checks to maintain performance while achieving the fluid, dynamic behavior described in the brief.
