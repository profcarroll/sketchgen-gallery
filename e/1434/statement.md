This sketch creates a continuously evolving field of interconnected geometric forms that shift and distort over time. Using the WEBGL renderer, it displays a collection of abstract shapes that dynamically warp and tessellate, driven by a controlled sinusoidal frequency. The shapes maintain visual connections through lines that form a complex network, while occasionally separating to create tension before reuniting at specific nodal points. The motion is entirely self-generated without user input, with each frame creating subtle but perceptible changes across the entire canvas. The visual effect combines organic flow with mathematical precision, creating an immersive field of evolving geometric relationships that maintain both coherence and constant transformation.
