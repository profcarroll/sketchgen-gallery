An exercise in quiet, asynchronous generative rhythm. Rather than imposing sweeping geometric movement or interactive complexity, this sketch fixes an array of pale cells within a dark, stable matrix and frees only their luminosity. Each square oscillates along its own periodic breath, slightly detuned in both temporal rate and initial phase from its neighbors. As waves of illumination drift and interfere across the grid, the composition evokes organic phenomena like bioluminescent tide pools or distant urban windows glowing in the night.
