This sketch creates an immersive, fluid environment filled with amorphous color masses that drift and flow gently within a contained space. Using the WEBGL renderer, it employs spherical particles with dynamic movement patterns—each blob has its own velocity and trails that follow its path, creating continuous, mesmerizing motion. The blobs subtly interact with each other through their shared centering force and boundary constraints, forming an organic, weightless ecosystem where color and form blend and shift over time. The visual effect is calming and hypnotic, capturing the essence of fluid dynamics in a suspended, dreamlike medium.
