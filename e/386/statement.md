This sketch depicts an arid, terracotta landscape with a pale beige sky and scattered cracks in the ground. It simulates the natural movement of dust devils that begin as small ephemeral swirls and gradually organize into larger, more coherent patterns. The visualization evolves continuously without user input, using simple particle-like elements to represent the swirling motion of wind and dust. The scene uses a limited palette of earth tones and pale sky colors to create a cohesive, naturalistic atmosphere where the viewer observes a self-evolving desert environment.
