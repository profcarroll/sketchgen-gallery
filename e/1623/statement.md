This sketch creates an ever-changing field of delicate white dandelion seeds floating and spiraling across a sky gradient. The seeds drift naturally with gentle motion and leave subtle trails behind them. When a viewer clicks anywhere on the canvas, the seeds near the click point converge and burst into new life as blooming flowers. These flowers grow from their point of impact, starting with a stem and gradually expanding petals and a central disk until fully formed. The effect transforms the floating field into a living, responsive ecosystem where each interaction triggers a new bloom, creating a continuous cycle of motion and growth across the screen.
