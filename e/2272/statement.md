This sketch portrays a macro-scale view of an underground mineral deposit, rendered in three dimensions to emphasize depth and texture. The dark basaltic tiles form a rugged foundation, while glowing veins of oxidized copper and radiant gold pulse rhythmically through the rock, mimicking the flow of subterranean fluids under tectonic pressure. The viewer can interact with the scene by dragging their mouse, which disturbs the veins' paths, causing them to swirl and redirect. The use of 3D rendering allows for a rich interplay of light and shadow across the mineral surfaces, enhancing the sense of depth and geological complexity.
