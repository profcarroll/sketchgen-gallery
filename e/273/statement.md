This sketch creates an abstract electric circuitry visualization with glowing pathways and flowing data streams. It features a lattice structure where multiple pathways intersect, causing momentary energy bursts at junction points. The system uses particle-based motion to simulate continuous data flow, with particles moving along predefined paths. When particles converge at critical points, their movement slows dramatically and triggers a pulsing energy burst that radiates outward before resuming normal flow. The visual effect combines vibrant colors, fluid motion, and dynamic interactions to represent an active, living network.
