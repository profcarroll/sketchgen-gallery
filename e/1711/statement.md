This sketch simulates a rain-streaked windowpane through which a miniature cityscape is faintly visible. Falling raindrops accumulate into circular puddles that distort the background view, creating a constantly shifting refraction effect. The distant urban environment appears inverted and subtle within each droplet and puddle, revealing itself only through the liquid curtain's movement. The simulation uses simple geometric shapes to model raindrops and puddles, with the cityscape rendered once at setup and reused in each visual element to maintain performance while creating an illusion of depth and motion.
