This sketch simulates the growth and evolution of crystalline biological masses that exhibit both organic fluidity and rigid geometric structures. The viewer observes a continuously evolving landscape of interconnected forms that morph and shift over time. Each mass grows from a central point, developing either smooth, flowing folds or sharp, crystalline ridges depending on its internal state. The 3D rendering uses WebGL to create depth and lighting effects that emphasize the complex surface textures of these formations, with fine fractal patterns emerging along their edges and folds. The structures move slowly across the canvas while continuously growing and changing shape, creating an ever-shifting composition that resembles both cellular development and mineral crystallization.
