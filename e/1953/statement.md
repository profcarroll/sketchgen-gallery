This sketch renders a medieval tapestry scene with a dynamic, glowing arterial network beneath a pale surface. The viewer observes a richly textured background that evokes the feel of ancient textile, while luminous veins pulse and shed material in real-time. The artery structures grow and decay organically, with vibrant tendrils peeling away to become floating particles that drift through space. The animation captures both the structural integrity and the process of deterioration of this imagined network, creating a continuous visual narrative of life and decay within a static tapestry-like surface.
