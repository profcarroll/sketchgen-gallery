Brief

The viewer sees a captivating and complex steampunk sculpture that constantly rotates. The focal point is a dynamic assembly of orbiting splines, appearing to be constructed from aged tarnished copper and deep emerald glass. These splines circle a central core, which is characterized by a glowing pool of molten bronze. The most striking element is the glass-like tracery of the splines, which acts as a prism, catching and refracting vibrant, simulated rainbow light throughout the continuously moving, mechanical display.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a steampunk-inspired mechanical sculpture with a central glowing core and orbiting splines. The composition features a molten bronze pool at the center surrounded by eight rotating splines made of refractive glass-like tracery. Each spline orbits independently while its own internal structure continuously shifts, creating a prismatic effect that refracts vibrant rainbow hues across the rotating assembly. The entire scene rotates slowly to emphasize the continuous mechanical motion, with each element maintaining its own visual identity while contributing to an overall sense of complex, evolving machinery.
