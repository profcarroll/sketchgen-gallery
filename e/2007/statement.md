This sketch models the dynamic geological processes of deep-sea hydrothermal vents through an evolving particle system. The visualization depicts mineral formations as particles that undergo continuous erosion and deposition—particles drift chaotically due to noise-based forces, occasionally aggregating to form crystalline structures. The color palette shifts gradually over time, simulating the chemical changes during mineral precipitation. Connections between nearby particles form a lattice-like structure that constantly evolves as particles move and age, creating an ever-changing pattern reminiscent of natural crystal growth and fracture processes in oceanic environments.
