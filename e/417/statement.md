This sketch portrays a tranquil coastal scene through a close-up view of a sand mound scattered with delicate seashells. The composition uses layered visual elements to evoke the gentle motion of ocean waves and sunlight playing on a beach. Sand particles form the base, subtly shifting in place to suggest subtle movement. Translucent shells are distributed across the scene with slight rotation animations and shimmer effects that mimic light refraction. Subtle water ripples emerge from the bottom of the canvas, rising and fading to simulate oceanic motion. The entire composition moves slowly and naturally without user interaction, fulfilling the automatic motion requirement through animated elements like rotating shells, rippling water, and shifting sand particles.
