This sketch creates a dynamic, immersive field of interconnected particles that continuously shift and evolve in three-dimensional space. Using the WEBGL renderer, it visualizes a complex network where thousands of points move according to fluid, organic forces while maintaining visible connections between nearby elements. The system employs spatial partitioning to efficiently manage particle interactions, ensuring smooth animation within strict performance constraints. As particles move through the virtual space, they occasionally stretch and reconnect along luminous pathways, creating a sense of tension and release that mimics natural flow patterns. The color palette shifts subtly over time, enhancing the feeling of continuous motion and transformation.
