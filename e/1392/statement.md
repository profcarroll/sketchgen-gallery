This sketch creates a stylized interior view of stacked server racks filled with glowing data streams. The scene features geometric metal server racks arranged in a row, each with detailed panels and chips that represent computational components. Through this structured environment flow abstract ribbons of light that pulse and weave between the racks, representing data tensors moving through the system. The cool blues and greens of the lighting suggest high-tech computing activity, while the organized paths of the ribbons imply the logical flow of information through a massive computational network. The viewer observes an immersive, technical landscape where organized chaos reigns in the form of constant data movement.
