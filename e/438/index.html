Brief

The canvas fills with several distinct particles that orbit around a central, invisible point. Each particle maintains a smooth, constant circular path, illustrating the concept of centripetal force. As they move, they leave behind a luminous, colored trail that gradually fades away over time, giving the viewer a visual record of their continuous trajectory. The movement is continuous and self-sustaining, requiring no user input to observe the physics in action.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch visualizes the concept of centripetal force through a system of orbiting particles. Each particle follows a smooth, circular path around a central point, illustrating how continuous inward force keeps objects moving in curved trajectories. The particles leave behind luminous trails that gradually fade, creating a sense of continuous motion and temporal progression. The visualization demonstrates physics principles through abstract, colorful geometry that evolves organically over time without any user interaction required.
