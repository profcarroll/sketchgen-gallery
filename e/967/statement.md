This sketch creates a visual representation of sound as a field of glowing nodes that respond to harmonic structures. When activated by a click, nodes near the impact point illuminate and expand, sending out pulsing waveforms that ripple outward. The system translates audio energy into visible light patterns through a combination of particle physics and harmonic frequency mapping. Each node represents a frequency component that blooms with color when triggered, creating an immersive visualization where the complexity of the waveform pattern reflects the harmonic structure of the chord played. The visual effect is deliberately still until activation, then transforms into a dynamic field of interconnected luminous structures that fade over time.
