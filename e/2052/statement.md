This sketch visualizes the slow, inevitable process of geological sedimentation across a mountain range. Using layered shapes and thousands of individual particles, it depicts how earth tones gradually accumulate and settle over time. The organic motion simulates gravity's persistent force on material, showing how landscapes evolve through countless small movements. The color palette shifts from deep earth tones in the base layers to lighter, more varied hues in the sediment, emphasizing the natural progression of deposition. The constant, subtle animation creates an immersive experience of geological time, where viewers witness the patient work of nature's forces shaping the terrain.
