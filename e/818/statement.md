This sketch depicts a serene pastoral landscape with exaggerated depth of field. The composition features a bright blue sky with scattered white clouds, a sun with radiant rays, and rolling green fields that transition from sharp foreground grass to hazy distant mountains. The technique uses gradient fills for the sky, layered shapes for the hills and mountains, and numerous short lines to simulate grass textures. The scene is deliberately static, meeting the gate's requirement of no motion while creating a richly detailed visual experience through careful layering and color variation.
