This sketch creates an ethereal, dynamic lattice of glowing lines that pulses with subtle color shifts and spontaneous energy bursts. The system uses a combination of particle movement and line segments to form a constantly shifting network against a dark background. Lines breathe with soft pulsing effects while occasionally breaking apart in energetic bursts that fade away, only to reform as the lattice reorganizes itself. The visual effect is achieved through careful management of color, alpha blending, and controlled randomness to create an organic, unstable structure that feels alive and constantly in motion without any external input.
