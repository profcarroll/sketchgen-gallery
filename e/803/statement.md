This sketch creates a dynamic, energetic kaleidoscope by combining smooth, flowing globular splines with sharp crystalline shards. The splines continuously evolve through subtle movements while the shards move gently across the canvas. Where these elements intersect, tension lines form—connecting points between splines that come within proximity of each other. These connections visually represent "tension" as they pull and radiate outward from geometric intersections, creating a constantly shifting pattern of opposing forces that fill the entire canvas with movement and energy. The visual result is a sense of controlled chaos where organic flow meets precise geometry, all without any user input required to initiate the animation.
