This sketch creates an abstract textile tapestry where thousands of flowing fibers move organically across the canvas, interwoven with rigid geometric knots. The fibers continuously animate with noise-based motion patterns, creating a sense of depth and texture reminiscent of woven fabric. When the viewer drags their cursor, nearby fibers react sharply—pulling and warping in response to the movement, causing color saturation and brightness to swell outward from the cursor's path. The piece also responds to audio input through subtle visual pulses that amplify with sound levels, adding another layer of interactivity to the flowing, organic composition.
