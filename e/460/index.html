Brief

The canvas presents an abstract, high-altitude panorama of a vast, nocturnal metropolis. The dominant forms are luminous geometric patterns composed of intersecting lines and illuminated nodes. These patterns are not fixed; they perpetually coalesce, tracing and highlighting the unseen underlying architectural skeleton of the city. The color palette is dominated by deep, dark backgrounds contrasted sharply by bright, vibrant streaks of pulsating light. The entire composition moves autonomously, giving the impression of a pulsing, electrical heartbeat beneath the urban sprawl.
