This sketch simulates the hypnotic, slow-motion flow of metallic dye in a 1960s pen plotter drawing. Using a combination of sine waves and Perlin noise, it creates dense, overlapping loops that meander across the canvas with the viscous quality of thick pigment. The warm metallic hues and fine ink lines evoke the era's mechanical precision while capturing the fluid motion of materials that move slowly but persistently. Each line is built from hundreds of points that accumulate over time, creating a rich tapestry of movement that feels both organic and controlled.
