This sketch presents a still, contemplative visualization of geological time frozen in mineral form. It depicts a central fossilized bouquet of rare rainforest blooms, crystallized through vast geological processes, rendered in muted Victorian botanical aesthetics. The composition features layered strata with intricate line work that mimics the textures found in historical botanical engravings, emphasizing permanence and the slow accumulation of history. The viewer is invited to observe the delicate preservation of ancient life within a mineral landscape, where each element contributes to a sense of time suspended and eternal.
