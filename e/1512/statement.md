This sketch portrays a distant planet illuminated by a network of glowing city lights that form intricate patterns across its surface. A sweeping gradient sweeps across the planet's surface, representing the transition from night to day, with dynamic lighting that shifts and moves over time. The visual effect is created using a large number of points mapped onto a spherical surface, each point colorized according to its position relative to the moving light source. The scene rotates slowly to provide a sense of depth and planetary motion, while thousands of stars fill the background space.
