This sketch creates an abstract, glowing data visualization composed of interconnected rotating geometric planes and lines. The scene is rendered in 3D space using WebGL, with each plane rotating independently along multiple axes to create a sense of fluid motion and depth. The color palette draws from the early 2010s broadcast graphics era, featuring vibrant hues with transparent strokes that suggest digital data streams. Multiple planes float in space, connected by subtle lines, while floating points add visual density and enhance the three-dimensional effect. The continuous rotation generates an ever-shifting composition that maintains constant motion without any user interaction, fulfilling the requirement for self-generated visual change during the idle watch window.
