This sketch creates a mesmerizing radial pattern of nested, pulsating arcs that expand outward from the center of the canvas. Each arc pulses rhythmically while gradually increasing the gap between successive ripples, creating an illusion of growing energy and motion. The entire field shifts through the visible spectrum of colors in a continuous, smooth transition, enhancing the sense of depth and vitality. The visual effect is both calming and dynamic, with the pulsation and color shift working together to create a hypnotic, self-contained animation that evolves over time without any external input.
