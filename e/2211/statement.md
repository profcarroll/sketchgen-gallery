This sketch renders a three-dimensional molecular helix structure inspired by DNA, composed of colored spheres representing nitrogenous bases (A, T, G, C). The structure rotates slowly in space while maintaining its helical form. Luminous bonds connect complementary base pairs (A-T and G-C) with a rhythmic pulsing effect that simulates the dynamic nature of hydrogen bonding in DNA. The visual metaphor captures both the stability of the molecular backbone and the fleeting, essential interactions that hold the structure together, rendered through systematic geometric patterns and color-coded elements.
