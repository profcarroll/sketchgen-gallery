This sketch creates an interactive painting environment that responds to mouse gestures with layered, textured strokes. As users drag their mouse across the canvas, colorful paint is applied in dynamic, gestural marks that accumulate over time. The system captures the fluid motion of drawing and translates it into a rich visual texture, mimicking the build-up of physical paint. The work demonstrates how digital media can emulate traditional artistic processes through responsive interaction and layered visual feedback, creating an evolving mural-like composition from simple mouse movements.
