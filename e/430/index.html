Brief

The canvas functions as a large, textured wall surface, allowing the viewer to create layered, overlapping art. As the user drags the mouse across the canvas, colorful paint is applied, leaving behind dynamic strokes and abstract drips. Different hues can be selected and painted onto the surface, allowing for the creation of dense, varied graffiti murals. The resulting work accumulates texture and depth, giving the illusion of physical aerosol paint building up over time. The entire process is focused on recording the user's gestural input into lasting visual form.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an interactive painting environment that responds to mouse gestures with layered, textured strokes. As users drag their mouse across the canvas, colorful paint is applied in dynamic, gestural marks that accumulate over time. The system captures the fluid motion of drawing and translates it into a rich visual texture, mimicking the build-up of physical paint. The work demonstrates how digital media can emulate traditional artistic processes through responsive interaction and layered visual feedback, creating an evolving mural-like composition from simple mouse movements.
