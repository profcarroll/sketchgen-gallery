This sketch creates an immersive, slowly evolving composition reminiscent of Mark Rothko's color field paintings. Using multiple overlapping semi-transparent circles with animated hues, it simulates the gradual diffusion and transformation of color that characterizes Rothko's work. The fields shift subtly in saturation and hue over time, creating a sense of depth and movement beneath thick, velvety paint. Through careful control of blending modes and layering techniques, the piece achieves a meditative quality where colors softly bloom and recede, capturing the contemplative atmosphere of Rothko's monumental canvases while maintaining the dynamic, ever-changing nature of digital art.
