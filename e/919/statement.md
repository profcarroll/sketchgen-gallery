This sketch creates a dynamic field of glowing particles that form a cohesive cloud. The particles drift gently with Perlin noise-based motion, maintaining a soft, organic flow. When sound is detected, the entire cloud scatters outward in a dramatic burst, then slowly reforms as the audio fades. The visual effect uses alpha blending for smooth fading and color variations to enhance the ethereal quality of the particle field. The system responds to audio input through p5.sound's amplitude detection, triggering the scattering behavior while maintaining the idle state when no sound is present.
