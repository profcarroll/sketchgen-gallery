This sketch depicts a continuously collapsing ziggurat structure made of dark glass prisms, viewed from eye level. The entire formation appears to be in perpetual decay, with segments sliding and cascading downward in a steady, dramatic motion. Each frame, the structure's geometry shifts subtly through physics-based movement, creating an organic sense of instability and collapse. When clicked, the viewer triggers additional segments to peel off and join the cascade, increasing the intensity of the falling debris. The scene uses WebGL rendering for 3D depth and dynamic lighting effects, with each segment fading as it falls to simulate the passage of time and the progression of decay.
