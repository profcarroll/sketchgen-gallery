This sketch depicts a futuristic scene of a hovering disc-shaped craft illuminating a corn field with pulsing cyan light beams. The work creates an immersive, constantly shifting visual experience through the interplay of geometric forms and animated lighting effects. The corn stalks form a grounded landscape while the craft hovers above, casting dynamic beams that pulse and shift across the ground. The technique uses WebGL rendering for efficient 3D drawing, with carefully managed animation timing to ensure the scene remains perpetually active without requiring viewer interaction. The visual rhythm emerges from mathematical wave functions that control the beam intensity and pattern movement, creating an otherworldly atmosphere that feels both organic and technologically advanced.
