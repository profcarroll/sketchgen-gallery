This sketch creates a cosmic landscape that simulates the vastness of space through a deep indigo backdrop populated by twinkling stars and subtle Milky Way glows. The composition uses layered rendering techniques to create depth, with stars moving at different speeds and sizes to suggest distance. Sudden color bursts represent supernova flares that punctuate the otherwise serene cosmos. The entire scene evolves autonomously without user input, creating an immersive, ever-changing celestial environment.
