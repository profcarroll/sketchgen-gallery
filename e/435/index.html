Brief

The canvas depicts an isometric, multi-lane race course designed for a marble race. A vibrant countdown timer is prominently displayed, counting down to zero. A marble starts at the beginning of the course, and the goal is located at the finish line. The viewer guides the marble across the track by controlling its movement, aiming to keep the marble moving toward the objective before the timer expires. The environment is three-dimensional, giving the race a deep, modeled appearance.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive isometric marble race course rendered in 3D using p5.js WEBGL mode. The viewer begins at the start line with a vibrant countdown timer, and must guide the marble through a winding track toward the finish line. The environment features a multi-lane racecourse with a dynamic camera that orbits the scene, creating a sense of depth and motion. The marble moves automatically forward once the race starts, while the countdown timer provides urgency. The scene includes lighting effects and a visually distinct finish line, giving the race course a modeled appearance with a clear objective for the viewer to pursue.
