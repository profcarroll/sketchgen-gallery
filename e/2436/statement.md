This sketch presents a static visualization of a crystalline lattice structure composed of interlocking geometric forms. The composition features a dense arrangement of mineral-like crystals that appear to fit together with perfect precision, forming a self-similar pattern across scales. Each crystal is constructed from multiple overlapping geometric primitives—spheres, boxes, and tori—arranged in a way that suggests molecular assembly. The color palette evokes polished minerals under magnification, with hues shifting between deep ambers, rich greens, and warm oranges. Connections between crystals are rendered as faint lines that emphasize the structural coherence of the lattice. The entire scene remains motionless, capturing an idealized moment of crystalline self-assembly.
