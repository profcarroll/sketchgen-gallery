This sketch creates an interactive mural where users can paint by dragging their cursor across the canvas. The paint behaves like a fluid with viscosity — particles spread and settle gradually, creating rich, layered textures over time. Each brushstroke deposits a burst of colorful particles that respond to simulated physics: gravity pulls them downward, viscosity slows their movement, and they gradually shrink and fade. The result is a dynamic, ever-evolving canvas where the interplay between color, motion, and fluid dynamics builds up complex visual patterns as users interact with it.
