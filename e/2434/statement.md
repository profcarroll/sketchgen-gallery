This sketch creates a serene landscape of rhododendron blooms reflected in still water, with the added interactivity of ripples that disturb the perfect reflections. The scene is rendered using WebGL for a three-dimensional effect, where vibrant red and pink blooms appear to float above a deep blue-green pool. The water surface features a subtle pre-generated reflection pattern that captures the essence of the blooms' colors. When clicked, the water responds with gentle ripples that dynamically distort this reflection, creating a mesmerizing interplay between stillness and motion. The design emphasizes the contrast between the static beauty of the blooms and the dynamic, ever-changing nature of the water's surface.
