Brief

A slow, continuous stream of overlapping translucent blue circles rises gracefully from the dark bottom edge of the canvas. These softly flowing, aqueous shapes move upward, creating a sense of perpetual tide. Superimposed over the shifting blue flow are countless small, distinct pointillist stars, adding a glittering contrast to the smooth, deep hue of the rising tide.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an ethereal underwater scene where translucent blue circles rise slowly from the bottom of the canvas, mimicking the flow of water or liquid tide. The circles overlap and blend smoothly, creating a continuous, fluid motion that suggests depth and movement. Scattered throughout this aqueous flow are countless tiny pointillist stars that sparkle against the dark background, adding texture and contrast to the smooth, deep blue tide.
