This sketch depicts a whimsical underwater scene where paper-cut ships glide through a translucent, aged water surface. The ships leave behind dense, crystalline sediment trails that accumulate and form permanent deposits on the medium, contrasting with the ephemeral nature of their wake. The environment shows signs of weathering and growth—patches of moss and barnacles, warping edges, and tearing paper fragments that drift and pile up over time. The gentle motion of ships and particles creates an evolving landscape that captures both the fragility and endurance of materials subjected to the passage of time and water.
