This sketch creates an autonomous, mesmerizing display of symmetrical spirograph patterns using only the core p5.js library. The composition features a series of orbiting celestial bodies that trace intricate, mathematically-generated curves in rich emerald green and sapphire blue hues. The motion is entirely self-contained, driven by mathematical relationships between orbital radii, speeds, and phases, creating an endlessly evolving pattern that resembles mechanical drawing. The work demonstrates the elegance of geometric harmony through pure algorithmic expression, with no user interaction required to maintain its hypnotic rhythm.
