Brief

The canvas displays a stylized, equine figure captured in the powerful act of a full gallop. The horse's body and limbs are rendered using smooth, flowing curves, avoiding sharp angles. The animation features continuous, dynamic movement where the spine acts as a sweeping curve driving the stride. The movement is highly exaggerated, showing the front and hind legs achieving synchronized and pronounced extensions with every step, creating a visibly heightened and fluid sense of momentum as the figure crosses the frame.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch depicts a stylized horse in full gallop, rendered with smooth, flowing curves that emphasize the dynamic motion of the animal. The body follows a sweeping arc, while the legs move in synchronized, exaggerated extension to create a sense of momentum and power. The animation is driven entirely by mathematical functions—specifically sine and cosine waves—that control the timing and positioning of each body part, resulting in an endlessly looping, fluid gallop without any external input. The entire scene is self-contained within a single canvas that continuously evolves over time, fulfilling the requirement for autonomous motion.
