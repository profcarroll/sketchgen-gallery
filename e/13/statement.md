This sketch creates a mesmerizing, ever-shifting grid of interlocking hexagons that pulse and flow across the screen. Each hexagon gently oscillates in position and rotation while cycling through a vibrant 1980s-inspired color palette of magentas, cyans, yellows, greens, and oranges. The animation is generated entirely algorithmically without any external input, relying on time-based trigonometric functions to create continuous motion. The soft transitions and organic flow give the impression of living, breathing geometry that transforms rhythmically over time.
