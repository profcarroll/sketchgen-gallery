Brief

The viewer stands immersed in a towering, speculative architectural landscape, experiencing a controlled dynamism that suggests an endless, vibrant structure. Gigantic geometric planes define the space, bisected by luminous, glowing emerald green arterial seams. These powerful light sources are echoed by subtle, tessellating crystalline reflections across the geometry, giving the illusion that the entire environment refracts the deep green light. The perspectives shift and evolve constantly, creating an overwhelming yet stable sense of boundless, constructed space.
