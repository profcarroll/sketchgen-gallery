This sketch creates a mesmerizing deep-sea ecosystem where bioluminescent jellyfish drift in perfect synchronization through a field of geometrically folded origami structures. The scene features a colony of glowing blue-green jellyfish that pulse rhythmically and move in harmonious orbits, while angular, folded geometric forms undulate gently in the background. The entire composition bathes in an ethereal glow that simulates the natural bioluminescence of deep-sea creatures, creating an immersive, kinetic ballet of light and movement that evolves continuously without any external input.
