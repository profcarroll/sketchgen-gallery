This sketch depicts a stylized cross-section of a tidal zone with multiple horizontal bands representing different water layers. The bands continuously oscillate in a fluid, turbulent motion that simulates the breathing rhythm of liquid, mimicking the natural ebb and flow of tides. Each colored band moves independently with its own phase, creating an organic wave-like effect across the canvas. A prominent darker band marks the high water line, which becomes more agitated as the tide expands and contracts, sharpening the boundaries between color gradients. The simulation uses sine wave functions to generate smooth, natural-looking motion without complex physics or particle systems, keeping performance within the frame budget while maintaining visual interest through dynamic color transitions and subtle surface effects.
