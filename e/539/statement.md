This sketch creates a mesmerizing, complex network of glowing wires that pulse with rhythmic data streams. The composition features a dense tangle of interconnected lines forming an intricate web structure, with luminous points traveling along the paths in waves. These streams periodically brighten as they propagate and split at major junctions, creating angular nodes of light that coalesce and then fade back into the main network. The entire system simulates a dynamic flow of energy across a rigid cable framework, producing an hypnotic visualization of interconnected data pathways. The interplay between continuous movement, periodic splitting, and angular node formation creates a compelling sense of both order and organic complexity.
