This sketch creates a vast, textured arid savanna landscape under a pale beige sky. The ground features rich terracotta coloring with intricate cracks and fissures, simulated through procedural line drawing. Low scrub vegetation adds subtle texture to the expanse, while distant rock formations break the horizon with atmospheric haze. The scene is illuminated by a low-angle light source that casts dramatic shadows within the earth's crevices. Swirling dust devils drift across the baked ground in organized, atmospheric patterns, creating movement and depth without overwhelming the composition. The entire scene evolves naturally over time through the animated dust devils, demonstrating the passage of time and weather patterns in this harsh desert environment.
