This sketch depicts a slow geological transformation of delicate paper-cut ships suspended in a translucent medium. The vessels sail gently across the canvas, their fragile hulls gradually solidifying and encasing themselves in intricate crystalline scaffolds. The process unfolds without user intervention, showing how whimsical forms slowly become mineralized monuments of time's passage. Through subtle decay and growth patterns, viewers observe the patient transformation from ephemeral paper craft to enduring stone-like structures.
