This sketch presents a highly detailed, colossal astronomical clock mechanism rendered in 3D space. The composition focuses on the intricate brass gears, cams, and connecting mechanisms that suggest a hidden divine process. Using WEBGL rendering, it creates a static yet complex scene with careful attention to mechanical detail - from the central gear system to the interlocking cam arrangements. The warm aged brass color palette contrasts beautifully against the dark background, emphasizing the craftsmanship and precision of the simulated mechanism. The viewer is invited to observe this eternal complexity in perfect tranquility, with all elements carefully positioned to create a sense of infinite mechanical harmony within a single frame.
