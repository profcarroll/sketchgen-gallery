This sketch creates a highly symmetrical woven pattern inspired by traditional Persian rug designs. The composition features a central field with jewel-toned colors and stylized floral motifs arranged in radial symmetry, surrounded by an intricate border made of interlocking chevron shapes. The design employs mathematical precision through grid-based lattices and rotational symmetry to achieve the deep visual complexity characteristic of handwoven textiles. The color palette uses saturated hues in a harmonious scheme that evokes the rich dyes found in traditional Persian carpets, while the geometric patterns demonstrate the careful craftsmanship and mathematical elegance inherent in this ancient art form.
