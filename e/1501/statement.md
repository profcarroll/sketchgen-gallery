This sketch simulates the behavior of iron filings responding to a magnetic field. Thousands of metallic-looking particles are scattered across the canvas, each moving according to a combination of noise-based randomness and proximity to the mouse cursor. When the mouse moves, particles are instantly drawn toward it, creating dense, flowing patterns that shift and reorganize in real-time. The effect demonstrates how individual elements can respond collectively to an external force, forming dynamic, sculpted trails and accumulations that mirror the behavior of ferromagnetic materials under magnetic influence.
