This sketch creates an immersive, flowing field of glowing, amorphous planes that drift and shift across the screen in a mesmerizing dance. The planes are rendered using procedural geometry with noise functions to create organic, ever-changing shapes that seem to breathe and pulse. Semi-transparent and softly glowing, they interact to form sharp, vibrant boundaries where they overlap, creating a striking contrast against the dark background. Interspersed among this fluid field are clusters of sharply defined geometric structures—angular cubes that appear to cut through the current of motion. The composition maintains a gentle, hypnotic rhythm through subtle rotations and movements, evoking an ambient electronic soundscape through visual motion alone. The use of WEBGL renderer allows for depth and spatial complexity while maintaining performance within the strict frame budget.
