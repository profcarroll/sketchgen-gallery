This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of expanding, pulsating rings that radiate from the center of the canvas. The rings grow steadily outward while simultaneously throbbing with a rhythmic motion, creating an hypnotic, self-sustaining visual rhythm. The composition appears as a living field of expanding light and color, with each ring contributing to the overall dynamic complexity through subtle variations in speed, hue, and timing.
