This sketch creates a continuously evolving field of geometric forms that pulse and warp in three-dimensional space. Using the WEBGL renderer, it generates a dynamic composition of rotating boxes and spheres that shift position and scale over time. The color palette cycles through hues while maintaining visual coherence, and subtle distortions cause the shapes to separate and reform rhythmically. The system uses efficient rendering techniques to maintain performance within strict frame budget constraints, creating an immersive, abstract environment where complex forms emerge from simple geometric building blocks.
