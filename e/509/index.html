Brief

The canvas displays a continuous field of complex geometric shapes and abstract lines, perpetually evolving within a dynamic, expanding space. These forms exhibit controlled, periodic pulsatility alongside localized warping and tessellating distortions. The color palette is never static, instead undergoing visible chromatic shifts and rippling effects. The overall structure pulses rhythmically, and during peak distortion, the interconnected segments momentarily pull apart, separating into distinct, visible vectors as the geometric field momentarily destabilizes before reforming.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a continuously evolving field of geometric forms that pulse and warp in three-dimensional space. Using the WEBGL renderer, it generates a dynamic composition of rotating boxes and spheres that shift position and scale over time. The color palette cycles through hues while maintaining visual coherence, and subtle distortions cause the shapes to separate and reform rhythmically. The system uses efficient rendering techniques to maintain performance within strict frame budget constraints, creating an immersive, abstract environment where complex forms emerge from simple geometric building blocks.
