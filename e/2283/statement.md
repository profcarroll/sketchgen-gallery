This sketch depicts a mechanical heart composed of interlocking clockwork mechanisms and decaying biological tissue. The composition features rotating gears with detailed teeth and organic blob-like structures that slowly dissolve and reform over time. The viewer observes a perpetual process of decay and regeneration, where metallic brass components gradually melt into viscous red tissue, mimicking the exploratory movements of slime mold. A subtle mouse drag disrupts this rhythm by causing localized sections to accelerate their decay, creating violent ripples across the mechanical organ before it resumes its languid dissolution. The piece explores themes of decay, transformation, and the fusion of inorganic and organic systems through a slow, hypnotic visual process that maintains a sense of both order and chaos.
