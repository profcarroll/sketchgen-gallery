This sketch visualizes abstract data grids representing multi-dimensional tensors flowing through space. The system consists of interconnected nodes that influence the movement of thousands of glowing particles in real-time. Each particle's position shifts based on its distance from these nodes, creating complex, evolving patterns. When clicked, the central data stream recalculates its flow by relocating the influencing nodes and reconfiguring the connections between particles, producing radically different output patterns while maintaining the overall abstract structure of data flow visualization.
