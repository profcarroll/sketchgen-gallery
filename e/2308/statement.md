This visualization captures the slow, organic process of ceramic stress relief through developing hairline fractures. The piece depicts a glazed ceramic surface where microscopic cracks gradually form and propagate across time. Using a combination of procedural noise textures for the base glaze and dynamic crack generation algorithms, it shows how internal tension creates complex fracture patterns over time. The subtle movement and evolving visual structure create an impression of material transformation without requiring any viewer interaction, emphasizing the continuous, almost imperceptible changes occurring within the ceramic's structure.
