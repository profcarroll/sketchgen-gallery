This sketch depicts the cyclical transformation of a solitary tree through the seasons, from vibrant spring growth to summer's lush canopy, through autumn's brilliant color shift, and into winter's snowy dormancy. The visualization captures both the visual evolution of the tree's foliage and the environmental changes that drive this natural cycle. As time progresses, the leaves transition from bright greens to warm oranges and reds before falling to the ground. Snow then blankets the scene, melting away to reveal small green buds that burst into full blossoms, completing the cycle. The technique uses a single tree structure with dynamically changing leaf properties and particle systems for snowfall, all synchronized to an internal time-based season progression.
