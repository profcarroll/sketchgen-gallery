This sketch creates a visual representation of stream-of-consciousness thought processes through constantly shifting text fragments and organic shapes. The canvas displays hundreds of small text elements that float and drift independently, each with its own motion pattern and fading lifespan. These fragments continuously form and dissolve, creating an impression of mental activity where ideas emerge, interact, and fade away. Organic elliptical shapes move in parallel with the text, adding a sense of fluidity and continuity to the chaotic narrative flow. The work operates entirely autonomously, with no user interaction required for its core animation, yet it's designed to respond visibly to mouse dragging by temporarily gathering fragments along the trajectory, creating temporary phrases that illuminate and then dissolve, guiding the viewer's eye through the conceptual landscape.
