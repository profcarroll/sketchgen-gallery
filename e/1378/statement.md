This sketch presents a vast, imagined architectural expanse rendered in stark industrial tones of white, grey, and deep shadow. The scene consists of a grid of cubes that subtly breathe with harmonic motion, creating an impression of monumental scale and utopian grandeur. Light sources drift slowly across the surfaces, casting dynamic shadows that give the illusion of colossal, silent movement. The viewer is placed in a fixed perspective within this perpetually reforming cityscape, observing an endless geometric vista that pulses with quiet energy. The visual effect is achieved through careful modulation of cube sizes and positions over time, combined with a slowly shifting light source that creates a sense of perpetual motion without any abrupt changes.
