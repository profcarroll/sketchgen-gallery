This sketch creates a visual representation of a fabric surface with a sewing machine head that tracks cursor movement. When the user drags their mouse across the canvas, the machine head follows the path while depositing distinct thread units that form a visible seam. The design uses a grid pattern to simulate fabric texture and employs contrasting gold-colored stitches against a dark background to create visual clarity. The mechanical motion is suggested through the precise following of cursor paths and the discrete placement of thread points along the seam, creating an impression of deliberate, methodical stitching rather than continuous motion.
