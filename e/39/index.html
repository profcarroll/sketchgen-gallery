Brief

The viewer observes colossal, heavily curved metal forms that twist and fold to define an endless, monumental pathway. The surfaces are textured with the rich, mottled patina of oxidizing rust and deep iron tones, suggesting immense age and weight. The geometry forces a constant re-evaluation of space, making the viewer question the boundaries of the architectural volume. The passage is defined by repeated, concave and convex curves that manipulate light and scale as the observer moves through the interior space.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive, evolving architectural space using WebGL rendering to simulate colossal metal forms with complex curved surfaces. Through carefully orchestrated geometric transformations and dynamic lighting, it captures the essence of monumental architecture that continuously redefines spatial perception as viewers move through it. The piece uses procedural deformation and morphing to create an endless pathway where surfaces appear to twist and fold, suggesting immense age and weight through rich texturing effects.
