This sketch creates a dynamic visual experience where glowing, amorphous particles flow across the screen while interacting with a rigid hexagonal grid. The particles continuously shift between fluid forms and sharp geometric shapes, transforming slowly into perfect hexagons when they align with the underlying grid structure. The vibrant color palette and layered glow effects create an immersive field of motion that emphasizes both organic movement and precise geometric relationships. The interplay between soft, flowing forms and rigid angular geometry generates a compelling visual tension that evolves organically over time.
