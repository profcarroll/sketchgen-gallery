This sketch creates an intricate, deeply symmetrical spirograph pattern using filled polygonal areas instead of continuous lines. The composition features multiple rotating spirals and polygonal shapes that interlock and overlap to form a cohesive geometric tapestry. The jewel-toned palette evokes the grandeur of deep space, with rich contrasting colors that shift slowly across the canvas. All motion is continuous and self-generated, simulating the rhythmic dance of celestial bodies in orbit. The patterns are rendered entirely through filled shapes, creating a sense of depth and complexity that emerges from the mathematical relationships between the rotating elements.
