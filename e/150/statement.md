This sketch creates a serene pastoral landscape using layered geometric shapes and gradient techniques. The composition builds depth through overlapping horizontal bands—sky at the top, distant mountains in soft blues, midground hills with gentle undulations, and foreground grass with detailed blade-like strokes. A subtle radial warm glow emanates from the center to enhance the atmospheric quality without overwhelming the natural color palette. The technique emphasizes smooth transitions and organic motion through sine wave functions while maintaining strict stillness as required by the technical constraints.
