This sketch creates a mesmerizing stained glass mosaic that continuously shifts and flows. The composition is built from geometric tiles arranged in a hexagonal pattern, each with rich, saturated colors that subtly drift through the hue spectrum over time. The entire scene pulses with a deep, slow rhythm that causes individual tiles to undulate gently, creating an ethereal light play across the surface. Dark grout lines separate the colorful elements, mimicking traditional ceramic tile work while maintaining the luminous quality of stained glass. The overall effect is a calming, organic movement that transforms the static canvas into a living, breathing artwork without any external input required.
