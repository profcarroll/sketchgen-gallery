This sketch presents a mesmerizing mechanical sculpture composed of a central rotating bronze core surrounded by delicate orbiting splines. The core, rendered in rich copper tones with subtle rotational movement, serves as the gravitational center around which the splines revolve. Each spline is a continuous looping curve made of multiple points that follow organic, flowing paths through 3D space. The splines periodically shift from their warm copper hues to vibrant emerald green, creating dynamic visual contrast and rhythm. The entire composition moves in a continuous, hypnotic dance with no external input required—just the natural progression of time and mathematical motion creating an immersive, self-contained mechanical ballet.
