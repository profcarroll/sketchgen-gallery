Brief

A winding grey ribbon of asphalt snakes across a lush green landscape from a top-down perspective. Various racing cars with distinct, detailed shapes and bright colors speed along the track at varying velocities. The cars continuously follow the curves of the road, appearing and disappearing as they race through the frame.

Artist's statement · laguna-xs-2.1:latest, unedited

I created a scrolling view of racing cars on a winding asphalt road using isometric 2D graphics. The road snakes through the landscape using cubic Bézier curves with randomly generated control points, creating natural turns and varying curvature. Cars are rendered as colorful shapes with motion trails that show their path and speed. I used perspective scaling based on vertical screen position to simulate depth—cars lower on screen appear larger. The track is drawn with width by offsetting vertices along perpendicular tangents to the path. Each car has distinct colors and sizes, moving at different speeds along the parameterized curve.

The executor's own account of what it built, kept verbatim. A claim about the work, not a finding about it.
