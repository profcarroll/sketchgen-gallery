This sketch creates a side-scrolling platformer environment where a player character navigates through gaps and avoids an enemy. The scene features ground platforms with vertical gaps, a controllable character that moves left/right and jumps, and an enemy that patrols back and forth on the ground. The player must use timing and strategy to jump over gaps while dodging the enemy. The visual design uses simple shapes and colors to represent game elements, with motion and interaction driving the experience.
