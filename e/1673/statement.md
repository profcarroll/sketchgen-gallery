This sketch creates a mesmerizing stained-glass window effect with interconnected geometric panels that pulse and glow rhythmically. The viewer observes a complex structure of vibrant polygons arranged in a static composition, where light sweeps across the surface in a steady, hypnotic motion. The light beams contract and expand in a pulsating cycle around the internal edges of the glass pieces, creating an oscillating glow that transforms the entire scene over time. The constant motion and rhythmic pulses generate an immersive visual experience that captures the ethereal quality of light passing through colored glass.
