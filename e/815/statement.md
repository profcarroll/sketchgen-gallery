This sketch depicts an ethereal architectural interior defined by intersecting geometric planes, infused with pulsating emerald green energy. The viewer observes a dynamic space where nodes—points of concentrated luminosity—pulse rhythmically and connect through glowing energy lines. The structure warps around these nodes as planes bend and shift, creating a sense of perpetual motion and energetic flow. The entire scene exists in a crystalline environment that reacts to the power source by distorting and fracturing, mapping the energy throughout the architecture. Through constant animation and interaction between elements, it conveys the feeling of an otherworldly space alive with charged, flowing forces.
