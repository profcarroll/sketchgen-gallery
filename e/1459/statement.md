This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic scene where overlapping translucent circles drift slowly across the screen like a flowing tide. Against this current, thousands of tiny stars move in visible counter-currents. When these opposing forces intersect, collisions generate momentary flares that expand into dissipating rings of light. The visual effect captures the tension between opposing flows, creating a dynamic interplay of motion and light against a deep cosmic backdrop. The technique uses particle systems for both the continuous flow and the pointillist stars, with collision detection triggering the flare effects to create a sense of interaction within the cosmic environment.
