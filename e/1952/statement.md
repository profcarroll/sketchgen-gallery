This sketch presents a monochromatic visualization of a tangled network of data streams that collapse upon reaching junctions. The work evokes the clean, precise aesthetic of 1960s plotter drawings while employing charcoal-like textures and scale. Streams begin as vibrant streaks moving across the canvas, following their own trajectories until they encounter a central node or junction. Upon contact with these points, the streams fail catastrophically, shattering into brittle linear shards that scatter outward. The simulation is static in its execution—no motion occurs unless explicitly triggered by interaction—but the visual impact of failure and disintegration creates a compelling narrative about systems breaking down under pressure. The entire composition is rendered using only basic drawing primitives, maintaining the starkness and mechanical precision of its inspirations.
