This sketch visualizes the internal mechanics of a combustion engine using WebGL rendering for dynamic 3D motion. It depicts four pistons moving up and down within cylindrical chambers, with a spark effect triggering each piston's downward stroke. The mechanical energy transfer is illustrated through a transaxle and interlocking gears that rotate in synchronization with the pistons' motion. The metallic color palette of grays and blacks contrasts with bright yellow flashes from the spark events, emphasizing the power conversion from explosive force to rotary motion. All elements animate continuously without user input, demonstrating the cyclical nature of engine operation through the constant, self-driven movement of components.
