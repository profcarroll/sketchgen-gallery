This sketch presents a contemplative visualization of an ancestral lineage rendered in the style of an Edo woodblock print. The composition features interconnected geometric forms that pulse and shift like the flow of genetic material through time. The cross-hatching effect emerges from the interplay of lines and nodes, suggesting deep relationships within a family tree. Color transitions ripple across the field, mimicking the subtle shifts in hue found in traditional woodblock prints, while organic movement conveys the dynamic nature of lineage and inheritance. The work evokes both the permanence of artistic tradition and the fluidity of biological connection, creating a visual meditation on how ancestral patterns persist through shifting forms.
