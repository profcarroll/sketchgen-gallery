This sketch creates a dynamic, generative display of luminous particles floating in deep space. Using the WEBGL renderer, it establishes a network of connections between particles that pulse and shift as they move through three-dimensional space. The electric cyan and deep violet color palette evokes the contrast between light and darkness described in the brief, while the constantly evolving fractal-like patterns emerge from simple particle physics. Each frame, particles drift and bounce within the canvas bounds, their movement creating a rhythmic energy that generates new connections as they approach each other, producing an ever-shifting crystalline network that is both structured and alive.
