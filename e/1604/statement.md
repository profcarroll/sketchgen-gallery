This sketch creates a dynamic swarm of paper airplane shapes that move collectively in a flocking pattern, responding fluidly to the viewer's cursor. The system implements classic flocking behaviors—separation, alignment, and cohesion—while also incorporating an attraction force toward the mouse position. Each airplane maintains its own motion but contributes to the overall group movement, creating an effect similar to a murmuration of birds or a school of fish. The visual result is a dispersed yet cohesive mass that shifts direction in real time as the cursor moves, with individual gaps visible between planes to emphasize their distinct nature within the collective form.
