This sketch creates a mesmerizing visualization of pulsing concentric rings that emanate from a continuously moving central point tracing a figure-eight pattern. The rings expand outward in vibrant gold hues while gradually fading, creating an infinite sense of radiating energy. The central point's movement follows a mathematical figure-eight path, causing the entire field to pulse with rhythmic motion that fills the entire canvas.
