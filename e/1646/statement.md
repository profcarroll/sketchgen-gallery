This sketch creates an immersive, ever-shifting text vortex using characters from James Joyce's *Finnegans Wake*. Hundreds of semi-transparent letters flow in a continuous spiral, forming a hypnotic background stream. When clicked, the characters rush toward the center, creating a momentary burst of clarity and high-contrast density—like old newsprint suddenly focused. The effect is both organic and mechanical, with characters gradually fading back into the swirling chaos after the click. The visualization emphasizes the cyclical nature of language and meaning, where individual words gain prominence only briefly before dissolving back into the collective text.
