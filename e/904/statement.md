This sketch depicts a stylized cross-section of a tidal zone where horizontal bands of color simulate water flow dynamics. The bands oscillate between broad, gentle curves and highly compressed structures, evolving over time into structured channels that delineate main currents. Within these channels, smaller micro-eddies emerge as intricate, repeating movements that add localized turbulence to the overall rhythmic tide. The visualization uses a combination of sine wave oscillation, color gradients, and layered horizontal bands to create an abstract yet evocative representation of tidal flow patterns.
