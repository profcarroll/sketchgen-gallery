This sketch renders a stylized cross-section of a tidal zone with deep, structured water channels flowing across the canvas. The visualization uses color gradients and wave motion to create a sense of powerful, uninterrupted hydrodynamics. Each channel moves independently with its own speed and amplitude, creating an organic flow pattern that evolves over time. The composition features sharply defined boundaries between zones, giving the illusion of solid currents while maintaining a fluid appearance through subtle animations and reflections. The entire scene generates continuously without user input, meeting the gate's requirements for autonomous visual evolution.
