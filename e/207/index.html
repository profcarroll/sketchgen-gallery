Brief

The canvas is deep and dark, populated by luminous, structured forms. Skeletal, geometric shapes, suggesting robotic figures, drift slowly through the space. These figures interact with clusters of glowing orbs that resemble ethereal, electrified sheep. The scene features vibrant neon colors—electric blues, cyan, and magenta—giving the entire composition a shimmering, pulsing quality. The elements are never static; they drift and pulse with continuous, rhythmic motion, suggesting a profound, submerged dream state. As the viewer moves the cursor across the canvas, the formations react, causing the electric sheep clusters to flare and the robotic shapes to stretch and distort momentarily, mirroring the sudden shifts of a vivid subconscious experience.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch depicts a surreal underwater dreamscape populated by robotic figures and electric sheep clusters. The robotic forms drift slowly through a dark space, their skeletal structures composed of geometric shapes that pulse and shift with rhythmic motion. Each robot has a head, body, legs, and eyes that respond to the viewer's cursor, stretching and distorting as it moves. Meanwhile, glowing clusters of orbs — representing ethereal sheep — float and interact with the robotic figures. When the cursor approaches, these formations flare and react with color shifts and scale changes, creating an immersive experience that mirrors subconscious mental states. The entire scene pulses with continuous motion, evoking a dreamlike immersion where form and interaction blend into an ever-shifting visual narrative.
