Brief

The canvas presents a dynamic, cross-sectional cutaway view inside a running combustion engine. The viewer observes the core mechanics, including pistons moving within their cylinders. Colored fluid flows visibly represent the timed intake and exhaust processes, which are managed by actuated valves. The movement radiates outward as connecting rods visibly articulate and transfer power from the pistons to the central crankshaft. The entire mechanism is shown in three dimensions, with a clear focus on the articulation points and bearing connections where the rods meet the crankshaft, demonstrating the continuous, rhythmic motion of the power cycle.
