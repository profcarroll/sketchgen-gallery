This sketch creates a serene yet dramatically lit pastoral scene using layered geometric shapes to represent rolling fields, distant mountains, and an intense sun. The composition emphasizes the contrast between sharp foreground elements—such as grass patches with exaggerated shadows—and softly blurred, atmospheric distant peaks. By carefully positioning these elements and using color gradients, I've achieved a sense of deep spatial recession that enhances the feeling of sunlight and shadow. The scene is static but richly detailed, evoking the calm and grandeur of an open landscape under intense illumination.
