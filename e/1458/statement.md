This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic environment where translucent, overlapping circles flow slowly across the canvas like a tide of light. The circles drift gently in their own paths, and when they intersect, they create momentary bursts of intensified glow that pulse with luminous energy. Scattered throughout this flowing light field are stars that leave faint, arcing trails behind them as they move. These stars periodically flare with bright light, creating a sense of movement and interaction within the static background. The effect is both calming and dynamic, evoking the feeling of drifting through a star-filled void where particles of light interact and create fleeting moments of brilliance.
