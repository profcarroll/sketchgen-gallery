This sketch creates a dynamic visualization of data streams emerging from a central, contained structure. The tendrils begin as simple lines that grow and evolve in complex patterns, with each one having its own color, growth rate, and movement characteristics. As the viewer's cursor moves across the canvas, it influences the direction and behavior of these streams, causing them to multiply and become more intricate. The central structure remains constant but becomes increasingly small in scale compared to the vast network of luminous data flows that surround it. The visualization uses WebGL rendering to create smooth, flowing lines with depth and dimension, emphasizing the three-dimensional nature of the information space while maintaining the core idea of a contained origin point.
