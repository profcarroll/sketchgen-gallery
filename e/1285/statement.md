This sketch creates an abstract tapestry that juxtaposes flowing organic forms with rigid geometric elements. The composition features hundreds of curved fiber paths that meander across the canvas in rich jewel tones, creating a sense of movement and fluidity. These organic threads are interrupted by sharply defined geometric knots—squares, triangles, and circles—that appear forcefully superimposed onto the flowing background. The stark contrast between the soft, voluminous curves and the angular, structured elements establishes a visual tension that captures the essence of natural flow meeting pure geometry. The work is deliberately still, with no animation or motion, allowing viewers to contemplate the interplay of these opposing forces within a single moment.
