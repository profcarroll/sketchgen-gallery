This sketch creates an immersive visualization of a glass column filled with iridescent oil, simulating the residual echoes of countless human conversations. The work captures the ethereal quality of sound as it transforms into visual form through the medium of liquid light. Organic particles move within the column following subtle noise-based trajectories that shift over time, creating ever-changing patterns of color and movement. The simulation uses a combination of particle physics and procedural color generation to create an organic, flowing effect that never settles into repetition. The entire display is illuminated by simulated ultraviolet light that enhances the iridescent qualities of the oil, creating a mesmerizing, continuous movement of contained color that requires no interaction to be observed.
