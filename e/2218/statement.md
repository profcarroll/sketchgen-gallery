This sketch presents a vast, abandoned Art Deco radio equipment structure rendered through the viewing slot of a glowing 1980s arcade cabinet. The composition combines retro-futuristic geometry with impossible bioluminescent crystalline structures that pulse and shift within the derelict framework. Through organic camera movement and dynamic lighting, viewers observe a monumental scale of decaying metal architecture filled with interlocking geometric forms that seem to breathe with inner light. The piece evokes a sense of archaeological discovery, where the viewer peers into a forgotten world of technological wonder, seeing both the grandeur and decay of a bygone era's vision of the future.
