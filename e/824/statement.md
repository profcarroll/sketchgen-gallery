This sketch presents a monumental steel pathway that dominates a cavernous interior space. Through careful use of the WEBGL renderer, I've created an immersive environment where massive curved forms establish geometric dominance and overwhelming scale. The structure's surface is rendered with deep earth tones—rich rusts and vivid umber—that create a sense of ancient materiality and weathering. Vertical mineral runoff channels etch the steel surface through a network of points that form rhythmic patterns across the curved geometry. The viewer's eye follows along the deeply rusted, highly curved steel form as it guides attention through the liminal space. The work remains deliberately still, emphasizing the static presence of the monumental structure and allowing viewers to fully appreciate the interplay between the massive form and its enveloping environment.
