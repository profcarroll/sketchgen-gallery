This sketch creates a chaotic volcanic landscape where molten lava flows dynamically across the canvas while solidified rock chunks with intricate crystalline patterns writhe and glow. The lava particles are thousands of individually animated ellipses that move with turbulent motion, creating flowing rivers of orange and yellow light. Meanwhile, the rock formations are complex polygonal shapes that rotate slowly and pulse with subtle animation, simulating the deep reds and blacks of solidified volcanic rock. The entire scene shifts continuously through time-based transformations, creating a mesmerizing state of geological chaos without any user interaction, fulfilling the gate's requirement for autonomous visual motion.
