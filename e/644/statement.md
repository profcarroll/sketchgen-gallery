This sketch creates a dynamic, layered graffiti texture by allowing viewers to draw horizontal strokes with their mouse. As users drag across the canvas, new line segments are added to the existing network, building up a dense composition of overlapping strokes. The visual properties—color and thickness—respond dynamically to cursor movement speed and position, creating an organic flow that transforms the static canvas into a living artwork. The technique uses simple line drawing with variable weights and hues to simulate the layered look of spray paint or marker graffiti, responding only to user interaction without any automatic animation.
