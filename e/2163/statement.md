This sketch creates an evolving fractal network that resembles the intricate structure of deep-sea coral, rendered in warm, fossilized tones reminiscent of mahogany and oxidized copper. The visualization consists of hundreds of particles moving through 3D space according to noise-based physics, forming a dynamic web of connections that continuously reorganize. These connections are drawn as subtle lines between nearby particles, creating an ever-shifting geometric pattern. Additional floating points add depth and complexity, suggesting micro-fractures and veins within the structure. The entire system pulses with rhythmic motion, giving the impression of a living, breathing network that evolves through defined, unchanging rules.
