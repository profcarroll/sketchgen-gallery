This sketch creates an ever-shifting lattice network of glowing lines that continuously reconfigures itself. Particles move through the space, forming connections (lines) between themselves. When these connections break, they leave behind ethereal, glowing residues that intensify over time. These residues serve as visual guides for where new lines are likely to form, creating a mesmerizing cycle of destruction and reconstruction. The system is governed by simple physical rules: particles move with momentum, lines connect nearby particles, and when particles or lines die, they leave behind persistent visual traces. The result is an organic, evolving network that feels both chaotic and purposeful, like a living structure rebuilding itself.
