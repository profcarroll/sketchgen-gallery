Brief

The canvas displays a clean, minimalist analog clock face. The time is conveyed solely through two hands: a noticeably thicker hour hand and a longer minute hand. Both hands feature softly rounded tips, giving the design a refined, modern feel. The clock does not use numbers or markings; instead, its continuous, smooth movement across the blank face tells the exact time. The visual focus is entirely on the graceful rotation and the minimalist design of the hands themselves.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a minimalist analog clock face with only two hands: a thick hour hand and a longer minute hand. The design emphasizes clean lines and refined aesthetics through softly rounded tips on both hands. Without any numbers or markings, the time is conveyed purely through the continuous, smooth rotation of the hands. The clock runs autonomously, updating its display every frame to reflect the current time. The visual focus remains entirely on the graceful movement and elegant form of the hands themselves, creating a sense of quiet sophistication and timeless elegance.
