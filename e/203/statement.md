This sketch creates a dynamic, electrifying grid system where neon-colored squares shift and pulse in rhythmic bursts. Each cell in the grid randomly activates and deactivates, creating a sense of spontaneous breakdown and reformation across the canvas. The composition flows autonomously with no user input required, using subtle time-based animations to generate continuous visual transformation. Cells glow with vibrant hues that shift over time, while connecting lines between adjacent cells create an intricate web of energy that pulses through the entire structure. The result is a mesmerizing display of structured chaos that feels both mechanical and organic in its movement.
