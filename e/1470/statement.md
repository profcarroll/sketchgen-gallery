This sketch visualizes a harmonic system of pendulums that swing in synchronized, rhythmic patterns inspired by a pentatonic scale. Each pendulum is represented by a glowing disc that moves through space in a continuous, flowing motion. The colors and timing of the swings are carefully orchestrated to create a mesmerizing, immersive field of movement that evolves over time. The visualization uses WEBGL rendering to give depth and allow for smooth, continuous animation without any user input required.
