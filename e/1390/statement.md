This sketch creates a nostalgic arcade circuit board simulation with a shifting grid of glowing neon pixels. It features geometric sprite clusters (squares, triangles, and lines) that pulse and interact with the viewer's cursor. When the mouse moves across the screen, it triggers expanding collisions that activate nearby grid cells and generate temporary bursts of light. The effect combines static grid elements with animated particles and responsive sprites to evoke a sense of digital decay and rebirth, capturing the joyous energy of retro gaming interfaces while maintaining an organic, evolving pattern.
