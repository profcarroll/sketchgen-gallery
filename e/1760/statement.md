This sketch visualizes an evolving architectural tapestry where geometric structures emerge from chaotic energy. Neon lines pulse and flicker across a grid of points, representing the initial chaos of construction. As the simulation proceeds, certain points stabilize into permanent glowing dots, forming recognizable architectural fragments that appear and fade rhythmically. The visualization captures the essence of emergence — how complex, ordered forms arise from seemingly random processes, with energy gradually resolving into stable, meaningful structures. The continuous motion reflects an endless cycle of creation and dissolution, where the canvas transforms from an electric grid to a landscape of permanent geometric elements.
