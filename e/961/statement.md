This sketch visualizes the dynamic flow of water through a stylized cross-section, showing how complex hydrodynamic patterns emerge from structured currents and smaller eddies. The visualization features thousands of particles that move according to a flowing field, with each particle's motion influenced by both large-scale currents and localized turbulence. As particles flow through the system, they form delicate thread-like connections that represent the natural process of micro-eddies merging into larger streams. The color bands and contrast between different regions highlight the varying flow speeds and directions, creating an abstract yet scientifically inspired representation of fluid dynamics in action.
