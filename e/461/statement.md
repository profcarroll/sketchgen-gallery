This sketch creates a dynamic 3D visualization of a marble rolling through a complex, funnel-shaped channel. Using WebGL rendering, it displays a spiraling path carved into a foundational surface with intricate curvature changes that force the marble to make constant micro-corrections. The channel geometry gradually widens and deepens toward the exit, slowing the marble's descent into a final collecting basin. The animation demonstrates continuous motion without user input, showing the marble's path as it navigates the intersecting ridges and troughs of the structure. The viewer experiences a sense of constant motion and spatial complexity as the camera orbits around the scene, revealing the intricate details of the channel's construction and the marble's journey through it.
