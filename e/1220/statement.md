This sketch creates an immersive, dynamic tapestry of motion and structure using p5.js's WEBGL renderer. The canvas displays thousands of glowing particles that flow organically through space, interrupted by sharply defined geometric knots that rotate in harmony with the cosmic movement. The interplay between fluid, curving threads and rigid, angular elements evokes the feeling of a vast, living textile unfolding in real-time. Audio input drives both the particle motion and visual scale, creating an ever-shifting composition where sound and sight merge into a single, cohesive experience.
