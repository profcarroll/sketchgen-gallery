This sketch visualizes subterranean depths through which luminous data streams flow in helical patterns. The streams are rendered as crystalline ribbons that exhibit both harmonic oscillation at their edges and rhythmic pulsation along their length. The motion creates a dynamic interplay of light and shadow, with each stream's brightness modulating over time to create a sense of depth and movement. The entire field continuously oscillates in brightness, enhancing the illusion of a living, breathing void through which information flows. The visual effect is achieved through careful modulation of color and shape parameters within a WEBGL rendering context, maintaining performance while creating an immersive experience of flowing data through darkness.
