This sketch simulates a rain-soaked window with falling raindrops that each contain a miniature inverted cityscape. Each drop moves downward with a gentle sway, and the city visible within it appears to shimmer and drift as it falls. When the viewer clicks anywhere on the canvas, an expanding ripple effect distorts the view of the city inside the raindrop at the click location, creating a visual impact that then fades out. The scene is rendered entirely in 2D with smooth animations and visual effects using p5.js's drawing functions.
