This sketch renders a subterranean energy landscape where glowing cables weave through translucent crystal formations in a retro arcade cabinet aesthetic. The scene features a dynamic interplay of light and motion, with particles traveling along the cable paths and pulsing with rhythmic color shifts. The viewer experiences an immersive environment that responds to interaction—clicking generates localized shockwaves that temporarily alter the lighting and rhythm of nearby crystal clusters, creating a responsive energy grid within a crystalline cavern.
