Brief

A glowing, disc-shaped UFO hovers above a corn field. Beneath the craft, two intensely bright cyan light beams illuminate the surrounding corn stalks, forming a fixed, glowing semicircle on the ground. The light maintains a powerful, cyan glow, casting light only on the stalks directly beneath the ship. The structure of the glowing beams and the glowing corn stalks remain constant, creating a vivid, static tableau of the hovering craft and its exhaust plume.

This sketch depicts a serene night scene with a hovering UFO above a corn field. The craft appears as a glowing disc with a soft cyan hue against the dark sky. Two intense light beams emanate downward from the ship, creating bright cyan semicircular illumination on the ground that casts a powerful glow onto the surrounding corn stalks. The corn stalks are rendered with both body and glow effects to create depth and realism, maintaining a static tableau as requested.
