This sketch captures a serene yet dramatic pastoral scene at sunset, where rich greens of rolling fields transition into atmospheric misty mountains. The composition uses vertical gradient skies with warm oranges and cool blues, creating depth through layered shapes and subtle noise-based textures. Dramatic shadows are cast across the landscape using overlapping rectangles that simulate ground contours. A bright sun sits on the horizon, emitting rays to emphasize the lighting direction. The palette is saturated but not overwhelming, reflecting the intensity of sunset while maintaining a peaceful mood. The entire canvas remains static, fulfilling the no-motion requirement by depicting a still moment in nature’s grandeur.
