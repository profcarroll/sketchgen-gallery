This sketch creates a dynamic, flowing field of mineral slurry and biological matter that simulates the slow, creeping movement of slime mould. Using a particle system with a noise-based flow field, it generates organic, ever-shifting patterns across the canvas. The particles are influenced by both a continuous background flow and user interaction through dragging, creating ripples and bursts of energy as the viewer perturbs the system. The visual effect combines subtle color variations, smooth motion, and a glowing ambient light to evoke a sense of deep, mysterious biological processes at work.
