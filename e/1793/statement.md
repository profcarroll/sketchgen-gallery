This sketch presents a dynamic field of interconnected, morphing geometric forms that pulse with rhythmic energy. The system consists of dozens of fluid entities drifting across the canvas, constantly shifting in size, speed, and position while maintaining visual connections to nearby peers. These connections form a luminous web that intensifies and dissipates over time, creating patterns of cohesion and release. The color palette undergoes abrupt transitions through highly contrasting hues — from deep purples to blazing oranges — emphasizing the jagged oscillation between calm equilibrium and explosive bursts of movement. The entire composition evolves without external input, driven solely by its internal physics and timing, resulting in a mesmerizing display of continuous transformation and visual tension.
