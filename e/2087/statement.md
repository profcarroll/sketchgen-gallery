This sketch creates a mesmerizing simulation of cosmic motion, rendering thousands of glowing particles that orbit around a central point with natural, curved trajectories. Each particle follows centripetal force while experiencing subtle perturbations that create organic, flowing patterns. The particles leave luminous trails that fade over time, dissolving into faint points as they move through space. The visual effect suggests a vast, dark void filled with energy — an endless dance of gravitational influence and natural motion where individual particles become part of a larger, cohesive field of movement.
