This sketch creates a cosmic flow of interconnected, vibrant shapes that evolve through time to form a mesmerizing display of geometric transformation. The system begins with a balanced structure of glowing spheres connected by lines, then gradually shifts into crystalline facets that pulse and shimmer with internal luminescence. Through continuous animation, the shapes morph between smooth forms and sharp crystal structures while maintaining their connections, creating an ever-changing pattern of energy flow. The viewer witnesses a cycle where geometric harmony transforms into chaotic entropy and back again, all within a constantly shifting three-dimensional space filled with luminous particles and flowing connections.
