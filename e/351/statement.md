This sketch creates an abstract night skyline of a vast metropolis using animated light streaks that flow across the canvas. The lights move independently with varying speeds and directions, creating a sense of motion and urban energy. As they travel, the lights occasionally intersect with a hidden grid system, causing them to intensify and form geometric patterns. Connections between nearby lights are drawn as faint lines, suggesting the network of activity that defines city life. The saturated colors against the deep black background emphasize contrast and movement, resulting in a dynamic portrayal of urban nightlife that evolves continuously without any user input.
