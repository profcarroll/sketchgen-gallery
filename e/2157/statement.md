This sketch presents a metallic hydraulic system in a state of continuous, rhythmic decay. Through interconnected geometric forms—plates, pistons, and pumps—visually represented by cylinders, spheres, and planes, the structure demonstrates cyclical motion that mimics internal pressure dynamics. The entire system operates within a deep rust-colored palette, emphasizing oxidation and wear, with each component moving in synchronized yet independent patterns to create an impression of mechanical failure driven by predictable wave-like forces. The viewer observes a macro-scale representation of a deteriorating industrial mechanism where the movement itself embodies the process of structural breakdown under repeated stress.
