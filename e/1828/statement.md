This sketch visualizes a central axis supporting an array of swinging discs that move independently yet occasionally synchronize into temporary, unified rhythms. Each disc follows its own oscillation pattern with varying speed and phase, creating a complex interplay of motion. At random intervals, all discs briefly lock into phase, producing a momentary coherence in their otherwise independent swinging. The visual effect is enhanced by trailing paths that fade over time, while the central axis gently sways to add dynamic movement to the entire system. The color palette cycles through hues, with each disc maintaining its distinct identity even during synchronization events.
