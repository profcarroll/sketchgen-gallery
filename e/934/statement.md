This sketch renders a vast arid landscape dominated by deep terracotta earth tones and sharply defined cracks. The scene features a beige sky overhead and numerous dynamic dust devils that traverse the cracked ground, leaving visible sediment trails. The composition uses procedural generation to create a textured ground surface with thousands of crack formations, while the dust devils exhibit continuous motion with randomized paths and momentum. The entire canvas changes dynamically over time without any user interaction, creating an immersive desert environment that meets the gate's motion requirements through the constant movement of the dust devils and their trails.
