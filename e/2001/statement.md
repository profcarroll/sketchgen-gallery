This sketch presents a geometric architecture inspired by 1920s Bauhaus design principles, featuring interlocking planes that create a complex, structured maze. The composition uses forced perspective to draw the viewer's eye into deep space, with clean lines and sharp transitions that define the industrial aesthetic of the movement. When the mouse moves across the canvas, certain structural elements dynamically respond by shifting color, rotation, or intensity, creating an interactive experience that alters the perceived depth of the architectural space. The scene is rendered in 3D using p5.js WEBGL mode, with dynamic lighting that follows mouse position to emphasize the shifting spatial relationships between geometric forms.
