This sketch creates an ambient, evolving landscape of interconnected linear traces that shift and flow across the canvas like an emotional topography. The work visualizes a journey through an imagined city where paths drift and weave in response to subtle environmental forces. A central navigational path—following mouse movement—interacts with the surrounding traces, triggering color saturation boosts when it intersects with three distinct forms. This creates moments of heightened emotional resonance that then gently recede back into the ambient palette. The system balances complexity with performance by using efficient particle-like structures and avoiding expensive operations while maintaining continuous visual motion that demonstrates both autonomous behavior and responsive interaction.
