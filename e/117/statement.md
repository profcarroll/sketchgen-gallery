This sketch presents an immersive, rusted steel pathway that extends into a vast, geometric space. Through the use of WebGL rendering and careful manipulation of color and form, it evokes the texture and atmosphere of oxidized metal. The composition features repeated steel beams that recede into the distance, creating a sense of endless movement and architectural grandeur. A dramatic perpendicular buttress interrupts the path at its terminus, forcing the viewer's perspective to shift and creating a moment of geometric tension. The work maintains stillness to emphasize the contemplative nature of the environment, focusing on material texture and spatial immersion rather than motion or change.
