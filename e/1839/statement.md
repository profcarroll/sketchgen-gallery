This sketch depicts a vast, reflective cityscape that transforms through a continuous day-night cycle. The architectural structures—towering, angular buildings with wet, glossy surfaces—respond to shifting atmospheric light while pulsing with rhythmic bioluminescence. As the light pulses hit these wet surfaces, concentric ripples spread outward and gradually dissipate, creating a dynamic interplay between the artificial glow and natural reflection. The entire scene evolves without user input, driven purely by time-based animations that simulate both the passage of day and night and the subtle, organic movement of light across urban surfaces.
