This sketch creates an endlessly evolving field of interlocking geometric patterns that expand from the center of the canvas. Using a series of spirograph-inspired shapes rendered in deep sapphire blue and glowing emerald green, it generates a mesmerizing, symmetrical dance of curves and spirals. The visual effect emerges through layered motion — each pattern moves at its own pace, creating an intricate tapestry of overlapping forms that continuously shift and interact. The result is a hypnotic visualization of systematic growth, where the viewer observes a complex, harmonious field of interwoven shapes that fill the entire surface in perpetual motion.
