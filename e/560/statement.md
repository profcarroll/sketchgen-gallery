This sketch creates a dynamic, living composition of interweaving geometric forms that continuously morph and move across the canvas. The shapes—circles, triangles, and squares—oscillate in position and size while cycling through vibrant color shifts. Their movements are governed by wave-like oscillations, creating a sense of fluid motion and organic growth. Connections between nearby forms pulse and fade, suggesting an underlying network or biological structure. The entire system evolves autonomously without user input, generating an ever-changing visual experience that feels both mechanical and alive.
