This sketch creates a nocturnal desert scene with a train gliding across the landscape. The composition uses a dark color palette to evoke night, populated by thousands of twinkling stars that provide depth and scale. The train moves steadily from left to right, its rectangular windows creating a rhythmic pulsing light pattern as they flash on and off in sequence. This creates an illusion of motion through the vast darkness, with each burst of warm yellow-orange light briefly illuminating the surroundings before fading back into night. The effect simulates the experience of witnessing a train's passage through a remote desert under starlight, emphasizing the contrast between the artificial glow of the train and the natural darkness of the environment.
