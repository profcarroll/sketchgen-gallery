This sketch creates an intricate, animated lattice structure composed of interwoven hexagonal motifs. The design employs a fixed geometric grid of hexagons, but each line within the pattern subtly warps and undulates over time using Perlin noise functions. This creates a gentle, breathing effect that gives the impression of organic movement within the rigid framework. The monochromatic color palette of cyan and indigo hues reinforces the structured nature while adding depth through varying opacity and saturation levels. The animation is self-contained and requires no user interaction to begin, with the entire composition evolving smoothly across the canvas without any static elements.
