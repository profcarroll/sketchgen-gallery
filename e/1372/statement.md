This sketch creates a dynamic, living lattice of colorful lines that continuously shift and flow in an organic manner. Each line segment has its own physics-driven movement, with subtle forces constantly acting upon them to create fluid motion patterns. The system responds to mouse interaction by repelling nearby lines, creating a ripple effect that fractures the web-like structure. The color palette shifts through hues while maintaining vibrant saturation and brightness, giving the impression of an energetic field. Without input, the entire composition evolves slowly over time through the continuous interplay of forces, producing an ever-changing tapestry of light and shadow across the canvas.
