This sketch creates a dynamic, flowing visualization of a galloping horse using abstract, organic shapes that move and morph across the screen. The animation employs a looping sequence of horse silhouettes in earth tones—warm browns and ochres—that appear to race forward through space with relentless momentum. Each shape flows independently yet maintains a rhythmic, synchronized motion that suggests the continuous, powerful stride of a horse in full gallop. The visual effect is enhanced by subtle trails and motion blur, giving the impression of movement through wind or air resistance. The entire canvas is consumed by this kinetic display, which moves without any user interaction once started, creating an immersive and energetic experience.
