This sketch renders a dynamic DNA double helix using p5.js in WEBGL mode. The structure consists of interconnected spheres forming the backbone, with base pairs labeled A, T, C, and G that form complementary pairs. The entire molecular structure continuously winds, unwinds, and folds in a repeating cycle, simulating the biological processes of genetic material transformation and protein synthesis. The animation uses real-time mathematical calculations to create the helical motion while maintaining the correct base pairing relationships. The visualization employs continuous rotation and subtle positional adjustments to demonstrate the dynamic nature of DNA structure.
