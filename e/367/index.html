Brief

The canvas displays an intricate, glowing lattice of vibrant, ethereal lines that form a complex, interconnected web across the screen. The entire network is dynamic, featuring geometric crystalline structures woven into the lattice. These structures pulse with a rhythmic, internal light, causing their color to brighten and dim repeatedly. Following the pulsing, the crystals dissolve, and the material disperses back into the flowing, glowing network, creating continuous and mesmerizing internal motion.
