This sketch simulates a tidal zone cross-section using layered bands of color that move rhythmically to mimic ocean tides. Each band has its own speed and wave amplitude, creating a natural-looking flow. A darker band marks the high water level, contrasting against the lighter, exposed areas below. The visualization moves autonomously without user input, showing the slow, cyclical motion of tide levels. The technique uses sine waves to create smooth, continuous motion across multiple horizontal bands, with additional elements like rocks to enhance the realism of a coastal scene.
