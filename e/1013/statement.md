This sketch creates an interactive water ripple effect that responds to clicks. When a user clicks anywhere on the canvas, a pebble drops and generates concentric ripples that expand outward. The ripples pulse twice before beginning a rapid dissipation process. During this fading phase, a temporary faint ring remains visible briefly before vanishing entirely. The visual effect uses layered ellipses with varying alpha transparency to simulate the physics of water waves. A soft chime sound plays upon each click, initiated by the first user gesture required by the gate's testing environment. The entire animation is contained within a single, self-contained p5.js sketch that meets all performance constraints while delivering a visually satisfying response to user interaction.
