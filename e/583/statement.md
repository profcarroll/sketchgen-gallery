This sketch creates an evolving floral tapestry inspired by rhododendrons through abstracted natural forms that shift and interlace in a continuous animation. Using a grid-based approach with 150+ dynamically generated shapes, it renders soft, jewel-toned petals that gently sway and morph over time. The composition features sharp, clear boundaries between forms while maintaining a breathing quality through subtle color shifts, position changes, and organic movement patterns. Each frame builds upon the previous one to create a hyper-realistic yet fluid pattern where colors bleed and solidify along defined edges, creating a sense of living, growing texture that transforms without stopping.
