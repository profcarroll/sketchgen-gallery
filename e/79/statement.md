This sketch creates an mesmerizing, self-contained tessellation of interlocking hexagons that appear to flow and rotate in a hypnotic pattern. Using a vibrant 1980s color palette with dynamic hues, the hexagons shift through varying transparency levels, creating a watery, fluid effect where adjacent shapes seem to interact and merge. The motion is entirely algorithmic—no user input required—and driven by time-based calculations that produce continuous, seamless animation. The pattern's complexity emerges from the interplay of rotation, color shifts, and layered transparency, evoking both geometric precision and organic movement in a single, mesmerizing display.
