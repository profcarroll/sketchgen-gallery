This sketch creates a dynamic, electric grid field using neon-colored vector lines that pulse and wave across the canvas. The structure consists of intersecting horizontal and vertical lines forming a dense network, but instead of static geometry, each line is animated with continuous wave-like distortions that ripple through the vertical axes. The glowing effect comes from alternating between cyan and magenta hues that pulse in intensity, while the wave motion creates a sense of energy flowing through the grid system. The visualization operates autonomously without user input, with lines continuously shifting and pulsing to create an immersive, electric field experience.
