This sketch renders an abstracted deep-sea coral reef using the blocky, saturated aesthetic of 1980s arcade graphics. Through thousands of geometric facets—spheres, boxes, and torus shapes—each particle pulses with rhythmic motion that mimics the organic flow of underwater life. The constant, flowing camera movement and time-based transformations create an immersive, living landscape where vibrant colors shift and interlock across the canvas, evoking both the complexity and tranquility of a vast oceanic ecosystem.
