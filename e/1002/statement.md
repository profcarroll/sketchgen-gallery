This sketch creates an endless series of concentric rings that emanate from the center of the canvas, expanding outward at a steady pace. Each ring is drawn with a grayscale stroke that gradually fades away as it grows, creating a smooth, hypnotic pattern of diminishing intensity. The rings are generated continuously and fade out naturally, maintaining a consistent expansion rate without any external input or interaction. The visual effect is one of continuous motion and fading presence, like ripples on water or waves in a pool, but with the monochromatic quality of grayscale tones that emphasize the pure geometric progression of the rings.
