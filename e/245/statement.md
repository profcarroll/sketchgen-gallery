This sketch depicts a whimsical fleet of paper-cut style ships navigating across a canvas. The ships move with deliberate, slow motion away from the viewer, maintaining their stylized appearance with simple geometric forms and vibrant colors. As they sail, each ship leaves behind a fading trail of semi-transparent points that gradually dissolve into the background. The continuous motion creates an ephemeral sense of passage and departure, with new ships appearing randomly to maintain the ongoing flow. The visual effect combines gentle animation with subtle color variation to evoke a dreamlike maritime scene.
