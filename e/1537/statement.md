This sketch creates a mesmerizing vortex of semi-transparent text characters that swirl and flow across the screen like liquid words. The characters follow natural, organic motion patterns with a subtle vortex effect, constantly shifting and overlapping. When clicked, the characters instantly coalesce into legible phrases with high contrast—transforming from an amorphous stream into coherent, readable text. This momentary clarity is followed by the text dissolving back into its original flowing state. The visual effect captures the tension between chaos and order, representing how meaning can emerge from disorder before returning to it. The technique uses physics-based movement with velocity damping and vortex dynamics, combined with a simple click interaction that triggers an immediate transformation in character behavior.
