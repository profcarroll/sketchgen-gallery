This sketch creates an immersive tide pool environment where vibrant anemones respond dynamically to mouse proximity. The composition features a deep blue ocean background with gently moving particles that simulate water currents. Multiple colorful anemones are scattered throughout the lower portion of the canvas, each with undulating tentacles that open and close rhythmically. When the mouse approaches any anemone, its tentacles retract and fold inward, creating a realistic reaction that transforms the overall shape of the tide pool's edge. The scene remains static until user interaction triggers the responsive behavior, emphasizing the delicate balance between marine life and their environment.
