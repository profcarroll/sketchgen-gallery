This sketch visualizes the dynamic interplay between architectural geometry and light through computationally generated rays. Central to the composition is a glowing light source that emits rays tracing paths across rigid geometric planes. These rays are not static—they gradually dissipate over time, transitioning from sharp, defined lines to soft, diffuse glows. The scene captures temporal passage by rendering the ephemeral nature of light interacting with enduring structure. The visualization emphasizes how computation can be made physical and visible, transforming abstract light behavior into a tangible visual phenomenon.
