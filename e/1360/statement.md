This sketch presents a mechanical system of interlocking gears rotating in a dark, industrial 3D space. The visualization demonstrates how motion transfers through a gear train, with smaller, faster gears driving larger, slower ones. A central flywheel spins with great torque at the end of this chain, representing the culmination of the mechanical energy transfer. Viewers can interact by dragging their mouse across the canvas to change the rotation speed and direction of the entire system. The scene subtly pulses with light that responds to the average speed of the gears, enhancing the sense of mechanical rhythm and energy flow within the rotating machinery.
