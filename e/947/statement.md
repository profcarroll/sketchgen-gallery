This sketch creates a monochromatic urban landscape where thousands of lights form a glowing cityscape. Using a spatial grid optimization, it efficiently connects nearby lights with subtle lines that create the illusion of depth and movement. The major intersections are defined by angular crystalline clusters that glow from within, using radial gradients to simulate internal luminosity. These elements combine to produce a sense of vast urban sprawl rendered in a single, continuous animation without any external input, relying solely on the natural motion of the lights and rotating clusters.
