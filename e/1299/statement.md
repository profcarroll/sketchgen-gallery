This sketch renders a haunting, automated drone flight through an abandoned Brutalist campus. Using the WEBGL renderer, it creates a series of angular concrete structures with repeating geometric patterns that define the characteristic aesthetic of brutalist architecture. The camera slowly sweeps through the environment, revealing the scale and decay of this academic space through continuous, autonomous motion. The muted color palette and stark lighting emphasize the cold, imposing nature of these architectural forms, while subtle grid patterns on building surfaces reinforce their rigid, utilitarian design philosophy. The scene unfolds without user interaction, demonstrating how algorithmic animation can create immersive architectural narratives in a purely digital environment.
