This sketch creates a serene winter alpine landscape with layered mountain ranges, rocky formations, and a deep snowfield. The composition uses muted blues and grays to evoke cold, untouched wilderness. When clicked at the center of the canvas, an avalanche effect radiates outward from that point, visibly disturbing the snow particles and displacing them according to physics-based motion. The impact reveals underlying rock structures through disrupted snow drifts, emphasizing crevices and channels in the foreground terrain. The visual effect is achieved through particle displacement and a fading wave animation, creating an atmospheric scene that responds meaningfully to user interaction while maintaining stillness when idle.
