This sketch depicts a dynamic urban landscape that transitions through day and night cycles. Massive angular buildings are softened by lush green plant life growing from their surfaces, creating a harmonious blend of architecture and nature. As the light shifts throughout the day, bioluminescent flora emits gentle glowing pulses that cast colorful reflections on wet city surfaces. Tiny luminous plankton drift slowly across reflective pools, adding subtle movement to the scene. The entire composition evolves autonomously, showing how urban environments can nurture life and light through natural processes.
