This sketch presents a visual representation of a piano keyboard with both white and black keys. When a user clicks on any key, it visually depresses into the surface and simultaneously generates a colorful ripple effect that emanates from the clicked key's location. The ripples expand outward in increasing circles, fading as they grow larger, creating a vibrant visual feedback loop. The design maintains a clean aesthetic with clear contrast between keys and their interactive elements, ensuring that each interaction is both intuitive and visually satisfying.
