This sketch creates a mesmerizing visual experience of rhythmic pulses expanding from random points. The black canvas serves as a void where red and black pulses rapidly expand outward, leaving glowing trails that fade slowly behind them. Each pulse is a glowing ring that grows at its own speed, creating an organic, chaotic dance across the screen. The system accumulates these lingering lights into a persistent layer of luminous residue that slowly dissipates over time. The viewer's interaction with the mouse adds new pulses and scatters existing lights, creating a constantly evolving pattern of motion. The visual rhythm is maintained by the consistent timing of pulse expansion, while the fading trails create an impression of temporal depth and continuity.
