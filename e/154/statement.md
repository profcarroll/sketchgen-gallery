This sketch creates an immersive, ever-shifting cosmic environment that evokes the tranquility of a late-night sky. Through softly pulsing, nebulous forms drifting across the canvas, it captures the quiet luminosity of deep space. The background shifts from midnight blues to deeper violets, punctuated by gentle bursts of neon pink and emerald green that shimmer like distant stars. The piece responds to cursor movement with immediate, expanding ripples of light that pulse and fade, creating an interactive yet serene atmosphere where viewers can trace their presence through the cosmic void.
