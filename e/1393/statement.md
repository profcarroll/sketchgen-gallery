This sketch visualizes the physics of sound generation through a magnetic speaker system. It depicts a stationary magnet next to a speaker cone, showing how magnetic vibration causes the cone to oscillate. The visualization creates expanding concentric ripples from the cone's movement, representing propagating sound waves. These colorful rings grow outward and fade as they spread, creating a continuous dynamic pattern that illustrates energy propagation. The system demonstrates how mechanical motion translates into acoustic waves through the simple but effective mechanism of magnetic vibration driving a physical speaker element.
