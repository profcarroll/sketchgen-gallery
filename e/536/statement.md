This sketch renders an atmospheric urban cityscape using flowing, light-streaked lines that move across a monochromatic grid. The scene simulates constant subtle motion through particles that drift and bounce within the frame, occasionally colliding to form momentary geometric flashes. These visual elements create an impression of continuous, dynamic activity without requiring user interaction. The technique employs a particle system with fading trails and occasional shape overlays to generate a sense of depth and movement, capturing the essence of an ever-changing urban environment through abstract, flowing visual rhythms.
