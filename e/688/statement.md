This sketch creates atmospheric color washes through layered vertical planes that gently drift and interact across the canvas. Each plane moves at a different speed and carries its own hue offset, creating a sense of depth and motion. The composition features a subtle rhythmic chroma shift that intensifies the central boundaries of the planes, drawing the viewer's eye through the core of the work. The colors are rich and saturated but softly blended, with overlapping planes creating complex interactions where hues mix and modulate over time. The effect is one of slow, organic movement and color transformation, evoking atmospheric phenomena like cloud layers or light gradients in a landscape.
