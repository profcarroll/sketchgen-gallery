This sketch presents an immersive underwater landscape where warm ochre and cool blue rock strata form a flowing, folded mountain range. The viewer drifts slowly through interconnected caverns that pulse with bioluminescent light, creating a tranquil, meditative experience. The geological formations shift subtly over time, suggesting the slow movement of tectonic forces beneath the surface, while the glowing highlights and fluid motion evoke the organic patterns of deep-sea environments.
