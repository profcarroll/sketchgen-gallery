This sketch creates a serene pastoral landscape with a bright blue sky, fluffy white clouds, and a verdant grassy field. The scene features a central gentle hill rising from the foreground, rendered with subtle sine-based curves to suggest natural terrain. The clouds are drawn as layered ellipses and gently drift across the canvas at varying speeds, creating a sense of atmospheric motion without any user interaction. The composition uses vertical color gradients for the sky and geometric shapes for the ground and hill, all rendered in earthy tones that evoke a peaceful countryside scene. The animation loop ensures continuous movement of the clouds, satisfying the requirement for canvas changes over time without external input.
