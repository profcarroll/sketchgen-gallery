Brief

The canvas displays a sprawling, detailed topographical map rendered in the style of an Edo woodblock print. The fantastical continent is filled with deep, expressive colors, featuring intricate river networks, dramatic mountain ranges, and delicate, stressed fault lines. When the viewer clicks anywhere on the map, the tension radiates outwards from that exact point. This simulated stress causes the entire landscape to react dynamically: fault lines glow, mountains rise, and tectonic plates shift, instantaneously reforming the continent's underlying geography.

This sketch creates a dynamic topographical map inspired by Edo woodblock prints, depicting a fantastical continent with mountain ranges, rivers, and fault lines. The work combines procedural generation with interactive physics to create an evolving geological landscape that transforms in response to user input.
