This sketch creates a minimalist analog clock face with polished copper hands that smoothly track the passage of time. The design uses a neutral background with carefully calculated hand positions that update continuously to reflect the current hour and minute. The hands feature appropriate thickness and rounded tips to create a realistic metallic appearance, moving in continuous sweeps rather than discrete jumps. The implementation maintains visual consistency across different canvas sizes through responsive scaling while ensuring smooth animation through precise angle calculations that incorporate seconds for fluid motion.
