Brief

The canvas displays a minimalist analog clock face, set against a neutral background. The core elements are two distinct metallic hands—hour and minute—emerging from the center. These hands possess noticeable thickness and softly rounded tips, and their color is specifically rendered as polished copper, exhibiting a rich, warm sheen. The hands move in a continuous, smooth sweep, accurately tracking the passage of time across the face. The entire sketch operates autonomously, displaying the time regardless of any input.
