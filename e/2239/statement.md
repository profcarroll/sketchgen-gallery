This sketch creates a vast cosmic gas cloud scene using thousands of glowing particles that slowly drift and pulse in three-dimensional space. The visualization depicts a galactic nursery with layered strata of bioluminescent gas, where each particle represents a tiny fragment of the cosmic environment. The gentle swirling motion and pulsing effects simulate the dynamic nature of stellar nurseries, while the layered planes suggest the structured formations found in interstellar space. The entire scene evolves independently without user interaction, creating an immersive experience of deep space dynamics that fills the canvas with ethereal, glowing structures moving through the void.
