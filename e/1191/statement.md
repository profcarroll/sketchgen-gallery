This sketch creates an abstract textile composition where organic flowing fibers contrast with geometric knots. The fibers drift and bounce across the canvas in fluid motion, their colors shifting subtly to suggest depth and movement. The knots appear as rigid, angular shapes that weave through the flowing forms, creating tension between chaos and control. Audio input from the environment drives visual changes—volume modulates particle intensity while frequency analysis generates dynamic waveform visualizations that respond to sound. The entire composition evolves autonomously once started, with no external input required after the initial click.
