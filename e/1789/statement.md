This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of neon-lit geometric forms that move only when they physically collide with other shapes. The scene begins with a static array of octahedra and hexagonal prisms floating in three-dimensional space, each following its own helical path along a designated axis. When two forms intersect, they immediately enter a state of energetic motion, their colors shifting dramatically as they pulse and rotate. The visual effect is one of coordinated chaos – shapes remain perfectly still until collision triggers a ripple of movement that spreads through the entire system. The color palette shifts from vibrant neon hues to electric blues and purples, creating an otherworldly spectacle that feels both organic and mechanical in its behavior.
