This sketch creates a luminous ring of interconnected white dots that pulses with a continuous wave pattern. The formation flows with synchronized motion across the entire ring, then contracts into a single beating center point before expanding back into its full circular form. The visual effect captures the rhythmic contraction and expansion of light, creating an organic pulsing motion that repeats continuously without any user interaction. The implementation uses mathematical sine waves to create smooth, flowing animations while maintaining the integrity of the circular structure throughout the transformation cycles.
