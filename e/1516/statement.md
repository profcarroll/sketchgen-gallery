This sketch depicts the slow, patient revival of a coral reef through abstract branching forms that begin pale and fragmented before gradually blooming into rich, saturated hues. The visualization uses color transitions over time to represent the biological process of life returning to the depths, with each coral structure pulsing gently as it evolves from dormancy to vibrancy. The subtle motion and gradual color shifts create an immersive sense of recovery and renewal in a deep-sea environment.
