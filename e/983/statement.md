This sketch creates a persistent ink trail that responds to mouse movement, with each stroke building up a dynamic layer of color and intensity. The visual effect emerges from the accumulation of semi-transparent black points that fade over time, creating clusters where the cursor has lingered. As the drawing continues, these clusters become more defined and concentrated, forming a rich pattern that reflects the user's motion through the canvas. The system uses a simple but effective technique of maintaining an array of trail points, updating their ages each frame, and rendering them with opacity that decreases over time. This approach creates a visually compelling result that balances persistence with natural fading, producing an organic feeling of accumulation and decay in the ink patterns.
