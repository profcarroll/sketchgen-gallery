This sketch creates a static, grid-based typographic composition inspired by Swiss modernism. Using a strict 40-pixel grid system, it arranges bold, geometric text elements within defined rectangular blocks that emphasize negative space and visual contrast. The design adheres to the principles of Swiss typography—clear hierarchy, alignment, and structured layout—through controlled juxtaposition of text and graphical separators. The work presents a finalized poster design, deliberately still and unchanging, as required by the evaluation criteria.
