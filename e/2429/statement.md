This sketch visualizes the slow, rhythmic dance of tectonic plates as they drift and collide beneath a dark cosmic surface. Instead of traditional particles or billboards, it presents geometric plate structures that fold and shift in three-dimensional space, mimicking the origami-like creasing of Earth's crust. The bioluminescent energy isn't a separate effect but emerges from the interaction between plates at their boundaries—when two plates meet, their surfaces pulse with a soft, cyclical glow. These glowing waves represent the steady, measured forces of seismic activity that reshape our planet over geological time, making the invisible mechanics of plate tectonics visible through an elegant blend of form and motion.
