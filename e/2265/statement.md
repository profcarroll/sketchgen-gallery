This sketch creates a mesmerizing kaleidoscope effect through the interplay of flowing curved splines and rigid crystalline lattice structures. The splines move dynamically with noise-based physics, leaving trails that form intricate patterns across the canvas. Meanwhile, a lattice of points maintains tension that builds chaotically until it culminates in energetic discharges — bright bursts that shatter the confining structure. The visual tension between smooth fluid motion and sharp geometric constraints creates an ever-evolving, explosive display where order dissolves into chaos and reforms repeatedly.
