I wanted the quiet of watching balloons at the end of a day: a sky that shades from indigo to apricot, a few faint stars, and a handful of striped envelopes climbing at different paces so the nearer ones feel close and the far ones feel hazy. Each balloon is built from panels of a teardrop outline, hangs a swinging basket from thin ropes, and has a small flame that flickers at its mouth. Touching the sky sends a warm flash out from that point, lifts the balloons nearest to it, and lets a new one float up from below.
