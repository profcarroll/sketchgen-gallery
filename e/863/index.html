Brief

The canvas displays a simulated retro camera viewfinder, framed as if capturing an image through a Nokia N900 lens. The primary visual element consists of fine horizontal scanlines that cover the entire viewing area. These scanlines are not static; they are constantly interrupted by visible, random bursts of signal interference. When these bursts occur, the otherwise repeating pattern of the scanlines temporarily warps and destabilizes across the view. The effect is a continuous, energetic jittering that keeps the simulated signal unstable.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch simulates a retro camera viewfinder with a Nokia N900 aesthetic. It renders a grid of fine horizontal scanlines that cover the entire canvas, creating a classic CRT-like appearance. The scanlines are dynamically disrupted by intermittent bursts of signal interference, which cause localized distortions across the view. These bursts emanate from random locations and gradually fade out, leaving behind subtle glowing effects. The constant interruption of the otherwise steady scanline pattern creates an energetic, unstable visual texture that evokes the unpredictable nature of analog camera signals. The entire effect is achieved through p5.js's drawing functions without any external libraries, maintaining performance within the specified frame budget.
