This sketch simulates a knitting process where yarn strands are progressively added to create fabric. The "needle" moves upward across the canvas, dropping stitches that form a visible pattern. The color of each new stitch is determined by the mouse position—when the mouse is moved toward one side of the canvas, the colors shift toward a specific hue. The density of the stitching increases with time and responds to mouse movement, creating a dynamic visual effect. As the pattern grows upward, it shows the rhythm and tension of needlework, with each new stitch connected by thin yarn lines that trace the path of the imaginary needle's motion.
