This sketch presents a serene alpine landscape with snow-capped peaks against a vibrant sky and green foothills. The scene is deliberately still until the viewer interacts with it by clicking anywhere on the canvas. Upon that single click, the tranquility is shattered as a dramatic avalanche begins cascading down the mountain slopes. The effect is rendered through particle systems where snow particles are initially distributed across the peaks, then rapidly fall and spread across the lower terrain in a realistic avalanche simulation. The technique uses simple geometric shapes for mountains with layered triangles to create depth, and particle physics to simulate the falling debris. The viewer sees a complete transformation of the landscape from peaceful stillness to dynamic motion, all triggered by a single user gesture.
