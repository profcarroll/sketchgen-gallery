This sketch simulates the growth of fungal mold in a substrate environment, rendering delicate thread-like filaments that branch and interact with their surroundings. The visualization captures the organic complexity of mold networks through constantly evolving structures that grow from initial points, each filament maintaining its own age and thickness while occasionally depositing mineral crystals. The scene features a muted, earthy color palette that contrasts with vibrant crystalline deposits, creating an ethereal geological landscape where natural processes of growth and mineralization unfold over time. The simulation runs continuously without user input, showing the dynamic nature of these biological processes through the constant motion and development of the network structures.
