This sketch presents a handwoven textile pattern rendered through mathematical precision and organic flow. The composition features a central symmetrical floral motif composed of interlocking circles and petal shapes in deep jewel tones—brown, indigo, and dark green—evoking the richness of traditional weaving. The pattern is framed by a geometric border constructed from alternating triangles and squares that follow strict mathematical rules. The entire design maintains perfect symmetry across all axes, creating an intricate balance between structured geometry and natural organic forms. The viewer observes an elegant tension between the rigid mathematical framework and the flowing, growth-inspired motifs that suggest both the structure of weaving and the unpredictability of nature.
