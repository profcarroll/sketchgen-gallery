Brief

The canvas displays a dynamic field of vibrant, streaking color. The individual streaks represent particles moving across the screen. These particles are governed by invisible repulsive forces, causing them to continually push and interact with one another. Instead of scattering randomly, the constant mutual repulsion organizes the particles into distinct, stable, cyclical spiral formations, creating a mesmerizing, ever-shifting pattern of light and color that exists independently of any user input.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of colorful streaking particles that organically form stable spiral patterns through mutual repulsion forces. Each particle moves independently while constantly interacting with its neighbors, creating emergent behavior where the repulsive interactions organize the particles into cyclical spirals that shift and evolve over time. The system uses physics-based motion with velocity damping and edge wrapping to maintain continuous movement within the canvas boundaries. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of particle trails, color cycling, and subtle central forces that guide the formation of the spiral structures without direct control or user input.
