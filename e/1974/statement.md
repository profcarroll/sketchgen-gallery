This sketch depicts an intricate underground root system rendered in the style of a medieval tapestry. The composition features interwoven organic threads that suggest ancient, weathered woody structures, with a rich sense of depth and texture at human scale. Crystallized elements within the root network emit a warm, luminous glow that radiates outward in soft radial gradients, creating an illuminated effect reminiscent of light filtering through stone caverns. The visual approach emphasizes the tactile quality of woven fibers while suggesting the mysterious, ancient nature of subterranean environments.
