This sketch creates a mesmerizing cascade of colorful, crystalline rectangular blocks that continuously fall and spiral in an endless motion. Each block displays iridescent material properties through dynamic color shifts and crystalline shine effects, creating a vibrant curtain of shifting light and form. The blocks accumulate in a perpetual spiral descent, with physics-based movement including gravity, friction, and bouncing off screen edges. The composition is enhanced by a central vortex that draws the eye while maintaining the overall sense of continuous, hypnotic motion. The visual effect relies on HSB color cycling and layered transparency to create depth and luminosity without complex rendering techniques.
