Brief

The canvas displays a glowing, isometric multi-lane race track surface. Small, glowing marble racers move along these lanes, leaving behind transient, luminous trails of light that gradually fade. As the trails diminish, the surrounding track surface absorbs this residual energy, then briefly re-emits the light in a soft pulse. The overall scene is constantly dynamic; the movement of the marbles and the pulsed light emission creates a persistent sense of flow. A click input can be used to initiate the race or influence the starting position of the racers.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a dynamic, glowing isometric race track with multiple lanes where marbles move in continuous motion. Each marble leaves behind luminous trails that gradually fade, while the track surface absorbs and re-emits light energy as soft pulses. The visualization uses WEBGL rendering for 3D depth and lighting effects, with particles drawn as point clouds rather than full meshes to stay within performance budgets. The scene is constantly in motion, creating a sense of continuous flow, with click input allowing users to reset the race or influence starting positions. The visual effect combines particle physics with light emission and absorption to create an immersive, glowing environment.
