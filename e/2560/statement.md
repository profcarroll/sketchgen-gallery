This sketch visualizes the surface of dark slate cross-sectioned to reveal fossilized wood structures. Using a grid-based system of deforming lines that respond to noise functions over time, it creates intricate fractal patterns reminiscent of petrified grain and crystalline decay. The stark white chalk lines delineate these structures against the dark background, emphasizing the tactile quality of geological time. The composition remains still, offering an observational study of natural structural formations frozen in time.
