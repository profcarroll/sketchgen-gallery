This sketch renders a static nighttime cityscape with sleek drones navigating between towering buildings and across a dark harbor. The scene is deliberately motionless until a viewer moves their mouse over specific regions of the harbor or city, triggering complex geometric formations in which the drones temporarily arrange themselves into structured patterns before dispersing back into flight. The visual design emphasizes high contrast, volumetric lighting effects, and clean architectural forms to evoke a futuristic urban environment viewed from across the water.
