This sketch depicts vast, organic sand dunes that continuously reshape and migrate across the screen, simulating the slow passage of geological time. Using a combination of layered ellipses and subtle noise-based movement, the formations flow organically with undulating slopes and detailed striations that traverse all surfaces. The earthy color palette of browns, beiges, and ochres creates a sense of depth and texture, while the constant motion suggests an endless, subterranean flow. Each dune moves independently through a field of similar forms, creating a dynamic landscape that evolves continuously without any external input, capturing the patient persistence of natural erosion and deposition.
