This sketch visualizes the slow decay of architectural memory through the transformation of solid beeswax forms into a viscous honey pool. Initially, warm golden rectangles represent defined structures that gradually shrink and lose their edges, becoming fluid amorphous blobs. The simulation captures the entropic process where discrete architectural elements dissolve into continuous liquid form, with colors shifting from pale amber to deep gold as they settle. The viewer observes an ever-flowing landscape of memory yielding to time's gravitational pull, rendered through thousands of particles that morph seamlessly from solid geometry to liquid pools.
