This sketch creates a visual representation of glass powder reacting to a sudden pneumatic vacuum. Initially, particles form a stable, structured pattern in the center of the canvas. When clicked, the entire composition erupts in a chaotic explosion as particles scatter outward in all directions. The effect is achieved through a particle system with physics-based movement and velocity, creating an organic, unpredictable dispersion that fills the frame. The viewer witnesses a controlled burst of color and motion, representing the transition from stillness to dynamic chaos.
