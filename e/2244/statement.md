This sketch creates an evolving macro-scale pattern that evokes the intricate, layered textures of medieval manuscript illuminations, rendered through a material language of corrugated cardboard. The composition shows structured patterns forming and dissolving across the canvas, with each element drifting and pulsing in subtle synchronization. The visual process mimics the gradual emergence of coherence from chaos, using a spatial grid system to efficiently connect nearby elements while maintaining the organic flow of fibrous, layered materials. The interplay between forming and dissolving structures creates a sense of continuous metamorphosis, where patterns gradually coalesce into coherent forms before breaking apart again.
