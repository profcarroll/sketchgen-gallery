This sketch creates a monochromatic architectural diagram of biological dendritic branching patterns. Using a grid of nodal points, it generates complex angular structures that expand rhythmically across the canvas. Each point grows and branches in a controlled, mechanical way that mimics organic neural networks while maintaining strict geometric order. The visual effect emerges from the interplay between individual nodal growth and the connections between them, creating a structured yet dynamic composition that suggests both clinical precision and natural complexity. The rhythmic expansion occurs through sine wave modulation of connection lengths, producing an organic flow within a systematic framework.
