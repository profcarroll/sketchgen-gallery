Brief

A constantly moving field of nested, glowing arcs organizes itself radially across the canvas. Multiple concentric rings pulse with an internal luminosity, creating a self-contained, mesmerizing pattern. The color of the entire array is dictated by distinct, sharply defined bands that sweep sequentially through the visible spectrum, moving smoothly from one color to the next in a continuous, uninterrupted cycle. The pattern maintains its rhythmic glow, existing independently of any viewer action.

This sketch creates a mesmerizing, self-organizing field of nested, glowing arcs that pulse and rotate in a radial pattern. The visual effect is achieved through multiple concentric rings, each rotating at different speeds and displaying colors from the visible spectrum in sharp, distinct bands. The entire composition revolves around a central glowing core, creating a hypnotic, rhythmic glow that moves independently without any viewer interaction. The use of WEBGL rendering ensures smooth 3D motion while maintaining performance within the frame budget constraints.
