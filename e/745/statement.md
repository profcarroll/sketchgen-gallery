This sketch depicts two distinct streams of particles flowing across the canvas in continuous, fluid motion. The particles are colored in complementary hues (deep blue and pink) and move according to a shared but subtly different noise-based flow field, creating natural-looking currents. One stream exhibits rhythmic pulsing — its particle size expands and contracts in time — while maintaining physical separation from the other stream through spatial constraints. The visual effect mimics flowing water or energy currents, with smooth motion and organic patterns that persistently animate the canvas without requiring user interaction.
