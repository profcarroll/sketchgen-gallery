This sketch creates a fluid, organic transformation of diagonal stripes that evokes the texture of corrugated cardboard at a microscopic scale. Through carefully animated color gradients and rhythmic motion, the stripes appear to slice and re-form in a continuous, mesmerizing pattern. The saturated 1970s color palette — featuring mustard yellows, burnt oranges, forest greens, and deep browns — bleeds and shifts across the diagonals, creating an effect reminiscent of humidity-damaged cardboard fibers. Each stripe moves independently with its own speed and angle, producing a complex interplay of light and shadow that transforms the canvas into a living, breathing geometric structure.
