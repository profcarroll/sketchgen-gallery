This sketch depicts a vast, subterranean network of interlocking steel filaments that pulse with a slow, rhythmic breathing motion. The structure resembles a dense root system made of heavy sheet steel, textured with gritty rust and dark oxidized patches. Through the use of WEBGL rendering, I've created a three-dimensional thicket where light catches the corroded edges of interwoven paths. The filaments expand and contract in unison, creating an organic breathing effect within the rigid metallic framework, while subtle variations in color and texture simulate the appearance of weathered steel.
