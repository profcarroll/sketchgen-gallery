This sketch presents a minimalist analog clock face rendered in muted tones, focusing purely on the cyclical movement of time through smoothly curved copper-toned hands. The design avoids any numerical markers or decorative elements, emphasizing clean lines and the elegant flow of motion. The hour and minute hands are drawn with a warm, metallic sheen that contrasts subtly against the neutral background, creating a timeless aesthetic that highlights the passage of time through pure visual rhythm. The clock operates using standard p5.js time functions to calculate hand positions, ensuring accurate representation of the current time without any external dependencies or interactive elements.
