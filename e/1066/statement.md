This sketch creates an evolving composition of overlapping colored planes that flow with subtle wave motions. Each plane moves independently with its own rhythm, amplitude, and frequency, generating a constantly shifting color field across the canvas. The technique uses sine and cosine functions to create natural-looking wave patterns that blend together to form rich, saturated color layers. The soft boundaries between planes are achieved through careful layering and alpha blending, creating an organic feel where colors subtly shift and interact without harsh edges. The result is a meditative visualization of color in motion, with the viewer observing a deep, ever-changing field of chromatic energy.
