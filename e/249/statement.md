This sketch depicts a cosmic dance of orbital mechanics and light. A central planetary orb is suspended in deep space, surrounded by hundreds of geometric satellites moving along intersecting orbital planes. The satellites trace complex paths that cross each other, creating intense glowing nodes at their intersection points. These nodes flare with pulsing light as the system evolves, demonstrating the interplay between mechanical geometry and dynamic energy. The visualization uses WEBGL rendering to create a three-dimensional space where the motion appears continuous and resonant, capturing the elegant precision of celestial mechanics while emphasizing the beauty of gravitational interactions through light and color.
