Brief

The canvas simulates a view of a planet's interior and surface, observed from orbit. The scene displays deep geological strata where the crust is constantly being reshaped by massive, slow-moving thermal plumes. These plumes of plasma emerge from the mantle, creating visible, glowing streams of heat energy that continuously interact with and push the underlying planetary crust.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch simulates an orbital view of a planet's interior, depicting geological strata and thermal plumes emerging from the mantle. The visualization uses WebGL rendering to create a volumetric effect with glowing plasma streams that continuously reshape the crust. The scene shows deep layers of rock in different hues, with dynamic thermal plumes rising through them, creating a sense of constant geological activity. The viewer observes this process from orbit, seeing how heat energy from deep within the planet interacts with and deforms the surface structure over time.
