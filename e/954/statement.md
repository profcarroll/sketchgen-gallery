This sketch renders a serene alpine winter scene with layered peaks, rocky formations, and snow-covered terrain. The composition uses cold color palettes with deep blues and soft whites to evoke the stillness of a mountain landscape. When clicked, the canvas responds with an animated avalanche effect that sweeps across the foreground, visibly displacing the pristine snow and leaving trails of underlying rock. The technique combines simple geometric shapes with procedural particle systems to create both the static landscape and dynamic avalanche, using minimal draw calls within the specified frame budget while maintaining visual coherence throughout the scene.
