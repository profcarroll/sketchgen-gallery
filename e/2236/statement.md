This sketch creates an ethereal geological surface that responds to user interaction like a living mineral formation. The canvas displays a field of flowing light trails that resemble vein patterns in polished jade, with particles moving through three-dimensional space and leaving luminous paths behind them. When viewers drag their cursor across the surface, they actively draw abstract flow lines that cause the mineral patterns to coalesce and crystallize in real-time. The visualization uses WebGL rendering to create a volumetric effect with glowing particle trails, while maintaining strict performance constraints to ensure smooth animation even at high detail levels. The work captures the dynamic interplay between continuous motion and static gem-like structures, presenting an immersive, interactive geological map that responds directly to viewer presence and movement.
