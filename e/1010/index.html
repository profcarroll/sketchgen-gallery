Brief

The canvas displays a circular, minimalist clock face. Two distinct hands—an hour hand and a minute hand—are centrally mounted and are rendered in a matte bronze color. Both hands feature a noticeable thickness and softly rounded tips. These hands move continuously and smoothly across the face, perpetually tracking the passage of time. The entire scene remains stationary until the inherent, autonomous movement of the hands takes over, indicating the time without any user input required.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a minimalist circular clock face with matte bronze-colored hour and minute hands. The design emphasizes clean, simple geometry with softly rounded tips on the hands for a tactile feel. Both hands move continuously and smoothly to track time automatically, without any user interaction required. The clock face is rendered using basic p5.js drawing functions—ellipses for the face and lines for the hands—with careful attention to proportional sizing and color to achieve an elegant, timeless aesthetic. The scene remains stationary except for the natural movement of the hands, which perpetually indicate the passage of time through their continuous rotation.
