This sketch renders a complex three-dimensional kinetic sculpture composed of mechanical brass splines that orbit rhythmically around a central core. The brass elements appear heavily tarnished and oxidized with deep green patinas, while the central core solidifies into a semi-viscous amber material that drips and glows. The entire assembly operates as a self-sustaining machine with elegant motion, where polished metals interact with warm luminous hues to create an intricate steampunk aesthetic. The sculpture's movement is driven by the continuous rotation of the system and individual particle oscillations, all rendered through a single continuous spline mesh connecting the orbiting elements.
