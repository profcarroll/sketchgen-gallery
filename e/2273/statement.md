This sketch depicts an underwater geological cross-section where layers of sedimentary rock interlace with intricate clockwork mechanisms shaped like fossilized seashells. The scene unfolds in a three-dimensional space rendered using WebGL, with translucent amber-hued layers representing time-accumulated deposits. Within this stratified environment, geometric gears—each one resembling a coiled spiral shell—rotate rhythmically in synchronized motion, mimicking the ebb and flow of tides. Viewers can interact by dragging their mouse to alter both the rotational speed and apparent depth of these mechanical elements, causing the patterns to compress or expand like waves rippling through sediment. The warm amber and ochre palette evokes the coloration of beeswax and ancient mineral deposits, creating an atmosphere that merges geology with mechanical artistry.
