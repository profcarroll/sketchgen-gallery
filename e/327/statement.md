This sketch depicts a whimsical nautical scene with paper-cut ships sailing across a pale blue sea, each leaving behind dissolving wake trails. The ships are rendered with rich details including wooden textures, masts, and sails, using a muted nautical color palette of browns, teals, and sun-bleached tones. Each ship moves at its own pace, slowly drifting from left to right while generating a fading trail of connected lines behind it. The continuous animation features subtle wave motion on the water surface and dynamic positioning of ships that recede into the distance, creating an immersive and ever-changing maritime landscape.
