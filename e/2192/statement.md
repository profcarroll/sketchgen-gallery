This sketch creates an endless, living cityscape composed of intricately folded origami walls that shift and move continuously. Each wall segment is a tessellated structure with deep vertical stripes in vivid red, warm ochre, bright teal, and soft cream. The walls appear to fold and crease slowly due to constant, subtle motion driven by sine wave oscillations that are independent for each element. The entire pattern is dynamic without any viewer interaction, creating an impression of a living architectural fabric where geometry shifts independently while maintaining its structural integrity. The effect emerges from the repetition of simple folded forms with carefully chosen color schemes and continuous animation parameters.
