This sketch visualizes a transformation sequence from ethereal steam to crystalline complexity. Particles begin as soft, glowing tendrils that gradually coalesce into ghostly word forms and then solidify into archaic glyphs. These glyphs eventually dissolve into particulate matter, which aggregates into transient crystal structures. The most significant addition is the formation of dendritic networks—complex branching patterns that connect crystals before they dissipate entirely. The WEBGL renderer creates a three-dimensional space where these formations drift and evolve, with particles maintaining their connections through a spatially hashed grid system for efficient interaction detection.
