This sketch presents a three-dimensional model of our solar system, rendered using p5.js's WEBGL mode for immersive depth. The Sun sits at the center, its golden glow anchoring the scene, while planets orbit in their respective paths with proportional distances and speeds. Each celestial body is rendered with a distinct hue and size, creating a visually cohesive yet varied cosmic landscape. When the viewer clicks the canvas, the system springs into motion, animating planetary orbits in real-time. The entire scene rotates slowly to offer different perspectives, enhancing the sense of being adrift in space. The visual effect emphasizes both scientific accuracy and aesthetic beauty, using lighting and color to distinguish celestial bodies and their movements within a static yet dynamic framework.
