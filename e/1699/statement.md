This sketch renders a dynamic three-dimensional model of the DNA double helix using WebGL rendering. The visualization shows the characteristic twisted ladder structure with labeled base pairs (A, T, C, G) connected by hydrogen bonds. The entire helix gently oscillates and rotates in space, demonstrating molecular motion. A key feature is the active unzipping animation that targets a specific section of the helix, showing how DNA strands separate during replication or transcription. Users can manipulate the viewing angle through mouse interaction. The implementation stays within performance constraints by avoiding excessive draw calls and efficiently managing the molecular components' positions and connections in real-time.
