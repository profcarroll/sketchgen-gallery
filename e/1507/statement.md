This sketch creates an abstract, living honeycomb pattern that evolves continuously without any user input. The composition consists of irregular polygons that breathe and shift in size, rotating slowly while transitioning through a dynamic color palette of deep reds, bright oranges, and cool blues. Each shape pulses with subtle rhythmic motion, creating a visual impression of warm, organic energy flowing through an intricate geometric structure. The constant animation gives the viewer a sense of observing a living system where temperature-like variations manifest as shifting hues and pulsing intensities across the entire canvas.
