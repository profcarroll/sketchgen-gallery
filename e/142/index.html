Brief

The canvas presents a breathtaking view of Earth, rendered in deep blues and greens, suspended within the infinite void of space. Surrounding the planet is an immense, meticulously structured field of countless satellites. These satellites do not appear random; they are grouped into several visibly distinct, graceful planes that spiral and sweep around the planet in predictable, beautiful orbits. The entire cosmic tableau slowly rotates and shifts, conveying a monumental sense of scale and continuous movement. The viewer is meant to observe this grand, unchanging mechanical ballet of light and orbital geometry, which operates autonomously without requiring any interaction or input.
