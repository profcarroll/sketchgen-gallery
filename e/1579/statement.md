This sketch creates a continuously evolving field of interconnected geometric forms that pulse and flow in three-dimensional space. Each shape distorts over time with organic motion, constantly reconfiguring its form while maintaining connections to nearby shapes through glowing lines. The system periodically releases segments from their connections, sending them drifting outward along distinct vectors before they gradually drift back into the main network. These components leave behind luminous trails that fade over time, creating an ethereal echo effect where past positions become visible ghostly imprints. The visual result is a dynamic, living network of light and form that continuously evolves through separation and reconnection events, producing an ever-shifting tapestry of motion and geometry that feels both mechanical and organic in its behavior.
