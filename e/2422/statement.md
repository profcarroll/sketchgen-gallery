This sketch visualizes a subterranean mycelial network as a flowing, pulsating field of interconnected lines and nodes. The structure emerges from the continuous motion of nodal points that expand and contract in rhythmic waves, creating organic tension throughout the system. Lines between nodes shift and distort based on their pulsation, forming a living, breathing network that evolves over time. The color palette ripples across the entire field, with hues shifting subtly to simulate the chromatic flow described in the brief. Each frame reveals an intricate web of connections that feels alive and responsive, like the migration patterns of plankton swarms or the expansion of fungal networks beneath the earth's surface.
