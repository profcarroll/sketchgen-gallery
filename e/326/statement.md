This sketch creates a mesmerizing, self-regulating architectural tapestry through the interplay of neon grids and organic particles. The composition features pulsing, undulating grids that shift in hue and form across the canvas, creating a luminous backdrop reminiscent of futuristic urban environments. Within this structured framework, thousands of subtle particles coalesce into evolving biological forms, bridging the gap between rigid geometric design and fluid organic movement. The continuous motion emerges from mathematical relationships rather than external input, offering viewers an immersive journey through the dynamic dialogue between architecture and life, structure and growth.
