This sketch creates a serene interactive experience where viewers can click anywhere on the canvas to trigger both sound and visual effects. Upon each click, a soft chime sounds immediately and a smooth ripple expands outward from the point of impact, gradually fading as it spreads across the calm surface. The visual design emphasizes flow and tranquility through gentle color transitions and controlled animation timing. The entire interface remains static between interactions, responding only to direct user gestures, creating an elegant balance between responsiveness and stillness.
