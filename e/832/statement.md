This sketch creates a dynamic, interconnected field of glowing particles that move through three-dimensional space. The particles continuously drift and bounce within the canvas boundaries, leaving trails of light as they move. As particles age and decay, they respawn in new locations, maintaining a constant flow of motion and energy across the screen. Connections form between nearby particles, creating an ever-shifting web of lines that pulse with fading intensity. The color palette shifts through hues as particles age, while their movement is guided by noise-based dynamics to create organic, unpredictable patterns. The entire system operates in real-time within a WebGL context, generating a complex, immersive visual experience without exceeding performance constraints.
