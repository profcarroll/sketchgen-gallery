This sketch depicts a serene yet dramatic pastoral landscape at sunset, capturing the essence of rolling green fields stretching toward distant misty mountains. The composition uses vertical gradient coloring to simulate the warm hues of dusk, with layered hills in the foreground rendered through subtle noise-based undulations to suggest natural terrain. Plant tufts are individually placed for texture and detail, while long shadows cast across the foreground define the topography. Distant mountains appear softened by atmospheric haze, creating a sense of depth and scale. The scene is static—no animation or motion—but the rich color palette and careful attention to light and shadow create an immersive, contemplative moment in nature's daily rhythm.
