This sketch depicts a wooden case slowly filling with metallic coins that drift down from the top. The animation simulates the continuous passage of time and steady accumulation of donations, creating an ever-growing pile at the bottom of the container. The coins are rendered as golden circles with physics-based movement including gravity, friction, and bouncing off the sides of the case. The scene unfolds automatically without any user interaction, demonstrating how simple particle systems can create compelling, organic-looking behavior. The visual effect suggests a peaceful, ongoing process rather than a chaotic event, using only basic geometric shapes and color to convey both form and materiality.
