This sketch renders an evolving three-dimensional mold structure composed of delicate mycelial filaments and embedded crystalline formations. The scene simulates organic growth through time-based animation, where mycelial segments extend and branch dynamically across the substrate while crystals slowly expand and rotate. The rendering uses WEBGL for volumetric depth and spatial complexity, creating an immersive environment that captures both the intricate surface textures of fungal networks and the geometric precision of mineral deposits. The entire composition evolves autonomously without user input, presenting a continuous visualization of biological development within a deep, dark substrate.
