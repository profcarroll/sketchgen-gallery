Brief

The canvas displays a simulated biological environment populated by several circular cells. The visualization tracks the process of mitosis, showing the distinct stages of cell division. Chromosomes appear as structured, thread-like masses that condense, align along the cell equator, and then separate into individual chromatids. These chromatids are observed moving toward opposing poles, leading to the reformation of multiple daughter cells. The entire process is a continuous, cyclical animation detailing the life cycle of the cell.
