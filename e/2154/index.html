Brief

The canvas presents a massive, cross-section view of geological strata, rendered in heavy, raw poured concrete. Layers are visible, showing immense pressure and the passage of deep time. The structure is dominated by deep, fractured fissures and displaced segments, documenting a monumental moment of catastrophic failure. The viewer observes the structural tension and displaced geometry of the earth, experiencing a visualization of massive, prehistorical geological upheaval. The piece is stationary and unreactive, presenting a monumental document of structural decay.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a monumental cross-section of geological strata rendered in raw, poured concrete textures. The composition reveals immense pressure and deep time through layered sedimentary rocks that appear fractured and displaced. Massive fissures cut through the structure, showing the catastrophic failure at a central junction where geological forces have created dramatic displacement. The viewer observes structural tension and disturbed geometry, experiencing a visualization of prehistorical upheaval. The work is deliberately still, presenting an unchanging document of geological decay and massive earth movements that occurred over eons.
