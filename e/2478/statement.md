This sketch depicts an industrial landscape where massive concrete structures undergo a continuous cycle of formation, tension, collapse, and dissolution. The monolithic forms emerge from nothingness, stretch taut under structural pressure, then gradually slump and fracture into rubble before resetting to begin again. The palette uses muted grays and earth tones to emphasize the heavy, inorganic quality of the machinery. Each structure follows a defined lifecycle that repeats autonomously across the canvas, creating a visual rhythm of creation and decay. The motion is slow and inevitable, capturing the continuous, mechanical nature of industrial processes where construction gives way to controlled demolition and rebuilding.
