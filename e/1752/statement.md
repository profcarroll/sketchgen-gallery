This sketch presents a tranquil yet dynamic scene of rhododendron blooms and foliage rendered in rich, saturated colors. The composition features hundreds of organic flower clusters that gently sway and pulse, creating an undulating effect across the canvas. A perfectly still water surface mirrors the blooms above it, doubling their vivid presence. Upon clicking anywhere on the canvas, a ripple disturbance propagates outward from the center, breaking the mirror-like reflection and sending waves of distortion across the water's surface. The visual technique uses layered ellipses to simulate bloom structure, with foliage elements extending outward from each cluster. The scene remains motionless until user interaction triggers the ripple effect, emphasizing the stillness of nature before disruption.
