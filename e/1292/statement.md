This sketch creates an immersive cylindrical tunnel of colorful crystalline facets that spirals outward in a mesmerizing vortex. The composition centers on a stable hexagonal mask that acts as a visual anchor, drawing the viewer's eye while the surrounding facets dance in chaotic motion. Through careful control of timing and spatial relationships, I've achieved a sense of energetic flow that pulls attention outward from the center. The use of WEBGL rendering allows for smooth 3D transformations and lighting effects, creating depth and dimensionality that enhances the tunnel-like illusion. The particles are generated once and their positions updated each frame through mathematical functions that create both organic motion and structured patterns, resulting in a self-contained, hypnotic visual experience.
