This sketch depicts a mechanical shredder in operation, with a stack of colorful books falling into a rotating mechanism. The scene combines several visual elements: books descend vertically through the shredder, their motion triggers glowing sparks at friction points, and steam plumes rise from the gears while fine dust particles hang suspended above the central apparatus. I used p5.js's WEBGL renderer to create a 3D environment where geometric shapes represent the mechanical components—gears with teeth, shredding plates, and books in various sizes and colors. The animation simulates physics with falling objects, particle systems for sparks and dust, and rotating mechanical parts that respond to time-based motion. The visual effect emphasizes the interaction between the books and the shredder mechanism through dynamic spark generation and the continuous motion of the gears.
