This sketch explores the concept of equilibrium and disruption through geometric forms that oscillate between stability and chaos. The composition begins with a structured field of interlocking squares, triangles, and hexagons arranged in a regular grid pattern, representing a state of balance. As the animation progresses, this equilibrium shatters into a high-contrast explosion of fragmented shapes, each distortion growing more intense until they begin to reorganize into a new, visibly different patterned arrangement. The color palette shifts subtly throughout these transitions—from muted harmonious tones during stasis to high-energy contrasts during the collapse—visually representing the tension and release inherent in the process. The movement is organic yet controlled, showing how complex systems can transition through disorder back into a new form of order.
