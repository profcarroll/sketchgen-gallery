This sketch depicts an impossible megalith that grows upward through stacked geometric segments, defying gravity and accumulating material in a continuous, ascending cascade. The structure is composed of chalk-like blocks rendered in a cool, reflective palette that suggests both the fragility and permanence of architectural ambition. Viewer interaction toggles the direction of growth—pushing the structure to ascend or descend—and clicking introduces new segments that peel away from the base, adding to the accumulating mass. The visual effect emphasizes an ongoing, self-reinforcing process of construction and deconstruction, where each action perpetually reshapes the form through the interplay of physics and aesthetic form.
