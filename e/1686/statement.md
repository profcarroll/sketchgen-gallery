This sketch depicts a continuous cross-sectional view of water with defined rhythmic wave patterns. The composition consists of overlapping horizontal bands of color that move in synchronized, cyclical motion to simulate the ebb and flow of tides. Each band oscillates vertically according to its own phase while also shifting horizontally to create visible wave crests and troughs. The visual effect is achieved through layered sine wave functions applied to the vertical positioning and horizontal displacement of each band, creating a kinetic representation of water movement that evolves continuously without user input.
