This sketch creates a dynamic interplay between smooth, flowing particles and sharp crystalline shards within a hexagonal boundary. The particles are influenced by the shards' forces, creating tension lines that pull the curves taut where they intersect with the geometric structures. The visualization shows a constantly shifting pattern of opposing forces—particles flowing smoothly while being pulled and constrained by rigid, angular elements. The system demonstrates emergent behavior through simple physics interactions, creating an intricate kaleidoscopic effect that evolves organically without external input.
