This sketch creates an abstract, architectural pattern of layered neon lines that evoke the structure of 1920s Bauhaus design. The composition features a complex branching network of glowing lines that pulse and flow with cyclical motion, resembling petrified internal coral or energy fluctuations. Through continuous geometric animation in 3D space, the piece visualizes an abstract field of movement and structure, where the entire pattern evolves autonomously without user input. The design balances mathematical precision with organic flow, creating a sense of perpetual energy moving through a suspended, structural web.
