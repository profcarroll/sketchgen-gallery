This sketch presents an interactive periodic table visualization where chemical elements are arranged in a structured grid. Each element is represented by a colored box displaying its atomic symbol and number. The entire chart gently animates with a subtle breathing effect, creating an ambient, living quality to the display. When a viewer clicks on any element, it slightly expands and its background color shifts to highlight the selection, revealing detailed information about that specific element. The design maintains a clean, organized layout while providing engaging interactivity through hover effects and click responses. The animation remains subtle and continuous without requiring user input, creating an environment where viewers can explore the periodic table at their own pace.
