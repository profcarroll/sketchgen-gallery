This sketch creates a dark, smoky void filled with rotating metallic shards that have jagged, chrome-like surfaces. Each shard is composed of randomly generated polygonal shapes with sharp edges that reflect deep emerald light. The shards float slowly through space, rotating at different rates and amplitudes, creating an immersive, almost crystalline effect. The motion responds to audio input, with the intensity of sound affecting both the movement and rotation speeds of the shards. The visual texture is achieved through careful use of lighting and color in a WebGL environment, giving each shard a reflective quality that makes them appear metallic and three-dimensional despite being constructed from simple geometric forms.
