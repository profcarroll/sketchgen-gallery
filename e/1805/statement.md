This sketch depicts a continuous, ethereal dance of steam and form. Soft luminous tendrils swirl and flow across the canvas in a hypnotic pattern driven by noise-based movement. As these particles coalesce, they form archaic, glowing glyphs that stabilize briefly before beginning to dissolve. The dissolution process is not a simple vanishing—it transforms into glowing particulate matter that drifts away and fades into the void. This visual cycle of formation, illumination, and decay creates an immersive atmosphere where the ephemeral nature of existence is rendered through flowing, luminous particles and the organic shapes they briefly assemble.
