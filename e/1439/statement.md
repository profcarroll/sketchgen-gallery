This sketch creates a luminous, glitching vector grid that evokes the aesthetic of 1980s digital art. The grid consists of interconnected points that pulse with varying intensities, creating a field of neon light that shifts over time. Periodically, the structural integrity of the grid breaks down in random bursts—lines fragment and flicker before snapping back into place—creating an effect of energy disruption and restoration. The visual rhythm mimics digital interference patterns and network instability, while maintaining an underlying geometric order that gives it a sense of controlled chaos.
