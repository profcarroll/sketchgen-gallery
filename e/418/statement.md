This sketch visualizes the interplay between light and architecture through dynamically cast rays that intersect with a rigid geometric structure. As the light source moves in a circular path, its rays illuminate surfaces and create complex shadow patterns that shift across the building's planes. The visualization emphasizes precise intersection points where rays meet structural elements, rendering both the pathways of light and the abstract geometry of shadow boundaries. The scene uses WebGL for 3D rendering to capture the spatial relationships between light source, structure, and projected illumination.
