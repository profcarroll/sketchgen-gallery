This sketch creates an immersive, dreamlike environment where luminous forms emerge from deep darkness. The scene features animated glowing shapes that pulse and shift in subtle harmony with time. These forms cast dynamic neon shadows that ripple across the canvas, creating an ever-evolving landscape of light and shadow. The interplay between the solid forms and their ephemeral shadows suggests a fluid, otherworldly space where architecture is defined by illumination itself. The gentle motion and color shifts create a hypnotic effect that draws viewers into this synthetic reality, where light and darkness dance together in a continuous, evolving composition.
