This sketch visualizes the propagation of information through a field of ionized gas, inspired by the cascading energy of falling dominoes. The scene begins with a line of dominoes that sequentially fall, initiating rhythmic waves of energy. These waves manifest as swirling, expanding patterns of colored particles that represent the dissipation of ionized gas. The simulation captures both the structured nature of the initial trigger and the chaotic, cyclical movement of information as it spreads through the field. The viewer observes a continuous interplay between order (the dominoes) and chaos (the gas particles), demonstrating how localized events can generate complex, flowing patterns across a contained space.
