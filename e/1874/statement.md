This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of twelve circular discs suspended in a rhythmic formation, each swinging with its own unique oscillation pattern. The discs move in synchronized yet independent motions, occasionally colliding with their neighbors in a way that briefly alters their trajectories. The entire array drifts slowly across the canvas while gently swaying up and down, creating an organic, flowing motion. Periodically, the pendulums lock into temporary rhythmic patterns, demonstrating synchronization before breaking apart again. The visual effect is enhanced by colored trails that fade over time, creating a sense of continuous movement and interaction between the elements. The collision physics add an element of unpredictability to the otherwise harmonious system.
