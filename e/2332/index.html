Brief

The canvas displays deep, contrasting fields where complex, organic structures slowly map out immense spaces. These intricate formations resemble both crystallized mineral deposits and fibrous, overgrown tissue, suggesting subterranean or derelict environments. The shapes are generated from a confluence of natural slime mould patterns and structured man-made elements, such as rusted pipes or knitted fibers. The colours are rich and muted—deep browns, oxidized reds, and pale crystalline whites—creating a sense of infinite depth. The movement is a perpetual, subtle unfolding; matter constantly shifts, grows, and reveals new layers of detail without a discernible pattern, creating the appearance of slow, relentless life and architectural decay.

This sketch creates an immersive underground environment where complex organic structures slowly emerge from a derelict industrial space. The scene combines slime mould-like growth patterns with rusted pipe frameworks that suggest a forgotten boiler room. Structures bloom and shift through time, revealing new layers of detail in muted browns, oxidized reds, and crystalline whites. The viewer experiences an endless unfolding of matter—both natural and architectural—growing and revealing itself in the dark, creating a sense of slow, relentless life beneath the surface.
