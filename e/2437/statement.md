This sketch visualizes an electrocardiogram as a continuous, glowing neon trace moving across a dark background. The line depicts the irregular, chaotic contours of heartbeats with sharp spikes and dips rendered in vibrant electric blue. The core visual element is a single luminous path that scrolls steadily from left to right, simulating the persistent passage of time and life force measured across the screen. The trace maintains its own self-contained motion, creating an effect of observing a vital rhythm constantly flowing through space.
