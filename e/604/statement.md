This sketch creates a mesmerizing musical backdrop with floating staves that serve as a translucent foundation for animated musical notes. The notes drift and float across the screen in graceful trajectories, pulsing with glowing intensity as they move. The entire composition responds dynamically to ambient sound through a microphone input, causing the notes to change their speed and luminosity based on audio levels. The visual effect captures the rhythmic flow of music through abstract, flowing particles that maintain a sense of musicality while reacting organically to environmental sounds.
