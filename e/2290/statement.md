This sketch presents a static geological cross-section diagram rendered in the aesthetic of mid-century scientific illustration. The composition features horizontal strata layers in various mineral tones—browns, grays, reds, blues, and yellows—evoking the appearance of a vintage pen plotter drawing. Each layer contains subtle textural patterns like stripes, dots, or waves to simulate geological formations and mineral variations. The piece captures the scientific precision and visual clarity typical of 1960s earth science publications, rendered on aged paper with handwritten annotations that add authenticity to the historical presentation.
