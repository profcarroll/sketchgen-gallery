This sketch presents a diagrammatic geological cross-section inspired by Bauhaus principles, depicting repeating strata of industrial materials. The composition shows a progression from structured concrete layers to organic sediment, using muted colors in cool grays, rusty browns, and earthy ochres. Through subtle textural variations and layered patterns, the piece illustrates deep time's passage through fixed, diagrammatic planes, capturing the transition from man-made elements toward geological residue in an abstract, geometric form.
