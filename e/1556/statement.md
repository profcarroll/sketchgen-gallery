This sketch depicts an abstract ice rink scene where figure skaters move in fluid, continuous motion. The skaters are represented as glowing points that leave behind ethereal trails in a palette of emerald green and copper hues. The trails fade over time, creating a sense of momentum and flow as the skaters glide across the ice surface. The movement is smooth and organic, with subtle bouncing physics that simulate the natural dynamics of skating. The entire scene pulses with a gentle luminosity, evoking the mesmerizing quality of light on ice during a performance.
