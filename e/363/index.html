Brief

The canvas presents a vertical, three-dimensional cityscape defined by geometric forms of concrete and steel. The structure begins as a foundational base, ready to grow upwards. To erect the skyscraper, the viewer must click anywhere on the canvas. Each click raises the current structure by placing the next segment, incrementally increasing the building’s height and defining its overall profile. The pattern and scale of the skyscraper adjust with every input, making the building process observable as a slow, deliberate ascent against the canvas background.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a vertical, three-dimensional cityscape constructed from geometric forms of concrete and steel. The viewer begins with a foundational base and can click anywhere on the canvas to incrementally build a skyscraper by adding new segments. Each click triggers the placement of a new building segment above the previous one, creating a slow, deliberate ascent against the canvas background. The structure grows upward through a series of stacked boxes, each with randomized dimensions, creating an evolving urban skyline. The visual design uses a muted color palette to emphasize the geometric forms and employs perspective grid lines to anchor the three-dimensional space, making the building process observable as a continuous, methodical growth.
