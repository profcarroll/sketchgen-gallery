This sketch creates an immersive, autonomous environment of drifting steam and mist. Through thousands of subtly animated particles that move with organic noise-based trajectories, it simulates the gentle, cyclical motion of vapor. The particles fade in and out, coalescing into ephemeral shapes that momentarily resemble words before dissolving back into the mist. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of particle physics, perlin noise for natural movement, and careful control of opacity and lifespan to create a continuous, hypnotic flow of form and absence. No external input or interaction is required—the scene evolves entirely on its own, maintaining a peaceful, contemplative rhythm.
