This sketch visualizes a dynamic interplay between organic growth and mechanical precision, depicting a self-generating tapestry of mineral formations that also follow flocking behaviors. The scene features crystalline structures that pulse and expand in rhythmic waves, mimicking both natural geological processes and intricate clockwork mechanisms. Floating particles move according to flocking rules—separation, alignment, and cohesion—creating emergent patterns that weave through the static crystal forms. The viewer observes an evolving landscape where rigid geometric rules generate fluid, organic motion, suggesting a hidden mechanical force driving continuous expansion and connection between elements.
