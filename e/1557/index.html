Brief

The canvas displays a massive, complex webbing of overlapping wires and thick cables. The physical structure of the tangle remains constant, forming an intricate, chaotic backdrop. Superimposed upon this static mess is a network of defined, crystalline grids. Along these sharp, visible paths, bright light pulses flow in rhythmic, pulsing waves. The energy appears to travel along specific, predetermined routes, creating a sense of contained, organized motion despite the overwhelming entanglement of the wires. The entire system glows and animates continuously.
