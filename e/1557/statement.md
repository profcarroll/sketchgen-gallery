This sketch depicts a complex, layered network of interwoven cables and structured grids. The background presents an entangled web of dark, organic cable paths that crisscross the canvas in chaotic patterns. Superimposed on this tangle are sharp, crystalline grid structures that define clear, geometric routes. Bright, pulsing particles travel along these predefined paths, creating rhythmic waves of light that contrast with the static, tangled mass beneath. The visual effect suggests organized energy flowing through a chaotic system—motion contained within disorder, with the vibrant particles acting as luminous pulses that animate the otherwise inert structure.
