This sketch creates a digital landscape of technological detritus—abandoned pixels arranged in a sparse grid. Like biological decay, vibrant moss slowly spreads across the canvas, reclaiming each colored square. The moss grows organically with subtle movement and randomness, while simultaneously fading the underlying pixels it touches. This process transforms the rigid, artificial structure into something organic and evolving, suggesting how nature reclaims technological artifacts over time. The result is a gentle, continuous animation that shows both the persistence of digital remnants and their gradual dissolution.
