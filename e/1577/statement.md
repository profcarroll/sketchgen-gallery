This sketch presents an architectural illusion of a vast, liminal space defined by monumental curved pathways. The work consists of multiple steel sheets arranged in a circular arc, creating a hypnotic sense of continuous flow that abruptly interrupts into a perpendicular segment. Using WebGL rendering, the composition employs carefully calculated geometric forms to suggest the weight and texture of rusted metal while maintaining a static, contemplative quality. The visual tension between smooth curvature and sharp transition creates an unsettling disruption that emphasizes the viewer's position within this architectural void.
