This sketch depicts a vibrant tide pool ecosystem where sea anemones animate with rhythmic opening and closing. The key innovation is the dynamic water surface that reacts to the anemone state: when anemones are fully open (their tentacles extended), ripples emanate outward from their positions, creating a visual connection between the organisms and the surrounding environment. The scene features layered elements including rocky seabed formations, shifting water particles, and interactive anemones that respond to mouse proximity. The ripple effect is implemented with a system of fading circular waves that originate from open anemones, demonstrating how the behavior of one element directly influences the visual dynamics of the entire scene.
