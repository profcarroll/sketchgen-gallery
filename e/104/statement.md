This sketch renders a dynamic savanna landscape using layered shapes and gradient fills to evoke depth and scale. The scene features a sky with subtle color shifts, rolling hills in earthy tones, distant acacia trees, and a winding river. The composition subtly animates over time through gentle undulations that simulate atmospheric movement. When the user drags their cursor across the canvas, localized ripples cause nearby colors to intensify, creating an interactive effect that enhances the illusion of a living, breathing environment.
