This sketch depicts a vast deep-sea environment populated by crystal formations and shimmering mineral lattices. A sweeping beam of light cycles across the scene, illuminating and activating the crystalline structures in rhythmic pulses. The visualization emphasizes the grand scale of geological activity through a combination of glowing crystals and scattered particles that respond to the directional force of the light beam. The technique uses WebGL rendering for efficient 3D drawing with batched point clouds and simplified crystal geometry to maintain performance while conveying the immersive, monumental quality of the underwater landscape.
