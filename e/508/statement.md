This sketch presents a geometric meditation on the interplay between solid architecture and fluid nature. Through the use of WEBGL rendering, it creates a series of monolithic pyramidal forms that emerge from an animated water surface. The water displays dynamic caustic patterns generated through thousands of individual particles, creating a constantly shifting refraction effect that mirrors and transforms the geometric structures above. The viewer witnesses a tension between the immovable, precise geometry and the flowing, luminous water — where light and shadow create a mesmerizing spectacle of refracted color and form. The entire composition exists in a state of perpetual motion, with the water surface continuously evolving while the pyramids remain anchored in their geometric precision.
