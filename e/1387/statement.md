This sketch renders a cosmic vista of the Milky Way using layered visual effects to evoke depth and motion. The background features a nebula rendered with noise-based gradients in indigo, purple, and deep blue hues, subtly shifting over time to simulate drifting gas and dust. Thousands of stars are scattered across the canvas, each twinkling independently at different rates to create an organic shimmer effect. Occasional bursts of light—supernovas—flash dramatically across the scene before fading, adding moments of intense energy against the serene backdrop. The entire composition moves slowly through gentle motion, suggesting vast cosmic time and space, all without any external input beyond the initial canvas setup.
