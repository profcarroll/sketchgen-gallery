This sketch renders a steampunk kinetic sculpture centered around a molten bronze core that pulses with internal currents of vibrant fire. Multiple curving splines orbit the core in a rotating cubic framework, each featuring an oxidized copper patina and patches of sea glass coloration. The entire structure slowly rotates, creating a mesmerizing, continuous motion that fills the canvas with intricate, glowing geometry. The visualization uses WEBGL rendering to achieve depth and complex 3D transformations while maintaining performance within the specified frame budget constraints.
