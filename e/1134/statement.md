This sketch depicts a continuous sawmill process in 3D space where cylindrical logs roll toward a spinning blade, are sliced into rectangular planks, and then fall to form a neat pile. The animation demonstrates physics-based motion with rolling logs, cutting mechanics, and falling planks that settle into organized stacks. Using p5.js WEBGL renderer, the scene creates a convincing industrial workflow with realistic 3D transformations, rotation, and gravity simulation. The cycle repeats endlessly as new logs enter the mill, maintaining constant visual motion without any user interaction required.
