This sketch depicts an immense clockwork automaton assembled entirely from taut brass gears and coiled rubber bands, with bioluminescent crystalline structures pulsing throughout the mechanism. The viewer observes a complex interplay of mechanical motion and ethereal light: gears rotate slowly in synchronized patterns while rubber band coils cast glowing shadows across the metallic surfaces. Hundreds of tiny crystals pulse with rhythmic bioluminescence, creating a continuous, hypnotic rhythm that animates the entire structure. The scene is dominated by the constant flux of luminous energy flowing through the exposed workings, giving the impression of a living, breathing mechanical organism powered by its own inner light.
