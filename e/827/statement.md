This sketch creates a dynamic, evolving system of glowing cells that emerge and fade over time. Each cell has a lifecycle, gradually decaying until it dissipates into a luminous trail that persists briefly before disappearing. As cells die, they leave behind glowing echoes that form intricate networks of connections between nearby living cells. The entire field shifts constantly through random motion and natural birth/death cycles, creating an ever-changing lattice of light that pulses with organic rhythm. Viewers witness a subtle ebb and flow as the system balances new emergence against fading decay, producing a mesmerizing display of interconnected luminosity.
