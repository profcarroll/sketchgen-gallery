This sketch visualizes an infinite, glowing lattice structure that pulses and shifts continuously. The canvas is filled with thousands of luminous lines connecting nodes in a grid pattern, creating an interconnected circuit board effect viewed from above. The entire network flows and ripples with time, generating complex visual motion through color shifting, pulsing connections, and subtle camera movement. Nodes glow softly in vibrant hues while lines pulse with energy, producing an immersive, dynamic visualization that feels alive and constantly evolving without any external input.
