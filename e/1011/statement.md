This sketch creates a mesmerizing depth effect using overlapping colored layers that move in synchronized wave patterns. Each horizontal layer moves independently with varying phases and amplitudes, creating a sense of fluid motion reminiscent of deep-sea currents. The rich, saturated colors shift across the canvas in a way that suggests underwater depth and movement. The waves maintain consistent amplitude throughout, giving the impression of structured, rhythmic flow rather than random motion. The technique uses sine wave functions to generate smooth, continuous undulations across each layer, with multiple frequencies combined to create complex yet uniform patterns. The color palette shifts smoothly through the hue spectrum, enhancing the sense of depth and movement as layers interact with one another.
