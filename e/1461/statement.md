This sketch depicts a whimsical underwater scene where paper-cut ships drift across a translucent ocean surface. Each vessel shows signs of advanced decay through visible patina effects, moss growth, and water-damaged tears that slowly detach from the main structure. The ships leave behind intricate crystalline salt and mineral deposits in their wakes, which gradually dissolve into the environment. The deterioration process is physically realized through animated tears that detach and drift away, as well as particles that form from the ship's disintegration. The visual effect combines organic decay with geometric paper-cut aesthetics, creating a delicate balance between fragility and persistence in this aqueous world where time has worn away the edges of these vessels.
