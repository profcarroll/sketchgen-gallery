This sketch creates a retro-styled, dynamic road perspective using p5.js in WEBGL mode. Viewers experience a sense of motion as the road curves and changes direction beneath them, simulating the feeling of driving through a countryside landscape. The viewer controls steering with left/right arrow keys, which smoothly curves the perspective to create an immersive effect. The road features lane markings, roadside details, and branching side paths that give depth and a sense of continuous travel. The implementation uses a fixed number of road segments that move toward the viewer, creating an illusion of motion while maintaining performance within strict frame budget constraints. All visual elements are drawn in real-time with no external libraries, relying solely on p5.js's WEBGL capabilities to achieve the dynamic perspective effect.
