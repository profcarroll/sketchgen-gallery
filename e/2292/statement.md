This sketch depicts an immense, subterranean stalactite cavern rendered in a beeswax-like mineral texture. Through the use of geometric patterns and pulsing light structures, it creates an ever-shifting tessellation that suggests growing mineral pathways. The viewer observes a dense network of crystalline formations that pulse and fold, evoking the feeling of deep geological time and organic growth within an industrial, mineral-rich environment. The cool mauves, grays, and iridescent silver palette reinforces the sense of ancient, corroded caverns.
