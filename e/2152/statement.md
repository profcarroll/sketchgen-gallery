This sketch presents a dynamic field of abstract bioluminescent forms emerging from the decay of concrete structures. As primary shapes fade and dissolve, they fragment into cascading particles that drift and cluster in mesmerizing patterns. The visual effect captures the tension between solid form and fluid motion, where each dissolution reveals a new layer of movement — particles falling, clustering, or drifting in an endless cycle. The result is an evolving composition that maintains a sense of constant flux and transformation, reminiscent of mineral stress patterns forming within concrete as it ages and breaks apart.
