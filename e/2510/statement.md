This sketch portrays an underwater ecosystem where ancient petrified mangrove roots form a stable foundation against a dark ocean backdrop. Superimposed on this geological base are glowing lines that pulse and shift rhythmically, mimicking the behavior of biological neural networks or circuitry. The animation is driven by an invisible pendulum that creates a steady, hypnotic motion—each line responds to this rhythmic force as it moves through space, creating a visual metaphor for deep-sea bioluminescence interacting with timeless geological structures. The interplay between organic and geometric elements suggests the convergence of life and time within an oceanic environment.
