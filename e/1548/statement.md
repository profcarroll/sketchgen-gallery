This sketch creates an evolving field of fluid, oceanic forms that drift and interact within a dark space. The shapes—gentle spheres moving with slow, rhythmic motion—glide across the canvas like floating elements of water or light. When two forms cross paths, they generate a transient bloom of luminous energy that leaves behind shimmering trails. These trails gradually fade out, creating a sense of continuous transformation and flow. The visual rhythm emerges from the subtle interplay between the drifting forms and their interactions, evoking the slow, hypnotic motion of deep ocean currents or atmospheric phenomena. The composition is dynamic yet serene, filled with an underlying pulse that suggests life and movement without becoming chaotic.
