Brief

The canvas displays complex, glowing geometric visualizations consisting of interconnected planes, lines, and nodes. These elements are locked in a continuous, subtle, and evolving motion, including slow rotation and periodic changes in intensity. The system emphasizes the glowing glows concentrated at the intersections of the planes and lines, while the connecting lines pulse dramatically whenever two planes pass near each other. Overlaid on this visualization are two separate, legible blocks of broadcast typography—"You're watching" and "sketchgen TV"—which slowly and independently drift across the screen.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This visualization presents a dynamic, glowing geometric space where interconnected planes, lines, and nodes create an evolving pattern of light and motion. The system uses rotating transparent planes that subtly shift position over time, with wireframe outlines that emphasize their structure. Glowing points appear at the intersections of these elements, creating a luminous network effect. Connecting lines pulse dramatically when planes approach each other, adding rhythmic intensity to the composition. Two typographic elements—"You're watching" and "sketchgen TV"—drift independently across the screen in synchronized but distinct motions, maintaining legibility while contributing to the overall sense of movement and broadcast identity. The entire scene operates within a 3D space using p5.js WEBGL mode, ensuring depth and perspective are maintained throughout the continuous animation.
