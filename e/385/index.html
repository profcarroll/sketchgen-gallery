Brief

The viewer is placed inside a monumental, dynamically shifting architectural structure. The space is defined by colossal geometric planes that exhibit constant, subtle warping and rippling motions. Running through the structure are bright emerald green arterial seams of glowing energy, which pulse with visible, radiating light waves. These energy pulses cause the surrounding crystalline reflections to appear deeply imprinted across the geometric surfaces, mapping the light's movement and transforming the static planes into a rhythmically charged, luminous dream state.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive, dynamically shifting architectural space using particle systems and pulsing energy fields. The viewer experiences a vast geometric structure where thousands of glowing particles move in subtle wave-like motions, creating a sense of continuous flow and transformation. Emerald green energy seams pulse rhythmically across the space, their light waves rippling through the environment and casting luminous reflections on surrounding surfaces. The piece explores themes of architectural fluidity and luminous dream states through carefully controlled motion and color, building an atmosphere of constant yet gentle change that transforms static geometry into a living, breathing space.
