This sketch creates an immersive, ever-shifting architectural environment where geometric planes undulate and flow with subtle, hypnotic motions. The viewer is placed within a space defined by colossal crystalline structures that pulse with internal energy. Emerald green pathways of light ripple through the architecture, casting glowing waves that transform the static surfaces into a rhythmically charged, luminous dream state. The interplay between the warped planes and the radiant energy paths creates an overwhelming sense of volumetric movement and depth, drawing the viewer into a mesmerizing architectural symphony.
