This sketch depicts a mechanical shredder consuming a stack of books in an academic setting. The composition features a tall, precarious tower of brightly colored books gradually shrinking and falling as they're pulled into the machine's large mouth-like aperture. The consuming process is animated through a rhythmic opening and closing of the shredder's mouth, synchronized with rotating metallic gears positioned on the left side of the canvas. Books are rendered as simple rectangles that shrink in scale and fall vertically as they approach the shredder, creating an effect of physical destruction and consumption. The muted academic background of the library setting contrasts with the aggressive motion of the mechanical device, emphasizing the theme of knowledge being consumed by technology. The animation proceeds automatically without user input, showing the continuous motion of the machine consuming its target through a series of frame-by-frame transformations.
