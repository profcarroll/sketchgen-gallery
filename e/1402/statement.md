This sketch implements a cellular automaton simulation on a grid of colored squares, where each cell transitions between dormant, transitioning, and active states based on its neighbors. The system evolves autonomously, creating ripple-like patterns that spread across the canvas. When clicked, users can influence the simulation by setting initial active cells at the click location, which then propagate through the grid according to defined transition rules. The visualization shows a dynamic interplay between order and chaos, mimicking organic life-like behavior.
