This sketch depicts an immersive tide pool environment where animated anemones react to mouse proximity by opening and closing, creating dynamic visual effects that influence the surrounding water. The scene features textured rocks scattered across a sandy seabed, with water particles flowing realistically beneath the surface. When users move their mouse near the anemones, they respond by partially opening or closing, which affects the reflected sunlight intensity on the water's surface. The visualization uses layered drawing techniques to create depth and motion, with color and transparency effects that make the underwater world feel alive and interactive.
