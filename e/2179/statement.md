This sketch visualizes an evolving RNA network as a dynamic system of glowing nodes and connections. The scene features thousands of particles that continuously shift and reform, creating a hypnotic pattern of growth, collapse, and reorganization. The visualization employs a spatial hashing technique to efficiently manage particle interactions, preventing performance issues from all-pairs calculations. When users click anywhere on the canvas, a ripple effect is triggered that accelerates the system's evolution and illuminates new structural pathways, demonstrating how localized energy input can dramatically influence the entire network's behavior.
