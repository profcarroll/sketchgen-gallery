This sketch renders a serene yet dramatic pastoral scene under intense sunlight. The composition uses layered horizontal bands to depict rolling green fields that gradually transition into distant mountains and a blue sky. By employing color gradients, subtle texture points, and exaggerated shadows, the work establishes strong depth and contrast. The sun is positioned high in the sky, casting long shadows across the foreground, while atmospheric haze softens the distant horizon. This technique creates an immersive landscape that feels both tranquil and dynamic, capturing the essence of a sun-drenched countryside with rich visual detail.
