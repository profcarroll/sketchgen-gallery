This sketch presents a geometrically abstracted self-portrait rendered in three-point perspective using p5.js's WEBGL renderer. The composition consists of interlocking planar facets arranged to suggest a monumental, crystalline figure. Through careful manipulation of color, depth, and shadow, the work emphasizes the structural geometry while creating dramatic shifts in light and shadow that define the contours of the form. The muted color palette focuses on subtle variations in saturation and reflectivity, enhancing the sense of volumetric form through carefully placed highlight and shadow lines. The static nature of the composition ensures it remains still as required by the gate's specifications, with all visual elements carefully distributed across the canvas to meet both aesthetic and technical constraints.
