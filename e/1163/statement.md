A continuous EKG waveform pulses across a static background, with each heartbeat influencing its own color. As the green trace moves from left to right, it morphs through hues of blue, green, and red—not just to mimic medical data but to visualize the vital signs of the canvas itself. The motion is steady, the rhythm mechanical, yet the color shifts breathe with an implied life, turning diagnostic data into an abstract, glowing pulse.
