This sketch depicts a cosmic subterranean environment where energy solidifies into mineral formations. Through the WEBGL renderer, I've created an immersive scene with slowly evolving crystal structures that pulse rhythmically as they grow and mature. The background consists of floating particles that move in harmony with the crystal formations, creating a sense of deep space movement. New crystals spawn periodically, growing from base structures into complex geometric forms with branching extensions. The color palette uses deep blues and purples to represent the void, while the crystals glow with luminous hues that pulse and shift over time, suggesting the slow transformation of cosmic energy into solid mineral deposits. The entire scene evolves organically without external input, creating a continuous visual narrative of geological formation under distant cosmic influence.
