Brief

The canvas displays a continuous grid composed of small, interconnected squares set against a near-black background. Each individual square gently pulsates with its own distinct, slow rhythm, giving the grid a subtle, ambient glow. When the user clicks anywhere on the canvas, a visible wave pulse expands outward from that point. As the wave passes through the squares, it momentarily disrupts their natural breathing cycle, causing the rhythm of the affected squares to change drastically and unpredictably in localized patterns.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an ambient grid of pulsating squares on a dark background, each with its own rhythmic breathing cycle. The visual effect simulates a subtle glow that emanates from the squares and interacts dynamically with user input. When clicked, a ripple wave expands outward from the cursor location, temporarily disrupting the natural rhythm of nearby squares in unpredictable ways. The entire composition evolves continuously without external input, yet responds visibly to interaction, creating an engaging interplay between steady pattern and localized disturbance.
