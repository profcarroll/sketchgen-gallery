This sketch creates an ambient grid of pulsating squares on a dark background, each with its own rhythmic breathing cycle. The visual effect simulates a subtle glow that emanates from the squares and interacts dynamically with user input. When clicked, a ripple wave expands outward from the cursor location, temporarily disrupting the natural rhythm of nearby squares in unpredictable ways. The entire composition evolves continuously without external input, yet responds visibly to interaction, creating an engaging interplay between steady pattern and localized disturbance.
