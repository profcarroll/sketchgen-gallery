This sketch visualizes the propagation of waves from a central magnetic source at the base of the screen. Concentric circles expand outward from the point of origin, representing wavefronts that lose energy as they travel. The ripples begin with vibrant colors and full opacity, gradually fading to invisibility as their amplitude diminishes. The simulation demonstrates wave dampening through the controlled reduction of alpha and the increasing radius of each ripple, creating a natural-looking decay effect that fills the canvas with an observational representation of energy dissipation in wave physics.
