This sketch creates a dynamic field of compass-like pointers that respond to user interaction. Initially, the pointers slowly drift toward a shared north direction, creating an organic, flowing motion across the canvas. When the cursor enters the field, individual pointers immediately orient themselves toward the cursor's position, creating a responsive and visually engaging effect. As the cursor moves, the pointers follow with smooth transitions, and when the cursor leaves, they gradually return to their collective drift pattern. The visual result is a constantly shifting landscape of directional intent that demonstrates both individual responsiveness and group coherence, using minimal but effective visual elements to convey complex motion dynamics.
