The sketch creates a deep indigo field with layered EKG waveforms in varying copper tones—from warm gold through bronze to icy blue. Each ridge of glacial formations casts a distinct colored trace across the canvas, their hues shifting naturally as they intersect and layer. The visual suggests both medical monitoring equipment and geological strata, frozen in a moment of continuous observation. The copper wire lace effect emerges from the intersection of multiple sine-wave layers, each rendered with a slightly different hue to create depth and the impression of intricate metalwork against the dark field.
