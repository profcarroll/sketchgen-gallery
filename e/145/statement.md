This sketch creates an abstract visualization of market volatility through dynamic geometric forms that continuously evolve. The composition features fluctuating elliptical lines that move and bounce across the canvas, representing the unpredictable nature of financial markets. Scattered throughout are red and green nodes of varying sizes and irregular shapes, symbolizing market events or data points. The simulation runs entirely autonomously without user interaction, with all elements continuously updating their positions and properties to create a sense of ongoing financial activity and volatility. The visual rhythm mimics the unpredictable oscillations of stock market movements through pure geometric motion and color contrast.
