This sketch simulates a drone fly-through of an abandoned brutalist college campus. The viewer navigates through stark concrete architecture defined by angular, monolithic forms that dominate the landscape. The scene is softened by vibrant green weeds pushing through cracks in walkways and foundations, creating a contrast between harsh geometric structures and organic growth. The continuous lateral and depth movement of the drone creates an immersive exploration experience, with the camera drifting smoothly through the deserted grounds while maintaining visual coherence between architectural elements and natural disruption.
