This sketch creates an immersive underwater environment where aquamarine light fields pulse and shift through space, intersecting with reflective sculptural forms. The glowing particles float in a dark oceanic void, responding dynamically to the luminous fields that create a sense of movement and depth. The polished obsidian forms are rendered as perfectly smooth black spheres that reflect the ambient light, creating stark contrasts between the ethereal glow of the light fields and the deep, flawless darkness of the surrounding structures. The scene is animated with subtle camera movement and particle physics that give the impression of floating through an alien aquatic realm where light and shadow dance in perpetual motion.
