This sketch depicts the slow, methodical growth of a fungal mycelium network spreading through compressed earth strata. The organic branching filaments emerge from random starting points and grow outward in sinuous paths, guided by noise-based direction changes that simulate the mycelium's response to environmental factors. The layered brown and gray geological strata provide the dense substrate through which the mycelium slowly penetrates and spreads, with each filament's growth rate and path determined by a unique noise function. The viewer observes the patient, persistent expansion of this underground network as it gradually consumes and transforms its surrounding mineral environment over time.
