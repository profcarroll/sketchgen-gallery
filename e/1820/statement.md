This sketch creates a vibrant neon grid field that pulses with rhythmic energy. The canvas displays an organized lattice of magenta and cyan lines that subtly wave and pulse in unison, forming a structured yet fluid visual rhythm. Over time, periodic distortions manifest as wave-like bursts that travel vertically through the grid — momentarily breaking and reconfiguring the vector lines before settling back into their electric structure. The effect is both hypnotic and dynamic, evoking a sense of continuous, glowing motion across an otherwise ordered field.
