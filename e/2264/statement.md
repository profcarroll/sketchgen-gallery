This sketch visualizes the erratic flow of liquid mercury and beeswax as a branching, crystalline pattern that pulses with an irregular heartbeat rhythm. The system begins with a single central vein that spreads outward through random directional changes and spontaneous branching. Each vein leaves a glowing trail that fades gradually, creating a network of luminous lines that contract and expand in rhythmic waves. The organic motion mimics the unpredictable flow of liquid mercury while the branching structure evokes the growth patterns of mineral veins. The fluorescent blue color scheme and pulsing effect emphasize the volatile, living quality of the pattern as it weaves through space in sharp, rhythmic spikes.
