This sketch creates a continuous scrolling heartbeat visualization on a black canvas. A series of glowing green or red circles move steadily from right to left, simulating the rhythmic spikes and dips of a heartbeat. The visualization responds to user interaction: clicking toggles the color between neon green and emergency red, while dragging vertically adjusts the height of the electrical spikes. The effect is achieved through a particle system where each heartbeat element is updated independently, creating a smooth, organic motion that mimics cardiac rhythms.
