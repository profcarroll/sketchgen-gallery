This sketch creates a mesmerizing lattice pattern using intersecting lines that subtly shift over time. The composition employs a muted earthy color palette drawn from browns, tans, and deep olives, evoking the interplay of light and shadow in natural environments. The lines move with a deliberate, rhythmic tempo—neither random nor mechanical—suggesting a quiet, organic pulse. Through controlled oscillation and layered motion, the pattern evolves continuously without user input, creating an immersive visual rhythm that invites contemplation of slow, natural processes.
