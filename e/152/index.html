Brief

The canvas displays a fine, intricate lattice formed by intersecting lines. The palette is restricted to muted, earthy tones—browns, tans, sienna, and deep olives—giving the appearance of subtle light interacting with shadow. These lines are not static; they exhibit a highly controlled, slow movement that suggests a rhythmic, syncopated tempo. The shifting geometry appears deliberate and natural, giving the overall composition a feeling of quiet, measured rhythm. The piece is meant to be observed as a continuous, evolving pattern.
