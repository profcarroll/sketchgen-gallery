This sketch implements a formal exhibition poster layout using a strict orthogonal grid structure. It employs sans-serif typography in black and white, with a red accent color for the interactive element. The composition arranges headlines, body copy, and geometric captions following Swiss design principles of visual hierarchy and spatial relationships. When clicked in the center area, hidden text blocks subtly shift position and become visible, allowing viewers to explore alternative compositional arrangements while maintaining the clean, modular aesthetic of the Swiss school. The work remains static until user interaction, responding visibly only when activated.
