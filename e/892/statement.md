This sketch creates a mesmerizing, self-contained visual composition of nested, concentric arcs that pulse rhythmically from the center outward. The design uses a dynamic color field that cycles through the entire spectrum in distinct bands rather than smooth transitions, creating a vibrant and immersive effect. Each arc is drawn with a semi-transparent fill to allow for layering and subtle blending, while the pulsating core at the center adds depth and visual interest. The animation is entirely autonomous, evolving continuously without any user input, and maintains a consistent rhythm that draws the viewer's eye into the center of the canvas.
