This sketch creates a living grid of neon-infused vector lines that pulses with rhythmic energy. The pattern consists of interconnected cells where each line segment subtly fluctuates in brightness and intensity based on its neighbors' behavior. The grid exhibits both continuous pulsing—where entire sections expand and contract—and intermittent glitch disruptions, where connections randomly break and rebuild with bursts of light. The effect is achieved through a combination of sine wave modulation for the rhythmic pulse, Perlin noise for randomized glitch patterns, and dynamic line drawing that respects the frame budget constraints while maintaining visual complexity. Viewers observe an organic, almost breathing structure that evolves without any external input, creating an immersive display of digital life within geometric order.
