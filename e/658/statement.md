This sketch creates an immersive, abstract environment of glowing, interconnected lines that pulse with vibrant electric blue and neon green hues. The structure forms a complex lattice that continuously flows and shifts in three-dimensional space, resembling multi-dimensional data grids or neural networks. The viewer witnesses an ever-shifting pattern of luminous connections that respond dynamically to user interaction. When clicked, the structures intensify their glow and accelerate their flow in the vicinity of the click point, revealing how the underlying patterns reorganize and refocus, creating a mesmerizing visualization of information architecture in motion.
