This sketch creates an immersive, flowing river narrative through a field of interconnected geometric patterns that echo the texture and style of woodblock prints. The canvas is filled with a continuous, repetitive arrangement of carved shapes that move and rotate in harmony, suggesting the winding course of a river. Each pattern is composed of interconnected lines forming regular polygons, with inner details that mimic the grain and texture of woodblock carvings. The patterns flow across the screen in a gentle, organic motion, creating dense, intricate visual pathways that guide the viewer's eye in a continuous journey. The color palette shifts subtly over time, maintaining deep saturation while the geometric forms create a sense of depth and movement through their interconnected nature and flowing animation.
