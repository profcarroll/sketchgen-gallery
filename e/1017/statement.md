This sketch creates a mesmerizing lattice of interlocking hexagons that pulse rhythmically across the canvas. Each hexagon expands and contracts in a continuous, hypnotic motion that simulates structural tension, as if the entire tessellation is on the verge of collapse. The glowing boundaries of each hexagon intensify the sense of dynamic energy, while the color palette shifts gradually through the spectrum to create visual harmony. The pattern fills the entire canvas with an ever-evolving field of luminous geometric forms, where the pulsating rhythm creates a compelling sense of motion and impending release.
