This sketch depicts a dynamic network of interconnected geometric forms that continuously morph and pulse across the screen. The forms drift smoothly while oscillating in size, creating a fluid, organic movement pattern. Periodically, these forms erupt into bursts of energy, expanding dramatically before settling back into their regular motion. The color palette shifts through vivid, saturated hues that cycle in a hypnotic rhythm, with each form maintaining its own unique color identity while connecting to others in the network. The composition pulses with an internal rhythm that creates a mesmerizing visual effect, appearing to breathe and flow across the entire canvas without any external input.
