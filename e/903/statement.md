This sketch creates a vibrant, dynamic field of moving particles that leave glowing trails across the screen. Each particle moves with its own velocity and wraps around the edges of the canvas, creating continuous motion. Occasionally, particles repel each other when they come close, causing temporary groupings where they swirl together before dispersing again. The visual effect is one of fluid, interwoven movement — a constantly shifting tapestry of color and force that feels both chaotic and harmonious. The fading trails enhance the sense of motion and depth, making the scene feel alive and ever-changing.
