This sketch depicts a mechanical shredding apparatus in motion, showing the violent destruction of stacked books. The scene features rotating gears and metal plates that form the core mechanism, along with dynamic visual effects of debris being torn apart. Fine wood splinters and paper pulp are ejected outward in all directions, while steam vents from the machine's interior. The animation creates a dense, swirling plume of debris below the shredder as it operates, capturing the chaotic energy of the mechanical destruction process through particle systems that simulate the various materials being shredded.
