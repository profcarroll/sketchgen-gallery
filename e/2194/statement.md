This sketch visualizes an intricate lattice of interlocking hexagonal tessellations that pulse with glowing electrical energy. The hexagons continuously warp and crystallize in response to energetic pulses, creating a mesmerizing pattern of structural transformation. When the energy reaches its peak, localized failure occurs—hexagonal segments visibly fracture and dissipate, restarting the cycle across the entire lattice. The effect is achieved through a combination of real-time deformation, spatial grid-based neighbor detection, and dynamic particle systems that respond to both time-based oscillations and proximity-based connections.
