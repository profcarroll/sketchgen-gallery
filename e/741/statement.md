This sketch creates an abstract composition of boldly colored diagonal stripes that continuously warp and shift across the canvas. Using a combination of sine waves and cosine functions, each stripe oscillates in its vertical position and shape, creating a ripple-like effect that organizes into layered planes. The stripes move at different speeds and phases, producing a dynamic, structured movement where the geometry appears to rearrange itself systematically. The warm, saturated colors (HSB color mode) provide strong visual contrast while maintaining an abstract, patterned form that evolves over time without any user input.
