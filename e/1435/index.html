Brief

Two distinct, vibrant streams of colored particles flow independently across the canvas. The streams maintain their overall fluid, liquid-like movement, forming contrasting paths. When the two particle streams come into close proximity, they briefly exchange colors, causing the particles within the shared space to adopt the hue of the other stream before resuming their original contrasting palette.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates two distinct particle streams that flow independently across the canvas with vibrant, contrasting colors. Each stream maintains its own identity while exhibiting fluid, liquid-like motion through gentle random movements and boundary bouncing. The key interaction occurs when particles from different streams come into close proximity—triggering a temporary color exchange where particles briefly adopt the hue of their neighboring stream before resuming their original colors. This creates an elegant visual tension between the two flows, emphasizing both their individual characteristics and their dynamic relationship as they cross paths.
