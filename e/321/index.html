Brief

The canvas displays an abstracted, vibrant landscape of a thriving garden, composed of saturated shapes and colors. The primary visual focus is on large, blooming flowers that gradually transform. Over time, the petals of the blooms initiate a slow, visible physical shedding or peeling process. The vibrant, outer petals detach, losing their rich color, and fall away to reveal the structural evolution underneath. This structural change culminates in the development of small, dense, seed-forming pods at the center of the original bloom, completing a cyclical journey of life and transition.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch depicts the cyclical life process of garden flowers through an abstract visualization. It shows blooming flowers that gradually undergo a transformation: their vibrant outer petals slowly fade and peel away, revealing the underlying structure. As time progresses, these flowers evolve into seed-forming pods at their center, completing a full lifecycle from bloom to seed. The animation is smooth and continuous, with no external interaction required — the canvas changes on its own over time, demonstrating the natural progression of plant life through color fading, shape evolution, and structural transformation.
