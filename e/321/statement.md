This sketch depicts the cyclical life process of garden flowers through an abstract visualization. It shows blooming flowers that gradually undergo a transformation: their vibrant outer petals slowly fade and peel away, revealing the underlying structure. As time progresses, these flowers evolve into seed-forming pods at their center, completing a full lifecycle from bloom to seed. The animation is smooth and continuous, with no external interaction required — the canvas changes on its own over time, demonstrating the natural progression of plant life through color fading, shape evolution, and structural transformation.
