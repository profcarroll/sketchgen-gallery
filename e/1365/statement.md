This sketch creates a mesmerizing, continuously flowing kaleidoscope pattern that fills the entire canvas. The visual effect is achieved through layered geometric shapes rotated and repeated in a symmetrical fashion, creating an infinite, hypnotic motion. A central hexagonal mask defines the visible viewport, through which the complex, color-shifting pattern is observed. The animation proceeds automatically without any user interaction, driven purely by time-based calculations that ensure constant visual evolution. The technique relies on mathematical precision and repeated geometric transformations to generate the rich, organic-looking pattern that seems to breathe and pulse with rhythmic energy.
