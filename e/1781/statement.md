This sketch immerses viewers in a cinematic journey through an abandoned Brutalist college campus, where nature has reclaimed the man-made environment. The scene features towering concrete structures gradually dissolving under layers of dark forest loam, with rusted rebar and vibrant green moss peeking through. As the viewer glides through this decaying landscape, they witness the slow, inevitable triumph of organic growth over industrial architecture. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of batched 3D geometry rendering, carefully positioned point clouds for the forest loam coverage, and line-based vine structures that appear to grow organically from the crumbling walls. The continuous camera movement creates a sense of forward motion while maintaining a contemplative, almost reverent tone toward nature's reclamation process.
