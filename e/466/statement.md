This sketch depicts a tranquil beachfront market stall scene centered on a mound of pale yellow sand. The composition features clusters of seashells arranged in natural patterns, each shell rendered with subtle color variations from creamy white to pale pink. The technique uses layered ellipses to create the translucent quality of shells while maintaining the coarse texture of sand through point scatterings and gradient fills. The stillness of the scene is emphasized through careful placement and variation of shell sizes, creating a peaceful study of natural beach bounty without any animation or motion.
