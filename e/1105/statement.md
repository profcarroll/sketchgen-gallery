This sketch simulates the continuous knitting of a hexagonal fabric pattern. The canvas displays a strip of fabric that grows downward from the top edge as new stitches are automatically added to the structure. Each stitch is a regular hexagon with a stable, repeating color palette that maintains visual consistency across the entire piece. The pattern moves downward at a steady pace, creating an effect of perpetual growth. The motion is generated entirely through code without any user input, fulfilling the requirement for autonomous canvas change. The visual rhythm mimics the steady, mechanical process of knitting where new elements are continuously added to form a larger whole.
