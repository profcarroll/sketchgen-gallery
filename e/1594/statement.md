This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic environment through the interplay of slowly moving translucent circles and drifting pointillist stars with light trails. The circles flow gently across the canvas, overlapping and creating moments of intensified glow where they intersect. Stars move through this field, leaving behind arcing light trails that blend with the circle background. The visual effect emphasizes a sense of continuous motion and luminous interaction within a vast, serene space, evoking the feeling of floating through a cosmic stream where light and form pulse in gentle harmony.
