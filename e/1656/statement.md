This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of neon-lit geometric forms composed entirely of octahedra and hexagonal shapes that move in synchronized helical spirals. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of particle-based motion systems and constant rotation to create a sense of depth and energy. The shapes cascade across the screen in repeating spiral patterns, each maintaining its own unique phase while staying in perfect synchronization with others. The colors pulse and shift continuously, creating a vibrant, futuristic glow that fills the entire canvas. The motion is entirely self-contained and continuous, drawing viewers into an immersive, kinetic geometry that feels both organic and algorithmically precise.
