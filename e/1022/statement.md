This sketch creates a mesmerizing steampunk sculpture centered around a rotating emerald cube that pulses with bioluminescent energy. The composition features intricate glass tracery components that orbit the core in synchronized patterns, their translucent surfaces glowing with internal light veins that flow and pulse rhythmically. Tarnished copper splines trace elegant orbital paths around the central structure, while vibrant energy veins animate throughout the piece, creating a sense of artificial life and mechanical harmony. The entire assembly rotates slowly in 3D space, producing a captivating display that feels both futuristic and organic, as if the machine is breathing with its own inner rhythm.
