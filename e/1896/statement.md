This sketch renders an atmospheric sonar visualization with rhythmic, cyclical motion that expands from the center. Instead of constant lines, glowing crystalline formations dynamically assemble from discrete points along helical paths. The visual effect uses a series of evolving helixes that grow outward, each composed of individual glowing particles that connect to form geometric structures. The system creates an immersive field of pulsing, expanding crystal formations that continuously materialize and dissipate, evoking the sensation of sonar waves interacting with crystalline matter in deep space.
