This sketch presents a dramatic 3D architectural scene featuring a modern concrete structure cantilevered from a lush green knoll. The composition uses geometric slabs intersecting at sharp angles to create visual tension and depth. The scene is rendered using WebGL for realistic lighting and perspective, with a slowly orbiting camera that provides a cinematic view of the architecture. Trees are scattered around the base of the structure, adding natural context to the industrial design. The entire scene animates autonomously, demonstrating the dynamic interplay between architectural form and environmental setting through continuous rotation and subtle lighting changes.
