This sketch depicts taut stacks of rubber bands arranged in a mechanical, self-contained system. Each band consists of ten segments that gently oscillate under tension, with subtle vertical motion creating the illusion of organized impermanence. The bands pulse rhythmically and occasionally snap when internal tension reaches a critical threshold, causing their segments to expand outward before snapping back into place. These rhythmic sequences of tension and release propagate across the arrangement in a structured, echoing pattern. The visual effect is created through simple geometric representation of each band's segments with dynamic tension visualization, producing a mesmerizing mechanical dance of elastic forces.
