This sketch depicts a dark, fragile ziggurat composed of geometric glass prisms in a continuous gravitational collapse. The structure appears to be disintegrating systematically, with segments shedding and cascading downward under the influence of gravity. The muted color palette emphasizes the crystalline nature of the material, while subtle rotations and lateral movements create an organic sense of decay. Viewer interaction through clicking causes additional sections to peel away and accelerate into the ongoing cascade, amplifying the sense of structural instability. The visual effect uses a combination of particle-like segments with dynamic physics simulation to create a convincing sense of volumetric collapse.
