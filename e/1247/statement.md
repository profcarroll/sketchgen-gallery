This sketch creates an ethereal underwater scene with dense, shifting fog and a sweeping lighthouse beam. The composition uses thousands of individual particles to simulate fog density, each moving at different speeds to create a sense of depth and motion. A dynamic beam sweeps across the mist, creating bright arcs that cut through the gray atmosphere. The scene incorporates subtle moonlight reflections as shimmering points that flicker independently, adding texture to the otherwise smooth, flowing motion. The entire effect is achieved through careful layering and blending techniques within p5's 2D rendering, creating an immersive, tranquil yet dramatic underwater environment that evolves without any user input.
