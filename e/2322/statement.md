This sketch creates an organic, flowing pond surface using a network of evolving ripples that move naturally across the canvas. Each ripple is rendered as a soft, irregular polygon to simulate the appearance of cut paper fragments floating on water. The ripples have meandering paths driven by Perlin noise, creating a continuous, liquid quality without any viewer interaction. The composition evolves slowly over time, with each fragment growing and fading out as it moves, producing an ever-shifting pattern that mimics the gentle undulations of a pond's surface.
