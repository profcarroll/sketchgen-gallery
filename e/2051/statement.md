This sketch creates a dynamic simulation of iron filings responding to magnetic forces. Particles settle gently on screen with subtle noise-based motion, forming a field of metallic dust that slowly reorganizes itself. When the cursor moves across the canvas, it acts as an invisible magnet drawing nearby particles toward its path, creating intricate trails of clustered metal dust. These trails gradually disperse over time, maintaining an ephemeral quality where the composition is perpetually shifting and evolving. The visual effect captures the delicate balance between natural settling and external influence, rendering a quiet yet dynamic field of simulated metallic particles.
