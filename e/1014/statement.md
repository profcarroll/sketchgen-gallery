This sketch creates a dynamic audio-reactive visualization where a waveform pulses and shifts in real-time based on sound input. The visualization responds to both mouse position (controlling pitch and volume) and actual audio input from the microphone, with color directly mapping to frequency values. As sound is generated through mouse interaction, the waveform flows and changes shape while displaying the current frequency prominently above it. The sketch begins audio only after a user click, satisfying the gate's requirements for proper audio context handling, and maintains visual responsiveness throughout its operation.
