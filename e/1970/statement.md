This sketch depicts a living network of glowing cells that pulse and flow across the canvas like an organic ecosystem. The cells continuously evolve—moving, connecting to neighbors, and regenerating—while the entire field is slowly immersed in a rhythmic pulse of color. The simulation uses a grid-based spatial hash for efficient neighbor detection, preventing performance issues as thousands of particles interact. The constant background light shift creates a sense of deep time and biological rhythm, while the interconnected cells form a dynamic lattice that adapts and reorganizes itself over time. Viewers witness an abstract but alive system where pattern emerges from simple rules, evoking the beauty of cellular processes and natural growth patterns.
