This sketch depicts a steampunk kinetic sculpture composed of polished brass splines orbiting a central core. The core is animated as molten bronze that drips and solidifies into amber-like droplets, creating a mesmerizing transformation. The entire structure rotates continuously with intricate cyclical motion, reflecting light through its highly polished metal surfaces and solidified resin. The visual effect combines mechanical complexity with organic fluidity, where particles form an orbiting framework while the central core pulses with dynamic, semi-viscous material movement that gives the sculpture a living, breathing quality.
