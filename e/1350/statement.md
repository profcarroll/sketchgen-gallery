This sketch creates a flowing, liquid light field that responds to the luminance of an image. Particles move through a vector field derived from the image's brightness, following streams of light that vary in direction and intensity based on the source photo. The particles maintain vibrant color from the image while flowing smoothly across the canvas. Junction points where streams intersect retain sharp boundaries, creating distinct, glowing paths that define the evolving network. The result is a constantly shifting field of luminous, interconnected flows with an organic, liquid quality.
