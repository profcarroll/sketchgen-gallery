This sketch creates a sweeping drone fly-through of a brutalist concrete campus, rendering massive angular geometric structures in a desaturated grey palette. Using the WEBGL renderer, it draws towering stairwells and repetitive architectural elements that shift and scale as the camera moves through space. The animation is entirely self-contained, with no user input required after initial load, creating an immersive journey through decaying industrial geometry. The camera follows a smooth, organic path that emphasizes the scale and repetitiveness of the brutalist architecture, with each frame rendering a complex scene of concrete planes and structural elements that fill the canvas without exceeding performance budgets.
