This sketch creates a static, mineral-rich network where geometric paths intersect to form crystalline deposits. The work depicts an underground geological structure composed of intersecting tunnels that are rendered as thick, earth-toned lines. At every point where these paths cross, vibrant crystals emerge with sharp, pointed formations. The scene is entirely observational — no animation occurs, and the composition fills the entire canvas with a structured, mineral-filled landscape. The viewer encounters a still, detailed visualization of intersecting structural elements reinforced by crystalline growths, evoking a sense of geological time and mineral formation.
