This sketch depicts a slow, irreversible process of erosion in a bog environment. The field of root-like structures gradually dissolves and shifts over time, with individual segments slowly decaying and occasionally disappearing to simulate the natural breakdown of organic matter. The muted browns and deep greens create a sense of aged peat and humus, while the continuous subtle movement suggests the slow, inevitable degradation of these biological forms back into their environment. Viewers observe this quiet transformation without intervention, experiencing the passage of time through the gradual dissolution of geometric networks that once formed coherent structures.
