This sketch creates a digital grid structure dominated by glowing hexagonal modules connected by energetic lines. Neon pixel clusters pulse with rhythmic energy, sending out waves that propagate along the connections between adjacent modules. The scene features a deep blue color palette punctuated by vibrant neon hues, creating a sense of perpetual digital life flowing through the network. The system generates its own motion and rhythm without user input, with waves continuously radiating from module clusters to create an organic, living feel within the geometric framework.
