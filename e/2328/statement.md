This sketch creates a microscopic cross-section of amber-toned resin where two luminous energy filaments—blue and green—flow toward a geometrically complex junction. The filaments pulse rhythmically as they approach each other, their paths rendered as glowing veins that interact at the point of convergence. At the intersection, intense light radiates outward in rhythmic energy flow lines, creating a mesmerizing visual representation of dynamic interaction within the solid medium. The scene uses WebGL rendering to achieve depth and volumetric effects while maintaining performance within strict frame budget constraints, presenting an immersive view into the mineral's inner energy dynamics.
