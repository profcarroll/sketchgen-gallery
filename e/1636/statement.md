This sketch depicts an ancient agricultural landscape in a dry, bright sky, where terraced steps create a layered hillside. The visual narrative follows the interaction between geological time and human cultivation: the steep, stepped terrain holds deep ochre hues that suggest the slow accumulation of earth and silt over generations. A subtle stream flows down from the top, carrying fine deposits that gradually build up on the lower steps, changing their color and texture—evoking the slow, persistent process of deposition. The viewer's interaction introduces a new element into this timeless scene: clicking tills a patch and plants shoots that grow and blend with the existing crop. The animation captures both natural motion (swaying crops, flowing water) and human intervention (the cultivation), creating a sense of cyclical activity within the static landscape.
