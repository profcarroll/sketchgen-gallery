This sketch embodies the strict geometric discipline of Swiss Style typography through a meticulously grid-based composition. Using a 12-column system across an 8-row structure, it arranges large, asymmetric sans-serif letterforms in stark contrast against a pristine white background. The work emphasizes visual tension through careful placement and negative space, with minimal structural elements that guide the eye across the canvas. The design's authority emerges from its mathematical precision and deliberate use of typography as both content and form, creating an immediately recognizable yet austere typographic poster.
