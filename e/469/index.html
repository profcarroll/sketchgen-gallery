Brief

A breathtaking, vibrant ethereal lattice of light spans the canvas, depicting an intricate network of glowing lines. This network is dynamic, marked by segments that periodically break apart, only to reform through visible, lingering spectral trails. These trails occasionally solidify into complex, crystalline geometric structures that pulse with rhythmic, internal light. This pulsing energy resonates through the lattice, causing adjacent lines to visibly vibrate and ripple, and generating faint, momentary sparks that ripple and travel along the length of the glowing strands.
