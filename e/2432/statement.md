This sketch renders intricate root-like structures that grow and evolve over time, mimicking the slow, mechanical process of underground tunneling through earth. Each system begins as a single point and grows outward in branching segments, accumulating stress as it develops. The tension builds until sudden crystalline fractures occur, creating explosive bursts of geometric shattering that propagate like waves through the network. The viewer's interaction—by dragging their cursor across the canvas—amplifies existing stress points, triggering cascading fractures that spread throughout the system. The graphite-like aesthetic and precise, plotter-like movements give the impression of mechanical processes unfolding under pressure, with each fracture pattern emerging as a moment of sudden release from accumulated strain.
