This sketch creates a fluid, glowing field of amorphous planes that drift slowly across the canvas. Each plane has its own velocity and color, creating a sense of continuous motion punctuated by pockets of varying speed. The planes maintain a soft luminous glow through layered translucent ellipses, and their positions are updated with periodic motion to create zones of rapid movement that contrast with the gentle overall drift. The result is an ever-changing field of light that appears both organic and mechanical in its flow.
