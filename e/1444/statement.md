This sketch creates a dynamic nighttime harbor scene populated by miniature drones that respond to viewer interaction. When the user moves their cursor, the drones navigate the environment in a fluid, responsive manner, following the mouse position with physics-based motion. A single click toggles between free-flight mode and pre-set formation patterns, where drones align into geometric configurations. Each drone emits a bright searchlight beam that sweeps across the cityscape and harbor, creating dramatic illumination effects. The visualization uses WebGL for 3D rendering to achieve depth and lighting effects while maintaining performance within the strict frame budget constraints.
