Brief

The canvas displays neon-infused vector lines and glitching geometric structures that evoke the aesthetics of early digital projections. The composition appears to be constantly calculating, featuring interconnected grids and rapidly resolving forms reminiscent of wireframe blueprints. When the user clicks anywhere on the screen, the entire visual field momentarily destabilizes, causing a dramatic shift in color palette and forcing the geometric forms to rapidly reconfigure as if restarting the system.

This sketch creates a dynamic, glitching geometric composition using WebGL rendering that simulates early digital projection aesthetics. The scene features interconnected wireframe grids and neon-infused vector lines that continuously shift and reconfigure. A key interaction causes the entire visual field to destabilize on click—triggering a dramatic color palette shift and rapid reconfiguration of forms, evoking the feeling of a system restarting or recalculating.
