This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of glowing, fluorescent rings that slowly spiral inward toward the center of the canvas. Each ring maintains its own rhythm of motion while contributing to a cohesive, hypnotic pattern. The animation is achieved through carefully modulated sine waves that control each ring's radius over time, creating a continuous, rhythmic collapse toward the center. The visual effect relies on the HSB color space for vibrant, electric hues and a consistent stroke weight that ensures each ring remains clearly visible against the dark background. The motion is smooth and constant, without any abrupt changes or random elements, resulting in an immersive experience where the viewer observes the continuous dance of expanding and contracting circular forms as they inexorably move toward the center.
