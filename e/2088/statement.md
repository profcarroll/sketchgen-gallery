This sketch creates a serene, ever-shifting landscape of interconnected geometric forms that drift slowly across the screen. The particles—ellipses that gently change size and position—move with fluid motion while maintaining subtle connections to nearby forms. Colors shift gradually through near-monochromatic palettes, creating a sense of deep equilibrium rather than dramatic change. The visual field pulses softly, with no abrupt bursts or energetic movements, offering a meditative experience of continuous, harmonious transformation. The interconnections between forms create a living network that evolves without disruption, embodying the concept of peaceful, rhythmic motion.
