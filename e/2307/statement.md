This sketch depicts a campfire scene where sparks emerge from the base and ascend into a darkening night sky. The particles begin as warm orange and yellow flames and gradually transition to cooler blues as they rise, creating an illusion of mapping the night sky. As sparks drift upward, they leave behind glowing trails that slow down and cool, eventually forming recognizable patterns. The scene captures the ephemeral nature of fire while transforming it into a cosmic metaphor, where the temporary forest fire appears to map the deepest reaches of the night sky.
