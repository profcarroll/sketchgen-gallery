This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of pendulum-like discs swinging in hypnotic patterns. Each disc is tuned to a different frequency in a pentatonic scale, creating a harmonious yet unpredictable rhythm. The pendulums swing independently with randomized arcs and varying amplitudes, producing complex intersecting curves as their trails blend together. The visual result is a constantly evolving, fluid pattern that appears to breathe and pulse with organic motion, filled with the subtle beauty of mathematical harmony made manifest through continuous, non-repeating movement.
