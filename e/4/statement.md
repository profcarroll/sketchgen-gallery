This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of concentric circles that pulse and ripple in a continuous, hypnotic rhythm. The circles expand and contract with an elastic, fluid motion driven by sine wave functions, creating a sense of undulating energy across the screen. Each circle is rendered with a bright, fluorescent hue from the color wheel, cycling through vibrant colors to enhance the glowing effect. The pattern fills the entire canvas and moves autonomously without any user interaction, producing an immersive visual experience that demonstrates the expressive potential of p5.js's drawing capabilities through simple mathematical motion.
