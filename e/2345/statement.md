This sketch depicts a meditative landscape of atomic-scale crystalline structures dissolving and reforming in an endless cycle. The viewer observes thousands of iridescent silicate glass formations drifting through space, each slowly rotating and dissolving over time. The geometry flows organically, with subtle color shifts that suggest the molecular dance of entropy at work. The effect is achieved through a particle system of 500 geometric crystals that drift, rotate, and gradually fade out as they age, creating an immersive visualization of deep molecular time. The WEBGL renderer enables the three-dimensional movement and rotation of each crystal structure while maintaining performance within the strict frame budget constraints.
