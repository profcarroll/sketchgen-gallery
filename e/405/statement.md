This sketch creates an interactive stovetop interface where users can rotate a central control dial to adjust gas flow across four circular burners. As the dial is turned, dynamic blue and yellow flames appear and shift around the burners, responding to the dial's position in real-time. The visualization includes a realistic stovetop surface with burners and a control dial that visually reacts to user input through mouse dragging, while also automatically cycling through states when idle to demonstrate continuous motion.
