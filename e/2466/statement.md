This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic landscape filled with slowly drifting, translucent circles that form a fluid, glowing background. The circles move with gentle, cyclical motion, leaving behind shimmering light trails that dissipate into sparkling particulate clusters. The composition evokes deep celestial space through soft, iridescent colors and continuous, undulating movement, with each circle's trail and sparkle effects contributing to the sense of endless, flowing cosmic energy.
