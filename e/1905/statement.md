This sketch creates a mesmerizing, symmetrical composition of interlocking polygonal forms that evoke the rich, jewel-toned patterns of stained glass. Using a dense array of rotating polygons in deep sapphire blue and glowing emerald green, it generates complex, hypnotic structures that fill the entire canvas. The patterns are constructed entirely from filled polygons rather than continuous lines, creating a tapestry-like effect with overlapping, interwoven geometries. The subtle rotation and varying polygon counts produce an ever-shifting yet harmonious visual rhythm that captures the essence of intricate stained-glass artistry through algorithmic generation.
