This sketch creates a warm, sunset-drenched seascape with a stylized sailboat drifting across the horizon. The scene features sharp, high-contrast ripples on the ocean surface that respond organically to the movement of waves. The entire composition is rendered with a petrified wood texture, which flows through both sky and water to create an organic, eroded appearance. Sunset colors—amber, orange, and deep purple—blend softly across the canvas, creating a dreamlike atmosphere where the viewer observes the slow passage of time as the boat glides toward the horizon. The visual effect is achieved through procedural noise patterns that simulate erosion and texture, creating depth without complex geometry or particle systems.
