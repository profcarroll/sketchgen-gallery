This sketch creates a hypnotic geometric composition using large, hard-edged shapes in a bold color palette of mustard yellow, burnt orange, and deep teal. The shapes—rectangles, triangles, arcs, and polygons—drift and pulse slowly across the canvas in a rhythmic pattern reminiscent of 1970s supergraphics. The viewer's perspective can be altered by dragging the mouse, which rotates and scales the entire arrangement. The piece explores the tension between static geometric forms and dynamic motion, creating a meditative yet visually striking field that shifts and evolves without external input.
