Brief

The viewer confronts a self-portrait built entirely of interlocking, abstracted planar facets. These planes are rendered with extremely sharp, defined edges, giving the entire form a highly faceted, crystalline structure. As ambient light interacts with the surface, the viewer observes dramatic shifts in how the planes catch the illumination; this interaction creates sudden, crisp highlights contrasted by deep, carved shadows across the geometry. The artwork emphasizes the sheer physical complexity and the powerful interplay between light source and the reflective angle of each distinct, defined plane.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a static, crystalline self-portrait composed of interlocking geometric facets rendered in WebGL. Each facet is a planar surface with sharp edges and defined lighting interactions that create dramatic highlights and deep shadows across the form. The composition uses abstracted planes arranged in three-dimensional space to evoke the complexity and physicality described in the brief. The artwork is deliberately still, responding only to its own internal lighting dynamics without any motion or animation.
