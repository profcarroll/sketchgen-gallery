This sketch creates a serene, still landscape of sandy dunes with scattered seashells, capturing the essence of natural beauty through careful attention to texture and light. The sand surface is generated using Perlin noise to create organic grain patterns that suggest the tactile quality of fine sand. Each shell is individually placed with variation in size, color, and rotation to mimic natural scattering. A subtle pearlescent highlight on each shell catches imagined light, emphasizing their delicate translucency and curved surfaces. The composition maintains a peaceful stillness, with no motion or animation, inviting contemplation of geological stratification and the quiet persistence of natural forms.
