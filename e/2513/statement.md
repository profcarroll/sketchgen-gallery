This sketch renders a continuously evolving geological visualization composed of multiple nested, asymmetrical rings that shift and morph over time. The layered structures appear to form organically, with each ring exhibiting its own rhythm of distortion and color change, creating an impression of sedimentary or biological growth patterns. Colors transition abruptly and irregularly across the layers, emphasizing the dynamic, rule-governed chaos of the visual process. The composition fills the entire canvas in a way that suggests an endless, self-organizing system—each frame revealing new configurations while maintaining the overall sense of geological time and natural transformation.
