This sketch presents a microscopic world where rigid crystal structures gradually dissolve into viscous sludge. At the center, discrete geometric shapes—representing broken puzzle pieces—slowly lose their sharp edges and defined forms. Each particle begins as a solid form with angular geometry, but over time transforms into soft, amorphous blobs that blend seamlessly with the surrounding mineral-rich background. The process creates an illusion of organic flow, where hard boundaries soften and merge, revealing the underlying transformation from structured to fluid states. The viewer witnesses this metamorphosis through continuous motion and color shifts, capturing the essence of materials transitioning from solid to liquid states at a granular scale.
