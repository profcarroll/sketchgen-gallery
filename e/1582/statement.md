This sketch creates a vibrant, pulsing neon grid that simulates an unstable architectural field. The visual effect emerges from a structured network of cyan horizontal lines and magenta vertical connections that periodically fail and reform. The breaking occurs exclusively on the vertical axes, creating a dynamic tension between stability and chaos. The glowing, vector-based lines pulse with synchronized light rhythms while segments randomly disappear and reappear in controlled bursts. The viewer witnesses an architectural system that's perpetually in flux, where order gives way to disorder in cyclical pulses of neon brilliance, all rendered in real-time with minimal computational overhead to maintain the performance requirements.
