Brief

The canvas presents an immense, indoor aquatic landscape viewed from above. The focus is the expansive surface of the water and the deep, reflective floor below. As the surface ripples, the interplay of overhead light and liquid movement causes dramatic, vividly defined shadows to play, shifting and deepening across the pool floor in continuous, rhythmic patterns. The scene is a study in dynamic light and liquid geometry.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive underwater scene viewed from above, simulating the dynamic interplay of light and water. Using WebGL rendering, it generates a vast pool surface with rippling waves that cast shifting shadows across the reflective floor. The continuous animation demonstrates how light interacts with liquid geometry, creating a rhythmic pattern of highlights and deepening shadows that evolve over time without any user input.
