This sketch creates a dynamic, self-sustaining network of morphing geometric forms that continuously shift in space and color. The visual system operates without external input, generating an ever-changing pattern through internal motion and color oscillation. Forms drift across the canvas with fluid trajectories while their sizes pulse gently, creating a sense of organized but unstable movement. Connections between nearby forms are drawn as translucent lines that fluctuate in harmony with the shifting hues, forming a living web of color and geometry. The abrupt changes in saturation and hue across the structure create a dramatic visual rhythm that evolves independently, producing an immersive spectacle of form and color that perpetually transforms on its own.
