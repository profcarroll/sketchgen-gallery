This sketch depicts a weathered brick wall in motion, capturing the slow, relentless decay of aged masonry. Through layered techniques, it simulates how bricks flake and spall under the pressure of embedded roots, creating a living, evolving texture. The visual field uses rich earth tones to evoke an ancient structure, with subtle movements and random events that suggest time's passage. Brick faces undergo continuous minor shifts in position and opacity, while root tendrils weave through the mortar, and countless tiny particles simulate the spalling process. The result is a mesmerizing blend of stability and fragility, where decay is both visible and organic.
