This sketch creates an immersive cosmic environment where translucent, glowing circles flow continuously across the screen like a slow tide of light. Against this backdrop, thousands of pointillist stars pulse with bright, rhythmic intensity while drifting in the opposite direction. When these stars encounter the flowing circles, they generate temporary ripples that spread outward and fade back into the stream — a visual metaphor for how energy and motion interact within an ever-changing universe. The piece captures both the calm serenity and dynamic tension of deep space, with movement driven entirely by the passage of time.
