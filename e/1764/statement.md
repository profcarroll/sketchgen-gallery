This sketch depicts a colossal indoor pool of highly reflective water with dynamic ripples that cast sharp, geometric shadows across the surrounding walls and floor. The scene is rendered using WebGL for performance, with a water surface created through animated ripple effects that continuously deform. Light sources in the environment cast strong shadows onto the pool's floor and vertical surfaces, creating abstract shadow patterns that define the architectural structure. The visualization emphasizes the interplay between reflective water, dynamic lighting, and sharp geometric shadows to convey the volumetric quality of the space.
