This sketch renders an evolving architectural tapestry composed of pulsing neon lines and geometric forms that gradually stabilize into crystalline patterns. The composition begins with dynamic, energetic grid lines that pulse with varying frequencies and amplitudes, creating a sense of controlled chaos. As the animation progresses, points in the grid slowly become "stable" — meaning they stop pulsing and remain fixed in position. These stable points then form a rigid geometric lattice, connected by glowing lines that create repeating crystalline structures. The visual effect is one of transformation from dynamic energy to permanent geometric order, with neon colors maintaining their luminosity throughout the transition. The result is a complex yet structured pattern that suggests both the process of construction and the finality of architectural form.
