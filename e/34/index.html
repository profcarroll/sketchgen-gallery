Brief

The canvas features slow, constantly evolving abstract shapes and lines, mimicking the depth of space or swirling nebulas. These geometrical elements, rendered in a rich, cinematic palette of deep blues, violets, and glowing accents, continuously shift and morph across the screen. Titles or abstract text fragments slowly resolve and fade into the background, giving the appearance of a grand, evolving cinematic reveal. The composition remains dynamic, with all visual changes occurring automatically and responding subtly to ambient sound present through the microphone.

This sketch creates an immersive, evolving cosmic landscape using WebGL rendering in p5.js. The composition features slowly morphing, glowing shapes that drift through a three-dimensional space, evoking the vastness and mystery of nebulae and deep space. Colorful spheres shift and pulse in synchronized motion, while abstract text fragments resolve and fade in the background, suggesting a grand cinematic reveal. The entire scene is rendered dynamically without any user input, with all visual changes occurring automatically over time. The sketch is designed to be responsive to ambient sound through its audio analysis capabilities, though it also functions autonomously, creating an endless, hypnotic visualization of cosmic motion and transformation.
