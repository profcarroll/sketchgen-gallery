This sketch transforms real-time microphone audio into a luminous, flowing line that stretches across the canvas. The line's shape and intensity fluctuate according to the sound's waveform, creating a direct visual representation of the ambient soundscape. As sound enters through the microphone, the glowing line immediately reacts—expanding and contracting in response to both volume and frequency variations. The technique uses p5.js's audio analysis capabilities to capture live input and map it into dynamic visual geometry, producing an immersive, responsive visualization that changes with every sound.
