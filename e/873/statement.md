This sketch creates a continuously shifting abstract composition using bold geometric bands and fine linear stripes. The bands are six-sided polygons that rotate and scale in unison, each filled with a vibrant, randomly selected color. Meanwhile, thousands of thin lines (stripes) rotate across the canvas at varying speeds, creating a high-contrast pattern of intersecting geometry. All motion is self-generated without user interaction, and the composition remains perpetually active, shifting its form through time. The harsh edges and rigid geometric forms ensure no smooth transitions or gradients are present, giving the piece a clean, mechanical aesthetic.
