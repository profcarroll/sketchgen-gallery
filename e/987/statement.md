This sketch simulates the process of knitting by generating a growing strip of fabric on screen. Starting from the top, it builds rows of stitches that descend vertically, each row maintaining a consistent horizontal pattern with alternating colors. The animation proceeds automatically without any user input, showing the continuous evolution of the knitted material as new rows are added. The visual rhythm mimics traditional knitting patterns, where each stitch is clearly defined and contributes to a larger textured surface. The movement is smooth and steady, reflecting the mechanical repetition inherent in the craft, while the color cycling creates a visually pleasing striped effect that evolves over time.
