This sketch creates a continuous, flowing vortex of semi-transparent textual characters inspired by James Joyce's *Finnegans Wake*. The characters swirl and eddy in an organic pattern, forming a dense, amorphous stream that dominates the canvas. Every few seconds, the flow momentarily organizes itself into perfectly legible, sequential lines of text — a stark contrast to the swirling chaos — before collapsing back into its continuous vortex form. The effect uses a particle system with physics-based movement, color blending, and periodic reorganization to create an evolving visual narrative that captures both the fluidity and structure inherent in Joyce's text.
