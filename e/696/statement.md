This sketch portrays a futuristic cityscape undergoing continuous atmospheric cycles of day and night. Towering wet architectural structures dominate the scene, while bioluminescent plant life pulsates with twilight energy. These glowing elements cast radiating ripples that form fractal patterns, which then deposit crystalline trails onto the rigid surfaces. The crystalline scores interact with pooled liquid, generating metallic deposits that charge the liquid and cause it to stratify into distinct luminescent layers. The piece captures the dynamic interplay between organic bioluminescence and mechanical architecture through a continuous loop of glowing particles, ripples, trails, and layered liquids.
