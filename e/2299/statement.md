This sketch presents an interactive 3D subway map where stations and rail lines form a dynamic network. When a viewer clicks on any station node, the entire system undergoes a transformation—stations shift position, lines stretch or compress to connect new paths, and the whole structure reforms into a new configuration. The visual design uses distinct colors for each line and labeled circular nodes to represent stations, creating an abstract yet recognizable transit map. The interaction is immediate and visually satisfying, showing how a single point of influence can ripple through a complex system, altering its topology without losing the essential connectivity between nodes.
