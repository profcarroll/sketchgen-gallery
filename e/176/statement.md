This sketch portrays a futuristic cityscape in a continuous cycle of day and twilight. The architecture features angular, wet surfaces that reflect the shifting atmospheric hues—deep blues at dawn transitioning to warm oranges as dusk approaches. Bioluminescent plankton clusters drift slowly through the scene, gradually aggregating into flowing paths of light that spread across the polished building facades. The entire metropolis appears suspended in an ethereal, breathing current, with dynamic lighting effects and subtle pulsations creating a sense of life and movement within the urban environment. The WebGL renderer enables the fluid geometry and reflective surfaces that define the piece's visual character.
