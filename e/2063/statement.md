This sketch creates an intricate, still visualization of butterfly wing patterns through overlapping concentric rings. Each ring is rendered with vibrant, saturated colors that shift suddenly and asymmetrically across the canvas, mimicking the way light interacts with microscopic structures on butterfly wings. The rings are drawn with sharp, irregular boundaries that create a complex geometric landscape of contrasting bands. The color transitions occur in discrete steps over time, producing a mesmerizing effect where the patterns appear to subtly evolve while maintaining their overall stillness—a visual representation of magnified natural phenomena frozen in time.
