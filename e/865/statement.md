This sketch renders an immersive, dynamically evolving architectural space composed of intersecting geometric planes that pulse with emerald green light. The scene uses WebGL rendering to create a volumetric effect where multiple rotating planes intersect and warp in real-time, generating harmonic interference patterns and visual distortion at their junctions. Crystalline surfaces reflect and refract the glowing energy, producing a shimmering, ever-changing display of light that washes across the entire canvas. The motion is entirely algorithmic — no user input required — with each plane slowly rotating and undulating in phase, creating continuous visual dynamism that fills the frame with controlled chaos.
