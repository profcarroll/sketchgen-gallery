A kinetic study of opposing collective dynamics, exploring how simple local interactions generate intricate macroscopic tension. Two distinct colonies—one radiant in warm amber, the other in sharp cyan—traverse the same canvas under decentralized flocking rules. While members within each flock coalesce and coordinate their headings, a repulsive barrier activates whenever the swarms meet, cleaving the groups into sweeping, hydrodynamic detours before they re-converge in continuous fluid balance.
