This sketch depicts the slow, contained process of crystallization emerging from a dark obsidian field, triggered by a sequence of falling dominoes. The visual metaphor unfolds through geometric forms that gradually grow and reorganize, with each domino's fall initiating new crystallization events. The viewer observes the emergence of sharp, pyramidal crystal structures that expand outward from specific nodal points, creating an intricate lattice pattern across the canvas. The dark background emphasizes the contrast of the emerging structures, while the timing of the dominoes controls both the pace and location of new growth, creating a sense of structured chaos in the formation process.
