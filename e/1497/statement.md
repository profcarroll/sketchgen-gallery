This sketch depicts a moody street scene where umbrellas bloom and sway in response to falling rain. The composition features 30 umbrellas that sequentially unfurl from closed to open positions, each with its own timing and color. As the rain falls continuously across the canvas, the open umbrellas sway independently in response to a subtle wind effect that varies over time. The scene uses a muted blue-gray color palette to evoke a rainy atmosphere, with umbrellas rendered as curved canopies with distinct saturated colors. The rain is represented by countless vertical streaks that cascade steadily down the screen, creating a continuous rainfall effect. The fluid motion captures both the individual behavior of each umbrella and the collective sway of the crowd, giving the impression of people sheltering from the rain while maintaining their own rhythmic movements.
