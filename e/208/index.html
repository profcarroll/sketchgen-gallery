Brief

The canvas displays an abstract, volatile representation of a stock market. Numerous dynamic lines sweep and intertwine, visualizing fluctuating market activity. These lines connect to nodes, which appear in vibrant red and green, varying in size and placement across the display. The core visual effect is the pulsation: whenever any dynamic line approaches or passes near a red or green node, its intensity visibly brightens and pulses in synchronization with the node, creating a lively and continuously changing visualization of market energy.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch visualizes a volatile stock market through abstract, dynamic lines that sweep across the canvas and connect to nodes that pulse with energy. The nodes appear in vibrant red and green, representing market upswings and downswings, while the lines represent the flow of activity between them. When lines approach these nodes, they intensify and pulse in sync with the node's rhythm, creating a constantly shifting and energetic visualization. The system uses spatial hashing to manage interactions efficiently and avoids performance bottlenecks by limiting connections per frame.
