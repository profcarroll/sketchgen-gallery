This sketch implements a cellular automaton simulation on a 1024x1024 grid, where each cell can be either active or inactive. The system evolves according to Conway's Game of Life rules, creating dynamic patterns that continuously transform and propagate across the canvas. The viewer can interact with the simulation by clicking anywhere to toggle individual cells, thereby influencing the subsequent evolution of the cellular patterns. The simulation runs automatically, with visual changes occurring without user input, and responds to clicks by allowing direct manipulation of cell states. The grid-based approach ensures efficient computation while producing visually compelling emergent behavior from simple local rules.
