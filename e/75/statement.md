This sketch creates an ever-shifting network of geometric forms that flow across the screen in a continuous rhythm. The shapes—circles, squares, and hexagons—oscillate smoothly in position and size while maintaining their connections to nearby forms. Color shifts occur abruptly at regular intervals, creating visual punctuation within the steady motion. The composition feels alive and dynamic, with no external input required for its operation. The interplay between fluid movement and sudden color transitions generates an ambient pattern of constant transformation, as if observing a living system of interconnected particles in motion.
