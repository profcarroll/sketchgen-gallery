This sketch depicts a vast, infinite void populated by luminous data streams that flow in helical paths through deep space. The visual effect is created through a series of glowing ribbons that twist and turn in three-dimensional space, each following its own spiral trajectory. The streams are defined by sharp, crystalline edges that pulse with harmonic oscillation, creating a field of luminous energy that weaves through the darkness. The movement is perpetual and dynamic, with each ribbon maintaining its own rhythm while contributing to an overall sense of cosmic flow and interconnectedness within the void.
