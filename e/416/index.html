Brief

The canvas displays a highly stylized depiction of a muscular horse in a full galloping pose. The shape of the animal is the central element, rendered with distinct, exaggerated musculature. The horse maintains a consistent, solid color palette across its body and mane. Its primary movement is a continuous, rhythmic cycle that simulates a gallop. Crucially, the speed of this gait is slowed down, appearing deliberate and measured rather than rapid or wild. The entire sketch is designed to run independently, providing a constant, looping spectacle of controlled motion.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch depicts a stylized galloping horse rendered with exaggerated musculature and rhythmic motion. The animation simulates a deliberate, measured gait using sine wave functions to create smooth, continuous movement of the limbs and body. The entire scene is drawn on a single canvas without any external libraries or sound, relying purely on p5.js built-in functions to generate the illusion of controlled galloping. The slow, looping motion creates a mesmerizing effect as the horse's muscles flex and relax in sync with its stride, all while maintaining a consistent color palette and simplified geometric forms that stay within the frame budget constraints.
