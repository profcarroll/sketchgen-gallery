This sketch creates an immersive underwater environment where thousands of semi-transparent, organic particles drift through a deep oceanic space. The particles are influenced by subtle, visible currents that create gentle, circular motion patterns. When triggered by a user click, a row of falling dominoes causes a sudden geometric congregation of rigid, luminous particles to rapidly stack into perfect crystalline formations. The muted blue and green color palette evokes the mysterious depths of the ocean while the particle system demonstrates both fluid organic movement and abrupt geometric transformation. The scene transitions from serene drift to dramatic crystallization, capturing the tension between natural flow and sudden structural change in an aqueous landscape.
