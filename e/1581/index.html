Brief

A complex, three-dimensional steampunk kinetic sculpture is displayed, featuring several polished, vibrant brass splines. These splines orbit around a central glowing core of viscous, molten bronze. The entire structure is constantly in motion, creating an illusion of dynamic, intertwined gears and radiant energy, allowing the viewer to observe the intricate, continuous rotation and the metallic interplay of light on the brass surfaces.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch renders a dynamic, three-dimensional steampunk kinetic sculpture composed of orbiting brass splines around a central molten bronze core. The scene features continuous rotation and subtle vertical motion that creates an illusion of interlocking gears and radiant energy. The brass elements are rendered with metallic hues that shift across oxidized copper tones, while the central core pulses with a glowing, viscous bronze glow. The entire structure maintains constant motion without external input, creating an autonomous visual experience where viewers can observe the intricate dance of light and shadow across the polished surfaces of the rotating components.
