This sketch explores the slow degradation of richly saturated color layers into crystalline structures. Deep blue and purple planes slowly shift and erode over time, revealing sharp geometric forms beneath. The effect is achieved through layered drawing operations where colored rectangles gradually fade, particle systems simulate erosion, and geometric crystal shapes emerge from the shifting surface. The viewer observes a continuous transformation from soft, blended color fields to defined, microscopic crystalline patterns, suggesting geological processes of erosion and mineral formation.
