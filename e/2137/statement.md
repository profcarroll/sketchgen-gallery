This sketch depicts a viscous lava-like material that emerges and spreads across a surface in response to a chain reaction of falling dominoes. The viewer observes a slow, organic expansion where each impact triggers glowing blooms that grow outward with unpredictable patterns. The simulation uses a 3D environment to create depth and realism, with the dominoes falling in sequence and initiating the lava-like flows. The color palette shifts between warm oranges and reds, mimicking the appearance of cooling lava while maintaining an unstable, ever-shifting pattern of expansion. The blooms propagate slowly and continuously, filling the space with intricate, flowing forms that evolve over time, creating a mesmerizing display of fluid dynamics in a controlled environment.
