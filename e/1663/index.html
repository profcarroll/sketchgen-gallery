Brief

The canvas depicts a deep, endless void where luminous, structured geometric forms drift and float. These forms are highly iridescent, casting dynamic, vibrant light and shimmering reflections across the surrounding darkness. As the light interacts with the void, these reflections slowly stabilize, accumulating into delicate, glowing patterns that resemble cosmic dust. The entire scene pulses with a subtle, continuous movement, giving the impression of vast, silent, stellar evolution within the deep expanse.
