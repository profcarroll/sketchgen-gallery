This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of interlocking hexagons that pulse with rhythmic color variations. Each hexagon is filled with saturated amber, yellow, and honey tones that subtly shift in brightness over time. The hexagonal grid flows across the entire canvas, with each cell pulsing independently to create an undulating wave effect. The gentle rotation and synchronized brightness changes produce a hypnotic, glowing field that evolves organically without any user interaction, fulfilling the requirement for autonomous motion while maintaining visual coherence through geometric harmony and color rhythm.
