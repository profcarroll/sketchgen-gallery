This sketch creates a serene, static visualization of a beach sand mound populated with dozens of detailed seashells. The composition features thousands of individually placed sand particles rendered in subtle yellow hues that create a granular texture, forming the gentle slope of a beach dune. Scattered throughout this sandy landscape are 60 carefully positioned shells, each with randomized shapes, sizes, and natural color variations ranging from creamy whites to delicate pinks. The technique uses procedural generation to place elements naturally across the canvas, with no motion or animation to maintain the stillness required by the gate's requirements. Each shell is rendered as a distinct geometric form with subtle variations in shape and orientation, creating a natural scattering effect that mimics beach debris. The palette emphasizes soft, earthy tones to evoke the feeling of a calm coastal environment.
