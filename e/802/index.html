Brief

The canvas showcases a complex, rotating steampunk kinetic sculpture dominated by rich, oxidized metallic surfaces covered in verdigris patina. At its core, a solid, rotating cube glows intensely with thick, viscous molten bronze, which features distinct internal currents of liquid fire. Orbiting around this luminous core are intricate splines, appearing to be crafted from tarnished copper and segments of sea glass. These splines constantly rotate in patterned orbits, dynamically catching and reflecting the vibrant, intense glow of the molten center, creating a mesmerizing display of engineered mechanics and raw, subterranean heat.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch depicts a steampunk kinetic sculpture with a central glowing cube made of molten bronze and orbiting splines crafted from tarnished copper and sea glass. The scene uses WebGL rendering to create a 3D environment where the core cube rotates slowly while emitting intense light, and the surrounding splines trace intricate orbital paths that dynamically reflect this glow. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of textured spheres and continuous rotation animation, simulating the mechanical complexity and atmospheric heat of a large-scale kinetic sculpture.
