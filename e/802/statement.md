This sketch depicts a steampunk kinetic sculpture with a central glowing cube made of molten bronze and orbiting splines crafted from tarnished copper and sea glass. The scene uses WebGL rendering to create a 3D environment where the core cube rotates slowly while emitting intense light, and the surrounding splines trace intricate orbital paths that dynamically reflect this glow. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of textured spheres and continuous rotation animation, simulating the mechanical complexity and atmospheric heat of a large-scale kinetic sculpture.
