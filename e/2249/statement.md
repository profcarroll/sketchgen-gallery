This sketch creates an immersive, evolving visualization of a geological crystal lattice cross-section. Using a structured tessellation pattern, it generates translucent geometric cells that shimmer with deep, saturated hues and subtle internal movements. The design mimics the appearance of crystalline structures formed over geological time, with each cell displaying intricate internal lines and soft glows that suggest light emanating from within. The slow color shifts and dynamic distortions create an organic sense of passage, while the layered frame structure evokes the natural boundaries of stone formations. The result is a field of luminous geometry that pulses with quiet energy, inviting viewers to perceive the slow, patient processes of natural crystallization.
