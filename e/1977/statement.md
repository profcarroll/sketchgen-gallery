This sketch begins with an intense zoom into a single point of light, from which geometric patterns burst outward in rapid motion. As the camera pulls back, these ephemeral forms dissolve and reorganize into the structured complexity of a stained-glass panel. The visual journey moves from chaotic particle dynamics to ordered, luminous geometry, capturing both the explosive energy of the initial light and the serene permanence of the final architectural form. The interplay between fleeting motion and enduring structure creates a meditation on how chaos can resolve into elegant order.
