This sketch creates a futuristic spaceship control panel with interactive switches and buttons arranged in a dashboard layout. The interface features geometric design elements with distinct color coding for active and inactive states. Users must click the switches and buttons in a specific sequence to unlock the hyperspace activation feature. When the correct sequence is entered, the central windscreen section activates, displaying a dynamic hyperspace effect with thousands of particles streaking toward the center of the screen. The visualization uses a particle system with motion towards a focal point to simulate the sensation of entering hyperspace, while maintaining performance within strict frame budget constraints by limiting particle count and draw operations per frame.
