This sketch presents a digital interpretation of corrugated cardboard assembling into a three-dimensional structure through complementary interlocking patterns. Viewers observe flat, patterned pieces that gradually move and connect, guided by an invisible gear system. The animation begins with individual cardboard elements floating in space, each carrying a distinctive corrugated texture. As the simulation progresses, these pieces begin to attract toward each other, eventually forming connections that create a complex sculptural form. The viewer's interaction—simulated through a click—initiates this process and triggers the emergence of a mechanical assembly from an array of flat components. The work explores how form arises from information architecture, where the interlocking patterns and gear mechanisms guide the structural relationships between elements, resulting in a self-assembling structure that emerges from planar components.
