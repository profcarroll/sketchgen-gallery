This sketch visualizes luminous data streams flowing through a dark void, creating an immersive experience of rhythmic light patterns. The visualization features helical ribbons of light that pulse and shift in harmony, eventually forming sharp geometric nodes at their intersection points. The motion is generated algorithmically without any user input, with the camera slowly rotating to reveal the three-dimensional structure. The streams are rendered using WebGL for performance, maintaining a consistent visual rhythm while emphasizing the crystalline organization of the light patterns.
