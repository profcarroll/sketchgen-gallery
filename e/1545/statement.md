This sketch depicts a cosmic dance of luminous orbs drifting through deep space, moving in slow, cohesive patterns that evoke the vast, invisible currents of galactic motion. Each orb maintains its individual glow while subtly grouping with others to form larger, fluid movements across the canvas. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of random initial positions and velocities, combined with group-based attraction forces that pull nearby orbs toward one another, creating a sense of organic flow without any direct user input. The pulsing animation gives each orb a breathing quality, enhancing the feeling of life within the cosmic void. The scene evolves naturally over time, requiring no interaction from the viewer to maintain its mesmerizing motion.
