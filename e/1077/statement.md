This sketch creates a mesmerizing, dynamic tessellation of rotating hexagons that pulse with glowing, high-contrast colors. The system responds to both mouse clicks and audio input, causing the pattern to shift dramatically—hexagons collapse into fractured segments on click and react to sound levels by changing rotation speed and color. The visual tension builds through continuous stress fractures radiating from the hexagon centers, while connections between nearby cells maintain the overall lattice structure. The piece explores themes of structural instability and transformation, where viewer interaction and audio input directly influence the emergence of new visual patterns from an initially stable system.
