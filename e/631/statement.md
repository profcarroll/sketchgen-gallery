This sketch creates an immersive, accelerating tunnel through a crystalline molecular space using p5.js's WEBGL mode. Particles flow rapidly forward while glowing shards drift along visible energy conduits embedded in the tunnel walls and floor. The scene features dynamic bioluminescent lighting that shifts over time, creating an ethereal depth. The visualization uses a combination of particle systems, geometric structures, and pulsing light effects to simulate movement through organized chaos, with all elements advancing automatically without user input.
