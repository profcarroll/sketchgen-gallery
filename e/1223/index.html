Brief

Pale squares sit in an even grid on a near-black ground. Each one carries its own slow cycle of opacity, offset from its neighbours, so the field never brightens or dims all at once; a viewer watching for a few seconds sees it breathe unevenly across its width. Nothing changes position or size.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a field of pale, slowly pulsing squares arranged in a regular grid. Each square follows its own independent cycle of opacity changes, offset from its neighbors to create a wave-like breathing effect across the entire canvas. The subtle variations in timing produce an organic, undulating rhythm that never synchronizes fully — a gentle, hypnotic pulse that draws attention without being jarring. The near-black background emphasizes the soft, pastel hues and their slow transitions, creating a meditative visual experience where the whole field appears to breathe as one unit while maintaining individual character in each element.
