Brief

The canvas displays an ethereal, continuous flow of translucent, overlapping circles, resembling a slow cosmic tide. Interspersed within this current are visible pointillist stars that gently pulsate. These stars do not maintain a single color, but instead cycle through a spectrum of bright, rainbow hues, creating a dynamic and luminous environment that moves constantly across the canvas.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic environment where translucent, overlapping circles flow continuously across the canvas, mimicking a slow tidal motion. Embedded within this fluid current are countless pointillist stars that gently pulse with rainbow hues, cycling through the entire spectrum to create a dynamic luminous field. The visual effect combines smooth organic movement with subtle color transitions, evoking the feeling of being immersed in a vast, ever-shifting cosmic tide where light and motion dance together in constant flux.
