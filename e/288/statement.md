This sketch creates a serene seascape populated by whimsical paper-cut ships gliding across a water surface. Each vessel moves independently with subtle variations in speed and direction, leaving behind delicate fading wake trails that dissipate into the scene. The ships feature rich wood textures and weathered details, rendered with simple geometric forms that capture the essence of aged maritime craft. The composition evokes a gentle sense of departure and movement without any user interaction, as the canvas continuously evolves through the autonomous motion of the vessels and their ephemeral trails.
