Brief

The canvas displays five distinct, colored rectangular pads arranged horizontally in a single row. Each pad features smoothly rounded corners and occupies a unique color space. When a user clicks on one of the individual pads, that pad's appearance changes—it lights up—and simultaneously generates a corresponding musical note. The pads remain visible and stationary until they are interacted with.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a minimal interactive musical interface consisting of five colored pads arranged horizontally. Each pad has rounded corners and a distinct hue in the HSB color space. When clicked, a pad lights up and plays a corresponding musical note — the frequency increases with position from left to right. The visual feedback is immediate and clear: lit pads change appearance by increasing their brightness and stroke weight, while sound is generated through oscillators that respond only to user interaction. The design avoids unnecessary complexity, focusing on clean aesthetics and responsive interaction within strict performance constraints for headless execution.
