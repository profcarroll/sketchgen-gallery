This sketch depicts a vast, iridescent crystalline landscape in constant motion, where massive geological forces tear apart and reshape the terrain. The scene features hundreds of interlocking crystal formations that grow and shear violently in real-time, creating dazzling trails of fractured light and deep color. Each crystal structure pulses with internal energy, branching out in dynamic patterns as they fracture and reconfigure. Flowing mineral veins weave through the landscape like living arteries, adding movement and depth to the otherwise static geological chaos. The viewer witnesses an endless, powerful geological event unfolding across a suspended mountain range, where light and shadow dance across fractured crystal surfaces in a continuous, mesmerizing display of natural forces at work.
