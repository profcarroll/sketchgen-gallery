Brief

The canvas displays a dramatic, high-contrast nighttime scene featuring a vibrant cityscape overlooking a busy harbor. Numerous drones navigate this environment, forming intricate, evolving geometric patterns across the night sky. As the patterns shift and reform, they are punctuated by sudden, bright bursts of light. The entire display is dynamic and mesmerizing, continually adjusting its choreography in response to the viewer's mouse movements and clicks, allowing them to actively cycle the drones into new formations and light displays.
