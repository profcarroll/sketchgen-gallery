Brief

The canvas displays abstract, architectural planes defined by solid geometry. Luminous rays emanate from an unseen source, slicing across these rigid structures and etching complex patterns onto their surfaces. As the computed rays intersect the architectural mass, they create vibrant, ephemeral crystalline points of light. These glowing shadows shift and dissipate over time, manifesting patterns such as diffraction, until the field of light stabilizes, offering a continuous study of how computational energy interacts with solid, planar geometry.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch explores the interaction between computational energy and solid geometry through abstract architectural planes and luminous rays. I've created a dynamic scene where geometric structures float in space, intersected by thousands of glowing particles that move according to complex mathematical patterns. The rays emanate from an unseen source and slice through the rigid forms, creating ephemeral points of light that shift and dissipate over time. The crystalline patterns emerge not from direct collisions, but from the careful choreography of movement and intersection, forming a continuous study of how abstract energy interacts with planar geometry. The viewer witnesses an evolving field of light that stabilizes into coherent patterns while remaining perpetually in flux.
