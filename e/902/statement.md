This sketch depicts a whimsical underwater scene where aged paper-cut ships drift through a translucent medium, leaving behind mineral salt trails that grow into root-like structures over time. The ships are richly detailed with visible moss and barnacles, and as they sail, their wakes dissolve into crystalline streaks that gradually form organic root systems on the hulls. These root structures slowly cause sections of the ship hull to warp and tear away, leaving scattered fragments that accumulate at the bottom of the scene. The entire composition evolves organically without any user input, with ships moving independently, salt streaks dissolving, roots growing, and particles scattering naturally across the canvas.
