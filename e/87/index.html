Brief

The viewer stands at the edge of an overwhelmingly vast, artificially lit indoor pool. The water surface occupies the entire visible area, reflecting a bright, diffused sky that seems to originate from nowhere. The space is cavernous, defined by smooth, muted architectural elements that recede into a misty background. The water itself is never static; gentle, rippling movements cause light shafts to refract across the pool floor, shifting the visible colour from pale aqua to bright silver. The scale of the empty environment creates a quiet, unsettling sense of infinite depth.
