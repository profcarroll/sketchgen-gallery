This sketch creates an immersive, infinite pool environment using WebGL rendering in p5.js. The scene features a vast water surface that reflects a diffuse, artificial sky, with gentle ripples causing dynamic light refractions across the pool floor. The cavernous architecture recedes into misty depth, enhancing the sense of infinite space and quiet unease. Through procedural animation and subtle color shifts, it evokes the unsettling scale and stillness of an empty, artificially lit aquatic space.
