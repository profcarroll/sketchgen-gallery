This sketch simulates a biological ecosystem of self-replicating cells that grow and divide autonomously. The canvas displays dozens of brightly colored, rounded shapes that continuously multiply through an automated replication cycle. Each cell ages and gradually swells before undergoing a visible separation process where it pinches in two, creating two daughter cells. This cycle repeats indefinitely, causing the population to grow exponentially without any viewer interaction. The visual effect is achieved through simple physics-based animation where cells swell and split with smooth transitions, creating an organic, living system that evolves on its own.
