This sketch simulates the organic growth of filamentous mold spreading across a substrate surface. Using WebGL rendering, it creates thousands of delicate mycelial filaments that grow slowly and organically from random starting points. The filaments branch out in three-dimensional space, penetrating into the substrate with realistic depth and lighting effects. Each filament is rendered as a continuous line with variable thickness and color, creating a complex network that evolves over time. The simulation uses spatial hashing to manage performance while maintaining the visual complexity described in the brief. The scene continuously evolves without user interaction, demonstrating natural growth patterns through procedural generation and physics-based movement.
