Brief

The canvas displays a vast, evolving grid composed of many interconnected squares. Individual cells transition between states and colors based on complex life-and-death rules, creating dynamic, shifting patterns across the entire surface. This simulated biological activity causes the entire field of cells to move and change continuously. Over time, the background surrounding the lively grid subtly and perpetually darkens, lending a gradual sense of passage and depth to the otherwise cyclical and automatic visual spectacle.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a dynamic grid of interconnected cells that evolve according to complex life-like rules. Each cell transitions between active and dormant states, with connections forming and dissolving based on proximity and state. The entire field pulses with organic movement as patterns propagate across the canvas. Simultaneously, the background gradually darkens, creating a sense of temporal depth that enhances the mesmerizing, ever-shifting visual spectacle.
