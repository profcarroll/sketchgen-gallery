This sketch renders a mesmerizing visualization of luminous data streams flowing through a vast dark void. The composition features multiple helical ribbons that twist and spiral in three-dimensional space, each with its own color, speed, and path. The streams are organized into crystalline structures with sharp edges and glowing interiors, creating a sense of depth and directional flow. The motion is continuous and complex, with the camera slowly moving to reveal the full scope of the intertwining patterns. The visual effect emphasizes the self-sustaining nature of the light, creating an immersive experience that suggests both order and chaos within the vast subterranean space.
