This sketch creates an ethereal atmosphere of drifting steam that gradually forms into fleeting, luminous shapes resembling forgotten words. Through a combination of noise-based particle movement and spatial hashing for efficient neighbor detection, thousands of soft particles drift across the canvas, occasionally coalescing into stable formations. These glowing structures pulse with internal light before dissolving back into vapor. The animation uses a grid-based system to prevent expensive all-pairs comparisons while maintaining the organic, flowing motion characteristic of steam. The result is a mesmerizing, slow-motion meditation on memory's ephemeral nature, where meaning briefly emerges and fades like mist in the air.
