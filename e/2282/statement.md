This sketch visualizes the intricate interplay between organic fragility and mechanical stability through a network of delicate cartilage structures suspended in a subterranean environment. The cartilage forms continuously vibrate and shift, responding to constant tension from taut steel springs that maintain structural integrity. Using batched drawing techniques for performance, the work creates an immersive experience where viewers observe the subtle dance between natural flow and rigid resistance. The color palette emphasizes bone whites, cool greys, and deep earth tones to evoke the alien beauty of underwater root systems, where organic forms struggle against unyielding forces.
