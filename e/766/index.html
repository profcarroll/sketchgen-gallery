Brief

The canvas presents a perpetually shifting abstraction of bold, intersecting geometric bands. These bands are composed of vivid, intersecting planes that are themselves segmented by visible vertical or diagonal stripe patterns. The color palette is rich and deeply saturated, cycling through bold contrasts. The overall composition is continuously dynamic, forming a mesmerizing, interlocking tapestry of lines and segmented color that shifts slowly and constantly across the display.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a mesmerizing, interlocking tapestry of shifting geometric bands that continuously evolve across the canvas. Using a combination of rotating arcs and dynamic line overlays, it generates a richly saturated color field where bold intersecting planes are segmented by visible stripe patterns. The composition is built from multiple animated bands that move at different speeds and angles, creating an ever-changing visual rhythm. The subtle pulsing motion in the stripe patterns adds depth and complexity, while the color palette cycles through vivid contrasts to emphasize the interplay between forms. Each frame presents a new configuration of these abstract geometric relationships, producing an hypnotic effect that shifts slowly yet constantly across the entire display.
