This sketch creates an evolving abstract quilt where geometric patches weave across the canvas in a continuous day-night cycle. Each patch is a rectangle with dynamic color that slowly transitions through the full spectrum of daylight—from deep night blues to vibrant sunrise oranges and bright zenith whites. The patches move gently in organic patterns, their colors shifting over time as they reorganize, creating an impression of flowing light and temporal passage. The visual effect represents the slow, inevitable progression of time through the changing hours, with each element maintaining its form while continuously transforming.
