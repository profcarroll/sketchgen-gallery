This sketch simulates the generative process of weaving through a system of animated threads that move and interact within a loom. Each thread has its own color, tension, and movement pattern, creating an organic flow that resembles the fluid motion of fabric being woven. As threads cross paths and form connections, they temporarily stabilize into geometric motifs like squares, diamonds, and triangles before continuing their weaving dance. The system uses spatial hashing to efficiently manage thread interactions and maintain performance, while the color palette shifts over time to create evolving visual patterns. The constant motion of the threads, punctuated by moments of stabilization, captures the dynamic tension between chaos and order inherent in the weaving process.
