This sketch creates a mesmerizing cosmic scene where luminous orbs drift through space guided by invisible spiral energy fields. The orbs—each with unique colors and sizes—move in response to these central forces that manifest as slow, swirling currents radiating from the canvas center. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of physics-based motion and carefully crafted force fields that create organic, hypnotic movement patterns. The orbs pulse gently as they drift, creating an ethereal glow that enhances their luminous quality, while the visible spiral fields provide a sense of invisible cosmic forces at work. The result is a constantly shifting display of light and color that feels both chaotic and harmonious, evoking the sensation of floating through a vast, dynamic space filled with invisible currents.
