This sketch creates an evolving cosmic dance by simulating two celestial bodies orbiting a central point in opposition, generating intricate spirograph patterns. The motion of these virtual planets traces arcs and closed loops across the canvas, forming vibrant, symmetrical designs that shift rhythmically over time. Using a combination of orbital mechanics and color theory, it produces a mesmerizing visual effect that feels both harmonious and dynamic—capturing the essence of cosmic choreography through mathematical precision and aesthetic vibrancy.
