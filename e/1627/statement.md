This sketch creates a dynamic stained-glass simulation that evolves through a full day's cycle. The composition features 80 geometric panels of varying shapes and colors arranged across a dark canvas. As time progresses, sweeping beams of light traverse the structure, illuminating the internal edges where they create glowing halos. These halos expand and contract while shifting in angle, mimicking the sun's trajectory from dawn to night. The scene transitions through warm morning hues into cool evening purples, with the entire effect rendered in real-time using WebGL for performance. The viewer experiences a continuous, immersive visualization of light moving through architectural space without any user interaction.
