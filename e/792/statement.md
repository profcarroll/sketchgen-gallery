This sketch creates an atmospheric winter landscape with layered geological formations and drifting snow. The scene features rocky terrain etched with natural channels that respond to user interaction through a triggered avalanche effect. The viewer experiences subtle environmental motion through camera movement and snow physics, while clicking produces a localized avalanche that deposits fresh snow specifically within the crevices of foreground rocks. The technique uses WEBGL rendering for depth perception, procedural generation for terrain elements, and particle systems for atmospheric snowfall that responds to both time-based animation and user input events.
