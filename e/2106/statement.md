This sketch depicts a vast, subterranean machinery network rendered in patinated brass tones, where industrial components drift slowly through time. The scene captures the essence of deep time through the continuous motion and cyclical patterns of mechanical parts, while overgrown vines snake between them, suggesting life reclaiming decayed space. The viewer observes a silent rhythm of endless mechanical drift, with brass gears and pipes moving imperceptibly across the canvas, punctuated by organic growth that breathes life into the static industrial expanse.
