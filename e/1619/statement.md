This sketch depicts the collapse of a perfectly symmetrical mandala structure made of colorful sand grains. The work begins with a stable, intricate pattern that gradually vibrates as wind force increases. At a critical moment, the symmetry fractures and grains begin to detach and fall in an asymmetrical cascade, transforming the ordered structure into scattered debris. The visualization uses physics-based particle movement with gravity and wind forces to create the dynamic collapse effect. The piece explores themes of stability, fragility, and the transformation from order to chaos through a visual metaphor of geometric perfection breaking apart under external forces.
