A layered collage of drifting vintage photographs and torn paper scraps creates an ethereal archival atmosphere. The pieces move slowly across the canvas with organic, pendulum-like motion, occasionally freezing in place to create moments of contemplative stillness. Sepia-toned photographs fade into the background while textured paper scraps with visible frayed edges drift atop them, their delicate imperfections emphasized by pre-computed edge overlays that simulate torn, weathered material.
