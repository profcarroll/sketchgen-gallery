This sketch creates an ethereal field of glowing, organic planes that flow across the screen in a continuous, mesmerizing motion. The planes drift slowly and smoothly, maintaining their amorphous, fluid appearance while occasionally undergoing a dramatic transformation into sharp geometric shapes that align with an underlying hexagonal grid. The visual effect captures the tension between organic movement and geometric order, creating a sense of fluid evolution where natural forms gradually adapt to structured patterns. The interplay between the continuous flow and periodic transitions produces a hypnotic, ever-changing landscape that feels both organic and systematically ordered.
