This sketch depicts a glass container filled with flowing liquid, capturing the visual dynamics of a continuous pour. The scene features a geometric transparent container against a neutral background, with liquid drops falling from above and splashing into the existing fluid below. I used particle-based simulation for the falling drops, incorporating gravity and viscosity to create realistic motion. Upon impact, the drops generate ripples that propagate outward, simulating surface tension effects. The liquid level steadily rises within the container, creating a subtle distortion effect on the background as it fills. The technique focuses on fluid simulation using simple physics principles while maintaining visual appeal through color and timing of the animation.
