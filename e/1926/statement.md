This sketch visualizes an architectural scene where geometric forms meet a luminous liquid surface, driven by a central pendulum mechanism. The composition features nested bioluminescent crystal formations that pulse and glow in rhythmic response to the pendulum's motion. Through the WEBGL renderer, I've created a sense of depth and volumetric complexity with multiple crystal structures rotating independently while maintaining their geometric integrity. The pendulum's swinging motion transfers kinetic energy through the system, causing crystals to respond with synchronized pulsing effects and subtle positional shifts, creating an mesmerizing interplay between rigid architectural forms and fluid, organic light patterns.
