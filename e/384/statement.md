This sketch implements a cellular automaton simulation with dynamic visual effects and evolving patterns. The system consists of a grid of cells that follow simple rules for birth, survival, and death, creating emergent behaviors over time. Each cell pulses with residual glow when it's born or dies, and the entire canvas background rhythmically pulses between dark and light. Active cells are connected visually through thin lines to their neighbors within a two-cell radius, forming an organic web of interaction. The simulation runs autonomously without user input, producing continuously shifting patterns that evolve across generations.
