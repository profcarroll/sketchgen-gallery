This sketch simulates the geological process of mineral vein formation within a quartz-filled seam. It depicts a damp, translucent environment where vibrant colors diffuse and blend organically, mimicking oxidation and crystal growth over time. The visual field is structured by delicate rubber band constraints that create tension and guide the flow of color. Particles move gently through space, slowly shifting hues and blending with others, creating stochastic streaks that suggest the quiet passage of geological time. Translucent lines from the rubber bands provide structural boundaries that frame and direct the movement of the colored particles, giving a sense of both stability and dynamic change within the mineral formation process.
