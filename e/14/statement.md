This sketch creates a mesmerizing, ever-shifting lattice of glowing lines that appear to breathe and flow across the canvas. Using a combination of trigonometric motion, color cycling, and dynamic line drawing, it generates an intricate web of intersecting paths that evolve without any user input. The system uses 500 independently moving lines, each with its own speed, direction, and hue, creating a complex interplay of light and shadow that feels both organic and mathematically precise. The subtle fade effect and continuous color shifting give the impression of a living current of light, constantly redefining itself in an endless dance of motion and form.
