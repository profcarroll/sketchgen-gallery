The sketch loads a photograph and translates it into pure motion: invisible currents derived from the image's luminance guide 1200 particles that flow across the canvas. Each particle samples and inherits the color from the region of the source image it occupies, creating an animated reinterpretation where photographic data becomes the score for generative movement. The image emerges not as a picture but as a choreography—viewers recognize the source through the flow patterns alone as particles swirl and drift, building up trails that echo the photograph's structure in motion and color.
