This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of rotating hexagonal tessellations that evoke the fluid motion of digitized liquid. The visual style employs a saturated 1980s color palette with vibrant hues and dynamic saturation shifts. Each hexagon in the grid rotates independently while its color cycles through varying levels of chroma, creating a hypnotic interplay of motion and color. The continuous rotation and shifting saturation produce an autonomous, ever-evolving pattern that appears to flow like a living liquid structure. The use of WEBGL rendering ensures smooth 3D transformations and depth perception, while the algorithmic color modulation creates natural-looking transitions between vibrant and muted tones across the entire canvas.
