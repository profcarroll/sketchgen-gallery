This sketch creates a stylized sonar visualization with geometric helical patterns expanding from the center. The visual effect is achieved through a system of particles that spiral outward while oscillating in a precise helix pattern, creating sharp, defined waveforms that fill the canvas. The color scheme transitions from deep blue to violet hues, emphasizing the atmospheric quality of the radar-like display. The rhythmic pulsations and geometric precision give the impression of controlled energy waves propagating through space, with visual connections between points reinforcing the structured helical motion without overwhelming the composition.
