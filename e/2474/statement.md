This sketch presents a detailed vertical cross-section of a geological fault rendered entirely in earth tones and corrugated cardboard textures. The composition features layered sedimentary rocks with varying hues of browns and ochres, each layer subtly textured to simulate the appearance of weathered cardboard. Embedded within these geological strata are countless fossilized root systems—complex networks of thin brown lines radiating from central points, creating a sense of deep time and preserved biological history. The entire scene is static and unchanging, emphasizing the eternal compression and preservation of natural materials. Through careful attention to color, texture, and density of elements, the work evokes the patient accumulation of geological time while maintaining an almost tactile quality that invites contemplation of the ancient processes that shaped our world.
