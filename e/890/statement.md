This sketch renders an abstracted urban landscape using flowing light streaks that interact and converge to form crystalline structures. The composition consists of hundreds of moving streams that leave trails across the canvas, with their paths dynamically calculated and drawn in monochromatic tones. At key intersection points where streams cross, they solidify into geometric clusters that rotate slowly, creating a sense of energy flow through the urban environment. The scene evolves autonomously without user input, with new clusters forming at stream intersections while older ones are periodically removed to maintain visual balance and performance within the constraints of the p5.js framework.
