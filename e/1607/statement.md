This sketch simulates a view through a rain-streaked window, where falling raindrops distort the vision of tiny, upside-down cityscapes within each droplet. The raindrops move dynamically down the screen, their paths subtly swaying to mimic wind, while each contains a miniature city that pulses gently with a rhythmic glow. The visual effect creates an atmospheric interplay between the motion of rain and the calm, cyclical light of the contained worlds, evoking a sense of quiet observation and gentle movement.
