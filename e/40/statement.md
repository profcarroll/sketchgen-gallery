This sketch creates a dynamic, self-contained visualization of fluid geometric forms that continuously morph and interact with each other. The canvas displays interconnected shapes—circles, rectangles, and ellipses—that move in synchronized yet independent patterns, oscillating between smooth transitions and sudden transformations. Each form evolves through time with shifting colors, changing sizes, and rotating orientations, while also maintaining visual connections to nearby forms through subtle lines. The entire system operates without any user input, driven purely by mathematical oscillations that create a sense of natural rhythm and internal balance, suggesting an organic cycle of motion and stability.
