This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of dynamic, diagonal stripes that evoke the bold, vibrant color palettes of the 1970s. Through continuous motion and smooth easing techniques, the stripes shift and transform across the canvas in rhythmic patterns. Each stripe moves at its own speed and width, creating an illusion of depth as they appear to pull apart and reform. The combination of diagonal orientation, color transitions, and fluid motion produces an hypnotic effect that evolves organically without any user input, fulfilling the requirement for autonomous visual change.
