This sketch renders a luminous, evolving tapestry of neon lines and particles that pulse and flow in three-dimensional space. The composition features thousands of glowing points and connecting lines that continuously shift and morph, creating an abstract yet structured visual rhythm. Every so often, the chaotic motion resolves into fleeting geometric forms—suggesting architectural grids or blueprints—before dissolving back into fluid, electric patterns. The piece embodies the dynamic, cumulative nature of learning and design, with motion and structure coexisting in a perpetual dance of form and energy.
