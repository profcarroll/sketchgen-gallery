This sketch creates a static, geometric composition inspired by 1920s Bauhaus design principles. It arranges a grid of modular tiles in primary colors—red, blue, and yellow—against a white background, forming a structured machine-like framework. The work emphasizes precision, mathematical proportions, and rational order through its strict grid layout and limited color palette. Each tile is uniformly sized and positioned to create a sense of architectural stability and industrial discipline characteristic of early modernist design movements.
