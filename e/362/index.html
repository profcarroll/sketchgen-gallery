Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch renders a stylized, muscular horse in mid-gallop using simplified geometric shapes and vibrant colors to suggest speed and momentum. The animation is self-contained, with the horse moving rhythmically across the canvas through continuous position and rotation changes. The motion appears natural due to sinusoidal oscillations in both translation and rotation, creating a cyclical galloping effect that maintains visual interest without requiring any user input or complex interactions.
