This sketch visualizes a dynamic network of flowing light streams that emerge from the luminance patterns of a source photograph. The particles follow vector fields derived from image brightness, creating fluid motion paths that shimmer with the colors of the original image. Each particle carries its own color information sampled directly from the photograph, while maintaining sharp boundaries where streams intersect, mimicking crystalline separation. The result is an ever-shifting tapestry of radiant trails that pulse and flow like liquid light across the screen, responding to the structure and contrast of the source imagery.
