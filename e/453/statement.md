This sketch visualizes an impossible architectural structure composed of fractured geometric shards suspended in a vast, dark void. The shards fall under gravity but momentarily halt their collapse, achieving a tense balance between dissolution and structural integrity. Rendered in muted teal hues using WebGL, the scene shows shards dissolving into particulate mist as they fall, while those that stabilize appear to resist the chaos around them. The viewer observes this moment of defiance — where gravitational forces are countered by an ephemeral stability, creating a sense of architectural tension and spectral beauty.
