This sketch creates an immersive underwater environment using a field of semi-transparent particles that move slowly in circular currents. The visualization captures the sensation of depth and organic motion through a combination of subtle color gradients, particle swaying, and periodic ripple effects. By rendering all particles as points rather than complex 3D shapes, it maintains performance while still conveying the fluid, suspended quality of an aquatic ecosystem. The gentle, continuous motion creates a meditative effect that draws viewers into the imagined depths below.
