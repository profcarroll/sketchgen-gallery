This sketch simulates an autonomous cellular growth and division process. It begins with a cluster of small, rounded cells that grow steadily until they reach a size limit. At this point, each cell initiates a division process: it constricts its internal structure before splitting into two daughter cells. The simulation is entirely self-contained, running without user input or interaction, demonstrating continuous life cycles of growth, constriction, and replication across the canvas. Viewer attention is drawn to the rhythmic pulsing and splitting of cells as they cycle through their life stages, creating a dynamic visual rhythm.
