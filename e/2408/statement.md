This sketch presents a microscopic cross-section of a colossal alien organ through an abstracted geological structure. Using layered rectangles with subtle movement and color shifts, it evokes the slow accumulation and bleeding of organic forms. The composition uses deep, saturated colors that slowly shift hues over time, creating an ever-transforming visual field that suggests both vast scale and biological decay. The layered approach creates depth while maintaining a sense of organic flow, as if observing the internal structure of a massive, semi-organic entity at the grain level.
