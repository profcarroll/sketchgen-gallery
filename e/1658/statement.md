This sketch creates a mesmerizing visualization of centripetal force through a swarm of luminous particles that orbit an unseen central point. Each particle follows a circular path while leaving behind a glowing trail that gradually fades, creating a sense of continuous motion and gravitational pull. The visual effect is achieved through careful management of particle physics—applying constant inward force to simulate gravity—combined with trail rendering that uses alpha blending to create smooth, fading lines. The entire scene operates without user input, demonstrating the principles of orbital mechanics in a hypnotic, self-contained animation.
