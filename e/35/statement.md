This sketch creates a meditation on color and form inspired by Mark Rothko's luminous abstract paintings. Using a carefully selected palette of deep, saturated hues, I've constructed layered rectangles that pulse and breathe with subtle, organic motion. The composition avoids sharp edges and rigid geometry, instead allowing the colors to diffuse into one another through overlapping translucent layers. Each rectangle subtly shifts in size, position, and opacity over time, creating an impression of depth and emotional resonance. The slow, undulating movement mimics the gentle breathing of a living canvas, evoking the contemplative quality found in Rothko's work while maintaining the dynamic energy of digital media.
