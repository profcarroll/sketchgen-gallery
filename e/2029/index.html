Brief

The scene presents a cavernous, abandoned architectural space, hinting at the former grandeur of a speculative city. Viewers observe vast sheets of oxidized steel and concrete, dominated by the colors of deep rust, oxidized reds, and metallic grays. The geometry is fundamentally broken, appearing less like solid structures and more like particulate decay. Slow, continuous motion simulates the structural degradation, causing sheets and fragments to subtly peel, crumble, and rain down as flakes of rust and stressed metal, transforming monumental forms into ephemeral piles of decay.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch portrays the gradual decay of a grand architectural space through the lens of crumbling steel and concrete. Viewers witness massive structural elements slowly breaking apart into smaller fragments, with each piece exhibiting its own motion and aging process. The rusted reds and metallic grays dominate the palette, evoking the passage of time and neglect. As fragments age, they disintegrate further into smaller pieces that drift downward, simulating a continuous collapse. The scene moves slowly but constantly, with subtle rotations and drifting motions that create an atmosphere of inevitable decay and transformation from monument to ruin.
