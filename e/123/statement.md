This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of glowing, amorphous shapes that float and undulate across the canvas with fluid motion. Using Perlin noise for organic movement and color transitions, the abstract forms pulse with varying saturation and luminosity, evoking the nostalgic glow of 1980s neon aesthetics. The continuous, self-generated animation creates an atmospheric, breathing effect as shapes dissolve and reform without any geometric boundaries or fixed patterns.
