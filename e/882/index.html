Brief

A captivating, multi-component steampunk sculpture rotates around a central core. Elaborate globe splines, crafted from tarnished copper and deep emerald glass, orbit this structure. The core itself is a steadily rotating cube, while the glass tracery exhibits a profound internal luminescence. Deep emerald glass glows with visible, slow-moving energy veins that flow through the complex crystalline structure, creating a continuously shifting and mesmerizing spectacle of shape, copper tones, and radiant green light.

This sketch embodies a steampunk-inspired sculpture where a central rotating cube serves as the core structure. Surrounding this core are orbiting globes crafted from deep emerald glass that slowly circle around it in synchronized yet subtly varied paths. Within these globes, slow-moving energy veins pulse with luminous green light, creating an atmosphere of intricate, glowing complexity. The scene rotates continuously to showcase its multi-layered geometry, with each component contributing to a mesmerizing interplay of form and radiant color that evokes the aesthetic of mechanical artistry merged with ethereal energy.
