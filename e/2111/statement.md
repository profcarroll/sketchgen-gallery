This sketch transforms the concept of a Persian rug's intricate woven pattern into a geological representation of deep-time fault lines crystallizing. The composition begins with a robust border that alternates between diamond and square shapes, mimicking the layered strata of geological formations. The central field displays a complex lattice grid where each cell reveals stylized motifs that suggest the emergence of fault lines over time, using a color palette evoking earth tones and mineral deposits. The entire pattern maintains strict mathematical symmetry while suggesting the slow, deliberate process of geological formation through its layered construction and fault-line motifs.
