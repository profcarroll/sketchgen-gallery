This sketch explores the quiet interplay between decay and formation in a damp, earthbound landscape. The crumpled cardboard substrate represents the fragile remnants of structure, while delicate mineral deposits slowly emerge and dissolve across its surface. The muted browns and deep earth tones dominate the palette, punctuated by brilliant prismatic crystals that drift slowly through the scene. The effect captures the patient, rhythmic forces of entropy and crystallization, where formations bloom and fade in a continuous cycle. Through simple geometric shapes and subtle animations, it presents a meditative study of natural change and structural fragility at a macro scale.
