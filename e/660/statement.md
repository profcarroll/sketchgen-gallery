This sketch depicts a dynamic, geometric system composed of angular fragments that oscillate between states of stability and violent disruption. In the stable phase, the fragments pulse with radiant connections forming an intricate network, visually representing contained energy. Periodically, the system enters a shattering phase where these connections snap and fragments fly outward in sharp, chaotic motion. The scene then transitions into a reforming state where the fragments gradually return to a new, cohesive structure. The movement is cyclical and self-sustaining, with no external input required to drive the animation. The visual effect emphasizes tension and release through color, motion, and structural transformation.
