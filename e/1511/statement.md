This sketch depicts a vast, frigid seascape dominated by a massive glacier face rendered in deep blues and stark whites. The scene captures the slow, continuous process of calving where huge chunks of ice break away from the main glacier face. These ice chunks tumble into the deep water below with dynamic movement emphasizing gravity and water displacement. The visualization includes dramatic waves and spray that drift slowly through the cool air, creating a sense of vastness and natural power. The implementation uses performance-conscious techniques to render thousands of particles efficiently within WebGL's constraints, ensuring smooth animation even on resource-constrained environments.
