This sketch renders a highly symmetrical textile pattern inspired by classic Persian rugs, featuring deep jewel tones and intricate geometric motifs. The composition is built upon an underlying lattice grid filled with stylized floral and vine elements that echo traditional rug weaving. A distinct outer border frames the design using a continuous interlocking band of large alternating diamond and square shapes, creating a rich, handcrafted aesthetic. The pattern maintains strict symmetry across vertical and diagonal axes, emphasizing the mathematical precision found in traditional Persian art. The work presents an elaborate, still composition that captures the complexity and elegance of historical textile craftsmanship through digital rendering techniques.
