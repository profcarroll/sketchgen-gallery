Brief

The canvas presents a deep-space view featuring a central, brightly lit sun surrounded by a detailed, three-dimensional model of the solar system. Visible around each planet are detailed rings, and the entire scene is set against a background of glowing starfield dust illuminated by the sun. Planets and celestial bodies are rendered in full three dimensions, following luminous, glowing paths that trace their elliptical routes. While the system orbits and drifts constantly, motion is most visible and dynamic when the mouse cursor is clicked down, causing the celestial bodies to react and animate against the stellar backdrop.
