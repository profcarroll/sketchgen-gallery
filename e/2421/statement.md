This sketch visualizes the slow, powerful accumulation of tension in a soap film membrane, causing it to fold and wrinkle into complex geometric patterns reminiscent of ceramic tile joints. The simulation uses a grid-based spatial hash to efficiently connect nearby particles, creating intricate lattices that evolve over time. As tension builds through oscillating forces, the surface transforms from smooth and taut to rigid with deep, repeating folds. The iridescent color palette shifts between cool blues and teals, enhancing the sense of dynamic tension and structural complexity as the membrane's surface responds to its own internal forces.
