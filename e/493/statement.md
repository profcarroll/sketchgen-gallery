This sketch creates an abstract self-portrait using three-point perspective with interlocking crystalline planes that shift through color and light. The composition consists of multiple geometric structures rotating slowly in 3D space, each maintaining its structural integrity while undergoing continuous subtle changes in saturation and brightness. The viewer observes a meditative, breathing effect where sections of the geometry gradually brighten and dim across the color spectrum, creating an illusion of dissolution and reformation. The use of WEBGL rendering allows for smooth 3D transformations and lighting effects that maintain visual coherence while staying within the performance constraints of the environment.
