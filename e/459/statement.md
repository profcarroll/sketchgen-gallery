This sketch depicts an otherworldly abyssal landscape where geometric pyramidal structures rise from the ocean depths, emitting vibrant bioluminescent light. The scene captures the dynamic interplay between these crystalline towers and the unseen deep-sea currents that shape luminous particle streams. Through constant motion and color shifts, the viewer experiences a constantly evolving tapestry of light and flow, with particles organizing into temporary tendril-like formations before dissolving back into the current. The use of WEBGL rendering allows for smooth 3D movement and complex lighting effects that give the impression of depth and volumetric presence in this alien underwater realm.
