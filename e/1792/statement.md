This sketch depicts a vast, reflective cityscape that evolves through time, with ambient lighting shifting between day and night cycles. The scene features rigid architectural structures mirrored on wet surfaces that respond to this changing light. What makes this piece unique is the slow emergence of glowing, phosphorescent patterns—akin to ancient runes—that etch themselves across the wet stone surfaces. These patterns gradually appear, move, and dissipate, creating a mesmerizing dance of luminous geometry and reflection. The viewer observes an ever-shifting urban environment where the built world comes alive through bioluminescent processes that are both mysterious and beautiful. The entire scene operates in 3D space with dynamic lighting, camera movement, and animated elements—all within the constraints of a single p5.js sketch that runs continuously without user interaction.
