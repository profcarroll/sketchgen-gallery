This sketch creates a mesmerizing, ever-changing electrocardiogram display that pulses across a deep black canvas. The waveform is generated with highly irregular patterns—unpredictable spikes and dips that mimic the chaotic rhythm of a living heart. Using a combination of cyclical peaks, random noise, and smooth undulations, the visualization flows continuously from left to right with an electric neon glow. The three-layered approach gives depth and complexity, with each line contributing to a rich, dynamic field of energy that captures both the biological reality and abstract beauty of cardiac activity. The result is a hypnotic, self-contained animation that pulses with its own internal rhythm.
