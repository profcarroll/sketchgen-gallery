Brief

The viewer encounters an impossible, deeply complex architectural structure built of fractured, shifting planes. Teal light glints off the geometric facets, which are simultaneously undergoing decay and replication. Massive structural stresses appear across the surfaces as self-similar fractal patterns bloom outwards, causing sections of the geometry to dissolve into swirling, particulate mist. The architecture never settles; segments shift, angles warp, and the boundary between solid structure and dissolving vapor is constantly blurred.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch explores the concept of an ever-shifting architectural structure composed of geometric planes that fracture, replicate, and dissolve into mist. Using WebGL rendering, it creates a constantly evolving visual field where self-similar fractal patterns emerge and decay. The viewer witnesses a dynamic interplay between solid forms and dissipating particles, with structural stresses manifesting as shifting angles and morphing surfaces. The piece emphasizes the tension between stability and chaos through continuous geometric transformation.
