This sketch explores the concept of an ever-shifting architectural structure composed of geometric planes that fracture, replicate, and dissolve into mist. Using WebGL rendering, it creates a constantly evolving visual field where self-similar fractal patterns emerge and decay. The viewer witnesses a dynamic interplay between solid forms and dissipating particles, with structural stresses manifesting as shifting angles and morphing surfaces. The piece emphasizes the tension between stability and chaos through continuous geometric transformation.
