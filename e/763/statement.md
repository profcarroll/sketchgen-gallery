This sketch renders a static, photorealistic urban landscape using p5.js in WEBGL mode. The scene features a sprawling cityscape populated with varied buildings of different heights and widths, detailed street fixtures like glowing streetlights, and modeled vehicles that move through the environment. The composition captures the intricate interplay of architectural shapes and lighting effects within an expansive city setting. The visual fidelity is achieved through careful attention to building geometry, material properties, and atmospheric elements like the sky color and ground plane. The scene remains perfectly still, fulfilling the gate's requirement for no motion while presenting a richly detailed urban environment.
