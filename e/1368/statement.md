This sketch visualizes tidal movement through a series of horizontal color bands that rise and fall in rhythmic synchronization. Each band represents a different depth level in the water column, with darker bands highlighting the peak water level during high tide. The gentle sine wave motion creates a hypnotic, breathing effect that mimics natural oceanic fluctuation. The visualization uses layered transparency and carefully modulated colors to create depth while maintaining clean, flowing forms that capture the essence of tidal dynamics without complex particle systems or excessive detail.
