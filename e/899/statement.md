This sketch depicts a subterranean data landscape where luminous streams flow in helical patterns through dark space. The visualization uses WEBGL rendering to create three-dimensional pathways that continuously loop and twist. Each stream emits subtle sparks that leave behind glowing residue particles, creating a sense of mineral accumulation in the void. The motion is autonomous and ceaseless, with streams rotating slowly around the viewer while maintaining their structural helical forms. The technique combines procedural generation of helical paths with particle systems for the sparking effects, all rendered in a dark environment to emphasize the luminous elements.
