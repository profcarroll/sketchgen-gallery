This sketch depicts a viscous fluid environment where amorphous masses drift slowly, displaying complex internal structures and color transitions. The blobs are not solid but rather fluid entities with shifting boundaries, created through noise-based deformation of polygonal shapes. Each blob features striated patterns that move and change over time, suggesting internal pressure and flow dynamics. The transparent vessel container holds a dark, dense background that enhances the contrast and depth of the floating masses. The movement is deliberately slow and organic, emphasizing the thick, heavy nature of the fluid medium. The color gradients within each blob create a sense of depth and volumetric complexity, while the striations add surface detail that suggests internal flow patterns or chemical processes. This creates an hypnotic, ever-shifting portrait of a dense fluid system where the boundaries between entities are ambiguous and constantly redefining themselves.
