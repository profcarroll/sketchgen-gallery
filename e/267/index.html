Brief

The canvas displays abstract, flowing gradients that intersect and blend, evoking dynamic data fields typical of a 1990s television broadcast. These intersecting color fields exhibit a slow, cyclical rotation, creating a constant background movement. Superimposed across this abstract motion is the text "Channel 11 News," rendered in a stylized, retro typeface. The text is not static; it animates with subtle, deliberate movements that emphasize the professional, evolving nature of the broadcast graphic package. The entire scene maintains a perpetually shifting, yet structured, appearance.

This sketch creates a retro television broadcast aesthetic with flowing gradient backgrounds and animated text elements. The "Channel 11 News" text appears both as a static element and through particle-based animation, where individual particles gently move in synchronized but subtle patterns to emphasize the evolving nature of broadcast graphics. The entire scene maintains a structured yet perpetually shifting appearance through mathematical motion and color transitions that occur without user interaction, creating an immersive background effect that feels alive and dynamic.
