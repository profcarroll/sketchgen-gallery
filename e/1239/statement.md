This sketch creates a mesmerizing scene of metallic shards floating through a dark, smoky void. Using the WEBGL renderer for 3D rendering, it generates hundreds of jagged, chrome-textured shards that slowly drift and rotate in space. The center of the void pulses with a deep emerald light that subtly breathes in rhythm, casting dynamic glints and reflections across the metallic surfaces. Each shard is procedurally generated with irregular, jagged edges to simulate broken glass or crystalline fragments, while their slow, floating motion creates an ethereal sense of weightlessness. The pulsing light source creates a breathing effect that animates the scene without overwhelming it, producing a calm yet dynamic visual field where the shards seem to float in an otherworldly, luminous void.
