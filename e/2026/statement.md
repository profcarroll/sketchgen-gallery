This sketch depicts a cross-sectional view of an arcade cabinet's bioluminescent root network system. The viewer observes glowing cyan, magenta, and electric lime veins pulsing with rhythmic energy throughout an organic branching structure. The dark textured background mimics retro arcade hardware with subtle scanline effects and chromatic aberration for period authenticity. As the energy flows through the network, roots brighten and dim in a systematic cycle, responding to mouse interaction that creates ripples disrupting the natural flow. The visual effect is achieved through particle-based rendering with pulsing color values, cursor interaction detection, and layered compositional elements creating depth and retro atmosphere.
