Brief

A continuous grid of small, interconnected squares rests on a near-black background, each square exhibiting its own slow, subtle breathing rhythm. Interacting with the canvas by clicking initiates an expanding wave that radiates outward from the point of contact. As this wave passes over the stationary squares, each square within its path flashes a burst of highly randomized, vibrant colors before returning to its steady, rhythmic pulse.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a mesmerizing grid of squares that pulse gently with their own breathing rhythm, set against a near-black background. Each square has its own subtle animation cycle, creating a sense of organic life across the canvas. When clicked, an expanding wave radiates outward from the center, causing affected squares to flash vivid, randomized colors before returning to their steady rhythm. The interaction and continuous motion make for a calming yet responsive visual experience.
