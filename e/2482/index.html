Brief

The canvas presents a breathtakingly detailed, macro view of deep-sea mineral crystallization. Countless interwoven particles form complex, luminous crystalline structures that appear to be undergoing constant geological change. The entire composition is a dynamic simulation of the interaction between deep-sea minerals and the slow, persistent force of erosion. The viewer observes the intricate growth patterns as solid deposits appear to shed, recrystalize, and shift in micro-increments.
