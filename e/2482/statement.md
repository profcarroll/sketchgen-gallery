This sketch simulates the mesmerizing process of deep-sea mineral crystallization, capturing the slow, persistent geological forces that shape underwater landscapes. Through a dynamic field of glowing particles, it illustrates how minerals grow, connect, and reorganize over time. The cool blue, silver, and green color palette evokes the mysterious depths where these formations occur, while the subtle movements suggest both growth and erosion. The viewer witnesses an abstract representation of mineral dynamics—particles that form intricate connections, appear to shed, and recrystallize in micro-increments, reflecting the continuous geological change beneath the ocean surface.
