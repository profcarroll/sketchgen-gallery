This sketch simulates the growth of filamentous mold across a textured substrate, capturing both the delicate branching structures and the accumulation of sediment in crevices. Using WebGL rendering, it creates an organic ecosystem where mold filaments spread dynamically and sediment slowly settles into the deepest parts of the surface. The simulation avoids performance bottlenecks by batching particles and precomputing geometry, ensuring smooth animation within strict frame budget constraints.
