Brief

The canvas displays an intricate and deeply textured simulation of filamentous mold growth. Delicate, vibrant filaments spread across and penetrate a detailed substrate surface. The primary features are the fibrous mold structures and the accumulating sediment, which collects visibly within the deepest, narrowest crevices of the substrate, giving the surface a heavy, organic look. The scene is constantly changing as the mold grows and the sediment slowly settles, creating the impression of a quiet, continuously evolving ecosystem.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch simulates the growth of filamentous mold across a textured substrate, capturing both the delicate branching structures and the accumulation of sediment in crevices. Using WebGL rendering, it creates an organic ecosystem where mold filaments spread dynamically and sediment slowly settles into the deepest parts of the surface. The simulation avoids performance bottlenecks by batching particles and precomputing geometry, ensuring smooth animation within strict frame budget constraints.
