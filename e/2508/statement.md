This sketch renders a dynamic, three-dimensional double helix structure composed of four distinct colored base spheres representing Adenine (red), Thymine (green), Cytosine (blue), and Guanine (yellow). The structure continuously spirals and subtly oscillates in space, creating a fluid, living appearance. The bases are labeled with their chemical initials for identification. Transient hydrogen bonds form between complementary base pairs—Adenine-Thymine and Cytosine-Guanine—creating glowing connections that appear, persist briefly, and then dissolve to reform elsewhere along the helix. The visualization uses WebGL rendering for 3D effects and dynamic lighting, with the entire structure slowly rotating to reveal its spiral form from all angles.
