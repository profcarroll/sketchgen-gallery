This sketch renders a three-dimensional model of the solar system with a central star and orbiting planets scaled to their relative proportions. The scene displays realistic orbital paths that glow brightly, connecting all celestial bodies in their movements. Upon clicking the canvas, the animation begins, showing planets moving along their orbits while maintaining the glowing trails behind them. The visualization uses WebGL rendering for 3D effects, dynamic lighting, and smooth camera rotation to create an immersive cosmic experience.
