This sketch renders a serene pastoral landscape using layered gradients and organic shapes to evoke depth and tranquility. The composition features a sky that gradually transitions from white at the horizon to a soft blue, with distant mountains rendered as a hazy silhouette against the sky. Rolling hills in the midground are created with noise-based height variations for natural undulation, while the foreground grass field adds texture through subtle wave patterns. White clouds drift slowly across the sky, enhancing the sense of stillness and timelessness. The scene uses only static drawing methods and no animation beyond the minimal cloud movement, creating a peaceful, immersive vista that captures the feeling of expansive countryside under sunlight.
