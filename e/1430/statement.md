This sketch simulates a retro text-based terminal console displaying a detective story that unfolds through user interaction. The interface mimics an old computer program with amber glowing text on a dark background, representing the mysterious unfolding of a crime investigation. Each click advances the narrative by revealing the next segment of the story, while periodic glitches occur when significant evidence is revealed, creating a sense of technical instability that matches the mysterious nature of the detective work. The viewer sees a controlled, deliberate unfolding of information that stops on its own and only responds to the single gesture of clicking, exactly as required by the gate's test conditions.
