This sketch creates a mesmerizing abstract composition of diagonal stripes that continuously shift, warp, and interact with each other. Each stripe is rendered as a dynamic plane whose geometry evolves over time through sine wave deformations. The stripes move independently at different speeds and angles, occasionally syncing their motion to form temporary lattice-like structures. The bold colors in HSB mode create vibrant visual contrast while the continuous animation produces an hypnotic effect of perpetual reforming mathematical order. The composition exists purely as a self-contained visual spectacle with no external input required.
