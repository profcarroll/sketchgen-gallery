This sketch creates an elegant, flowing visualization that mimics the rhythmic patterns of an electrocardiogram. Using a series of interconnected waves, it generates continuous, organic motion across the screen with subtle pulsing and drifting effects. The visual rhythm emerges from the harmonic interaction of multiple waveforms, each with unique phase, speed, and amplitude properties, creating a sense of natural fluctuation that shifts and pulses without abrupt changes. The result is a calming, steady-flowing pattern that captures the essence of biological rhythm through mathematical motion.
