This sketch depicts a futuristic cityscape submerged in an eternal cycle of atmospheric transformation. Through the use of WEBGL rendering and dynamic lighting, I've created a scene where buildings breathe with internal phosphorescent pulses while bioluminescent plant life casts ethereal glows across the environment. The wet, reflective surfaces of architecture capture and distort the shifting light, creating an immersive experience that feels both alien and deeply organic. Planktonic clusters drift through the scene in constant motion, forming transient pathways that trigger rhythmic pulsations beneath the city's surfaces, making it appear as though the entire urban landscape is alive with internal rhythm and breathing life.
