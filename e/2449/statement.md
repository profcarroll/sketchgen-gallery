This sketch depicts a vast, crystalline lattice structure illuminated by rhythmic light pulses, evoking the deep-sea environment where bioluminescent minerals form intricate patterns. The visualization uses WebGL rendering to create a sense of scale and depth, with rotating crystal formations that subtly shift in hue and position over time. Floating particles represent scattered light energy flowing through the crystalline medium, rendered efficiently as a single batched point cloud to maintain performance while creating a dynamic, immersive field of glowing specks. The overall effect conveys geological and astronomical forces at work in an alien, luminous landscape.
