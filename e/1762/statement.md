This sketch creates a structured geometric lattice using neon vector lines that pulse and flow with rhythmic motion. The grid consists of interconnected vertical and horizontal lines that undulate in wave patterns, creating a sense of continuous energy. Periodically, discrete fractal breakdowns emerge from the top of the canvas and drift downward, blossoming into glowing yellow fractal patterns before fading away. These breakdowns interrupt the structured field with intricate, organic growth patterns that move vertically through the field, creating moments of visual disruption against the otherwise ordered geometric flow. The entire composition maintains a steady rhythm while allowing for these spontaneous bursts of complexity.
