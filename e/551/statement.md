This sketch creates a mesmerizing pattern of concentric arcs that pulse rhythmically from the center of the canvas. Each arc expands and contracts in a smooth, wave-like motion while continuously shifting through the entire color spectrum. The animation is entirely self-contained and requires no user interaction to maintain its visual rhythm. The technique uses trigonometric functions to calculate arc positions and colors, creating an organic, flowing effect that fills the entire canvas with dynamic, ever-changing geometry. Viewers witness a hypnotic display of color and motion that evolves continuously over time.
