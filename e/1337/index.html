Brief

Floating octagonal frames drift through a vast, hazy liminal space. Each frame holds a curious photograph that subtly rotates and shifts in scale. The background is a soft, atmospheric gradient of muted colors, creating the feeling of an empty, dreamlike void. The octagons move independently across the canvas, bobbing and weaving in a continuous, ghostly motion.

Artist's statement · gemma4:26b, unedited

I created a dreamlike, liminal scene featuring drifting octagonal frames that float through a hazy, atmospheric void. Each frame contains a photograph that shifts and scales independently, creating a sense of memory or ghostliness. I used a pre-rendered background gradient to maintain high performance and implemented a clipping mask within each frame to contain the drifting imagery.
