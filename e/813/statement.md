This sketch creates a retro camera viewfinder simulation with dynamic scanlines that move continuously across the screen. The visual effect mimics old mobile phone displays by rendering horizontal lines that shift position with subtle jitter and occasional abrupt pauses or skips. These interruptions create an unstable transmission illusion, while the dark bordered frame reinforces the nostalgic aesthetic of vintage electronics. The animation runs autonomously without user input, satisfying the motion requirement through consistent canvas changes over time.
