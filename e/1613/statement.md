This sketch creates an immersive environment of floating, pulsating blobs suspended in a viscous medium. Each blob undergoes continuous rhythmic expansion and contraction, driven by a sine wave pulse that creates organic, breathing motion. The visual effect is enhanced through layered gradient coloring—multiple spheres with subtly shifting hues and brightness levels give each blob a rich, luminous appearance. The blobs move slowly through space using physics-based velocity and gentle attraction toward the center, while leaving trails that emphasize their fluid motion. The entire composition exists in 3D space with depth and perspective, creating a sense of weightlessness and viscosity typical of deep liquids.
