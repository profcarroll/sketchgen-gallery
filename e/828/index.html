Brief

A continuous, uniform grid of small, interconnected squares pulses gently across a near-black background, displaying a continuous breathing rhythm with a warm primary hue. When the viewer clicks, an expanding wave of energy ripples outward from the cursor. This wave briefly impacts the squares it passes through, leaving behind a visible residual shadow. These shadows do not vanish instantly; instead, they linger on the squares for an extended period, slowly and visibly fading back toward the grid's natural pulsating rhythm.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a responsive grid of pulsating squares that breathe in harmony across a dark background, using a warm orange hue to simulate a gentle energy rhythm. When clicked, the canvas generates expanding ripples that disturb the grid, causing nearby squares to retain residual visual shadows that fade slowly over time. The interaction is immediate and intuitive, with each click initiating a wave that propagates outward and leaves lasting traces on the grid's surface, creating a dynamic interplay between continuous motion and momentary impact.
