Brief

The canvas features a vibrant, chaotic field of retro-styled pixel shapes that constantly shift and glow. When the viewer interacts by dragging the cursor across the canvas, the established shapes react by scattering outward along the cursor’s path. These scattering pixels momentarily form intricate, glowing, geometric lattices, displaying structured patterns before gracefully fading back into the background's random, vibrant chaos.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a dynamic, glowing particle field with retro aesthetics that responds to user interaction. The particles move autonomously in a chaotic pattern, forming temporary geometric connections as they approach each other. When the user drags their cursor across the canvas, it triggers a scattering effect—particles burst outward along the cursor's path and form intricate lattices before fading back into the background. The visual experience combines organic motion with structured patterns, creating an immersive, interactive environment where the viewer can influence the chaotic system through direct manipulation.
