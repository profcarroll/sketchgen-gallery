This sketch creates a deep-sea bathymetric scene rendered in 3D WebGL, featuring sharp topographical ridges rising from an obsidian floor. Thick translucent layers of amber beeswax partially obscure the terrain, while fine luminous sediments drift and settle into the carved crevices. The composition simulates geological time through slow, continuous movement of particles and undulating wax layers, evoking a sense of ancient, deep-sea processes unfolding beneath the ocean floor.
