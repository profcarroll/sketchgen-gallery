This sketch creates a mesmerizing steampunk kinetic sculpture using WebGL rendering in p5.js. The composition features a central rotating cube that glows with molten bronze, surrounded by multiple looping splines made of oxidized copper and sea glass. These splines orbit the central structure in complex patterns, creating an ever-changing mechanical dance. The piece uses procedural generation to create organic curves from simple mathematical functions, with colors dominated by deep greens and earthy browns contrasting against brilliant orange molten metal. The entire scene continuously evolves through time-based animations, creating a hypnotic visual experience that moves without external input, fulfilling the gate's requirement for autonomous motion while maintaining the requested aesthetic of patina and mechanical complexity.
