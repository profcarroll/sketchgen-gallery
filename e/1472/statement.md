This sketch renders an ethereal jellyfish using layered undulating waveforms that pulse and flow autonomously across the canvas. The organism's body is composed of multiple translucent, bioluminescent layers in deep blues and bright cyans, each expanding and contracting in sync with its own rhythmic motion. Floating particles drift through the space, enhancing the luminous, dreamlike quality of the form. The fluid, organic movement emerges from mathematical wave functions that continuously evolve over time, creating an immersive visual experience without any external interaction. The jellyfish appears to breathe and drift gently, maintaining its mesmerizing form through self-generated animation.
