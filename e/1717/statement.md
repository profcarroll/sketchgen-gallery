This sketch depicts a viscous fluid medium containing several amorphous, brightly colored blobs that gently float and shift within the enclosed space. Each blob has a complex internal structure revealed through sharp geometric fractures that cut through its surface, creating an intricate interplay of light and shadow. The simulation runs continuously without user interaction, with each blob slowly pulsating and drifting while maintaining its containment within the fluid environment. The rich color palette and dynamic movement create a mesmerizing effect where the viewer observes the subtle transformations of these organic forms as they float through space.
