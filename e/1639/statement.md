This sketch creates an ambient, evolving field of overlapping, floating rectangles that pulse and breathe with deep, saturated colors. The composition suggests a slow, continuous shift in color depth through subtle motion and luminosity changes. When a user interacts by clicking the canvas, the existing transitions deepen dramatically—causing overlapping masses to swell and sharpen their contrast. The visual effect emerges from layers of soft-edged rectangles drawn with alpha blending, glow effects, and layered transparency that create rich, organic transitions between hues.
