This sketch depicts a futuristic urban landscape that transitions through a complete day-night cycle. The scene features angular, reflective buildings that respond to the changing light of the sky, with each structure catching and reflecting the shifting colors of dawn, noon, and dusk. Between these architectural forms grows an organic ecosystem of bioluminescent plants that pulse gently in synchronization with the ambient lighting. The entire composition operates autonomously through time-based animation, creating a living urban environment where technology and nature coexist in harmony, with the light cycle driving both the architectural reflections and the plant glow effects.
