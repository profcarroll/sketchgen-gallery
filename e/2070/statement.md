This sketch depicts the transformation of a delicate sand mandala into a towering central pillar through the forces of erosion and accumulation. Initially symmetrical and intricate, the structure gradually destabilizes under increasing wind force, causing particles to detach and fall inward toward the center. Rather than dispersing outward, these grains accumulate in the center, forming a solidifying spire that grows slowly upward from the former mandala's precise center. The earthy color palette of browns, ochres, and tans creates a naturalistic feel as particles coalesce into a monument of solidified dust, representing the powerful force of falling sand gathering into a singular form.
