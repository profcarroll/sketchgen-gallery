Brief

The canvas displays a complex, evolving biological mass simulating slime mold growth on a substrate. The structure is rendered in intricate three-dimensional detail, with dynamic lighting emphasizing the material's surface qualities. The expanding masses are characterized by sharp, angular folds, which exhibit visible, crystalline fracturing. These folds and ridges give the structure a rigid, mineralized appearance as the masses continue to shift and expand over time. The entire composition is a self-contained, continuous organic simulation, requiring no direct input to observe its transformation.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch simulates the growth and transformation of a biological mass resembling slime mold, rendered in three-dimensional space with dynamic lighting. The structures evolve over time, expanding and shifting through organic movement while maintaining sharp, angular crystalline fractures that define their rigid, mineralized appearance. The simulation uses a combination of fluid motion and sudden structural changes to create an ever-shifting landscape of interconnected ridges and folds, capturing both the fluidity and rigidity inherent in biological growth patterns.
