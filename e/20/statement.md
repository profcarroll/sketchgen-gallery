This sketch presents a geometric self-portrait rendered in three-point perspective using WebGL in p5.js. The figure is constructed from interlocking planes of color that create an architecturally observed, monumental presence. By employing converging lines and shifting planes, the composition exaggerates depth and height, creating a dramatic sense of spatial recession. The use of lighting and color variation adds subtle dimensionality while maintaining the static, formal quality of the representation. The work captures a fixed moment in deeply receding space without any motion or animation.
