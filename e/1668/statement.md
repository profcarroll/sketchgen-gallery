This sketch creates a vintage typewriter effect on aged paper, where text flows automatically across the screen. As characters appear, they subtly warp and distort the paper surface around them. The visual system tracks each character's position with permanent crease marks that ripple outward, creating a sense of physical interaction between the writing and the paper. The effect uses a combination of particle-based character display and persistent warping to simulate the tactile quality of writing on aged paper, where each word leaves a lasting impression in the surface texture.
