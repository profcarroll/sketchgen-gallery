Brief

The canvas displays a continuous grid of small, interconnected squares, settled against a near-black background. Each square subtly breathes, giving the appearance of slow, gentle respiration. When the user clicks anywhere on the grid, an outward-propagating wave pulses from the impact point. As this wave travels, it temporarily halts the gentle breathing movement of every square it touches, creating a visible pause in the rhythmic animation. The effect dissipates quickly, leaving the squares to resume their subtle, continuous respiration.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a mesmerizing grid of softly breathing squares against a dark background. Each square gently pulses with a rhythmic breathing motion, creating a calming, organic feel. When the user clicks anywhere on the canvas, a wave of influence propagates outward from the click point. Squares touched by this wave temporarily halt their breathing animation, creating a visible pause before they resume their gentle movement. The effect is subtle yet noticeable, emphasizing the interconnectedness of the grid elements and providing an interactive experience that responds to user input with a ripple-like visual response.
