Brief

The canvas displays a dense, active field of tiny, pointer-like shapes, simulating compass needles. These pointers are constantly in gentle motion, drifting and oscillating even when the cursor remains still. When the user moves the cursor across the canvas, the entire field reacts immediately and dramatically, causing the needles to aggressively shift their orientation toward the cursor's location. As the cursor travels, the pointers dynamically track the movement, and when the cursor finally leaves the area, the needles gradually and smoothly settle and realign themselves toward a shared, stable north position.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch simulates a magnetic compass field with thousands of tiny pointer elements that respond to user interaction. The needles continuously drift and oscillate in gentle motion, creating an organic, living field. When the cursor moves across the canvas, all needles immediately react by aligning toward the cursor's position, creating a dramatic visual response. As the cursor moves, the entire field dynamically tracks this movement, with each needle smoothly transitioning to its new orientation. When the cursor stops moving, the needles gradually settle back into a shared northward alignment, creating a mesmerizing interplay between individual motion and collective behavior. The effect is both responsive and calming, demonstrating how simple physics-based forces can create complex emergent patterns.
