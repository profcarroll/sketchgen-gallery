This sketch presents a static, atmospheric still life rendered in three-dimensional space using WebGL. The composition centers on a silver candelabra with a single lit candle, a clear jug of water, and a colorful fruit platter arranged on a brown table within a dark room. The lighting emphasizes the sculptural textures and clean lines of each object, creating deep contrast between the warm glow of the flame and the surrounding shadows. The scene is deliberately motionless, frozen in time to evoke quiet observation and contemplation.
