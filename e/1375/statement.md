This sketch simulates the growth and decay of a slime mold on a textured substrate using organic particle dynamics. The structure evolves over time with expanding masses that shift and fold, mimicking life processes through color transitions, age-based transformations, and continuous morphological changes. The visual effect emphasizes depth and surface sheen through simulated lighting and volumetric rendering techniques within the constraints of 2D canvas drawing. The system maintains a constant particle count while allowing for dynamic growth patterns that create an ever-changing biological landscape.
