This sketch presents a highly abstracted self-portrait rendered through interlocking geometric facets viewed in three-point perspective. Using the WEBGL renderer, it constructs a structured, manufactured form composed of multiple planar surfaces that appear to catch and reflect light at sharp edges. The color palette emphasizes cool grays with specular highlights that carve out the form's geometry, creating deep shadow lines that enhance the sense of depth and constructed nature. Each facet is drawn as a box with consistent lighting and material properties, creating a uniform yet complex visual texture that conveys both order and abstraction.
