This sketch simulates the steady growth of a knitted fabric by continuously adding new rows of patterned stitches from the bottom up. The visual effect captures the methodical, repetitive motion of knitting needles working through yarn, creating a vertical strip that expands over time. Each stitch follows a precise geometric pattern using a limited color palette, maintaining consistent spacing and structure as new rows are added. The animation runs autonomously without any external input, presenting a calming representation of continuous creation.
