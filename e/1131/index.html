Brief

The canvas displays a horizontal arrangement of five distinct colored rectangular pads. These pads are uniform in shape and are each accented by a subtle, luminous border that glows softly. The pads remain still and await interaction. When the viewer clicks any single pad, that pad visually lights up, and simultaneously, a corresponding musical note sounds. The luminous border around the clicked pad pulses briefly, confirming the interaction and enhancing the visual feedback.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a static arrangement of five colored rectangular pads arranged horizontally across the canvas. Each pad has a subtle glowing border that creates a luminous effect. When a viewer clicks on any single pad, it lights up with a brighter color and its surrounding border pulses with a glowing animation to confirm the interaction. Simultaneously, a corresponding musical note plays through the computer's speakers — each pad produces a different pitch in a musical scale. The visual feedback is immediate and intuitive, creating an interactive musical instrument where clicking pads triggers both sound and light effects. The entire interface remains motionless until activated by a single click, fulfilling the requirement for stillness until interaction begins.
