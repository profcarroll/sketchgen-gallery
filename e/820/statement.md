This sketch creates an immersive, ever-shifting field of glowing, amorphous planes that drift silently through a three-dimensional space. Each plane moves independently with its own velocity and color, creating a mesmerizing sense of depth and continuous motion. The planes subtly shift in hue over time, blending into each other as they float past one another. The visual effect is achieved using a combination of 2D ellipses rendered in a 3D-like space, with careful attention to transparency and movement dynamics. The result is a calming, breathable visualization that feels alive without being chaotic, filling the entire canvas with an organic flow of color and motion.
