Brief

The viewer observes a geometric study featuring monolithic, pyramidal architectural structures where solid land meets a reflective sea. The color palette emphasizes muted earth tones contrasted against deep aquatic blues. The primary dynamic element is the water surface, which is perpetually rippling. The water's reaction is defined by proximity: the intensity of the ripples increases directly and dramatically as the surface approaches the base of the pyramidal formations, creating a visible energy gradient. The scene is a self-contained, ceaseless study of form and fluid mechanics.
