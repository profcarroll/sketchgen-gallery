This sketch presents a serene yet dynamic architectural scene where pyramidal structures emerge from a reflective sea surface. The geometric forms are rendered with muted earth tones, contrasting against deep blue waters that perpetually ripple. The water's motion intensifies near the base of each pyramid, creating an energy gradient that visually connects the solid landforms to the fluid aquatic environment. The entire composition operates autonomously, with no external input required — the scene evolves through time-based animation and physics-driven interactions.
