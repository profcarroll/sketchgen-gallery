This sketch creates an abstract visualization of market volatility as a dynamic network of glowing, fluctuating lines that weave across the screen like a medieval tapestry. The system features hundreds of animated line segments that continuously flow and pulse, with their paths subtly distorted by the presence of distinct red and green nodes scattered throughout the composition. These nodes act as focal points where the flowing lines undergo rhythmic sinusoidal oscillations, creating an intricate pattern of movement and interaction that evokes the unpredictable yet interconnected nature of financial markets. The visual effect emerges from the continuous interplay between the flowing elements and the fixed node positions, producing a mesmerizing, ever-changing tapestry of motion.
