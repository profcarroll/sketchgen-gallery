This sketch creates a dynamic rain-covered windowpane scene with interactive puddles. Raindrops streak down the glass at varying speeds and sways, collecting into puddles that form at the bottom of the screen. Each puddle gradually transforms from a regular cityscape view to a glowing crystalline structure over time. When new raindrops impact existing puddles, they trigger subtle internal light flashes that pulse through the crystal forms. The entire scene is self-contained and continuously evolving without any user input, with all visual elements generated algorithmically using p5.js's drawing functions within strict performance constraints for smooth animation.
