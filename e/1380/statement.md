This sketch creates a dynamic optical experience through a series of vibrant, colorfully striped geometric shapes that continuously shift and morph across the canvas. Using a combination of rotation, oscillation, and size modulation, the stripes flow in smooth, rhythmic patterns that create an ever-changing visual composition. The 1970s-inspired saturated palette delivers high contrast while the mathematical motion generates complex, non-repeating arrangements that persistently reform without any user interaction. Each stripe operates independently with its own speed, angle, and phase, creating a layered, organic flow that fills the entire canvas with animated color.
