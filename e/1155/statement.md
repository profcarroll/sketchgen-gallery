This sketch presents an interactive jigsaw puzzle experience viewed from above. The canvas displays numerous interlocking puzzle pieces of varying shapes and colors floating within the frame. Users can click and drag loose pieces to move them around the canvas. When pieces are positioned close enough to their correct neighbors, they automatically snap together and form connections. The seams between correctly connected pieces glow with a subtle light effect that intensifies when the entire puzzle is completed. This creates an engaging visual feedback loop where users can see their progress as they assemble the image, with the glow serving as both a functional indicator of successful connections and a celebratory highlight when the final piece is placed.
