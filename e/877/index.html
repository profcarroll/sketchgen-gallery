Brief

The canvas displays a vast, organic lattice composed of interconnected, glowing cells. The pattern constantly shifts and evolves in a natural, flowing manner. When cells die or change, they do not instantly vanish, but instead leave behind visible trails of residual light. These ghostly trails follow the original paths, lingering and slowly fading over several distinct cycles, giving the entire field a luminous, ephemeral quality. The movement is entirely autonomous, continually generating and dissipating light patterns without requiring any user interaction.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an evolving organic lattice of glowing cells that move autonomously across the screen. Each cell leaves a luminous trail behind it, creating a sense of motion and continuity as patterns shift and flow. The system uses a fixed number of particles with persistent trails, allowing viewers to perceive the collective movement and interaction within the field. The visual effect emphasizes the ephemeral quality of light and motion through fading trails and continuous, self-generated patterns that require no user input to maintain their dynamic behavior.
