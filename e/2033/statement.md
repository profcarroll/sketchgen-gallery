This sketch creates a deep-sea hydrothermal vent environment using layered corrugated panels that breathe rhythmically in three-dimensional space. The panels, rendered as cardboard-like geometric forms with subtle texture, serve as a physical substrate for intense, saturated energy plumes that continuously pulse and flow. Neon-colored energy fields emerge from within the panels, simulating chemical reactions meeting digital interference. Viewers can interact by dragging their cursor across the screen, causing the glowing plumes to glitch, flare, and intensify along the path of input, creating a dynamic visual representation of how physical materials respond to energetic forces. The scene uses WEBGL rendering to create depth and dimensionality while maintaining performance within the frame budget constraints.
