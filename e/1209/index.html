Brief

In a 3D scene, a sawmill mechanism processes cylindrical logs by slicing them into rectangular planks. The planks fall from the machinery and collect at the bottom of the frame, forming a massive, chaotic, and irregular pile that continuously grows. The movement is constant as logs are fed through the saw and more planks are added to the growing heap.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch depicts a continuously operating sawmill mechanism in a 3D space. Cylindrical logs are fed into a rotating sawblade that cuts them into rectangular planks. The resulting planks fall from the machinery and accumulate at the bottom of the scene, creating a growing, chaotic pile. The visualization uses WebGL rendering for three-dimensional effects, with physics simulation including gravity and rotation to animate the falling planks. The scene features a constant cycle of log input, cutting, and plank accumulation that creates an organic, dynamic heap forming over time.
