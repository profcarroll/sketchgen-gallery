This sketch creates a dynamic, audio-responsive visualization of geometric forms that cascade through space using octahedral and hexagonal symmetries. The shapes move along rectilinear axes while simultaneously tracing synchronized helical spirals, creating complex intersecting patterns that shift and pulse in rhythm with sound input. Neon-hued geometric forms appear to flow and accelerate, responding to audio energy by adjusting their speed and size. The visualization fills the entire canvas with an immersive field of motion that combines mathematical precision with organic fluidity, using only basic polygonal geometry to create a sense of volumetric complexity and kinetic energy.
