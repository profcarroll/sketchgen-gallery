I have created a visual meditation on isolation and the vastness of the unknown. By placing photographic fragments within floating octagonal frames, the sketch mimics the feeling of drifting through a dark, liminal void. The frames move with a slow, heavy momentum, using subtle sine-wave oscillations and extreme scale shifts to suggest a weightless, drifting existence. The wide dispersion of these objects and the deep, dark gradient background are intended to evoke a sense of lonely, infinite space.
