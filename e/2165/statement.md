This sketch creates an abstract visualization of intersecting linear segments that evoke the aesthetic of retro 1980s digital schematics. Using a system of self-organizing line segments, each with its own movement pattern governed by Perlin noise, the composition generates a constantly evolving geometric lattice. The lines flicker with internal light through careful control of color and transparency, creating an ethereal glow that suggests both technical precision and organic flow. The result is a perpetual motion system where patterns continuously form and reorganize themselves, filling the canvas with a mesmerizing display of vector art that feels both futuristic and nostalgic.
