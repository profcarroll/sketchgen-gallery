This sketch creates an infinite series of expanding geometric rings that originate from the center of the canvas and propagate outward. Using a monochromatic grayscale palette, the rings appear as clean white strokes against a black background, creating a continuous visual sweep across the screen. Each ring expands at a constant rate while simultaneously fading out, producing a hypnotic, flowing effect where new rings continuously emerge to fill the void left by fading ones. The pattern maintains a steady rhythm without any user interaction, filling the entire canvas with an unbroken wave of geometric motion that appears to pulse and flow from the center outward.
