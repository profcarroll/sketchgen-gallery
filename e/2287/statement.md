This sketch creates an immersive three-dimensional visualization of intersecting geometric planes that pulse with rhythmic wave patterns. The structures float in space, each rotating and distorting in synchronization with their own internal wave phase, creating a sense of continuous flow and interconnectedness. As the viewer observes, the planes appear to breathe and shift, with luminous lines connecting nearby structures, suggesting an underlying network of energy or data flow. The color palette shifts dynamically through the hue spectrum, with each structure maintaining its own identity while contributing to a larger harmonic composition. The entire scene slowly rotates, allowing viewers to perceive the complex interplay of form and movement from multiple perspectives.
