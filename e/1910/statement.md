This sketch creates an immersive deep-sea sonar visualization that evolves from simple sweeping lines into complex three-dimensional crystal lattices. The animation begins with rhythmic radar sweeps in violet and deep blue hues, which expand and pulse to form spiraling helix patterns. As these patterns develop, they transition through stages of matter — from flowing lines to solid geometric formations, then to discrete points that assemble into intricate lattice structures. The visualization uses batched drawing techniques to maintain performance while creating a mesmerizing effect where the viewer witnesses the emergence of increasingly complex, interlocking geometries across the canvas surface.
