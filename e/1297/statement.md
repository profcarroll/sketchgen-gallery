A lighthouse on a dark headland sends a soft wedge of light sweeping back and forth across a navy night, while five layers of mist slide sideways at different speeds, thin and slow high up, thick and quicker near the ground. The beam is a stack of translucent wedges that narrow and brighten toward the middle. Each patch of fog remembers how lit it is, so mist turns warm cream as the light passes through and cools slowly back to grey blue behind it. The whole scene is a few hundred flat shapes and needs no input.
