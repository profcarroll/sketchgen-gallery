This sketch creates an abstract textile tapestry through the interplay of fluid organic fibers and rigid geometric knots. The fibers flow across the canvas with gentle, natural movements, their positions subtly shifting to create a sense of continuous motion and soft curves. Embedded within this fluid medium are structured angular knots that provide defined contrast and visual anchors. The composition uses spatial hashing to efficiently manage interactions between fibers, drawing subtle connections that enhance the textural complexity. The jewel-toned color palette creates rich visual depth while maintaining the contrast between chaotic softness and controlled geometry.
