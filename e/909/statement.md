This sketch creates an abstract, evolving composition of overlapping planes that pulse and flow across the canvas like large-scale paint strokes. Each plane moves independently with its own rhythm, creating a layered visual field where deep, saturated colors shift and undulate in response to continuous wave motion. The animation mimics the gestural quality of abstract painting through fluid, rhythmic movement and dynamic color transitions, producing an immersive and constantly changing visual experience that responds to no external input beyond time itself.
