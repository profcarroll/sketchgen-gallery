This sketch depicts a luminous, evolving lattice field that captures the essence of an ancient tapestry's electrical discharge patterns. The canvas displays thousands of glowing cells that drift and reform in a perpetual dance, each leaving trails of residual light as they fade and regenerate. Connections between cells pulse with intense energy when they form, creating a web of light that shifts and reconfigures constantly. The background cycles through slow, wave-like color transitions, intensifying the glow of the connections while maintaining an atmospheric depth. The visual effect evokes both the mysterious patterns of lightning in a humid jungle canopy and the intricate weaving of medieval tapestries, where history is mapped through luminous threads that linger long after their origin.
