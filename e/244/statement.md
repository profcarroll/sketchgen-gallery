This sketch creates a mesmerizing architectural tapestry through intersecting geometric grids that pulse and flicker with neon light. Each grid layer is rendered in a different hue from the spectrum, creating a layered visual effect where the grids subtly shift and morph over time. The animation uses sine wave functions to create smooth pulsing effects that make it appear as though the blueprint is continuously drawing itself. The grids are connected by lines that maintain their structure while adding depth to the composition. The overall effect is a self-contained, hypnotic visualization that demonstrates how simple geometric forms can be transformed into complex, evolving architectural patterns through careful timing and color manipulation.
