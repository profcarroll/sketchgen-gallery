This sketch creates an immersive underwater landscape featuring crystalline pyramids that rise from a dark ocean floor. The pyramids are geometrically precise structures with dynamic lighting that pulses in rhythmic waves. Each pyramid has its own color palette that shifts continuously through the HSB color space, creating a mesmerizing interplay of deep blues and vibrant hues that reflect the varying depths of an underwater environment. The scene is constantly animated with subtle camera movement, pulsing light effects, and synchronized color cycling that gives the impression of living, breathing architecture beneath the waves. The pyramids appear to glow from within, their surfaces reflecting the ebb and flow of luminescent energy as they ascend through the dark oceanic space.
