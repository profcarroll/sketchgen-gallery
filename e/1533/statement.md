This sketch creates a continuous, organic visualization of water using stacked horizontal bands that oscillate vertically to simulate tidal movement. The system combines a slow, rhythmic sine wave motion with high-frequency surface ripples that create a constantly shifting texture across the water's surface. A persistent dark band marks the peak of each tidal cycle, serving as a visual anchor for the viewer's perception of the water's natural rhythm. The visualization operates entirely autonomously, creating an immersive representation of ocean dynamics through carefully modulated color gradients and movement patterns that maintain visual interest without any user interaction.
