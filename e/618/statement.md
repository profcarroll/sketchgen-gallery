This sketch creates a serene, sunlit pastoral scene using geometric shapes and layered color to depict rolling fields leading into distant mountains under a bright sky. The composition uses a fixed set of randomly generated patches for the fields, triangular mountain silhouettes, fluffy white clouds, and a radiant sun. All elements are drawn once with no animation or motion, fulfilling the static nature requirement while creating a richly textured landscape that feels both expansive and grounded in natural detail.
