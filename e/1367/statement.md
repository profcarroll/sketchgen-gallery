This sketch creates a meditative, luminous composition of floating, layered color fields that seem to emanate their own inner light. Through careful manipulation of color saturation and transparency, the layers appear both solid and ethereal, with subtle breathing motions that suggest life within the static canvas. The deep, resonant palette draws the viewer into an immersive visual experience, where each layer bleeds softly into the next, creating a sense of depth and volumetric presence. The slow, organic movement of these color masses evokes contemplation and emotional resonance, as if observing a world of light and color that exists in its own dimension.
