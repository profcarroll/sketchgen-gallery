This sketch presents a dynamic cross-sectional view of Earth's interior,Animating the descent through geological layers from crust to core. The visualization uses layered spherical sections with color gradients representing different rock types and temperatures, creating a vivid portrayal of Earth's internal structure. As the camera smoothly moves downward, viewers witness the transition from cool blue crust through warm red mantle to the glowing hot core. The rotation effect adds depth and dimensionality to the geological cross-section, while subtle particle effects simulate the intense heat and pressure found at Earth's center. The rendering employs WebGL for efficient 3D graphics and maintains a consistent frame rate despite the complex visual elements, ensuring the viewer experiences a continuous, immersive journey through our planet's interior.
