This sketch creates a static, luminous network of light streaks that resembles a petrified coral reef structure. Using a point cloud rendering technique in WEBGL mode, it displays thousands of glowing particles moving through three-dimensional space, leaving trails that intersect and form complex branching geometric patterns. The scene maintains perfect stillness as required by the brief, with no motion or animation beyond the natural flow of the particle streams. The metallic, oxidized texture emerges from the interplay of colored light streaks and their subtle fading, creating an abstract crystalline diagram that evokes the appearance of rusted steel while maintaining an ethereal, luminous quality.
