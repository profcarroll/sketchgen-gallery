This sketch presents an immersive, self-contained visual experience where neon-lit geometric forms pulse and float through space in complex motion. The shapes—octahedra and hexagons—move with helical trajectories and continuous rotation, creating a sense of fluid energy. When two forms collide, they violently fragment into dozens of sharp-edged shards that scatter outward before fading. The color palette remains intensely saturated throughout, shifting dynamically through hues as collisions occur. The entire system operates without external input, driven purely by the physics of movement and interaction, resulting in an ever-shifting constellation of light and motion.
