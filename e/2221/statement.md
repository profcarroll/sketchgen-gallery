This sketch creates an immersive, organic coral-like structure using fractal principles and stained glass aesthetics. The visualization features hundreds of glowing, translucent polygons that pulse and shift in fluid patterns reminiscent of deep-sea coral formations. Each structure grows and evolves through time with a gentle, rhythmic animation, while the viewer can navigate deeper into the fractal landscape by dragging their mouse to pan and zoom. The jewel-toned colors shift and flow, creating an ethereal, bioluminescent effect that captures both the mathematical precision of fractals and the organic beauty of underwater ecosystems.
