This sketch creates a continuous, erratic EKG pattern that scrolls from left to right across a black canvas. The neon yellow line simulates a chaotic heartbeat through unpredictable spikes and dips, with occasional large irregularities to mimic real EKG variability. The animation runs smoothly without user interaction until the canvas is clicked, at which point it begins its motion. The visual effect is achieved by drawing a series of points that shift across the screen over time, creating a trail-like motion that maintains visibility as new data replaces old.
