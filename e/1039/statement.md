This sketch renders a static, abstract textile pattern using flowing geometric lines and vibrant saturated colors. The composition resembles a woven tapestry where organic curves intersect with rigid angular forms to create a complex knot-like structure. The visual effect is achieved through layered shapes and careful color placement, without any animation or motion. The work presents an intricate, unchanging diagram of light and color that evokes the complexity of hand-woven art while maintaining a structured, geometric aesthetic.
