This sketch depicts a surreal aquatic scene where whimsical paper ships drift through a translucent, aqueous medium. As they sail, each vessel leaves behind dissolving wake trails composed of crystalline salt deposits that gradually solidify into brittle mineral scaffolds. The most significant evolution from the original is the introduction of hull encasement—when ships decay sufficiently, their hulls become permanently encased in mineral formations, transforming the transient scene into a permanent, mineralized tableau. The interplay between the fragile paper vessels and the accumulating sediment creates an evolving landscape where the ephemeral becomes eternal.
