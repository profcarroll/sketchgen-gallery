This sketch presents a steampunk-inspired kinetic sculpture composed of polished brass splines orbiting a central rotating core. The visual effect captures the essence of intricate clockwork engineering with metallic reflections and continuous motion. The central core appears as a solid, luminous electrum sphere that subtly drips with animated droplets, suggesting a molten quality while maintaining its polished appearance. Orbiting particles move in synchronized spirals, connected by smooth spline lines that form an elaborate, three-dimensional network. The entire composition rotates slowly, creating dynamic lighting effects and ambient reflections that enhance the rich, metallic glow of the brass elements. The piece conveys a sense of mechanical harmony and timeless craftsmanship through its constant, mesmerizing motion.
