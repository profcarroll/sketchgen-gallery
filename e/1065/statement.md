This sketch creates a minimalist analog clock face with smooth, continuous motion. The design features a clean, unnumbered circular face and two distinct hands — an hour hand and a minute hand — rendered in a warm metallic copper tone. The hands are thick and polished, sweeping autonomously around the clock face without any user interaction. The implementation calculates hand positions based on real-time updates, ensuring accurate timekeeping through continuous animation. The visual style emphasizes simplicity and elegance, with no decorative elements beyond the essential components of a functional timepiece.
