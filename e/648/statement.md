This sketch creates an immersive, evolving environment of impossible crystal architecture floating in deep teal space. Using WebGL rendering, it generates dynamic geometric shards that rotate and subtly shift, appearing to experience internal structural stress. The architecture continuously fragments—shards shatter into ethereal particle clouds while new structures form elsewhere. The visual effect emphasizes tension and dissolution through color transitions, controlled particle systems, and organic camera movement. Viewers witness a perpetual process of construction and destruction, where rigid geometric forms gradually fragment and disperse into a surrounding mist of particles, suggesting forces of gravity and shearing pulling the impossible geometry apart.
