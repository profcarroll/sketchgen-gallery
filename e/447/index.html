Brief

The canvas displays a dynamic molecular model of a double helix. The structure is formed by linked spheres, labeled A, T, C, and G, which represent the complementary bases of DNA. The visualization captures the process of replication, showing a central segment of the helix gradually unzipping. As the separation occurs, complementary strands are synthesized and extend, accurately showing the two newly formed, growing molecules. The scene is a continuous, slow animation of the genetic material replicating itself.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch visualizes the replication of a DNA double helix using p5.js in WEBGL mode. It shows two complementary strands forming a double helix structure that gradually separates, with new base pairs being synthesized along the process. The animation is self-contained and runs continuously without user input, demonstrating the molecular mechanics of DNA replication through geometric forms and color-coded bases (A, T, C, G). The visualization uses dynamic movement to represent the unwinding and reformation of the helix, capturing both structural integrity and the biological process of replication.
