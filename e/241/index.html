Brief

The canvas depicts a sprawling, photorealistic urban landscape, rich with color and architectural detail. The viewer is placed within a major city street, observing large amounts of activity from multiple angle points. The primary visual elements are towering, detailed buildings and numerous modeled vehicles that populate the streets. There is constant motion, seen through cars driving past and ambient movement in the background suggesting life in the city. The individual vehicles are distinguished by visible, modeled front lights. A mouse drag input allows the viewer to pan, change the camera viewpoint, and navigate through the extensive urban map.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a bustling urban environment using p5.js's WEBGL renderer to simulate a city street from a first-person perspective. It features towering buildings with detailed facades, moving vehicles with distinct front lights, and ambient city activity. The viewer can navigate the scene by dragging the mouse to pan and rotate the camera viewpoint, allowing exploration of the expansive urban landscape.
