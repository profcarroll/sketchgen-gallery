This sketch depicts a dynamic campfire scene where glowing sparks rise from a warm orange flame and gradually transform into cool blue-white stellar particles. As the sparks ascend and dissipate, they drift apart and form recognizable constellations in the night sky above. The visual effect creates a cosmic mapping over the heat of the fire, with particles transitioning through color and behavior as they age. The scene is rendered using batched drawing techniques to maintain performance within the frame budget, with particles forming trails and connecting lines that bloom into constellation patterns across the dark canvas.
