This sketch presents a cinematic journey through a transition from a harsh, concrete landscape to an ethereal underground crystal cavern. The viewer experiences a steady forward motion through a desaturated environment populated by massive brutalist concrete structures that gradually give way to sharp, luminous quartz formations. The visual shift occurs as the camera moves deeper into the subterranean space, revealing increasingly crystalline mineral structures that dominate the scene at city-block scale. The contrast between the weathered concrete and pristine quartz creates a dramatic architectural narrative, emphasizing the passage from decay to natural formation through a continuous, immersive first-person perspective.
