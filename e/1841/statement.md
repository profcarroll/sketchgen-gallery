This sketch creates a serene, ever-flowing cosmic scene where translucent, overlapping circles drift slowly across the canvas like an endless tide. The circles pulse gently in hue and opacity, their movements creating a subtle rhythmic flow that evokes the vast, timeless motion of space. Scattered throughout this ethereal backdrop are countless pointillist stars that shimmer with prismatic hues and leave delicate trails of light behind them. The entire composition moves without user input, generating a continuous, immersive visual experience that captures the quiet dynamism of the cosmos — a gentle, hypnotic dance of light and form.
