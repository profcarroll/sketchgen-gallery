Brief

The canvas displays a continuous, slow-moving stream of translucent circles that appear to flow up from the bottom, creating a tide of varied shades of blue against a dark background. These overlapping shapes drift and change subtly. When the viewer clicks anywhere on the canvas, a visible ripple expands from the point of contact, temporarily pushing the nearby circles outward before they relax and flow back into the overall tide.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive underwater scene with a continuous flow of translucent blue circles rising from the bottom of the canvas. The circles drift slowly upward, overlapping and blending to form a dynamic tide of color. A subtle ripple effect is triggered on mouse click, temporarily pushing nearby circles outward before they return to their natural flow. The visualization relies on smooth motion and color transitions to evoke a sense of depth and fluidity.
