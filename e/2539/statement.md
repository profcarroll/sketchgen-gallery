This sketch creates an immersive underwater ecosystem of kelp-like structures that rhythmically form, expand, and collapse in a continuous cycle. Each structure is composed of interwoven arcs and loops that simulate the organic tension and release found in deep-sea environments. The visual effect captures the delicate balance between structural integrity and inevitable collapse, with vibrant but muted colors that emphasize the constant mechanical tension. Structures slowly grow into complex forms before gradually slumping under their own weight, eventually fracturing into particle systems that drift away. A subtle user interaction can temporarily intensify this mechanical tension, causing localized disruptions in the overall pattern, creating a dynamic interplay between order and chaos. The entire system operates without external input once started, continuously evolving through its own natural rhythm.
