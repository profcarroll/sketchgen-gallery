This sketch depicts a scene of gentle decay and movement within a translucent aqueous medium. Paper-cut ships drift slowly away, aged with visible tears, barnacles, and mineral deposits that reflect the passage of time. Behind them, crystalline salt streaks flow downward, leaving trails in the medium, while detached paper fragments drift and accumulate at the bottom. The visual effect is achieved through a layered approach: ships are drawn with age details like tears and barnacles using simple geometric shapes; salt streaks are rendered as vertical lines that move down the canvas; and fragments are small drifting particles that gradually settle at the base. All elements are animated independently to create a cohesive sense of ongoing deterioration and movement within a single, serene scene.
