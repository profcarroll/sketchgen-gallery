This sketch presents a stylized isometric view of a data center interior filled with stacked server racks. The visualization emphasizes metallic geometry and the flow of computation through abstract data ribbons that weave between the hardware. At various locations within the racks, pulsating hotspots emit intense light that casts sharp geometric patterns across the metallic surfaces. These light patterns dynamically pulse and wash over the environment, creating a vivid representation of computational intensity. The scene is rendered entirely in WEBGL with performance-conscious techniques to maintain visual complexity while staying within frame budget constraints.
