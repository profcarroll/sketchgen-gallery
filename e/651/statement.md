This sketch creates a dynamic stovetop visualization with realistic heat effects. The circular countertop features four glowing burners that activate heat patterns when the mouse moves near them. These patterns appear as shimmering, colorful distortions that spread outward from active burner zones, with colors shifting based on which burners are active. The technique uses perlin noise to simulate fluid-like movement of the heat waves, creating an organic, living effect across the counter surface. The viewer sees a luminous wash of color that suggests intense temperature variation and realistic thermal dynamics, with the patterns responding both to burner activation and subtle mouse interactions.
