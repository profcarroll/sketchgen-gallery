This sketch visualizes the explosive fracturing of a pressurized gas mass into thousands of glass-like particles. The scene begins with a glowing, cohesive gas sphere that slowly builds internal tension. When the pressure reaches critical mass, the gas violently explodes into fragments that scatter outward in unpredictable patterns. These fragments—represented as sharp points in 3D space—multiply and accelerate as they radiate from the center, creating a crystalline cascade that rapidly fills the screen before dissipating back into the void. The visual effect emphasizes both the controlled buildup of energy and the chaotic release, with particles fading out over time to simulate their dissipation into a vacuum.
