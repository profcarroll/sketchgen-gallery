This sketch creates a richly woven textile pattern using a structured grid of repeating motifs. The composition features three distinct design types—floral elements, geometric knotwork, and interlocking rhombuses—that tile seamlessly across the canvas. Each motif is carefully rendered with jewel-toned colors and precise proportions to evoke the complexity and luxury of traditional weaving. The entire piece is framed by pronounced geometric border bands composed of continuous chevron patterns, reinforcing the structured nature of the textile. The work remains completely static as required, presenting a detailed, symmetrical composition that demonstrates both mathematical precision and artistic design through its repetitive yet varied pattern construction.
