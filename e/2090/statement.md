This sketch creates an immersive, evolving subterranean landscape where crystalline and biological structures merge into organic forms that appear to crawl and shift through space. The environment features thousands of glowing particles that move in slow, undulating patterns, mimicking the behavior of slime mould while maintaining the geometric qualities of crystal formations. Through constant animation and shifting light effects, viewers experience an ever-changing map of interconnected passages that pulse with internal illumination, creating a sense of continuous exploration and growth within an alien underground realm. The WEBGL renderer allows for complex 3D interactions and depth perception that enhances the feeling of being immersed in this luminous, organic space.
