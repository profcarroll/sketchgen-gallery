This sketch creates a mesmerizing visual rhythm of expanding concentric rings that emanate from the center of the canvas. Each ring propagates outward with its own unique speed and color, creating a continuous, pulsating pattern. The rings exhibit a pronounced ripple effect as they expand, achieved through sine wave modulation applied to their edges. The motion is completely automatic and self-contained, requiring no viewer interaction. The technique uses a particle-like approach where each ring is a dynamic geometric shape that evolves over time, with new rings continuously emerging while older ones fade away. The result is an hypnotic display of rhythmic expansion and contraction that fills the entire canvas with flowing, wave-like motion.
