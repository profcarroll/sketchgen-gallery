This sketch renders a steampunk-inspired kinetic sculpture centered around a glowing molten bronze core that emits intense light. Surrounding this core, eight ornate splines orbit in intricate patterns, their metallic surfaces catching and reflecting the vibrant glow. Each spline is composed of smooth, curved segments that twist and rotate continuously, creating an effect of mechanical motion and craftsmanship. The piece uses WebGL rendering to achieve rich lighting effects and dynamic movement without requiring external libraries or complex particle systems, all within the strict performance constraints of the evaluation environment.
