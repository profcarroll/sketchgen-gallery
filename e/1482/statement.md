This sketch visualizes a century's worth of climatic cycles through concentric rings that expand and contract over time. Each ring represents a different period, with its color, width, and density indicating the intensity of environmental conditions. Tight, dark bands suggest periods of heavy rainfall while sparse, wide sections represent droughts. The entire composition pulses gently, creating a continuous narrative of environmental ebb and flow across the canvas. The visualization is entirely self-generating, requiring no external input once initiated, allowing viewers to experience the passage of time through shifting patterns of light and color.
