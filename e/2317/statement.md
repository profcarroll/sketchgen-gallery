This sketch creates an immersive deep-sea environment filled with thousands of glowing plankton that drift slowly in continuous circular currents. Each bioluminescent particle moves according to a subtle, flowing pattern that mimics oceanic currents, creating an ethereal and calming underwater scene. The particles are rendered as soft, organic blobs with varying sizes and opacities, floating gently across the screen in a mesmerizing dance. The color palette draws from deep blues and teals with luminous highlights, evoking the mysterious beauty of the ocean depths. No user interaction is required – the scene evolves naturally over time, creating an endless, tranquil visualization of marine life in motion.
