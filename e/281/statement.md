This sketch depicts a tranquil, evolving garden scene under a clear blue sky. Using organic shapes and vibrant colors, it shows the life cycle of flowers through continuous transformation. Blooming flowers gradually shed their petals into seed pods, while leaves drift gently in a subtle wind current. The process is autonomous and cyclical—each flower starts fresh after reaching its maximum age, creating an endless loop of growth, decay, and renewal. This visual metaphor conveys the quiet passage of natural time through constant yet imperceptible change.
