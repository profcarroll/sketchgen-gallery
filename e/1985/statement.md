This sketch simulates the organic spread of wet ink on newsprint, capturing the unpredictable and continuous motion of capillary action. Dark liquid shapes appear randomly across the canvas and gradually bloom outward, mimicking how ink diffuses into paper fibers over time. The subtle texture of newsprint provides a realistic backdrop, while the dynamic growth of each ink blob creates an ever-changing visual field that reflects the passage of time through its uncontrolled, natural processes.
