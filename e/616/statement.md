This sketch renders a bustling urban scene using WebGL for high-performance 3D rendering. It features a grid-based cityscape with buildings of varying heights, streetlights, and vehicles moving along roads. The environment includes dynamic lighting effects from vehicle headlights and glowing signs, creating a sense of depth and activity. The composition uses batched geometry and optimized drawing techniques to maintain performance within strict frame budget constraints while delivering a rich, immersive cityscape.
