This sketch depicts an automated mechanical contraption that demonstrates a sequence of interconnected actions. The scene begins with weights dropping due to gravity, which in turn sets gears in motion through a system of ropes and pulleys. As the gears rotate, they activate a watering can mechanism that tips over, releasing water toward a central flower. The animation shows fluid splashing, rotating gears, and flowing water rivulets. The viewer observes a continuous automated process where each action triggers the next, creating a cascading effect from mechanical motion to liquid flow, all within a single unbroken visual narrative.
