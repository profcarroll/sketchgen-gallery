This sketch creates a retro-style driving simulation with a curved road that extends into the distance. The viewer controls a vehicle using arrow keys—steering left/right and accelerating forward while having the ability to brake. The environment features periodic visual obstacles along the edges of the road and side roads branching off at intervals. The perspective is rendered using a series of diminishing road segments that curve gently, creating a sense of depth and motion as the vehicle moves forward automatically. The scene demonstrates classic 3D perspective techniques with a simple but effective visual style reminiscent of early driving games.
