This sketch creates an endless, mesmerizing display of expanding concentric rings that originate from the center of the canvas. Each ring simultaneously grows outward and ripples like liquid, creating a hypnotic, organic flow. The rings feature a vibrant fluorescent color palette with dynamic hue shifts and subtle alpha fades. The visual effect is achieved through careful mathematical modeling of waveforms that create the rippling motion while maintaining smooth, continuous expansion. The result is an ever-changing, self-contained pattern that fills the entire canvas with a sense of perpetual motion and fluidity, reminiscent of natural phenomena like water ripples or biological growth patterns.
