This sketch creates a vibrant, ever-changing arcade grid using p5.js. It features animated geometric shapes—circles, squares, and triangles—in neon colors that move and bounce around the screen. The shapes continuously morph in position, direction, and rotation, creating a dynamic, chaotic visual effect reminiscent of classic video game displays. As shapes interact with each other, they generate colorful particle bursts that simulate digital fireworks. When clicked, the canvas responds with an immediate explosion of particles centered on the mouse position, providing tactile feedback to user interaction. The entire scene evolves autonomously without any input, fulfilling the requirement for continuous motion while maintaining a visually engaging and playful aesthetic typical of retro arcade experiences.
