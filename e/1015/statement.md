This sketch creates two distinct streams of colorful particles that flow continuously across the canvas. Each stream maintains its own identity with vibrant, contrasting hues—blue and purple for one stream, orange and pink for the other. The particles move according to a dynamic flow field, giving them organic, flowing motion. When the streams approach each other, they exhibit a gentle repulsive behavior, curving away in harmonious patterns rather than intersecting or mixing. The visualization emphasizes continuous motion and spatial relationships through visual connections between nearby particles, creating an ever-shifting yet cohesive display of fluid dynamics within a fixed canvas space.
