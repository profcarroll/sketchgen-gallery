This sketch creates an endless cascade of colorful, translucent glass-like blocks that spiral and tumble across the screen. Each block has irregular, organic shapes and varies in size, movement speed, and color hue. The animation features a continuous spiral motion combined with gravity effects, creating a mesmerizing flow of light and color. The blocks appear to refract brilliant hues as they move, simulating the effect of light interacting with glass surfaces. The composition maintains a constant stream of falling elements without any still points or centers, creating an eternal, dynamic display that fills the entire canvas with motion and visual interest.
