This sketch creates a dynamic, abstract composition using geometric bands that continuously shift and reconfigure across the canvas. The bands are defined by sharp angular polygons that morph in real-time through sinusoidal distortion effects, creating an ever-changing sense of depth and structure. Each band moves independently with its own rotation speed and color hue, cycling through a vibrant retro palette. The composition maintains a bold, high-contrast aesthetic while delivering fluid motion that keeps the viewer engaged without any external input or user interaction.
