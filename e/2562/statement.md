This sketch renders a cross-section of geological strata as if viewed through the lens of a Victorian natural history cabinet. The composition features layered formations of petrified wood and shimmering mica sheets, arranged in a cathedral-like scale that suggests deep time and preserved natural processes. Through subtle pulsing animation and carefully chosen oxidized color tones, the work evokes the feeling of ancient life force trapped within stone, creating an ethereal visualization of geological history that pulses with its own quiet rhythm. The scene captures both the delicacy of preserved organic matter and the grandeur of Earth's layered chronology.
