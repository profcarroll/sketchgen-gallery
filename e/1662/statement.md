This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic environment where translucent circles drift slowly across the screen, each shifting through hues and pulsing with luminosity. The circles leave behind glowing trails that fade gently as they move, forming a continuous flow of light that evokes the sensation of a cosmic tide. The visual effect is achieved through careful management of transparency, color cycling, and position tracking to create an immersive, hypnotic field of motion. The implementation uses a semi-transparent background to allow trail effects to accumulate, while maintaining performance within strict frame budget constraints.
