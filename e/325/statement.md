This sketch depicts an otherworldly deep-sea landscape where geometric pyramidal structures rise from the ocean floor. The crystalline forms are rendered with translucent materials that reflect light and cast sharp shadows against the deep blue ocean background. The scene features subtle motion through constant camera movement and animated pyramids that gently rotate and sway. Fine particles represent sediments swirling in the water, dynamically lifted by flowing currents to create an ever-shifting landscape of pale dust and crystalline geometry. The use of WEBGL rendering allows for realistic lighting effects and a three-dimensional perspective that immerses viewers in this alien underwater environment.
