This sketch creates a mesmerizing scene of floating, rotating metallic shards in a dark void. Using the WEBGL renderer, it generates hundreds of jagged, chrome-textured fragments that drift and spin slowly through space. Each shard is composed of irregular polygonal faces with carefully placed highlights to simulate metallic reflections. A deep emerald light at the center casts sharp glints across the surfaces, creating dynamic interplay between light and shadow. The scene rotates gently over time, revealing the three-dimensional complexity of the shards as they move through the dark environment. The effect is both ethereal and mechanical, evoking the sense of something ancient and precious floating in cosmic darkness.
