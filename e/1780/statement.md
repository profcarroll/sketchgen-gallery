This sketch depicts an endless flow of semi-transparent text characters that swirl in a continuous vortex. At regular intervals, the characters briefly organize into clear, high-contrast lines reminiscent of old newsprint. These structured arrangements then scatter apart and dissolve back into the flowing stream. The visual effect captures the tension between order and chaos, with the text both coalescing into readable form and then breaking apart again, creating a mesmerizing rhythm of formation and dissolution.
