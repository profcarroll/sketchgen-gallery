This sketch portrays a cosmic void with a central monolith structure that slowly rotates in three-dimensional space. The composition uses a dark background with thousands of distant stars to create depth and scale. The monolith is rendered using geometric simplicity—a collection of points forming a metallic, angular form that subtly shifts its orientation over time. The hypnotic motion suggests immense, timeless presence within the vastness of space. The visual technique emphasizes clean, minimal forms against deep darkness to evoke both the cold sterility of space and the imposing scale of the mysterious structure.
