Brief

A uniform, continuous grid of interconnected squares pulses gently, with each square exhibiting its own subtle, rhythmic breathing motion. The canvas remains alive with this soft pulsation until the viewer interacts with it. When the screen is clicked, a visible wave propagates outward from the point of impact. The wave flashes a bright, randomized color palette, but its most distinct effect is leaving behind a residual shadow on every square it passes through. This shadow is not instantaneous; it persists for an extended period, slowly darkening and then gradually fading back to the square's normal, steady pulsation.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a living grid of interconnected squares that breathe gently with a rhythmic pulse, each square subtly expanding and contracting in harmony. The canvas comes alive with this continuous motion until a viewer interacts with it through a click. When clicked, a bright flash propagates outward as a wave, leaving behind persistent shadows on every square it touches. These shadows don't instantly fade but linger, gradually darkening before slowly returning to the square's normal pulsation. The visual effect transforms an initially calm, organic rhythm into a dynamic, interactive experience where each interaction creates a ripple of lasting change across the entire grid.
