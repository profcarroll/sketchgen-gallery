This sketch creates an immersive, dynamic lattice structure that evokes the feeling of a vast brass celestial dome. Using WebGL rendering, it constructs a complex network of interwoven lines that branch and flow organically through three-dimensional space. The structure appears to float with internal lines that slowly pulse and shift, suggesting liquid energy moving through metallic framework. Rich antique brass tones dominate the color palette, creating depth and mechanical elegance. The entire composition is completely self-contained, evolving without any user input through subtle rotations, pulsing glows, and floating particles that traverse the lattice structure. The viewer experiences a sense of infinite complexity and organic flow within a geometrically precise framework, as if observing a microscopic cross-section of some vast, living mechanical universe.
