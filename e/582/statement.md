This sketch presents a dynamic cross-sectional view of a combustion engine's internal mechanics. It visualizes the cyclical motion of pistons within cylinders, connected by connecting rods to a rotating crankshaft, all driven by simulated spark ignition. Brightly colored fluid flows illustrate the intake and exhaust processes, showing how gases move through ports during each stroke. The scene is rendered in 3D using WEBGL for depth perception and mechanical accuracy, with continuous animation that captures the rhythm of engine operation without user interaction.
