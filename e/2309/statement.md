This sketch portrays a cross-section of ancient geological time, rendering horizontal layers of pale chalk over a dark slate base. The layers subtly shift and blur over time, mimicking the slow dissolution and redeposition caused by mineral-rich water dripping through rock. The effect is achieved through animated horizontal bands that gently oscillate, combined with a continuous stream of falling drips that grow and fade as they descend. The visual interplay between the static sedimentary layers and the dynamic water elements creates an impression of deep time's slow but persistent geological processes, revealing the earth's hidden history at an immediate scale.
