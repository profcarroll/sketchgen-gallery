Brief

The viewer is presented with an immersive, sprawling cityscape viewed from an open perspective. Towers and buildings populate the ground, creating a detailed urban landscape. The scene is not static; the primary element of change is the dramatic interplay of light and shadow. As the simulated time progresses, the angle and length of the shadows cast by the buildings dynamically shift across the pavement and adjacent structures, giving the entire scene a cyclical, living movement.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch renders an immersive urban landscape using p5.js's WEBGL mode, where buildings of varying heights are arranged in a grid across the scene. The composition simulates a dynamic day-night cycle through the shifting angle of a directional light source that acts as the sun, casting moving shadows beneath each structure. As time progresses, the interplay between light and shadow creates a living, evolving cityscape that changes continuously without any user input. The visual effect emphasizes the cyclical nature of urban environments, where the passage of time is reflected not just in the sky but in how light interacts with architecture.
