This sketch embodies the principles of Swiss modernism through a rigid grid-based typographical poster, yet introduces subtle rhythmic variations in negative space to create visual tension. The composition uses mathematical functions to modulate cell dimensions across rows and columns, generating an undulating rhythm that guides the viewer's eye. The result is a structured, ordered framework where the spacing itself becomes a dynamic element, emphasizing the relationship between text elements and their surrounding empty space. The interplay of regularity and controlled variation creates a sense of measured movement within the static design, reflecting how Swiss typography balances precision with visual interest.
