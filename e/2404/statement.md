This sketch creates an immersive, slow-motion environment of crystalline structures that pulse and dissolve into flowing, iridescent patterns. The visual system uses a single batched point cloud for thousands of particles, each moving with organic sine-based motion while maintaining a consistent field of glowing points. The particles' colors shift through a dynamic hue cycle, creating a sense of continuous color bleeding across the canvas. Additional floating spheres contribute to a liquid-like glow that permeates the entire composition, suggesting both solid geometry and fluid movement in a seamless, undulating rhythm.
