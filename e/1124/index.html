Brief

The canvas displays a dynamic, cross-sectional cutaway of a running internal combustion engine. Pistons are shown moving up and down inside their respective cylinders, powered by the rotation of the crankshaft. The visualization highlights the timing mechanism: the intake and exhaust valves are clearly visible and open and close with precise timing, demonstrating their relationship to the piston's position. The overall composition uses mechanical shapes and colored elements to illustrate the continuous, cyclical process of combustion.
