This sketch visualizes a dynamic, cross-sectional cutaway of a running internal combustion engine. It shows pistons moving up and down within their cylinders, driven by the rotation of a crankshaft. The timing mechanism is highlighted through clearly visible intake and exhaust valves that open and close in precise synchronization with the piston's position. Mechanical shapes and colored elements illustrate the continuous, cyclical process of combustion, emphasizing how the engine operates as a coordinated system of moving parts.
