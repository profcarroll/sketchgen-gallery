This sketch presents a digital jigsaw puzzle where colorful, interlocking pieces are scattered across a dark canvas. Each piece displays a fragment of a vibrant landscape image, and users can click and drag them around the screen. When a piece is moved near its correct destination, it snaps into place to help complete the picture. The interface simulates the satisfying interaction of physical puzzle assembly through visual feedback and snapping mechanics, with pieces maintaining their image fragments while being draggable and responsive to user input.
