This sketch creates a mesmerizing visual effect of concentric rings that emanate rhythmically from the center of the canvas. Each ring expands outward at its own pace while pulsing in color intensity with a gentle, hypnotic rhythm. The system operates entirely autonomously, creating a continuous meditative cycle without any user interaction required. The fluorescent hues shift gradually over time, and each ring's expansion is controlled to create a harmonious flow that fills the screen with dynamic, flowing energy.
