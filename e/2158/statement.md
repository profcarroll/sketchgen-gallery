This sketch presents a static, crystalline lattice structure composed of interconnected rubber bands forming a rigid geometric pattern. The composition uses a fixed grid of points connected by lines to simulate the tension and connectivity of elastic bands. The viewer experiences an unchanging, deeply ordered arrangement that evokes the sense of cold, silent stability found in geometric mineral formations. The visual effect emerges from the interplay between the sparse connections and the regularity of the underlying lattice, creating a sense of structural integrity without any motion or animation.
