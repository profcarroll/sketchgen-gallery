This sketch creates an abstract textile tapestry using flowing thread paths that weave across the canvas. The composition features hundreds of interconnected lines that drift and shift gently, mimicking the movement of countless threads in a woven fabric. A dynamic color wash overlays this texture, with its saturation level responding to mouse position — from muted pastels to vibrant hues — suggesting the passage of different dyes through the interwoven pattern. The technique uses Perlin noise to create natural-looking motion and multiple layered shapes to build up the complex, flowing texture.
