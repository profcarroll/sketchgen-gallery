This sketch renders a stylized portrait of HAL-2000, the iconic AI from "2001: A Space Odyssey." Using geometric forms and a dark color palette dominated by blacks and metallic grays with warning red accents, I've created a static yet detailed illustration. The central optical sensors are rendered with intense glowing effects using layered ellipses and gradient fills. The composition emphasizes clean lines and angular geometry to convey the machine's sophisticated intelligence while maintaining a sense of stillness and contemplation.
