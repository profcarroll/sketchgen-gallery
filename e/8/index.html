Brief

The canvas displays several expanding concentric rings that pulsate steadily. These rings appear in vibrant, fluorescent colors, creating a pulsing visual rhythm. Unlike previous versions, the color variation is tightly controlled, restricted entirely to a specific, limited temperature range. The rings continuously grow and contract, cycling through this contained, brilliant palette, creating a mesmerizing and self-contained optical display.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a mesmerizing optical display of pulsating concentric rings that expand and contract rhythmically. Each ring cycles through a carefully controlled color palette ranging from deep red to bright yellow, maintaining a consistent temperature range throughout the animation. The rings grow and shrink in a synchronized pattern, creating a hypnotic visual rhythm that captivates the viewer with its steady, self-contained motion. The use of sine wave timing ensures smooth, natural-looking oscillation while the limited color palette maintains visual coherence across all elements.
