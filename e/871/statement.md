This sketch creates an ever-evolving interplay between organic fluidity and geometric precision. Hundreds of glowing, amorphous planes drift and morph across the screen in subtle, hypnotic motions, their colors slowly shifting through the spectrum. Overlaid on this organic flow is a dynamic hexagonal grid that pulses and undulates with wave-like motion, creating a visual tension between the natural and structured. The planes are rendered using WebGL for smooth performance, with each one having its own animation parameters and color properties. The result is a mesmerizing composition where the rigid angular structure of the grid contrasts beautifully with the flowing, organic movement of the planes, creating an immersive, constantly changing visual experience that feels both alien and familiar.
