This sketch presents a three-dimensional model of our solar system, rendered with accurate relative scaling and orbital mechanics. The central star emits light while planets orbit around it at speeds proportional to their real-world orbital periods. Each celestial body is represented by a sphere with appropriate size and color, and their orbits are shown as faint luminous trails. The entire scene rotates slowly to provide perspective, and interaction begins when the viewer clicks the canvas — triggering the animation of all planetary movements. The model uses realistic proportions scaled for visual clarity within the confines of the canvas, offering an immersive yet accessible representation of space.
