This sketch creates an immersive, ever-shifting tapestry of deeply saturated color fields that float and breathe across the canvas. Using layered rectangles with soft edges and subtle glow effects, it simulates vast, overlapping planes of color that softly blend into one another. The composition relies on gentle, organic motion—slow floating movements, subtle size variations, and continuous hue shifts—to create a sense of profound depth and resonance. The result is a meditative visual experience where color fields appear to float in space, constantly transforming through their interwoven tones without any direct user interaction.
