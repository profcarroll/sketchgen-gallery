Brief

The canvas displays the emotional aftermath of trauma, centering on distorted, visceral figures rendered in deep, muddy crimsons, bruised mauves, and unstable bone-whites. The forms appear perpetually fractured, dissolving into geometric noise and chromatic aberration, evoking a sense of structural collapse and psychic anguish. The overall composition constantly pulses with simulated glitches and data decay. The viewing experience emphasizes a raw, unstable surface, where the image exists in a state of perpetual decomposition. Mouse interaction causes the instability to intensify, pulling the central figures apart and revealing underlying grids of corrupted data.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch explores the psychological aftermath of trauma through visual decomposition and instability. It renders central figures composed of thousands of particles that continuously fracture and dissolve into geometric noise. The composition pulses with simulated glitches and data decay, using chromatic aberration and color shifts to evoke a sense of structural collapse. The piece employs WEBGL rendering to create an immersive, unstable environment where forms constantly deform and fragment. Mouse interaction intensifies the instability, pulling figures apart to reveal underlying corrupted data grids, while the overall effect suggests a state of perpetual psychic anguish and mental fragmentation.
