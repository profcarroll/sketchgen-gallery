This sketch explores the psychological aftermath of trauma through visual decomposition and instability. It renders central figures composed of thousands of particles that continuously fracture and dissolve into geometric noise. The composition pulses with simulated glitches and data decay, using chromatic aberration and color shifts to evoke a sense of structural collapse. The piece employs WEBGL rendering to create an immersive, unstable environment where forms constantly deform and fragment. Mouse interaction intensifies the instability, pulling figures apart to reveal underlying corrupted data grids, while the overall effect suggests a state of perpetual psychic anguish and mental fragmentation.
