Brief

The canvas displays a dense, complex tangle of cables and wires forming a continuous network. These structures are traversed by vibrant, glowing data streams that pulse and flow through the system. The streams move rhythmically, traversing all cable thicknesses and intensifying as they approach major junctions, where they visibly brighten and briefly split into secondary streams. The animation is constantly shifting and intricate, simulating the flow of energy data through the network. No user interaction is required to observe the luminous, complex visual flow.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a dynamic visualization of a complex network system, resembling data centers or electrical grids. The cables form a tangled web across the canvas with connections that flow through junction points. Vibrant glowing streams move rhythmically along these pathways, pulsing and brightening as they approach major intersections where they sometimes split into secondary flows. The visual effect simulates the constant flow of information or energy through interconnected systems, with each stream maintaining its own color and timing while interacting with the overall network structure. The system is entirely self-contained, generating new streams continuously and allowing the network to evolve over time without any user input required.
