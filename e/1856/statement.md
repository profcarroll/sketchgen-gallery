This sketch renders a static, highly symmetrical textile pattern inspired by traditional Persian rugs. The composition features a central lattice grid filled with stylized floral motifs in rich jewel tones, arranged with perfect vertical and diagonal symmetry. Around the edges lies a continuous border composed of interlocking triangles and squares, creating a dynamic yet harmonious frame that emphasizes the overall geometric balance. The work presents an intricate visual field that invites contemplation of pattern repetition and cultural artistry, executed entirely through geometric drawing primitives within the constraints of static display.
