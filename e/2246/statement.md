This sketch presents an evolving geological cross-section of poured concrete, revealing crystalline strata deep within its structure. The work depicts a mesmerizing, impossible mineral physics where sediment layers continuously lift and fall in counter-gravity formations, creating a meditative display of structural transformation. Through subtle, slow-moving horizontal planes rendered in varying shades of gray and earth tones, the piece suggests the passage of time and the slow settling of materials. The floating particles represent sediment that moves against gravity's pull, creating an illusion of impossible physics while maintaining a calm, contemplative atmosphere. The viewer observes this self-contained geological process unfold without any external input, experiencing the gentle transformation as a continuous, evolving visual narrative.
