This sketch creates a dynamic field of interconnected, pulsing waves that simulate subterranean seismic activity. The visual field is dominated by deep blues transitioning to indigo and cobalt, with each wave element moving independently at its own rhythm. The waves ripple and pulse continuously across the canvas, creating an impression of silent energy flowing beneath the surface. When a user interacts by clicking or touching the canvas, localized ripples emanate outward from the point of contact, disrupting the ongoing pattern with visible flare effects that pulse and dissipate. The technique uses sine wave functions to create organic motion, combined with HSB color space for rich, gradient-based visuals that maintain consistent depth and saturation across all elements.
