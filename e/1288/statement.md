This sketch depicts a sawmill process where cylindrical logs are continuously fed through a cutting mechanism and transformed into rectangular planks. The animation shows logs descending from the top of the frame, being sliced into planks that then fall toward the bottom. As the planks reach the base, they automatically settle into a perfectly organized row. The visual metaphor captures the mechanical precision and rhythmic flow of industrial timber processing, with logs appearing as rotating cylinders and finished planks as stacked rectangles that organize themselves into neat lines at the bottom of the screen.
