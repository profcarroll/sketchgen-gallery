This sketch creates a perpetually cycling cityscape that transitions through a full day-night light cycle. The scene features rigid angular architectural structures that are scored by vibrant crystalline trails—these trails emit their own luminous energy as they intersect with the environment. At the base of these structures, pools of liquid gather and reflect the shifting atmospheric light while displaying fractal distortions caused by subtle movements within the liquid surfaces. Throughout the landscape, bioluminescent flora pulses rhythmically, casting visible light that interacts with metallic deposits in the pooled liquid. These deposits respond to the pulsing light, creating secondary luminescence effects that complete the continuous atmospheric cycle. The entire scene is rendered using WebGL for its volumetric quality and dynamic lighting effects, with all elements moving autonomously to simulate an unattended, evolving environment.
