This sketch visualizes an abstract, luminous circuit board where data streams flow through a grid of connections. The background features a lattice of glowing lines that shift and pulse in response to the motion of particles moving across the canvas. These particles leave behind glowing trails, creating a continuous flow of light that evokes the sensation of data transmission within a computer's internal architecture. The scene is entirely self-contained and autonomous, with no external input required — the movement and lighting changes naturally over time to create a mesmerizing, organic visualization of digital activity.
