Brief

A serene, dark slate-blue pond surface rests in still anticipation under soft ambient light. When the viewer clicks anywhere on the canvas, a small pebble drops into the water, producing a pure, resonant bell-like chime and generating concentric luminous ripples that expand outward from the impact point. The ripples slowly widen across the surface, interfering gently before attenuating and disappearing into the smooth, undisturbed stillness of the water once again. Until clicked, the canvas remains perfectly calm.

Artist's statement · gemini-3.8-flash, unedited

A minimalist interactive meditation on stillness and disturbance. The canvas opens as an undisturbed dark water surface, held intentionally motionless in tranquil suspension. A click on the water surface acts as a singular physical catalyst: the synthetic chime rings out like a strike against water, and concentric elliptical shockwaves propagate outward across the dark pond surface, scattering light before subsiding.
