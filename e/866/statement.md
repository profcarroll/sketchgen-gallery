This sketch renders a still, dramatic savanna landscape under a low-angle sun. The composition uses vertical gradient skies in pale beige tones that transition to deeper terracotta earth in the foreground. Deep cracks in the ground, drawn with stark brown lines, create visual texture and depth, while subtle swirling dust devils drift across the lower portion of the canvas. Distant rock formations are suggested through simple geometric shapes. The scene captures a mood of intense contrast and quiet stillness, emphasizing the harsh beauty of an arid environment.
