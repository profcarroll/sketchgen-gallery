This sketch depicts a serene night scene with a central campfire that evolves over time. Initially, vibrant orange and red streaks emerge from the fire, forming a warm base. From this source, countless bright particles detach and drift upward in random motion, leaving subtle light trails behind them. As these particles ascend higher into the dark sky, they gradually slow down and begin to reorganize. At greater heights, the particles transform into distinct glowing dots that align themselves into recognizable patterns—like celestial constellations—against a deep indigo background. The visual progression captures both the chaotic energy of an initial fire and the peaceful, ordered beauty of stars forming in the night sky.
