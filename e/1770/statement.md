This sketch creates an interactive stovetop simulation with a control dial that adjusts heat levels across four burners. When users drag the dial, it activates corresponding burners with realistic flame effects that include flickering particles and gradient coloring. The metal surrounding each burner glows red with increasing intensity as heat rises. A heat shimmer effect radiates outward from active burners, creating an illusion of intense thermal energy rising from the surface. The interface responds dynamically to user interaction while maintaining a visually consistent and physically plausible simulation of heat transfer and combustion effects.
