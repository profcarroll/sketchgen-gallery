Brief

The canvas displays a radial array of nested, glowing arcs originating from the center. This field is constantly in motion, pulsating rhythmically from the core outward. With each pulsation phase, the colors of the arcs smoothly cycle through the entire visible spectrum, creating a continuous wash of hue. The entire pattern is self-contained, but the outermost rings are highlighted by a noticeable, growing delay and a heightened amplitude during their pulse. The resulting effect is a mesmerizing, continuous wave of light and color emanating from the center.
