This sketch creates a mesmerizing radial field of nested, glowing arcs that pulse rhythmically from the center outward. The arcs continuously shift through the visible spectrum, creating a wash of color that emanates from the core. Each pulse phase is synchronized across all rings, but the outermost arcs exhibit a noticeable delay and heightened amplitude, producing a wave-like visual effect. The animation runs autonomously without any user input, relying entirely on time-based transformations to generate the continuous motion.
