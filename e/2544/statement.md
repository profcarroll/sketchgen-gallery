This sketch depicts an organic, microscopic cross-section of root systems and mycorrhizal filaments growing in a dark, wet environment. Using a particle-based approach with fluid, branching lines that constantly explore space, the visualization captures the unpredictable yet systematic nature of biological growth. The wet ink effect is achieved through careful stroke weights and color choices, while the pale textured background simulates newsprint. The system evolves organically without pattern, creating an ever-changing web that suggests continuous, slow biological processes.
