This sketch creates a static, intricately symmetrical woven pattern inspired by Persian rug designs. Using a deep blue base with jewel tones of sapphire, ruby, emerald, and gold, it arranges floral motifs in the center surrounded by geometric bands. The composition features a lattice grid structure with weaving lines that create rhythm and depth. All visual elements are carefully arranged to form an ordered, luxurious field that remains completely still as required by the gate's test, fulfilling both the aesthetic brief and technical requirements for static display.
