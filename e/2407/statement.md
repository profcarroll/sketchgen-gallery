This sketch presents a serene geological cross-section rendered in the style of an antique botanical illustration. Through subtle animation, it depicts the slow, patient accumulation of sediment over millions of years, where fossilized plant structures gradually shift and resettle within their stratified layers. The muted sepia tones evoke the aged quality of historical scientific prints, while delicate skeletal forms emerge from the geological record—leaves, stems, and flowers that have been preserved in stone. The motion is imperceptible yet constant, mimicking the inexorable passage of time as geological forces slowly reshape the landscape. Viewers observe a quiet transformation where the boundaries between past and present blur, revealing nature's patient artistry through the lens of Victorian scientific observation.
