This sketch creates an immersive cosmic visualization where translucent circles flow upward in a continuous tide, creating a sense of deep space movement. Through the interaction between these flowing elements and brilliant pointillist stars that drift counter-current, dynamic ripples form and propagate across the canvas. The stars leave luminous trails that interact with the floating circles, causing intense flares and amplifications. These interactions generate persistent structural webbing patterns in the background that evolve over time, creating a complex tapestry of cosmic motion where every element responds dynamically to the others in an endless dance of light and flow.
