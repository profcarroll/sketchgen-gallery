Brief

The canvas displays a deep, cosmic expanse dominated by a continuous tide of overlapping translucent circles that slowly flow upward. Floating through this fluid current are brilliant pointillist stars that drift counter-current to the flow. As the circles pass, they create visible, temporary ripples that linger and react to the light trails left by the stellar stream. These interactions cause the overlapping circles and the fading light trails to flare with intense, amplifying luminescence. Following the flares, the flowing water leaves behind faint, complex structural webs and filamentous webbing patterns in the background, which are themselves susceptible to flaring and glowing in response to the ongoing movement of the fluid and the stars.

These interactions generate persistent structural webbing patterns in the background that evolve over time, creating a complex tapestry of cosmic motion where every element responds dynamically to the others in an endless dance of light and flow.
