This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic environment where translucent circles flow slowly across the canvas, leaving behind glowing afterimages that gradually dissipate. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of particle movement and semi-transparent rendering, with each circle periodically creating fading trails that interact dynamically with nearby shapes. As these afterimages fade, they subtly shift the colors of adjacent circles, creating a mesmerizing, perpetually evolving display of light and color that evokes the fluid, luminous qualities of cosmic space.
