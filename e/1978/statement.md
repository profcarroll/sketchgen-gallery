This sketch presents an aerial view of a continuously knitting fabric strip, where the pattern evolves over time with both regular stitching and irregular disruptions. The work captures the tension between order and chaos in automated processes—while the fabric grows steadily, periodic "stalls" manifest as knots and tears that disrupt its continuity. These interruptions create an unpredictable visual rhythm that suggests the struggle of mechanical systems against their own limitations. The viewer observes a vast, seamless surface gradually deteriorating and reforming, embodying both the effort and fragility inherent in continuous production.
