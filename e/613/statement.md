This sketch creates a dynamic field of glowing, amorphous planes that drift independently across the canvas. Each plane has its own color, size, and motion pattern, creating a constantly shifting composition. The visual focus is on the intense boundaries where these planes meet — connections between nearby planes are drawn with increasing contrast that makes overlap points appear sharply defined and luminous against the surrounding glow. The field moves autonomously without user input, creating an immersive, organic flow of color and light that evolves over time.
