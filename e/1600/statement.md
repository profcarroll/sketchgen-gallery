This sketch depicts the quiet, continuous transformation of a digital landscape as organic moss spreads across it. The grid of abandoned pixels—once bright and distinct—gradually fades away as moss grows, revealing the natural process of decay and rebirth. When pixels are consumed by the moss, they shatter into colorful splinters that float away, creating an impression of constant, gentle destruction and renewal. The composition evolves organically over time, with no external input required, showing how life and death interweave in a continuous cycle.
