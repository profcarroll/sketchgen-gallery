This sketch visualizes the slow, powerful motion of geological folding and crumpling within a vast cross-section of metamorphic rock. Using a particle system with spatial grid optimization, it renders thousands of earth-toned particles that move in response to simulated tectonic forces. The particles flow and fold in rhythmic waves across the screen, creating mountainous structures that rise and subside over time. Connections between nearby particles form an intricate web that emphasizes the interlocking nature of geological stress, while particle trails leave visible paths through the rock layers. The constant motion suggests the immense pressure and slow violence of tectonic plate collisions, presenting a dynamic visualization of Earth's deep geological processes.
