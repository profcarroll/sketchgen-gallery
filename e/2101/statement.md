This sketch depicts a geological cross-section of petrified earth with layered strata that gradually splits open to reveal glowing crystalline forms. As the fissure widens, miniature inverted worlds emerge from within the crack — geometric crystals that pulse and shimmer with internal light. The animation captures the slow tectonic separation, showing how the opening fault reveals increasingly luminous structures. The viewer observes a transition from solid stone to the emergence of ethereal, glowing forms, evoking the idea of hidden beauty revealed through geological processes.
