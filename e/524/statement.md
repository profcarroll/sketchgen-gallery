This sketch renders an expansive urban environment using p5.js in WEBGL mode. It features a collection of randomly generated buildings with varying sizes and heights, arranged across a ground plane that represents the city street. The scene includes dynamic shadows cast by the buildings, which shift continuously to simulate the passage of time as the sun moves across the sky. The entire scene is animated without user interaction, demonstrating real-time lighting effects through the use of projected shadow geometry that updates each frame based on a calculated sun angle. The visual style emphasizes architectural forms and their interplay with light and shadow in an open-world cityscape.
