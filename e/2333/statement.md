This sketch creates an evolving tessellation of geometric forms inspired by 1920s Bauhaus design principles. The composition features a grid of polygonal tiles that continuously pulse and shift in size, rotation, and color, creating a dynamic field of light and shadow. Each tile is composed of multiple layers: a primary geometric shape with an inner pattern that subtly changes over time. The entire structure pulses rhythmically, with tiles gently moving across the canvas while maintaining their geometric integrity.
