Brief

The canvas displays a highly detailed mechanical object, rendered entirely in polished brass and styled as a 1920s Bauhaus-inspired pocket watch. The composition centers on the contrast between a delicate, superficial outer covering and the complex, organized mechanisms underneath. These internal structures—featuring visible gears, levers, and pipes—are shown in continuous, choreographed motion, giving the impression of a precisely functioning, clockwork exercise.
