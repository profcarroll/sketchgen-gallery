This sketch presents a meticulously detailed mechanical pocket watch inspired by 1920s Bauhaus aesthetics. The composition explores the contrast between an elegant outer brass casing and the intricate internal mechanisms that drive its function. Through continuous animation, visible gears rotate with precise timing, levers move in choreographed patterns, and pipes pulse with subtle motion—each element contributing to a unified impression of perfectly synchronized clockwork. The viewer observes a self-contained world where mechanical elegance meets functional complexity, rendered entirely within the geometric constraints of a WebGL canvas.
