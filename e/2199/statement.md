This sketch creates an ever-shifting geological landscape where crystalline formations emerge and grow from a rhythmic pendulum mechanism. The viewer witnesses a continuous flow of material that pulses with organic energy, triggered by mechanical dominoes that fall in sequence. As each domino strikes the surface, it sends forth expanding blooms that gradually solidify into temporary crystal structures. These formations are not static — they breathe and shimmer with subtle color shifts, their surfaces subtly distorted by noise functions that simulate molecular movement. The pendulum's oscillation adds a constant underlying rhythm to the entire composition, causing everything to gently sway in synchronization. The result is an abstract representation of geological processes captured through a mechanical trigger system, where each bloom becomes both an explosion and a crystallization event, leaving behind temporary records of fluid dynamics that slowly fade into solid form.
