Brief

Translucent, amber-hued plumes of beeswax drift and pulse through a dark, deep-sea environment. The plumes exhibit a rhythmic, flowing diffusion, moving with the heavy, organic viscosity of melting wax. Within the undulating structures, layered mineral deposits are revealed as dense, swirling streaks of ochre, gold, and charcoal. The entire composition breathes with a continuous, slow-motion pulse, mimicking the hydrothermal activity of a deep-sea vent.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive underwater environment where translucent amber plumes of beeswax float and pulse through deep-sea darkness. Using a particle system with noise-based movement, the simulation captures the slow, organic viscosity of melting wax as it drifts through the ocean. The particles breathe with rhythmic pulsing that mimics hydrothermal activity, while subtle hue shifts reveal layered mineral deposits in ochre, gold, and charcoal tones. The camera slowly orbits the scene to enhance the sense of depth and motion, creating a mesmerizing portrayal of deep-sea vent ecosystems where matter transforms through time and pressure.
