This sketch presents a monumental architectural composition of overlapping geometric panels that slowly drift and shift apart, rendered in the style of 1960s pen plotter drawings. The work features deeply saturated, glowing color planes that appear to bleed into one another, creating a sense of mechanical disintegration. Through subtle breathing motions, gentle rotations, and layered transparency effects, the panels float and morph in space, suggesting both stability and decay. The composition captures the essence of mid-century modern design while evoking the tension between order and chaos through its slow, methodical movements.
