I have created a desolate, 3D industrial landscape that captures a sense of heavy, rusted decay. The scene is constructed using a sprawling floor of textured sheet steel and a network of skeletal pipes that drift through a dark, atmospheric void. To evoke the passage of time and the feeling of abandonment, I implemented a system of rhythmic, oily droplets that fall from the overhead structures, catching the dim, directional light. The composition uses low-detail geometric primitives and batched rendering to maintain a high sense of scale and depth without exceeding the performance budget.
