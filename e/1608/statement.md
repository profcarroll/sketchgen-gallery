This sketch creates an ethereal jellyfish using segmented, prismatic light structures that flow and ripple across the screen. The composition features multiple layered waveforms with sharp geometric edges that pulse and drift, evoking the refractive quality of light passing through translucent forms. The piece uses a dark canvas background to make the luminous elements pop, with particles floating in the jellyfish's body creating bioluminescent flashes. The segmented design gives the impression of discrete beams of light rather than smooth gradients, while the constant motion creates an illusion of suspended liquid light drifting through space.
