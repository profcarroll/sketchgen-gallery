This sketch creates a dynamic network of glowing data streams flowing through an intricate web of cables and wires. The visualization features hundreds of thin cables connecting various points across the canvas, with luminous pulses traveling along them in rhythmic patterns. Each pulse has its own color, speed, and direction, creating a sense of continuous electrical communication throughout the abstract infrastructure. The scene animates itself without user interaction, with streams appearing to flow continuously along the cable paths, producing a mesmerizing effect of interconnected digital activity that fills the entire canvas.
