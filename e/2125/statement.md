This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of static geometric patterns etched into the surface of a ceramic tile. Rather than animating elements, it presents a complex arrangement of intersecting lines and polygonal forms that create an intricate visual texture. The patterns are composed of multiple nested polygons with varying rotations and scales, arranged across the canvas to form a cohesive, hypnotic composition. The interplay between different geometric shapes and their intersections creates depth and visual interest without any motion or animation, embodying the concept of etched, permanent design elements that remain perfectly still while conveying a sense of ordered complexity.
