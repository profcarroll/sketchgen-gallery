This sketch depicts the transformation of a perfectly symmetrical mandala into a singular central pillar through the process of structural collapse and gravitational reassembly. Initially, thousands of sand-like particles form an intricate geometric pattern with rich color variations and subtle vibrations. As wind gradually increases, the structure begins to destabilize and individual grains detach from their positions. Unlike scattering, these falling grains are magnetically drawn toward the center point where they accumulate into a growing vertical column. The visual effect shows a slow-motion metamorphosis from complex symmetry to focused dramatic unity, with each grain transitioning from its original position in the mandala to a new role as part of the central pillar.
