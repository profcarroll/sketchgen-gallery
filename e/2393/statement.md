This sketch visualizes a vast underground cavern formed from stacked layers of corrugated cardboard, resembling travertine rock formations. The scene presents a geological dig site perspective where massive layered structures pulse with rhythmic energy. Through dynamic lighting and pulsing connectivity lines, the work suggests deep, rhythmic forces flowing through subterranean strata. The architectural tableau combines geometric precision with organic texture, using subtle wave distortions to simulate the natural striations found in travertine deposits while maintaining the industrial materiality of cardboard.
