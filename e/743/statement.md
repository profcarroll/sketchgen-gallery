This sketch depicts a static, highly detailed mechanical portrait of HAL-2000, a humanoid machine characterized by metallic grays, deep blacks, and tarnished tones. The composition captures operational wear through subtle scratches, panel misalignments, and structural seams, rendered in a fixed, observational form. Using only p5's core API and avoiding any motion or sound, the work presents an unchanging yet intricate visualization of a complex mechanical structure.
