This sketch presents an underground railway network in a state of quiet decay, rendered with a monochromatic palette of concrete and rust. The viewer sees remnants of infrastructure—cracked walls, fragmented supports, and structural fissures—that evoke the scale and weight of forgotten systems. Overlaid on this static architecture flows a vibrant bioluminescent energy: glowing veins that pulse and meander through the decay, creating an impression of life forcing its way through rigid constraints. The interplay between the cold, geometric forms and the fluid, pulsing glow creates a dramatic contrast, making the luminous flow the central focus and dynamic element of the scene.
