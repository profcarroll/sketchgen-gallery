This sketch depicts a serene seaside scene at sunset, rendered in soft pastels of pink, orange, and teal. The composition features a stylized sailboat drifting slowly across the horizon, gently undulating waves beneath it, and a sky that subtly shifts color with interaction. Through motion, the boat glides smoothly from right to left, while the waves rhythmically pulse with gentle sine-based movements. When users drag their mouse across the canvas, liquid ripples form on the water's surface, causing the sky's hues to subtly shift — simulating a breeze passing through the scene. The piece captures both tranquility and subtle dynamism, using only p5.js core features without external libraries.
