Brief

The canvas displays impossible, architecturally complex structures that appear suspended in a dreamlike state of perpetual instability. Geometry is emphasized through fractured planes and dissolving segments, creating a visual language of structural stress. The composition utilizes a muted, otherworldly color palette, giving the impression of viewing the architecture through a haze of mist. The entire piece is defined by constant, subtle motion, as the perspective shifts and the impossible buildings seem to breathe around the viewer.
