This sketch creates an immersive, dreamlike visualization of impossible architectural structures suspended in space. Using WebGL rendering, it generates multiple complex geometric forms that appear to breathe and shift with subtle, organic motion. The composition employs a muted, otherworldly color palette and emphasizes structural stress through fractured planes and dissolving segments. Each structure consists of rotating triangular elements and floating planes that create an illusion of perpetual instability, as if viewed through a misty haze. The constant, subtle motion throughout the scene—driven by time-based transformations—ensures the canvas changes visibly over time without requiring user interaction, fulfilling the gate's motion requirements while maintaining the architectural complexity described in the brief.
