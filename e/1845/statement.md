This sketch depicts a vast desert landscape that exists primarily in deep shadow, where sudden flashes of light illuminate isolated patches of the textured ground. The scene captures the cyclical nature of light and shadow through rhythmic pulses that appear at random locations across the canvas. Each flash momentaneously brightens particles near its center while leaving the rest of the terrain in darkness, creating an effect of stark contrast and sudden illumination. The technique uses a particle system to simulate sand texture and global composite operations to create glowing light effects that blend with the dark base color, emphasizing the dramatic interplay between light and shadow.
