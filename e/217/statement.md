This sketch creates a dynamic, swirling backdrop reminiscent of early 1990s broadcast graphics. Using WebGL rendering, it generates multiple overlapping gradient layers that rotate slowly and independently, producing an ever-shifting field of color and light. Each layer consists of concentric ellipses with varying opacities and hues, creating depth through subtle motion and luminous transitions. The continuous rotation of these geometric fields mimics the energy of technical displays from that era, where information was conveyed through structured visual rhythm rather than static imagery. Viewers observe a constantly evolving pattern of color bands and energy fields, maintaining structural stability while allowing the light and color to continuously morph across the canvas.
