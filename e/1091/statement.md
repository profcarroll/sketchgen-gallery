This sketch creates an immersive, evolving collection of three-dimensional geometric forms that pulse and drift through a dark void. The visual experience is structured like an orchestral performance: quiet passages feature deep blues and violets, while sudden bursts of gold and crimson emerge during crescendos. Each shape—spheres, boxes, and tori—swells and rotates in synchronization, intersecting and moving through space with rhythmic motion. The palette cycles instantly on each click, changing both the color scheme and the tempo of movement, creating a dynamic visual suite that responds to user interaction.
