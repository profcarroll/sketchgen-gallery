This sketch creates a dynamic field of retro-styled pixel particles that move chaotically across the canvas, occasionally organizing into glowing geometric lattices. The particles are influenced by physics-based forces and proximity interactions, creating emergent behavior that shifts between disorder and structured order. Every few seconds, the entire field briefly coalesces into a lattice pattern that sequentially illuminates, then dissolves back into randomness—capturing the tension between chaos and form in a visually striking way.
