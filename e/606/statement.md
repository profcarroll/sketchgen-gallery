This sketch creates an immersive deep space scene using p5.js's WEBGL renderer to achieve a vast, cosmic perspective. The composition features thousands of twinkling stars rendered as individual points with varying sizes and brightness, arranged throughout three-dimensional space. Flowing dust lanes weave through the void in intricate, fractal patterns that continuously shift and drift, suggesting enormous interstellar structures in motion. A central Milky Way galaxy glows with nebulous light, constructed from noise-based particle distribution that creates a sense of depth and luminosity. The scene is animated without user input, with subtle rotation and dynamic movement creating an ever-changing cosmic tapestry that fills the entire canvas.
