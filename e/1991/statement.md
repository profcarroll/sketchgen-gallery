This sketch depicts a mechanical shredder in action, processing a stack of books into particles of debris, steam, and sparks. The scene is rendered using WebGL for immersive depth and motion. A central book stack feeds material into an industrial shredder apparatus containing rotating gears and tearing plates. As the machinery operates, it generates multiple particle systems: falling debris from paper and wood, rising steam from friction points, and sharp sparks from metal contact. These particles accumulate into a swirling vortex at the base of the shredder, colored like rusted steel to evoke the appearance of an underwater hydrothermal vent. The viewer observes this mechanical destruction through a slowly orbiting camera perspective that captures both the chaotic motion and the steady buildup of waste material. The entire scene operates within strict performance limits to ensure smooth animation even in constrained environments.
