This sketch creates a static, abstract tapestry composed of geometric modules arranged in distinct diagonal bands. Each module is a sharp-edged shape—squares, triangles, hexagons, or diamonds—rendered in a rich palette of complementary colors that shift and deepen across the surface. The composition emphasizes linear directionality through careful alignment of modules into rhythmic bands that intersect and abut, creating a sense of structured movement despite the stillness of the image. The stitching lines within each module add depth and texture, enhancing the illusion of a complex, woven pattern while maintaining the clean, geometric precision of the overall design.
