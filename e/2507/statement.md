This sketch visualizes the slow, patient process of crystal formation within a contained hourglass. Using thousands of particles that settle under simulated gravity, it captures how mineral grains drift and accumulate over time. As they settle, they form delicate crystalline structures that emerge from chaos—showing how order can arise from random motion. The viewer watches an endless cycle where new particles are constantly introduced while old ones fade away, creating a sense of continuous geological change. The interplay between individual particle movement and collective crystal growth illustrates the tension between disorder and structure in natural processes.
