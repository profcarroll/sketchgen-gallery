Brief

The canvas presents a highly detailed, stationary cross-section of a massive, rigid machine. Interwoven brass gears, cam systems, and levers occupy the space, depicting a complex mechanical structure at a colossal scale. The mechanism is perpetually active, operating in precise, repeating, non-fluid cycles. The entire assembly generates a mesmerizing, predictable motion rhythm, driven by internal systems that keep the brass components turning and interlocking.
