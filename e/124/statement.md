This sketch creates an immersive, ever-shifting cityscape where architecture reflects the dynamic interplay of light and atmosphere. Buildings are rendered with wet, reflective surfaces that mirror the sky's constant transformation from twilight to night. Bioluminescent flora pulses softly within the structures, while floating masses of luminous plankton drift slowly across the scene, creating a mesmerizing counterpoint to the environmental rhythm. The entire composition evolves over time without user input, driven by procedural animation and color transitions that simulate natural atmospheric cycles.
