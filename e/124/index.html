Brief

The viewer experiences a colossal, angular cityscape immersed in continuous atmospheric cycles, transitioning through dramatic twilight hues. The structures are coated in wet, reflective surfaces that act as mirrors for the sky and light. The ambient glow comes from pulsing bioluminescent flora integrated into the architecture. Most dynamically, small, clustered masses of luminous plankton drift and ripple across the highly saturated, wet surfaces, appearing to float in slow, mesmerizing counterpoint to the cycles of day and night. The entire scene is a constantly changing, time-driven spectacle.
