A reflective ice rink surface with sweeping white lines that continuously transform into luminous vertical pillars of frozen light. The static points along circular paths create dynamic columns that rise and fall with sinusoidal motion, while independently-moving pillars create a layered 3D landscape of crystalline structures. The WEBGL renderer emphasizes the ethereal quality of light refracting through ice, with subtle color shifts and translucent geometry suggesting both motion and permanence in an ever-changing frozen environment.
