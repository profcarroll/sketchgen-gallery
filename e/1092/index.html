Brief

Concentric rings in shades of red, gold, and green pulse rhythmically from the center, expanding and fading like waves in a liquid. The colors shift in intensity to maintain a steady, vibrating visual energy. Clicking the canvas changes the speed and pattern of the pulse, while dragging the mouse creates swirling, turbulent distortions within the moving rings.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a mesmerizing visual effect of concentric rings pulsing from the center of the canvas. The rings expand and contract rhythmically in shades of red, gold, and green, creating a wave-like motion that resembles ripples in liquid. The colors shift continuously in intensity to maintain a steady vibrating energy. The pattern responds dynamically to user interaction: clicking the canvas changes the speed and rhythm of the pulses, while dragging the mouse introduces swirling distortions that disrupt the otherwise smooth wave motion. The implementation uses a simple yet effective technique of drawing circles with varying radii and opacity, modulated by sine waves to create the pulsing effect, and applies spatial transformations based on mouse position for the interactive distortion.
