This sketch renders an impossible architectural landscape composed of fractured, self-similar planes that continuously morph under the influence of structural stress. Using WebGL rendering, it creates a series of deforming geometric surfaces that appear to dissolve into mist or warp into crystalline shards. The muted teal color palette and constant motion simulate tension and transformation, with each plane undergoing independent rotation and deformation over time. The visual effect is achieved through procedurally generated grids of quads that are subtly distorted in real-time, creating an overwhelming sense of continuous geometric transformation without using excessive computational resources.
