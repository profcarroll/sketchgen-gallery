This sketch depicts a mesmerizing underwater world where amorphous, glowing masses drift through a fluid medium. Within this suspended environment, smaller crystalline structures emerge and begin to move independently. As these crystals approach each other, they are drawn into attraction and eventually fuse together, forming increasingly larger geometric clusters with sharp polyhedral edges. The visualization captures the slow, hypnotic process of crystal aggregation and structural growth, creating an ever-evolving landscape of light and form within a dark, ethereal space.
