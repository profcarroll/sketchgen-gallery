This sketch renders a dynamic 3D cross-section of an internal combustion engine, showing pistons moving within cylinders driven by simulated gas expansion. The crankshaft rotates via connecting rods, while intake and exhaust valves open and close in sync with piston motion. Colorful fluid streams trace the flow through intake and exhaust ports, illustrating the continuous engine cycle. The visualization uses WebGL for 3D rendering and includes subtle audio feedback that responds to a single user click.
