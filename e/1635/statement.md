This sketch depicts a mechanical system that springs into motion when a watering can tips over. The animation begins with stationary gears and weights, then triggers the tipping action that sends water flowing through the apparatus. As the can tips, it releases droplets that follow gravity and form rivulets that travel down to a central flower. The entire sequence operates autonomously without user input, showing an intricate chain reaction where mechanical components initiate fluid dynamics. The viewer observes the interplay between rigid mechanical elements and the fluid motion of water as it cascades into the target flower, creating both visual rhythm and dynamic interaction.
