This sketch creates an immersive visualization of data flowing through a crystalline canyon system. Using the WEBGL renderer, it generates multiple parallel ribbons of light that move autonomously through space, each with its own color, width, and flow characteristics. The ribbons are constructed using sine wave functions with noise-based variations to create organic, flowing movements that suggest data transmission through an underground network. The glowing edges and constant motion create a sense of depth and continuous flow, as if viewers are witnessing information streams moving through a vast subterranean space. The technique uses particle-based geometry with batched shape drawing to maintain performance while creating the illusion of complex volumetric movement.
