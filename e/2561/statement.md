This sketch depicts a vast, evolving industrial landscape where colossal crystal formations emerge from metallic grids, growing and shifting in endless cycles of geological transformation. The viewer observes a dark, mineral-rich environment dominated by rust-colored crystalline structures that pulse with slow, rhythmic growth patterns. Using WebGL rendering, the composition features thousands of individual crystal elements each following their own temporal path while maintaining overall coherence through shared geometric principles and color palettes. The scene unfolds without external input, driven purely by mathematical progression and organic expansion, creating an impression of continuous material change over geological time scales.
