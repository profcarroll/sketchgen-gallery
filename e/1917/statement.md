This sketch depicts a dynamic, immersive field of luminous orbs influenced by invisible force streams emanating from the center of the canvas. Each orb drifts independently along paths shaped by these outward currents, creating an organic flow that appears both chaotic and harmonious. The orbs leave behind trails composed of distinct color bands that fade gradually, revealing their motion history in a continuous stream of chromatic light. The interplay between the central forces and swirling motions generates a mesmerizing choreography of glowing particles, each pulsing softly with its own rhythm while being guided by the overarching energy field.
