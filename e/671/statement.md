This sketch depicts an isometric racetrack with glowing bioluminescent paths where marbles race toward a finish line. The scene features dynamic debris that glows and is attracted to the path, while three marbles move through lanes at different speeds. A countdown timer emphasizes urgency as time ticks down. The visualization uses WEBGL for 3D rendering with dynamic lighting and camera movement, creating an immersive racing environment with particle effects and timed competition elements.
