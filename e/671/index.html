Brief

The viewer sees an isometric, multi-lane racetrack hosting a competitive marble race. The course is composed of sections marked by a glowing, solid bioluminescent path. The scene is dynamic: small, iridescent specks of debris are scattered across the track, and these specks actively glow as they are attracted to and accumulate along the bioluminescent path. A marble moves through the lanes, racing toward a clearly defined goal. Overlaid on the course is a countdown timer, emphasizing the urgency of the marble reaching the finish line before time expires.

The visualization uses WEBGL for 3D rendering with dynamic lighting and camera movement, creating an immersive racing environment with particle effects and timed competition elements.
