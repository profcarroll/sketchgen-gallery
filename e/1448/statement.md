This sketch creates a static exhibition poster inspired by the Swiss design movement's rigorous grid systems and extreme contrast principles. The composition uses a strict 12×8 grid to organize typographic elements and geometric shapes in a high-contrast black-and-white scheme. Large title blocks dominate the top, while the rest of the canvas is filled with alternating text elements and rectangles that follow precise alignment rules. The work emphasizes formal structure and visual tension through careful spacing, consistent typography, and the systematic arrangement of elements that reflects the academic severity typical of Swiss design.
