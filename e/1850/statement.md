This sketch depicts a translucent paper sheet slowly deteriorating under aquatic conditions, populated by whimsical paper-cut ships that sail across its surface. As the ships move, they leave behind trails of sediment and mineral deposits, while their weathered hulls show subtle tears and moss growth. The most dramatic effect occurs at the edges of the sheet, where detached paper fragments accumulate into dense, mountainous drifts of solidified sediment. The scene captures the slow, inevitable decay of these delicate maritime artifacts through a blend of organic motion and crystalline mineral formations. The visual narrative unfolds without external input, with the canvas changing naturally over time as ships appear, decay, and dissolve into the accumulating debris field at the sheet's margins.
