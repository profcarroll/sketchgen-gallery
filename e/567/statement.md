This sketch creates an ambient grid of breathing squares against a dark background. Each square gently pulses in rhythm, creating a subtle, hypnotic effect. When clicked, the grid responds with an outward wave pulse that disrupts nearby squares, leaving lingering shadows that fade slowly. The interaction is both meditative and reactive—allowing viewers to engage with the piece through simple clicks while maintaining an underlying sense of calm motion. The visual effect emerges from a balance between organic breathing motion and precise wave propagation, creating a space where stillness and movement coexist.
