This sketch depicts a glass hourglass with an impossible sand flow that moves upward instead of downward. The visualization shows thousands of tiny reddish-orange particles that continuously cycle their motion in a perpetual reversal of gravity's pull. The particles are drawn within the confines of a stylized hourglass structure, with top and bottom bulbs connected by a narrow neck. The movement appears cyclical and automatic, representing time's reversal through the particle dynamics rather than any physical force. The minimalist background focuses attention entirely on this impossible flow, creating an abstract meditation on temporal mechanics through purely visual motion.
