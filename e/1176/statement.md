This sketch depicts a continuous sawmill process where cylindrical wooden logs move through a mechanical cutting system and are transformed into thin rectangular planks. The animation shows logs descending through a sawmill mechanism, being sliced into multiple planks that then fall vertically to the bottom of the canvas. These planks automatically settle into neat vertical stacks, demonstrating the industrial cycle of wood processing. The visual design uses earthy tones and metallic hues to evoke the textures of wood grain and industrial machinery, with smooth 3D rendering showcasing the geometric transformation from cylindrical logs to flat planks in a constantly cycling production process.
