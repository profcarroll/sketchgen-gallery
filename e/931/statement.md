This sketch renders a serene pastoral landscape under a dramatic sunset. The composition uses layered horizontal bands to create depth, with distant mountains softened by atmospheric perspective and foreground grass rendered with thousands of individual blades to suggest texture. The scene is static and deliberately still, capturing the grandeur of a fixed vista through color gradients, shape variation, and careful attention to light and shadow. The rich earth tones of green, gold, and blue evoke the feeling of a vast, tranquil countryside at twilight.
