This sketch portrays a monumental knitted wool tapestry rendered in the vastness of space. Through an endless field of glowing yarn strands, the viewer witnesses the slow unraveling of a massive textile — each fiber animated by noise and time to create organic movement. The deep blue gradient backdrop suggests cosmic depth, while the luminous threads appear to weave themselves into crystalline formations that seem to emerge and dissolve across the canvas. This is not merely a visual effect but an immersive experience of fiber art at an epic scale, where light interacts with countless threads to produce an ethereal glow.
