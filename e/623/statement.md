This sketch creates an immersive, cascading visualization of luminous particles that drift downward in a continuous stream. Using a large number of individually animated elements, each particle has its own speed, sway, and fading opacity to simulate a fluid, liquid light effect. The particles originate from the top of the canvas and flow at varying rates, creating a dynamic, ethereal composition filled with deep emerald and electric cyan hues. The movement is entirely self-contained, with no user interaction required, and continuously evolves over time to maintain visual interest and flow.
