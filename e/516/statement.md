This sketch presents a stylized cityscape rendered in WEBGL where viewers can interact with the environment by clicking to create scorch marks on the pavement. The scene features a grid of roads and towering buildings that define the urban layout, while damage particles appear at click locations and gradually fade away over time. The visual technique uses spherical particles with alpha transparency to simulate temporary surface damage that persists briefly before disappearing, creating a sense of interaction and temporal decay within the city environment.
