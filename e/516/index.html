Brief

The viewer experiences a sprawling, open-world cityscape populated by characters and vehicles. The primary method of interaction is controlling a player character or vehicle using directional inputs to traverse the map. Conflict is initiated by pressing a dedicated action key, which sends out an attack that visibly impacts the pavement. The core visual element of the environment is the temporary damage left by these attacks: they manifest as distinct scorch marks that remain on the pavement for a short duration before visibly and realistically fading away.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a stylized cityscape rendered in WEBGL where viewers can interact with the environment by clicking to create scorch marks on the pavement. The scene features a grid of roads and towering buildings that define the urban layout, while damage particles appear at click locations and gradually fade away over time. The visual technique uses spherical particles with alpha transparency to simulate temporary surface damage that persists briefly before disappearing, creating a sense of interaction and temporal decay within the city environment.
