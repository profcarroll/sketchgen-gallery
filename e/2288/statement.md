This sketch depicts the continuous, rhythmic growth of crystalline structures forming within deep subterranean mineral veins. The viewer observes geometric patterns slowly emerging and stabilizing in a dark, mineral-rich environment. Crystals begin as small formations, gradually expanding with organic motion before settling into stable, glowing geometric shapes. The constant pulsing rhythm mimics the natural flow of geological processes, while occasional resets simulate the continuous stress and release cycles that drive crystal formation. The blue-green color palette evokes the cool, mineral tones of deep earth formations, creating a sense of patient, eternal growth within an underground world.
