This sketch presents a cinematic journey through an abandoned Brutalist college campus that transforms from imposing concrete structures to a lush natural landscape. The viewer experiences a slow glide past massive walls that gradually crumble and dissolve, revealing patches of exposed rebar beneath creeping vines and moss. As the perspective continues, the entire scene becomes completely covered by a thick blanket of forest loam, symbolizing nature's quiet, persistent return to dominance. The work uses batched WebGL drawing techniques to efficiently render thousands of particles and structures while maintaining smooth animation.
