This sketch presents a mesmerizing visualization of concentric rings that continuously contract inward while gently pulsating. The rings are rendered with a shifting, iridescent color palette that moves smoothly through the hue spectrum, creating an effect reminiscent of metallic surfaces or light refracting through water. Each ring consists of multiple overlapping circles that subtly spiral and pulse in unison, forming a cohesive, hypnotic pattern that draws the eye toward the center. The motion is both rhythmic and organic, with each ring having its own unique timing and color characteristics that blend into a unified whole, creating an ever-shifting, self-contained universe within the canvas.
