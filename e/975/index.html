Brief

A row of brightly colored, simplified keys are displayed across the canvas. The keys remain stationary until the viewer interacts with them. When a key is pressed, it immediately produces a clear musical note and initiates a vibrant visual effect. This effect causes the key's initial glow to expand rapidly, pulsating with a secondary color that complements the primary color of the key. The combination of light and sound changes the state of the instrument every time a key is clicked.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a musical instrument composed of twelve colored keys that respond to user interaction. Each key, when pressed, produces a distinct musical tone and initiates a vibrant visual effect. The visual response manifests as a pulsing glow that expands from the center of the pressed key, using a color complementary to the key's primary hue. The interface remains static until a user gesture—specifically, a click on one of the keys—triggers both audio and visual responses, transforming the state of the instrument in real-time. This interaction creates a unified sensory experience where sound and sight are intrinsically linked to each key press.
