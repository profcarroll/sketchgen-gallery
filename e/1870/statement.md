This sketch presents a dynamic, three-dimensional model of our solar system rendered in real-time using p5.js's WEBGL mode. It features the Sun at the center, surrounded by orbiting planets scaled according to relative sizes and distances. Each celestial body moves along its own orbital path with distinct speeds and rotation rates, creating an accurate representation of planetary motion. The viewer can observe glowing orbital paths and dynamic trails that connect each planet as it orbits, emphasizing the continuous movement through space. Upon clicking, the system animates, showing the planets in motion against a starry background, allowing exploration of their relationships and trajectories in a visually engaging way.
