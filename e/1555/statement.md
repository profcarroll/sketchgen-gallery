This sketch creates a dynamic, sound-reactive field of neon-lit geometric forms using only octahedrons and hexagonal shapes. The composition operates in 3D space with constantly shifting positions, rotations, and colors that pulse in response to audio input. Shapes cascade through the viewing volume while intersecting and overlapping each other in complex patterns. The visual intensity increases with audio amplitude, creating a rhythmic field of light that fills the entire canvas. The piece uses p5.js WEBGL mode to achieve true three-dimensional movement and interaction between forms, with each shape's behavior modulated by real-time sound analysis through FFT and amplitude detection.
