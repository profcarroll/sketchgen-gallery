This sketch creates a three-dimensional simulation of a marble rolling through a sculptural funnel channel. The environment is rendered using WebGL for 3D graphics, with the marble's motion governed by simple physics including gravity and collision detection with the channel walls. The channel itself is a complex, curved path that narrows and widens as it progresses, punctuated by alternating ridges and troughs that force the marble to bounce and change direction unpredictably. The viewer observes the marble's journey through this dynamic landscape, seeing how its trajectory shifts dramatically when encountering the sculptural obstacles before settling back into the flow of the channel. The scene rotates slowly to show the full complexity of the three-dimensional space.
