This sketch creates an immersive passage through abstract atomic space, featuring luminous crystalline structures that pulse with internal green light. The visualization employs WEBGL rendering to create a three-dimensional environment where crystal formations move steadily deeper into the scene. Each crystal is composed of geometric components that pulse rhythmically, emitting cascading waveforms that ripple outward from their surfaces. The constant motion and glowing effects create a sense of deep, accelerating passage through molecular space, with the viewer experiencing a continuous flow of luminous energy patterns against an otherwise dark backdrop.
