This sketch creates an immersive, ethereal environment of constantly shifting crystalline structures that flow across the entire canvas. Each structure resembles a complex geometric bubble with interconnected glowing lines, pulsating with subtle color shifts and smooth motion. The animation uses a fixed number of 200 structures that continuously drift and rotate in an endless cycle, creating depth and visual interest without requiring any viewer interaction. The gentle, natural rhythm of movement evokes a sense of perpetual motion and delicate beauty, as if watching microscopic worlds unfold in slow motion.
