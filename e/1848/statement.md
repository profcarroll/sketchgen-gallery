This sketch presents an abstract tapestry composed of interlocking geometric modules arranged in a rigid grid pattern. Each module is a simple geometric shape—square, triangle, hexagon, or diamond—rotated at precise angles to form a crystalline, architectural whole. The modules are laid out in a structured, repeating pattern that emphasizes sharp, angular relationships and the interplay between defined forms and complementary color fields. The design focuses on structural rigidity and systematic repetition, creating a coherent visual structure through precise geometric alignment and consistent angular shifts across the canvas.
