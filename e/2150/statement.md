This sketch presents a static, macroscopic view of deep earth geology composed of stratified layers that evoke the weight and texture of ancient beeswax. The composition shows a vertical cross-section of sedimentary rock formations, each layer containing its own sub-strata with subtle variations in color and opacity. The viewer observes an accumulation of geological material that suggests immense time and pressure, rendered through careful attention to the visual properties of earth tones and layered structure. The work emphasizes the organic quality of geological formations while maintaining a sense of stillness and permanence.
