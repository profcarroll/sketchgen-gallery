This sketch presents a monumental steel pathway that begins as a sweeping, upward arc before making a sudden, dramatic perpendicular shift. The composition emphasizes the structural weight and engineered movement of the form through severe geometry and sharp transitions. The pathway curves dramatically into existence, then abruptly shifts direction, terminating in a multi-tiered platform composed of sharply delineated layers. The static, unchanging scene captures the permanence and gravity of this architectural transition, rendering the viewer as an observer within a vast, empty space confronting the imposing presence of the structure.
