This sketch creates a responsive grid of pulsating squares that react to user interaction. The background is nearly black, with each square gently breathing in a steady rhythm. When clicked, an expanding wave pulse radiates outward from the point of impact, disrupting nearby squares' natural pulsation patterns. Affected squares temporarily lose their consistent rhythm and display erratic behavior before smoothly returning to normal. The visual effect demonstrates both continuous motion and localized disruption, creating an engaging interplay between order and chaos across the canvas.
