This sketch creates an ethereal musical composition where notes drift gracefully across a floating sheet of music. The piece visualizes the continuous flow of melody through five parallel lines, with each note appearing on the left side and flying across to disappear on the right. The notes vary in size, speed, and opacity to create a sense of movement and depth, while maintaining a stable, serene composition that feels suspended in space. The asynchronous motion gives the illusion of an endless, unfolding musical narrative.
