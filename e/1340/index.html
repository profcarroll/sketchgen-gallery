Brief

A collage of overlapping, semi-transparent photographs drifts slowly across the canvas, creating a sense of fading memories. Soft, diffused motes of light, resembling gentle glowing particles, float through the composition. The images and the light motes move autonomously, creating a sense of depth and an ethereal, shifting atmosphere.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an ethereal collage of overlapping, semi-transparent photographs that drift slowly across the canvas, evoking a sense of fading memories. The images move autonomously with subtle bouncing and drifting motions, while thousands of soft, diffused light motes float gently through the composition. The visual effect suggests a dreamlike atmosphere where past moments linger and shift, creating an immersive experience of nostalgic depth and ambient movement.
