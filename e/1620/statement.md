This sketch creates an mesmerizing cascade of colorful rectangular blocks that spiral outward across the canvas. Each block falls with physics-based movement—affected by gravity, friction, and bouncing off walls—while rotating as it travels. The system continuously generates new blocks at random positions, varying in size and hue to create a constantly evolving tapestry. Blocks spiral in a large counter-clockwise pattern, never collapsing into the center, maintaining their cascading motion through a combination of initial velocity and angular momentum. The visual effect is sustained by a semi-transparent background that leaves trails, creating an ever-changing flow of color and movement.
