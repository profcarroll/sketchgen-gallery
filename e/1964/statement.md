This sketch depicts a subterranean geological cross-section showing sedimentary rock layers and an underground river system. The composition captures the essence of ancient stone formations rendered in the precise, fine-line style of a vintage pen plotter drawing. The scene features horizontal strata with subtle textural details, representing thousands of years of layered sedimentation. Within this rigid geological framework flows a network of delicate, dynamic lines that suggest an underground current — not as a violent torrent but as a persistent, barely perceptible flow that seems poised to burst through the porous rock matrix. The technique uses minimal, precise line work with subtle animation to create the impression of constant, gentle motion beneath the surface.
