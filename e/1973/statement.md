This visualization depicts the grand, sweeping patterns of oceanic energy differentials as seen from orbit. The sketch creates flowing currents across the canvas using layered sine waves that move at different speeds and amplitudes, representing the massive forces shaping Earth's water systems. These currents pulse and reshape themselves slowly, reflecting how global wind patterns continuously influence ocean movement. The technique uses multiple overlapping waveforms with varying frequencies and phases to create a dynamic, organic flow that evolves without user interaction, demonstrating the continuous, cyclical nature of planetary energy systems.
