This sketch depicts an evolving, organic network of crystalline formations that pulse and grow in rhythmic synchronization. The visuals evoke the slow, self-organizing emergence of fungal or mineral structures—like a petrified rainforest slowly taking shape. Through a combination of geometric branching and particle flocking mechanics, it creates intricate, looping patterns that radiate from central cores outward. The entire composition pulses with a subtle rhythm, giving the impression of life slowly being sculpted into permanence. The viewer witnesses an endless, mesmerizing dance of form and motion, where each structure grows, evolves, and eventually dissolves in an eternal cycle.
