This sketch creates an immersive underwater visualization that emerges from complete darkness. The scene begins as a void, with no movement or change, waiting for a single click to activate. Upon activation, deep blue and cool green spirals burst forth from the darkness, rapidly growing in complexity and weaving intricate patterns through three-dimensional space. Each spiral starts subtly as a simple arc and evolves into dense, interconnected geometry that simulates bioluminescent phenomena in the deep ocean. The visualization uses WebGL rendering to create a volumetric effect, with glowing points and continuous lines forming an organic, self-assembling pattern. The entire display is controlled by a single user gesture, making it both mysterious and responsive - the viewer's action triggers the emergence of the spectacular aquatic light show from absolute darkness.
