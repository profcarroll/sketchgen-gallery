This sketch visualizes the mental process of solving a crossword puzzle. It begins with a faint grid structure representing empty cells, overlaid with ghostly glowing paths suggesting potential word placements. The entire composition pulses gently with an inner light, hinting at the cognitive activity that precedes solidification of ideas. When clicked at the center of the canvas — the single gesture available to the viewer — the grid reacts by locking in a section of the puzzle, causing the connecting paths to brighten and solid blocks to appear. The interaction transforms abstract possibilities into defined structure, capturing how thought becomes concrete through deliberate action. The work emphasizes both the fluidity of mental exploration and the clarity that emerges from focused decision-making.
