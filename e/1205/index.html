Brief

The canvas presents an abstract composition made of several overlapping planes of deep, rich, and highly saturated color. These color fields are softly defined, giving them a diffused, atmospheric quality. Across the entire surface, horizontal wave formations of color subtly flow, shifting the chroma of the planes. This movement is rhythmic and consistent, manifesting as a steady, uniform wave pattern that washes across the composition from side to side.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an abstract composition of overlapping color planes that flow with rhythmic horizontal waves. Each plane contains a different saturated color and moves at its own pace, creating a layered atmospheric effect. The wave patterns shift smoothly across the canvas from side to side, with chroma variations that pulse gently through time. The result is a dynamic, ever-shifting color field where depth and motion emerge from simple geometric repetition and subtle color modulation.
