This sketch renders a steampunk-inspired sculpture composed of a central rotating core surrounded by orbiting emerald glass spheres. The piece uses deep teal and emerald hues with oxidized copper accents to create a rich, mechanical aesthetic. Multiple bioluminescent effects are implemented through layered glowing spheres with varying opacities and pulsating internal lights. The entire composition rotates slowly while individual elements pulse with internal light movement, creating a mesmerizing, constantly shifting tableau that embodies the requested interplay between deep material tones and vibrant inner luminescence.
