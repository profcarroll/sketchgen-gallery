Brief

The canvas displays a breathtaking, multi-component steampunk sculpture rendered in deep emerald glass and tarnished copper. Multiple elaborate globe splines orbit a central, rotating structure. The core visual element is the deep emerald glass tracery, which houses complex, shifting bioluminescent patterns that glow from deep within the material. The overall piece appears highly mechanical and glowing, displaying rich tones of oxidized copper contrasting sharply with the vibrant, internal teal and green luminescence. The entire apparatus rotates and pulses with internal light movement, creating a constantly shifting, mesmerizing tableau.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch renders a steampunk-inspired sculpture composed of a central rotating core surrounded by orbiting emerald glass spheres. The piece uses deep teal and emerald hues with oxidized copper accents to create a rich, mechanical aesthetic. Multiple bioluminescent effects are implemented through layered glowing spheres with varying opacities and pulsating internal lights. The entire composition rotates slowly while individual elements pulse with internal light movement, creating a mesmerizing, constantly shifting tableau that embodies the requested interplay between deep material tones and vibrant inner luminescence.
