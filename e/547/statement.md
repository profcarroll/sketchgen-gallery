This sketch simulates an evolving ecosystem of amorphous, glowing blobs that spread and connect across a 3D space. The blobs—represented as spheres with dynamic movement and color—slowly drift and interact with each other through delicate filamentary connections. The scene uses WebGL rendering for depth perception and subtle animation, creating a mesmerizing effect reminiscent of slime mold or biological growth patterns. Each blob moves independently within physical constraints, and the connections between them dynamically form based on proximity, forming an ever-shifting network across the canvas.
