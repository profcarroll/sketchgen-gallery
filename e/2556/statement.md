This sketch renders an intricate, symmetrical textile pattern reminiscent of a handwoven Persian rug. The composition features a dense lattice grid of stylized floral and geometric motifs in rich jewel tones, arranged with mathematical precision and perfect repetition. The central field is framed by a continuous border composed entirely of repeating crescent shapes that create a sense of layered depth and architectural structure. Through careful attention to color palette, symmetry, and pattern interweaving, the work captures the essence of traditional fiber art while maintaining a static, contemplative quality that invites quiet observation.
