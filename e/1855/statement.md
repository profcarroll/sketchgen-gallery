This sketch creates a mesmerizing, continuously evolving field of interconnected three-dimensional geometric structures. Volumetric planes rotate and orbit in harmony, creating a sense of synchronized motion that occasionally converges into singular orbital patterns. The glowing nodes at intersection points pulse with subtle intensity variations, enhanced by a periodic flicker effect that adds depth to the spatial relationships. Thin connecting lines between planes pulse visibly when close together, emphasizing the dynamic geometry. Two text blocks drift slowly across the screen as network branding, maintaining visual interest while preserving the immersive quality of the constantly shifting structure. The result is an otherworldly, hypnotic visualization that feels both mechanical and organic in its movement.
