Brief

The canvas displays an intricate, ethereal lattice constructed from delicate, glowing lines. These bright lines form a vast network of connectivity, but occasionally, momentary gaps appear, breaking the continuous flow of light. The visual impact is defined by a powerful contrast: the vibrant luminescence of the glowing threads sharply contrasts against a surrounding, deep darkness. The structure appears to shift and subtly evolve over time, creating the illusion of a delicate, living web of light and shadow that continuously changes.
