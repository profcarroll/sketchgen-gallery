This sketch creates an ever-shifting lattice of glowing lines that pulse and evolve over time. The network appears to breathe with life as individual threads subtly move and occasionally break their connections, forming temporary gaps in the web. The stark contrast between the bright, luminous threads and deep black background emphasizes the ethereal quality of the structure, while the continuous animation gives it a sense of organic motion—like a living web of light and shadow that's always in flux.
