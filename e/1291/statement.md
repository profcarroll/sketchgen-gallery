This sketch creates a mesmerizing pattern of nested, circular rings that pulse with rhythmic harmonic waves. Each ring expands and contracts in perfect synchronization, creating a symmetrical, hypnotic effect across the canvas. The rings are colored exclusively in deep purples and magentas, shifting subtly through hue over time to create depth and movement. The animation is entirely self-contained, requiring no user interaction to be visible, and runs continuously with a steady rhythm that draws the viewer's eye into the center of the composition where the rings appear to breathe and flow together in an endless cycle.
