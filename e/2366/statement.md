This sketch creates an immersive, meditative nebula of bioluminescent gas using a combination of organic particle motion and spatially-aware connections. The scene features deep blues and vivid purples that evoke the vastness of space, with glowing particles that drift slowly and rhythmically through three-dimensional space. Each particle pulses with its own internal rhythm, creating ripples that propagate along trailing paths, suggesting the cyclical life forces within stellar nurseries. The connections between nearby particles form a web-like structure that reinforces the sense of cosmic interconnectivity. By using spatial hashing instead of all-pairs distance calculations, the sketch maintains performance while creating an organic, flowing network that evolves over time without becoming chaotic or visually overwhelming.
