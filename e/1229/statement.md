This sketch creates a dynamic, layered composition of photographic fragments that drift and rotate slowly across the canvas. Each fragment is a randomly generated grayscale texture with organic shapes and patterns. The elements move independently with subtle velocity and rotation, creating an immersive, flowing visual experience. Clicking anywhere triggers a sudden, randomized rearrangement of all fragments, while dragging allows users to pull specific fragments across the screen. The continuous motion and interactive response create a sense of fluidity and control, where the viewer can influence the composition without disrupting its underlying rhythm.
