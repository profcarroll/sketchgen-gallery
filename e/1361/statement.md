This sketch depicts a boiling water simulation inside a transparent glass vessel using p5.js's WEBGL renderer. The scene shows bubbles rising from the bottom of the container, growing as they ascend, and popping at the surface. The water surface is rendered with subtle distortion effects that simulate light refraction, while the glass container appears semi-transparent. The animation runs autonomously without user input, demonstrating turbulent motion through physics-based particle movement and continuous bubble generation. The visual effect creates an immersive representation of boiling water's dynamic behavior, emphasizing the constant churning motion and the interaction between light and liquid.
