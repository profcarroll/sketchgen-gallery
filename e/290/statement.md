This sketch creates a dense, animated network of glowing data streams flowing through interconnected cables. The visualization uses a combination of geometric cables with streaming particles and central junctions that pulse with energy and split into multiple streams. The system operates entirely autonomously with no user input required - streams move continuously along cables, junctions pulse rhythmically, and new streams are generated at random intervals. The visual effect creates an organic, living network that constantly evolves while maintaining a structured, geometric framework of connections.
