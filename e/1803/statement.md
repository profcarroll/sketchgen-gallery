This sketch renders an abstract, high-altitude view of a sprawling metropolis at night, where luminous energy flows through the urban environment as a network of dynamic light trails. These streaks of color intersect and form intricate geometric lattice structures that periodically stabilize into rigid crystalline forms. The visualization captures both the continuous flow of urban life and sudden moments of ordered structure, creating a sense of tension between chaos and architecture. The scene evolves organically without external input, driven purely by the motion of particles and their interactions within a spatial grid system.
