Brief

A continuous, visible trail of deep, high-contrast ink is laid down across the canvas whenever the cursor moves. This ink does not simply fade; instead, it accumulates and organizes into intricate, geometric, crystalline structures along the path of travel. These formations are governed by the speed of the cursor, and when the user moves quickly, the resulting crystalline edges flash with a momentary, shimmering metallic sheen. The entire system is reactive, with the drawing and transformation of the persistent ink structures directly controlled by the movement of the input cursor.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a reactive drawing system where cursor movement leaves behind persistent ink trails that organize into crystalline structures. The ink accumulates along paths and forms geometric patterns based on motion speed. When the cursor moves quickly, the resulting edges flash with metallic shimmer effects. The system uses spatial hashing to manage particle interactions and builds complex formations without overwhelming performance constraints.
