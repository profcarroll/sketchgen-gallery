This sketch creates an immersive field of softly glowing geometric shapes that subtly animate in three-dimensional space. The scene features a dynamic interplay between ambient, muted glows and vibrant bursts of color that respond to user interaction. When users click or drag across the canvas, localized ripples emanate from the cursor position, temporarily altering the hue and complexity of surrounding shapes before fading back into the background field. The visual effect achieves a sense of organic flow and responsiveness through carefully controlled particle systems and geometric transformations, creating an engaging yet meditative experience where the viewer's actions directly influence the evolving visual landscape.
