This sketch simulates the physical stress of a vintage paper sheet under continuous typing. As text appears character by character, each printed letter causes visible warping and fiber fraying around its position. The effect is achieved through particle systems that simulate paper fibers peeling away from freshly typed characters, combined with subtle warping distortion applied to each character. The simulation uses a texture background to represent aged paper, and fracture patterns radiate outward from each new character, mimicking how physical stress accumulates over time on paper surfaces. The result is a continuous visual representation of paper aging under the mechanical pressure of text generation.
