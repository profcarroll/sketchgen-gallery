This sketch presents a dynamic field of interconnected, morphing geometric forms that shift through abrupt color transitions while flowing continuously across the canvas. The visual system creates an ever-changing organic network where each form pulses and drifts independently, occasionally bursting into energetic expansion before settling back into fluid motion. Connections between nearby forms create a web-like structure that pulses with the same dramatic color shifts, emphasizing the continuous transformation of both individual elements and their collective relationships. The result is a self-contained, evolving visual field that demonstrates both individual form dynamics and emergent network behavior through its autonomous motion and color modulation.
