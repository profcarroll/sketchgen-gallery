This sketch simulates the slow accumulation of geological strata through time, presenting a vertical cross-section where sepia-toned photographs represent layers of sediment. The composition includes translucent photo fragments arranged in overlapping bands that move slowly across the canvas, mimicking the gradual buildup of memory over eons. Interspersed among these sediments are crystals that grow gradually from random points, each with its own shimmering surface effect. Fine mineral particles drift slowly downward through the scene, adding texture and movement to the otherwise still layers. The muted ochre, dusty umber, and pale amber color palette evokes the warmth of ancient geological formations, while the continuous animation suggests an endless process of accumulation and change.
