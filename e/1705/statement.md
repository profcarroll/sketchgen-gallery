This sketch depicts an evolving ecosystem of self-replicating cellular structures that grow, move, and divide autonomously. Each cell pulses with internal rhythm before initiating a complex division process, where the narrowing connection point visibly constricts and pulses with intense light just prior to separation. The visualization captures the cyclical nature of cellular replication through continuous motion, growth, and fragmentation, creating an organic field of expanding forms that fill the canvas space over time.
