This sketch depicts a mechanical lattice of onyx rods arranged in a grid, each rod composed of multiple connected segments that bend rhythmically under the influence of taut springs. The rods exhibit continuous, subtle motion as they strain against spring tension before snapping back into precise alignment. The dark, polished surfaces of the rods create a heavy, mechanical pulse of activity across the canvas, with each segment subtly varying in color and angle to simulate the stress and recovery cycle of the structured grid. The visual effect captures the essence of tension and release through carefully calculated motion and spring physics.
