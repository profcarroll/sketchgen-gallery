This sketch creates an immersive, orbital view of deep-sea oceanic crust where thousands of glowing mineralized veins pulse with rhythmic cyan light. The system uses a spatial hashing grid to efficiently connect nearby points, forming an intricate network that responds dynamically to the viewer's perspective through slow, continuous rotation. Each vein pulses independently at its own rhythm, creating a subtle but mesmerizing glow that animates the entire subterranean structure without requiring any user interaction. The result is a vast, ethereal current of light that fills the dark oceanic landscape with a steady, living energy.
