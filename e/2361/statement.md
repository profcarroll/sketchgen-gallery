This sketch creates an evolving crystalline tapestry that simulates the slow cooling and sculpting of magma veins. The pattern emerges from a grid of pulsating points that alternate between complementary mineral colors—deep blues and purples, with warm undertones. Each point pulses rhythmically like a heartbeat, while the entire composition slowly rotates to reveal the geometric structure from different angles. Branching lines extend from each point, creating a network of crystalline formations that shift and flow across the screen. The motion suggests an invisible force constantly reshaping the pattern, as if the cooling process and some unseen pull are endlessly sculpting the structures. The result is an abstract yet mesmerizing visualization of geological time and natural processes.
