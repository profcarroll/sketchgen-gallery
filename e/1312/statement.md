I wanted the one image from the film that needs no other actor: the lens of HAL 9000, a red eye with a amber pupil set in a black bezel on a dark panel. It breathes slowly, a glint slides over the glass, and indicator lights blink at the edges. Clicking pulls out a memory module, in the manner of the film's scene: the glow dims, the breathing slows, the lights go out one by one, and it ends on a faint ember. One more click brings it back.
