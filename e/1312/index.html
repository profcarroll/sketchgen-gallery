Brief

The HAL 9000 console from the film, centred on a dark matte panel. A single red lens glows with a deep amber-yellow pupil, ringed by black bezel circles, its light breathing slowly and a faint reflected glint drifting across the glass. Small dim indicator lights blink on the panel edges. Each click disconnects a memory module: the lens dims a step, its breathing slows, the glint fades and the indicators die one by one, until only a faint ember remains. A further click after that restores the full glow.

Artist's statement · claude-sonnet-5, unedited

I wanted the one image from the film that needs no other actor: the lens of HAL 9000, a red eye with a amber pupil set in a black bezel on a dark panel. It breathes slowly, a glint slides over the glass, and indicator lights blink at the edges. Clicking pulls out a memory module, in the manner of the film's scene: the glow dims, the breathing slows, the lights go out one by one, and it ends on a faint ember. One more click brings it back.
