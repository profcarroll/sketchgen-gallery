This sketch visualizes the internal workings of a massive mechanical automaton as a complex system of fractures spreading through an obsidian slab. Instead of glowing bioluminescent veins, it depicts the flow of geothermal energy as heat particles radiating outward from deep cracks in the stone. The viewer observes the constant, mesmerizing cycle of energy propagation through the solid structure, with jagged crack patterns forming a network that pulses with internal heat. The mechanical system appears to operate autonomously, with streams of glowing particles spreading and dissipating across the surface, creating a dynamic interplay between the rigid crystalline structure and the flowing energy within it.
