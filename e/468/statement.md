This sketch creates a dynamic geometric network of interconnected fragments that respond to user interaction. The system begins in a stable, crystalline arrangement where fragments gently oscillate in place while maintaining connections to nearby neighbors. When clicked, the entire structure violently shatters into chaotic motion, with fragments scattering outward in sharp, luminous bursts. After reaching peak chaos, the fragments gradually decelerate and begin reorganizing themselves back toward a new equilibrium state. The visual effect captures the tension between order and disorder, showing how disruption can lead to reformation while maintaining the underlying structural principles that govern the system's behavior.
