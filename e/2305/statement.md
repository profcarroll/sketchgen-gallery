This sketch depicts a vast geological cross-section of Earth's history, rendered as flowing layers of mineral deposits and eroded rock strata. Through the slow, autonomous motion of these layers—simulating uplift and decay—viewers witness an abstracted yet vivid representation of immense geologic time. The composition uses deeply saturated colors and subtle animations to evoke the feeling of a canyon wall in constant, slow transformation. The viewer observes a continuous, mesmerizing interplay of color, form, and motion that suggests both the violence and patience inherent in natural processes.
