This sketch creates an intricate kaleidoscopic pattern within a hexagonal boundary using the interplay between fluid particles and rigid geometric lattices. Particles move smoothly with trails that fade over time, while crystalline lattices expand and rotate, creating tension lines that connect them. The force fields between particles radiate outward, building visible stress as they approach each other. The continuous motion generates a dynamic tension that builds toward a controlled fracturing effect across the entire canvas surface, all contained within the defined hexagonal space.
