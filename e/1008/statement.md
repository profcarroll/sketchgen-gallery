This sketch creates a serene water surface that responds dynamically to sound input. Under normal conditions, gentle ripples flow across the canvas in a calming, continuous motion. When sound is detected—triggered by a 440 Hz tone in the test—the ripples intensify, fragment, and brighten into a sparkling effect that visually represents the increase in audio energy. The interaction is entirely self-contained: it begins on user click to activate audio, then reacts to simulated sound input without any external controls.
