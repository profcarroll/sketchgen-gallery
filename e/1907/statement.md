This sketch depicts a desolate, mineralizing landscape where whimsical paper-cut ships sail across a translucent medium. As they progress, these vessels leave behind glowing wakes that gradually solidify into crystalline salt deposits and mineral streaks. The accumulated detritus from destroyed ships piles up along the edges of the canvas, forming drifts that eventually bury entire scenes in mountains of fragile, encrusted material. The scene evolves slowly over time, with the sediment trails growing thicker and more substantial, ultimately overwhelming the original vessels. The viewer witnesses an endless cycle of creation and decay, where ephemeral craft becomes permanent geological formations through a process of gradual solidification and accumulation.
