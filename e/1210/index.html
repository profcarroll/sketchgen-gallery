Brief

The canvas displays a large, nearly complete landscape image made of jigsaw puzzle pieces. Most pieces are already interlocked to show a detailed natural scene, but several loose pieces remain at the edges. A viewer can click and drag these loose pieces to move them around the canvas.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a jigsaw puzzle landscape scene where most pieces are already interlocked to form a natural scene with grass, paths, and trees. Several loose puzzle pieces remain at the edges of the canvas that viewers can click and drag around. The implementation uses a grid-based approach to arrange the majority of puzzle pieces while randomly placing a selection of pieces to represent loose items. The visualization features a simplified landscape using basic shapes and colors to depict grass, dirt paths, and trees. The interactive elements allow users to manipulate the loose pieces by clicking and dragging them across the canvas.
