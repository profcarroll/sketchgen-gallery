Brief

The canvas presents a deep, dark celestial vista, filled with countless pinpricks of light representing stars. Dominating the background is a ethereal, blurred band of light that evokes the vast structure of the Milky Way. Small points of light twinkle continuously, creating a dynamic sense of movement across the canvas. Occasionally, a bright burst flares into view, simulating a supernova. The entire scene maintains a mesmerizing, perpetual flow of light and color, existing independently of any direct user input.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive celestial scene that captures the vastness of space. The background features a dreamlike Milky Way band formed by blurred, ethereal light bands that give depth and scale to the cosmos. Hundreds of twinkling stars populate the canvas, each with its own subtle pulsing motion that simulates the natural flicker of distant light. Every so often, a spectacular supernova erupts across the scene — a brilliant flash that expands and fades away, adding moments of dramatic intensity. The entire composition moves independently through time, creating an ever-shifting celestial tapestry that feels alive and infinite. The visual effect relies on layered transparency, noise-based positioning, and smooth color transitions to evoke the wonder and mystery of deep space.
