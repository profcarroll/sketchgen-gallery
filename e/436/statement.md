This sketch creates an immersive cosmic environment using particle physics and noise-based movement to simulate fluid dynamics in space. Particles drift slowly across the dark canvas, leaving luminous trails that interact with each other through a subtle flow field system. The scene features soft glowing lights with varying hues from deep blues to purples, creating a mesmerizing effect as particles move and trails fade over time. Occasional flares appear randomly to add visual interest and depth, mimicking the appearance of distant stars or cosmic phenomena. The entire composition evolves organically without user input, maintaining a hypnotic quality that captures the essence of a tranquil yet dynamic cosmic space.
