This sketch depicts a physics-driven marble rolling through a funnel-shaped channel and descending a tilted ramp. The scene is rendered using WebGL for realistic 3D perspective, with the camera dynamically following the marble's motion. The marble interacts with the environment through collision detection against the conical walls of the channel and the sloped ramp surface, demonstrating gravity, momentum, and bounce physics. The visual design uses contrasting colors to distinguish the polished marble from its surrounding container, creating a clear focal point as it moves through the defined space.
