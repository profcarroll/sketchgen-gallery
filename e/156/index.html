Brief

The viewer stands deep within an impossible, monumental cityscape defined by intersecting geometric planes and receding perspectives. The color palette is limited to aged concrete, oxidized steel, and subtle washes of atmospheric cyan, emphasizing the sheer scale and speculative nature of the environment. Towering, brutalist structures seem to unfold into an endless void, guided by repeating, interlocking geometries. As the view quietly shifts, the overlapping massive forms and recessed walkways undergo slow, perpetual reformation, giving the impression of a perpetually unfolding, colossal architectural dream state.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive, ever-shifting architectural landscape using WebGL rendering in p5.js. The composition features multiple intersecting geometric planes that slowly reconfigure themselves in a perpetual state of transformation, evoking the sense of a massive, brutalist cityscape unfolding into infinite space. The limited color palette of aged concrete and oxidized steel tones, accented with atmospheric cyan washes, emphasizes the monumental scale and speculative nature of the environment. Through subtle camera movement and continuous geometric transformations, viewers experience an architectural dream state where towering structures seem to breathe and shift, suggesting a living, evolving urban cosmos. The motion is entirely procedural and self-contained, creating a mesmerizing visual rhythm that unfolds without external input.
