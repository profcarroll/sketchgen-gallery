This sketch renders a dynamic urban environment using p5.js in WEBGL mode. The scene features a sprawling cityscape composed of randomly generated buildings and moving vehicles, all rendered with simulated lighting and depth. The viewer can explore the environment by dragging the mouse to shift the camera perspective, creating an immersive, interactive experience. The animation includes subtle movements like passing cars and atmospheric lighting that give the illusion of continuous urban life. The implementation adheres to performance constraints while maintaining visual complexity and responsiveness to user input.
