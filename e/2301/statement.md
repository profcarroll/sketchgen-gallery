This sketch creates an immersive underwater environment that evokes the feeling of a sonar scan through an ancient, petrified forest. The scene features thousands of luminous particles moving in fluid currents, punctuated by subtle fault lines that undulate across the canvas. When a viewer drags their cursor, a glowing tether extends from the mouse position, attaching to a stylized kite shape. The kite's movement resists the pull of this invisible line, causing dynamic tension that alters the surrounding currents. The color palette shifts between deep blues, greens, and violets to emphasize the aquatic depth, while the fault lines create a sense of geological history beneath the surface. The interaction reveals how a guiding force can influence chaotic, flowing systems, showing the relationship between controlled movement and natural fluid dynamics in this submerged landscape.
