This sketch depicts a vast cosmic expanse filled with countless stars and the diffuse glow of the Milky Way. The scene features a slowly shifting nebula background that creates a sense of depth and motion through time. Throughout the animation, occasional supernovae erupt with brilliant white cores that rapidly expand and fade away, creating dramatic bursts of light against the dark cosmic void. The effect is achieved by continuously generating new explosion events with carefully controlled fading animations, while maintaining the overall starfield pulsation to suggest the vast scale and timeless nature of space.
