This sketch depicts a weathered brick wall undergoing slow, organic decay through the intrusion of root tendrils and gravitational forces. The scene uses WebGL rendering to create depth and scale, with bricks rendered as textured 3D boxes that gradually lose structural integrity over time. Dark brown root systems force their way into mortar joints, creating fissures that lead to partial collapse. The simulation shows vertical stacks of bricks beginning to dislodge and fall downward, accelerating into a dark void beneath, while particles simulate debris and dust from the crumbling structure. The entire scene is in constant motion, with no external input required – it evolves autonomously through time-based animation.
