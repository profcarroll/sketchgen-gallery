This sketch depicts a mesmerizing deep-sea environment where geometric pyramidal structures rise from the abyssal floor. The formations are crystalline and translucent, emitting luminescent residue particles that drift and accumulate under the influence of an unseen deep-sea current. Through careful use of WEBGL rendering, I've created a sense of depth with pyramids that ascend from the dark ocean floor, their surfaces rendered with subtle lighting effects. The residue particles form streams and trails that flow across the scene, creating a constant motion of bioluminescence against the black backdrop. The viewer witnesses a choreographed dance of light and structure, where the current's invisible force organizes the shed material into visible patterns of glowing motion. The scene captures both the stark beauty and dynamic energy of deep-sea environments through carefully controlled particle systems and geometric forms.
