This sketch renders an immersive cosmic landscape where thousands of twinkling stars form a deep, star-filled expanse. The background features a slowly shifting nebula in deep blues and indigos, evoking the vastness of space. Occasional supernovas flash across the canvas with brilliant white cores that rapidly expand and fade, adding dramatic bursts of light to the otherwise serene scene. The entire composition evolves continuously without any user interaction, creating an ever-changing celestial vista that captures both the tranquility and the occasional spectacular drama of the universe.
