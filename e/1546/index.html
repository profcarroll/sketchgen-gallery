Brief

The canvas displays a deep, cosmic expanse featuring countless pinpricks of light forming a starscape, overlaid by the blurred, ethereal glow of the Milky Way. The overall mood is deep blue and indigo, punctuated by the occasional supernovas. These luminous events appear as brilliant white cores that flash rapidly and quickly fade back into the cosmic background. The stars continuously twinkle, and the deep void itself appears to subtly drift and undulate, creating a constantly evolving celestial vista that requires no interaction to observe.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch renders an immersive cosmic landscape where thousands of twinkling stars form a deep, star-filled expanse. The background features a slowly shifting nebula in deep blues and indigos, evoking the vastness of space. Occasional supernovas flash across the canvas with brilliant white cores that rapidly expand and fade, adding dramatic bursts of light to the otherwise serene scene. The entire composition evolves continuously without any user interaction, creating an ever-changing celestial vista that captures both the tranquility and the occasional spectacular drama of the universe.
