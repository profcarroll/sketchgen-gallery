This sketch creates a serene, cosmic environment where translucent circles gently flow across the canvas in slow, continuous motion. Embedded within this fluid backdrop are countless tiny stars that drift through space, each leaving a delicate luminous trail behind them. The composition is filled with subtle, organic movements and soft color transitions that evoke the feeling of floating through deep space. Occasional ripples emerge from random points across the canvas, spreading outward before fading back into the cosmic flow, adding moments of gentle disturbance to the otherwise tranquil scene. The entire piece is a meditation on the slow, eternal dance of celestial bodies in the vastness of space.
