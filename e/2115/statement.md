This sketch creates a subterranean lava flow visualization using thousands of glowing particles that pulse and breathe in rhythmic waves. The central molten rock glows with intense reds and oranges, expanding and contracting slowly to simulate the natural rhythm of volcanic activity. The scene depicts a dark underground environment where the glowing magma dominates the composition, creating an immersive representation of subterranean fire. The technique uses particle systems with sine wave-based pulsation to create organic, breathing movements that feel alive and dynamic without overwhelming the viewer with excessive detail or computational complexity.
