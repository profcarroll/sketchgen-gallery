This sketch renders a highly intricate, symmetrical textile pattern inspired by traditional Persian rugs. Using jewel tones and a visible lattice grid structure, it creates an illusion of continuous, interwoven vine patterns rather than discrete motifs. The composition balances complexity with harmony through careful repetition and geometric relationships, resulting in a fixed, decorative piece that suggests both depth and order. The visual effect emerges from the interaction of layered shapes and symmetrical axes, evoking the rich, detailed craftsmanship of classical Persian textile design.
