This sketch depicts an evolving garden ecosystem where central pods act as seed containers, filling with luminous particles that gradually disperse outward. The composition uses vibrant saturated colors in HSB mode to create a dreamlike, organic atmosphere. Pods grow and fill with seeds over time, then release them in flowing motion patterns that drift away from their origins. The visual rhythm captures the cyclical nature of life and dispersal through color, motion, and form relationships. Each frame shows continuous transformation as particles accumulate and scatter within an abstract green landscape.
