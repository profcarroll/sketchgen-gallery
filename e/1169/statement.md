This sketch creates a mesmerizing, ever-evolving abstract field contained within a hexagonal boundary. The visualization combines two dynamic systems: a flowing vector field that generates intricate patterns and pulsing tension points that emit force fields. Particles move through the field, influenced by both the underlying flow and the repulsive forces from tension points. As these tension points grow and fade, they continuously reshape the particle paths, creating a sense of systemic stress and expansion. The hypnotic interplay between the smooth flow patterns and the sharp, pulsing tension lines produces an intense, vibrant energy field that feels both contained and dynamically expanding.
