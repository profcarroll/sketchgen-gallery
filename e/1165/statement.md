This sketch creates a dynamic visual transformation from chaotic particle motion to an organized crystalline lattice. Initially, thousands of colorful particles move randomly and collide, creating a turbulent data flow. Over time, these particles begin to align into a stable geometric structure, forming a pulsing crystal lattice that breathes with rhythmic energy. The piece explores the transition from disorder to order, using particle physics simulations and harmonic pulse effects to visualize this process. The visual system responds to audio input through p5's FFT analysis, allowing the environment to react to sound frequencies.
