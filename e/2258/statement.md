This sketch visualizes the subterranean forces of tectonic plate collision through dynamic light currents that pulse and shift in response to oscillating pressures. The particles represent compressed material deep underground, moving in rhythmic patterns that echo the slow buildup and release of immense geological forces. The central compression force simulates the tension between colliding plates, while noise-based motion adds organic unpredictability to the flow. Connections between nearby particles form a web-like structure that continuously reconfigures, representing the dynamic stress fields that characterize deep earth processes.
