This sketch creates a meditative, color-field composition inspired by Mark Rothko's monumental paintings. Through five large, overlapping planes that pulse with subtle color shifts and gentle movements, the work embodies Rothko's exploration of pure color and emotion. The colors transition smoothly through a carefully chosen palette, shifting in intensity and saturation over time without any user interaction. The planes breathe and subtly shift position, creating an organic rhythm that mimics life itself. This continuous, autonomous animation maintains the viewer's attention through its patient, hypnotic quality, where the visual experience emerges from the interplay of color, form, and time rather than any dramatic gesture or movement.
