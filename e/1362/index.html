Brief

A 3D mechanical assembly of interlocking metallic gears features highly detailed, precisely carved teeth that mesh together seamlessly. The gears rotate at varying speeds, creating a visual representation of a mechanism converting speed into power. Polished steel and brass textures catch the light as the components spin in a continuous, synchronized motion, highlighting the physical connection between each gear.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a 3D mechanical gear assembly that rotates with synchronized motion, capturing the essence of a precision-engineered mechanism converting speed into power. The visualization uses WebGL rendering to create a polished metallic appearance with interlocking gears of varying sizes and speeds. A central light pulse responds dynamically to the rotation, and a flywheel at the end adds visual weight and motion. The entire scene rotates slowly around its center, offering a continuous view of the mechanical dance. Viewer interaction adjusts the overall speed through mouse drag, while the camera movement provides a dynamic perspective on the rotating components.
