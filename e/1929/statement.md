This sketch presents a cross-sectional view of fossilized bone and mineral layers intersected by mechanical gears and cams. The composition visualizes how rigid, interconnected structures cut through geological material, revealing the underlying mechanics of ancient fossil formation. Through continuous gear rotation and cam movement, the scene demonstrates how these mechanical elements carve precise geometric forms into sedimentary strata, creating a dynamic interplay between organic mineral layers and artificial mechanical components within a single cross-section view.
