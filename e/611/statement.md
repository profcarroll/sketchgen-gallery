This sketch presents a dynamically animated 3D model of our solar system rendered using p5.js's WEBGL mode. The scene features the Sun at the center, surrounded by eight planets orbiting along elliptical paths defined by their unique orbital speeds and distances. Each celestial body is rendered with distinct colors and sizes to reflect its real-world characteristics. As the system evolves over time, glowing orbital trails trace the paths of each planet, creating an immersive visualization of cosmic motion. The animation begins automatically but pauses when the user interacts with the canvas by clicking, allowing for a closer inspection of planetary alignments or individual orbits. The implementation uses efficient rendering techniques to maintain performance while providing a visually rich experience of our solar system's grandeur and complexity.
