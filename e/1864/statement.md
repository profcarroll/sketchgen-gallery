This sketch creates a dynamic tide pool cross-section where vibrant marine life is immersed in a flowing current. The scene features anemones gently swaying with organic motion, rocks forming the seabed, and sand at the base. Suspended particles flow outward from unseen sources, creating visible trails that move with the current's direction. These particles follow a perlin noise-based flow field, which generates a natural-looking propagation pattern across the entire water surface. The visual effect emphasizes both the stillness of the tide pool environment and the continuous motion of the underwater currents.
