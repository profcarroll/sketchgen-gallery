This sketch renders a deep-sea mineral formation composed of layered, interconnected geometric forms that evoke the complexity and depth of hydrothermal vents. The work consists of numerous interlocking clusters—each containing curved bands and structural elements—that appear to grow from the ocean floor. Using WebGL rendering, it creates a three-dimensional space where viewers can orbit around the scene, discovering the intricate cross-sections of each cluster. The visual design draws upon geological patterns, with organic shapes punctuated by sharp edges and subtle highlights that suggest mineral deposits. When the viewer drags across the canvas, the clusters subtly rearrange their positions, revealing new geometric relationships within the network of overlapping forms. This interaction allows for a dynamic exploration of the formation's internal structure, inviting contemplation on the slow, subterranean processes that create such natural wonders.
