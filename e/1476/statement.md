This sketch creates a mesmerizing night scene populated by hundreds of glowing fireflies that drift gently across the screen. The fireflies flicker with natural randomness and subtly synchronize their movements in periodic bursts, creating rhythmic visual patterns. At regular intervals, these synchronized moments coalesce into clear Morse code dots and dashes spelling out "SOS" in the upper portion of the canvas. The entire composition unfolds automatically without any user input, presenting a hypnotic blend of individual randomness and collective synchronization that evokes both the organic chaos and structured beauty of natural phenomena.
