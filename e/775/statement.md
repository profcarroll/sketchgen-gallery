This sketch creates a mesmerizing nighttime cityscape with a dynamic water surface that captures and distorts light from passing drones. The scene features a dramatic harbor view with high-contrast buildings and a pulsating, wave-like water surface that continuously shifts in color and form. Drones periodically illuminate the scene as they fly through the air, occasionally arranging themselves into geometric patterns that create momentary, mesmerizing light formations. The water surface reflects these lights with a dynamic, rippling effect that pulses and distorts, creating an immersive interplay of light, movement, and reflected color across the entire canvas. The entire composition evolves organically without any user input, creating a continuously shifting visual experience that captures the ethereal quality of light dancing on water in a city at night.
