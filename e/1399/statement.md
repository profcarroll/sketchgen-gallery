This sketch creates a stylized ice rink scene with abstract figure skaters that glide across the reflective surface. Each skater leaves behind a colorful, fading trail that accumulates and flows like light streaks on ice. The cool blue color palette dominates the composition, with vibrant accents highlighting the paths of movement. The scene is entirely self-contained and dynamic, with no external input required - the skaters continuously move and update their trails, creating an ever-changing pattern of glowing motion across the canvas. The visual effect emphasizes the fluidity and ephemeral nature of skating, where movement creates luminous traces that fade over time.
