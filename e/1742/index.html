Brief

The canvas displays intricate, symmetrical spirograph patterns that appear to swirl and evolve across the plane. These geometric patterns suggest the complex paths traced by planets in deep space. The color palette is dominated by rich, shimmering gold and ethereal lilac tones. The formations move smoothly across the screen, creating a continuous, mesmerizing dance of interconnected shapes. There is no required interaction; the display is self-sustaining, always generating and shifting new forms.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch generates mesmerizing spirograph patterns that swirl and evolve across the canvas, evoking the celestial dance of planets in deep space. Using a rich palette dominated by shimmering gold and ethereal lilac tones, it creates intricate symmetrical designs that shift organically over time. The patterns emerge from mathematical relationships between rotating circles, producing complex, interlocking forms that move smoothly without any user interaction. Each frame builds upon the previous one through carefully calculated trigonometric functions, creating a continuous visual narrative of cosmic motion and geometric harmony.
