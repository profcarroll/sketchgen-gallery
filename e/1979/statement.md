This sketch creates a static architectural installation composed of polished brass pipes and industrial glass tubing arranged in a structured network reminiscent of 1920s Bauhaus design. The piece features a vibrant cyan light that pulses rhythmically throughout the entire structure, creating an energized, glowing effect. The visualization uses WebGL rendering to efficiently draw a complex network of interconnected pipes while maintaining performance through spatial hashing for neighbor detection and reduced geometric detail for all spherical elements. The viewer observes a serene, systematic glow that moves through the installation without any external interaction, emphasizing the architectural form and its luminous quality.
