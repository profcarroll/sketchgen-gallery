This sketch creates an ethereal field of glowing, abstract forms that move in slow, interconnected patterns. The fluid shapes drift through space, leaving luminous trails behind them. When two shapes intersect, the collision point crystallizes into a sharp, geometric anchor — a pyramid structure — before dissolving back into the fluid flow. The visual effect emphasizes both the continuous movement and the sudden moments of stability that arise from the interactions between these abstract forms. The palette of deep blues, golds, and turquoises creates an otherworldly glow, while the geometric anchors provide a sense of structure within the chaos.
