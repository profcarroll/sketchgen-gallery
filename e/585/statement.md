This sketch creates a deep cosmic environment using translucent overlapping circles that slowly pulse and flow. The background features a field of gently animated, semi-transparent circles that create depth and movement through their subtle pulsing motion. Scattered throughout this fluid space are delicate glowing filament patterns that resemble cosmic webbing. These threads begin as static patterns but then start to subtly pulse with internal light, creating a ripple effect across the dark background. The visual effect is achieved through careful layering of translucent shapes and smooth animations that suggest the vastness and quiet energy of deep space without overwhelming the viewer with detail or complexity.
