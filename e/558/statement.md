This sketch creates a dynamic, evolving visual system where chaotic retro-styled pixels float randomly across the canvas, interacting with each other through simple spatial relationships. At regular intervals, temporary geometric lattice structures emerge and pulse rhythmically, expanding and contracting in a cyclical motion. These pulses cause the surrounding pixels to organize into visible patterns, creating a sense of structure emerging from chaos. The work explores themes of emergent order, rhythmic transformation, and visual entropy — a digital dance between disorder and design that unfolds autonomously over time.
