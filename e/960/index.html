Brief

The canvas displays a dynamic field of vibrant, streaking color formed by countless luminous particles. These particles move autonomously, creating continuous, visible trails across the surface. As they interact, repulsive forces guide the flow, causing the particles to slowly organize and settle into several distinct, stable, and cyclical spirals of light. The overall appearance is one of patterned, mesmerizing, and contained orbital energy.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of glowing particles that move autonomously across the canvas, forming dynamic trails and organizing into stable spiral patterns. Each particle has its own color hue that slowly cycles through the spectrum, while its motion is influenced by repulsive and attractive forces from nearby particles. The system simulates orbital energy as particles gravitate toward each other in rhythmic, cyclical formations. Audio input from the user's microphone drives subtle changes in particle movement, creating a responsive visual experience where sound becomes visible through the flow of light. The simulation runs entirely within the p5.js environment without external libraries or addons beyond standard p5 features.
