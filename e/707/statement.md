This sketch creates a simulated retro camera viewfinder with animated scanlines that rapidly shift across the entire canvas. The visual effect is achieved through a series of horizontal bands that move vertically at different speeds, creating a strobing pattern. The scanlines use high-contrast colors against a dark background to emphasize their movement. The animation is entirely self-contained and requires no user input to generate the effect. The flickering pattern dominates the entire viewing area with constant cyclical motion, evoking the aesthetic of classic analog cameras.
