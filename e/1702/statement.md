This sketch depicts a continuous cycle of geometric transformation, where sharp angular forms oscillate between order and chaos. It begins with a stable, interconnected structure of boxes that gradually collapses into fragments. These pieces then reassemble into intricate branching patterns—non-repeating fractal structures that grow outward from the original form. The visual rhythm captures both destruction and creation, showing how energy disperses and then organically reforms into increasingly complex patterns. The color palette remains consistent throughout, emphasizing the transition from controlled equilibrium to dynamic growth. The animation runs autonomously without external input, creating an endless loop of disruptive beauty that transforms the canvas continuously over time.
