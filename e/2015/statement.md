This sketch creates an immersive, bioluminescent coral reef environment that pulses with hypnotic geometric patterns. Using WebGL rendering, it generates thousands of particles that flow in layered vortex motions, creating the illusion of deep-sea currents and living coral structures. The composition alternates between spheres, boxes, and torus shapes to simulate different types of reef formations, while a carefully chosen palette of saturated colors evokes the intense, otherworldly glow of bioluminescent marine life. The camera slowly orbits the scene, revealing the complex layered architecture of the reef, all without any user input—this is an autonomous visualization that captures both the organic flow and geometric complexity of underwater ecosystems through the lens of retro arcade aesthetics.
