This sketch depicts a visceral field of geometrically fragmented forms undergoing slow, organic decay. The composition consists of thousands of small, rotating spheres that gradually shift and dissolve toward the center of the scene, while thick, flowing crimson fault lines propagate across the canvas in unpredictable patterns. The visual effect captures the essence of emotional breakdown through stark, mechanical cleavages against a backdrop of organic motion—each fragment pulsing with its own rhythm of dissolution. The palette emphasizes rich reds and deep purples to convey a sense of trauma and transformation, creating an unsettling yet mesmerizing representation of internal collapse.
