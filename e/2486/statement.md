This sketch creates an immersive, evolving field of glowing geometric nodes arranged in a grid pattern. Each node pulses with its own rhythm while responding to waves that emanate from a central point and propagate outward. The visual system generates complex emergent patterns through the interaction of multiple wavefronts, where adjacent nodes resonate with varying intensities and hues based on their proximity to active pulses. When viewers click anywhere on the canvas, they trigger new pulses that disrupt and amplify the existing rhythm, creating an interactive experience where each gesture introduces unpredictable variations in the synchronized field dynamics. The continuous animation produces a mesmerizing, organic flow of color and form that shifts and evolves over time.
