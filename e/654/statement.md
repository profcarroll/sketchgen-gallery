This sketch depicts a metallic sphere rolling autonomously through a complex, geometric channel structure. The sphere navigates an intricate path formed by a series of funneled walls and randomly positioned crystalline barriers. Using physics-based movement with gravity and collision detection, the sphere responds dynamically to the channel's topography and the asymmetrical obstacles. The scene rotates slowly to show the three-dimensional complexity of the environment, while the sphere's motion demonstrates how it bounces and changes direction upon contact with various structural elements. The visual design emphasizes the interplay between smooth geometric forms and abrupt lateral deviations caused by the random placement of barriers.
