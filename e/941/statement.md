This sketch depicts a glass vessel filled with liquid, into which a continuous stream of colored fluid pours from above. The visualization captures the dynamic interaction between the incoming stream and the contained liquid through several key elements: particles representing the flowing liquid that gradually settle and interact with the surface; vortex formations that emerge at impact points and spiral outward as they dissipate; and the transparent glass boundary that contains the scene. The visual effect is achieved through particle physics, including gravity and velocity-based movement, combined with a simple but effective particle system for both the stream and the impact vortexes. The entire scene operates without user input once started, creating an observational experience of continuous fluid dynamics within a contained space.
