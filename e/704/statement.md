This sketch creates an abstract aerial view of a bustling cityscape using flowing light streaks that move across a gridded plane. The composition features hundreds of streams that originate randomly and flow with gentle directional changes, creating a sense of endless motion and rhythm. At major intersections — defined by the underlying grid — these streams coalesce into starburst formations that pulse with energy. The monochromatic grayscale palette emphasizes the structured movement, suggesting the hypnotic, ceaseless activity of a sleepless metropolis viewed from above. The visual effect is one of ordered chaos, where individual elements form part of a larger, harmonious pattern.
