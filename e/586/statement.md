This sketch depicts a weathered paper-cut ocean scene where translucent ships drift slowly across a medium, leaving behind crystalline trails and signs of decay. The ships show visible tears, warping, and colonization by barnacles and moss, evoking the passage of time and water damage. Their movement is defined by a slow, ceaseless decay, with ethereal wakes solidifying into delicate salt deposits that streak and leach across the surface, documenting the fading vitality of the paper vessels.
