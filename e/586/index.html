Brief

The canvas depicts a translucent sheet that serves as a medium for whimsical, paper-cut ships. These ships are shown enduring significant weathering, featuring visible tears, warping, and colonization by barnacles and moss. They appear to drift across the surface, leaving behind ethereal, dissolving trails. These wakes solidify into delicate streaks of crystalline salt deposits and mineral discoloration that gradually streak and leach across the surrounding medium, documenting the passage of time and water damage. The scene’s movement is defined by this slow, ceaseless decay and the gentle recession of the vibrant paper into delicate, crystalline ruin.
