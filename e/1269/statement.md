This sketch creates a dark, smoky void filled with jagged metallic shards that slowly float and rotate. At the center, a pulsating emerald light rhythmically illuminates the scene, casting defined shadows across the chrome surfaces and creating bright glints that catch on the shifting textures. The shards are procedurally generated with irregular, jagged shapes that give them a fragmented, crystalline appearance. The emerald light's pulsation creates a hypnotic rhythm that drives the entire scene, while the slow rotation of each shard adds to the dreamlike, meditative quality of the piece. The WEBGL renderer allows for proper 3D lighting and shadow effects, with each shard reflecting the central light source in different ways to create depth and metallic realism.
