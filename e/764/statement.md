This sketch depicts a serene yet dramatic sunset landscape featuring rolling green hills bathed in warm, saturated light. The composition uses vertical gradient background transitions from deep night blue to vibrant orange, creating the illusion of the sun's position and its influence on the scene. Multiple layered hills with varying shades of green and subtle noise-based shapes give the terrain a natural, undulating appearance. Long, exaggerated shadows stretch across the foreground, emphasizing depth and topographical variation. A glowing sun with radiating rays adds warmth and drama to the scene, while careful color choices and lighting effects convey the intensity of the sunset's glow on the landscape.
