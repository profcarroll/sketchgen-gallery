This sketch depicts the slow, organic diffusion of mineral deposits across crystalline steel strata in a desert gorge. The composition shows rust-colored formations that slowly expand and contract in undulating waves, creating an illusion of movement through static elements. Each stratum is rendered with multiple concentric circles to simulate the spreading of minerals through porous surfaces, while subtle texture lines add depth and visual complexity. The continuous animation captures the patient, relentless process of geological change, where only the boundaries of these formations shift over time. The color palette emphasizes earth tones and metallic rusts, evoking the harsh beauty of desert landscapes where mineral deposits slowly seep through steel-like structures.
