This sketch creates an immersive drone flight through a decaying college campus, blending brutalist architecture with lush organic growth. The scene features massive concrete structures rendered in muted greys, punctuated by vibrant moss and vines in emerald greens that contrast the cold geometry. Through a continuous cinematic camera movement, viewers experience the environment's journey as walls crumble away, revealing rusted rebar beneath the invasive plant life. The animation unfolds autonomously without any user interaction, simulating an aerial exploration of this abandoned space where nature has reclaimed the architectural remnants.
