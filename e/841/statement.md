This sketch renders a highly detailed, static cross-section of a coastal mound, emphasizing the granular texture of pale yellow sand and the subtle variations of seashells embedded within. Each grain of sand is individually rendered with attention to its position and slight color variation, creating a densely packed micro-environment. Seashells are scattered throughout this landscape, each one uniquely shaped and colored to reflect natural diversity. The composition remains motionless, capturing a frozen moment in geological time.
