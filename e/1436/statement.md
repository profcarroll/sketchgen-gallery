This sketch creates an abstract visualization of a glowing circuit lattice that pulses with simulated data flow. The composition features a grid of interconnected nodes that emit varying intensities of light based on their position in the system's rhythm. The connections between nodes fluctuate in brightness to simulate data transmission, while individual nodes pulse gently like heartbeat signals. The entire structure appears to breathe and flow with an organic energy pattern, creating a mesmerizing effect that suggests continuous information processing within an abstract digital network.
