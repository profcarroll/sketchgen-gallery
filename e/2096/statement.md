This sketch visualizes an electrocardiogram pattern as a glowing neon line that scrolls continuously across a dark void. The waveform is generated algorithmically with a repeating rhythm of peaks and dips, creating a mesmerizing effect of steady energy transmission. The line features multiple layers of glow and subtle variations in opacity to create depth and visual complexity. As the pattern scrolls from left to right, it maintains its cyclical structure while appearing to pulse with controlled, rhythmic energy. The visualization captures the essence of biological data rendered as an abstract, hypnotic display.
