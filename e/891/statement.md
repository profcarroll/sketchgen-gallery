This sketch creates an immersive, accelerating tunnel through abstract atomic and molecular structures using WebGL rendering. It features a bioluminescent foundation with pulsing light waves that cast ephemeral diffraction patterns across crystalline forms. The scene uses a combination of particle systems, dynamic geometry, and light interference effects to create a sense of constant motion and transformation. As particles flow through the tunnel, they interact with each other in a spatially hashed grid to maintain performance while creating complex visual relationships. The entire environment pulses rhythmically, giving the impression of living, breathing molecular structures.
