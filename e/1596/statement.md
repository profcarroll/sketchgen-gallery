This sketch creates a dynamic, abstract cityscape at night where streams of light flow across the canvas like urban illumination. The lights move in a subtle flow field, creating an organic yet structured movement pattern that mimics traffic or electricity flowing through a dense metropolitan grid. When these luminous streaks intersect, they temporarily form geometric shapes—triangles, squares, and pentagons—that appear and dissolve with a sense of spontaneous energy. The effect builds complexity as intersections occur, creating an ever-shifting pattern of light and geometry that captures the essence of a bustling cityscape from a high-altitude perspective, where individual lights become part of a larger, interconnected system of illumination.
