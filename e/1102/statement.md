This sketch presents an interactive jigsaw puzzle where colorful, interlocking pieces are scattered across a plain background. Each piece displays a segment of a larger, vibrant image. Users can select and drag individual pieces to reorganize them. When a piece is moved close to its correct location, it snaps firmly into place, eventually assembling the complete image. The implementation uses p5.js to manage canvas interactions, piece movement, and snapping mechanics, creating an engaging user experience within strict performance constraints.
