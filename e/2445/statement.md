This sketch creates a ceramic tile wall mural that captures the essence of a decaying pattern over time. The composition features irregularly sized tiles arranged in a grid with uneven grout lines that simulate aged craftsmanship. Each tile is rendered with randomized fractures and broken segments to suggest weathering and deterioration. The overall effect conveys the passage of time through deliberate imperfections in an otherwise geometric pattern. The outermost border reinforces this theme with continuous, interlocking chevron shapes that frame the entire scene in a traditional yet worn aesthetic. The color palette employs deep, jeweled tones against a contrasting field to emphasize both the intricate details and the sense of decay.
