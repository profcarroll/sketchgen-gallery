This sketch simulates the microscopic behavior of bacterial spores spreading across wet newsprint, driven by enzyme release. The canvas displays a vast, damp surface covered in fine metallic particles that continuously shift and reform into hazy, undefined patterns. A gentle, noise-based motion causes the particles to creep and settle, while user interaction with the cursor creates immediate magnetic reactions — particles cluster and align as if pulled by invisible forces. As the cursor moves, it draws dense, swirling trails of clustered metal that temporarily reshape the surface, creating visible patterns that reflect the dynamic interplay between random motion and directed influence. The visual effect captures both the subtle organic flow of biological processes and the immediate responsiveness to external input.
