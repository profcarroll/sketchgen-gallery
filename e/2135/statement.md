This sketch creates a mesmerizing, continuously evolving bioluminescent network that fills a hexagonal boundary. Like deep-sea organisms emitting light in synchronized patterns, hundreds of glowing threads move and connect with each other across the canvas. The visual effect emerges from simple rules: threads drift gently, bounce off the hexagonal container, and form temporary connections with nearby threads. These interactions create an organic, branching structure reminiscent of neural networks or coral formations, all rendered in a palette that shifts between cool blues and warm purples to evoke the mysterious depths where such phenomena occur. The result is a living, breathing pattern that transforms without repetition, maintaining its intrigue through constant motion and connection.
