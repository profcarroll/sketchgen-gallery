Brief

The canvas displays a dynamic, abstract aerial view of a metropolitan cityscape. Structures are composed of interconnected geometric shapes representing towering buildings and grids, rendered primarily in cool grays, blacks, and deep blues. Movement is central, featuring constant, flowing streaks of light and abstract, kinetic shapes that traverse the composition, simulating the rapid flow of traffic and people. The view never settles, giving a sense of constant, overwhelming energy. The viewer can interact by clicking anywhere on the canvas, which highlights the click point and causes a localized ripple effect of color and geometric reorganization around that point.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a dynamic, abstract cityscape using p5.js in WEBGL mode. The scene features towering geometric buildings with illuminated windows, constantly moving particles that simulate traffic or people, and flowing light streaks that traverse the composition. The viewer experiences a sense of overwhelming urban energy as the camera slowly orbits the scene, revealing the interconnected structures and kinetic movement. Interactive clicks generate localized ripple effects that temporarily disrupt the flow, demonstrating how the viewer can influence the abstract environment. The visual style emphasizes cool grays, deep blues, and vibrant accents to convey the feeling of a bustling metropolitan area without literal representation.
