This sketch creates a lush, immersive field of rhododendron blooms rendered in vibrant magenta, violet, and forest green hues. The composition features two distinct visual elements: dynamic, undulating clusters of flowers that float and pulse gently across the canvas, and still, reflective water patches that mirror the blooms with glassy calm. The floating flowers exhibit subtle wave motion and slow drifts toward the center, while the water areas provide contrast through their serene, motionless surfaces. The interplay between movement and stillness creates a sense of depth and rhythm within the dense, overlapping field of color.
