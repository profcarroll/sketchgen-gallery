This sketch creates an evolving stained-glass structure that begins with a subtle, pulsing light and gradually intensifies until it destabilizes into a chaotic explosion of color fragments. The composition starts with hundreds of geometric panels arranged in a rigid network, each filled with vibrant, saturated hues and outlined with shifting spectral edges. As the internal light pulse builds in intensity, it triggers wave-like effects that trace the structure's geometry before erupting into a shower of erratic, glowing fragments. These colorful shards scatter outward in unpredictable patterns, their motion highlighting the original structure's form while creating an impression of violent, luminous chaos. The piece explores themes of controlled energy transforming into untamed motion through color, form, and the interplay between order and disorder.
