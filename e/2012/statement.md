This sketch simulates the intricate texture of a polished granite countertop by combining randomly distributed particles with flowing mineral veins. The background features a dark mottled surface populated with thousands of tiny flecks in varying shades of gray, white, and charcoal. Subtle, meandering streaks of lighter minerals weave through the pattern, creating a natural-looking stone grain structure. Each click regenerates the entire composition, producing a new random arrangement while maintaining the characteristic visual complexity of real granite. The technique uses noise functions to create organic curves for the veins and random placement with collision detection to avoid particle clustering near these mineral streaks, resulting in a convincing simulation that fills the entire canvas with texture and depth.
