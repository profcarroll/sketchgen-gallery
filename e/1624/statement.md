This sketch presents an organic, flowing chessboard where geometric pieces drift slowly across a fixed grid. The visualization creates an illusion of deep, submerged movement by combining smooth transitions between random targets with gentle, periodic oscillations that vary per piece. Each piece maintains its own rhythm and direction while appearing to be carried by an invisible current, resulting in a mesmerizing effect of continuous, natural motion over a structured foundation. The visual design emphasizes fluidity through the combination of easing functions, individual timing variations, and subtle rotational movement.
