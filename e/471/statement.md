This sketch creates an ever-shifting network of glowing points and lines that evokes the feeling of a complex mechanical or digital system in flux. The visualization begins with a structured grid of points connected by lines, but these connections rapidly fragment and reform as the entire structure pulses with electric energy. Using Perlin noise and sine wave modulation, the points gently move in organic patterns while maintaining their grid relationship. The color palette draws from deep industrial tones of night sky and metal, punctuated by sharp flashes of electric blue and white that continuously reorganize themselves across the canvas. The chaotic dissolution of structure into a beautiful tangle reflects the instability described in the brief, all rendered within the constraints of performance and visual fidelity.
