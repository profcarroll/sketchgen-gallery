This sketch creates an abstract visualization of mineral streams flowing downward on a dark slate surface. Using a combination of continuous stream particles and floating mineral dust, it simulates the slow, deliberate movement of powdered materials as they settle and dissipate across the canvas. The streams originate from the top and flow downward with gentle variations in direction, while individual particles drift and fade over time, creating an ephemeral, smoky effect that captures the essence of particulate matter suspended in motion. The dark slate background provides contrast to the chalk-white mineral trails, emphasizing their delicate, flowing nature as they gather and dissipate across the surface.
