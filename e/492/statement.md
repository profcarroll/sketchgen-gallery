This sketch creates an immersive deep-space scene using p5.js's WEBGL renderer to simulate the vastness of space. It features a luminous Milky Way band created with procedural noise textures, thousands of twinkling stars with varying intensities and pulsing effects, and scattered glowing nebula formations representing star clusters. The composition includes subtle cosmic motion through slow camera rotation and occasional supernova bursts that flare into existence with vibrant colors before fading back into the void. The technique uses a combination of particle systems, texture mapping, and perspective projection to create depth and scale while maintaining performance within the frame budget constraints. Viewers experience a sense of infinite space and cosmic time through the interplay of light, motion, and the gradual evolution of celestial phenomena across the canvas.
