This sketch creates a viscous, living representation of creamy sauce and macaroni, with clusters of yellow-orange sauce that gently wobble and smooth, curved macaroni shapes that undulate with subtle motion. The scene responds to mouse interaction by stretching and pulling nearby macaroni pieces into flowing streams that follow the cursor path. The visual effect combines organic motion with a sense of fluidity and elasticity, creating an immersive experience where viewers can manipulate the dish's structure through direct interaction. The gentle, continuous wobble gives each element a living quality, while the drag interaction provides immediate feedback that transforms the static composition into a dynamic, interactive scene.
