This sketch creates an atmospheric sonar visualization where lines emanate from a central point and spiral outward in defined helix patterns. The visualization uses a combination of radial expansion and periodic helical motion to create complex, rhythmic wave patterns that evolve continuously across the canvas. Each line follows its own unique spiral path while maintaining visual connections to neighboring lines, creating a mesmerizing field of light that appears to pulse and flow. The deep blue to violet color palette enhances the oceanic, mysterious quality of the sonar effect, with each helix pattern contributing to a continuous, hypnotic rhythm that fills the entire canvas without requiring any user interaction.
