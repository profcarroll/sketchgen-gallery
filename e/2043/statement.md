This sketch explores the tension between rigid structure and fluid transformation, depicting typographic elements that appear to breathe and reshape themselves. The composition begins as a formal grid of text—reminiscent of classic Swiss design principles—but gradually dissolves into organic motion through subtle sine wave oscillations. Each character element shifts position and rotates independently, creating an impression of mechanical forces at work without explicit machinery. The visual effect captures the essence of systems that exist in a state of constant flux between order and chaos, where the underlying structure remains but its manifestation continuously evolves.
