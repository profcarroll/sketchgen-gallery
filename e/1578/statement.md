This sketch constructs an abstract self-portrait using interlocking geometric facets that create a sense of depth through architectural form. The composition is built from a series of boxes arranged in space, each contributing to the overall fragmented silhouette while maintaining structural coherence. Directional shadow lines traverse these surfaces, carving volumes and emphasizing the three-dimensional quality of the form. Through careful lighting and the interplay between solid geometry and dark shadow, the piece presents a sculpted moment where light and shadow define presence and volume. The work avoids particle systems or complex animations in favor of a fixed architectural abstraction that conveys mass through planar surfaces and dramatic chiaroscuro.
