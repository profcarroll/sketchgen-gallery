This sketch transforms the nostalgic arcade cabinet concept into a geological interpretation of deep subterranean strata. Rather than glowing biomes suspended in liquid circuits, viewers witness monumental sedimentary layers revealing ancient geological history. The composition uses flowing copper-toned paths that meander through the canvas, creating an impression of time and depth. The technique employs layered rectangles for the rock formations and flowing lines for the liquid pathways, all rendered in 3D space to emphasize the geological perspective. The viewer experiences a journey through layers of earth, with subtle movements suggesting the slow passage of time and the continuous flow of geological processes.
