This sketch creates an immersive, evolving cosmic environment using overlapping translucent circles and delicate filamentous webbing. The circles slowly drift across the canvas with a gentle, undulating motion that mimics celestial flow, while the filaments shift and morph dynamically in response to both time-based animation and the movement of the background elements. The composition is intentionally minimal—avoiding complex particle systems or high-frequency updates—to ensure visual continuity and a serene, contemplative atmosphere. The interplay between the fluid circles and the subtle webbing creates an ethereal sense of structure within the vastness, evoking the quiet complexity of deep space.
