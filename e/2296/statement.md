This sketch presents a Victorian-era mechanical map as a complex interplay of layered paper, rotating gears, and geometric polyhedra. The composition evokes the intricate clockwork systems of the late 19th century, where information flows through connected mechanisms. Overlapping sheets of cartography provide the base layer, while carefully detailed gear teeth rotate in synchronized motion. Information is visualized through geometric forms that connect across the canvas, each representing nodes in a vast network. The entire scene pulses with subtle motion, suggesting the continuous operation of a grand mechanical system, where patterned layers and precise mechanisms work together to convey complex data flows.
