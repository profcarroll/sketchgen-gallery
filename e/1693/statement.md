This sketch creates a static, highly detailed Persian rug pattern with intricate woven textures and strict vertical symmetry. The composition features a central lattice grid where each repeating motif consists of interwoven vine patterns that mirror perfectly along the vertical axis. The design employs rich jewel tones—deep purples, greens, blues, and reds—to create a complex visual texture reminiscent of traditional Persian carpet weaving. All elements are carefully arranged to maintain perfect symmetry across the entire surface, with no motion or animation, allowing viewers to appreciate the mathematical precision and ornamental complexity of the textile pattern. The work emphasizes the relationship between geometric structure and organic flow, creating depth through layered decorative elements that appear to interlock seamlessly.
