This sketch presents a dark obsidian surface populated with sharp crystalline geodes that emerge from the rigid black substrate. The piece begins in stillness until a user clicks anywhere on the canvas, which triggers an immediate and aggressive fracture pattern. Once activated, cracks spread rapidly across the surface in violent, branching paths that consume the obsidian, transforming it into a field of kinetic energy through intense bursts of saturated color. The visual effect is achieved through sharp geometric forms, sudden color transitions, and dynamic crack propagation that moves with high velocity from the point of impact. The viewer witnesses a dramatic transformation from static order to explosive energy as the rigid surface yields to the force of fracturing.
