This sketch depicts a mechanical pendulum's rhythmic swing causing sudden, brittle fractures to propagate through a damp, fibrous membrane made of corrugated cardboard. The viewer experiences a mesmerizing cascade of structural tears and fractures that emerge in sync with the pendulum's oscillation. The visual effect uses WEBGL rendering to create three-dimensional tearing patterns that grow and shift continuously, with each tear structure displaying its own unique fracture pattern. The pendulum's motion drives the timing of these fractures, creating a sense of mechanical inevitability and organic destruction simultaneously. The corrugated cardboard texture is simulated through noise-based deformation of polygonal shapes, while the fractures appear as sharp, irregular lines that radiate from each tear point.
