This sketch renders a steampunk-inspired sculpture composed of geometric crystalline structures suspended around a central rotating cube. The piece combines deep emerald greens with oxidized copper browns and bright energy discharges, creating a sense of volumetric complexity through layered forms. The animated elements include orbiting points that emit glowing trails, pulsating crystal formations that expand and contract, and intense energy bursts that radiate from the central structure. The viewer experiences a dynamic interplay of motion and light as the entire composition evolves in real-time, driven by a continuous animation loop that maintains visual complexity without exceeding performance constraints.
