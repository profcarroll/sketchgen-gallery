Brief

The canvas depicts a shallow, translucent medium containing whimsical, paper-cut vessels. Initially, the ships sail away, leaving behind delicate, dissipating wake trails. Over time, the scene undergoes a gradual deterioration: the ships develop visible patina, moss, and barnacle buildup, while their paper edges warp and tear. These trails and the vessels themselves begin to break down, leaving behind crystalline salt streaks and mineral deposits. These deposits grow into root-like crystalline structures that envelop the surrounding medium. Finally, the detached fragments of the hulls dissolve completely, leaving only the remnants of the crystal growth floating in the fading translucence.
