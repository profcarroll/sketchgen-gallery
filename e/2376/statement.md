This sketch creates an organic, evolving ink diffusion effect on a textured paper surface. Inspired by the slow blooming of wet ink on porous newsprint, it generates dynamic, branching formations that spread and fade over time. The visual technique uses layered ellipses with varying opacities to simulate the absorption and diffusion of ink into paper fibers, while subtle noise-based texture adds realistic paper grain. Each ink spot begins as a small point and gradually expands outward, sometimes branching into secondary formations, creating an ever-changing landscape of dark indigo blooms against the dry white background. The effect captures the irreversible, organic quality of liquid ink spreading through porous material, with each formation following its own growth pattern and fading naturally over time.
