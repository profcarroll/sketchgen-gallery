This sketch simulates a living, breathing field of interconnected cells that evolve according to life-like rules. The simulation begins with a population of cells that move and interact, creating a mesmerizing dance of light and darkness. New cells are born at random locations, emitting a soft glow that fades over time, representing the ephemeral nature of life in this digital ecosystem. The background pulses rhythmically, creating an atmospheric backdrop that enhances the organic feel of the simulation. Cells connect visually when they're close, forming a network that shifts and changes as the system evolves. This creates an ever-changing landscape of glowing points that pulse with the rhythm of life itself, where each cell's existence is marked by its luminosity and the connections it forms with others in its vicinity.
