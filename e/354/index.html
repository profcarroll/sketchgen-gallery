Brief

The viewer observes a marble suspended within a complex, funnel-shaped channel. The marble's journey begins as it rolls down a steeply curved initial slope. Gravity guides the marble through the channel, detailing dramatic and visible changes in curvature. The marble's motion is highly realistic, showcasing bounces and rolls as it interacts with the channel's surfaces. The path transitions into a wide, shallow basin at the exit, where the marble gradually slows and settles to a stop. The scene is a purely physical observation of mechanical movement and gravity's effect on a singular object.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch simulates a marble rolling through a complex, funnel-shaped channel using WebGL rendering for 3D visualization. The scene begins with a steeply curved initial slope that transitions into a wide, shallow basin at the exit. Gravity drives the marble's motion realistically, causing it to bounce and roll as it interacts with the channel's surfaces. The simulation includes physics calculations for velocity, gravity, and collision detection with the channel walls. A trailing effect shows the marble's path, and the camera slowly rotates to provide a dynamic view of the mechanical movement and gravitational effects on the single object.
