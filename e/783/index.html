Brief

The canvas displays a stylized equine figure rendered with continuous, flowing curves, capturing the powerful dynamism of a full gallop. The horse’s anatomy emphasizes fluidity, replacing sharp angles with sweeping, curved forms in both the body and limbs. The movement is inherently rhythmic, depicting a powerful stride characterized by pronounced extension in the front legs and a strong, deliberate follow-through of the hind legs. The overall composition emphasizes momentum, presenting a lively and perpetually moving depiction of the animal.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch renders a stylized galloping horse using continuous, flowing curves to capture the animal's dynamic movement. The composition emphasizes momentum through sweeping body forms and rhythmic limb motion, where each part of the horse sways and moves in harmony with the gallop's natural flow. The animation is self-contained, requiring no user input, and demonstrates fluidity over time via subtle sine-based oscillations applied to key anatomical points. The visual rhythm mimics the powerful stride of a running horse through dynamic, curved forms that suggest motion without sharp transitions or static poses.
