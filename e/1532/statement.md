This sketch creates an evolving, abstract landscape using intersecting geometric fields that morph and shift in real-time. The composition features multiple layers of animated shapes—circles and rectangles—that respond dynamically to sound input from the microphone. Vibrant, shifting colors create a sense of depth and motion as the fields continuously transform. The outermost elements exhibit pronounced wave-like curves that emphasize the organic flow of the system. The entire scene is self-contained and reacts autonomously to audio stimuli, creating an immersive, ever-changing visual experience where sound directly influences form and color.
