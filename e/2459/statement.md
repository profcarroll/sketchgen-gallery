This sketch presents the mechanical heart of a giant automated loom, rendered in brass and polished metal. It depicts intricate gears and cams in constant motion, driving threads across a tapestry that transforms before our eyes. Clear geometric forms—gears with teeth, cam shapes—rotate and interlock to form a complex mechanical rhythm. But as viewers interact by clicking, they destabilize the field: a ripple effect spreads from their point of contact, causing the solid forms to dissolve into hyper-detailed geometric color fields that pulse and reform. The viewer's touch accelerates this dissolution, revealing an underlying process where structure gives way to chaotic beauty—a mesmerizing dance between order and fragmentation.
