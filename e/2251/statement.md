This sketch depicts a subterranean chamber where ancient stone and bronze fragments slowly collapse into one another, revealing hidden geometric patterns in the rock. The viewer observes a perpetual process of erosion and time's passage, with each fragment rotating and shifting independently as it gradually disintegrates. Earthy tones dominate the palette—deep reds, browns, and grays that evoke the feel of compressed earth and carved marble. The entire scene is subtly animated, with particles gently falling and revealing intricate fault lines in the rock face, creating a sense of deep temporal processes unfolding beneath the surface. The viewer witnesses an endless, grinding march of decay that exposes the underlying structure of the landscape.
