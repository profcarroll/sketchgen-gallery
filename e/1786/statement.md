This sketch creates a vibrant, retro-futuristic digital landscape dominated by a continuous hexagonal grid that pulses with electric blue light. Each hexagonal module acts as a glowing node that periodically emits radial pulses of neon light, creating an organic flow across the canvas. The entire composition is animated through subtle color cycling and pulsing effects, with modules randomly activating and deactivating to simulate a living, breathing digital environment. The visual effect emphasizes luminous movement and structured geometry working in harmony to create an immersive, glowing experience that feels both technological and ethereal.
