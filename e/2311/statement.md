This sketch creates a nostalgic 1980s arcade cabinet display panel with intricate glowing circuits. The work features rhythmic geometric flashes of neon colors pulsing through circuit paths that move gently across the screen. Using only basic p5.js drawing functions, it simulates the look of glowing plastic etched pathways on a dark background, with sharp color pulses and subtle movement that evoke the energetic feel of retro arcade electronics. The visual effect is achieved through carefully modulated stroke weights, pulsing alpha values, and strategic flash effects that create the impression of stored energy flowing through the circuits.
