This sketch creates an ethereal field of interconnected 3D structures that drift and rotate through space. Each structure consists of multiple glowing planes that pulse with organic motion, connected by a network of lines that subtly shift in intensity. The system reacts dynamically to approaching structures: when two separate planes come within a critical distance, the connecting lines instantly pulse bright white and gold, creating visual moments of interaction that emphasize the flow of the geometry. The result is a continuously evolving, luminous landscape where motion and connection create an immersive sense of depth and spatial relationships.
