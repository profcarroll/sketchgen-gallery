This sketch renders an intricate mechanical blueprint for a celestial clock that exists in perpetual motion yet remains eternally stalled. The composition features delicate, precise lines drawn in the style of 1960s pen plotters on textured drafting vellum. Complex geometries of gears, linkages, and abstract forms shift and reorganize continuously across the canvas, creating a sense of structured yet asymmetrical movement. The blue-purple color palette evokes both cosmic depth and mechanical precision, while the constant subtle animations suggest an eternal, beautiful but unusable mechanism.
