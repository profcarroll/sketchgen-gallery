This sketch creates an endless cascade of rectangular blocks falling across the screen with varying sizes, colors, and motion patterns. Each block has a metallic, dull appearance created through careful manipulation of HSB color values and layered fill effects that simulate a reflective surface. The blocks are generated continuously and move under simulated physics including gravity, friction, and bouncing off screen edges. The visual effect is one of constant kinetic motion with trails left by the semi-transparent background, creating a mesmerizing stream of geometric forms that fill the entire canvas.
