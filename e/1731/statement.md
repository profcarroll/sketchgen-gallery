This sketch renders an immersive, ever-shifting urban landscape where bioluminescent light cycles through deep night to ethereal dawn. The cityscape features angular buildings with wet, highly reflective surfaces that continuously shift in hue and intensity, creating a living, breathing environment. The scene pulses with rhythmic color transitions and dynamic reflections that ripple across the wet architecture, giving the impression of an alive ecosystem. Through subtle camera movement and time-based animations, the viewer experiences a continuous transformation of light and atmosphere that makes the urban environment feel organic and responsive to an unseen ambient energy source.
