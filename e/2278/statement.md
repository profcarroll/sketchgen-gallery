This sketch depicts a dark obsidian floor fractured by deep, jagged cracks that pulse with rhythmic tidal pressure. Massive angular shards of volcanic glass tilt and slide across the surface, driven by unseen forces. The scene responds to user clicks with violent ruptures that send chunks tumbling through the dark environment. The effect uses WebGL rendering to create a sense of depth and volume, with dynamic lighting that highlights the sharp edges and polished planes of the shifting rock. Each shard has its own movement pattern and rotation, creating a convincing simulation of geological forces at work.
