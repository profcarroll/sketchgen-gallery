This sketch depicts an expansive arid savanna landscape dominated by deep terracotta earth tones and high contrast between ground and sky. The composition features cracked terrain with exaggerated shadows, sparse low scrub vegetation, and subtle swirling dust devils drifting across the surface. The technique uses layered gradient skies, procedurally generated cracks and vegetation, and animated particle-like dust devils to create atmospheric motion. The viewer observes a constantly shifting but slowly evolving desert scene that captures the essence of vast, arid landscape through color, texture, and gentle animation.
