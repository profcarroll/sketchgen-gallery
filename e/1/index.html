Brief

The canvas presents a dark background featuring a slow, continuous stream of overlapping translucent circles, all rendered in a uniform cool hue. These circles are constantly moving upward, simulating the rise of a tide. The viewer interacts with the canvas by clicking anywhere; this action generates a visible ripple effect that momentarily pushes the nearby circles away from the point of impact. Following the click, the circles resume their natural movement, gradually drifting back into the slow, ongoing tide.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a calming visual tide effect using overlapping translucent circles that slowly rise upward on a dark background. The circles are rendered in a cool blue hue to evoke water movement. When a user clicks anywhere on the canvas, a ripple effect pushes nearby circles away from the click point, creating a natural interaction with the flowing stream. After the click, the circles gradually return to their smooth, continuous motion, maintaining the serene, organic feel of rising water.
