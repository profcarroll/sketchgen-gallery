This sketch creates a mesmerizing geometric tapestry that resembles stained glass windows woven into a medieval tapestry. Using thousands of angular polyhedra — tetrahedra, octahedra, dodecahedra, and icosahedra — the composition forms a complex tessellating pattern across the canvas. The viewer's interaction triggers localized snapping behavior: when they click or drag, nearby geometric forms abruptly shift into new rigid positions, visibly altering the overall mosaic before settling back into place. This creates an illusion of woven, rigid materials that respond dynamically to touch while maintaining their structural integrity in static moments. The dark binding lines between shapes emerge from the connections formed by proximity, creating a sense of unity and tension within the geometric network.
