This sketch creates a dynamic, pulsing field of concentric rings that radiate outward from the center of the canvas. Each ring is composed of multiple overlapping circles rendered in vibrant fluorescent hues that cycle through the color spectrum. The entire pattern expands and contracts smoothly due to an elastic easing effect driven by a sine wave function, creating a rhythmic visual field. The animation runs continuously without any viewer interaction, with each ring having its own phase offset to create a sense of layered motion. The use of HSB color mode ensures vivid, saturated colors that maintain their vibrancy across the pulsing animation.
