This sketch presents a mechanical universe of brass clockwork sculptures suspended in three-dimensional space, each component following its own orbital rhythm. The composition evokes the geometric precision and kinetic elegance of early 20th-century industrial design, where every rotating gear and cantilevered arm moves in harmony with mathematical precision. The viewer observes an endless dance of interlocking mechanisms, each one a testament to the beauty of repetitive motion and structured chaos. Through careful attention to lighting and material properties, the work suggests not just movement but the very essence of time itself as a series of ordered cycles.
