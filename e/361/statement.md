This sketch renders a cosmic scene of interstellar space with a dynamic starfield, floating geometric monoliths, and shifting nebulae. The composition uses WebGL rendering to create depth through parallax movement of stars, slowly rotating structures, and volumetric nebulae that shift colors over time. The visual rhythm is hypnotic and continuous, requiring no user input to maintain its mesmerizing motion. The entire scene evolves organically within the constraints of a fixed frame budget, with all geometry precomputed and optimized for performance in a headless environment.
