This sketch creates a mesmerizing kinetic sculpture centered around a glowing molten bronze core, with ornate orbital structures that trace ethereal paths in a slow, ceaseless motion. Using WebGL rendering, it combines steampunk geometry with flowing light effects to evoke the feeling of a living, breathing machine. The scene features multiple splines rotating in different planes, each constructed from oxidized copper mesh and deep sea glass fragments, all moving in harmony around the central pool of molten material.
