Brief

The canvas displays a dense grid of small squares set against a near-black background, illuminated by a persistent, warm hue. Each square exhibits a subtle, independent breathing motion, expanding and contracting rhythmically. When the viewer clicks anywhere, a visible wave pattern propagates outward from the point of impact. This wave's passage is amplified by the current audio input: the squares passing through the wave flash a bright color specifically when the sound reaches its lowest recorded frequency, before slowly settling back into their breathing cycle.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a dynamic, responsive grid of breathing squares against a deep black background. Each square gently expands and contracts in a rhythmic breathing motion. When the viewer clicks anywhere on the canvas, a ripple wave emanates from the click point, causing nearby squares to flash brightly. The visual effect is amplified by live audio input: when low-frequency sounds are detected, squares randomly flash with a warm orange color, creating an immersive interaction between user input and sound. The entire composition pulses with subtle movement and responds visibly to both clicks and audio stimuli.
