This sketch renders an endless, evolving crystalline network that simulates the slow, organic movement of tectonic plates. Through a combination of subtle positional shifts, rotations, and color variations, each crystal in the grid appears to breathe and shift within its geometric framework. The visualization uses simplified 3D forms to maintain performance while conveying the sense of immense geological time and pressure. The entire composition moves continuously without user interaction, creating an immersive sense of vastness and perpetual transformation.
