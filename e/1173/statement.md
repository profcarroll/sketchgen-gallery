This sketch presents a static row of five smoothly rounded rectangular pads, each with a distinct color. When a user clicks on any pad, it immediately plays a corresponding tone and illuminates with a brighter color to provide visual feedback. The design emphasizes clean interaction and immediate response, using p5.js's sound synthesis capabilities to generate unique tones for each pad. The pads are arranged horizontally in the center of the canvas, maintaining consistent spacing and uniform dimensions to create a cohesive interface. The visual design uses subtle color variations and rounded corners to give the arrangement a polished, modern appearance while ensuring clear tactile feedback on interaction.
