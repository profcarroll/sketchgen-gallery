This sketch renders a serene, high-altitude view of a glowing metropolis at night, where streams of light streak across the canvas in organized patterns. At intersections of these light trails, stable geometric forms emerge—triangles, squares, and pentagons that shimmer with crystalline precision. The visualization captures a sense of perpetual stillness and structured beauty, as if the city's energy has been frozen into perfect, luminous lattice structures. The composition is entirely observational, requiring no interaction to maintain its delicate, unchanging harmony.
