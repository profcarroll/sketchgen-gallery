This sketch depicts a continuously growing vertical strip that evolves from simple horizontal stripes into complex interlocking patterns. The visual metaphor captures the transformation of textile motifs into glowing brass-like circuitry with organic, vine-like formations. The animation creates an illusion of self-generating biological mechanisms through rhythmic pulsing and evolving structures that grow upward. Each frame introduces new segments to the vertical strip while older ones fade out, creating a sense of continuous growth and dynamic energy. The warm brass color palette and smooth organic motion evoke both mechanical precision and natural flow, suggesting an ancient stellar observatory driven by an internal pendulum rhythm.
