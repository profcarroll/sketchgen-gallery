This sketch depicts a cosmic landscape where luminous geometric forms drift through deep space, their movements creating cascading trails of light. The structures pulse with emerald and cyan hues, casting glowing paths that branch and connect in fractal patterns. As these light trails persist, they coalesce into intricate, repeating sheet-like formations that populate the void with an endlessly evolving network of luminous geometry. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of rotating toroidal forms and particle-like trail systems that evolve over time, creating a sense of continuous motion and growth within the dark canvas of space.
