This sketch creates an mesmerizing, self-sustaining pattern of expanding concentric rings that pulse rhythmically from the center outward. Each ring grows steadily while simultaneously pulsing in and out, creating a dynamic wave-like effect that fills the canvas with luminous, jewel-toned color. The overlapping forms shift through a rich spectrum, generating a deep sense of color and dimensionality as they propagate. The pattern maintains a continuous, cyclical rhythm without any external input, embodying the concept of self-sustained growth like natural formations such as coral or rust patterns on steel surfaces.
