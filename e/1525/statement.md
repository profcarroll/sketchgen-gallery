This sketch creates a mesmerizing visual effect where random letters drift downward like snowflakes, gradually forming coherent words through attraction and cohesion forces. The particles move slowly with gentle physics—affected by gravity, wind, and interactions with nearby letters. As they gather, they form temporary words that appear and fade over time, creating an ever-shifting landscape of meaning emerging from chaos. Viewers witness the organic formation and dissolution of language, observing how simple rules can create complex emergent patterns.
