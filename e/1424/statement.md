This sketch creates an abstract, evolving cityscape from a bird's-eye view at night. Using WebGL rendering, it depicts a vast urban energy matrix with flowing light streaks (lines) and glowing nodes that form an intricate circuit board pattern. The scene continuously evolves as the camera slowly orbits the space, revealing the dynamic interplay between geometric connections and luminous points. The deep blues and blacks contrast dramatically with brilliant white and cyan lights, creating a sense of massive electronic infrastructure pulsing with energy across the urban expanse. The visual effect captures both the scale of a sprawling metropolis and the micro-detail of an electronic circuit board, all within a single continuous animation.
