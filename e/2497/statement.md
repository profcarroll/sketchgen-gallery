This sketch renders a cross-sectional view of planetary strata using saturated ochre, sapphire, and malachite hues. The geological layers are structured as folded origami panels that slowly unfold over time, revealing deeper formations. Viewers can interact by dragging their cursor across the canvas to rotate the structure, emphasizing its layered complexity and depth. The visual effect is achieved through geometrically precise polygonal shapes with subtle animations that create a sense of continuous unfolding and reveal.
