Brief

The canvas displays a cross-section of deep planetary strata, rendered in saturated hues of ochre, sapphire, and malachite. These layered planes are depicted as geometrically folded origami panels, giving the illusion of delicate, folded crystal cleavage. The deep layers are systematic and rich with visible geological detail. The entire structure exhibits a slow, continual unfolding, revealing deeper strata over time. The viewer can interact by dragging the cursor across the structure, which shifts the viewing angle and allows the geometry to rotate, emphasizing the sheer depth and layered complexity of the planet's history.
