This sketch creates an ever-shifting field of glowing, amorphous planes that drift across the screen with a mesmerizing rhythm. Each plane maintains its soft, luminous quality while gradually shifting from diffuse edges to more defined geometric forms through subtle distortions and transformations. The planes transition between shapes—ellipses, rectangles, and triangles—while floating in synchronized yet independent motion patterns. Their colors shift subtly in hue over time, creating a continuous visual narrative of organic softness and emerging geometric clarity. The effect is purely observational, requiring no input from the viewer as the planes continuously evolve through their own internal dynamics and interactions with each other.
