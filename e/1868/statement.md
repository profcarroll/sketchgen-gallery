This sketch explores the dynamic tension between stability and chaos through a continuous cycle of geometric transformation. It begins with a structured arrangement of angular forms suspended in apparent equilibrium, then triggers a dramatic collapse into fragmented pieces. These fragments scatter chaotically before rapidly reassembling into complex, repeating lattice patterns that fill the entire space. The visual rhythm captures the essence of structural evolution—where rigid order gives way to disorder and back again, creating an mesmerizing loop of form and transformation that unfolds autonomously across the canvas.
