This sketch presents a static comparison between a real landscape photograph and its painted interpretation. The left panel displays the original photograph at full resolution, while the right panel shows an abstracted version rendered through layered brushstrokes that mimic the colors and composition of the source image. The painting is generated by sampling color values from the photo and translating them into textured ellipses that vary in size and position to create a sense of artistic interpretation. The work explores how digital tools can simulate traditional media while preserving the essence of the original scene.
