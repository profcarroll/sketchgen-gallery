This sketch creates a dynamic, pulsating field of neon-lit geometric forms that cascade and intersect in three-dimensional space. The particles—comprising boxes, spheres, and cones—move in synchronized yet individual patterns, creating an evolving tapestry of light and structure. Each shape glows with vibrant hues from the HSB color space, while their constant rotation and cyclical motion generate a rhythmic dance across the canvas. The composition remains perpetually active, with no external input required, as each frame builds upon the last to create an endless cycle of intersecting light and form.
