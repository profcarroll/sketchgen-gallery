This sketch visualizes a dynamic combustion engine with four pistons moving rhythmically within their cylinders. The pistons oscillate vertically while fluid flows in and out through actuated intake valves timed with connecting rods. A spark explosion effect is simulated as pistons reach their peak, pushing the system downward and transferring kinetic energy to a visible transaxle and connected gears that rotate in response. The scene uses WebGL rendering for 3D depth and motion, with each component moving independently to create an immersive mechanical simulation.
