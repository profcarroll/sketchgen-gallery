This sketch presents a static, crystalline self-portrait composed of numerous interlocking planar facets arranged in three-dimensional space. Each facet is a flat polygon with subtle texture and carefully modulated lighting to simulate the appearance of etched metal or polished stone. The composition avoids smooth reflections in favor of broken, patterned specular highlights that reveal minute textural variations across surfaces. The fixed geometry allows viewers to examine how these highly defined materials alter perceived depth and reflective quality throughout the form, creating a sense of volumetric space without motion.
