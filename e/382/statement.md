This sketch depicts a dynamic cosmic system where self-contained crystalline shapes move through space, leaving behind dissipating energy trails. The shapes are constructed from sharp, faceted segments that emit internal luminescence, creating a sense of depth and motion. As they travel, the shapes form temporary connections with nearby structures, casting delicate webbing of light between them. The system uses simple physics for movement and collision detection, with each shape maintaining a trail that gradually fades out. Shadows are cast by the geometric forms to enhance the perception of three-dimensional depth and continuous motion across the canvas.
