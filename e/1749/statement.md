This sketch creates an ethereal atmosphere of swirling steam that continuously forms and dissolves into archaic glyphs. The visualization uses a particle system where individual particles flow with noise-based motion, gradually coalescing into stable, glowing formations. When enough particles gather and form a coherent structure, they stabilize into simple but recognizable archaic shapes that drift slowly across the screen. These glyph-like formations emit a soft internal luminescence before eventually dissolving back into the surrounding steam. The process creates an endless cycle of emergence and dissolution, evoking the mysterious quality of ancient scripts materializing from mist.
