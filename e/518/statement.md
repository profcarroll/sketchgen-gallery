This sketch creates a dense network of dark cables that form a complex web across the canvas. Within this framework, luminous data streams pulse and flow continuously, each with its own color, speed, and path. The visual design emphasizes clear separation between individual streams, especially where they intersect the cables, allowing viewers to trace distinct paths of energy moving through the system. The animation relies entirely on procedural movement and timing, creating an organic yet structured flow that fills the entire canvas with dynamic activity.
