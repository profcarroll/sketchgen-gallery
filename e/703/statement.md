This sketch creates a futuristic spaceship control panel with interactive switches and buttons arranged on a dark console. The interface features glowing red switches that light up when activated, and labeled buttons that respond to clicks. A central windscreen displays a starfield view that transitions dramatically when the correct sequence of switches and button presses is executed. The visualization simulates hyperspace travel through a shifting pattern of stars and geometric lines, creating an immersive sci-fi experience. The design uses p5.js's 2D drawing capabilities to create a detailed control panel with responsive elements and smooth visual transitions.
