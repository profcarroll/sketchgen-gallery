This sketch depicts a serene pastoral landscape with a stark contrast between foreground and background. The composition features rich, saturated green grass patches in the foreground that appear crisp and detailed under bright sunlight, rendered with individual vertical elements to emphasize texture and light. In the distance, a range of soft, muted mountains is rendered with layered transparency and atmospheric haze effects, creating a sense of depth and ethereal softness. The technique uses simple geometric shapes with color variations and transparency layers to simulate both the sharpness of near elements and the diffused quality of distant ones, achieving a strong sense of layered spatial depth without complex particle systems or computational overhead.
