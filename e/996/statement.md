This sketch presents a serene alpine winter landscape rendered in WEBGL with cold, muted tones. The scene features snow-draped rock channels and a crystalline atmosphere. When the viewer clicks the center of the canvas, an avalanche erupts, sending colorful mineral debris streaking across the exposed rock surfaces. The effect transforms the foreground's texture and color palette dramatically, showing how the harsh winter landscape responds to sudden natural forces. The animation uses particle systems and dynamic geometry to create a convincing sense of movement and change while maintaining performance within strict frame budget constraints.
