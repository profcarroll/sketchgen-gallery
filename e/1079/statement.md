This sketch renders a serene pastoral landscape at sunset using procedural techniques and layered shapes to create depth and texture. The composition begins with a sky gradient transitioning from warm highlights to deep shadows, followed by distant misty mountains that recede into the horizon. Mid-ground rolling hills are drawn with soft curves, while the foreground features detailed grass tufts and textured fields created through noise functions. Dramatic long shadows stretch across the landscape, enhancing the sense of depth and emphasizing the structural variation of the terrain under the warm glow of a fading sunset. The entire scene is rendered in a single static frame without animation, capturing a moment of quiet natural beauty.
