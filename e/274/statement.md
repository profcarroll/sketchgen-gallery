This sketch creates a dynamic system of interconnected geometric forms that continuously morph and evolve in response to structured oscillatory motion. Each form pulses and shifts position according to sine wave patterns, creating sharp, interlocking waveforms that govern their movement. The forms are connected by translucent lines that form a web-like structure, emphasizing the rhythmic relationships between them. Colors shift systematically through a defined palette, creating a rich visual experience where each element contributes to an overall pattern of motion and color. The system operates entirely autonomously, with no external input required—forms continuously generate new positions, sizes, and colors in a self-regulated dance that fills the entire canvas with evolving geometry.
