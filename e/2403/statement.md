This sketch renders a dense, interconnected network of nodes and connections that resembles a crystalline molecular structure or biological root system. The work begins in a static, serene configuration where colored nodes are linked by translucent lines forming an intricate lattice. When the viewer clicks anywhere on the canvas, the system responds with a dynamic reconfiguration: the clicked node and its neighbors shift subtly, causing adjacent connections to stretch and reform. This creates a cascade of movement that reveals the underlying stress points within the structure. The visualization emphasizes both the stability of the overall network and the localized disruption that occurs when a point is stimulated, creating a compelling interplay between order and transformation.
