This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of liquid light forms cascading downward in a dark space. The luminous sheets pulse rhythmically with emerald and cyan hues, moving smoothly with gentle ripples that swell and contract as they drift. Neon shadows reveal an underlying crystalline network structure through subtle connections between the forms. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of particle-based liquid forms, dynamic shadows, and geometric network connections rendered in WEBGL mode. The continuous, fluid motion creates an immersive experience where viewers observe shifting structures without any required interaction, with all animation happening autonomously across the canvas.
