This sketch creates a serene, flowing landscape of rhododendrons that appears to breathe and move with gentle, rhythmic waves. The individual blooms are rendered with layered petals and green foliage, each responding to subtle wave motions that simulate the slow current of wind through a field. The color palette is rich and saturated, evoking the lushness of spring growth. As the scene animates without any user interaction, the continuous motion gives the impression of natural life stirring in harmony, where every element contributes to an overall sense of calm vitality.
