This sketch creates a dynamic, sculptural scene centered around a glowing fire cube that rotates in space. Around this luminous core, five graceful emerald glass splines trace orbital paths, each maintaining its own curved trajectory while rotating together as a cohesive whole. The visual effect suggests depth and movement through the interplay of light, color, and form. The central fire cube is rendered with a transparent orange glow that emanates from within its structure, while the orbital splines appear crafted from rich emerald glass with subtle patina effects, their smooth curves creating an elegant sense of motion. The entire composition rotates slowly to showcase the interplay between the static fire core and the dynamic orbital elements, creating a mesmerizing sculpture in motion.
