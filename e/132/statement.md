This sketch creates an immersive, ever-shifting network of glowing lines that resembles a delicate spiderweb suspended in darkness. The structure is built from a lattice of points connected by lines that pulse through a vibrant color spectrum. Each connection subtly fades and reappears over time, creating an ephemeral visual effect where sections of the web momentarily dissolve before reconnecting. The continuous hue shifting gives the entire composition a hypnotic, living quality, as if the network is breathing or responding to some invisible force. Viewers witness a constantly evolving pattern of light and shadow that maintains its intricate weave while appearing transient and alive.
