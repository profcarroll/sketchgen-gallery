Brief

The scene presents a dizzying, immersive perspective within a colossal, speculative architectural space. Geometric planes define the environment, overlaid by glowing emerald green arterial seams that pulsate with visible, radiating waves of energy. These seams are complemented by complex, tessellating crystalline reflections, giving the appearance that the vibrant green light is being constantly fractured and redirected throughout the structure. The entire environment maintains a controlled but energetic dynamism, appearing to shift and breathe with rhythmic light pulses.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive, pulsing architectural space defined by geometric planes and glowing green energy waves. The scene features a series of rotating cube structures that shift subtly over time, overlaid with expanding radial pulses in emerald hues that simulate energy flow through the environment. Crystalline reflections appear as smaller, rapidly rotating boxes that fragment and redirect light throughout the space. The entire composition breathes with rhythmic motion, using only basic 3D primitives and simple animation techniques to create a sense of vast, living architecture.
