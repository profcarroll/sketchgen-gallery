This sketch creates an endlessly shifting kaleidoscope pattern inspired by the microscopic structure of a soap bubble. The visualization features radial symmetry with six-fold rotation, where each layer pulses and ripples in harmony with surrounding layers. The color palette transitions smoothly through warm sunset hues—deep reds, bright oranges, and molten yellows—creating a luminous glow effect that seems to emanate from within the bubble's surface. The pattern appears to magnify infinitely into a soapy surface, generating perpetual radial motion without any user interaction. The viewer observes an abstract, hypnotic field of glowing, pulsing circles that suggest movement deep within a luminous, microscopic space.
