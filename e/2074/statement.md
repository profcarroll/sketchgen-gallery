This sketch depicts a deep-sea ecosystem of luminous energy clusters that flow and pulse with rhythmic tension. The formations migrate through space like plankton swarms, driven by an underlying spring-like mechanism that creates subtle but continuous motion patterns. Each cluster pulses with its own rhythm while slowly shifting through a chromatic spectrum, creating an ethereal web of interconnected light. The visualization uses a combination of pulsation effects and spring-based physics to simulate the gentle, persistent currents of deep-sea trenches, where energy flows in rhythmic tension rather than chaotic motion. The clusters connect dynamically based on proximity, forming a living network that evolves over time without any external input.
