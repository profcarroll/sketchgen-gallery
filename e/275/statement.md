This sketch creates a structured composition inspired by Swiss Style poster aesthetics, using large geometric sans-serif letterforms arranged in a rigid grid. The design emphasizes high contrast with black type on white background and deliberate negative space. While maintaining strict horizontal alignment and optical rhythm, the letterforms subtly drift vertically in controlled, calculated disruptions that break the implied grid. Each letter's position varies slightly over time through sine wave functions, creating an intermittent, intentional instability that suggests motion without losing the overall compositional structure. The result is a static yet dynamic visual rhythm that embodies both order and gentle disruption characteristic of the Swiss Style movement.
