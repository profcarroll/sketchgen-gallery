This sketch renders a geometric sundial with three distinct solar sources positioned across the sky. Each sun orbits in its own circular path, casting intersecting shadows that dynamically shift and evolve across the hour markings of the sundial. The interplay of light and shadow creates a continuous temporal narrative, where multiple suns simultaneously illuminate and mark time on the stone surface. The visual effect emphasizes how different light sources can create complex shadow patterns that change continuously over time, illustrating not just the passage of hours but also the geometric relationships between celestial bodies and their projected shadows.
