This sketch creates a dynamic visual experience of a marble rolling through a complex, funnel-shaped channel using WebGL rendering. The scene features a continuous helical path that changes curvature, causing the marble to accelerate and decelerate naturally as it moves. The camera follows the marble's motion while emphasizing the dramatic shifts in momentum—particularly when the marble encounters sudden changes in slope that cause momentary pauses. The visualization demonstrates physics-based movement within a constrained environment, with smooth transitions between segments of the channel and realistic acceleration/deceleration effects. The entire scene operates autonomously without user input, continuously updating to show the marble's journey through the funnel structure.
