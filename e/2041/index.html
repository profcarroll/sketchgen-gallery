Brief

The canvas reveals a detailed, nostalgic landscape dominated by paper-cut shapes and intricate clockwork mechanisms. The scene features a sprawling city composed of gears, cogs, and aged wooden structures, all displaying the patina of time. Whimsical ships, crafted in the same paper-cut style, autonomously traverse this complex world. As they drift, they leave behind delicate wake trails that slowly dissipate. The entire canvas is in gentle, continuous motion, giving the viewer the impression of passing through a massive, operating timepiece.
