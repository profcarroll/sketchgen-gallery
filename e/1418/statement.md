This sketch creates an infinite tessellation of rotating hexagonal shapes using p5.js in WEBGL mode. Each hexagon has its own position, size, rotation speed, and color properties that evolve over time. The hexagons flow and rotate independently, creating a mesmerizing, breathing pattern across the canvas. The use of 3D rendering allows for dynamic camera movement and depth perception, while the continuous color shifts and varying transparency between adjacent cells give each shape a unique appearance within the collective pattern. The animation runs autonomously without any user interaction, fulfilling the requirement for self-contained motion.
