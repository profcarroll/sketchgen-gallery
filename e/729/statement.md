This sketch presents an isometric view of a multi-lane racetrack where marbles race autonomously, leaving behind glowing light trails that fade over time. The visualization uses WebGL rendering to create a three-dimensional perspective with bright, solid colors for the track and goal area. Each marble moves independently with varying speeds, following a dynamic path across the course while maintaining its own trail of luminous particles. The scene animates continuously without user input, showing the constant motion and formation of light trails that mark each marble's journey toward the goal. The visual effect emphasizes the competitive nature of the race through the interplay of movement, color, and the ephemeral quality of the glowing paths.
