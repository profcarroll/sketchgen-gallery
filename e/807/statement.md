This sketch creates an immersive, slowly shifting chromatic field using overlapping circular planes centered on the canvas. Each plane pulses with a gentle hue shift that originates at its center and fades outward, creating a sense of depth and emotional resonance. The composition avoids sharp boundaries or intense contrasts, instead using subtle transparency and gradual color transitions to suggest infinite layers of color moving in harmony. The viewer experiences a mesmerizing, almost meditative glow that emanates from the center, with each plane contributing to a unified field of shifting saturation and warmth.
