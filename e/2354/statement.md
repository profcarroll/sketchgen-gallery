This sketch creates a meditative visualization of fluid dynamics within a transparent vessel. The scene features amorphous blobs of highly viscous, colored fluid that drift slowly through deep space. These blobs continuously merge and reform, creating intricate internal boundaries that shift with complex color gradients and delicate striations. The motion is subtle yet persistent, driven by noise-based forces that ensure each blob evolves independently while maintaining the overall fluidic quality. The transparent vessel contains these patterns in a way that emphasizes both their organic movement and the deep space context they inhabit. The visualization requires no viewer interaction – it simply evolves on its own, offering a contemplative spectacle of constantly changing forms.
