Brief

The canvas displays an interlocking tessellation of hexagons. This geometric structure constantly morphs, rotating and shifting into asymmetrical clusters of varying sizes. The visual effects are rendered with a soft aesthetic, utilizing a vibrant, nostalgic 1980s color palette. The entire display is driven by continuous motion and is accompanied by a subtle, underlying soundtrack. No user input is required; the hypnotic patterns flow independently, creating a mesmerizing, evolving spectacle.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a mesmerizing, ever-shifting tessellation of interlocking hexagons that morphs continuously across the canvas. Using a vibrant 1980s color palette with neon pinks, electric blues, and sunny yellows, the pattern evolves through rotation, scaling, and positional shifts that form temporary clusters and asymmetrical arrangements. The motion is smooth and hypnotic, driven entirely by mathematical transformations within the draw loop without any user input required. A subtle ambient tone plays in the background to enhance the dreamlike quality of the visual experience.
