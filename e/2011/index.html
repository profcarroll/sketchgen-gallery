Brief

A technical architectural blueprint presented as a 3D wireframe on a deep navy blue background. Thin, bright cyan lines trace the floor plans, walls, and structural elements of a multi-level building in an isometric perspective. A fine, subtle grid pattern provides scale in the background. Dragging the mouse rotates the camera around the structure, allowing for an orbital view of the architectural layout.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch renders a technical architectural blueprint in a 3D isometric perspective, depicting a multi-level building structure. The visualization uses thin, bright cyan lines to trace floor plans, walls, and structural elements against a deep navy blue background. Users can orbit the camera by dragging their mouse across the canvas, enabling exploration of the architectural layout from different angles. The implementation leverages p5.js WEBGL mode for 3D rendering, with a grid-based background providing scale reference. The camera rotation is implemented using spherical coordinates to maintain consistent orientation during interaction.
