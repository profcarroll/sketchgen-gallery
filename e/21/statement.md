This sketch creates an immersive urban environment that transitions through a full day-night cycle. Using procedural generation, it builds a cityscape with angular buildings and organic plant life that responds to changing light conditions. The scene features dynamic color shifts from dawn's warm hues to sunset's deep purples, casting realistic shadows across structures. Organic elements sway gently in the wind, creating subtle movement that brings the artificial environment to life. The entire scene evolves automatically without user interaction, demonstrating how atmospheric changes can transform an urban setting into a living, breathing space.
