Brief

The canvas displays a vast, angular cityscape viewed through the cyclical changes of day and night. The primary focus is the gradual, immersive shift of light, moving from the warm glow of dawn through midday to the deep hues of sunset. This atmospheric progression casts dynamic color and shadows across the towering structures. Counterbalancing the rigid geometry of the buildings are delicate, vibrant pockets of organic plant life, which appear to weave and grow within the cracks and ledges. The scene feels alive due to subtle, continuous movements—the changing light, swaying vegetation, and procedural atmospheric effects—creating the constant illusion that the city is genuinely inhabited.
