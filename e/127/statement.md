This sketch creates a three-dimensional physics simulation of a marble rolling inside a funnel-shaped container. Using WebGL rendering, it displays a transparent funnel structure against which a red marble autonomously moves under the influence of gravity. The marble's motion is physically realistic, bouncing and sliding along the funnel's curved surfaces according to calculated collision normals. The scene continuously animates without user interaction, showing the marble's natural descent as it follows the laws of physics within the confined space.
