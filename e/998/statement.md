This sketch presents a dynamic, cross-sectional view of an internal combustion engine in motion. It visualizes the core mechanical components including pistons, connecting rods, and a crankshaft, all synchronized in their movement. The central focus is the timing gear train that connects the crankshaft to valve mechanisms, showing how rotational motion is translated into precise valve actuation. The animation demonstrates the coordinated timing required for each cylinder's intake and exhaust valves to open and close at the correct moments during engine operation. The visual design emphasizes clarity and mechanical accuracy, with the engine's internal components rendered in a clean, technical style that reveals their functional relationships without unnecessary detail.
