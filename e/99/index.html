Brief

The canvas displays a beautifully rendered, three-dimensional representation of the solar system. Planets orbit around a luminous central star, all observed with dynamic, changing light sources that cast realistic shadows across the orbiting celestial bodies. When the user presses the mouse down, the entire system initiates a visible, dynamic animation, causing the planets and their orbits to move and react within the light field.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a dynamically rendered 3D solar system using p5.js's WEBGL mode. The scene features a luminous central star around which eight planets orbit at different speeds and distances. Each celestial body is rendered with distinct colors and sizes, including Saturn with its characteristic rings. The lighting changes over time to simulate realistic shadowing and illumination effects. Upon clicking the canvas, the entire system initiates a new animation mode where all celestial bodies rotate more rapidly, creating an engaging visual experience that responds dynamically to user interaction while maintaining a scientifically inspired aesthetic.
