This sketch renders a stylized isometric server room with metallic racks and flowing data ribbons that trace dynamic paths between the towers. The scene features cool cyan and blue lighting throughout, which shifts to warm orange and yellow as the ribbons converge at pulsating hotspots located on the racks. The architecture remains fixed while the data flow continuously evolves, creating a rhythmic visual pulse across the entire composition. The piece explores the aesthetic of digital infrastructure through abstracted, flowing forms that suggest both the static structure and dynamic information flow within data centers.
