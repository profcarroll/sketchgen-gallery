This sketch creates a mesmerizing, self-evolving field of geometric forms that pulse and ripple across the screen in harmonious synchronization. Using WebGL rendering, it generates a tessellated landscape of abstract shapes that warp and morph over time through sine-wave distortions. The color palette shifts continuously, creating a chromatic dance that flows seamlessly between hues. The entire composition is algorithmically driven, with no user input required — each frame builds upon the last to form an ever-changing abstraction that feels both structured and alive.
