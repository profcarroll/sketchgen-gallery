Brief

The canvas displays a dense, continuously evolving field of complex geometric shapes and abstract lines. These forms exhibit dynamic, warping distortions that tessellate across the plane. The shapes do not simply move; they pulse with controlled, periodic pulsations and ripple through the space in a precise, sine-wave-driven manner.
