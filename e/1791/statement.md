This sketch depicts a cosmic scene of particles suspended in deep space, each tracing an orbital path around a central gravitational point. The orbits exhibit subtle wobbling due to periodic perturbations, creating visually interesting, non-uniform motion patterns. Each particle leaves behind a glowing trail that gradually fades, forming a luminous history of its journey. The visual effect is achieved by rendering all particles and their trails in batches using WebGL's efficient batch drawing capabilities, ensuring smooth performance even with hundreds of animated elements.
