This sketch presents a still, highly detailed mechanical portrait rendered in the style of industrial decay. The composition features a complex humanoid structure composed of interlocking panels and joints, rendered with aged metallic gradients that shift from oxidized browns to tarnished greens. Surface textures are created through thousands of subtle elements—micro-fractures, patinas, and panel seams—that together form an intricate portrait of advanced mechanical wear. The work is deliberately static, observing the quiet decay of a forgotten machine.
