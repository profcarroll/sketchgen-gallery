This sketch creates an abstract visualization of bioluminescent mangrove root structures in a tide pool environment. Using organic geometric forms and flowing particles, it depicts the interconnected, pulsating network of roots illuminated by soft, natural glows. The scene evokes a confined, glowing space where light appears to flow through dark root structures, responding to an unseen celestial force with rhythmic pulsations and subtle movements. The visualization uses WEBGL rendering to create depth and volume while maintaining performance within strict frame budget constraints.
