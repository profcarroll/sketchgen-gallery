This sketch creates a dynamic field of light composed of hundreds of glowing particles that move continuously across the canvas. Each particle leaves a fading trail behind it, creating a sense of motion and energy. The particles repel each other within a certain radius, generating wavy, flowing patterns as they navigate around one another. Occasionally, clusters form where multiple particles come together temporarily due to the interplay of their repulsive forces, creating bright knots that slowly dissipate. The visual effect conveys both individual movement and collective behavior, with the repulsion force being the primary dynamic element shaping the field's structure and flow.
