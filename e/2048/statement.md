This sketch transforms the rigid structure of a chessboard into an organic, pulsing root system that spreads across the canvas. The piece visualizes bioluminescent tendrils emerging from a knitted wool-like texture, creating an ethereal underwater scene where life blossoms slowly and rhythmically. Each root grows organically through the space, leaving trails that pulse with color and intensity. The overall effect suggests submerged vegetation or vascular networks, with the initial grid structure gradually dissolving into flowing organic forms that fill the canvas autonomously.
