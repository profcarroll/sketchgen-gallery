This sketch creates an evolving arid landscape with deep terracotta earth tones and a contrasting beige sky. Using procedural noise techniques, it generates richly textured terrain with visible cracks and fissures that suggest extreme dryness. The scene features subtle dust devils that drift across the surface, occasionally organizing into larger swirling patterns. The continuous motion and atmospheric effects create a sense of persistent, slow forces acting upon the desolate earth, capturing both the stillness and underlying dynamism of such environments. The visual evolution occurs naturally through time-based animation without external input, fulfilling the requirement for autonomous canvas change.
