This sketch creates a mesmerizing radial animation of concentric rings expanding from the center of the canvas. Each ring starts small and accelerates outward at a different rate, creating a sense of dynamic motion that builds momentum as it progresses. The rings maintain consistent color and form while continuously growing, producing an effect where the spacing between rings decreases rapidly as they approach the edges of the view. The pattern evolves organically without any external input, fulfilling the requirement for autonomous visual motion. The technique uses simple geometric shapes and controlled acceleration to achieve a calming yet engaging visual rhythm.
