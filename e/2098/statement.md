This sketch depicts the cross-section of an ancient wall under ongoing construction, rendered in the visual style of Edo period woodblock prints. The composition shows geometric tiles—representing construction elements—that appear to be settling and interlocking over time. Using a subtle animation technique, each tile gently moves and shifts into place, simulating the slow, methodical process of building. The aesthetic draws from the clean lines and minimal ink usage typical of woodblock printing, with soft shadows and texture lines that evoke traditional craftsmanship. The viewer observes an architectural process in motion, where tiles gradually find their positions within a larger structure, suggesting both the permanence and the continuous activity of construction.
