This sketch creates an interactive stovetop interface with a central dial that controls multiple burners. When you drag the dial, it illuminates burners in a circular arrangement and generates realistic gas flames that change color intensity based on the dial position. The entire burner assembly glows with a warm red ambient light that responds to flame activity. The visualization uses shadow effects to create depth and realism, simulating how heat radiates from the metal surface. The interaction is intuitive: turning the dial clockwise activates burners in sequence while adjusting flame color temperature from blue (lower) to orange-red (higher).
