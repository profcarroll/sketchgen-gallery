This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic environment where translucent, overlapping circles drift slowly across a dark void. The scene is punctuated by dissipating flares that emanate expanding rings, which subtly shift the colors of nearby circles as they pass through. These glowing afterglows — both their central flashes and surrounding expanding rings — move continuously and randomly, creating an ambient, evolving composition. The visual effect emphasizes fluidity and transformation, with color changes appearing as if the cosmic structures themselves are responding to invisible energy waves.
