Brief

The scene depicts a deep-sea abyss where geometrically precise, crystalline pyramidal structures rise from the seafloor. The process of deep-sea currents interacting with these formations causes them to shed visible, luminescent residue. This glowing detritus is constantly moved by the currents, drifting and accumulating across the abyssal plain. The shifting flow guides the particles, creating ethereal trails and glowing clusters of light that traverse the dark, deep environment.
