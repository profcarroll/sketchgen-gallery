Brief

The canvas displays a deep, layered cityscape viewed across the horizon. The primary movement is the continuous, cyclical transition of the sky's colors, shifting dramatically from cool morning tones through vibrant midday hues to deep twilight shades. The buildings are rendered as rigid, angular shapes against this colorful backdrop. The sense of life and habitation is generated by procedural light sources—such as countless windows or street lamps—that rhythmically blink on and off or subtly pulse in color. The entire scene undergoes slow, continuous atmospheric shifts, mimicking the passage of time with no required user interaction.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a dynamic, time-evolving cityscape where the sky transitions through a full day-night cycle without user input. Buildings appear as geometric silhouettes against shifting color gradients, while countless windows pulse rhythmically to simulate urban life. The entire scene progresses autonomously, with atmospheric changes and light patterns creating a sense of passage and habitation. The effect relies on procedural generation and continuous animation to maintain visual interest.
