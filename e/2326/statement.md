This sketch depicts a continuous, flowing vortex of semi-transparent textual characters that swirl and shift in an eternal dance. At irregular intervals, the flow temporarily slows and characters coalesce into clear, sequential lines of text—revealing legible messages before dissolving back into the stream. The effect evokes geological deposition, manifesting as shifting mineral crystallization made of wet ink on newsprint, where organized structure emerges from chaos and then dissipates like sediment settling into new forms. The viewer witnesses both the fluid motion of language in motion and its momentary clarity before returning to formlessness.
