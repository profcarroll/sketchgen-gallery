This sketch creates an interactive audio visualization that responds to user input through mouse movements. When clicked, it generates a sine wave tone whose frequency and amplitude are controlled by horizontal and vertical mouse position respectively. The visualization displays both the current frequency value in large text and a dynamic waveform that fluctuates with the sound's characteristics. As users move their mouse, they can hear pitch changes while watching the waveform morphology shift, creating an immersive connection between auditory and visual feedback. The design emphasizes clean, minimal representation of complex audio data through simple geometric forms that respond directly to sound properties.
