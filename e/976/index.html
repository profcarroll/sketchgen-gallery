Brief

The canvas displays a dynamic, generative visual representation of real-time sound. The lower portion of the screen is filled with a fluctuating waveform display, whose shape and color change in direct relation to the sound being generated. Above this visualization, a large text display prominently shows the current frequency value. The sketch remains still until the user clicks, which initiates the sound. After activation, moving the mouse horizontally controls the pitch, changing both the frequency number and the shape of the waveform. Moving the mouse vertically controls the volume, causing the amplitude and intensity of the visual display to change instantly.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an interactive audio visualization that responds to user input through mouse movements. When clicked, it generates a sine wave tone whose frequency and amplitude are controlled by horizontal and vertical mouse position respectively. The visualization displays both the current frequency value in large text and a dynamic waveform that fluctuates with the sound's characteristics. As users move their mouse, they can hear pitch changes while watching the waveform morphology shift, creating an immersive connection between auditory and visual feedback. The design emphasizes clean, minimal representation of complex audio data through simple geometric forms that respond directly to sound properties.
