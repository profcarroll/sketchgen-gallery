This sketch presents a cutaway view of an internal combustion engine in motion, illustrating the conversion of gas pressure into mechanical rotation. The viewer observes a piston moving up and down within a cylinder, with combustion gases visibly expanding to push the piston downward. This linear motion is transferred through a connecting rod to a rotating crankshaft, demonstrating the fundamental energy conversion process. The animation uses WebGL rendering to create a 3D cross-sectional representation, showing the interplay between mechanical components in real-time motion.
