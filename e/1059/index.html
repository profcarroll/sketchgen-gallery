Brief

The view is a dynamic, cutaway cross-section showing the inner workings of a running internal combustion engine. The viewer observes pistons cycling within cylinders. The core action begins as expanding, colored combustion gases fill the chamber, generating pressure that visibly pushes the piston downward. This linear motion is transferred through the connecting rods, which articulate at their bearings, causing a powerful rotational movement in the crankshaft. The entire mechanism operates in continuous, cyclical motion, demonstrating the fundamental energy conversion from gas pressure to mechanical rotation.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a cutaway view of an internal combustion engine in motion, illustrating the conversion of gas pressure into mechanical rotation. The viewer observes a piston moving up and down within a cylinder, with combustion gases visibly expanding to push the piston downward. This linear motion is transferred through a connecting rod to a rotating crankshaft, demonstrating the fundamental energy conversion process. The animation uses WebGL rendering to create a 3D cross-sectional representation, showing the interplay between mechanical components in real-time motion.
