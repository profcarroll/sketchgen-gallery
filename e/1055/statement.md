This sketch creates a dynamic, interactive field of colorful particles that respond to user input with chaos and structure. When idle, the particles move randomly in an energetic, unpredictable pattern. Upon dragging the mouse, the particles scatter outward from the interaction point, forming temporary glowing geometric lattices that pulse and fade. These structures guide nearby particles into more organized arrangements before the entire field resolves into a crystalline tapestry radiating from the point of disturbance. The visual effect combines organic motion with geometric order, creating an immersive experience where chaos transforms into harmony through user interaction.
