This sketch renders a static, geometric self-portrait composed of interlocking cubic facets arranged in 3D space. Each block is positioned and rotated to create a sense of depth and angular complexity, with careful lighting that emphasizes sharp edges and defined shadow boundaries. The composition avoids animation or motion, relying entirely on the arrangement and rendering of solid planes to evoke a sculptural quality. The use of WebGL allows for precise control over lighting and perspective, creating an abstracted yet structured representation that focuses on form and negative space rather than realistic likeness.
