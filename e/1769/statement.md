This sketch depicts an autonomous cycle of cellular replication where a single cell grows and contracts before splitting into two daughter cells. The visualization captures the biological process through abstract animation: cells expand smoothly until they reach a critical size, then undergo dramatic contraction forming a visible pinching point. At the moment of separation, this constriction pulses with bright light, emphasizing the membrane division. Each new cell inherits the properties of its parent and begins the cycle anew, creating a continuous, self-sustaining pattern of growth, division, and renewal across the canvas. The system is entirely self-contained, requiring no external input once initiated.
