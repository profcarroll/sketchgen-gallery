This sketch portrays the quiet, patient process of crystallization within petrified sediment. The scene depicts a deep, still void where mineral matter slowly coalesces into geometrically precise structures. Each crystal begins as a point and gradually grows through time, pulsing with a subtle rhythm that reflects its internal structural development. The sediment substrate provides a muted foundation against which these luminous formations emerge, creating a sense of profound accumulation over geological time. The viewer observes the slow, methodical transformation of ephemeral material into enduring crystalline form, where light and structure interplay in a serene, contemplative space.
