This sketch renders a detailed, animated cross-section of a four-stroke combustion engine using p5.js in WEBGL mode. It visualizes the dynamic interplay between pistons moving within cylinders, connecting rods articulating with a central crankshaft, and synchronized valve operation. Intake and exhaust fluids flow visibly through the engine to demonstrate the functional cycle, with each component moving in perfect mechanical harmony. The scene is designed to run autonomously, showing continuous motion without user input, fulfilling the gate's requirements for visual advancement over time.
