This visualization presents a slow, organic network of faint, glowing mycelial tracery etched into the textured background of corrugated cardboard. The sketch creates a dense field of branching structures that subtly pulse and reorganize over time, evoking the geological rhythm of underground biological processes. Each tracery path is composed of multiple points that gently animate through space, creating an effect of continuous, imperceptible movement that suggests deep time's passage. The cardboard texture provides a tactile, material foundation while the faint, glowing lines create an ethereal contrast, illustrating how complex, fibrous patterns can emerge from simple rules and time-based animation.
