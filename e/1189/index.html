Brief

Colorful, interlocking jigsaw pieces are scattered across the canvas, each showing a unique fragment of a larger, cohesive image. A viewer can click and drag individual pieces to reposition them. When a piece is moved close to its intended location, it snaps into its correct place, eventually forming the complete picture.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an interactive jigsaw puzzle experience where nine colored pieces are randomly scattered across the canvas. Each piece displays a unique number and contains a fragment of what will eventually form a complete image when properly assembled. Viewers can click and drag individual pieces to reposition them. When a piece is moved close enough to its intended location, it automatically snaps into place, gradually forming the complete picture. The implementation uses a simple but effective snapping mechanism that responds to user interaction while maintaining visual feedback during dragging operations.
