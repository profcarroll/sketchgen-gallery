This sketch creates an immersive tunnel experience through abstract molecular space using particle systems and dynamic distortions. The viewer feels accelerated motion as countless particles flow toward them, forming a structured yet ever-shifting landscape of atomic forms. At irregular intervals, violent crystalline distortions erupt, causing the central structures to shatter into glowing shards that scatter away from the main flow. The visual effect emphasizes the tension between order and chaos, where delicate molecular arrangements suddenly collapse into luminous fragments, creating a sense of overwhelming structural decay and reformation. The technique uses particle systems with noise-based movement, periodic distortion events, and dynamic shard generation to achieve this immersive passage through abstract atomic space.
