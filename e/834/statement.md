This sketch creates a dynamic urban environment observed from above, where buildings cast shifting shadows across wet pavement as the time of day progresses. The scene uses WebGL rendering to create a vast cityscape with volumetric structures and realistic lighting. Buildings are procedurally generated with varying heights and positions, casting dynamic shadows that move with a simulated sun. Water puddles appear on the ground, reflecting the surrounding environment and creating visual interest through their wet, glossy surfaces. The entire scene evolves autonomously without user input, demonstrating the passage of time through changing light angles and shadow positions.
