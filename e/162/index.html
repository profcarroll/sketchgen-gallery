Brief

The canvas presents a complex, three-dimensional mechanical scene centered around a contained fire source. Around this fire, a brass-toned cube rotates constantly, serving as the anchor point. Multiple sinuous splines and detailed globes appear attached to or orbiting this rotating cube. These globes and splines exhibit smooth, cyclical motion, evoking the intricate, intricate mechanisms of a steampunk design. The visualization is a dynamic, self-contained ballet of orbiting shapes, geometric rotations, and flickering light.

This sketch creates a steampunk-inspired mechanical scene centered around a rotating brass cube that serves as the core element. Multiple globes and splines orbit and move in synchronized, cyclical patterns around this central anchor. The entire composition is rendered in 3D space using WebGL, with smooth, continuous motion that evokes the intricate and dynamic nature of mechanical engineering. The visualization pulses with a subtle flicker, suggesting an inner fire or energy source, while maintaining a consistent rhythm that draws the viewer into its orbital ballet.
