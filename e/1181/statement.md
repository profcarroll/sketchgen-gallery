This sketch creates a mesmerizing tessellation of interlocking hexagons that gradually decay from the edges toward the center. The geometric lattice pulses with glowing electrical energy that drives the structure into a state of controlled erosion. Each hexagon maintains its individual integrity while slowly losing life, creating an organic sense of dissolution. The electrical pulses emanate randomly from the lattice points, creating a dynamic field of energy that both defines and threatens the structure. The visual effect suggests a system under stress, with energy flows that create tension and release, ultimately leading to a systematic breakdown of the ordered pattern.
