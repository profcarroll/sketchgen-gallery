This sketch renders an immersive, cathedral-scale void filled with petrified root systems that pulse and snap with mechanical energy. Through a first-person perspective gliding through dark space, viewers experience sharp, luminous linear filaments that move with rhythmic tension—like springs under stress. The visual effect emerges from thousands of individual root segments drawn as connected lines, each with subtle spring-like deformation and pulsing luminosity that creates an organic yet mechanical atmosphere. The scene's continuous motion builds a sense of automated progression through this ancient, glowing landscape.
