This sketch creates an immersive, animated view of a massive printing press in three-dimensional space. Using p5.js's WEBGL renderer, it depicts the intricate machinery of gears, rollers, and paper sheets in motion, with detailed textures and lighting effects that give a sense of industrial scale and mechanical rhythm. The scene continuously evolves with rotating components and moving papers, while a single click toggles between an active animation state and a paused focus mode, allowing viewers to examine the press's details more closely.
