This sketch depicts a futuristic medical visualization where an erratic EKG pattern scrolls continuously across a dark screen. The neon yellow line traces unpredictable heartbeats with sudden spikes and dips, rendered with a granular beeswax texture that pulses and expands organically. The effect combines smooth scrolling motion with chaotic particle interactions to create a dynamic, almost living representation of cardiac activity. The viewer witnesses an abstract yet recognizable portrayal of life's vital rhythms, rendered through the contrast of precise digital line work and organic, breathing particles.
