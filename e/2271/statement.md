This sketch depicts a vast underground root system glowing with cyan light, suspended in darkness. The branching network pulses organically as if breathing, while a jagged crack spreads across the canvas, fracturing the view. When clicked, the crack splinters aggressively and the entire wireframe structure vibrates with energy. The work uses p5.js's WEBGL mode to create a sense of depth and scale in an environment that feels both alien and familiar – like the interior of a massive cavern or the root system of some enormous subterranean organism. The visual rhythm of growth and fracture creates tension between organic life and geological disruption.
