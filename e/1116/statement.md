This sketch generates a dynamic, autonomous visual landscape where violet seismic waves fracture and pulse across the canvas. Using a particle system, each wave originates from a random point and evolves independently through time, growing, rotating, and shrinking in rhythmic patterns. The waves are composed of jagged, polygonal shapes that break apart and reform autonomously without any user input or interaction. The continuous evolution of these forms creates an ever-shifting fracturing rhythm that embodies the essence of uncontrolled, self-sustained motion.
