This sketch creates a serene sky scene populated by colorful hot air balloons that drift gently across the canvas. Each balloon has its own unique hue, size, and drifting pattern, creating a naturalistic flow. The balloons are rendered with a soft, glowing aura that emanates in distinct segments around their edges, giving them the appearance of structured light columns trailing from their outer boundaries. The animation is continuous and self-contained, requiring no user interaction beyond the initial load. The visual effect combines simple geometric forms with subtle motion to evoke a peaceful, dreamy atmosphere reminiscent of a calm sky filled with floating vessels.
