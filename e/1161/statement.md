This sketch creates a dynamic field of pulsating forms that expand and fade across a black canvas. Each pulse originates from a random point, grows rapidly, and leaves a persistent trail behind it. When pulses encounter the edges of the canvas, they distort into jagged, asymmetric ripples, breaking their circular symmetry. The system uses hundreds of independent pulses that drift and scatter without user interaction, creating an ever-changing composition filled with rhythmic motion and visual complexity. The subtle color shifts and varying speeds generate a sense of organic life within the minimalist black-and-white space.
