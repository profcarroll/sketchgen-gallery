This sketch creates an ethereal, ever-shifting lattice of glowing lines that pulse and flow across the canvas. The system simulates a dynamic energy field where lines continuously form, break apart, and reform, leaving behind vivid color residues that linger and influence the next generation of connections. These residues appear as saturated streaks that guide the reformation of the network, creating a visual narrative of continuous transformation and transient beauty. The result is a mesmerizing display of interwoven light trails that feel both organic and algorithmic, constantly in motion without ever becoming chaotic or overwhelming.
