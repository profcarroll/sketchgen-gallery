This sketch simulates the organic spread of sepia-toned watercolor ink on damp newsprint. Using a particle system with thousands of individually animated elements, it creates a chaotic yet fluid motion that mimics how ink seeps and spreads across porous paper. The muted earth tones—ranging from deep browns to warm ochres—emerge and blend organically as particles move, cohere slightly, and gradually fade into the background texture. Each particle behaves according to simple physics rules, moving with subtle randomness while slowly spreading and blending, creating an ever-shifting field of pigment that captures both the delicate delicacy and the accumulative nature of watercolor on damp paper.
