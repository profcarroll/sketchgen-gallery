This sketch creates an immersive, shifting aquamarine luminous field that evokes the serene beauty of submerged geological formations. Through a combination of slowly moving particles and floating crystalline forms, it simulates the interaction of light with mineral surfaces. The floating quartz-like crystals rotate gently while casting soft internal glows, and countless translucent particles drift through the field like suspended sediment. The subtle color variations and dynamic movement create an atmosphere of quiet underwater exploration, where light and form merge into a unified glowing environment. The visual effect is achieved through careful layering of translucent shapes and continuous animation that maintains a sense of perpetual motion without overwhelming complexity.
