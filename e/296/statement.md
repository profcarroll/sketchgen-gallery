This sketch visualizes market volatility through dynamic movement and interaction. Abstract red and green nodes drift across the canvas, while fluctuating lines weave between them. When a line passes near a node, it triggers an expanding ring effect that radiates outward and fades quickly. The constant motion and spontaneous interactions create a sense of unpredictability and change, reflecting the nature of financial markets. The scene evolves organically without external input, demonstrating how simple rules can generate complex, visually engaging behavior.
