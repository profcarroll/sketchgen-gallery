This sketch creates a continuous, self-contained animation of seismic activity and geometric fracturing. Violet-hued waves ripple outward from multiple points across a white canvas, expanding and contracting in rhythmic cycles. Simultaneously, crystalline hexagonal grids pulse and rotate independently, growing and shrinking in place without any user interaction. The composition maintains sharp angular forms while evolving through time, creating a dynamic interplay between organic waveforms and precise geometric structures. The animation proceeds entirely on its own, demonstrating continuous motion and visual change without requiring mouse input or external triggers.
