This sketch creates an immersive deep space nebula effect using hundreds of luminous orbs that drift and pulse independently across the screen. Each orb has its own size, color, movement pattern, and pulsing rhythm, creating a dynamic, organic field of light that responds to viewer interaction. When a user drags their mouse across the canvas, nearby orbs react by scattering outward and brightening, creating fluid light trails that emphasize the interconnectedness of the cosmic field. The visual effect is achieved through careful use of p5.js's color blending, transparency, and shadow effects to create soft glows and smooth motion, while maintaining performance within the strict frame budget constraints.
