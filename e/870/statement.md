This sketch creates a mesmerizing lattice of interlocking hexagons in perpetual motion. Each hexagon rotates and shifts subtly over time, creating an ever-changing pattern that feels both geometrically precise and fluidly organic. The network maintains its integrity while periodically fracturing—each hexagon momentarily breaks its form before reforming—creating a sense of fragile, living structure. The visual rhythm combines steady, hypnotic motion with sudden bursts of disruption, evoking the tension between order and chaos in dynamic systems.
