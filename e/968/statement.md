This sketch creates an interactive grid of musical pads that respond to user input with both visual and auditory feedback. The pads pulse gently in a steady beat, establishing a clear rhythmic tempo, while individual clicks trigger bright flash effects on the clicked pad and its immediate neighbors. Each pad plays a unique synthesized tone based on its position in the grid, creating an engaging interface where users can create simple musical sequences by clicking through the pads. The visual design uses color to distinguish each pad while maintaining a cohesive aesthetic that emphasizes the rhythmic nature of the interaction.
