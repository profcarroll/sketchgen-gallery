This sketch creates a dynamic, abstract visualization of underground hydrology that resembles etched ceramic tile patterns. The composition features an intricate network of branching lines that flow and pulse with organic rhythm, mimicking the movement of subterranean water systems. Through recursive line generation and subtle color shifts, it captures both the rigid geometric structure and fluid motion of underground river channels. The tessellated pattern emerges from countless interconnected segments that branch and diverge, creating a sense of continuous, self-sustaining flow across the canvas surface.
