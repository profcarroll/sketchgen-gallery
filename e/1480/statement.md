This sketch creates a mesmerizing snow globe interior where ethereal curtains of neon green and deep sapphire blue flow and pulse gently in slow motion. The aurora-like drapery moves continuously through a procedurally generated pattern, creating a celestial ambiance within the enclosed spherical world. Thousands of miniature snowflakes drift downward in soft trails, each following its own subtle sway path to create organic movement. When viewers drag across the canvas, the snow intensifies and swirls dynamically, causing the internal lights to gather into swirling bursts that respond to the interaction. The effect is achieved through efficient batching techniques, avoiding frame budget violations while maintaining the dreamy, interactive quality of a physical snow globe.
