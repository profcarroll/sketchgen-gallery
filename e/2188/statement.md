This sketch renders an immersive, self-contained visualization of deep-sea siphonophore crystalline structures at the scale of mountain ranges. Using WebGL rendering, it creates complex interlocking geometric forms that shift organically through time, evoking the skeletal architecture of these marine creatures. The bioluminescent color palette of deep sapphire, glowing cyan, and electric emerald pulses with subtle rhythmic motion, creating an organic, living system. Each structure is composed of multiple intersecting planes that rotate independently, forming intricate crystalline networks that float in three-dimensional space. The scene features constant, slow movement that creates a sense of depth and scale while maintaining the viewer's immersion in this alien underwater world.
