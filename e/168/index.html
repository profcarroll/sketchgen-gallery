Brief

The viewer is presented with a vast, impossible cityscape formed from fractured geometric planes. The structures appear non-Euclidean, suggesting a transition between worlds. The entire scene is bathed in soft, muted teal light, giving the planes a reflective, aqueous quality. Over time, the invisible laws of geometry rearrange themselves; the planes subtly shift, tilt, and refract light, causing the impossible architecture to breathe with slow, ambient motion. The viewer's role is purely observational, immersed in the continuous, silent decay and reorganization of the dreamscape.

This sketch presents an impossible cityscape composed of fractured geometric planes that subtly shift and refract over time. Using WebGL rendering, it creates a dreamlike architecture where the rules of Euclidean geometry dissolve into something more fluid and aqueous. The muted teal lighting gives the scene a reflective quality, and the slow, ambient motion suggests a continuous decay and reorganization of space. Viewers are immersed in an ever-changing dreamscape that breathes with quiet, silent transformation.
