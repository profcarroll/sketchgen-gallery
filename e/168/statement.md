This sketch presents an impossible cityscape composed of fractured geometric planes that subtly shift and refract over time. Using WebGL rendering, it creates a dreamlike architecture where the rules of Euclidean geometry dissolve into something more fluid and aqueous. The muted teal lighting gives the scene a reflective quality, and the slow, ambient motion suggests a continuous decay and reorganization of space. Viewers are immersed in an ever-changing dreamscape that breathes with quiet, silent transformation.
