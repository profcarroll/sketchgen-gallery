This sketch visualizes a complex network of suspended cables and data streams in three-dimensional space. The scene features hundreds of cables of varying thicknesses connecting random points in space, forming an intricate mesh structure. Luminous data streams flow continuously along these cables, appearing as glowing pulses that travel the full length of each cable regardless of its thickness. The visualization uses a pulsing animation technique where the streams move rhythmically along the cable paths, creating a sense of continuous energy flowing through the interconnected system. The entire scene rotates slowly to provide a comprehensive view of this three-dimensional network structure.
