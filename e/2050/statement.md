This sketch visualizes the concept of deep time through an evolving tapestry of interwoven threads that shift and flow across the canvas. Each thread is a complex, organic form composed of multiple connected segments that move according to a subtle noise-based force field, creating patterns reminiscent of a loom's shuttle weaving through time. The continuous motion suggests both the gradual transformation of landscapes and the rhythmic repetition of textile craft, with threads fading in and out of visibility to create an impression of vast, cyclical pressure. The HSBC color space provides rich, saturated hues that deepen and shift over time, enhancing the meditative quality of the visualization.
