This sketch creates an immersive architectural environment representing the interior of New Babylon. Through geometric structures and pulsating energy flows, it visualizes a controlled luminous dynamism where structural planes are laced with emerald green energy seams that pulse and radiate waves across surfaces. The scene features charged nodes at major junctions where energy concentrates, surrounded by crystalline reflections that fracture and distort under intense energy buildup. Using WebGL rendering, the composition fills the entire canvas with dynamic geometry that evolves continuously without user input, creating a sense of vast, living architecture in constant motion.
