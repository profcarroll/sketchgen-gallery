This sketch renders an underwater cityscape where mineral-rich architectural elements form a skeletal framework against deep ocean blues and violets. The composition features repeating geometric patterns that interweave throughout the rigid structure, creating a sense of monumental, otherworldly geometry. Vibrant bioluminescent stained glass patterns pulse gently across the scene, enhancing the fantastical atmosphere. The work uses a grid-based approach to organize architectural elements and employs subtle animation to create an immersive environment that evolves without external input.
