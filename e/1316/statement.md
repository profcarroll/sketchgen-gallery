This sketch creates a dramatic lighthouse scene where a sweeping beam of light cuts through turbulent fog. The fog particles swirl chaotically across the canvas using simple physics for motion, with each particle having its own speed and direction. The beam sweeps continuously in a rhythmic arc from left to right, its bright white light illuminating particles in its path and temporarily defining the fog into sharp, ribbon-like shapes. The color palette uses muted blues and grays for the fog contrasted against the pure white beam, creating a strong visual tension between the luminous light and the dense atmospheric mist. The scene evolves organically without any user input, with the motion driven purely by the animation loop and physics simulation.
