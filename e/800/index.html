Brief

A continuous field of vibrant color streams across the canvas. Thousands of small, visible streaks persist and drift, representing the paths of unseen particles. The primary movement is an organized, swirling orbital pattern, but this order is frequently disrupted. As particles pass near one another, they exert a visible repulsive force, causing their trails to sharply deviate, bend, and arc away from the perfect circular path. The entire field remains dynamic and constantly shifting, maintaining a mesmerizing, fluid quality.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of thousands of glowing particles that flow in organized swirling patterns while constantly interacting with each other. The particles follow orbital motion around the center of the canvas but are disrupted by repulsive forces when they come near one another, creating sharp deviations and arcs in their trails. Each particle leaves behind a colorful streak that persists as it moves, building up a complex tapestry of motion. The system uses spatial grid hashing to efficiently manage particle interactions without expensive all-pairs computations, ensuring smooth performance even with hundreds of simultaneous forces. The result is an ever-shifting, fluid landscape where order and chaos dance together in continuous motion.
