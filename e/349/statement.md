This sketch depicts a marble rolling through a sculpted container with physics-based movement. The viewer observes the marble following a curved path starting in a narrow funnel, transitioning to a steep angled slope, and finally widening into a shallow basin at the base. The animation demonstrates realistic gravity, bouncing, and friction as the marble navigates the varying surfaces of the channel until it comes to rest in the collecting basin. The scene is rendered using WebGL for 3D effects with lighting and shading that enhance the volumetric appearance of the marble and container geometry.
