An energetic op-art exploration of kinetic resonance and elastic easing. Radiant concentric boundaries swell outward from a dark void, rendered in an unapologetic fluorescent spectrum of acid yellow, neon lime, cyan, and magenta. Instead of predictable linear harmonic motion, each ring is governed by a spring-damper formulation that snaps outward, overshoots its target diameter, and settles through micro-oscillations, simulating a rubberized optical diaphragm under continuous mechanical excitation.
