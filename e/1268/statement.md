This sketch depicts a continuous sawmill process where cylindrical logs move through a cutting mechanism and are sliced into wooden planks. The animation shows logs entering the sawmill from the left, being cut apart into multiple planks that then fall downward under gravity. These planks accumulate in a chaotic, irregular pile at the bottom of the frame. Using WEBGL rendering, the scene creates depth with perspective, while earthy brown color tones and simple geometric shapes convey the organic texture of wood. The animation runs continuously without user interaction, showing the ongoing industrial process through particle-like motion and stacking behavior that builds up into an organic heap.
