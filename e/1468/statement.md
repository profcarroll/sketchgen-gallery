This sketch creates a dynamic flocking simulation of paper airplanes that fill the canvas with fluid, coordinated motion. The system implements classic flocking behaviors—separation, alignment, and cohesion—while responding to mouse movement by attracting the entire swarm toward the cursor position. Each airplane is rendered as a simple triangular shape with dynamic orientation based on its velocity vector. The simulation maintains a dense swarm of 150 objects that continuously adjust their positions and angles to create an organic, murmuration-like effect, with the viewer's interaction causing an immediate and coordinated response across the entire flock.
