This sketch creates a continuously shifting abstract composition of diagonal stripes that evolve in real-time without any user interaction. The design employs bold primary colors arranged in diagonal bands that rotate and morph subtly across the canvas. A halftone dot texture overlays the entire pattern, giving it a printed quality reminiscent of graphic design. The motion is generated through time-based calculations that drive color shifts, rotation changes, and the halftone pattern's opacity variations. The entire system operates autonomously using only p5.js core features, creating an endlessly evolving visual abstraction of geometric forms.
