This sketch depicts a meditative landscape where ordered geometric structures gradually dissolve into organic forms. The deep green bioluminescent "roots" pulse and grow in rhythmic patterns, suggesting natural processes beneath the surface. Interspersed throughout this field are scattered sheets of oxidized steel that drift and scrape against each other, creating chaotic movement within the otherwise serene composition. The entire scene breathes with slow, cyclical motion that evokes both geological time and the gentle flow of water, inviting contemplation on the interplay between decay and natural flow.
