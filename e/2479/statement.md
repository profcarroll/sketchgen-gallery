This sketch depicts a slow, organic fungal network emerging across a visible grid structure. The mycelium consists of branching threads that grow and evolve over time, with each thread segment having its own color and opacity. The underlying grid represents the "warp and weft" of a loom, providing a structured foundation against which the chaotic growth of the fungal patterns emerges. The network is constantly in flux - segments are added and removed, nodes shift slightly, and dead threads regenerate into new growth. The result is an ever-changing tapestry of organic decay, where the boundaries between structure and randomness blur continuously.
