This sketch creates a serene, dreamlike atmosphere of floating thoughts and steam-like bubbles drifting across the screen. The composition features a field of translucent pastel-colored bubbles that gently rise and drift apart, interspersed with floating text fragments representing ephemeral ideas. The scene responds to user interaction: clicking anywhere triggers a burst of bright, temporary bubbles that disrupt the peaceful flow, while dragging the cursor causes existing elements to gather and reorganize like scattered brainstorming thoughts. The muted color palette and slow, organic movements evoke the tranquil, contemplative space of a shower, where thoughts drift and dissolve naturally. The interaction design provides both disruption and organization, allowing viewers to engage with the floating ideas in a way that feels intuitive and responsive to their presence.
