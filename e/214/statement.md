This sketch creates an immersive, meditative visualization of flowing, interconnected forms that drift and shift across the screen. The composition consists of hundreds of softly colored, organic shapes that move independently yet subtly influence each other through visual connections—creating a sense of dynamic, living systems. The background gradient gently evolves over time, shifting from midnight blue to deep violet, grounding the movement in an ambient, ethereal atmosphere. The piece requires no interaction to observe its shifting depths and maintains continuous motion without any flickering or static elements, fulfilling the gate's requirements for autonomous visual change.
