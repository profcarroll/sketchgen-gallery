Brief

The canvas displays stylized biological cells, represented by gradually changing, vibrant circular shapes. The simulation illustrates the process of cell division, beginning with a single parent cell. Over time, the cell structures display internal condensations—filaments that grow and move toward the center. These filaments then pull apart, reorganizing the main structure until the original cell cleanly separates and resolves into multiple, smaller daughter cells. The colors and shapes are fluid and dynamic, representing the organized movement and complete cycle of biological replication, concluding with a stable grouping of new cells.
