This sketch creates a dynamic, abstract cityscape using flowing streams of colored light that move across a dark canvas. The visual system consists of multiple continuously moving streaks (streams) that leave trails behind them, creating a sense of kinetic energy and urban flow. At irregular intervals, these streams briefly stabilize to form sharp geometric polygons that overlay the flowing motion, representing moments of stillness or intersection in the city's rhythm. The composition uses color theory with hue shifts across the spectrum to create visual variety while maintaining a cohesive dark urban aesthetic. The entire system operates without user input, relying on procedural generation and time-based animation to continuously evolve the visual experience.
