This sketch transforms the concept of data streams into a geological interpretation of onyx formations carved by rhythmic pendulum motion. Instead of a tangled web of cables, viewers observe a dense array of striated stone blocks that define pathways for luminous data streams. These streams move along the geological structures with rhythmic pulses, their energy visible as glowing orbs that travel through the stone. Where streams cross paths, they create transient flares of light at intersections, mimicking how data might merge and interact in a digital landscape interpreted through stone's natural flow lines and carved passages. The result is an abstract visualization where technology and geology coalesce into a single, pulsing visual experience.
