This sketch presents a vast, intricate network of glowing lines and nodes that evokes both futuristic circuitry and urban blueprints. The scene unfolds in three-dimensional space with a sweeping camera movement that mimics an aerial flyover across a structured landscape. The color palette combines rich metallic blues and deep copper tones with bright electric green pathways that trace light through the system. As the viewer moves their cursor, nearby elements subtly refract and flare, creating an illusion of ambiguous technology and urban sprawl. The entire composition pulses with a slow, hypnotic rhythm driven by time-based animations, while maintaining a visual balance between complexity and coherence. The work explores themes of interconnected systems, technological abstraction, and the passage of time through geometric relationships.
