Brief

The viewer observes a cinematic descent over a desolate, cratered lunar landscape. Grey, textured terrain with deep shadows and jagged rock formations sweeps past the camera. The background is a pitch-black void filled with distant, static stars. The camera maintains a smooth, continuous forward motion, gliding low over the craters and ridges to simulate a spacecraft flyover.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch simulates a cinematic flyover of a lunar landscape using p5.js in WEBGL mode. The viewer observes a descent through a desolate cratered terrain with deep shadows and jagged rock formations. Stars fill the background as a pitch-black void, while the camera maintains smooth forward motion. The scene is constructed from procedurally generated points representing craters and ridges, creating a textured, low-poly representation of the moon's surface that moves past the viewer to simulate flight.
