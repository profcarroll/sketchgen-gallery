This sketch presents an abstract architectural facade composed of layered geometric planes in deep, muted tones. Through the continuous motion of a shifting light source, it explores how illumination and shadow interact dynamically across built forms. The interplay between solid surfaces and cast shadows creates a rhythmic visual flow, illustrating how architectural interruptions influence the quality and movement of light. As time progresses, the position of the light source subtly changes, causing shadows to creep and distort across the planes, offering a contemplative study in spatial dynamics and light behavior.
