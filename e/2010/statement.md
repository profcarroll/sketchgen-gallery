This sketch renders the Mandelbrot fractal, a mathematically infinite and infinitely detailed pattern that emerges from a simple iterative equation. The visualization fills the entire canvas with swirling, colorful regions that reveal ever more complexity at every zoom level. Users can click anywhere to zoom into that coordinate, or drag to pan across different parts of the fractal landscape. The rendering uses a classic escape-time algorithm to color each pixel based on how quickly its corresponding complex number escapes to infinity, creating vibrant gradients that highlight the fractal's boundary structure.
