This sketch presents a static yet dynamic representation of our solar system in three-dimensional space. The scene begins as a serene, unchanging cosmos with a central star and planets frozen in their orbital positions. Upon clicking anywhere on the canvas, the entire system springs into motion — planets begin to orbit their star along luminous paths that trace their journeys through space. The viewer is invited to witness the eternal dance of celestial bodies, where each planet moves at its own unique pace and hue, creating a visual symphony of cosmic choreography. The use of WEBGL rendering allows for a rich depth perception, while the orbital paths glow subtly against the black void, emphasizing the vastness and order of the solar system's architecture.
