This sketch renders a still, intricate organic tapestry inspired by rhododendrons, combining rich jewel tones with delicate pastels in an interwoven structure. Using a network of connections between particles, it creates a gossamer web that links areas of vibrant color to softer hues, evoking the sense of deep organic complexity and natural interlacing found in rhododendron forms. The composition is deliberately static — no motion occurs once drawn — emphasizing the fine detail and structural linkages of the design.
