This sketch visualizes the molecular structure of DNA as a dynamic double helix made of colored spheres representing base pairs. The visualization shows a continuous process where a segment of the helix begins to unwind and separate into two single strands. These separated strands then undergo a folding motion that mimics how they might behave in biological processes. The scene uses 3D rendering with rotating camera angles to show the complex structure from multiple perspectives, with hydrogen bonds illustrated as connecting lines between complementary base pairs. The animation demonstrates both the mechanical separation and the subsequent folding behavior of DNA strands.
