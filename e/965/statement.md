This sketch creates a mesmerizing, continuously shifting geometric composition using bold diagonal stripes that evolve and interact to form complex, self-repeating patterns. The implementation uses a dynamic system of polygonal stripes with varying angles, speeds, and colors that shift over time through trigonometric functions. Each stripe's orientation changes independently while maintaining the overall visual rhythm, creating an emergent lattice structure as they overlap and intersect. The color palette shifts gradually through the hue spectrum, and additional circular patterns rotate in the background to enhance the fractal-like quality of the composition. The entire system is fully self-contained, requiring no user input to operate, producing a dense, hypnotic visual field that demonstrates both mathematical precision and organic flow through its continuous motion.
