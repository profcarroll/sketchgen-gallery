This sketch renders a dense architectural diagram that simulates dendritic biological branching through a lattice of angular lines. The structure pulses rhythmically from its central core outward, mimicking organic growth patterns while maintaining geometric precision. The visual effect emerges from the harmonic interaction of numerous nodal points that expand and contract in controlled oscillations, creating an impression of ordered, self-organizing life spreading across the surface. The matte, textural tones evoke the tactile quality of plaster while the sharp angular connections reference both structural engineering and biological branching patterns.
