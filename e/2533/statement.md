This sketch depicts an abyssal ocean environment centered around colossal hydrothermal chimney structures. The chimneys are rendered as mineral deposits with layered cylindrical forms that extend deep into the ocean floor. Emerging from these structures are bioluminescent geometric forms that pulse with electric emerald and cyan hues, creating an ethereal glow that suggests living organisms thriving in this extreme environment. The scene captures the vastness and depth of the ocean floor while emphasizing the luminous life that exists within it.
