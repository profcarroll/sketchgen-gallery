This sketch visualizes a microscopic geological outburst through the interaction of two contrasting materials: organic wool fibers and rigid silica spicules. The scene is driven by invisible superheated steam pressure that causes chaotic motion in the soft fibers while creating sharp, angular movement in the mineral structures. The fibers weave and tangle in organic trails, while the spicules move with mechanical precision, occasionally colliding and interlocking. Steam particles burst upward from the center, creating a constant force field that animates the entire composition. The result is a dynamic representation of intense pressure and transformation at a microscopic scale, capturing both the violence and beauty of geological processes.
