This sketch depicts a dynamic cross-section of geological strata undergoing tectonic forces. Through subtle wave motions, simulated slippage, and occasional fracturing, the layered rock formations evolve over time to suggest the slow, powerful processes that build mountain ranges. The use of WEBGL rendering allows for a more immersive 3D perspective while maintaining the performance constraints necessary for smooth animation. Viewer interaction is minimal, as the geological drama unfolds autonomously through procedural animation techniques that create an ever-changing landscape of stone and sediment.
