This sketch depicts the emergence of crystalline structures within ancient, subterranean mineral veins. The viewer observes a slow, pulsing growth process where individual crystals form and extend outward through branching tendrils. The color palette shifts between deep blues and vibrant greens, mimicking bioluminescent effects while maintaining a geological aesthetic. Each crystal grows organically over time, with its branches extending and pulsing in rhythm with the passage of time, creating an illusion of life awakening within the mineral framework. The scene suggests the slow, patient process of crystal formation occurring deep underground, where mineral veins pulse with energy and structure evolves through natural growth cycles.
