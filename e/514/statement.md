This sketch creates an abstract television graphics visualization reminiscent of 1993 broadcast design. It features multiple rotating gradient layers that shift from green to deep cyan across the canvas, with each layer slowly spinning in a continuous clockwise motion. The composition uses geometric forms including intersecting circles and rectangles, arranged to create dynamic visual depth through layered transparency and color transitions. The animation is entirely self-contained, running without user interaction, with smooth, subtle movements that evoke the professional yet ever-changing aesthetic of early digital television graphics systems.
