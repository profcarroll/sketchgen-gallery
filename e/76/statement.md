This sketch creates a dynamic nighttime cityscape over a harbor populated by miniature drones that move through the air, leaving glowing contrails behind them. The scene features towering buildings and water, with drones systematically sweeping focused searchlight beams across the urban landscape. The viewer can interact with the scene by clicking or dragging the mouse to shift the formations of the drones into new patterns of light. The entire composition uses WebGL rendering for 3D effects, with continuous animation that responds to user input through motion and visual changes across the canvas.
