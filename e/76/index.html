Brief

The canvas displays a dynamic, high-contrast nighttime cityscape over a harbor, populated by numerous miniature drones. These drones move through the air, emitting glowing contrails, and systematically sweep focused beams of searchlights across the towering buildings and water. The overall scene is constantly animated by the movement of the flying drones. The viewer interacts with this scene by clicking or dragging the mouse, which causes the formations of the drones to shift and cycle into new, intricate patterns of light across the harbor.
