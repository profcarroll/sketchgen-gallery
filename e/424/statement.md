This sketch creates an immersive, ever-shifting garden of rhododendron blossoms that respond to both time and user interaction. The scene features a soft watercolor palette of purples and pinks, with each blossom gently pulsing and drifting across the canvas like petals carried by a breeze. The blossoms are generated procedurally in a fixed number, ensuring performance while maintaining visual complexity. When the user moves their cursor across the canvas, the colors deepen and individual blooms flare with intensified hues, creating a responsive layer that makes the scene feel alive and interactive. The constant motion and subtle color shifts give the impression of a living garden, where each moment brings a new configuration of blooming forms.
