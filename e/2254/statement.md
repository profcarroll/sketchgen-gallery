This sketch presents an architectural marvel constructed from glass prisms that exists in a perpetual state of instability. The structure appears to cascade and slide downward in a continuous geometric collapse, rendered in dark tones that emphasize the fragility of the glass. The viewer experiences both natural motion and a responsive element: clicking anywhere on the canvas triggers a cascade effect where segments peel off and accelerate their descent. The piece combines physics-based animation with interactive feedback to create an immersive visualization of an architectural structure that is both beautiful and inherently unstable, where each click becomes a catalyst for the ongoing collapse.
