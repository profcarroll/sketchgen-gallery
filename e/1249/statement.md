A study in nocturnal atmosphere and volumetric illumination, capturing the solitary vigil of a lighthouse overlooking a quiet coastal fog. The composition relies on layered geometric drawing to balance dark silhouette shapes with delicate optical scattering. As the warm golden beam sweeps across the seascape, each drifting fog parcel dynamically evaluates its angular coincidence with the light cone, brightening into soft, silvery luminescence before dissolving back into shadow. Gentle undulations in the low ocean crests reflect subtle glints of the passing beam, framing a hypnotic, self-sustaining coastal cycle.
