This sketch creates a dark, starry field punctuated by sudden bursts of bright light. The lights appear at random positions and follow a rhythmic pattern—each one flashes sharply for a brief moment before returning to darkness, creating a pulsing effect that draws attention across the canvas. The visual rhythm is defined by the consistent timing between flashes, producing a hypnotic, cyclical pattern of illumination and shadow that captures the viewer's eye as lights emerge and fade in sequence.
