This sketch renders an abstract, high-altitude view of a bustling metropolis at night using dynamic light particles. Thousands of glowing points move independently across the canvas, leaving luminous trails that form intricate geometric patterns through their connections. The visual effect is created by simulating thousands of particles with velocity vectors, maintaining trails for each, and drawing lines between nearby particles using a spatial grid optimization to avoid expensive all-pairs distance calculations. Color shifts enhance the dramatic contrast between the brilliant light trails and the dark urban backdrop, creating an evolving network of illumination that suggests constant motion and interconnectedness throughout the cityscape.
