This sketch presents a serene winter alpine landscape dominated by muted blues, whites, and cool grays. The composition features distant snow-draped mountains against a soft blue-gray sky, with sharp rock formations in the foreground. When viewers click at the center of the canvas, an avalanche is triggered that scatters debris and deposits fresh snow drifts over the existing rock structures. The technique uses particle physics to simulate falling snow and debris, with a fixed number of particles that are dynamically updated each frame. The viewer experiences a transformation from still landscape to animated avalanche, demonstrating how a simple interaction can create a dramatic natural event within an otherwise static scene.
