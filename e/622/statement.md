This sketch depicts a dynamic urban environment that shifts through a simulated day-night cycle. The viewer observes a grid of buildings casting long shadows across a textured ground plane, with the sun's position changing smoothly over time to create evolving light and shadow patterns. The scene uses WEBGL rendering to create depth and perspective, with buildings rendered as 3D boxes and their shadows cast dynamically onto the ground. As the simulation progresses, the sky color transitions from daylight blue to twilight darkness, while the sun moves across the canvas, causing shadows to sweep across the plaza. The visual effect emphasizes the interplay between architectural geometry and natural light, creating a sense of time passing through the constant shifting of illumination and shadow patterns.
