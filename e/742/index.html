Brief

Overlapping planes of deep, rich color dominate the canvas, forming softly defined, monumental fields. These color blocks are intersected and bordered by defining vertical layers. The composition is characterized by a subtle, ethereal chroma shift that perpetually alters the palette. This continuous shift does not change the overall forms, but instead intensifies the visibility and depth of the outermost vertical boundaries, giving the structure of the entire piece a dynamic, vibrating quality. The colors move and deepen without any physical input or user interaction.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a serene, ever-shifting field of overlapping colored planes that seem to breathe and pulse with subtle chromatic movement. The composition uses layers of softly rounded rectangles in varying opacities and sizes, arranged to create monumental fields of color that shift slowly through time. The colors move through a deep, rich palette without any user interaction, creating an immersive experience of calm transformation. A continuous wave-like motion is added through subtle vertical layers that vibrate across the canvas, intensifying the sense of depth and dynamic energy while maintaining the peaceful quality of the overall piece.
