This sketch creates a dynamic, geometric marble run using p5.js in WEBGL mode. The scene features a complex channel constructed from layered, angular metal walls that guide a polished marble downward through gravity-driven motion. The marble's path is not straight — it makes lateral deviations as it navigates the sharp curves and intersecting ridges of the channel. As it approaches the bottom, the marble enters a widening collecting basin where its motion gradually slows to a natural stop. The entire scene is rendered with lighting effects that emphasize the metallic geometry and the marble's movement, creating an engaging visual representation of physics in action.
