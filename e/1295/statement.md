This sketch creates a complex, multi-stranded knot structure using WebGL rendering to achieve dense weaving patterns across the screen. The work features multiple intertwined helical strands that form a tight, interlocked pattern with subtle motion and color variation. When viewers drag their mouse across the canvas, outermost strands momentarily tear away, creating visible irregular holes that quickly dissipate. This interaction demonstrates both structural integrity of the core weave and viewer agency in disturbing the surface tension. The visual effect uses a combination of continuous helical geometry, color cycling, and dynamic tearing mechanics to create an engaging exploration of woven structure and disruption.
