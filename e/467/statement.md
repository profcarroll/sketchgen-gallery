This sketch creates an immersive interplay between architectural mass and light through a dynamic system of rays and geometric planes. The scene features a central light source that moves in a circular orbit, casting light paths that illuminate rigid surfaces. As rays travel through space, they interact with the architectural planes, creating glowing scatter effects where light strikes the surfaces. The visualization emphasizes the computational nature of light interaction through thousands of points that trace their paths in real-time, forming intricate shadow patterns and illuminating surfaces with a pulsing glow. The architecture consists of rotating geometric planes that serve as both structural elements and reflective surfaces, creating a continuous dance between solid form and light's passage.
