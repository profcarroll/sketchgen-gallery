This sketch creates a dynamic, immersive field of interconnected particles that flow and shift in continuous motion. The system uses a simplified spatial hashing technique to efficiently connect nearby particles, forming an evolving web of glowing lines that pulse and change with the viewer's interaction. When clicked, the entire field reacts with a surge of energy that redistributes particles and shifts the color palette dramatically. Dragging creates visible trails that distort the flow, adding ethereal light effects. The visual effect is achieved through batching particle rendering into a single point cloud and limiting connection counts per frame to maintain performance while preserving the organic, chaotic beauty of the system.
