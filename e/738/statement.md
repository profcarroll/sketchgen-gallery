This sketch renders a dynamically rotating molecular double helix in 3D space using p5.js's WEBGL renderer. The structure consists of labeled spheres representing nucleotide base pairs (adenine/thymine and guanine/cytosine) held together by geometric hydrogen bond connections. The entire helix continuously rotates, creating a mesmerizing visual effect. When the canvas is clicked, a segment of the helix begins to uncoil and separate, with the complementary base pairs visibly pulling apart over time. The animation demonstrates both the structural integrity and dynamic behavior of DNA through an interactive visualization that responds to user input while maintaining performance within strict frame budget constraints.
