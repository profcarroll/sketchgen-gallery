This sketch creates a mesmerizing lattice of interlocking hexagons that pulse with an inner energy, simulating tension just before structural collapse. The grid responds dynamically to both time-based animation and user interaction — clicking anywhere triggers a localized surge in pulsing intensity that propagates through the structure. The visual effect uses color, motion, and geometric relationships to create an immersive sense of stress and release. An ambient soundscape evolves with the visuals, amplifying during moments of perceived instability. The system maintains performance by using fixed arrays and avoiding expensive operations per frame, creating a complex but controlled visual experience that feels both organic and engineered.
