This sketch creates an abstract, evolving landscape of intersecting geometric fields that pulse and shift in response to audio input. The composition features overlapping circular and rectangular planes that expand, contract, and distort based on the rhythm and intensity of music played through a microphone. As sound enters, wave-like ripples emanate from the center, causing all shapes to stretch and multiply in complexity. The color palette breathes with a shifting hue cycle, and the density of elements increases with volume, creating a kinetic energy that builds before settling into a serene harmonic diffusion. The visual feedback is immediate and intuitive—when silence plays, the fields stabilize; when music plays, they become alive with motion.
