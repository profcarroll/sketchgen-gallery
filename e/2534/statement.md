This sketch depicts a lighthouse scene with voluminous smoke plumes that rise and sway rhythmically, inspired by the visual metaphor of knitted wool. The composition captures the atmospheric tension between the solid structure of the lighthouse and the fluid, organic movement of the smoke. Using WebGL rendering, it creates an immersive environment where each plume is constructed from layered spheres with varying opacities to simulate the texture of knitted fibers. The animation emphasizes continuous motion through subtle swaying and upward drift, evoking a sense of ambient scale and natural rhythm that persists without viewer interaction.
