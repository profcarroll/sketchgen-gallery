This sketch creates an abstract nighttime cityscape through a system of flowing light streaks that move dynamically across the canvas. The visual metaphor captures the energy and constant motion of urban life through a field of particles that follow subtle flow patterns, creating a sense of depth and continuous activity. When these moving elements intersect, they briefly form geometric relationships—glowing points and connecting lines—that momentarily solidify the abstract flow into defined forms before dissolving back into the fluid abstraction. The technique uses particle systems with flow fields to simulate movement, combined with intersection detection to create emergent geometric moments that reflect the dynamic interplay of light and motion in a city environment.
