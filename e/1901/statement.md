This sketch creates a meditative cosmic environment where translucent circles flow slowly across the screen like an ethereal tide. As these circles move, they occasionally leave behind glowing afterimages that drift through the field. When these afterimages pass near other circles, they trigger pulsating corona effects around those circles, causing them to glow momentarily. The interaction is subtle and continuous, creating a sense of flowing energy through the void. The composition uses transparency, motion trails, and dynamic interactions to evoke a calm, immersive experience that feels both infinite and alive.
