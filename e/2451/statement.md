This sketch creates an immersive deep-field visualization of a vast constellation of polished brass rings in motion. Using WebGL rendering, it displays hundreds of interconnected rings orbiting in three-dimensional space, each with unique orbital characteristics, rotation speeds, and metallic hues. The rings spin and shift continuously against a dark void, creating a mesmerizing display that evokes the complexity and beauty of celestial mechanics. The viewer experiences this cosmic dance as if through an equatorial telescope, witnessing countless trajectories intersecting in an intricate, ever-changing mechanical universe that tracks time and movement through predictable, elegant orbital patterns.
