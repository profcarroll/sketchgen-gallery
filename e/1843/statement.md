This sketch simulates the effect of text being written on aged, vintage paper. As characters flow across the canvas, they subtly warp and shift due to the stress of continuous writing, mimicking the aging process of paper. The surface develops delicate hairline fractures that spiderweb outward from the text, tracking the accumulation of stress over time. These fractures appear and fade naturally, enhancing the feeling of an aged manuscript under constant use. The visual texture combines a subtle noise-based paper background with the organic motion of the text to create an immersive experience of decay and writing in motion.
