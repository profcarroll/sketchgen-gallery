This sketch visualizes an electrocardiogram (EKG) waveform as a continuously scrolling neon line against a deep black background. The line traces rhythmic patterns with both steady, pulsating surges and cyclical uniform spikes that maintain consistent intervals. Through careful generation of waveform data using sine functions and strategic placement of peaks and troughs, the visualization captures the essence of heartbeats while emphasizing the periodic nature of cardiac activity. The glowing effect created via p5.js's shadow rendering techniques enhances the neon aesthetic, making the moving line appear as a luminous trail through space. The animation runs autonomously without user input, fulfilling the requirement for self-contained visual motion.
