This sketch creates an immersive stained-glass visualization where countless glowing fragments spiral inward toward a central light source. The composition features a complex geometric structure formed by interconnected polygonal panels that emit vibrant, saturated colors and subtle pulsing highlights. Over time, colorful shards appear randomly across the canvas and begin moving in dynamic spiraling patterns, converging toward the center point of the composition. The effect creates an hypnotic, cyclical display of light and motion that fills the entire canvas with a mesmerizing interplay of glowing particles and structured geometry. The visual rhythm builds naturally through time, creating a self-contained spectacle without any user interaction required to initiate or sustain the animation.
