This sketch creates an abstract visualization of fossilized ammonite shells at a geological scale, focusing on the intricate spiral chambers and glowing data streams that flow through them. The work presents a dark, mineral landscape where deep spiral formations create natural fault lines, along which luminous cyan and emerald streams pulse and meander. The viewer observes a continuous, slow dance of information as the streams move through the spiral chambers, creating a sense of ancient geological time and digital flow simultaneously. The visual effect emerges from the interplay between the structured spiral geometry and the fluid, pulsing data paths that trace natural pathways through the chamber structures.
