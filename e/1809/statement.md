This sketch presents an ethereal field of interconnected geometric structures floating in deep space, featuring multiple glowing planes that drift and rotate in synchronized orbits. The visual system creates a sense of perpetual motion through continuous orbital paths and subtle rotations of each plane. The most striking element is the dynamic connection system between planes: when two planes pass within a critical distance, their connecting lines pulse with intense brightness, creating dramatic visual events that punctuate the otherwise gentle flow. The color palette emphasizes fluorescent blues, magentas, and whites, evoking both technological sophistication and otherworldly beauty. The entire composition exists in three-dimensional space with depth and motion that creates an immersive, hypnotic experience.
