This sketch creates an atmospheric visualization of rising steam that subtly transforms into fragmented word forms. Using a particle system with Perlin noise for organic movement, it renders thousands of luminous particles that drift and coalesce in gentle currents. The particles leave fading trails as they move, creating a sense of continuous motion and transformation. Occasionally, these ephemeral clouds form vague outlines resembling words like "steam," "mist," "fog," and "vapor" before dissolving back into the ambient flow. The technique emphasizes fluidity and organic behavior over rigid geometric forms, resulting in a dreamlike representation of vapor dynamics that feels both natural and mysterious.
