This sketch creates a rhythmic pulsing animation where red and black waves emanate from the center of the canvas. Each pulse expands outward at a steady pace, leaving behind a glowing trail that gradually fades. The animation runs continuously without user interaction, with new pulses generated at regular intervals to maintain the pulsing effect. The visual design uses HSB color space to create vibrant red hues that blend smoothly into transparency as the pulses expand, creating a mesmerizing temporal layering effect where older pulses fade into the background while newer ones emerge from the center.
