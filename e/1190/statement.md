This sketch presents an interactive jigsaw puzzle where users can drag and snap pieces into place. The interface shows a collection of irregularly shaped puzzle pieces floating on a background image, with the ability to click and drag them around the canvas. As pieces are moved and snapped into position, they become visible as part of the larger picture beneath the puzzle surface, revealing how they fit together. The interaction is designed for simple yet satisfying engagement, allowing users to manipulate the puzzle visually without complex controls or sound.
