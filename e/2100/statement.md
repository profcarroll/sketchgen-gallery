This sketch depicts a mesmerizing flow of pigmented liquid moving through a narrow channel, viewed through a subtle corrugated cardboard filter. The composition features thousands of organic particles drifting slowly at different speeds and hues, creating an ever-evolving tapestry of color that blends and separates naturally. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of particle physics with gentle drift patterns, color mixing techniques, and a procedural texture map that simulates the refraction of light through corrugated material. The result is a calming, non-directional display where colors flow organically without any clear beginning or end, capturing the ephemeral nature of fluid dynamics in an abstract, meditative form.
