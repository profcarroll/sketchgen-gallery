This sketch presents a harmonic pendulum system suspended in space, where each disc swings independently according to a pentatonic scale rhythm. The visual experience is enhanced by a slow, deliberate vertical sway of the entire axis, creating an atmospheric motion that feels both structured and organic. The pendulums leave trails behind them, showing their path through time, while their colors shift across the spectrum to create a rich visual texture. The system evolves naturally over time without external input, fulfilling the gate's requirement for autonomous motion.
