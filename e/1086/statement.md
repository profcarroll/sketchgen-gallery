This sketch creates a first-person journey through a brutalist concrete landscape. Using WEBGL rendering, it generates a series of massive monolithic structures that appear to scroll past the viewer as they move forward through space. Each block features detailed textures of dark grime and vibrant green moss streaks, creating a sense of weathered urban architecture. The camera glides steadily through this environment at a constant pace, generating momentum within the heavy geometric forms. The technique uses simple 3D primitives with custom texturing to create an immersive yet minimalist architectural experience that evokes the raw, unrefined beauty of brutalist design.
