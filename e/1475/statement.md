This sketch presents a simplified urban skyline composed of geometric buildings that respond to user input. When a key is pressed, the skyline grows upward with new floors, each maintaining the solid color and blocky profile of the existing structures. The visual effect demonstrates a cumulative growth pattern where each press adds a horizontal layer across all buildings, extending their silhouette against the background. The interaction is immediate and visually distinct, creating a sense of architectural progression as the viewer controls the city's vertical expansion through keyboard input.
