This sketch depicts two distinct streams of colorful particles flowing across a dark canvas. The left stream glows in blue hues while the right stream pulses in green tones, each maintaining its own vibrant identity as they move independently. When the streams come into proximity, their particles momentarily swap colors in a dynamic interaction that creates a visual blend before they separate again. The effect emerges from a simple physics model where particles move with randomized velocities and bounce off screen edges, while their color states are dynamically updated based on proximity to particles from the opposing stream. The result is an organic, fluid visualization of interacting currents that demonstrates both separation and connection through color exchange.
