This sketch creates an organic, evolving tapestry of color and motion that evokes the natural growth patterns of rhododendrons. Through a system of interconnected particles with spring-like forces, it simulates the gentle, breathing movement of flower clusters and leafy structures. The visual language balances rich jewel tones against soft pastels, creating depth and vibrance as particles subtly shift in hue, size, and opacity over time. The composition pulses with life, drawing viewers into a dynamic field where individual elements maintain their identity while contributing to a larger, flowing form—capturing the essence of natural motion through algorithmic expression.
