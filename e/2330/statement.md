This sketch presents a microscopic view of a beeswax lattice undergoing rapid mechanical stress and fracture. The geometric shards—represented as ellipses with varying sizes and orientations—move at high velocities across the canvas, building up stress until sudden collisions cause them to shatter and reform. The color palette shifts between intense contrasting hues (orange-red) during moments of high stress, creating a visual metaphor for the mechanical forces at play. Connections between nearby shards pulse with this stress intensity, forming a dynamic network that continuously reforms as shards collide and scatter. The constant motion and abrupt transformations evoke the fragility and resilience of crystalline structures under pressure.
