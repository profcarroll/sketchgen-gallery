Brief

From the center of the canvas, a series of concentric rings begin to expand outwards. These rings are filled with vibrant, fluctuating fluorescent colors that provide a dynamic backdrop. As they grow, the rings do not maintain a smooth curve; instead, they visibly distort, creating a fluid, wave-like ripple effect that travels across the surface. The entire pattern operates continuously, expanding and rippling across the canvas without any required user input.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a mesmerizing, continuously evolving pattern of concentric rings that emanate from the center of the canvas. Each ring expands outward with a fluid, wave-like distortion that creates a ripple effect across the entire surface. The colors shift dynamically through a vibrant fluorescent spectrum, cycling through hues to create a luminous, hypnotic visual experience. The system maintains a constant flow of expanding rings while ensuring smooth animation and preventing performance issues by carefully managing the number of elements and using efficient drawing techniques. The result is an organic, living pattern that fills the canvas with pulsing, glowing motion without requiring any user interaction.
