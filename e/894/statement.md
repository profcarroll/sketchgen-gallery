This sketch depicts a glowing disc hovering above a corn field, with its light source casting a dynamic, pulsating semicircular glow on the ground. The animation uses a combination of static corn stalks and a constantly evolving light pattern to create an otherworldly effect. The technique involves drawing a series of rectangles for the corn stalks, a central glowing disc, and a semicircular shape that changes radius and color intensity over time to simulate flickering energy. The pulsation effect is created through trigonometric functions and randomization, producing a mesmerizing pattern that washes across the field without any external input. The entire scene exists purely through continuous animation and color variation, creating a self-contained spectacle of light and movement.
