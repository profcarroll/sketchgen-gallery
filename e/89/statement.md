This sketch creates an immersive cosmic landscape using only 2D p5.js rendering. It simulates deep space by layering animated stars and nebula filaments against a deep indigo background. The technique uses multiple layers of particles with varying sizes, opacities, and speeds to create depth and motion, while subtle color shifts in the nebula elements produce a glowing, ethereal effect. The scene continuously evolves without user interaction, giving the impression of infinite cosmic drift and twinkling stellar motion.
