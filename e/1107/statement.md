This sketch depicts a weathered brick wall in a state of gradual decay, where thick root tendrils slowly penetrate the mortar joints and cause structural damage. The scene uses a limited earthen color palette dominated by deep reds, browns, and grays to evoke a sense of neglect and age. Bricks are rendered as 3D objects that gradually tilt and shift due to the persistent pressure from growing roots. Spalling particles break off the bricks over time, creating an active erosion effect. The roots themselves appear as organic tendrils that grow and branch through the wall structure, forcing cracks and fissures in the masonry. The entire composition creates a sense of slow, inevitable collapse under the force of nature reclaiming man-made structures.
