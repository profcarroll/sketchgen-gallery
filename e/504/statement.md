This sketch renders a simplified yet visually engaging three-dimensional model of our solar system using p5.js in WEBGL mode. It features a central sun surrounded by six orbiting planets, each with unique sizes, colors, and orbital speeds. The scene rotates slowly to provide a dynamic view, with each planet following its own elliptical path around the sun. The orbits are traced as faint gray ellipses, enhancing the sense of motion and depth. Saturn is specially rendered with visible rings. The entire system animates smoothly without any user interaction, fulfilling the requirement for autonomous visual change while staying within performance constraints.
