This sketch depicts a quiet library space where the rigid structure of wooden shelves gradually yields to the soft accumulation of knitted wool threads. The palette draws from muted earth tones that suggest both decayed wood and weathered textiles, creating a sense of slow time passing. The shelves appear to settle slightly with each frame, their surfaces subtly animated by simulated wood grain and aging effects. Meanwhile, thousands of tiny wool threads drift gently across the canvas in slow, organic movements—some fading into the background, others catching light—representing the accumulated history of use and care that builds up over time. The overall effect is one of gentle ruin and patient accumulation, where each element contributes to a larger narrative of time's quiet erosion.
