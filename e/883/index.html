Brief

The canvas reveals a massive, open-world cityscape, displaying towering buildings and expansive ground surfaces. Time is a visible element, as the buildings cast dynamic shadows that sweep across the pavement. The ground surface itself is highly textured, showing differential weathering and accumulated grime that visibly settles and darkens in the crevices and areas untouched by the current shadows, creating a sense of deep, simulated reality. The environment is constantly changing as the simulated time of day passes, revealing the minute details of the aged urban terrain without requiring any direct interaction from the viewer.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch renders an open-world cityscape using the p5.js WEBGL renderer to create a sense of depth and time. Buildings of varying sizes rise from the ground, casting dynamic shadows that shift across the textured pavement as simulated sunlight moves across the sky. The scene evolves slowly over time without any user interaction, giving viewers a contemplative glimpse into an urban environment where light and shadow shape the mood and reveal architectural details.
