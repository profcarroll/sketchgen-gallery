This sketch depicts a mechanical system of interlocking brass gears driving a boiling water vessel. The animation begins with large rotating gears that connect through a mechanism to agitate the water below. As motion transfers through the gears, it causes bubbles to rise from the heated water surface and steam to emerge. Nearby, translucent ice chunks melt rapidly into droplets that flow across the water's surface. The scene captures the continuous transformation of mechanical energy into thermal motion, showing phase changes in a single evolving visual narrative. The entire composition uses only p5.js core functions with no external libraries.
