This sketch creates a glowing electric blue neon line that scrolls steadily from left to right across a black background, simulating an EKG heart monitor pattern. The line features intense fluorescent spikes and irregular rhythmic dips that pulse with a heartbeat rhythm. The visualization uses a combination of sine waves and noise to create the erratic but rhythmic motion characteristic of cardiac activity. The neon effect is achieved through layered alpha blending and careful color management in HSB mode, creating a luminous trail that fades as it moves. The pattern continuously generates new points while maintaining a fixed length, creating the illusion of an ongoing medical monitor display with smooth, flowing motion.
