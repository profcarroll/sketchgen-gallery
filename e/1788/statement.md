This sketch creates an infinite lattice of glowing lines and interconnected nodes that pulse in synchronized emerald light, evoking the image of a vast, vibrating circuit board. The structure is built from a regular grid of nodes connected only horizontally or vertically, forming a rigid network. Each node pulses with a rapid, rhythmic glow while the connections between them shimmer with shifting hues, creating an illusion of constant electrical current flowing through the system. The entire composition shifts subtly over time, maintaining its ethereal quality while the synchronized pulsing of all nodes creates a unified, hypnotic rhythm across the canvas.
