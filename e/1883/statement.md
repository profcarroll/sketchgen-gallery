This sketch creates an endless cascade of translucent, glowing blocks that spiral downward in a mesmerizing pattern. Each block is rendered with a semi-transparent glass-like material and features faint gold etchings that create subtle visual interest as they move through the frame. The blocks follow a spiraling trajectory influenced by gravity and rotational forces, creating an ever-changing chain reaction of falling geometry. The scene uses WebGL rendering for smooth 3D effects, and the motion is entirely autonomous without any viewer interaction required.
