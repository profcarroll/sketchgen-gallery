Brief

The canvas displays a series of concentric rings that originate from the center point and continuously expand outwards. These rings are rendered exclusively using a limited grayscale palette, cycling through various shades of black, white, and gray. The entire pattern pulses with a rhythmic, organic movement, making the rings appear to expand and contract simultaneously. There are no required interactions to view the full pattern, as the motion is purely automatic and self-contained.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a mesmerizing pattern of concentric rings that emanate from the center of the canvas, expanding and contracting in a rhythmic, organic pulse. Using a limited grayscale palette, the rings cycle through varying shades of black, white, and gray, creating a sense of depth and motion. The rings are rendered using pure p5.js drawing functions without any external libraries, relying on the built-in `ellipse()` function to draw each ring. The animation is entirely self-contained, with no user interaction required, as the rings continuously pulse in a synchronized yet slightly offset manner to create a hypnotic visual effect that fills the entire canvas.
