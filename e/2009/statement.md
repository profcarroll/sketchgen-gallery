This sketch depicts a subterranean fault line network filled with massive crystalline structures that pulse with phosphorescent light. The composition features thousands of interconnected stress fractures that glow and breathe in rhythmic cycles, creating an organic flow of light through the geological matrix. The crystals appear to grow and contract like living organisms, their surfaces reflecting and amplifying the pulsating energy that courses through the entire system. The overall effect suggests a dynamic, living geological structure where light flows naturally along fault lines, creating a mesmerizing interplay between solid mineral formations and ethereal illumination.
