This sketch renders a dynamic visualization of deep-sea sediment layers that shift and flow like migrating flocks. The composition consists of multiple horizontal strata with abrupt color transitions and organic flowing patterns. Each layer moves independently with its own speed and flow direction, creating an illusion of continuous reorganization. The color palette draws from deep blues and purples to evoke the mysterious depths, while the banding patterns simulate sedimentary structures. The visual effect emerges from the interplay between smooth, flowing movements and abrupt, stepped color changes that mimic geological processes and collective motion patterns found in nature.
