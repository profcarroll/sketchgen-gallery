This sketch creates an immersive, dark void filled with metallic shards that float and rotate slowly, each displaying a deep verdigris patina texture. At the center, a pulsating emerald light casts subtle shadows across the moving surfaces, creating an atmosphere of ancient, weathered metal in a smoky space. The scene is subtly responsive to audio input, where sound amplitude and frequency affect the movement of the shards, adding an interactive layer to the static visual experience.
