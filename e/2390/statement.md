This sketch depicts the slow erosion of a rigid ceramic floor structure into flowing sediment. Initially appearing as a grid of cleanly fractured tiles, the composition gradually dissolves and reorganizes into organic, overlapping fragments that accumulate like mineral deposits. The viewer witnesses the breakdown of a formal architectural element through time and interaction—each tile responds to contact with the cursor by breaking apart further, accelerating its dissolution into a flowing mass of sedimentary shapes. The piece explores how static structures gradually yield to natural forces, creating an ever-shifting landscape of fractured geometry that maintains visual coherence even as it degrades.
