This sketch creates a mesmerizing tessellation of rotating hexagons that fill the canvas in an intricate, interlocking pattern. The hexagonal grid continuously flows with each shape rotating at its own speed while shifting through a vibrant color palette. The viewer can interact with the piece by clicking anywhere on the canvas, which instantly triggers a dramatic shift in both the rotational dynamics and the entire color scheme of the pattern. The implementation uses a fixed number of hexagons with dynamic rotation and color updates, avoiding expensive operations like particle systems or complex spatial calculations that would exceed the frame budget. The visual effect emerges from the interplay between the continuous motion and the sharp color transitions, creating an ever-changing geometric tapestry.
