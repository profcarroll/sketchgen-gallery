Brief

The canvas displays a grid composed of small squares set against a near-black background, accented by a single warm hue. Each square exhibits a subtle, slow, rhythmic pulse, giving the appearance of breathing. When the user clicks anywhere on the canvas, a visible wave emanates outward from the point of impact. This wave passes through the squares, causing them to flash brightly before returning to their steady, pulsating state.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a responsive grid of breathing squares on a dark background, each pulsing with a gentle rhythm. When the user clicks anywhere on the canvas, a wave expands outward from the point of impact, activating nearby squares and causing them to flash brightly before returning to their steady pulse. The visual effect is subtle yet dynamic, combining organic motion with interactive feedback to create an immersive, tactile experience.
