This sketch renders the Mandelbrot fractal, a mathematical set that produces infinitely complex and beautiful patterns. The visualization uses color to represent iteration counts — areas that take more steps to escape are rendered with brighter hues, creating swirling filaments and intricate boundaries. Users can navigate the fractal by dragging to pan around different regions or clicking to zoom into increasingly detailed structures. The implementation efficiently calculates each pixel's Mandelbrot value using iterative complex number arithmetic, mapping the results to a color space that emphasizes the fine structural details of this timeless mathematical object.
