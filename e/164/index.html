Brief

The canvas depicts a continuous, chaotic grid of vibrant pixel art shapes. The overall aesthetic is retro and highly energetic. Shapes burst and flow across the grid, remaining suspended in a constant state of flux. The primary interaction is direct manipulation via the cursor: as the user drags the mouse across the surface, the vibrant shapes immediately track the pointer's movement. Furthermore, these shapes do not instantly vanish; they leave behind a brief, colour-coded trail that visibly maps the path of the movement before slowly dissipating into the grid.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a vibrant, chaotic grid where pixel art shapes dynamically respond to cursor movement. As you drag your mouse across the canvas, colorful bursts follow your path and leave fading trails behind them. The scene also features spontaneous, self-generated motion that continuously evolves even without interaction—creating an energetic, living visual field of constantly shifting forms and colors.
