This sketch renders a dynamic, three-dimensional cross-section of a combustion engine using p5.js's WEBGL mode. It visualizes the mechanical relationships between key components: pistons moving vertically within cylinders, connecting rods transferring motion from pistons to a central crankshaft, and the crankshaft rotating to simulate an engine cycle. The scene is rendered with clean, geometric shapes that emphasize the precision and motion of the mechanical system. The animation runs autonomously without user input, demonstrating how forces propagate through the engine's core structure in real time.
