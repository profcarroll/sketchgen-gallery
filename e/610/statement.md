This sketch presents a static, atmospheric still life within a darkened room, focusing on the interplay of light and reflection. A brown table dominates the composition, its polished surface reflecting the warm glow from a lit candle and the clear jug of water placed nearby. The scene is rendered using the WEBGL renderer to achieve depth and dimension, with carefully placed lighting that enhances the sculptural qualities of each object. The entire work remains motionless, emphasizing the stillness and contemplative nature of the arrangement, where reflections and shadows create a sense of quiet harmony among the objects.
