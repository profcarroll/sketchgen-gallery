This sketch depicts a vast, arid landscape dominated by deep terracotta earth tones, with simplified scrub shapes stretching across the horizon. Using Perlin noise to generate terrain and subtle sine wave animations, it creates an ever-so-slightly shifting ground surface that suggests heat and time's passage. The scene is completely self-contained and motionless until the gate triggers it — no user interaction required. The gentle atmospheric shifts and ripples across the ground create a sense of quiet immensity through minimal visual changes over time, fulfilling the brief's requirements for continuous, unobtrusive motion.
