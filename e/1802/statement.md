This sketch creates an abstract textile surface where geometric modules flow along a meandering river path. The modules—squares, triangles, hexagons, and diamonds—shift through a vibrant color palette while gently pulsing and undulating along the sinuous path. Rather than a static grid, the entire composition follows a continuous, organic curve that gives the illusion of movement across the field. Each module maintains its own rhythm of color transitions and subtle ripple effects, creating a dynamic textile-like surface where the visual flow emerges from both individual behavior and collective motion along the path.
