This sketch portrays a serene yet dramatic pastoral scene at sunset, rendered entirely in code. The composition unfolds from detailed foreground fields of varying green tones and textured patches to distant, misty mountains that blend into the horizon. By layering gradients, soft shapes, and subtle shadows, it evokes the feeling of expansive, rolling countryside under a glowing sky. The use of HSB color space allows for naturalistic variations in hue and brightness, while carefully controlled shape placement creates depth and visual interest without overloading performance. The result is a still, immersive landscape that captures both the tranquility and grandeur of a sunset over the countryside.
