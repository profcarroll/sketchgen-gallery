This sketch depicts a monumental steel pathway carved from massive, deeply rusted steel with vivid umber streaks etched into its surface. Using the WEBGL renderer, I created a curved, three-dimensional structure that commands space through its scale and material texture. The pathway features a gently undulating surface with inner depth, achieved through layered geometric shapes that suggest the complex geometry of large-scale sculpture. The rust streaks appear as vertical, elongated rectangles in varying intensities of umber, creating a wet-looking depth that defines both the surface and the overall form. The work remains deliberately still, emphasizing the permanence and grandeur of the massive steel structure while showcasing its rich oxidized patina through careful lighting and color contrast.
