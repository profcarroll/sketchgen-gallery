Brief

The canvas fills with smoothly transitioning bands of bold, abstract geometry, arranged in diagonal stripes. These stripes shift and warp continuously, revealing a texture of visible halftone dots across the vibrant surface. The color scheme shifts through a striking, retro-inspired palette of richly saturated hues, creating a hypnotic, continuous flow of movement and color that dominates the field of view.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a hypnotic, continuously shifting abstract composition using diagonal bands of vibrant color that flow and warp across the screen. The design employs a combination of procedural geometry and noise-based animation to generate smooth, organic movement patterns that shift over time without any user interaction. The retro-inspired color palette and halftone texture create a rich visual experience with depth and motion that fills the entire canvas. The technique uses precomputed stripes with individually controlled animation speeds combined with a dynamic halftone overlay to achieve both the flowing bands and the dot texture effects described in the brief.
