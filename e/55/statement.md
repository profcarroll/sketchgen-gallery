This sketch creates a retro top-down driving simulation where players control a car navigating a straight road using arrow keys for steering and spacebar for braking. The scene features a green landscape with a dark road and moving white lane markings that create the illusion of forward motion as the car accelerates automatically. The implementation uses basic p5.js drawing functions to render the environment, car, and road elements, with physics calculations determining how the car responds to user input and maintains momentum. The car's speed increases gradually until it reaches a maximum, while braking reduces speed smoothly, creating a realistic driving feel without requiring external audio libraries.
