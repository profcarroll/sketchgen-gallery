This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of pulsating wave patterns that respond to both autonomous motion and user interaction. The waves, rendered in vibrant neon hues, simulate the steady rhythm of a heartbeat through their synchronized pulsing and subtle color shifts. When users drag their mouse across the canvas, the entire field distorts and reacts with increased amplitude, creating an intuitive interface for manipulating the perceived rhythm. The visualization uses a combination of sine wave functions and distance calculations to create organic, life-like motion while maintaining performance within strict frame budget constraints. The result is a dynamic, responsive system that bridges the gap between abstract animation and tactile interaction.
