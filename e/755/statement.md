This sketch creates a futuristic spaceship control panel with metallic grays and blacks featuring glowing cyan indicator lights. The interface includes physical-looking buttons, switches, and analog gauges arranged in a realistic configuration. When the user clicks the central hyperspace activation button, the entire panel remains stable while the windscreen display dynamically transitions into a spectacular hyperspace effect. The visualization shows streaks of brilliant color moving rapidly across the screen, with a central flash that pulses to simulate the ship's entry into hyperspace. The design uses p5.js drawing functions to create a cohesive, immersive control interface that responds to user interaction while maintaining the aesthetic of advanced technology.
