This sketch renders a three-dimensional funneled channel structure with realistic physics-based marble movement. The visualization features a series of nested cylindrical channels that gradually narrow from top to bottom, creating a funnel effect. A red marble rolls continuously through this structure under the influence of gravity, demonstrating natural physics interactions including momentum conservation and collision responses. The camera orbits the scene dynamically to provide an immersive perspective, allowing viewers to observe the marble's path as it navigates the narrowing channels. The implementation uses p5.js WEBGL mode for 3D rendering with proper lighting and material properties, while maintaining a stable physics simulation that runs continuously without user input.
