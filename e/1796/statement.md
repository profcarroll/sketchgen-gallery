This sketch depicts a vibrant tide pool ecosystem with animated anemones that trigger ripples when fully open. The water particles flow in subtle swirling currents, influenced by these newly formed ripples that propagate outward from the anemone centers. The interaction creates an organic flow pattern where the background water dynamics respond directly to the ripple effects, producing a continuous, evolving visual field. The scene combines natural color palettes with fluid motion to simulate the complex interactions within a tide pool environment.
