This sketch simulates a dynamic, microscopic ecosystem within a heated cauldron. Using WebGL rendering for performance, it features thousands of irregular microbes that move independently and respond to boundary collisions. The scene is dominated by sickly greens, purples, and ochres, with each microbe leaving a fading trail behind it. Microbes form temporary connections when they come close together, creating an organic network effect that continuously evolves. The simulation uses spatial hashing for efficient neighbor detection, avoiding expensive all-pairs distance calculations, and batches drawing operations to stay within frame budget constraints while maintaining visual complexity and fluid motion.
