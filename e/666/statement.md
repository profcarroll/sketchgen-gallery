This sketch visualizes the flow of data streams through a dark void using luminous ribbons that move in a controlled yet organic manner. Each stream is composed of multiple points that evolve over time, creating continuous trails of light that drift across the canvas. The composition uses a limited palette dominated by deep blacks and intense glowing blues and cyans to emphasize the contrast between emptiness and movement. The streams are not chaotic but form distinct, cohesive bands that shift subtly in position and opacity, suggesting a natural flow of information through space without requiring viewer interaction.
