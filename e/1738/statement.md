This sketch depicts the passage of time through the physical act of writing on aged paper. As text continuously flows across the canvas, it creates both visible creases and microscopic fractures in the paper's surface. The characters appear and move with a gentle warping effect, while the accumulated stress from the text stream manifests as permanent structural changes — hairline cracks that spiderweb outward from each character position. The effect suggests how writing imprints not just information but physical memory onto material surfaces, documenting the continuous process of aging and decay through mechanical stress.
