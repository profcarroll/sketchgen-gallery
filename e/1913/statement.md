This sketch depicts a stylized lighthouse with a rotating beam sweeping across a dark ocean. The tower is rendered in cool whites and deep blues, with a prominent light source at the top that illuminates the surrounding water. The beam rotates smoothly and continuously, creating dynamic washes of color across the canvas. The visualization uses polar coordinates to create the rotating effect, with the beam represented as a large triangular shape that sweeps across the screen. The ocean background shifts subtly in response to the beam's illumination, creating an atmospheric effect that captures the essence of a lighthouse beacon cutting through the night.
