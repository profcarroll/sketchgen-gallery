This sketch creates an evolving kaleidoscope pattern confined within a hexagonal frame. The visualization combines flowing curved segments with sharp geometric lines that create a dynamic interplay between fluid motion and defined stillness. As the pattern evolves, deep color gradients shift smoothly across the canvas, generating vibrant pockets of space that seem to actively repel surrounding energy. The hexagonal boundary provides a clear framing device while the kaleidoscopic effect creates an infinite, self-replicating pattern that continuously transforms without external input, fulfilling the requirement for autonomous visual motion.
