This sketch simulates an underwater scene viewed through a curved glass surface. Using a combination of semi-transparent bubbles, light shafts, and flowing currents, it creates a sense of depth and motion that mimics the fluid environment of water. The bubbles float slowly upward with gentle distortions, while shafts of light penetrate from above, creating a dappled pattern across the canvas. The color palette emphasizes blues and greens to evoke an aquatic atmosphere, with subtle ripples and highlights adding to the realism of the submerged view. The constant motion without external input satisfies the requirement for autonomous visual change.
