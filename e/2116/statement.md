This sketch creates an organic, self-generating environment of crystalline structures that evolve slowly across the canvas. The patterns emerge from a combination of growing geometric forms and floating particles that interact with each other through subtle interference patterns. The viewer witnesses an ethereal, biological search through space — where light structures bloom and fade like living organisms, leaving behind trails of luminous particles that create intricate interwoven networks. The result is a vast, luminous landscape that appears to grow organically, with each frame revealing new connections and evolving forms that seem to pulse with their own inner life.
