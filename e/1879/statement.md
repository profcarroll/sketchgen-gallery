This sketch depicts a vast, reflective cityscape undergoing a continuous cycle of bioluminescent illumination. The architecture features wet surfaces that shimmer and reflect the dynamic lighting, which transitions smoothly through day and night. Pulsing light sources create ripples across these reflective surfaces, and as these ripples dissipate, they leave behind faint phosphorescent residue trails that continue to glow and shift in response to the ambient light conditions. The entire scene evolves organically without user input, driven by a time-based animation that creates an immersive, ever-changing urban environment.
