This sketch creates a macroscopic visualization of a deep-sea subterranean environment near hydrothermal vents. The composition features massive dark crystalline structures that emerge from the rocky matrix, contrasted with delicate glowing particle networks that pulse with electric cyan and deep violet hues. These geometric patterns flow through the void like crystalline webs or fractal connections, representing the active mineral activity beneath the ocean floor. The crystals are rendered using multiple geometric forms with rotating orientations to suggest their natural formation, while the particle connections create an intricate web of light that shifts over time, capturing the dynamic energy of the underwater environment.
