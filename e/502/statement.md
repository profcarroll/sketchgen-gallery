This sketch creates a dynamic tessellation of interconnected hexagonal shapes that continuously evolve through rapid color transitions. Each hexagon in the grid independently cycles through hue, saturation, and brightness values, creating a mesmerizing interplay of light and color across the entire canvas. The pattern maintains its geometric structure while delivering a visually striking, ever-changing display where abrupt color shifts and stark contrasts create a hypnotic effect. The animation is entirely self-contained, requiring no user interaction to begin, and demonstrates how simple geometric forms can produce complex visual experiences through carefully orchestrated color dynamics.
