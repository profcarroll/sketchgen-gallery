Brief

The canvas displays a circular stovetop surface featuring multiple circular burners. The user interacts with a central dial to adjust the heat setting. Turning the dial activates natural gas flames, which appear above the burners. As the dial is set, the flames change both their height and their color in direct proportion to the setting. Furthermore, the metal ring surrounding any actively burning burner emits a glowing red luminescence.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an interactive stovetop interface where users can adjust heat settings via a central dial. When rotated, the dial activates flames above the burners, with flame height and color dynamically responding to the heat level. Each burner features a glowing red ring that intensifies with activation, while individual flame particles rise and fade realistically. The design uses efficient particle systems and gradient rendering to create visually engaging effects without exceeding performance budgets, allowing for smooth interaction even on constrained hardware.
