A serene procession of hot air balloons rises continuously through a warm, glowing sky. Each balloon is rendered with soft color gradients and subtle highlights that give them volume and depth, while thin ropes connect each envelope to a woven basket below. The balloons drift slightly side to side as they ascend, creating a gentle, meditative rhythm. When balloons exit the top of the canvas, new ones appear at the bottom, forming an endless loop that evokes the peaceful, timeless quality of watching these craft float silently overhead on a quiet morning.
