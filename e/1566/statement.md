This sketch depicts a futuristic, living cityscape where architecture and nature blend into an organic, bioluminescent metropolis. The scene features angular buildings with reflective surfaces that catch and reflect the shifting light of a cyclical day-night cycle. Wet architectural surfaces create rippling reflections that move across the structures as the viewer's perspective shifts. Floating plankton clusters drift through the environment, gathering in specific locations to illuminate glowing pools of bioluminescent life. The city pulses with organic rhythm, where plants and particles respond to an unseen light source, creating a sense of a living ecosystem rather than a static urban environment. The visual effect emphasizes the interplay between technology and nature through dynamic lighting, fluid motion, and the continuous cycle of illumination and shadow.
