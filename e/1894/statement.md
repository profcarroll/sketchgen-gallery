This sketch presents a monumental steel pathway that commands the viewer's attention through its dramatic form and architectural presence. The sculpture begins with a sweeping curve that transitions into a sharp perpendicular shift, then extends onto a vast horizontal plane bordered by a towering monolithic retaining wall. The rusted steel surfaces are rendered with careful attention to light and shadow, emphasizing the weight and texture of the material against the smooth surrounding space. The composition draws the viewer through the space, inviting exploration of the interplay between curved and angular elements, and highlighting the sculptural quality of the steel as it moves through three-dimensional space.
