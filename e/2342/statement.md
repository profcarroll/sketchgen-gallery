This sketch depicts a bioluminescent circulatory system within an abyssal anglerfish, rendered as glowing vessels drifting through the deep ocean's darkness. The vessels pulse with rhythmic light patterns in deep blues and neon accents, creating an ethereal flow that suggests life's energy circulating through the creature's body. Each vessel moves slowly through space while maintaining its own pulsing rhythm, forming a continuous network of luminous pathways that convey the organic, living nature of deep-sea existence. The constant motion and gentle pulsing create an immersive experience that captures both the mystery and vitality of abyssal depths.
