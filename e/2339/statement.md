This sketch renders a stylized cross-section of a rainforest leaf using a Victorian botanical aesthetic. The composition features evolving geometric forms that represent cellular structures, vein patterns, and boundary junctions within the leaf's anatomy. Through subtle animations and color variations, it captures the intricate, repetitive geometries found at the intersection of different material elements in plant tissue. The visual style combines the deep, graphic solidity of Edo-period Japanese construction with delicate scientific detailing, creating an architectural sense of depth that reveals the complex organization of natural forms as they slowly reorganize on screen.
