This sketch visualizes sound wave propagation from a central source using expanding, vibrating rings. A magnet at the center generates new rings every few frames, which grow outward while fading in transparency. The rings use a hue-based color scheme that shifts over time, creating a dynamic and continuous visualization of energy spreading from a single point. The animation demonstrates wave physics through simple geometric expansion, with each ring representing a moment in time as the wave travels outward across the canvas.
