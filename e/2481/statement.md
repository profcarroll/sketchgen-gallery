This sketch depicts a microscopic view of ceramic tile grout, revealing the subtle bioluminescent life that pulses through its mineral-filled fissures. The visual effect simulates the slow, cyclical movement of spectral veins beneath the surface, with glowing clusters drifting and pulsing in rhythmic patterns. Through careful use of particle systems, organic motion, and spatially-aware connections, the work creates an immersive, almost geological sense of subterranean activity that feels both alien and deeply alive. The viewer is invited to witness this quiet, continuous excavation of light within the mundane structure of everyday flooring.
