This sketch presents an abstract, three-dimensional origami crane constructed from geometric planes that continuously fold and unfold in a rhythmic dance. The sculpture exists as a perpetual metamorphosis, with its components shifting through space in synchronized motion. Amidst this orchestrated movement, one plane detaches itself from the central structure and begins an independent journey—drifting erratically through the three-dimensional space with its own momentum and rotation. The viewer witnesses both the collective unfolding of the crane's form and the solitary, drifting path of the detached piece, creating a visual narrative about separation and autonomy within a structured whole. The vivid colors and smooth motion emphasize the delicate, paper-like nature of the sculpture while the detached plane's erratic trajectory adds an element of unpredictability to the otherwise controlled animation.
