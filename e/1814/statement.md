This visualization portrays an atmospheric sonar scan that evolves from rhythmic pulses to crystalline structures. Starting at the center, expanding rings pulse outward with glowing intensity, their energy transforming into geometric patterns. As these pulses radiate, they solidify into intricate crystal formations that rotate and shimmer, suggesting the birth and dissolution of structured echoes within an ethereal atmosphere. The continuous animation creates a mesmerizing spectacle of light and form without any user interaction, filling the canvas with a self-sustaining display of luminous geometry.
