This sketch depicts a mechanical cross-section of an arcade cabinet from the 1980s, featuring interlocking copper gears and aged brass piping. The scene is rendered in 3D using p5.js WEBGL mode to create depth and perspective. Copper gears rotate rhythmically at different speeds, meshing together in synchronized motion while brass pipes form a branching network throughout the cabinet. Luminous data streams flow continuously along the pipe paths as glowing pulses that travel the predetermined routes, creating a sense of information transmission through the mechanical system. The viewer observes a complex, interconnected mechanism where the gears' rotation and the flowing streams combine to create a dynamic visualization of synchronized mechanical processes.
