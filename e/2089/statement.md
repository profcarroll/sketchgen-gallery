This sketch renders a dynamic, luminous tide pool environment using subtle particle systems and ripple interactions. The scene features thousands of water particles that flow with gentle, noise-driven movements, responding to periodic ripples that propagate outward from random points. These ripples create visible spiraling currents that radiate across the water surface, simulating the way light and motion interact in a crystal-clear marine environment. The constant background movement creates an immersive, perpetually flowing scene where particles subtly accelerate around ripple sources, forming a mesmerizing pattern of luminous, swirling flows.
