This sketch depicts an evolving garden scene where plants grow organically from the ground upwards, starting as small sprouts and gradually maturing into full plants with colorful fruit. The growth is steady and controlled rather than erratic, simulating a natural life cycle. As plants reach maturity, they begin to sway gently in a simulated breeze, adding subtle movement to the otherwise static growth process. The scene transitions from a barren landscape to a thriving garden over time, capturing the essence of organic development and atmospheric motion.
