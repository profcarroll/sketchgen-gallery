This sketch creates a mesmerizing field of interlocking hexagons that pulse rhythmically with warm amber tones, evoking the feeling of malleable wax. The hexagonal grid subtly drifts and breathes on its own, responding to mouse interaction by tilting and warping in 3D space. As the mouse is dragged across the canvas, each hexagon reacts dynamically to the motion, simulating a tactile, organic response. The entire composition uses WebGL rendering for smooth 3D effects while maintaining performance within strict frame budget constraints.
