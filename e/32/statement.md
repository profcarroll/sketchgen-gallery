This sketch creates a fixed, geometric self-portrait rendered in three-point perspective using WebGL mode. The composition features sharp, modeled forms representing a human head and neck constructed from basic geometric primitives like spheres, cones, and cylinders. The scene is set against a receding backdrop of multiple planes that transition smoothly across a subtle blue-to-purple-to-pink chromatic gradient, creating an illusion of depth through color shifts and layered perspective. The entire work is deliberately static, with no animation or motion, focusing on the interplay between rigid geometric construction and fluid color transitions to explore how mathematical forms can suggest organic features while maintaining architectural clarity.
