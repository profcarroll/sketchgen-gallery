This sketch renders a mesmerizing three-dimensional steampunk mechanism featuring a central rotating core surrounded by orbiting brass components. The scene employs a tarnished green color palette to evoke oxidation and age, with polished electrum elements contrasting the weathered surfaces. The entire structure exhibits autonomous motion through continuous rotation and particle movement, creating a kinetic spectacle of interlocking mechanical parts. The brass splines form complex orbital patterns around the central core, while amber-like droplets continuously drip from the polished core, illustrating the theme of decay within the mechanical world. The visual effect is achieved through careful use of WEBGL rendering to create depth and dimensionality, with all geometric elements precomputed for performance optimization.
