This sketch creates an immersive, contemplative composition using layered translucent rectangles that shift and blend as the viewer interacts with it. The visual depth emerges from subtle color transitions and overlapping planes, evoking the emotional resonance of atmospheric paintings. Without interaction, gentle movement occurs through time-based animation, but a single click intensifies the diffusion—deepening the colors and creating more dramatic gradients. The use of WebGL ensures smooth rendering of these layered effects across the canvas.
