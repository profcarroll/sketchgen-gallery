Brief

The composition features large, rectangular planes of color stacked vertically, evoking the emotional depth of atmospheric paintings. These translucent layers overlap subtly, creating rich, diffused gradients where colors meet. The palette is deep and contemplative. The visual movement is gentle and immersive, drawing the eye through the depth of the merging color fields. While the composition maintains a subtle, continuous glow, interacting with the canvas—for instance, by clicking—intensifies the diffusion.
