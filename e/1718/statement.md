This sketch visualizes an underground tunnel network where flowing water streams leave behind mineral deposits and sediment at junctions and bends. The scene depicts a subterranean geology system with dark earth tones dominating the background, bright blue-green water streams moving through intersecting tunnels, and accumulating sediment particles that highlight the most complex areas of the structure. The visualization captures both the dynamic flow of water and the static accumulation of geological material, creating a rich tapestry of movement and residue that maps the hidden underground environment.
