This sketch visualizes a gravitational system where multiple particles orbit a central point. Each particle follows Newtonian physics, experiencing a constant inward pull that creates stable orbits. However, at random intervals, particles break free from their orbital paths, accelerating outward before potentially returning to their trajectories. The system demonstrates the balance between gravitational attraction and kinetic energy, with each particle leaving a fading trail behind it. The visual effect is a dynamic interplay of order and chaos, where the central point acts as a gravitational anchor while individual particles exhibit unpredictable bursts of movement.
