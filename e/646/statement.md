This sketch creates an immersive, evolving space where luminous fields of light interact with sculptural forms that float and shift in three-dimensional space. The piece explores the interplay between transparency and opacity through alternating materials—obsidian blackness and crystalline translucence—that dynamically transform as they rotate. Particles form a dense, ever-shifting light field that pulses with color variations, while geometric forms like spheres and boxes move independently, creating an atmosphere of floating, weightless architecture. The color palette shifts from deep indigo to aquamarine tones, evoking both the mystery of space and the intensity of light itself.
