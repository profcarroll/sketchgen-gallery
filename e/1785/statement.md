This sketch creates an ethereal field of glowing, abstract forms that drift in slow, intricate spiral patterns. The shapes interact with each other, leaving luminous trails that crystallize into geometric anchors at points of collision. These anchors become highly reflective surfaces, mirroring the shifting colors and movement of nearby drifting forms. The visualization combines fluid motion with sharp geometric elements, creating a mesmerizing interplay between organic flow and structured reflection, all rendered in a continuous, self-contained animation that evolves without external input.
