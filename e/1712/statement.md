This sketch creates an ethereal jellyfish using WebGL rendering to achieve a luminous, crystalline appearance. The creature consists of a central body made of jagged polygonal segments and flowing tendrils composed of sharp, prismatic shards that catch and refract light. The piece uses constant animation to create a sense of slow, organic movement while maintaining the illusion of floating in darkness. The viewer observes a self-contained spectacle where light and motion are intrinsic properties of the form itself, without requiring any interaction or input from the user.
