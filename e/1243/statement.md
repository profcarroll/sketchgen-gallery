This sketch depicts a lattice structure composed of interlocking hexagons that gradually deteriorates under immense stress. The hexagonal pattern is rendered in 3D space using WebGL, with each hexagon displaying a subtle glow and dynamic warping effect as it ages. Electrical energy pulses travel across the surface, actively eroding the structure from the edges inward. The visual effect is achieved through particle-based animations where hexagons progressively lose their integrity over time, and glowing energy waves emanate from various points to simulate stress propagation. The viewer observes a continuous process of decay where the lattice breaks down organically, with each frame showing an evolution toward complete dissolution.
