This sketch creates an immersive, ever-shifting geometric environment where luminous planes float in three-dimensional space. The composition features 20 independently moving planes that drift through the scene with gentle rotations and subtle z-axis motion, creating a sense of depth and continuous movement. Glowing connections link nearby planes, forming a dynamic web that pulses with varying intensity. The entire scene slowly rotates on its axis, with the rotation speed oscillating rhythmically to create a sense of acceleration and deceleration. The visual effect combines vibrant neon colors with subtle transparency and layered geometry to produce an otherworldly, hypnotic experience that evolves continuously without any user input.
