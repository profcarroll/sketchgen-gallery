This sketch creates a vibrant, dynamic trail that follows the user's cursor movement. Each particle originates at the cursor position with a bright, saturated color and immediately begins to grow while gradually shifting toward softer pastel tones before fading away completely. The effect is achieved by maintaining a live array of particles that are continuously updated and rendered each frame, with each particle's properties (size, color, opacity) changing over time according to its age. The result is a visually engaging wake that tracks motion with smooth, organic transitions, creating a sense of fluidity and motion in response to the user's interaction.
