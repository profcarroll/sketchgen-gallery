This sketch presents a rhythmic clock face with a blue hour hand that pulses gently at first, mimicking the steady beat of a heart. When the viewer clicks anywhere on the canvas, the hand's movement dramatically increases, causing it to rapidly spin and sweep around the face until the motion naturally slows down again. The visual effect demonstrates a transition from calm to dynamic motion triggered by user interaction, using mathematical functions to control the timing and acceleration of the spinning hand. The design emphasizes smooth, continuous animation within p5.js constraints while maintaining clear visual feedback for both idle and interactive states.
