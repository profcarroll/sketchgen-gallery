This sketch presents an interactive visualization of the periodic table of elements. It displays a structured grid where each element is represented by a distinct box containing its symbol, atomic number, and name, arranged according to standard chemical groupings and periods. When a viewer clicks on any element, that specific box becomes highlighted and expands to show detailed information about that element in a side panel. The design maintains the familiar organizational structure of the periodic table while enabling direct user interaction to explore individual elements. The visualization uses a clean, color-coded approach that makes both the overall structure and individual details immediately accessible.
