This sketch creates a dynamic swarm of paper airplane shapes that move autonomously across the canvas. Each airplane has its own erratic trajectory influenced by flocking behaviors—separation, alignment, and cohesion—that create natural group movements while maintaining individual unpredictability. The collective behavior responds to mouse position, causing the swarm to shift and reform as if drawn by an invisible force. The visual design emphasizes a chaotic yet cohesive motion through randomized colors and angular shapes, creating an effect where the planes appear to drift independently while forming emergent patterns. The movement feels both organic and controlled, with each airplane maintaining its own velocity and direction even as it reacts to nearby peers and the cursor's influence.
