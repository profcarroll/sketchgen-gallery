This sketch creates an abstract tapestry by combining fluid, organic textile patterns with rigid geometric knots. The textile layers flow smoothly across the canvas using noise-based deformation, creating richly saturated color fields that shift subtly over time. Meanwhile, thousands of angular knot shapes move independently through the composition, their rotation and position controlled by a shared time variable. The result is a complex, multi-layered field where painterly motion meets structured geometry, producing an illusion of deep, constant, quiet motion without requiring any viewer input.
