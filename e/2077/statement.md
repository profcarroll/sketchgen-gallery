This sketch creates an immersive visualization of a deep space environment populated by a swarm of glowing bacterial cultures that move in the organic, exploratory patterns characteristic of slime mould. The cultures are represented as angular, luminous shapes that drift through the darkness with a decentralized motion system. Each culture follows flocking behaviors—separation, alignment, and cohesion—while also incorporating noise-based erratic movements to simulate the slow, meandering search patterns of biological systems. The result is an ethereal dance across the canvas where large gaps naturally form between groups, creating an impression of vastness and organic flow. The dark background emphasizes the glowing forms, while the continuous motion suggests a living ecosystem in constant exploration of its environment.
