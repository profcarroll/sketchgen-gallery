This sketch depicts a sun-baked arid savanna using a combination of layered shapes and subtle animation to evoke the harsh, textural landscape described in the brief. The ground is rendered with a deep terracotta base color and thousands of visible cracks that catch light at a low angle, creating dramatic shadows. A distant rock formation breaks the horizon, softened by atmospheric haze that gradually fades into the sky. Across this scene drift faint, organized dust devils—simple ellipses moving in gentle, unpredictable patterns—that add motion and life to the static environment. The composition relies on minimal color variation and simple geometric forms to achieve a strong visual contrast between the earthy ground and the pale sky, while the subtle animation of the dust devils ensures the canvas changes over time without requiring any input from the viewer.
