This sketch creates an interactive snowboarding game where players control a character through a procedurally generated course filled with rigid sidewalls. The player uses arrow keys to move and jump, with the spacebar initiating jumps. The snowboarder experiences physics-based movement including gravity and momentum, while collisions with walls cause visible recoil and damage accumulation. As damage increases, the player's health bar fills until it reaches capacity, ending the game. The camera follows the snowboarder as they navigate the course, creating a dynamic side-scrolling experience. The visual design emphasizes clear feedback through color changes during impacts and a simple geometric aesthetic that works within the performance constraints of the environment.
