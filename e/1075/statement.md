This sketch depicts two vibrant streams of colored particles flowing across the canvas with distinct directions and personalities. Each stream maintains its individual trajectory while gradually approaching the other. When the particles from both streams come into close proximity, they momentarily interact—pulling inward toward each other to form a radiant star-like knot where their paths converge. This interaction creates a brief but intense visual moment before the streams separate once more, resuming their contrasting journeys across the screen. The effect is achieved through careful physics simulation where particles attract when near and repel when too close, creating fluid motion that feels both natural and choreographed.
