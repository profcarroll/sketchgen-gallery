This sketch depicts a geological phenomenon unfolding in the depths of an obsidian void. A luminous fissure spreads rapidly through dark space, composed of translucent calcified resin that pushes against the surrounding darkness. The scene uses WebGL rendering to create a sense of vast subterranean pressure and dynamic energy, with the fissure expanding and contracting in a continuous, relentless motion. The viewer witnesses the boundary between light and darkness transforming into a spreading fault line, rendered through layered textures and animated geometry that suggests immense tectonic forces at work.
