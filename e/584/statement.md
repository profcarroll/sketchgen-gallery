This sketch presents an isometric view of a marble racing course constructed with solid track boundaries and a clearly defined goal area. A small marble navigates this course toward the goal, leaving behind a glowing bioluminescent trail that illuminates its path. The entire scene remains static until the viewer interacts with it by clicking anywhere on the canvas, which triggers the marble's movement. The visualization uses WebGL rendering to create a three-dimensional perspective with lighting effects and depth. The marble's motion is guided toward the goal area while maintaining visual consistency with the track's design. The bioluminescent path serves both as a functional element showing the marble's trajectory and as an aesthetic feature that enhances the visual experience of the race course.
