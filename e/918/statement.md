This sketch creates an immersive pond-like surface that responds to ambient sound. In its idle state, the canvas displays subtle, natural undulations through animated wave patterns and gently pulsing ripples. When sound is detected—triggered by a single click that initiates audio input—the visual behavior becomes more dynamic and intense. The intensity of the sound directly controls the size, speed, and number of ripples spreading across the water's surface. Colors shift subtly to convey depth and movement, with hues ranging from cool blues to deeper purples. The effect is designed to be both calming and responsive, creating an environment where viewers can observe how sound influences visual form through a fluid, organic interaction.
