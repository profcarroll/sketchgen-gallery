This sketch renders a vast crystalline network suspended in space, composed of interconnected nodes that pulse with deep violet light. The structure forms a static yet dynamically animated web where each point emits expanding rings of luminescence. The visual effect emerges from the interplay between fixed geometric connections and constantly shifting glow patterns, creating an ethereal, celestial blueprint that appears both fragile and enduring. The viewer observes a mesmerizing field of light that pulses with harmonic rhythm, revealing the underlying architecture of a luminous, crystalline universe.
