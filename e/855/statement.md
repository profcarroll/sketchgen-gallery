This sketch presents a highly abstracted, geometric self-portrait rendered entirely through interlocking planar facets in a 3D space. The structure is composed of angular polygons arranged around a central axis to form a faceted, crystalline form that emphasizes depth and spatial contrast. Sharp directional lighting creates dramatic shadows and intense specular highlights across the surfaces, carving out the form's volume and emphasizing its geometric precision. The piece evokes the sensation of carved stone or cut crystal, where each facet reflects ambient light differently, creating a luminous, almost ethereal quality while maintaining a strong sense of structural integrity and visual balance.
