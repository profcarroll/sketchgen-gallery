This sketch creates a mesmerizing kaleidoscope pattern that continuously transforms through a vibrant sunset color palette. The visualization consists of multiple concentric layers of geometric segments arranged in radial symmetry, with each layer rotating at a different speed to create hypnotic motion. The colors smoothly cycle through deep oranges, fiery reds, and brilliant yellows using the HSB color model, producing an ethereal glow effect. The pattern's complexity emerges from the interplay between the radial symmetry and the dynamic deformation of geometric shapes, creating an illusion of perpetual, luminous motion without any external input or user interaction.
