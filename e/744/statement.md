This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of luminous, structured forms that cascade downward in sheets of vibrant emerald and cyan light. The sheets gently swell and contract with pulsating ripples, creating a continuous transformation between fluid liquid matter and crisp crystalline arrays. As each sheet reaches its peak pulse, it temporarily solidifies into precise geometric crystal structures before returning to its flowing state. The dark environment enhances the glowing effect, with the WEBGL renderer enabling dynamic 3D movement and rotation of the forms as they drift downward across the canvas.
