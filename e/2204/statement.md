This sketch depicts the slow, chemical passage of time through the formation of rust patterns on steel. Rather than showing a static geological structure, it captures the ongoing process of oxidation as reddish dendritic networks slowly spread across a dark metallic surface. The work emphasizes the weight and permanence of geological time by rendering the delicate yet immense process of decay in a single, still moment. Through careful attention to color palette—deep reds and oranges against dark steel tones—and branching patterns that suggest natural growth, the viewer is invited to contemplate how slowly but inexorably materials transform over vast periods of time.
