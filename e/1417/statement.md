This sketch creates an immersive visualization of luminous geometric forms cascading downward in a dark void. Using the WEBGL renderer, it generates hundreds of structured, glowing tori and spheres that drift and rotate with fluid motion, evoking sheets of liquid light flowing toward the bottom of the frame. The color palette is restricted to vibrant emerald and cyan hues, creating a mesmerizing, ethereal effect. Each form has unique properties including size, speed, and rotation, contributing to the sense of perpetual, cyclical motion. The forms continuously reset when they fall below the canvas, maintaining an endless flow that fills the screen with dynamic, glowing geometry.
