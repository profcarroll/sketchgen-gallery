This sketch creates a three-dimensional visualization of a DNA double helix using p5.js's WEBGL renderer. The structure consists of two intertwined strands made of colored spheres representing the four nucleotide bases (Adenine, Thymine, Cytosine, and Guanine), each assigned a distinct color. The helix continuously rotates to show its three-dimensional form, with the viewer able to interact by dragging the mouse to rotate the entire structure. The animation simulates the dynamic nature of DNA, where the strands can appear to unfold and reform as they spin, offering an educational yet visually engaging representation of genetic architecture.
