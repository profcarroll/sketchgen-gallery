This sketch renders an abyssal landscape of geometric pyramidal structures emerging from the deep-sea floor. Using WEBGL rendering, it creates a crystalline environment where deep currents dynamically organize luminous particles into flowing streams. The particles shed bioluminescent residue that drifts across the seabed, forming visible patterns at intersections of current streams. The scene pulses with electric blues and vibrant bioluminescence as pyramids cast dynamic shadows and particle systems respond to continuous flow fields. The composition balances mathematical precision with organic motion, creating an otherworldly underwater geometry that evolves without external input.
