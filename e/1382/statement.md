This sketch creates a dynamic, interactive field of fluid waves that shift between deep blues and warm reds. The waves pulse slowly across the canvas, forming an ever-changing pattern of interconnected forms that suggest underlying pressure and motion. When users drag their mouse across the surface, localized tremors radiate outward, intensifying the wave patterns and creating visible stress points where the color gradient is momentarily disrupted. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of sine wave animations, color mapping, and real-time interaction handling, all within strict performance constraints to ensure smooth operation in any browser environment.
