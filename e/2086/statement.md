This sketch depicts a cross-section of an immense terrestrial root system preserved in mineral resin, viewed from the deep ocean floor. The work captures the ancient, petrified structure with its earthy brown and gray tones, rendered through countless organic spheres that form the root matrix. Luminous veins of vibrant blue and green light pulse rhythmically throughout the structure, creating an illusion of contained life force and continuous data flow. The viewer observes this colossal underground network suspended in darkness, with the pulsing veins giving the hardened root system a sense of active, hidden vitality. The organic motion of the veins contrasts with the solid, mineral composition of the roots, creating a visual tension between stillness and energy.
