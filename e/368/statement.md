This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic environment where translucent, overlapping circles flow continuously across the canvas, representing a vast, slow-moving space. Scattered throughout this flow are pointillist stars that leave luminous trails as they drift, occasionally triggering momentary flares at their overlap points. These flares expand into distinct rings of radiating, dissipating light, creating visual echoes that brighten and fade in the deep background. The scene is filled with subtle structural wakes that briefly illuminate the cosmic flow, creating a sense of depth and continuous motion without overwhelming detail or excessive computational load.
