This sketch creates an organic hand-stitching effect by having a virtual sewing needle follow the cursor's path. Each stitch is rendered as a discrete segment of thread that varies in both length and spacing between consecutive stitches. The variation is based on the speed and direction of mouse movement, creating a natural-looking seam that responds dynamically to how the user drags their cursor across the canvas. The gold-colored thread appears to be sewn onto a grid-patterned fabric surface, with the needle's position marked by a small visual indicator. The result mimics the irregularity and rhythm of actual hand-sewing through subtle variations in stitch geometry rather than randomization alone.
