This sketch depicts a mechanical shredding apparatus in motion, capturing the violent destruction of paper through the grinding action of rotating gears. The composition focuses on the interaction between the static machinery and the falling books that are torn apart by the mechanism. As books descend into the shredder, they disintegrate into fine particles and sharp splinters that fly outward. Steam vents from the friction points of the gears, while dark residue accumulates on the metal surfaces. The viewer observes a continuous mechanical process where material is transformed from solid objects into fragmented debris through the relentless grinding motion of the rotating gears.
