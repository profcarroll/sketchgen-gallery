This sketch portrays a serene alpine landscape dominated by muted blues, whites, and greys under an atmospheric sky. The scene features scattered jagged rock formations resting on snow-dusted ground, with distant peaks shrouded in mist to suggest depth and chill. Upon clicking anywhere on the canvas, an avalanche is triggered: previously stable large rock formations scatter dramatically across the foreground, altering the landscape. The effect uses particle physics for falling debris and creates a sense of motion through gravity and wind forces. The viewer experiences both the stillness of the initial vista and the dynamic disruption caused by their interaction.
