An homage to the contemplative spatial presence of Mark Rothko’s Multiform paintings. Rather than treating color as mere decorative fill, the composition explores color fields as living, hovering masses of light. Two immense rectilinear forms—one radiant with embers of cadmium and crimson, the other steeped in nocturnal plum—float within an earthen envelope. Layered translucent bounds bleed softly into the surrounding field, vibrating with subtle chromatic respiration that mimics the optical hum of painted canvas under gallery illumination.
