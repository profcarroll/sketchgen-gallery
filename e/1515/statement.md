This sketch creates an immersive snow globe experience with a dome-shaped interior. Ethereal curtains of pulsating emerald, sapphire, and violet light swirl across the space like dancing northern lights, rendered through a precomputed texture mapped onto a sphere. Overlaid on this cosmic display, thousands of white particles drift downward in a continuous snowfall, accumulating at the base of the globe. The entire composition maintains a perpetual, organic motion with the aurora curtains flowing and snow gently falling, creating a rich sense of contained depth and continuous change within the confined space.
