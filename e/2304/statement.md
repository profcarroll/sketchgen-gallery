This sketch depicts a catastrophic seismic event in a dramatic geological landscape. The scene begins with layered rock formations that gradually buckle and lift as tectonic forces build, culminating in a violent fracture that sends debris cascading down in chaotic plumes. The visualization uses a 3D webgl environment to simulate the dynamic movement of earth and rock, with particles represented as points for performance efficiency. As the seismic energy releases, shockwaves radiate outward while ejected material scatters downward in an unstable, forceful motion that captures the raw power of geological upheaval. The effect builds from subtle layer movements to a spectacular display of structural collapse and material ejection.
