This sketch renders an evolving geometric tapestry where neon lines pulse and flicker across the canvas before stabilizing into permanent glowing forms. It begins with a grid of animated points that pulse with noise-based motion, creating a sense of energy and instability. As the animation progresses, these lines gradually stabilize, their pulsations diminishing until they become solid, luminous structures. The composition is enriched with architectural fragments that appear and fade, adding layers of complexity to the emerging patterns. The visual effect emphasizes transformation from chaos to order, where the ephemeral neon lines resolve into fixed, glowing geometric forms across the canvas.
