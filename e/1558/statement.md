This sketch simulates an evolving cellular automaton that generates complex, organic-looking patterns through color-shifting states. The system begins with a dynamic grid where cells transition between dormant, transitioning, and active states according to modified Conway's Game of Life rules. As time progresses, the patterns continuously flow and reshape, creating intricate structures that appear to grow and interact without external input. Color transitions shift over time, giving the impression of living forms emerging from chaos, with warm and cool hues creating visual depth and movement across the canvas. The simulation runs autonomously, producing an ever-shifting tapestry of interconnected geometric patterns.
