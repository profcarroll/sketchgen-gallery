This sketch creates an immersive visualization of a continuously flowing newsprint surface with vibrant ink streaks that transform into geometric crystalline patterns when the viewer interacts. The work captures the essence of beeswax melting and coalescing into patterned sheets through the metaphor of printing ink forming stable, luminous structures. The canvas displays an endless sheet of aged paper that scrolls from the edges, filled with erratic ink particles that move in organic, flowing patterns. When clicked, this continuous motion pauses, allowing the fluid ink to stabilize and form sharp, glowing geometric crystals that briefly illuminate the surface before the paper flow resumes its rhythmic movement.
