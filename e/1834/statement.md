This sketch depicts a cosmic expanse filled with billions of twinkling stars and the diffuse glow of the Milky Way. At the center of the canvas, a radiant light wave originates from a distant point source and rapidly expands outward in all directions. The wave begins its journey after a brief delay, growing continuously until it sweeps across the entire visible field of view. The animation conveys the immense, expanding energy moving through the starfield, with the wave's leading edge highlighted by increased brightness and width. The background nebula slowly shifts to enhance the sense of cosmic motion, while stars pulse gently in unison to create a cohesive visual rhythm. The scene uses WebGL rendering for efficient particle handling and smooth animation, creating an immersive depiction of cosmic expansion.
