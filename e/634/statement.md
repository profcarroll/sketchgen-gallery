This sketch creates a glowing particle trail that follows the cursor's movement, with particles dissipating over time. The visual effect is achieved through a particle system where new particles are generated at the cursor position when pressed, and each particle has its own velocity, size, and fading alpha value. The background uses a semi-transparent fill to create a trailing fade effect, while particles themselves gradually lose opacity and shrink until they disappear entirely. The system dynamically manages particle lifecycle to maintain performance within the frame budget constraints, producing a continuous, colorful wake that responds to cursor motion but also fades naturally when idle.
