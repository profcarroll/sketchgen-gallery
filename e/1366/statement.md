This sketch creates an immersive, shifting architectural space using a particle system that flows through a deep, aquatic palette of muted teals, violets, and pale greys. The particles undulate in a constant, hypnotic motion, forming an ever-reforming background that evokes the sensation of navigating an impossible, transitional realm. When viewers drag their cursor across the canvas, they can sculpt pathways through this dreamlike environment, leaving solid traces that respond to their movement. The scene is rendered using WebGL for efficient 3D rendering, with dynamic lighting and camera movement that creates a sense of depth and constant transformation, as if exploring infinite, unseen halls.
