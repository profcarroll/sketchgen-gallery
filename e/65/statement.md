This sketch creates an ethereal, pulsating lattice of glowing lines that shimmer and reconfigure slowly across the canvas. The structure appears fragile and alive, with each line gently pulsing in opacity and subtly moving to create a sense of continuous motion. Occasional gaps appear randomly in the network, causing segments to momentarily break connectivity and reform themselves. The viewer witnesses a mesmerizing interplay of light and shadow as the delicate web of connections constantly renews itself, evoking a feeling of delicate, ever-changing architecture that exists in a state of perpetual flux.
