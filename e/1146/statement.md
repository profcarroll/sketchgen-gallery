This sketch creates a continuous first-person journey through an abandoned, weathered concrete landscape. Using the WEBGL renderer, it builds a grid of monolithic structures with randomized dimensions and positions, each featuring moss streaks and rust marks to suggest decay. The viewer experiences automated forward motion through this desolate environment, with camera movement simulating travel. The visual palette emphasizes desaturation and muted tones to evoke the feeling of a forgotten brutalist campus, where concrete surfaces bear the marks of time and environmental forces.
