This sketch renders a geological cross-section depicting layered sedimentary rock formations in earthy tones of tan, ochre, and deep slate. Within these strata, intricate spiral patterns reminiscent of nautilus shells and fossil forms emerge and pulse rhythmically. A continuous wave-like motion simulates the ebb and flow of tides, causing the seashell patterns to drift and reshape themselves across the sedimentary layers. The visualization uses a single mesh for each sediment layer to maintain performance while creating an immersive geological scene that evolves slowly over time.
