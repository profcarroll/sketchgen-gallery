This sketch creates an evolving medieval tapestry where dyed wool threads form the foundational structure, interwoven with pulsing crystalline veins that represent internal energy. The composition breathes with subtle color shifts and rhythmic pulses, creating a sense of organic life within the static weave. The background threads establish a rich, textured base while the veins pulse and connect in a network that evolves over time, giving the impression of a living, breathing textile artifact.
