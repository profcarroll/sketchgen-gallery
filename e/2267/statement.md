This sketch renders a desolate, cinematic landscape of towering geothermal vents that resemble rugged mountain ranges. Using WebGL rendering and optimized geometry, it creates a vast terrain of mineral formations in deep earth tones of ochre, charcoal, and rust. The vents pulse rhythmically with a subtle expansion and contraction effect, simulating the slow breathing of geological forces. Faint glowing heat emanates from within the stony fissures, casting a dim light across the barren landscape. The visualization balances visual complexity with performance constraints, creating an immersive vista that captures both the grandeur and the quiet power of these immense natural structures.
