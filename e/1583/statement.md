This sketch creates an immersive, ever-changing indoor space centered around a reflective water pool. The scene uses WebGL rendering to build a sense of depth with carefully positioned walls and floor planes. Subtle ripples on the water's surface generate dynamic distortions that cast shifting shadows across the vertical walls and onto the floor below. The color palette employs deep ambient tones punctuated by bright light reflections, creating an atmosphere of calm contemplation. The continuous motion arises from the interplay between the gently undulating water surface and the constantly evolving shadow patterns, all without any user input or external stimuli—this is a self-contained, perpetually transforming environment that captures the quiet drama of light and reflection within a confined architectural space.
