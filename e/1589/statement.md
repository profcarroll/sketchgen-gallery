This sketch visualizes an erratic electrocardiogram (EKG) signal as a glowing neon line scrolling continuously across a black background. The waveform is generated algorithmically with irregular peaks and dips that mimic the unpredictable nature of unstable biological signals. The visualization uses p5.js's drawing context to apply a soft neon glow effect, creating a vibrant, dynamic display that captures the chaotic energy of a distressed heart rhythm. The scrolling motion simulates real-time data flow while maintaining the visual impact of an urgent medical signal.
