This sketch presents a serene pastoral scene with a bright, sun-drenched landscape. The composition features layered elements: a gradient sky transitioning from warm orange to cool blue, distant mountain ranges rendered in soft, blurred forms, and rolling green fields that create a sense of depth and texture. The visual technique employs simple geometric shapes and gradients to evoke the feeling of sunlight playing across terrain, with subtle variations in color and form to suggest natural complexity. The static nature of the scene invites contemplation while maintaining the illusion of a vast, atmospheric landscape.
