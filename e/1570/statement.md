This sketch presents an immersive, meditative exploration of cosmic light and motion. Floating within a dark void are luminous geometric forms—tori and spheres that shimmer with iridescent hues, drifting silently through space. Each form pulses gently in color and position, creating a hypnotic rhythm that evokes the feeling of liquid light flowing through infinite depths. The visual experience is entirely self-contained, requiring no input or interaction, as the forms continuously evolve on their own, driven by subtle physics and time-based transformations. This creates a serene, contemplative space where structure and motion blend into an ever-shifting, luminous dreamscape.
