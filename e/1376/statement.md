This sketch creates a drone's-eye view through an abandoned Brutalist college campus, capturing the imposing scale and decaying grandeur of concrete architecture. Using WebGL rendering, it generates a maze-like environment of geometric structures that seem to stretch infinitely into the distance. The camera smoothly glides along a predetermined path, revealing the repetitive, monumental facades that define Brutalist design — massive walls, stark planes, and raw concrete textures that evoke both the power and desolation of institutional architecture. The muted gray palette emphasizes the weathered state of these buildings, while the continuous animation suggests an autonomous journey through empty spaces, highlighting the scale and isolation of this architectural environment.
