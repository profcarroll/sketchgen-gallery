Brief

The canvas displays a detailed, circular stovetop surface. The user controls the heat output by interacting with a dial placed on the surface. When heat is applied, the metal surrounding the burners visibly glows red. Simultaneously, vibrant gas flames appear over the burners, and their color transitions smoothly across the burner based on the heat setting, shifting from cool blue to warm orange as the intensity increases.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a detailed stovetop surface with four burners arranged in a circle. Users interact with a central dial to control heat output across the burners. When heat is applied, the metal glows red around each burner and vibrant gas flames appear above them, transitioning smoothly from cool blue to warm orange as intensity increases. The visualization includes subtle shimmer effects on the stovetop surface and realistic flame particle systems that respond dynamically to user input. The implementation stays within strict performance constraints while maintaining visual complexity through careful optimization of drawing operations and particle management.
