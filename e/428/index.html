Brief

The canvas displays a dynamic grid of colored cells that represent timing and musical patterns. When the user clicks, a rhythmic sequence of shapes and colors illuminates across the grid, allowing the user to trigger and manipulate beats visually. Furthermore, the entire grid responds dynamically to external audio sources; the colors and overall pulsing intensity shift in time with the recorded sound, creating a live visual feedback loop between the microphone and the screen.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a dynamic visual representation of musical timing and rhythm through an interactive grid of colored cells. Each cell's hue, saturation, and brightness respond to live audio input from the microphone, creating a real-time color palette that pulses with the bass frequencies. When users click anywhere on the canvas, a rhythmic sequence illuminates across the grid, triggering visual beats that propagate through the system. The entire grid continuously reacts to audio patterns, with the intensity of the color shifts and pulsing effects directly tied to the volume and frequency content of the recorded sound. This creates an immersive feedback loop where users can both trigger and observe musical patterns as they manifest visually on screen.
