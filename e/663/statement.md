This sketch depicts an electrical circulatory system emerging from a rigid structural grid. The visual effect is created through numerous streams of light that originate randomly across the canvas and meander in complex paths. Each stream begins with a single point and gradually builds intensity as it progresses, causing the individual pulses to coalesce into thicker, more prominent conduits of luminous energy. The pulsing rhythm of these streams creates a sense of life within the system, as if electricity is flowing through an immense, living circuitry against the static backdrop of its geometric foundation. The technique uses particle-based animation with intensity buildup over time, all rendered in WebGL for performance and visual clarity.
