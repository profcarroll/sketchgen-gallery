This sketch presents a contemplative visualization of paper-cut ships preserved in geological time. The scene depicts vessels navigating through a translucent sedimentary landscape where mineral deposits have solidified their fragile forms. Ships emerge from the background with gentle motion and leave trails of sediment particles that accumulate over time. As they drift, these craft gradually decay into crystalline geodes—delicate structures that encase them in mineral scaffolding. The juxtaposition of vibrant paper edges against muted earth tones emphasizes the passage of deep time, transforming ephemeral paper crafts into enduring geological artifacts. The work explores themes of preservation, transformation, and the quiet accumulation of history through simple, repeated processes.
