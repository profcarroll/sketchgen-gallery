This sketch depicts a vibrant shallow tide pool environment with animated rocks, sand base, and swaying anemones. The key interaction is that when users move their mouse near the anemones, they respond by opening or closing. When anemones are fully open, this triggers a dramatic visual effect: bright, sparkling sunlight reflections appear on the water surface. The scene dynamically responds to both the natural movement of the anemones and user proximity, creating an immersive underwater experience where the environment reacts to interaction. The visual feedback is immediate and visually striking, with the entire water surface brightening and sparkling as a result of the anemones' openness state.
