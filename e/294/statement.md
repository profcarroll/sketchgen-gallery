This sketch creates a dark, immersive dreamscape populated by glowing particles that move through three-dimensional space. The scene is rendered using WebGL for depth and dynamic lighting effects. Particles float slowly in a vast void, occasionally forming connections as they approach each other, creating an evolving crystalline network. The camera orbits around the scene, revealing different perspectives of the glowing structures. The entire environment pulses with subtle motion, shifting colors and patterns that suggest underlying organic systems or cosmic forces at work. The viewer experiences an abstract representation of interconnectedness within a void, where light and shadow define form through their relationships rather than solid presence.
