Brief

The canvas presents a perpetually cyclical cityscape bathed in the dramatic transition between day and night. Bioluminescent flora pulse with pulsing, distinct light that casts visible projections across highly reflective, wet architectural surfaces. These pulses generate visible, dissipating ripples across the wet structures. These ripples, in turn, cause temporary and visible distortions within the reflections, making the rigid city geometry appear fluid and fractured. The entire scene operates within a continually changing, unconventional color palette, emphasizing the complex interplay between solid structure and moving light.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive, ever-shifting cityscape that transitions between day and night through a constantly evolving color palette. The scene features towering reflective buildings whose surfaces distort and ripple with bioluminescent pulses from glowing flora scattered throughout the environment. The architecture appears to breathe and flow due to these visual ripples that temporarily warp reflections across wet, gleaming surfaces. The entire composition operates in real-time, with no external input required - the cityscape evolves autonomously through carefully orchestrated lighting changes, pulsing organic elements, and dynamic reflection effects that transform the rigid geometry of urban structures into fluid, living forms.
