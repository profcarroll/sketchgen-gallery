This sketch creates an immersive digital landscape defined by a glowing grid of interconnected hexagonal modules. Each module pulses with electric blue neon light, creating rhythmic waves that propagate along the connections between adjacent units. The visual effect emerges from a continuous interplay of color cycling, radial pulsing, and dynamic network connectivity. Viewers witness an algorithmic ecosystem where inactive nodes periodically activate, causing the wave patterns to shift and flow across the entire canvas in a mesmerizing display of digital life.
