This sketch creates an immersive, derelict tunnel network using WebGL rendering to achieve deep perspective and spatial immersion. The viewer experiences a shifting underground environment where damp concrete walls reveal mineral deposits and graffiti through subtle texturing techniques. As the viewer drags their cursor across the screen, the perspective rotates and shifts, guiding the eye deeper into the labyrinthine space. The tunnel segments are procedurally generated with varying dimensions and rotations to create an organic, abandoned feel, while soft directional lighting casts deep shadows that enhance the sense of depth and decay. The motion is continuous but subtle, creating a compelling visual narrative without overwhelming the viewer.
