The canvas is alive with the ghostly trails of skaters who never stop. Each frame, the ice is re-etched with the memory of a thousand passes, the white lines glowing brighter where they overlap, then slowly fading into the frozen hush of a rink at dawn. I'm using a probabilistic fading system where each line segment has a chance to thin or disappear, creating natural variation in thickness and persistence, while the mouse click resets the entire memory of the ice surface.
