This sketch explores the dynamic interplay between order and chaos through rhythmic expansions of color and light. Red and black pulses emerge randomly across a dark canvas, growing rapidly and leaving behind trails that fade into the background. At times, these expanding forms distort into jagged, asymmetric ripples that break their circular symmetry before coalescing into large networks. The visual rhythm alternates between sudden bursts of activity and slower, more resonant fades, creating an evolving pattern that feels both organic and mechanical. The gentle drift of the larger networks mimics suspended raindrops caught in a woven loom, suggesting a delicate balance between movement and stillness.
