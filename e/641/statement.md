This sketch visualizes a dynamic lattice of glowing cells that continuously birth, grow, and decay in an endless cycle. The simulation creates a mesmerizing field of interconnected particles where new cells emerge with soft glows, linking to nearby neighbors through thin connecting lines. As cells mature and begin to decay, they flare brightly before dissolving into fading trails of light. The entire scene pulses rhythmically against a dark background, emphasizing the organic life cycle within the pattern. The visualization evolves autonomously without any user input, creating an immersive, ever-changing display that feels both mechanical and alive.
