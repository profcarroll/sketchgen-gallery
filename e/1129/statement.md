This sketch presents a minimalist mechanical clock with a clean, circular face and no markings. Two distinct hands—hour and minute—move in synchronized perpetual motion to indicate time. The hour hand is shorter and thicker, while the minute hand is longer and thinner, both rendered in a brushed nickel finish. The design emphasizes precision and elegance through continuous, cyclical movement without any numbers or indices, relying solely on the relative positions of the hands for time reading. The neutral background and simple geometric forms create a sense of calm minimalism, focusing attention on the fluid motion of the clock mechanism.
