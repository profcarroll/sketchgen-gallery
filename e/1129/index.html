Brief

The canvas displays a clean, circular clock face set against a neutral background. The time is conveyed solely by two distinct, metallic hands that feature a brushed nickel finish. These hands maintain a constant relationship, with the shorter hour hand and the longer minute hand rotating in synchronized, perpetual motion. The face remains unmarked by any numbers or indices, forcing the viewer to interpret the exact time purely through the relative positions and movement of the two polished metallic pointers. The visual elements suggest mechanical precision and minimalist elegance, keeping time through continuous, cyclical motion without requiring any user input to function.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch presents a minimalist mechanical clock with a clean, circular face and no markings. Two distinct hands—hour and minute—move in synchronized perpetual motion to indicate time. The hour hand is shorter and thicker, while the minute hand is longer and thinner, both rendered in a brushed nickel finish. The design emphasizes precision and elegance through continuous, cyclical movement without any numbers or indices, relying solely on the relative positions of the hands for time reading. The neutral background and simple geometric forms create a sense of calm minimalism, focusing attention on the fluid motion of the clock mechanism.
