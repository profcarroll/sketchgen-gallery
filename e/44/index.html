Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an immersive, hypnotic journey through deep space using p5.js's WEBGL renderer. The composition fills the canvas with a star-filled void, where large, stylized typography elements float toward the viewer from various depths. Each letter is rendered with volumetric quality through careful manipulation of depth-of-field effects—blurring elements as they approach and sharpening them as they recede. The text continuously cycles through cosmic-themed words like "NEBULA" and "STELLAR," moving through a simulated three-dimensional space while the camera slowly orbits around the scene. The entire experience is self-contained, requiring no user interaction, creating an automatic meditation on cosmic scale and movement. The use of WEBGL ensures that all rendering occurs in 3D space, with proper perspective and depth effects that make each text element appear to float within a vast, dark cosmos.
