This sketch creates an immersive deep-sea geological cross-section where layers of rock strata are revealed beneath the ocean floor. Intricate glowing deposits resembling woven threads pulse and shift subtly through the fixed geological surface, creating patterns that appear to emanate from within the matrix. The coloring uses a palette dominated by deep blues and brilliant cyan to evoke natural bioluminescence, with each thread deposit exhibiting a gentle pulsing effect that suggests life force flowing through the ancient rock layers.
