This sketch depicts an abstract garden ecosystem where luminous particles represent the cyclical process of life and collection. Organic, glowing particles spiral outward from their origins and gradually spiral inward toward central structures—pods and blooms—where they accumulate to form dense clusters. The scene evolves through continuous motion without any user input, showing how elements naturally draw together in an idealized environment. Particles animate in spirals before being attracted to the nearest available pod or bloom, creating a visual representation of growth, gathering, and completion within a vibrant, dreamlike landscape.
