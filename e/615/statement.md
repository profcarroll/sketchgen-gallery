This sketch creates a mesmerizing, self-sustaining field of glowing, organic shapes that drift and evolve across a deep, dark canvas. As the larger forms move and eventually dissipate, they break into countless smaller glowing particles that flow downward, pooling briefly before fading away. The continuous motion and natural decay of these elements create an immersive, hypnotic experience—each frame alive with subtle shifts in light and form, evoking a sense of organic life and cosmic calm.
