This sketch creates a rigid, grid-based typography composition inspired by the Swiss School aesthetic. Using a precise 12-column grid system, it arranges large, bold headlines alongside supporting text in geometric blocks that emphasize structure and visual contrast. The design employs pure sans-serif letterforms with careful attention to alignment, spacing, and negative space to achieve a highly formal, disciplined appearance. The entire composition is static and unchanging, presenting a finalized graphic that exemplifies the precision and clarity characteristic of Swiss design principles.
