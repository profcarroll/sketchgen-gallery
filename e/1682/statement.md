This sketch depicts a wooden case filled with a pile of coins that exhibits subtle, localized ripples and settling movements. The scene captures the organic, ever-changing nature of a coin pile through physics-based animation where individual coins respond to gravity, friction, and boundary collisions. Every so often, the entire pile undergoes small, wave-like disturbances that cause sections to shift and settle, creating a mesmerizing effect of continuous motion without abrupt changes. Viewers observe this quiet activity as a natural process of accumulation and reorganization, emphasizing the tension between stability and movement in a contained environment.
