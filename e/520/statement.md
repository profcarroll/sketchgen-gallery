This sketch depicts a serene pastoral landscape with rolling green fields stretching into the distance, punctuated by a distant mountain range. The composition uses layered shapes to create depth, with the mountains rendered as triangular forms in a softer blue hue against the sky. The fields are represented by soft ellipses with varying sizes and opacities to suggest natural unevenness, while subtle textured grass elements add visual complexity without overwhelming the scene. The color palette emphasizes lush greens and warm sunlight tones, creating a calm, static vista that evokes the feeling of an untouched countryside. The entire scene is rendered once using simple geometric shapes and strokes, maintaining a consistent, motionless aesthetic throughout.
