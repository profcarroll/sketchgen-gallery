This sketch presents an abstract, three-dimensional origami crane constructed from interconnected geometric planes that continuously fold and unfold in a rhythmic metamorphosis. The sculpture appears to breathe with organic motion despite its rigid mathematical structure, as each plane rotates and repositions according to sine and cosine functions that create harmonious, flowing transitions. The color palette consists of matte, sharp hues that define the crane's structure without visual distraction, emphasizing the interplay between geometric form and fluid motion. The entire composition operates independently in an endless cycle of folding and unfolding, creating a mesmerizing effect through its careful balance of mathematical precision and organic rhythm.
