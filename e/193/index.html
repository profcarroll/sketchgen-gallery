Brief

The canvas depicts a lone, central tree situated upon a gently sloping hill. Over continuous time, the tree undergoes the cycle of the four seasons. Leaves begin as bright green, shift through vivid reds and oranges, and gradually turn brown before falling to the ground. Following the leaf fall, gentle snow descends, accumulating on the hill and coating the bare branches. This snow eventually melts away, allowing small buds to appear, which quickly blossom into vibrant flowers. Finally, new, lush green leaves emerge, completing the cycle and preparing for the next repetition.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch depicts a seasonal cycle of a lone tree on a gentle hill using p5.js. The visualization begins with spring green leaves, transitions through autumn reds and oranges, then winter bare branches, followed by snow accumulation and melting, and finally blooming buds that transform into flowers before new green leaves emerge to restart the cycle. The animation is entirely self-contained, advancing automatically through time without user interaction, demonstrating the passage of seasons through color shifts, leaf transformations, and environmental effects like snowfall and growth.
