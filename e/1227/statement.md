This sketch depicts a menacing mechanical shredding apparatus with visible internal gears and blades, positioned below a stack of books that are being consumed by the machine. The scene features relentless motion as books fall into the shredder, where they are torn apart by rotating gears and sharp blades. Intense friction generates bursts of glowing sparks, while plumes of steam vent from metal joints. As books are shredded, fine dust particles and sharp splinters are ejected upward, accumulating in a swirling cloud below and visibly etching and scarring the remaining books above the machine's mouth. The animation uses WEBGL rendering to create depth and motion effects, with particle systems for sparks, debris, and steam that respond dynamically to the mechanical action.
