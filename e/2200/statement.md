This sketch presents a still, intricate tessellation inspired by stained-glass window tracery, rendered in rich amber tones reminiscent of solidified beeswax. The composition is structured within a deeply grooved petrified wood frame, creating a contrast between the warm, glowing geometry and the dark, immutable grain. Using a grid-based approach with organic distortions, the work creates complex geometric forms that appear to flow and shift subtly while maintaining perfect stillness. The beeswax elements are composed of irregular polygons with cross-hatching and internal lines that mimic the delicate patterns found in traditional stained glass, all set against a deep gradient background that enhances their luminous quality. The frame is constructed with multiple layers of grooves that add depth and texture to the overall composition.
