This sketch presents a dynamic, cutaway view of a two-cylinder combustion engine running in real-time. Using the WEBGL renderer, it visualizes the core mechanics: pistons moving up and down within their cylinders, connected to a rotating crankshaft through connecting rods. The engine's timing is illustrated by synchronized intake and exhaust valves that open and close as pistons move, with colored fluid flows showing the direction of air/fuel mixture and exhaust gases. The motion is entirely self-contained, driven by mathematical calculations without any user input or external triggers. The visualization emphasizes the cyclical nature of engine operation through continuous animation and precise mechanical relationships.
