This sketch depicts two opposing swarms of luminous particles that move autonomously across the canvas, creating intricate helical vortex structures as they interact. The particles flow in continuous motion, weaving and diverging while being drawn into tightly coiled ribbons of light where their paths converge. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of particle physics, color coding, and dynamic line connections that emphasize the tension and attraction between opposing forces. The system uses a fixed number of particles with swirling motion patterns to simulate natural fluid dynamics without explicit interaction rules, resulting in an ever-changing spectacle of color and movement.
