This sketch creates a vibrant, chaotic dance of animated bear figures that populate the entire canvas with fluid motion and bold jungle colors. Each bear has its own unique size, velocity, rotation, and color palette, creating an ever-changing field of movement where creatures bounce off edges, sway in rhythmic patterns, and continuously reorient themselves. The bears are rendered with simplified but recognizable features - bodies, heads, ears, eyes, noses, arms, and legs - all drawn using basic 2D primitives like ellipses and translated shapes. The animation is completely self-contained, with no external input required after the initial setup, creating a mesmerizing, continuous performance that fills the screen with joyful, unpredictable motion.
