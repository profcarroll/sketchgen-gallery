This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of pulsing, expanding rings that evolve autonomously on screen. Using p5.js's color modes and trigonometric functions, it generates a hypnotic pattern where each ring varies in size, hue, saturation, and opacity over time. The rings expand and contract rhythmically while their colors shift smoothly through the spectrum, creating an ever-changing visual rhythm. The entire composition moves without any user interaction, relying purely on mathematical motion and color variation to produce a continuously evolving spectacle of shifting light and form.
