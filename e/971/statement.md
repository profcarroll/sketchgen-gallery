This sketch creates a Rothko-inspired composition using layered color planes that move gently across the canvas. Each plane is rendered as a large, soft-edged rectangle with a subtle wave motion that creates the illusion of depth and shifting energy. The technique uses multiple overlapping shapes with varying opacities and wave parameters to achieve the characteristic floating color field effect. The animation proceeds smoothly without any user interaction, demonstrating how simple geometric forms and organic motion can evoke the contemplative quality found in Rothko's work.
