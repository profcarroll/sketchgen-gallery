Brief

A large, substantial stack of books rests precariously above a visible, open mechanical shredding apparatus. Beneath the stack, massive internal gears and tearing plates are actively rotating, consuming books as they fall. The action is violent and dynamic: books are visibly shredded into pulp and wood fragments. This mechanical action generates constant debris—shredded dust, splinters, and wood pulp—which flies out and strikes the remaining books in the upper stack, visibly embedding themselves into the book spines and pages. The colors are rich browns, creams, and metallic grays, contrasted by the chaotic movement of the debris.
