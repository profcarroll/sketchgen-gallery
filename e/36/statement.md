This sketch creates an immersive underwater scene with a slow, flowing tide of translucent blue-gray circles that rise from the bottom of the dark canvas. The circles drift upward at different speeds and sizes, creating a sense of depth and continuous motion. Thousands of tiny pointillist stars dot the background, providing a celestial contrast to the organic flow of the circles. The viewer can interact with this flowing tide by clicking anywhere on the canvas, which triggers a ripple effect that pushes nearby circles apart before they resume their gentle ascent. The subtle color variations and translucent layers create a dreamlike quality reminiscent of oceanic movement and cosmic space simultaneously.
