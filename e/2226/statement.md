This sketch creates an immersive, shifting tunnel of crystalline geometry that draws the viewer's eye into an endless spiral. The composition uses a palette of cool blues and teals mixed with warm peaches and lavenders to evoke the interplay of light through mineral formations. Through the constant rotation and pulsing movement of thousands of geometric particles—spheres, boxes, tori, and cones—the viewer experiences a sense of being pulled outward from a central void, as if navigating through a geological vortex. The visual rhythm builds intensity through layered motion and depth effects, creating an overwhelming yet mesmerizing experience of continuous transformation.
