This sketch depicts a sewing machine stitching a seam in real-time as the cursor moves across the canvas. The stitching mechanism creates individual stitches of uniform length (10 pixels) with consistent spacing (15 pixels) between them, forming a continuous seam that follows the path of the cursor. The visual representation shows discrete stitch segments rather than a continuous line, maintaining precise spacing and length throughout the process. The machine head is represented as a small tracking element that follows the cursor position, while the fabric surface features a subtle grid pattern to enhance the textile aesthetic. The implementation uses fixed-size stitch segments and maintains a bounded history of positions to ensure smooth performance within the frame budget constraints.
