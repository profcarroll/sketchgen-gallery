This sketch creates a dynamic visualization of an intricate cable network that resembles the infrastructure of a data center or computer system. The dark metallic cables form a complex tangle across the screen, with luminous data streams traveling along them in rhythmic pulses. Each stream is a distinct burst of neon-colored light that moves smoothly along the cable paths, creating a sense of continuous information flow and energy transfer. The animation runs continuously without user input, demonstrating the constant activity of such systems through the steady motion of glowing particles across the network structure.
