Brief

The canvas displays an intricate, complex network of interwoven cables and wires, forming a dense, structural tangle. The cables themselves appear dark and metallic, providing a framework for luminous data streams. Energy pulses, rendered in vivid, neon colors, travel along the thicker sections of the cables.
