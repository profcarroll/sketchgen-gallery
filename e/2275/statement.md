This sketch presents a microscopic view of a crystalline structure under constant fluid flow. The architecture is a rigid lattice of interlocking panels rendered in warm plaster tones, establishing a clear geometric framework. Within this fixed structure, a dynamic fluid—composed of hundreds of animated particles—flows continuously and interacts with the sharp edges of the panels. The scene captures both order and chaos: the stable framework provides a constant backdrop while the fluid material constantly shifts, creating connections between particles and the rigid geometry. When the viewer clicks, a ripple emanates from the point of impact, dramatically reshaping how the liquid segments intersect with the fixed architecture. The visual effect uses particle physics with Perlin noise for natural movement, and the click interaction creates a visible wave that distorts the fluid flow pattern, simulating the disruption of a crystalline structure by external force.
