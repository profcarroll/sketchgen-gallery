This sketch depicts the transformation of rigid crystalline structures into a viscous, muddy riverbed through the interplay of geometric dissolution and falling dominoes. The crystalline formations appear to erode and break down over time, with their sharp edges gradually softening into the flowing sediment below. Falling dominoes cascade through this evolving landscape, their mechanical motion contrasting sharply with the slow, heavy flow of the muddy riverbed. The piece captures a continuous tension between order and chaos, where structured geometric forms give way to fluid, organic movement. The visual rhythm is maintained by the steady fall of dominoes against the slowly changing crystalline structures, creating a sense of ongoing transformation.
