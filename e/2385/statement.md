This sketch renders a deep cross-section of fossilized coral as a complex mineral structure composed of layered formations that gradually peel back to reveal deeper, more densely packed layers. The visualization uses fractal patterns to create organic-looking strata with rich coloration in orange, red, and brown tones reminiscent of oxidizing steel. When clicked, the view focuses on a specific junction where layers meet, revealing intricate mineral structures and allowing viewers to trace the hidden depth of the fossilized formation. The animation creates a sense of slow geological revelation while maintaining an abstract representation of ancient life patterns within mineral accretion.
