Brief

The color palette gradients smoothly from cool emerald greens to deep warm amethysts, creating a mesmerizing depth. Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a mesmerizing kaleidoscope pattern that continuously evolves within a hexagonal boundary. Using p5.js's color mode and trigonometric functions, it generates flowing lines that sweep outward from the center while rotating in complex patterns. The color palette smoothly transitions from cool greens to warm amethysts through the HSB color space, creating depth and visual interest. Multiple layers of intersecting lines create intricate patterns that shift and flow over time, with each sweep of the pattern building upon the previous one to form a unified, constantly changing artwork. The motion is entirely self-generated without any user input, fulfilling the requirement for autonomous visual change.
