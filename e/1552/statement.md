This sketch creates a visual simulation of a piano keyboard with both white and black keys arranged in a recognizable pattern. When a user clicks on any key, a ripple effect originates from that point and expands outward across the surface of the key. The ripples begin with bright, random colors and gradually transition into a deep emerald green as they spread. The interaction is immediate and visually engaging, demonstrating both the physical layout of a keyboard and a dynamic response to user input through light effects.
