This sketch creates a mesmerizing subterranean space filled with organized streams of light. Using WebGL rendering, it generates multiple ribbons that flow in continuous helical patterns through the void. Each ribbon follows a precise mathematical path while maintaining its own color identity and pulsing luminosity. The composition appears to drift autonomously, creating an impression of phosphorescent energy moving through deep space. The visual effect relies on carefully calculated motion paths and consistent color transitions rather than random particle behavior, resulting in a cohesive, hypnotic display that evolves without viewer input.
