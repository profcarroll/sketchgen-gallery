This sketch depicts a nocturnal cityscape that evolves from abstract light streaks to a structured geometric grid. Initially, particles move erratically across the screen, simulating traffic light trails in a dark urban environment. As time progresses, these elements gradually coalesce into precise, orthogonal lines and illuminated nodes that form a rigid architectural framework. The transition illustrates how chaotic movement can organize into perfect, crystalline structures against a deep blue-black background. The visual rhythm emphasizes the underlying order of urban design emerging from apparent randomness.
