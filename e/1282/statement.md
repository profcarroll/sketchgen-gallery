This sketch creates a mesmerizing display of concentric rings that expand and contract in perfect harmonic rhythm. Each ring is rendered with a distinct fluorescent color, cycling through a vibrant spectrum from red to green to blue. The rings oscillate smoothly and predictably, mimicking the steady pulse of a sine wave. The motion is entirely self-generated, requiring no viewer interaction—each ring moves independently yet in harmony with others, creating an entrancing visual effect that fills the canvas with pulsing, glowing geometry.
