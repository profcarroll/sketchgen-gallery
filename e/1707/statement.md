This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic environment where translucent circles drift slowly across a dark void, punctuated by pointillist stars. From time to time, bursts of light radiate outward in expanding rings, leaving behind a lingering afterglow that gradually fades. The continuous motion and transient luminous events evoke the sense of a fluid, ever-evolving universe. The scene is entirely self-contained, with no external inputs required once initialized.
