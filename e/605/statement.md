This sketch visualizes a cross-sectional loudspeaker mechanism with a central magnet and oscillating wave patterns. The design features a prominent circular magnet at the center of the canvas, surrounded by visible magnetic field lines that extend outward. Radiating from this core are expanding concentric circles representing sound waves — these waves pulse and grow in size while fading in opacity, creating a dynamic visualization of vibrational energy. The animation runs autonomously without user input, with new wave patterns generated at regular intervals to simulate the continuous vibration of a speaker cone. The cool blues and whites of the waves contrast against the darker background, emphasizing their energetic movement.
