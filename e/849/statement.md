This sketch depicts a haunting marine scene of decay and transformation. Paper-cut ships drift through a translucent, salt-weathered medium, their hulls showing visible patina, barnacles, and structural damage. As they move, they leave behind dissolving wake trails that form mineral streaks of crystalline salt deposits. Parts of the ships detach into drifting fragments that accumulate at the bottom, while root-like structures extend from the hulls, constantly breaking and detaching. The scene captures the gentle, inevitable deterioration of maritime vessels in an environment where time itself is being dissolved by salt and decay. The visual language combines organic weathering with geometric crystalline patterns to create a sense of ongoing transformation.
