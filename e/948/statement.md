This sketch immerses the viewer in a tunnel of accelerating molecular space, where luminous crystalline structures pulse and shift in rhythmic synchronization. The scene uses WebGL rendering to create an abstract, geometric environment filled with moving forms that accelerate into the distance. Each crystal is composed of multiple rotating facets and a central core, responding to a subtle internal pulse that creates a sense of life and energy within the sterile molecular landscape. The viewer experiences a continuous motion through space, with crystals resetting at the rear of the tunnel to create an endless passage.
