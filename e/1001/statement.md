This sketch creates a mesmerizing kaleidoscopic pattern within a hexagonal frame using a combination of smooth globular splines and sharp crystalline lattices. The core visual tension comes from the interaction between two dynamic systems: a lattice structure that exerts outward forces on the canvas, and globular splines that flow around and react to these forces. The splines stretch, distort, and morph continuously as they respond to the rhythmic motion of the lattice, creating a complex visual tapestry of interwoven forms. The WEBGL renderer enables the 3D depth and perspective that gives the pattern its intricate, self-contained motion, while the continuous animation maintains the rhythmic tension described in the brief without requiring any user input after initialization.
