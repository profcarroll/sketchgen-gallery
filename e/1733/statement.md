This sketch creates an ethereal cosmic environment where translucent, flowing circles move like a slow tide across the canvas. Each circle carries a glowing trail that gradually dissipates into sparkling particles, creating a hypnotic interplay between continuous motion and ephemeral light. The visual effect is achieved through careful management of particle systems, with each circle maintaining a history of its path while continuously pulsing in luminosity. The field of stars gently pulses with varying brightness, adding to the sense of a living, breathing cosmic space.
