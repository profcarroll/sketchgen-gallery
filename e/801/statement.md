This sketch visualizes a tidal zone cross-section with layered water bands that rhythmically oscillate with the tide. The water's movement is represented through undulating colored bands that expand and contract in sync with simulated tidal cycles. Within this flowing structure, discrete plumes of lighter color emerge and move directionally, representing the organized flow patterns of water during tidal transitions. The visualization captures both the macroscopic rhythm of rising and falling tides and the microscale turbulence as distinct water currents form and dissipate, creating a dynamic interplay between large-scale wave motion and localized directional flows.
