This sketch creates a vibrant, ever-shifting geometric field reminiscent of stained glass and Bauhaus design. Through hundreds of dynamically evolving polygons—each with rotating, pulsing forms—the canvas presents a perpetual motion of color and structure. The visual effect emerges from simple rules: particles move with subtle velocity, bounce off screen edges, and their shapes morph through sinusoidal pulses and rotational changes. The result is an abstract, kaleidoscopic display where solid geometric forms dissolve into fluid patterns of pure light, capturing the essence of refraction as described in the brief.
