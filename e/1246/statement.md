A lighthouse on a dark headland, its lamp throwing a pale wedge of light back and forth over a night full of slow fog. The beam is a stack of shortening triangles that add up to a soft fade with distance, and three banks of translucent ellipses drift at different speeds; any puff the beam passes through warms from cold blue-grey to candlelight and thickens, then sinks back into shadow. Stars twinkle above, and the water below holds a faint column of moon glitter that flares whenever the beam sweeps across it. It asks nothing of the viewer; it is meant to be watched for a while.
