Brief

The canvas displays two distinct, continuous streams of vibrant colored particles. These streams flow smoothly across the screen, maintaining separate paths while showcasing contrasting hues. As the two colored rivers approach one another and enter a shared proximity, the interaction causes a momentary, fluid exchange of colors between the particles, making them blend into each other's palette before continuing their paths. The movement is continuous, requiring no viewer input to observe this vibrant dance.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch visualizes two distinct streams of colorful particles flowing in opposite directions across the canvas. The particles move smoothly and continuously, maintaining their separate paths while creating a dynamic interaction where they blend colors when coming near each other. The contrasting hues—blue-green and pink-cyan—highlight the fluid exchange that occurs in their shared proximity. The effect is a mesmerizing dance of color and motion, entirely self-contained without requiring any user input to observe.
