This sketch creates an immersive, dreamlike environment of pulsing luminous forms suspended in deep space. Using the WEBGL renderer, it generates a field of glowing particles that rhythmically pulse between emerald and cyan hues while drifting through a crystalline network structure. The scene is self-contained and continuously evolving without any user input, with particles moving in three-dimensional space and casting soft glows that reveal an underlying geometric framework. The visual effect suggests electronic life within a cosmic dream structure, where the pulsing forms and their shadows create an organic rhythm against a dark void.
