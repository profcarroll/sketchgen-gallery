This sketch presents a static, meditative lattice structure that evokes the appearance of bleached, calcified bone. Using the WEBGL renderer, it creates an intricate network of rigid, organic geometric forms that grow outward from a central point, forming repeating patterns across the entire canvas. The composition maintains perfect stillness while suggesting endless proliferation through its structured repetition and careful attention to form. The color palette draws heavily from rusty reds and pale ivories, creating a muted, timeless quality that emphasizes the static nature of the work. Each structure is built from simple geometric segments that define their form without any movement or animation, allowing viewers to contemplate the quiet complexity of the patterned arrangement.
