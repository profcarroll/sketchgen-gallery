Brief

The canvas displays a dynamic, flowing network of liquid light streams derived from a loaded photograph. Particles are continuously spawned, following an invisible field dictated by the source image's luminance. These streams translate the source's chromatic information into pure, liquid trails that flow and dance across the canvas. Where the individual streams overlap, their boundaries are maintained with crystal-clear definition, creating a brilliant, overlapping tapestry of light that moves ceaselessly across the frame.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a mesmerizing, flowing network of liquid light streams that emerge from a loaded photograph. Particles are dynamically spawned and guided by an invisible force field derived from the image's luminance — bright areas push particles away, dark areas attract them, creating a continuous, organic flow. Each particle samples color directly from the source image, rendering vibrant trails that dance across the canvas in a seamless tapestry of light. The system uses a toroidal wrapping technique to maintain continuity, and the particle count is optimized for performance while preserving visual complexity and fluid motion.
