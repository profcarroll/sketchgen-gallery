This sketch creates a dynamic radar scan visualization with glowing blue and violet trails expanding from a central point. The animation features hundreds of lines that spiral outward in circular patterns, creating a rhythmic sense of depth and motion. The visual effect is achieved through a particle system where each line element has its own speed and angle, continuously updating to form an ever-expanding network. The deep blue and violet color palette gives the impression of a high-tech radar display with glowing trails sweeping across the screen in a continuous, mesmerizing pattern.
