This sketch presents a first-person journey through a desolate, brutalist architectural landscape composed of massive concrete monoliths. Using p5.js's WEBGL renderer, it creates an immersive environment of geometric, weathered structures that move steadily past the viewer—simulating drone flight through an abandoned campus. The scene employs simple yet effective lighting and shading to emphasize the stark, angular forms characteristic of brutalist design, with subtle window details adding depth and visual interest. The motion is continuous and automatic, creating a sense of passage through space without requiring any user interaction beyond the initial click needed to activate audio.
