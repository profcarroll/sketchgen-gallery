This sketch creates an immersive subterranean data flow visualization using the WEBGL renderer for depth perception. I've crafted luminous streams that move slowly through a vast dark void, with each stream composed of connected points that fade in and out as they progress. The phosphorescent trail particles drift and dissipate within the abyss, creating a sense of continuous motion and information drainage. The scene emphasizes organic flow through gentle particle movement and subtle color variations, avoiding excessive detail that would exceed performance budgets. The viewer observes an infinite depth landscape where data streams appear to drain into darkness while leaving faint residual marks that slowly fade away.
