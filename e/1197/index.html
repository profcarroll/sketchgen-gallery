Brief

Violet seismic waves ripple across the screen while thick jagged lines fracture rhythmically into perfect, overlapping geometric circles. These fracturing shapes pulse independently without reacting to any user input; neither mouse movement nor clicks alter the display. The animation proceeds autonomously, creating a mesmerizing loop of independent motion where the canvas evolves solely on its own merit.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch visualizes an abstract seismic landscape where violet waves emanate from random points across the canvas, expanding outward in perfect circles. Overlaid on this are geometric fracturing shapes that pulse and rotate independently, creating a rhythmic, hypnotic pattern of motion. The animation is entirely autonomous — no user interaction affects the display — with each element evolving according to its own internal timing and physics. The visual effect combines organic expansion with precise geometric forms, producing a mesmerizing interplay between chaos and order that unfolds continuously without external influence.
