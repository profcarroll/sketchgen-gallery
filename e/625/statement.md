This sketch creates an abstract network of glowing particles that pulse rhythmically like a living data field. The composition features a dark background with luminous connections that subtly brighten and dim in synchronized waves across the entire structure. Each particle has its own hue and size, creating visual depth while maintaining cohesive color harmony. The pulsing effect is achieved through sine wave modulation applied to both connection alpha and particle brightness, giving the impression of breathing energy flowing through the network. The system operates autonomously with no external input, creating a mesmerizing display of interconnected information that appears to pulse with steady life.
