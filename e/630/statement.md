This sketch creates a dark, intricate digital network that pulses with life. It depicts a chaotic web of glowing data streams in muted tones, with luminous energy flowing through the system. At major junction points where streams intersect, secondary spiral streams are born, creating an effect of continuous digital breathing. The visual metaphor captures the idea of a living, pulsing digital ecosystem where information flows and transforms. The network appears to be constantly evolving as new streams emerge, existing streams fade, and secondary spirals form and dissipate, creating an organic sense of movement through a technological landscape.
