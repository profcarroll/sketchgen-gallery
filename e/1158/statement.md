This sketch presents a structured visualization of the periodic table elements arranged in their natural grid layout. Each element is displayed as a distinct tile with its chemical symbol and full name. The entire structure pulses with an ambient glow that continuously shifts in intensity and color hue, creating a subtle but mesmerizing animation. The glow originates from a consistent source that moves smoothly across the canvas, providing a sense of life and motion without disrupting the static nature of the periodic table itself. The effect is achieved through careful control of color blending and alpha transparency, ensuring the glow remains visible and animated while preserving the clarity of the underlying grid.
