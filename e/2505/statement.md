This sketch renders a crystalline mineral lattice structure that evokes the hidden circulatory system of a vast subterranean organism. Through careful use of 3D geometry and dynamic lighting, it creates an intricate network of intersecting lines that appear to pulse and shift in space. The cool, muted color palette emphasizes the geological nature of the form while maintaining an organic quality. The structure begins with generalized patterns visible from a distance before focusing into a dense, interconnected web that suggests both mineral formation and biological complexity. The viewer's perspective slowly rotates, revealing the full scope of this underground network as it appears to breathe with subtle motion.
