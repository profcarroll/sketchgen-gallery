This sketch creates a futuristic cityscape at twilight where rigid structures are etched with glowing crystal markings and bathed in perpetual wet reflections. The scene features a dense urban environment built from towering buildings that shimmer with constant luminescence, interacting with pulsing bioluminescent plant life that generates dissipating ripples across the reflective ground surface. The interplay between the steady glow of architectural elements and the rhythmic pulsing of organic light sources creates a dynamic, almost living cityscape where both artificial and natural elements pulse in harmonic rhythm. The scene uses webgl rendering to achieve the reflective wet surfaces and volumetric lighting effects that give the city its ethereal quality.
