This sketch creates an evolving landscape of abstract pathways that emerge from a newsprint texture, like faded memories bleeding through time. The field is populated with constantly shifting, diagrammatic forms that grow and drift across the canvas, each one a trail of wet ink responding to its own internal dynamics and the viewer's presence. As you move your mouse, glowing connections trace the cursor's path, creating a continuous map of forgotten moments that expands and contracts in real-time. The work uses layered drawing techniques with a procedural texture to evoke the feeling of a living document—something that remembers but never fully reveals its contents, always in flux between visibility and oblivion.
