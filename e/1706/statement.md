This sketch presents an interactive stovetop interface where a viewer manipulates a central dial to control burner heat. As the dial is turned, burners illuminate with increasing intensity, producing flickering flames of varying height. The core visual effect is the heat shimmer that radiates outward from active burners — a wavy distortion that intensifies with heat input. The simulation uses particle systems for flame dynamics and concentric ripple effects to model thermal radiation, creating an immersive, responsive kitchen visualization.
