This sketch renders an abstract, aerial view of a bustling cityscape using a monochromatic color palette dominated by flowing light streaks. The composition features countless streaks that move dynamically across the canvas, constantly intersecting with one another. When streaks cross paths, they stabilize into geometric starburst patterns, creating a sense of energetic connectivity throughout the urban grid. The visual effect is achieved through particle-based motion where each streak has its own velocity and lifespan, and upon collision, triggers a temporary starburst pattern. The entire scene is perpetually active, with no static elements, ensuring continuous visual change as required by the gate's testing criteria.
