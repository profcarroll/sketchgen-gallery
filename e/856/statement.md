This sketch creates an ever-shifting abstract composition of geometric planes that collide and interact in three-dimensional space. Using the WEBGL renderer, it draws multiple large diagonal planes that move with varying speeds and directions, each rotating independently to create complex visual dynamics. The planes are rendered with faceted surfaces that appear to crystallize as they shift, creating an illusion of depth and motion through careful manipulation of lighting and geometric form. The composition is entirely self-contained, requiring no viewer interaction, and evolves continuously to present a vivid, abstract visualization of perpetual motion and transformation.
