This sketch depicts the transformation of chaotic, random particles into an ordered geometric structure. Initially, particles move freely with unpredictable motion across the canvas, creating a vibrant, dynamic field of color. Over time, these particles begin to organize themselves into visible lattice patterns and straight lines that pulse with light. As the system evolves, the particles are drawn toward each other by invisible forces, forming crystalline, metallic tapestries with repeating fractal patterns. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of particle physics simulation and spatial hashing for efficient interaction detection, culminating in a frozen state where the chaotic elements have been transformed into a structured, glowing lattice structure.
