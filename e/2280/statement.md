This sketch depicts an underwater vista of a decaying clockwork network, where massive skeletal structures made of oxidized bronze and porous cardboard drift in a murky, mineralized space. The scene captures the slow, inevitable passage of time through the constant shedding of rust flecks that spiral downward and dissolve into the background. The geometric arrangements of gears and housing units provide a rigid, decaying counterpoint to the gentle downfall of memory, rendered as particles that slowly detach from central machinery and drift through the scene.
