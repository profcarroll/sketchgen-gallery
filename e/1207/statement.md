This sketch creates a visual and auditory response to user interaction by generating concentric ripple effects that emanate from the point of impact. When clicked, the canvas produces a soft chime tone accompanied by three distinct visual elements: an expanding ripple, a fading shockwave, and a temporary solid ring that gradually dissipates. The technique uses layered transparency and motion to create depth and temporal complexity in the visual response, with each element having its own speed of expansion and rate of fade. The audio is triggered only on the first user gesture to comply with browser security requirements for audio initialization.
