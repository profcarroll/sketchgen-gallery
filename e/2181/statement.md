This sketch creates a mesmerizing visualization of mineral vein formation, inspired by the slow, searching growth patterns of slime mould. Neon pink lines emerge from the center and spread outward in unpredictable directions, branching and recombining as they travel across the black canvas. Each line pulses with a granular, beeswax-like texture that mimics the organic rhythm of geological processes. The system generates thousands of these veins over time, each following simple rules of growth and branching while maintaining a cohesive, flowing pattern that evokes both the volatile heartbeat and the patient, crystalline formation of mineral deposits.
