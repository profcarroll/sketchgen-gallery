This sketch depicts the skeletal framework of a vast, decaying Gothic cathedral rendered in the precise, clean lines reminiscent of 1960s pen plotter drawings. The structure consists of interconnected nodes and beams that pulse rhythmically, creating visible distortions that ripple through the architectural form. Each nodal point expands and contracts in a cyclical pattern, causing the supporting arches to subtly warp and bend, giving the impression of structural life and decay. The stark black-on-pale background emphasizes the geometric precision of the lines, while the rhythmic pulsation creates an internal, hypnotic movement that transforms static architecture into a living, breathing form.
