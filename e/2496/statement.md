This sketch creates a static textile pattern composition rendered in deep jewel tones, featuring a repeating lattice grid of stylized floral motifs arranged symmetrically. The design is contained within a pronounced border made of stacked rhombus shapes that contrast with the organic fluidity of the central floral designs. The palette draws from rich mineral and gemstone hues—deep ocean blues, emerald greens, ruby reds, violets, and plums—to evoke the appearance of an expensive, detailed rug. The pattern structure remains fixed and decorative, with no motion or animation, fulfilling the brief's requirement for a static, contemplative visual experience.
