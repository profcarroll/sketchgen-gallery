This sketch presents a haunting aerial view of a decaying brutalist campus, where rigid concrete structures struggle against the relentless advance of nature. Through continuous camera movement, viewers witness an immersive drone flight that reveals the ongoing battle between human architectural ambition and the overwhelming force of encroaching vegetation. The scene unfolds organically without any input, with structures and plant life dynamically arranged to create a sense of vastness and time's passage. The monochromatic concrete buildings are rendered with subtle textural details, while overgrown vegetation emerges from courtyards, showing how nature slowly integrates with and deteriorates the harsh geometry. The visual tension between the brutalist forms and organic growth creates a contemplative space that reflects on themes of decay, resilience, and humanity's relationship with its built environment.
