This sketch creates a retro driving simulation where players experience the sensation of moving through a winding road environment. The viewer sits in a vehicle that automatically accelerates forward while they control steering with arrow keys and braking with the space bar. The road geometry features a central path that curves gently left and right, with branching side paths appearing intermittently along the route. As the simulation runs, visual elements shift to create motion effects: dashed road markings scroll past the viewer's vehicle, and side paths emerge from the roadside as the journey continues. The scene demonstrates classic driving game mechanics in an abstract, minimalist style that emphasizes movement and direction through purely geometric forms and color contrasts.
