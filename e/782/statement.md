This sketch creates an immersive tunnel through a molecular and atomic space where crystalline structures fracture into glowing shards. The visual experience combines rhythmic bioluminescent pulses with energy conduits that guide floating particles, simulating the interaction between superheated plasma and crystal formations. Particles emerge from the center of the tunnel, following attractor points along rotating conduits while gradually fading out and being replaced. The constant camera movement and pulsing background create a sense of acceleration through an abstract molecular environment, where each frame reveals the dynamic interplay between geometric structures and luminous particle flows.
