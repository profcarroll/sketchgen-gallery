This sketch creates a mesmerizing ripple effect by continuously generating concentric rings that emanate from the center of the canvas. Each ring expands outward with a pulsing animation and fades away over time, creating a visual representation of expanding waves. The vibrant fluorescent colors shift through the hue spectrum, giving the impression of a rhythmic burst of color and motion. The pattern is entirely self-contained, requiring no viewer interaction to observe its dynamic behavior, as rings continuously originate from the center point and propagate outward in an endless cycle of expansion and dissipation.
