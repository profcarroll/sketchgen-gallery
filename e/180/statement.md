This sketch depicts a playful forest gathering of stylized bear silhouettes that move in synchronized, rhythmic patterns across the canvas. Each bear is composed of simple geometric shapes—ellipses and arcs—that form organic, flowing forms with brown bodies, white eyes, and red cheek blushes. The figures continuously sway and shift position using sine wave motion, creating a choreographed dance that feels both natural and joyful. The color palette blends earthy tones with bright accents like yellow and red to emphasize the lively energy of the scene. The bears move in unison yet each has its own unique timing, producing an effect of collective motion without rigid synchronization, capturing the spontaneous joy of woodland creatures coming together.
