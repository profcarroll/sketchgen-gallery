This sketch creates a serene cosmic scene where translucent, overlapping circles flow slowly across the canvas, forming an ethereal tide of color and motion. Within this fluid environment, tiny luminous stars drift gently in the opposite direction, creating a compelling contrast between the continuous current of the circles and the subtle counter-movement of the stars. The composition evokes a sense of deep space, where vast, slow currents carry visual elements while smaller, independent movements hint at hidden dynamics.
