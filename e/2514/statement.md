This sketch depicts a microscopic honeycomb structure composed of porous, luminous cells that pulse with ethereal energy. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of organic motion and color modulation. Each honeycomb cell gently pulses in hue and brightness, creating an impression of living tissue or mineral formation. Through the cellular structure flow glowing streams of energy that move organically along noise-generated paths, their colors shifting dynamically as they travel. The interplay between the static honeycomb framework and the flowing energy creates a sense of deep subterranean life force moving through ancient material, with rapid color fluctuations and luminous ripples suggesting both heat and captured biological energy.
