This sketch explores the tension between rigid mechanical structures and organic fluidity through a dynamic lattice system. Particles flow in and out of alignment with each other, creating both sharp angular connections and soft, spreading textures. The visual system evolves from dense clusters to more open formations, echoing biological growth patterns or celestial mechanics. The interplay between individual particle movement and collective behavior generates an ever-shifting architecture where mechanical precision gives way to flowing materiality. The central forces pulse outward, creating rhythmic expansion and contraction that drives the entire system's evolution without external influence.
