This sketch renders a macro-scale visualization of an underground root network composed of delicate, branching pathways that resemble layered cut paper. The system uses thin, translucent cables to represent the root structure, arranged in a complex three-dimensional web that suggests natural growth patterns. Luminous energy streams pulse rhythmically along these pathways, creating a continuous flow of vibrant light that illuminates the entire network. The visualization employs subtle depth and motion to evoke the feeling of an intricate subterranean ecosystem where energy continuously moves through interconnected channels, mimicking the way nutrients and signals travel through real root systems.
