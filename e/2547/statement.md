This sketch presents a contemplative study of decay and forgotten information. It depicts the surface of aged newsprint with a creamy yellow texture, overlaid with layers of wet ink that creep and fade across the paper. The piece explores how time transforms materials—how the deep blue-black ink interacts with the fibrous paper, creating redundant marginalia and cross-outs. The static nature of the composition reflects on how forgotten information persists, slowly eroded by time. Through subtle motion and layered visual effects, it captures a quiet meditation on structural decay and the passage of memory.
