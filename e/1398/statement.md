This sketch creates a dynamic, audio-reactive visualization of kinetic shapes that pulse and flow across the screen in response to sound input. The composition features glowing spheres and geometric polygons that shift in size, color, and rotation based on the amplitude and frequency spectrum of live microphone input. Shapes continuously move across the canvas while their visual properties respond instantly to musical beats and bass drops, creating an abstract representation of rhythm through color, motion, and form. The visualization maintains a consistent frame rate and responsive behavior without external dependencies beyond the core p5.js library.
