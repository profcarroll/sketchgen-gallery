This sketch transforms the concept of intersecting planes into a microscopic mineral formation, focusing on the slow growth and connection of quartz veins. The viewer observes a dark, crystalline environment where glowing mineral structures emerge and expand from their origins. These veins grow gradually over time, creating an organic, geological aesthetic that pulses with subtle energy as they approach one another. The connections between nearby veins form a luminous network that intensifies when the structures come into proximity, evoking the slow, patient processes of natural crystal formation at a scale visible only through careful observation.
