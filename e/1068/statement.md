This sketch creates an interactive ripple effect that responds to user clicks. When a user clicks anywhere on the canvas, a soft chime sound plays and multiple concentric circles expand outward from the click point. The ripples maintain a fluctuating form as they grow, then rapidly shrink and disappear. The visual effect is achieved through layered ellipse shapes with varying opacity and timing, creating a smooth, organic ripple motion that appears to pulse and fade naturally. The audio component uses p5's sound API, triggered by the initial user interaction to ensure proper browser audio context activation.
