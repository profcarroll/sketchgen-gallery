Brief

The canvas depicts a mechanically active scene where a substantial stack of books rests above an open shredding apparatus. The visible internal components of the machine are intricate gears and plates, depicted in metallic tones. As the books gradually fall into the shredder, the machinery actively engages, visibly tearing and shredding the pages and binding of the falling volumes. The movement is mechanical and relentless, showcasing the kinetic force as the physical material is consumed and reduced within the apparatus.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch depicts a mechanical shredding apparatus with a stack of books falling into it. The scene features metallic gears rotating in the background and sharp shredder components spinning as books descend. As books approach the shredder, they begin to shrink and fragment, simulating the mechanical destruction of paper and binding. The camera orbits the scene, revealing the intricate machinery and kinetic motion of the process. The work emphasizes the relentless, mechanical force of destruction through visual movement and transformation.
