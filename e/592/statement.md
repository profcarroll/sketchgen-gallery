This sketch creates a retro-style driving experience with a constantly scrolling road that curves in the distance. The viewer controls a vehicle using arrow keys to steer and spacebar to brake, navigating around randomly positioned obstacles on the road edges. The road features a main path with parallel side roads, and subtle curvature effects at the horizon that give depth to the scene. The implementation uses efficient drawing techniques to maintain performance while creating an engaging visual representation of a moving highway environment.
