Brief

Violet seismic waves ripple across the screen while thick jagged lines fracture rhythmically in perfect, overlapping geometric circles. These fracturing lines pulse with a life of their own, moving independently without any user interaction required to sustain the animation. No mouse movement or clicks alter the pattern; the display remains an autonomous performance of vibrating shapes and shifting geometry that evolves continuously on its own.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates an autonomous animation of violet seismic waves rippling across the screen while thick jagged lines fracture rhythmically in overlapping geometric circles. The visualization features multiple concentric circles that continuously rotate and fracture into jagged segments, with pulsing effects that create a sense of organic movement. The waves emanate from random points on screen and expand outward, creating an interplay between smooth circular motion and sharp, irregular line work. All animation occurs autonomously without any user interaction, with each element following its own timing rhythm while maintaining harmonic visual relationships. The color palette emphasizes deep purples and violets that shift subtly in saturation and brightness to create depth and visual interest.
