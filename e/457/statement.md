This sketch simulates the industrial rhythm of a printing press by combining continuous motion with periodic crystallization. Moving stripes flow vertically across the screen at varying speeds, representing the endless stream of paper or material through the machine. Every few seconds, the motion pauses and geometric crystal patterns emerge, glowing and rotating to fill the space. The visual tension between fluid movement and sharp, frozen forms mirrors the mechanical precision and rhythmic repetition inherent in industrial processes. The piece operates entirely on its own, creating a mesmerizing display of controlled chaos that reflects both the efficiency and artistic potential of mechanical systems.
