This sketch depicts a microscopic world of liquid mercury flowing in an endless, swirling current. A bright kite structure floats within this fluid medium, tethered by a thin, luminous line that traces its path through the liquid. The kite's movement is guided by the viewer's mouse when clicked, allowing it to dance and interact with the continuous flow of the surrounding mercury. The scene captures the delicate interplay between controlled motion and natural fluid dynamics, where the kite becomes a point of connection between human intention and the organic flow of the environment.
