This sketch creates a dynamic, ever-shifting landscape of overlapping serpentine lines that flow across the canvas like living ribbons. Each line is composed of many points that move according to Perlin noise functions, creating organic, unpredictable motion patterns. The lines continuously morph and twist, weaving through each other in a choreographed dance of color and form. The visual effect emerges from the interaction of hundreds of animated points, each following its own path while being subtly influenced by global timing, resulting in a rich tapestry of movement that feels both chaotic and harmonious. The entire composition is self-contained, requiring no user input to generate its continuous, mesmerizing animation.
