This sketch explores the passage of time through a visual metaphor of mechanical decay and petrification. It depicts a close-up view of brass clockwork machinery constructed from corrugated cardboard, where time appears to slow as delicate mechanisms undergo calcification. The scene captures the gradual encapsulation of intricate workings in crystalline formations, creating a tableau of decay and transformation. The viewer observes gears rotating slowly, linkages fading away, and mineral deposits slowly forming around them — all rendered with a translucent sediment background that suggests deep geological time. The piece conveys the quiet, persistent march of time through subtle changes in motion, color, and form, transforming mechanical complexity into fragile, crystalline relics.
