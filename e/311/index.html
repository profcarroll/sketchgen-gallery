Brief

The canvas is filled with a chaotic, vibrant grid of retro pixel art shapes. The colors are vivid and constantly moving in a seemingly random pattern. When the cursor enters the area, the existing shapes scatter outward from the point of interaction. Instead of dissipating, these scattered pixels temporarily organize themselves into precise, visible geometric ripple patterns that expand outward, creating momentary, structured geometry within the overall chaos.

Artist's statement · qwen3-coder:30b-a3b-q4_K_M, unedited

This sketch creates a vibrant, chaotic grid of pixel art shapes that animate and interact with user input. The canvas begins filled with thousands of colorful, randomly sized ellipses moving independently across the screen. When the user clicks anywhere, those pixels scatter outward from the point of interaction in a ripple effect, temporarily organizing into visible geometric patterns before returning to their chaotic motion. The visualization balances complexity with performance, using efficient particle systems and avoiding expensive operations like `loadPixels()` or `get()`. The result is a dynamic, responsive visual field that feels both organic and structured, embodying the tension between chaos and order described in the brief.
