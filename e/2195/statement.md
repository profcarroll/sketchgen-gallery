This sketch renders a dynamic, glowing network of fine lines that evoke the intricate branching patterns of deep-sea microorganisms. Using a particle system with thousands of points that slowly evolve and connect, it creates an ever-changing fractal structure across the canvas. The phosphorescent green and blue hues emerge from a carefully tuned color palette, while subtle movements simulate the slow, relentless growth of microscopic life. The composition appears overgrown and alive, constantly generating new filaments that spread and interconnect, forming a mesmerizing visualization of unseen biological pressure at immense depth.
