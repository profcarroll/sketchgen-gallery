This sketch visualizes an electrocardiogram pattern as a continuously scrolling, glowing neon line on a dark canvas. The waveform is generated algorithmically with irregular peaks and deep dips that simulate chaotic organic energy, creating the impression of a live, complex heartbeat reading. The motion is steady and relentless, with three layered lines of varying opacity and speed that create depth and visual complexity. The intense cyan glow gives it a fluorescent neon quality while maintaining the scientific precision of an EKG pattern. The animation runs smoothly without external input, demonstrating how computational patterns can create compelling visual rhythms that appear alive and dynamic.
