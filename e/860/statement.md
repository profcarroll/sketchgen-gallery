This sketch creates a minimalist analog clock with a clean, uncluttered face and two distinct hands. The hour hand is thicker than the minute hand and both are rendered in subtle shades of gray to suggest brushed steel. The hands move smoothly and continuously, reflecting real-time passage without any numbers or markings on the dial. The design emphasizes the essential function of telling time through position alone, with careful attention to visual weight and proportion that makes each hand clearly distinguishable while maintaining a sense of elegance and simplicity.
