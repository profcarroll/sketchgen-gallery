This sketch renders a monochromatic savanna landscape dominated by deep terracotta earth tones with visible cracks and fissures. The scene features a pale beige sky above and a dramatic low-angle light source that casts long shadows across the cracked terrain. Faint dust devils drift across the ground in spiral patterns, occasionally coalescing into larger swirling formations. The composition captures an intense sense of stillness punctuated by subtle movement, with the constant animation of the dust devils providing dynamic contrast to the static landscape. The technique uses a combination of pre-generated cracked earth elements and particle-based dust devil systems that evolve over time.
