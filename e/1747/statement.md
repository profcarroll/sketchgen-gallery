This sketch renders a dynamic, pulsing cityscape from a high-altitude perspective. Thousands of glowing light trails move randomly across the canvas, creating dense intersections that spontaneously form geometric lattice structures—triangles, squares, and pentagons. These shapes pulse rhythmically, as if breathing with the city's energy, while the trails continuously reform and regenerate. The composition captures the organic, living quality of a sprawling urban environment through abstract motion and emergent patterns, all rendered in a single continuous canvas without any user interaction beyond the initial setup.
