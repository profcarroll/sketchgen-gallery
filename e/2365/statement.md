This sketch depicts the slow, mesmerizing process of ink permeating a white space, gradually pulling inward from the edges toward a central point. As the liquid ink accumulates, it transforms into an ordered geometric pattern, like dominoes falling in sequence. The viewer observes the controlled progression of particles moving inward, forming increasingly complex shapes that maintain their structural integrity through pure geometric order. The work explores themes of transformation and emergence, where chaos gives way to mathematical precision as the system evolves over time.
