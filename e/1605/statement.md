This sketch creates a geometric sundial with three animated suns that cast overlapping shadows across its surface. The suns orbit the center of the canvas at different speeds and angles, causing their shadows to shift and rotate continuously. Each sun projects both a circular shadow boundary and individual shadow lines from random points on the sundial face. The interplay of these three shadow systems creates complex intersecting patterns that evolve over time, illustrating the passage of hours through the changing positions of celestial bodies. The viewer observes a timeless yet dynamic representation of how solar motion determines time, with each sun contributing its own shadow signature to the overall temporal rhythm.
