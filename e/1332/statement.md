This sketch creates a vibrant 1970s-inspired mural using geometric abstraction and luminous arcs. The composition features interlocking shapes in saturated colors that flow and vibrate slowly across the canvas. A constant field of faint, glowing concentric arcs moves independently, creating a sense of underlying energy. When clicked, the entire arrangement instantly restructures while maintaining the foundational arc field, producing dramatic visual transformation. The piece uses the WEBGL renderer to achieve depth and color saturation reminiscent of the era's environmental graphics, with careful attention to the frame budget for performance.
