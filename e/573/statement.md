This sketch visualizes a dynamic system of geometric fragments in a state of constant flux, representing stressed equilibrium. The network of sharp, angular shapes moves autonomously across the canvas, with connections forming and dissolving between nearby elements. A subtle pulse effect radiates from each fragment, suggesting contained energy. Periodically, the entire system undergoes a violent collapse — fragments scatter chaotically — before reorganizing into a new stable configuration. This cycle of destruction and rebirth creates an hypnotic rhythm, capturing the tension between order and disorder in a self-sustaining loop.
