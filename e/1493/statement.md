This sketch depicts a colorful kite fluttering in the sky, responding dynamically to simulated wind gusts and controlled by mouse movement. The kite is represented as a simple geometric shape with physics-based motion that includes acceleration from wind forces and angular rotation for a natural fluttering effect. When the user clicks and drags, the kite follows the cursor, allowing interactive control over its position in the breezy sky. The visual design uses a soft blue sky background to contrast with vibrant kite colors, creating a serene yet dynamic scene where the kite's movement is both autonomous and responsive to viewer input.
