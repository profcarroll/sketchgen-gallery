Brief

The scene depicts a vast, deep-sea expanse where sharply defined, crystalline pyramidal structures emerge from the ocean floor. These geometrically precise pillars display evidence of natural decay, featuring subtle yet constant signs of advanced erosion and sediment accumulation at their bases. The overall color palette shifts between deep marine blues and the pale, mineral tones of the weathered crystal. The water surface maintains a slow, rhythmic movement, emphasizing the gradual, time-worn interaction between the living sea and the slowly degrading, magnificent architecture rising from its depths.

This sketch depicts an underwater landscape where crystalline pyramidal structures rise from the ocean floor. Using the WEBGL renderer, it creates a sense of depth and movement through careful lighting and camera positioning. The pyramids display signs of natural erosion with sediment accumulation at their bases and weathered mineral tones that shift between deep blues and pale crystal hues. The water surface features rhythmic wave motion that emphasizes the continuous interaction between the sea and these ancient architectural elements, creating an immersive scene of geological time and marine environment.
