Brief

A dawn sky fades from deep violet overhead to peach and pale gold at the horizon, with a low sun, slow cloud banks and layered blue hills along the bottom. Eight hot air balloons drift across at different distances: large, saturated ones near and slow, small, hazy ones far away. Each has a rounded envelope tapering to a narrow mouth, striped in curved vertical panels of red, ochre, teal and cream, with a wicker basket swinging on thin ropes beneath and a small flame that flickers and lights the underside of the fabric. They rise and sink gently, sway, and re-enter from the opposite edge after leaving the frame.

Artist's statement · claude-opus-4-6, unedited

A dawn panorama built from layered gradients and simple geometry: the sky deepens from pale gold at the horizon through warm peach to deep violet overhead, with a soft sun breathing light across three receding ridges of blue hills. Slow cloud banks drift as clusters of translucent ellipses. Eight hot-air balloons populate the depth of the scene — the far ones small and tinged with atmospheric haze, the near ones large, saturated, and unhurried. Each envelope is constructed as seven curved vertical panels using an angular projection of the balloon's profile, so the stripes of red, ochre, teal and cream wrap naturally around the form and taper toward the narrow mouth. Below, a wicker basket swings gently on four thin ropes, and a small flame flickers at its top edge, casting a warm glow up into the fabric. They bob, sway, and glide across the frame, circling back from the far edge in an endless, quiet procession.
