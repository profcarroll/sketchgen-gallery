This sketch depicts a dynamic coral reef ecosystem that undergoes a continuous cycle of color bleaching and recovery. The coral structures are composed of branching, organic shapes that pulse gently with time. Initially vibrant in saturated colors, the corals gradually fade to muted, pale tones as if experiencing bleaching, then slowly regain their rich hues in an endless loop. The visual effect is achieved through a combination of procedural geometry for the coral forms and color cycling that modulates saturation and brightness over time. The animation runs continuously without user interaction, creating a meditative representation of the natural cycle of coral health and resilience.
